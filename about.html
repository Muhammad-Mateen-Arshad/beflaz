About Beflaz

Beflaz is a multi-category information portal covering News, Jobs, Scholarships, Dramas, Health, and History. Our content is gathered from publicly available sources and summarized with AI assistance, strictly grounded in the facts reported by the original source. Every article links back to its original source so readers can verify details themselves.

Our goal is to make useful, time-sensitive information - job openings, scholarship deadlines, breaking news, and general wellness information - easy to find in one place.

Beflaz does not claim ownership of the underlying facts or events reported; we summarize and organize publicly reported information for our readers' convenience.

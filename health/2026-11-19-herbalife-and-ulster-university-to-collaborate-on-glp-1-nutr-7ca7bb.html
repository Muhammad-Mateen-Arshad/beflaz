Herbalife and Ulster University to collaborate on GLP-1 nutrition support research

Published on November 19, 2026

As GLP-1 weight-management therapies grow in popularity, researchers are turning their attention to how targeted dietary adjustments can support individuals during treatment. A new clinical trial led by Ulster University in partnership with Herbalife aims to examine how specialized nutritional support may help address physical and dietary challenges linked to these therapies.

Key Takeaways

  • Ulster University and Herbalife are studying the impact of protein- and micronutrient-enriched shakes for adults starting GLP-1 therapies.
  • The study forms part of the Peacetime obesity care initiative, which serves 9,000 individuals across Ireland and Northern Ireland.
  • GLP-1 medications suppress appetite, but research suggests they can lead to muscle loss and nutrient deficits.
  • The research aims to explore strategies that preserve lean muscle and maintain balanced nutrition during weight loss.

What the Source Says

According to information provided by Herbalife and project representatives, GLP-1 medications mimic naturally occurring satiety hormones and slow gastric emptying, prompting users to reduce daily calorie intake by approximately 10% to 30%. Data shows that these medications are used by about 11% to 12% of adults in the United States and 4% to 7% in the United Kingdom.

While effective at lowering overall body mass, GLP-1 therapies can create nutritional concerns. Research indicates that lean muscle accounts for roughly 25% to 39% of total weight lost by GLP-1 users. Additionally, a review published in Clinical Obesity found that 12.7% of GLP-1 patients developed a new nutritional deficiency within six months, particularly involving vitamin D, iron, calcium, magnesium, potassium, and choline.

To evaluate potential solutions, the Ulster University-led trial will investigate how nutrient-enriched shakes support individuals with obesity who are starting GLP-1 treatment. Embedded within the Peacetime program led by Prof Alex Miras, the project aims to shift focus toward preserving muscle mass and supporting sustainable nutritional health.

Practical Notes

For adults over 60 managing weight, preserving muscle strength and receiving adequate essential vitamins and minerals are key aspects of overall health. Specific dietary instructions or enrolment criteria for the trial were not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Always speak with your doctor before making changes to your diet, taking new dietary supplements, or starting any weight-loss medication.

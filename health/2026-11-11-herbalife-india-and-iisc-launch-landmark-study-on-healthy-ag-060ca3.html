Herbalife India and IISc Launch Landmark Study on Healthy Ageing

Published on November 11, 2026

Key Takeaways

  • Herbalife India and the Indian Institute of Science (IISc) have partnered to launch the 'Health & Happiness' research study focusing on healthy ageing in Indian adults.
  • The multi-center initiative will evaluate metabolic health, gut microbiome composition, biological markers, daily lifestyle practices, and community connection.
  • The project is designed to involve over 1,000 participants, with each individual tracked for approximately six months.

What the Source Says

As India's population grows older, researchers are looking beyond lifespan to focus on how well individuals age. Herbalife India and IISc report that while the impacts of metabolic health and nutrition are widely acknowledged, less is understood regarding how social ties, daily support systems, and biological markers interact to shape long-term wellness.

The study will take place across four key locations: Bengaluru, Mysuru, Hyderabad, and Chennai. Through a multidimensional approach, researchers plan to evaluate biological markers, physical health indicators, gut microbiome health, dietary choices, and general wellbeing. Mr. Ajay Khanna, Managing Director of Herbalife India, noted that the initiative aims to build scientific insights grounded in the everyday lives and habits of Indian adults.

Prof Deepak Saini of the Department of Developmental Biology and Genetics at IISc stated that the effort focuses on tracking real health changes over time using rigorous, independent methods. Research suggests that the data generated could eventually provide helpful insights for individuals, healthcare professionals, and policymakers making health-related decisions.

Practical Notes

Understanding the connection between daily choices, social involvement, and physical wellness remains a central focus of scientific research into healthy ageing. Details regarding participant registration or specific eligibility criteria are not specified in the source; please check the official notice for further details.

Always talk to your doctor before making any major changes to your diet, exercise routine, or daily health habits.

Can eating peanuts daily help protect your brain function?

Published on November 06, 2026

Key Takeaways

  • Research suggests that daily consumption of unsalted, skin-roasted peanuts may improve insulin response in specific brain areas of older adults.
  • The observed changes occurred in right-side brain regions associated with memory and cognitive function.
  • Scientists indicate that a combination of nutrients within peanuts likely contributes to these effects rather than a single component.

What the Source Says

A study conducted by researchers at Maastricht University Medical Center in the Netherlands, published in the journal Food & Function, examined the effects of daily peanut intake on brain health. The trial involved 31 healthy participants between 60 and 75 years old.

During one phase of the study, participants ate 60 grams of unsalted, skin-roasted peanuts every day—representing roughly two servings—for 16 weeks. In another 16-week phase, the same participants abstained from eating peanuts completely. This design enabled the research team to compare the outcomes of both dietary periods.

After the 16-week peanut intervention, tests revealed that insulin responsiveness improved in several areas on the right side of the brain. These regions included parts of the hippocampus, lingual gyrus, parahippocampal gyrus, and posterior cingulate gyrus, all of which are involved in memory and other thinking processes.

Dr. Peter Joris, the senior author of the study, noted that insulin helps support memory and cognitive performance in the brain. However, brain sensitivity to insulin can decrease as people age, an alteration that has been connected to cognitive decline. The researchers reported that multiple natural compounds and nutrients in peanuts likely work together to encourage brain health.

Practical Notes

While these findings suggest a positive connection between nut consumption and brain insulin function, details regarding long-term cognitive outcomes beyond 16 weeks or dietary recommendations for individuals with medical conditions are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Are Your Plant-Based Meat Alternatives Hiding Fungal Toxins?

Published on November 20, 2026

Key Takeaways

  • Research suggests fungal toxins are present in plant-based items, though amounts in tested samples fell below European Union safety guidelines.
  • Concentrations were generally higher in plant-based meat substitutes than in plant-based drinks.
  • Experts state the findings do not mean these products cause illness or should be removed from your diet.

What the Source Says

As reported by Men's Journal regarding a study published in Food Control, researchers from Cranfield University and the University of Parma tested 212 plant-based foods and beverages purchased in the United Kingdom. The testing screened 92 meat alternatives—including replacements for burgers, sausages, and chicken—and 120 beverages for 19 types of mycotoxins. These naturally occurring compounds are created by fungi and can enter seeds, grains, and legumes during growth or storage.

Fungal toxins were detected in every sample tested, but all measured amounts remained lower than recommended European Union guideline limits. However, plant-based meat substitutes generally showed higher concentrations than plant-based beverages, and certain items contained multiple toxins simultaneously.

Andrea Patriarca, a senior lecturer in mycology at Cranfield University, noted that these substances occur naturally and cannot be completely avoided. The researchers explained that their main concern involves cumulative dietary exposure over time, especially since established regulations to monitor mycotoxins in these newer, multi-ingredient plant products do not currently exist.

Practical Notes

The study focused on items purchased in the United Kingdom; specific contamination levels for products in other regions were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Rather than eliminating plant-based choices, the report recommends focusing on dietary variety and rotating different protein sources, rather than assuming a plant-based label automatically guarantees superior safety or nutrition.

Always talk to your doctor before making any major changes to your diet.

The Future of Aging Well May Have More to Do With Your Cells Than Your Skin Care Routine

Published on November 20, 2026

Key Takeaways

  • Research highlighted by Timeline suggests that healthy aging involves supporting cellular processes and mitochondrial function alongside standard skincare routines.
  • The compound Urolithin A, featured in the proprietary ingredient Mitopure, is studied for its role in helping the body clear out damaged mitochondria through mitophagy.
  • Timeline points to clinical findings suggesting an increase in muscle strength of up to 12 percent after 16 weeks of use.
  • Dietary supplements are not a replacement for physical strength training, a balanced diet, or professional medical care.

What the Source Says

As wellness discussions evolve, research is looking closer at cellular maintenance rather than superficial appearance alone. Timeline, a Swiss longevity company founded by doctors and scientists, focuses on mitochondrial health through its patented postbiotic ingredient, Mitopure. Mitopure delivers Urolithin A, a compound researched for its role in supporting cellular energy and muscle health. Unlike NAD+ precursors, research suggests Urolithin A works by aiding mitophagy, the body's natural system for removing compromised mitochondria.

According to Timeline, its work is supported by over 18 years of research and more than 10 clinical studies. Among its approved findings, data suggests muscle strength can increase by up to 12 percent after 16 weeks of use. The brand offers Mitopure in daily softgel capsules—which are vegan, non-GMO, gluten-free, and free of eight major food allergens—as well as strawberry-flavored gummies delivering 500 mg of Mitopure per two-gummy serving. Timeline also incorporates cellular science into topical products, including firming serums and exfoliators.

Practical Notes

For individuals navigating perimenopause or menopause, changes in body composition, hormones, and muscle mass make physical strength an important focus. While research regarding Urolithin A and mitochondrial health shows promise, the source clarifies that these dietary products should not be mistaken for a menopause treatment.

Supplements cannot take the place of essential lifestyle factors, such as regular strength training, nutritious eating, and appropriate clinical care. Please talk to your doctor before adding any new dietary supplement to your daily routine.

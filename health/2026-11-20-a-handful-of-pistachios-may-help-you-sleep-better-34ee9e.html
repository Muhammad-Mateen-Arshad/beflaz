A Handful of Pistachios May Help You Sleep Better

Published on November 20, 2026

Key Takeaways

  • Research suggests pistachios contain a unique combination of sleep-supportive components, including tryptophan, magnesium, vitamin B6, and naturally occurring melatonin.
  • A scientific review in Nutrition Research Reviews highlights how gut bacteria breaking down pistachio fiber and polyphenols might gradually release plant melatonin.
  • While initial laboratory models offer promising insights, researchers emphasize that current findings do not prove pistachios improve sleep, and human trials are ongoing.

What the Source Says

Difficulty getting adequate sleep is widespread, with nearly one-third of U.S. adults reporting less than seven hours of nightly rest. According to information cited from Food & Wine, a single one-ounce serving of pistachios contains about 71 milligrams of tryptophan. The human body utilizes tryptophan to create serotonin, which helps manage mood and sleep, and melatonin, which signals to the body that it is time to sleep.

The nuts also contain vitamin B6 and magnesium, which support standard nervous system activity. Magnesium specifically plays a role in the function of gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA), a neurotransmitter that helps calm nerve signaling. Dr. Marie-Pierre St-Onge, director of the Center of Excellence for Sleep and Circadian Research at Columbia University Irving Medical Center and the review's senior author, noted that pistachios are unique because they combine several sleep-related compounds within one whole food matrix. Laboratory digestive simulations suggested that gut microbes digesting the nut's polyphenols and fiber may slowly release plant-based melatonin, potentially supporting communication between the gut and brain.

Practical Notes

The published review was supported by funding from the National Institutes of Health and American Pistachio Growers. While human clinical trials are currently underway to measure real-world effects on sleep duration and quality, pistachios remain a nutrient-dense snack providing essential minerals, protein, healthy fats, and fiber. Exact recommended serving times or specific intake amounts for sleep benefits are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Readers should always consult their doctor regarding sleep concerns or before making dietary changes.

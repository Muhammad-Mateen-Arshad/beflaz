SNFCC Launches Active Aging Hub With Activities for Seniors

Published on November 12, 2026

Key Takeaways

  • The Stavros Niarchos Foundation Cultural Center (SNFCC) in Palio Faliro, Athens, has launched an Active Aging Hub focused on older adults.
  • The initiative provides structured programs centered on physical movement, memory stimulation, creative arts, digital learning, and social engagement.
  • Research by the National Center for Social Research (EKKE) accompanies the initiative to study the quality of life and social participation of older adults in Greece.

What the Source Says

The SNFCC Active Aging Hub was established to support mental well-being, movement, and community connections among older adults. Supported by NN Hellas and founding sponsor Lumidis Papagalos, the center offers diverse community programming alongside research conducted by the National Center for Social Research (EKKE).

The schedule provides tailored activities designed for cognitive and physical health:

  • Walking and Sensory Programs: "A Stroll in the Park" offers group walking sessions from November 19 to June 10. Additionally, "A Walk in Stavros Niarchos Park: Memory and Senses," organized with Alzheimer Athens for adults aged 60 and over, runs from December 15 through May 2027 to engage senses and encourage physical and mental stimulation.
  • Physical Fitness and Movement: The "Exercise, Memory, and Balance" program combines movement and cognitive exercises on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays. Weekday morning fitness advice, weekly Tai Chi classes, creative movement workshops ("Bodies in Motion"), and social dance events ("Time to Dance!") are also offered.
  • Technology and Art Workshops: Interactive memory enhancement classes in the computer lab and digital skill workshops run through February. Weekly Wednesday art sessions offer hands-on creative projects.

Practical Notes

Exact pricing details for every individual session and contact phone numbers are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Certain activities require advance registration or booking, whereas sessions held on the running track and Tai Chi classes operate on a first-come, first-served basis.

Before starting any new exercise routine or physical activity program, you should talk to your doctor.

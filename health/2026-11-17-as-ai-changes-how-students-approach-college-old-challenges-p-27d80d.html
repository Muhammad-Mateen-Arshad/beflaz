As AI Changes How Students Approach College, Old Challenges Persist

Published on November 17, 2026

Understanding how stress, financial demands, and institutional support affect learners can offer valuable context for adults following modern education trends or supporting family members in school.

Key Takeaways

  • Survey data from Inside Higher Ed indicates that emotional well-being and financial constraints are major factors affecting academic progress.
  • Flexible scheduling helps accommodate diverse learners, including students aged 25 and older.
  • Despite positive ratings for overall educational quality, many students experience stress related to essential daily needs and emerging digital tools.

What the Source Says

Research published by Inside Higher Ed from a survey of 5,034 undergraduate students across 310 mostly public institutions highlights significant personal challenges in higher education. Only 11 percent of surveyed undergraduates reported experiencing no major barriers to their academic success.

According to the findings, mental health is the most common hurdle, cited by 41 percent of students. Financial strain was the second most frequent concern at 37 percent. Furthermore, 61 percent of respondents stated that financial reasons forced them to go without at least one essential necessity, such as food, housing, or transportation, during the past year.

The study also examined how course options accommodate different groups. About 53 percent of all respondents feel very well served by their institution's course delivery options and schedules. Research suggests this share increases to 62 percent among community college students and adults aged 25 and older, who may need added scheduling flexibility.

Additionally, eight out of ten students routinely use artificial intelligence tools. While some indicate these resources assist with studying and understanding complex concepts, others face new anxieties, such as modifying their writing style to avoid false flags from detection software.

Practical Notes

Managing life stressors, financial concerns, and changing routines is an important part of overall well-being at any stage of life. Specific clinical strategies for stress management are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

If you or a loved one are experiencing health concerns or high levels of stress, please talk to your doctor.

ETHealthworld Nutrition and Wellness Awards celebrates India’s nutrition and wellness leaders

Published on November 27, 2026

Key Takeaways

ETHealthworld announced the recipients of its Nutrition and Wellness Awards on November 27, 2026. The awards highlight various organizations, individual professionals, and consumer initiatives operating across the health, clinical nutrition, and fitness sectors in India.

What the Source Says

According to ETHealthworld, several organizations and individuals received recognition across multiple wellness categories:

  • Clinical Nutrition and Supplements: Jolly Healthcare was recognized for Excellence in Clinical Nutrition, Ace Blend for Nutritional Supplements, and CLEVIRA for Ayurvedic Medicine. ITC Limited received the award for Maternal & Child Nutrition Product of the Year.
  • Featured Brands and Start-ups: Wellbeing Nutrition was selected as the Nutrition and Wellness Brand of the Year. Keystone NeoGreens was recognized as both the Emerging Nutrition and Wellness Brand and the Health & Wellness Start-up of the Year. Sri Sathya Sai Annapoorna Trust received the Farm-to-Table Brand of the Year award.
  • Individual Experts: Dr. Anu Mahajan was named Nutritionist of the Year. Dr. Rohini Somnath Patil and Dr. J.C. Balachandar (Dr. Bala) were recognized as Wellness/Fitness Gurus.
  • Technology, Initiatives, and Centers: Manipal Health Enterprises Limited received an award for AI Innovation in Wellness. Himalaya Wellness Company was recognized for its campaign, India's Stress Check. Recognized centers included the Maharishi Ayurveda Boutique Wellness Retreat in Rishikesh and Spartan Fitness in Gurgaon. Reliance Jio Infocomm Limited was cited for workplace wellness, and Natural Remedies Pvt. Ltd. was recognized for sustainable nutrition.

Specific details regarding product formulations, clinical evaluation methods, or health outcomes are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Practical Notes

Industry awards serve to highlight current trends and leading entities within the wellness sector. However, older adults considering new health solutions, nutritional supplements, or lifestyle programs should remember that award recognitions do not serve as individual medical endorsements. Always talk to your doctor before starting any new supplement, wellness service, or exercise program.

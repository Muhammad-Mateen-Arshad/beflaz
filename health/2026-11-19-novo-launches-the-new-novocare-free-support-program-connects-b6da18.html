Novo launches the new Novocare®: Free support program connects people with Wegovy® in addition to home delivery, everyday support, wellness partners and rewards

Published on November 19, 2026

Key Takeaways

  • Novo Nordisk has updated its free Novocare program to offer integrated lifestyle support and prescription access tools for Wegovy users.
  • The service includes coverage navigation, prescription refills, live assistance, and planned free home delivery for qualified members.
  • Members can connect with specialized wellness partners offering nutrition coaching, meal delivery, fitness tracking, and personal styling.

What the Source Says

According to Novo Nordisk, the redesigned Novocare membership experience provides digital support through a dedicated app and website. The program is designed for individuals interested in or prescribed Wegovy (semaglutide), an injectable or tablet medication indicated for weight management in people with obesity alongside diet and exercise.

The platform assists users in exploring costs, self-pay offers, and insurance options. Members can fill or refill prescriptions, use live chat or telephone support, and utilize quick checkout options. According to the announcement, free home delivery will soon be accessible to eligible members holding commercial insurance or government coverage, including Medicare Bridge. Novo Nordisk also plans to launch a rewards feature called Novocare Points by the end of the year, allowing qualified members to earn points redeemable for select partner offers.

To support daily wellness routines, Novocare incorporates health education, progress tracking, and community coaching. It connects users with partner resources including Berry Street for registered dietitian guidance, Factor for dietitian-approved GLP-1 meal plans, WHOOP for wearable health monitoring, and Stitch Fix for personal styling during body size changes. Complete eligibility details and specific pricing figures are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Practical Notes

Managing ongoing health goals involves addressing practical everyday needs alongside medical care. Integrated support options—such as personalized nutrition guidance, meal delivery services, and health tracking tools—may help individuals manage lifestyle changes more conveniently.

Be sure to talk to your doctor before beginning any new medication or making changes to your weight management routine.

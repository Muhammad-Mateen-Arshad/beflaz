A stronger approach to healthy aging

Published on November 16, 2026

As populations age, staying active remains essential for maintaining physical freedom and overall well-being. Recent discussions surrounding healthy aging highlight the growing importance of structured, intentional movement for older adults.

Key Takeaways

  • Focusing on resistance and balance exercises supports long-term mobility and daily independence.
  • Data from the 2021 Statistics Canada Census shows that more than 23% of Greater Victoria's population is aged 65 and older, which is higher than the national average.
  • Targeted strength training helps address sarcopenia, the natural age-related loss of muscle strength and mass.
  • Fitness programs for older adults should focus on tailored, balanced routines rather than pushing the body to its physical limits.

What the Source Says

According to the 2021 Statistics Canada Census, over 23% of Greater Victoria's residents are 65 or older. This demographic focus is visible in local exercise spaces, such as BPM Fitness Centre on Cloverdale Avenue. Owner Keelan Clemens, who brings 20 years of fitness industry experience, reports that nearly 60% of his clientele falls into this older demographic.

Clemens notes that the approach to healthy aging is evolving. Rather than assuming any physical activity is enough, fitness discussions increasingly emphasize intentional movement. Research suggests that resistance training plays a key role as people age by helping counter sarcopenia. Sarcopenia is the age-related reduction in muscle mass and strength that can adversely affect mobility, balance, bone density, and standard everyday activities.

Practical Notes

Achieving results through exercise does not mean straining the body to extreme points. Instead, guidance suggests working alongside qualified professionals—such as trainers with educational backgrounds in exercise science, biomechanics, or kinesiology—to design personalized programs aligned with individual goals, health needs, and physical limits. Specific exercise routines or facility schedules are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Before beginning a new physical fitness routine, please consult your doctor to ensure it is appropriate for your individual health context.

21 Ways Men Can Make Their Orgasms Even Better

Published on November 25, 2026

Key Takeaways

Research cited by experts indicates that male climax intensity is closely tied to overall physical health, vascular function, stress management, and mental engagement. Experiencing an orgasm releases natural neurochemicals that research suggests may support relaxation and mood regulation.

What the Source Says

According to James Elist, MD, a urologist specializing in male sexual dysfunction, orgasm intensity is influenced by lower stress levels, strong cardiovascular health, muscular fitness, and mental stimulation. Sex therapist Jon Prezant notes that climax strength often reflects how well background physiological systems—such as sleep, blood flow, hormone signaling, and stress regulation—are functioning.

Matthew Phillips, PhD, a sex therapist, explains that orgasms prompt a release of neurochemicals including dopamine, oxytocin, endorphins, and prolactin. These chemicals can counter the effects of chronic stress, support mood, and aid in sleep onset and deep rest.

The source outlines several physical techniques and considerations for men:

  • Perineum Stimulation: Arlene Goldman, PhD, points out that the perineum (the area between the scrotum and the anus) is packed with nerve endings near the prostate. Prezant suggests applying firm, steady fingertip pressure or a slow pulse to this area to add layer of sensation.
  • Prostate Focus: Prezant describes the prostate as a nerve-rich gland present in all male bodies. External massage around the area using lubricant, or gentle internal pressure toward the belly button, can create unique sensations.
  • Understanding Recovery Time: Following ejaculation, the body enters a refractory period where direct genital contact can feel overly sensitive. Sex therapist Michael Stokes, LMHC, and Prezant explain that blood flow, prolactin release, and nervous system recovery influence this recovery period. For older men, refractory times naturally range from several hours to a day or more. Pausing direct contact and focusing on slow breathing, touching other areas, or erotic thoughts can keep intimacy active while the body resets.

Practical Notes

Focusing on overall cardiovascular fitness, adequate rest, and stress reduction supports both general health and physical fulfillment. Specific personalized health strategies or medical evaluations are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Be sure to talk with your doctor if you have questions or concerns about your health, circulation, or intimate well-being.

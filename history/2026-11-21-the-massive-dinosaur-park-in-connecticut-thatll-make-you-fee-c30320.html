The Massive Dinosaur Park In Connecticut That’ll Make You Feel Like You’ve Stepped Into Jurassic Park

Published on November 21, 2026

The Discovery

Located in Oakdale, Connecticut, an immersive attraction known as The Dinosaur Place offers visitors a walk through a real forest filled with life-sized prehistoric recreations. As visitors navigate the winding woodland trails beneath the forest canopy, they encounter a landscape featuring a lily pad-covered pond and a charcoal-painted volcano sculpture streaked with red.

What Scientists Found

The park incorporates detailed sculptures and informational signs to teach visitors about ancient wildlife:

  • Brachiosaurus: Set near the water's edge, this large figure stands near the backdrop of the forest and playground.
  • Euoplocephalus: Displays heavy armor, spikes, bony plates, and unique bony eyelids from the Cretaceous period.
  • Styracosaurus: Styled in rust and brown shades with a head frill adorned by sharp, crown-like horns.
  • Triceratops: Positioned among forest ferns with its three horns pointed forward and mouth open.
  • Tyrannosaurus Rex: Recreated with open jaws and prominent teeth, accompanied by signage covering facts, size, weight, and pronunciation.
  • Therizinosaurus: Features a muted green and tan body with an extended neck near a gated section of the path.

Why It Matters

By placing sculptures along winding paths—including small predators with orange and black stripes hidden between fallen logs—the experience blends education with recreation. The site also includes a purple and green multi-level playground featuring climbing sections, tube slides, and a dinosaur head structure.

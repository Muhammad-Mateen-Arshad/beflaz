You Can Retire Comfortably On Social Security Alone In This Wonderfully Affordable Colorado Town

Published on November 27, 2026

The Discovery

Cañon City, situated in Fremont County at an elevation of roughly 5,300 feet, is defined by dramatic natural features shaped over vast spans of time. The region's defining geological site is the Royal Gorge, where the Arkansas River has carved a deep channel directly through granite. What Scientists Found

Geological processes in the area demonstrate millions of years of natural erosion. The river gradually sliced through the rock to form a steep canyon that drops more than 1,000 feet from rim to river. Alongside its ancient geological features, the town retains physical history in its downtown district along Main Street. Cohesive historic structures, including the Lincoln Bank building with its ornate stonework and distinct turret, showcase the durable building techniques of a past era.

Why It Matters

The location highlights an impressive combination of long-term geological history and preserved structural heritage. Key highlights of the area include:

  • A granite canyon plunging over 1,000 feet, created by millions of years of erosion from the Arkansas River.
  • The Royal Gorge Bridge, an engineering feat that held the world record as the highest suspension bridge for a extended period.
  • Main Street's historic architecture featuring brick facades and stone craftsmanship.
  • Local river habitats that support brown and rainbow trout populations.

DINO-A-LIVE DINOSAUR ODYSSEY brings 15 life-sized dinosaurs to Singapore

Published on November 17, 2026

The Event

A Japanese live theatrical production titled DINOSAUR ODYSSEY, created by DINO-A-LIVE, is coming to Singapore. Scheduled to take place from 3 to 6 December 2026 at the Resorts World Convention Centre in Resorts World Sentosa, the event marks the production's first large-scale indoor show outside of Japan.

What Visitors Will Experience

The approximately 70-minute show features 15 life-sized dinosaurs moving around the performance area rather than remaining as stationary exhibits. The featured species include:

  • Tyrannosaurus Rex and Triceratops
  • Brachiosaurus and Stegosaurus
  • Ankylosaurus and Parasaurolophus
  • Utahraptor, Suchomimus, Dakotaraptor, and Allosaurus

The non-verbal production uses music, dance, visual displays, and theatrical effects to recreate prehistoric settings. Audience members can observe realistic details close up, including blinking eyes, shifting muscles, and subtle body movements.

The Technology Behind the Dinosaurs

The realistic figures operate using DINO-TECHNE, a self-supporting mechanical suit system created by the Japanese firm ON-ART over more than two decades. Trained performers pilot the suits from within to replicate realistic dinosaur physical traits and motion.

Attractions and Ticketing Details

After drawing over 25,000 guests in Japan, the Singapore production expects over 30,000 visitors total, offering roughly 3,000 seats per performance. Attendees can also explore several venue features:

  • T-Rex Photo Experience: A S$30 add-on allowing groups of up to eight people to take a professional photo with a 12-metre-long Tyrannosaurus Rex, receiving a print and an Edokko Dinosaur Mini Plate (limited to 100 participants per show).
  • DINO Mart: A retail area offering over 100 Japanese products, including figures, fossil replicas, toys, stationery, lifestyle accessories, home décor, and collectibles.
  • DINO Café: Situated at Senmo Coffee, featuring a limited-edition menu of dinosaur-themed food and drinks.

Standard tickets cost between S$58 and S$128, with wheelchair-accessible entry set at S$38. A four-person family option is available for S$320, while a S$168 VIP tier includes front-row center seating, a Triceratops plush, and a photobook. A S$6 booking fee applies to purchases. Children aged three and older need a ticket, whereas children under three can enter free if seated on a parent's lap.

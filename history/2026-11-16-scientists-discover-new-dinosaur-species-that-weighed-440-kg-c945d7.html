Scientists discover new dinosaur species that weighed 440 kg at just two years old

Published on November 16, 2026

The Discovery

Palaeontologists have announced the identification of a new dinosaur species named Nyana rooiwalensis. The finding is based on exceptionally well-preserved fossil remains uncovered in modern-day South Africa. An artistic reconstruction of the creature was provided courtesy of the University of Withwatersrand.

What Scientists Found

Researchers conducted an analysis of the fossil remains and identified unique anatomical features that distinguish this animal from previously known groups. The findings reveal that the dinosaur reached a weight of 440 kilograms (880 pounds) when it was only two years old.

Further details regarding the species' adult dimensions, diet, or exact habitat are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why It Matters

The presence of distinct physical structures in such well-preserved fossils gives researchers new material to differentiate this species from established dinosaur groups.

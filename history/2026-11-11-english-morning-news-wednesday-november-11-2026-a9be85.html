Paleontological research continues to uncover fascinating details about ancient life through the study of fossilized remains. Recent developments from Spain and Thailand offer new perspectives on prehistoric creatures that inhabited the Earth millions of years ago.

The Discovery

Researchers have identified two distinct prehistoric species based on fossil evidence. In Spain, tiny fossils collected nearly 30 years ago were analyzed to reveal a previously unknown ancient amphibian species. Separately, fossil specimens found in southern Thailand led to the identification of a new species of giant rodent.

What Scientists Found

The fossil records provide key timelines for both ancient animals:

  • The Spanish amphibian lived about 66 million years ago, right before the extinction of the dinosaurs.
  • The giant rodent from southern Thailand lived approximately 34 million years ago.

Specific scientific names and exact physical dimensions for these species are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why It Matters

The amphibian finding demonstrates that stored fossil collections can produce remarkable discoveries decades after their initial collection. Meanwhile, the discovery of the giant rodent in Thailand suggests that giant rodents may have evolved independently across various regions of the world.

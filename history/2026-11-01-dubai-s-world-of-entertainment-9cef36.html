Dubai's World of Entertainment

Published on November 01, 2026

The Discovery

Dubai Holding Entertainment manages a collection of 20 leisure destinations, parks, and attractions across Dubai. Among its prominent sites is Global Village, which first opened its gates in 1997 and has hosted over 100 million guests over three decades. What Scientists Found

Specific geological or biological research is not specified in the source; please check the official notice. However, the venue provides extensive cultural and recreational features for visitors:

  • Season 31 operates under the theme "A More Wonderful World" and runs until May 2027.
  • The site includes 29 pavilions representing more than 90 cultures, featuring refreshed spaces such as the Sri Lanka Pavilion, the Russia and Azerbaijan Pavilion, and updated façades for Syria and Kuwait.
  • Visitors have access to over 3,500 shopping outlets and more than 250 dining venues offering street food, traditional dishes, and cafés.
  • An entertainment calendar features 40,000 performances, including stunt shows like Fast Live 2: The Police Strike Back, musical productions such as Bollywood Idol, and performances at the Kids Theatre.
  • The venue hosts over 200 attractions, including the Carnaval fun fair, Little Wonderers, The Dragon Kingdom, Secrets of the Lost City, The Horror Hotel, and the Tokyo Rush roller coaster.

Why It Matters

Global Village connects diverse international cultures by offering traditional crafts, regional food, and entertainment in one centralized location. Interactive tools like the Season 31 Passport and Kids Passport allow guests to collect stamps while visiting pavilions. Recent updates also focus on accessibility and convenience, including improved directional signs, upgraded parking, and accessible washroom facilities. Entry tickets for the destination start at AED25.

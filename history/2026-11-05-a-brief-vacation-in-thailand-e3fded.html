Published on November 05, 2026

The Discovery

During a day off on a business trip in Thailand, a traveler and a friend navigated via the BTS and a boat along the Chao Phraya River to visit Asiatique The Riverfront. While exploring the site—which features dining, shops, a Ferris wheel, and a swing ride—they discovered the newly opened "Jurassic World" attraction. What Scientists Found

Rather than a formal scientific site, the attraction provides an immersive walkthrough simulating a boat journey to Isla Nublar, costing nearly 1000 baht. The experience includes commemorative photos and features several prehistoric species and displays:

  • A Brachiosaurus welcoming guests at the entrance gates
  • An Ankylosaurus alongside a baby dinosaur
  • A Velociraptor area where four raptors reside and Blue displays special tricks
  • An Indominus zone where an unexpected mishap forces visitors to move along quickly
  • An area containing Pteranodons and Gyrospheres
  • A final encounter with a T-Rex that moves closer to guests before leading into a gift shop

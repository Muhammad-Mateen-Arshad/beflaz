Hong Kong's first dinosaur fossils go on display, revealing a giant over 20 meters long

Published on November 20, 2026

A landmark exhibition displaying the very first dinosaur fossils discovered in Hong Kong has opened at the Hong Kong Heritage Discovery Centre on Thursday. The event offers visitors an opportunity to view ancient remains uncovered locally, alongside additional fossil specimens brought from the Chinese mainland.

The Discovery

The featured specimens were uncovered in 2024 during excavations on Port Island in Hong Kong. To help visitors visualize the massive animal, the exhibition includes a model reconstruction depicting what the dinosaur may have looked like during its lifetime. Specific details regarding the exhibition schedule and admission fees are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What Scientists Found

Paleontological experts determined that the recovered remains date to the Cretaceous Period and likely originated from a single sauropod dinosaur. The recovered skeletal fragments include:

  • An ilium and pubis
  • A femur
  • Tail vertebrae

Based on the size of these fossilized bones, scientists estimate that the dinosaur was more than 20 meters long and stood over 3 meters tall at the hip.

Why It Matters

This discovery marks a significant milestone for regional paleontology. The specimens represent the first officially documented finding of this particular type of dinosaur along the southern coast of China, as well as the historic first discovery of dinosaur fossils within Hong Kong.

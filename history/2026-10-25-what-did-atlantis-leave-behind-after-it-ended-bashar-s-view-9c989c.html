What Did Atlantis Leave Behind After It Ended? Bashar's View of History and the Inheritance of Knowledge

Published on October 25, 2026

Explorations into the concept of Atlantis often focus on its downfall, but channeled perspectives offer a alternate view centered on what happened to its survivors and their knowledge.

The Discovery

Darryl Anka, who channels an entity named Bashar, presents a perspective on the transmission of ancient knowledge across eras. On July 30, 2022, Bashar Communications announced a talk titled "Hidden History." This event was introduced to examine the events surrounding the collapse of Atlantis, where its survivors relocated, and how their knowledge influenced modern culture.

What Scientists Found

This perspective is based on channeled narratives rather than history confirmed by archaeological excavation. In public statements, including an October 18, 2024 stream on "Wisdom From North," Anka outlined how this transfer of technology reportedly occurred:

  • Extraterrestrial technology may have been shared with an earlier civilization like Atlantis.
  • Following the destruction of Atlantis, survivors moved to the Mediterranean as well as Central and South America.
  • The knowledge carried by these survivors was passed on, linking to the construction of massive pyramids in Egypt and Central and South America.

Why It Matters

In a broadcast released on January 11, 2025, on "Next Level Soul," Anka detailed how knowledge crosses time. Instead of humanity resetting to complete zero when a civilization cycle changes, information remains in physical structures such as temples and pyramids. These clues allow later generations to rediscover ancient knowledge, enabling future societies to advance without needing to start over completely or repeat past destruction.

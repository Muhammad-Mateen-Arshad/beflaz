Paleontology News

Published on November 12, 2026

The Discovery

A broad array of fossilized remains and ancient artifacts highlights major findings across Earth's history. Recent reports reveal a 66-pound giant rodent discovered in Thailand, 115-million-year-old dinosaur blood, and soft tissue preserved for 450 million years. Additionally, researchers identified 1.4-million-year-old footprints belonging to a large human relative, as well as a 23-foot crocodylian that inhabited South America.

What Scientists Found

Studies of these ancient remains reveal critical insights into prehistoric life and early human behavior:

  • Early humans brought fire into caves 1.8 million years ago, and Ice Age populations in China created stone tools 146,000 years ago.
  • A 66-million-year-old tooth indicates that Tyrannosaurus rex was almost as warm as a human.
  • A 250-million-year-old fossil confirms that ancestors of mammals laid eggs.
  • Millipedes migrated onto land 80 million years before vertebrates did.
  • Spinosaurus spent the majority of its life underwater.
  • The first Diplodocus discovered outside North America was identified, and a giant dinosaur was unearthed under a Brazilian construction site.

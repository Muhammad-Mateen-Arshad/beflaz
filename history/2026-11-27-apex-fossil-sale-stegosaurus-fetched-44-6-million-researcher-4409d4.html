Apex Fossil Sale: Stegosaurus fetched $44.6 million; researchers can study fossil

Published on November 27, 2026

The Discovery

In 2022, commercial palaeontologist Jason Cooper uncovered the remains of a Stegosaurus on his private property in Colorado. The skeleton, which was preserved within sandstone, was later given the name Apex.

Specific details regarding the physical size or geological age of the specimen are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What Scientists Found

The discovery offers an important addition to the study of ancient life. Features of the fossil include:

  • A well-preserved Stegosaurus skeleton embedded in sandstone rock.
  • An arrangement that allows researchers the opportunity to study the fossil.

Why It Matters

Two years after its initial finding, the specimen sparked an intense bidding competition when Sotheby's listed it for sale in New York in July 2024. The fossil ultimately sold for $44.6 million, reaching a sum 11 times greater than the lowest estimate established by the auction house.

This historic transaction made Apex the most expensive fossil ever sold at an auction, establishing a new record for any natural-history specimen.

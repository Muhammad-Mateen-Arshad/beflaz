Rare dinosaur skeleton discovered in Montana sells for $47 million at auction

Published on October 29, 2026

The Discovery

A rare dinosaur skeleton found in Montana recently fetched $47 million at an auction in New York. The fossil was originally uncovered in 2019 by Montana rancher Jodi Pierson. Following the initial find, a dedicated team excavated the remains, and the skeleton was given the nickname "Jodi." The sale concluded after a fast-paced, 12-minute bidding war.

What Scientists Found

The excavated fossil is a specimen of Nanotyrannus, representing an unusually intact scientific discovery. Key elements of the find include:

  • The remains feature a nearly complete skull.
  • The fossil is one of only three substantial Nanotyrannus specimens known to modern science.

Details regarding the dimensions or geological age of the fossil are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why It Matters

At $47 million, "Jodi" ranks as the second most expensive dinosaur fossil ever sold at auction. Its high value underscores the extreme rarity of substantial Nanotyrannus remains available to researchers and collectors.

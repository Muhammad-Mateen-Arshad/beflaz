I actually used AI to create the game 'Path of Reflections' that I imagined a year ago!

Published on October 19, 2026

The Discovery

An idea discussed on November 4, 2025, was turned into a browser board game named "Path of Reflections: Mayu-sama's Unreasonable Life." As players roll dice to move the character Mayu-sama along a path, she uncovers various items. In one instance, she digs in a garden and finds a gold coin, and in another location, she finds an object that appears to be a dinosaur egg fossil. A sparkling visual display is featured whenever a rare item is found.

What Scientists Found

Scientific data regarding the origins, age, or structure of the fossilized item: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice. In the game itself, attempting to have the object appraised only results in the character paying an appraisal fee without receiving clear answers. Additionally, a figure known as the "God of Questions" can attach itself to the character, hindering normal movement according to dice rolls for two turns.

Why It Matters

The creation illustrates how written concepts can be turned into playable software through conversational assistance with ChatGPT. Key details of the game include:

  • An estimated completion time of 5 to 10 minutes.
  • A starting balance of 50,000 yen to navigate random life occurrences.
  • A mechanic where running out of money triggers an unexpected windfall rather than a game over.
  • Final outcomes offering various rankings, including Great Blessing, Middle Blessing, Small Blessing, Ending Blessing, Mystery Blessing, and a super rare God Blessing.
  • Inclusion of cheerful background music and custom audio effects.

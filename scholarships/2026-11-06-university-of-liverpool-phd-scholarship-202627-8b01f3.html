University of Liverpool PhD Scholarship 2026–27

Published on November 06, 2026

The University of Liverpool PhD Scholarship 2026–27 provides a 3.5-year fully funded studentship through the Department of Chemistry in the UK. Designed for high-achieving chemistry, physics, and materials science candidates, this funding supports advanced doctoral research in sustainable energy solutions. The application deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

This doctoral project focuses on developing sustainable energy technologies, specifically aimed at electrochemical ammonia production. The research plan explores electrolyte formulations, lithium-mediated nitrogen reduction, and novel materials to create cleaner and more efficient manufacturing methods.

Who Can Apply

Candidates interested in this studentship must satisfy the following entry standards:

  • Hold a Master’s degree with a minimum UK 2:1 classification or equivalent in Physics, Chemistry, Materials Science, or a related discipline.
  • Outstanding applicants possessing a First Class Bachelor’s degree in a relevant area may also be considered.
  • International candidates must demonstrate English language proficiency, achieving an IELTS Academic overall score of 6.5 with at least 5.5 in each component.

Benefits

The scholarship funding package includes the following financial support:

  • An annual maintenance stipend of £21,805 for the 2026–27 academic term.
  • Tuition fee coverage of £5,238 per year calculated at the UK rate.
  • A Research Training Support Grant to assist with academic expenses.

Full funding coverage for extra international tuition fees is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Interested applicants must submit an application using the university's online postgraduate research application portal for a PhD in Chemistry. It is required to include the complete project title and cite reference CCPR182 in the application. Candidates may also reach out directly to the project supervisor, Dr. Alex Neale, to discuss funding details and eligibility requirements.

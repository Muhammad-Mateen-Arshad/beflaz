Four International Scholarships for South Africans 2026

Published on November 17, 2026

Four International Scholarships for South Africans 2026 offer fully funded opportunities for undergraduate and postgraduate studies abroad. These funding options cater to South African students and postgraduate researchers planning to study in Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland. Deadlines for these opportunities approach before 5 December 2026.

Scholarship Overview

These international funding awards target South Africans seeking full-time academic programs or research positions across various global institutions. The covered options include the Lester B. Pearson International Student Scholarship at the University of Toronto, the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at UC Berkeley, the Gates Cambridge Scholarship, and the Swiss Confederation Excellence Scholarships.

Who Can Apply

  • University of Toronto: Final-year high school learners seeking a four-year undergraduate degree starting in September 2027 who receive a single institutional nomination from their high school.
  • UC Berkeley: South African citizens or forcibly displaced individuals living in Africa who hold an undergraduate degree from an African university, have no existing postgraduate qualification, and hold no dual citizenship or residency in the US, UK, Canada, or EU.
  • Gates Cambridge: South African candidates entering the general international selection round for the 2027/28 academic year.
  • Swiss Confederation: Early-career postgraduate researchers and artists under 35 years of age.

Benefits

Funding structures vary by provider. The University of Toronto covers full tuition, books, accommodation, and extra fees. UC Berkeley funds all study-related expenses, including tuition, housing, food, travel, and visa charges. The Swiss Confederation provides a monthly allowance of CHF 2,450 for research, PhD, or art tracks lasting between 12 and 36 months. Gates Cambridge award amounts are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Application procedures and closing dates depend on the specific institution:

  • University of Toronto: High school nominations close on 9 October 2026, and final scholarship applications must be submitted by 6 November 2026.
  • UC Berkeley: Applicants must secure admission to an eligible Master's degree course first, with scholarship applications generally due around 1 December.
  • Gates Cambridge: Full applications and supporting materials must be completed online by 11:59 pm UK time on either 8 December 2026 or 6 January 2027, based on the selected course.
  • Swiss Confederation: Submissions are made through Swiss diplomatic representations within South Africa.

Official notices emphasize that applying to these awards is free, and candidates should avoid fraudulent agencies charging fees.

PG&E STEM Scholarship Program 2026: Details and Eligibility

Published on November 07, 2026

The PG&E STEM Scholarship Program 2026, known officially as the Better Together STEM Scholarship Program, is an educational funding initiative provided by The PG&E Corporation Foundation. The awards support students from Pacific Gas and Electric Company's service area who are pursuing their first undergraduate degree in science, technology, engineering, or math. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

The PG&E Corporation Foundation recently awarded $327,500 in funding to 57 students through this program. The initiative is designed to help students build STEM careers and create innovative solutions for their local communities. The scholarship program is funded entirely by PG&E shareholders rather than customer rates. Since its launch in 2012, the initiative has awarded nearly $8 million to accomplished scholars.

Who Can Apply

To qualify for the scholarship, applicants must meet the following eligibility criteria:

  • Reside within the Pacific Gas and Electric Company service area in Northern and Central California.
  • Plan to enroll in full-time undergraduate study for the complete 2026-2027 academic year.
  • Be pursuing a first undergraduate degree in a science, technology, engineering, or math (STEM) field.
  • Attend an accredited four-year institution in California or a Historically Black College or University (HBCU) nationwide.

Recipients are evaluated based on academic achievement, financial need, and demonstrated leadership and participation in school and community activities.

Benefits

The program awards individual scholarships valued at $2,500, $5,000, or $10,000. Recipients may reapply annually for funding for up to five years. Additionally, PG&E's 11 employee resource groups and three engineering networking groups offer separate scholarship opportunities funded through employee contributions.

IRCC Study Permit and Visa Processing Times Update

Published on November 14, 2026

Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada has released an IRCC Study Permit and Visa Processing Times Update based on application data evaluated in November. This update provides revised wait time estimates for individuals applying for study permits, work permits, visitor visas, and super visas from various countries. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

This official announcement outlines processing timeframe shifts for temporary residence applications handled by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada. While study permit processing speeds improved for candidates in Canada and Pakistan, work permit wait times increased for applicants in Pakistan and the Philippines. Specific scholarship funding amounts are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

  • Study permit processing for applicants within Canada dropped from eight weeks to six weeks.
  • Study permit turnaround for applicants in Pakistan sped up from five weeks to four weeks.
  • Study permit wait times stayed at eight weeks for India, eleven weeks for Nigeria, four weeks for the United States, and six weeks for the Philippines.
  • Work permit wait times grew from nine to ten weeks for Pakistan and the Philippines, while remaining at 16 weeks in Canada, ten weeks for India and Nigeria, and three weeks for the United States.

Who Can Apply

Applicants seeking temporary residence in Canada, including international students, workers, visitors, and super visa applicants from Canada, Pakistan, India, Nigeria, the Philippines, and the United States, are affected by these updated estimates. Detailed academic or eligibility criteria are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

  • Official service standards for study and work permits remain at 120 days for domestic applications and 60 days for international submissions.
  • Visitor visa estimated processing times stand at 34 days for India, 57 days for Pakistan, 100 days for Nigeria, 12 days in Canada, 18 days for the United States, and 24 days for the Philippines.
  • Super visa turnaround estimates are 91 days for Pakistan, 28 days for Nigeria, 104 days for the Philippines, 87 days for India, and 78 days for the United States.

Cadet College Larkana Announces Admissions for Classes 7 and 8

Published on October 27, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Cadet College Larkana has announced admissions for Class VII and Class VIII for the 2027-28 academic session. Alongside standard admissions, the institution offers merit scholarships, reserved quotas, and limited self-financing seats. Candidates must submit their applications by December 26, 2026, ahead of the entrance examination scheduled for January 10, 2027.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility is determined by age limits and academic status. The institution strictly enforces age criteria without any relaxation:

  • Class VII: Applicants must be appearing in the Class VI Annual Examination 2027 and be between 11 and 13 years of age as of June 30, 2027.
  • Class VIII: Applicants must be appearing in the Class VII Annual Examination 2027 and be between 12 and 14 years of age as of June 30, 2027.

The entrance exam covers English, Mathematics, Science, Islamiat, Social Studies, General Knowledge, and either Urdu or Sindhi based on the candidate's medium of instruction. The exam syllabus follows the Sindh Textbook Board.

Benefits

The college awards five merit scholarships, comprising two positions for Class VII and three positions for Class VIII. Exact monetary values or financial coverage details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

A Shuhada quota is available for children of martyrs from the Armed Forces, Rangers, Frontier Corps, and Police who hold a Sindh domicile. Seats are also allocated under reserved quotas and self-financing options.

How to Apply

Application forms and prospectuses are priced at Rs. 3,000 and can be acquired directly from Cadet College Larkana or Askari Bank branches across Sindh. Applicants may also download the form online, provided they attach a pay order or demand draft of Rs. 3,000 issued to Cadet College Larkana.

Forms must be completed with personal, academic, guardian, and test centre information. Candidates must include documents attested by a Grade 17 or higher government officer, including:

  • Four passport-sized photographs
  • Previous academic records
  • Candidate's B-Form or birth certificate
  • Parent's CNIC, Domicile, and Permanent Residence Certificate (PRC)
  • Relevant supporting certificates for quota applications
  • A signed undertaking on Rs. 100 stamp paper

Completed application packages must be delivered by hand to the Examination Branch at Cadet College Larkana or sent via courier to the Principal by December 26, 2026. Test centres will be set up in multiple cities, including Larkana, Karachi, Sukkur, Hyderabad, Lahore, Islamabad, and Peshawar, subject to sufficient candidate numbers.

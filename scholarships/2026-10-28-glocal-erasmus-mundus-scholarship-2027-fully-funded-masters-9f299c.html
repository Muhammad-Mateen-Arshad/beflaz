GLOCAL Erasmus Mundus Scholarship 2027: Fully Funded Master’s in Europe With €16,800 Annual Stipend

Published on October 28, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The GLOCAL Erasmus Mundus Scholarship 2027 provides international students with funding to undertake a two-year, full-time Master’s degree. Conducted in English, the programme addresses global markets, local creativities, economic development, sustainability, entrepreneurship, and globalisation. Enrolled students move across a consortium of partner universities during their studies, with mobility locations including Glasgow, Göttingen, Barcelona, Uppsala, Bogotá, Kyoto, and Rotterdam.

Benefits

The scholarship offers financial support and educational benefits to successful candidates, including:

  • An annual stipend of €16,800.
  • Assistance with travel and visa expenses.
  • Full academic mobility across multiple consortium institutions.
  • One language course included in the tuition fee at the University of Glasgow during the first semester.

Who Can Apply

The programme accepts candidates from all countries worldwide. Applicants must meet the following general requirements:

  • Hold a strict 2:1 UK Honours degree or its international equivalent in an accepted discipline, such as International Relations or International Studies.
  • For Tracks B, F, and I, applicants must have completed at least 60 ECTS credits (or one-quarter of prior studies) in Business, Economics, Sociology, Politics, Modern History, Economic & Social History, or a combination of these fields.
  • Apply to only one study track. Please note that Track H is currently closed for applications.

How to Apply

To apply, candidates must select one study track aligned with their academic background and choose between applying for the scholarship or entering as a self-funded student. Applicants must submit either a Scholarship application form or a Self-Funded application form alongside their required academic documents before the application deadline on 7 January 2027. For complete funding conditions or specific allowance details not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

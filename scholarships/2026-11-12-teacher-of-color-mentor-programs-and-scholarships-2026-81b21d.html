Scholarship Overview

Educational initiatives and state investments focus on addressing persistent demographic disparities between public school teachers and their students. Data from the U.S. Department of Education for the 2023–24 school year reveals that nearly 75% of public school teachers were White, compared to 44% of students. Meanwhile, Latino students represented nearly 30% of total enrollment but only 11% of teachers, and Black students made up 15% of enrollment compared to 9% Black teachers.

These initiatives aim to counter historical job losses experienced by Black educators following the 1954 Brown v. Board of Education Supreme Court ruling. Additionally, recent data indicates that more than 720,000 students nationwide attend schools where fewer than half of teachers are fully certified, with nearly three-quarters of those students being Black or Latino.

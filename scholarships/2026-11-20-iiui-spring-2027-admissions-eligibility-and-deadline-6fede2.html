IIUI Spring 2027 Admissions: Eligibility and Deadline

Published on November 20, 2026

The IIUI Spring 2027 Admissions process is now open for students seeking undergraduate, graduate, doctoral, and diploma programs at the International Islamic University Islamabad. This opportunity is designed for candidates aiming to pursue higher education across disciplines such as engineering, business, law, sciences, and Islamic studies. Interested applicants must complete their application forms before the deadline on Friday, January 8, 2027.

Scholarship Overview

The university is offering admissions for four-year Bachelor of Science (BS) programs, two-year and four-year BBA degrees, five-year LL.B programs, two-year Master of Science (MS) degrees, and three-year PhD degrees. Academic options range from traditional subjects in humanities and social sciences to modern fields such as artificial intelligence, business analytics, computational mathematics, data science, and financial technology.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility requirements vary across academic departments and degree levels:

  • Undergraduate candidates generally require an Intermediate, Higher Secondary School Certificate (HSSC), or equivalent qualification. Students awaiting their final results are eligible to apply for undergraduate programs only.
  • Graduate candidates applying for MS programs must hold relevant previous academic qualifications and may be required to clear an entry test or interview. Valid GRE or GAT scores can be accepted where applicable.
  • Doctoral candidates must meet the university's postgraduate research and education requirements for PhD admission.
  • Applicants with foreign degrees, O-Level, A-Level, or Deeni Asnaad qualifications must submit equivalence certificates from the IBCC or HEC.

Benefits

Selected students can access several campus provisions and financial assistance opportunities:

  • Merit-based scholarships are awarded to high-achieving students. Exact award amounts are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Hostel accommodation and campus transport facilities are provided in accordance with university policy.
  • Facilitation is available for candidates with disabilities according to institutional policies.

How to Apply

All application forms must be submitted online through the university's official admission portal. While general undergraduate degrees typically do not require separate entry exams, specific programs such as the MBA and BS Bioinformatics involve separate assessment requirements. Candidates must complete all registration procedures online by Friday, January 8, 2027.

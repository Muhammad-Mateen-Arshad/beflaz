Pakistani Student Scholarships in Turkey 2026 Details

Published on November 21, 2026

The Pakistani Student Scholarships in Turkey 2026 offer educational opportunities for students from Pakistan to attend higher education institutions across Turkey. These funding programs are designed for Pakistani youth seeking degree programs, cross-cultural connections, and academic training in disciplines such as engineering, medicine, and artificial intelligence. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Educational exchanges between Pakistan and Turkey provide financial support to help international students study abroad. Around 5,000 Pakistani students are currently enrolled in Turkish universities, with approximately 200 receiving scholarships each year under various funding schemes. In 2026, 112 Pakistani scholars were selected for awards provided through the Presidency for Turks Abroad and Related Communities (YTB). Student groups like the Pakistani Students Association in Turkey (PSAT) offer ongoing assistance to help newly enrolled scholars adjust to campus life.

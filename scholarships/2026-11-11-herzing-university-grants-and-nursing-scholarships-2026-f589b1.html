Herzing University Grants and Nursing Scholarships 2026

Published on November 11, 2026

Herzing University has secured more than $7 million in federal multiyear funding, leading to new initiatives including the Herzing University Grants and Nursing Scholarships 2026. This funding is designed to assist full-time nursing students and expand academic support and mental health services for online and campus-based learners. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Herzing University was awarded three separate federal grant projects totaling over $7 million. The primary award is a five-year, $3.25 million Scholarships for Disadvantaged Students (SDS) grant provided by the Health Resources and Services Administration. In addition to nursing scholarships, federal funding supports student success programs at the Atlanta campus and suicide prevention services across all locations.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility for support depends on the specific project:

  • Nursing Students: Funding is directed to 59 eligible full-time students enrolled in the Associate of Science in Nursing (ASN) or ASN Bridge programs. This program is available at the Akron, Birmingham, Clarksville, Nashville, Orlando, and Tampa campuses, as well as to online students.
  • Atlanta Campus Students: Academic support and student services are expanded specifically for students at Herzing's Atlanta location through a $2.77 million Title III grant.
  • All Campuses and Online Students: Mental health promotion and suicide prevention services are provided to students at every Herzing location and online.
  • Additional Criteria: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

  • Annual Nursing Awards: Qualifying nursing students can receive up to $11,010 per year in scholarship funding.
  • Mental Health Resources: A $1.03 million grant funds suicide prevention, behavioral health promotion, early intervention, screening, and recovery support services.
  • Academic Services: Expansion of academic programs and the creation of an endowment fund at the Atlanta campus.

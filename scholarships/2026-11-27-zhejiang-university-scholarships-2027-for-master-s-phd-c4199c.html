Zhejiang University Scholarships 2027 for Master's & PhD

Published on November 27, 2026

The Zhejiang University Scholarships 2027 for Master's & PhD programs provide international candidates, including Pakistani students, funding opportunities to pursue postgraduate education in China. These awards support various advanced academic disciplines for the 2027 academic intake. Depending on the targeted funding scheme, application deadlines range from December 31, 2026, to May 31, 2027.

Scholarship Overview

Multiple financial aid options are available for postgraduate candidates, including the Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC Type B), the Zhejiang Government Scholarship, and university-level postgraduate schemes. Additional specialized opportunities comprise the Youth of Excellence Scheme, the China Studies Program PhD in China Fellowship, the ZJE Hai Scholarship, the ZIBS Scholarship, and the China-ASEAN Marine Scholarship. Supported fields encompass engineering, science, management, economics, humanities, social sciences, optical science, psychology, and marine studies for select English-taught master's degrees and doctoral qualifications.

Who Can Apply

To qualify for consideration, applicants must meet the following basic requirements:

  • Must be non-Chinese citizens holding a valid passport.
  • Master's degree applicants must hold a bachelor's degree and be under 35 years of age.
  • PhD candidates must possess a master's degree and be under 40 years of age.
  • Fulfill the academic standards and language proficiency criteria set by their chosen programme.

Benefits

Not specified in the source; please check the official notice for specific stipend rates, tuition coverage, and allowance details across individual funding categories.

Required Documents

Applicants are generally required to provide the following documentation:

  • Valid passport copy.
  • Official academic certificates and transcripts.
  • Proof of language proficiency.
  • Personal statement and research proposal (where applicable).
  • Two academic recommendation letters.
  • Provisional supervisor acceptance form (if required by the specific program).
  • Medical examination report and non-criminal record certificate (mandatory for CSC Type B applicants).

How to Apply

Interested candidates should submit their application online through the Zhejiang University International Student Application System. Applicants applying for the CSC Type B scholarship must also complete the Chinese Government Scholarship portal using Zhejiang University's agency number, 10335. Candidates must review individual scheme requirements and closing dates prior to submission.

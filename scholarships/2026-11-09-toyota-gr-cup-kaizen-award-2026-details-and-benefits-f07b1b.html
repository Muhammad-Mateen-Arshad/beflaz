Toyota GR Cup Kaizen Award 2026: Details and Benefits

Published on November 09, 2026

The Toyota GR Cup Kaizen Award 2026 recognizes drivers in the Toyota GR Cup racing series who demonstrate continuous improvement across the competition season. The latest award was presented to Sydney teenager Romeo Nasr during the end-of-year presentation dinner at Albert Park's Lakeside Pavilion in Melbourne. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

The Kaizen Award is an annual honor acknowledging driver progression throughout the racing series. Previous winners include Dylan Gulson from the inaugural 2016 season, Jarrod Hughes in 2022, and Ryan Tomsett in 2023. During the 2026 season, winner Romeo Nasr transitioned from karting to car racing via the TGRA 86 Scholarship Series in 2025. Over the five-round 2026 calendar, he recovered from a difficult opening round in Sydney to achieve a race win at Bathurst and secure 15th place overall in the standings.

Benefits

The primary prize for the Kaizen Award recipient is an international motorsport travel experience. Key benefits include:

  • A fully funded trip to visit the Toyota Racing facilities in North Carolina, United States, scheduled for the following year.
  • Attendance at the Coca-Cola 600 NASCAR event at Charlotte Motor Speedway over the Memorial Day weekend.

In addition to the Kaizen Award, cash prizes were awarded to the top overall series performers: Cooper Barnes received $50,000 as the overall series winner, runner-up Ben Stewart was awarded $30,000, and third-place finisher Brock Stinson collected $15,000.

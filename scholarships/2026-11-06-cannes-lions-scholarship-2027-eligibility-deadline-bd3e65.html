Cannes Lions Scholarship 2027: Eligibility & Deadline

Published on November 06, 2026

The Cannes Lions Scholarship 2027 is a fully funded opportunity designed for emerging creative and marketing professionals from underrepresented backgrounds. It provides selected individuals with access to training, mentorship, and international industry exposure at the festival in Cannes, France. Interested candidates must submit their applications by 4 December 2026 at 00:01 GMT.

Scholarship Overview

The programme awards 10 fully funded scholarships to outstanding participants representing 10 different countries. Candidates are split evenly across two specialized learning tracks:

  • Creative Academy (5 scholarships): Designed for creative professionals and engineers working in creative fields to refine their innovative thinking.
  • Brand Marketer Academy (5 scholarships): Aimed at professionals working in marketing strategy, digital communications, and client-side brand management.

Benefits

Recipients of the scholarship receive support to attend the event in France without paying major expenses out of pocket. Benefits include:

  • Funding for international travel to Cannes, France.
  • Fully covered accommodation for the duration of the programme.
  • A financial allowance (exact stipend amount is not specified in the source; please check the official notice).
  • Access via a Young Lions Festival Pass.
  • Participation in practical workshops, coaching sessions, and global networking opportunities.

Who Can Apply

Applicants must meet the following eligibility conditions:

  • Be at least 18 years old at the time of application and born on or after 26 June 1997.
  • Have at least two years of relevant professional work experience.
  • Be capable of learning and interacting in an English-language environment.
  • Demonstrate strong creative commitment and leadership potential within the industry.

Selection Process and Key Dates

Applications undergo a two-stage evaluation process, featuring a jury of over 40 global creative professionals who assess candidate potential. Important dates include:

  • Applications Open: 29 October 2026 at 00:01 GMT
  • Application Deadline: 4 December 2026 at 00:01 GMT
  • Notification of Results: 1 February 2027

How to Apply

Eligible candidates can apply online by visiting the official Cannes Lions Scholarship website. Applicants should thoroughly review the programme guidance before submitting their materials.

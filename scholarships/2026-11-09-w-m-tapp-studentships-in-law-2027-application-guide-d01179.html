W.M. Tapp Studentships in Law 2027: Application Guide

Published on November 09, 2026

The W.M. Tapp Studentships in Law 2027 provide financial support for academically exceptional law graduates aiming to pursue postgraduate legal degrees at the University of Cambridge. Managed by Gonville & Caius College, this opportunity is open to both UK and international applicants, with a strict application deadline of 5 January at 23:59.

Scholarship Overview

Administered by Gonville & Caius College, these awards assist prospective students undertaking advanced legal coursework or doctoral research within the Faculty of Law. Financial assistance is governed by the terms of the Tapp Trust. Courses in Criminology are excluded from funding, and applications for the Masters in Corporate Law are unlikely to succeed. Applicants are expected to explore additional funding schemes, including AHRC awards, Gates Cambridge Scholarships, and Cambridge Trust funding.

Who Can Apply

To be considered for the awards, candidates must fulfill the following requirements:

  • Hold a law degree from a recognized university in the UK or another country, or complete their degree no later than 1 September in their entry year.
  • Demonstrate outstanding academic capacity and potential for legal study or research.
  • Intend to register as a postgraduate student in an eligible Faculty of Law program at Cambridge.
  • Not be an existing member of Gonville & Caius College.
  • Secure official admission through the University of Cambridge Postgraduate Admissions Office.

Benefits

Award amounts are determined by assessing each successful applicant's financial circumstances and income from external sources. The financial package can include:

  • Payment of approved University and College tuition fees.
  • A maintenance allowance meeting the Postgraduate Admissions Office financial standards.
  • An extra annual College scholarship of £500.
  • Possible travel aid for overseas candidates, dependants allowances, and research grants.
  • College accommodation for up to three years, with PhD funding renewable annually based on academic progress.

How to Apply

Candidates must submit an official application form along with an academic transcript and two dedicated academic references. Electronic forms must not exceed five pages, ensuring text remains within specified boxes. Application portal URLs are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. All submission materials must be completed prior to the deadline on 5 January at 23:59.

Irish court overturns Gazan student visa refusal over “significant errors”

Published on October 30, 2026

This article provides an overview of fully funded study opportunities at University College Cork (UCC) in Ireland for Gazan students, alongside recent Irish High Court rulings regarding study visa processing.

Scholarship Overview

University College Cork offers fully funded university places for selected candidates, including four-year engineering degree programs. The university covers the complete costs associated with the degree to support students pursuing higher education in Ireland.

Who Can Apply

The funding described applies to Palestinian students from Gaza who successfully clear academic screening and university vetting processes. Comprehensive eligibility criteria, prerequisite subjects, and grade requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Recipients of this scholarship receive full financial backing. As confirmed by UCC, all costs associated with the degree program are entirely covered by the university.

How to Apply

Application procedures, official portals, and submission deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Applicants required to secure an Irish long-stay study visa must submit supporting documentation to the Department of Justice, including financial summary forms and evidence of English proficiency.

Important Visa and Legal Updates

Applicants should stay informed about recent immigration developments. The Irish High Court recently overturned a long-stay student visa refusal for a Gazan student who had been awarded a fully funded engineering place at UCC. Justice Anthony Barr ruled that the Department of Justice committed significant errors by overlooking submitted evidence of English language proficiency and a financial summary form. The judge directed the department to make a new decision within 10 working days, enabling the student to meet a November 23 evacuation flight deadline to begin studies in Ireland.

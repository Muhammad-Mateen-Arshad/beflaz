Artist Grants and Residencies 2026 Open Calls

Published on November 25, 2026

The Artist Grants and Residencies 2026 Open Calls provide various global funding, exhibition, and residency opportunities for visual artists, photographers, sculptors, and filmmakers. These opportunities target creative individuals at different stages of their careers seeking financial support or public exposure. Application deadlines range from December 1, 2026, through March 31, 2027, depending on the specific program.

Scholarship Overview

A wide range of organizations and institutions are offering funding awards, exhibition slots, and dedicated creative retreats. Program highlights include quarterly cash awards for visual artists and photographers, global film showcase trips to California, quiet residency stays in Florida, and high-value commission prizes for public art installations and wearable designs.

Who Can Apply

  • Visual artists, photographers, textile artists, sculptors, and filmmakers across diverse experience levels.
  • Applicants aged 18 and older for programs like the Innovate Grants.
  • Regional residents for location-specific initiatives, such as creators residing in eligible southern U.S. states for South Arts fellowships or local artists for Moab public art projects.
  • International applicants for global competitions including the World of WearableArt, Wildlife Photographer of the Year, and Sony Future Filmmaker Awards.

Benefits

  • Direct financial grants and cash awards ranging from $1,000 up to $30,000, with total competition prize pools reaching NZ$200,000.
  • Free lodging and inspirational work environments for creative residencies, such as the Bryson County Retreat near the Santa Fe River in Florida.
  • Public exhibition opportunities in international galleries, botanical gardens, and specialized display spaces.
  • Industry immersion events and travel funding to Sony Pictures Studios in California for shortlisted filmmakers.

How to Apply

Interested creators must prepare their work proposals, portfolio pieces, or project designs according to each organizer's listed rules before the specific program deadline. Some opportunities require entry fees, such as £35 for the Wildlife Photographer of the Year competition or €40 for the Material Works Art Award, while others offer fee waivers or free submission. Exact application links and detailed submission portals are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

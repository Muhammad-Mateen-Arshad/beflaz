Scholarship Overview

The source details medical training funding initiatives designed to help students pursue healthcare careers and serve their local communities. One featured program was established by Dr. Andrew Ross at Mosvold Hospital in KwaZulu-Natal to encourage local school pupils to undertake university degrees in medicine and other health disciplines.

In addition, the Umthombo bursary supports rural students attending institutions such as the University of KwaZulu-Natal for medical studies, including fields like dental therapy and general medicine. The government also offers bursaries to fund medical training locally and abroad to support public healthcare service.

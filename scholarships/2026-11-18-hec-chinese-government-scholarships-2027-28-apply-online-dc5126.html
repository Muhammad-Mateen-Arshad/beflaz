HEC Chinese Government Scholarships 2027-28: Apply Online

Published on November 18, 2026

The HEC Chinese Government Scholarships 2027-28 offer funded educational opportunities for Pakistani and Azad Jammu and Kashmir (AJK) nationals to study at universities in China. Administered by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) and the Chinese Scholarship Council, a total of 61 awards are available for undergraduate, master's, and PhD programs. Interested candidates must submit their applications online by January 5, 2027, at 4 pm Pakistan Standard Time.

Scholarship Overview

Offered under the Chinese Government Scholarship Program (Type-A), the funding covers 25 bachelor's degree slots, 21 master's degree slots, and 15 doctoral studies positions. Certain degree programs may also include Chinese language training as part of the curriculum.

Who Can Apply

To be eligible, applicants must meet the following qualification and age requirements:

  • Bachelor's Programs: Candidates must have 12 years of education (FA, FSc, intermediate, A-levels, or equivalent), be under 25 years old, hold valid USAT results, and present a valid CSCA score report.
  • Master's Programs: Applicants require a 16-year bachelor's degree, must be under 35 years old, and need a valid HAT score.
  • PhD Programs: Applicants must hold an MS, MPhil, or equivalent qualification, be under 40 years old, and possess valid HAT scores.

Candidates must score at least 50 out of 100 marks on their required USAT or HAT examinations. Dual nationals, applicants who have not completed their degrees by the deadline, and students currently holding another scholarship are ineligible. Applicants without test scores can register via the ETC portal until November 22, 2026.

Benefits

The scholarship provides full coverage of university tuition fees, free campus dormitory housing or an accommodation subsidy, a monthly living allowance, and medical insurance. Travel expenses and airfare are not covered. Applying for the scholarship is free, though candidates must pay the required registration fee for their USAT or HAT exams.

How to Apply

Applicants are required to complete forms on two separate online portals:

  • Build a profile on the HEC portal and select "Learning Opportunities Abroad" to submit the initial application.
  • Apply on the Chinese Government Scholarship portal by choosing Program Category Type A and typing Agency Number 5861 (assigned to HEC Pakistan).

Applying on both portals is compulsory for nomination. Printed applications should not be mailed to HEC or the Chinese Embassy at this stage, but applicants should keep copies of all completed forms.

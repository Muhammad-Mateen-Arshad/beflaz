TU/e Scholarship for Excellence 2027-2028: Guide

Published on November 16, 2026

The TU/e Scholarship for Excellence 2027-2028 is an academic merit-based funding opportunity offered by Eindhoven University of Technology in the Netherlands for high-achieving international Master’s students. Supported by the Dutch government’s Beethoven Programme, the initiative aims to attract top talent in engineering, artificial intelligence, computer science, and microchip innovation. Interested candidates must submit their application by 1 January 2027 at 23:59 Central European Time (CET).

Scholarship Overview

The scholarship is backed by the Beethoven Programme, a strategic investment by the Dutch government to strengthen the nation's semiconductor and technological ecosystem. Eindhoven University of Technology plans to award approximately 35 scholarships, comprising 20 grants for European Economic Area (EEA) students and 15 grants for non-EEA students. The award is highly competitive, with an estimated selection rate of around 2% of applicants.

Benefits

  • Funding of €7,500 per academic year for up to two years, totaling up to €15,000.
  • Monthly scholarship payments sent to a SEPA or Dutch bank account.
  • Academic recognition and exposure to an international research environment.

Please note that this is not a fully funded scholarship and does not cover full tuition fees or complete living costs. Recipients are expected to fund all remaining educational expenses. Additionally, non-EEA students must prove they have sufficient financial resources to meet Dutch visa and residence permit regulations.

Eligible Programmes

The scholarship applies to selected Master's degrees and specific study tracks starting on 1 September 2027, including:

  • MSc Artificial Intelligence and Engineering Systems
  • MSc Computer Science and Engineering (including Cybersecurity and Systems and Artificial Intelligence tracks)
  • MSc Data Science and Artificial Intelligence
  • MSc Industrial and Applied Mathematics (including Quantum Science and Technology and AI Fundamentals tracks)
  • MSc Mechanical Engineering (including Aerospace Systems Engineering track)
  • MSc Science and Technology of Nuclear Fusion

Who Can Apply

  • International applicants applying for an eligible full-time Master's degree at TU/e.
  • Holders of a non-Dutch university bachelor's degree, high school diploma, or both.
  • Applicants with no previous higher education degree earned in the Netherlands (exchange studies are permitted).
  • Candidates with a minimum Grade Point Average (GPA) of 7.5 out of 10 on their latest academic transcript.
  • Students eligible to obtain the required entry visa and residence permit for the Netherlands.

Applicants for European Double Degree programmes, joint Master's degrees with Tilburg University (JADS), or those holding admission decisions from prior intakes are ineligible.

How to Apply

There is no separate scholarship application form. Candidates who submit their Master’s degree application for an eligible programme by 1 January 2027 at 23:59 CET are automatically considered if they satisfy all requirements. For step-by-step instructions on submitting university applications, details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Shanghai Jiao Tong University Scholarships 2027 Guide

Published on November 14, 2026

The Shanghai Jiao Tong University Scholarships 2027 offer international students the opportunity to undertake postgraduate Master’s and PhD studies in China. Applications for international postgraduate admissions opened on 5 November 2026, with Chinese Government Scholarship deadlines scheduled for 15 December 2026 for the first round and 15 February 2027 for the final round.

Scholarship Overview

Founded in 1896, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) is a major research-intensive institution based in Shanghai, China. The university draws researchers and students worldwide through postgraduate degrees, research initiatives, and international academic partnerships. For the 2027 academic year, eligible international applicants can explore funding through the Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) programme alongside additional university funding options.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility requirements depend on the degree level and individual academic department:

  • Master’s Candidates: Must hold a recognised Bachelor’s degree or equivalent, meet language proficiency benchmarks, provide academic transcripts, and satisfy general international admission standards. Depending on the discipline, Master's studies may involve coursework, laboratory activities, research projects, and a dissertation.
  • PhD Candidates: Must hold a relevant Master’s degree or equivalent qualification, demonstrate an appropriate research background, and meet department language criteria to conduct research under university supervisors.

Benefits

Depending on the applicable scholarship category and official award conditions, CSC funding may provide university accommodation or accommodation subsidies. Specific allowance amounts, tuition fee coverage, and monthly stipends are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Applications opened on 5 November 2026. Prospective postgraduate students should review the official SJTU international admissions catalogue to confirm discipline requirements. Applicants must gather required documentation, such as academic certificates, degree qualifications, and transcripts, and submit their files before 15 December 2026 for the first round or 15 February 2027 for the final round. Note that securing a university admission offer does not automatically guarantee a CSC scholarship award.

PM Chromebook Scheme: Can Private School Students Apply?

Published on November 09, 2026

Many students are wondering, PM Chromebook Scheme: Can Private School Students Apply? This government initiative aims to distribute hundreds of thousands of digital devices to academic achievers across Pakistan to boost access to modern technology. Specific application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

The government created the Prime Minister’s Youth Chromebook Scheme to improve digital education and provide internet connectivity to talented youth. While early launch reports suggested that coverage might include students in registered religious seminaries and foundation-supported private schools, the published registration rules currently restrict eligibility strictly to government school students.

Who Can Apply

According to the official application criteria, qualification is presently limited to high-performing public school students under the following conditions:

  • Top-performing Grade 9 students enrolled in government schools across Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and Balochistan based on their Annual Examinations 2026 performance.
  • Top-performing Grade 10 students attending government schools under examination boards in Islamabad Capital Territory, Azad Jammu and Kashmir, and Gilgit-Baltistan.
  • Students currently attending private schools are not listed as eligible candidates on the registration site.

Benefits

Recipients of the scheme will receive a Chromebook device to help them access digital learning resources and online educational tools. Total funding allocations and individual monetary values are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Students can review the criteria on the official IBCC registration portal at pmychromebooks.ibcc.edu.pk. Due to differences between early announcements and current portal requirements, applicants seeking clarification about their institution should reach out to local education authorities before submitting an application.

Countries with Easiest Student Visa Processes for Pakistanis: Visa Requirements, Costs and Scholarships

Published on October 17, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Pakistani students considering studying abroad have several international options to evaluate, including Malaysia, Türkiye, the United Arab Emirates, China, Georgia, Hungary, Poland, Lithuania, Cyprus, Germany, and Italy. Countries like Türkiye and China provide dedicated scholarship opportunities for international applicants. Türkiye offers government-sponsored awards across undergraduate and postgraduate degrees, while China provides funding options through government and university channels for disciplines such as engineering, science, and technology. Eligibility requirements differ based on the study destination, degree level, and funding program. In China, applicants must secure appropriate study documentation based on course length, with X1 visas designated for studies exceeding 180 days and X2 visas for shorter programs.

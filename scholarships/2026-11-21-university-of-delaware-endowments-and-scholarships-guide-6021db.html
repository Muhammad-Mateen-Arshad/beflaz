University of Delaware Endowments and Scholarships Guide

Published on November 21, 2026

The University of Delaware Endowments and Scholarships Guide outlines financial aid opportunities provided through generous donor funds for new and returning undergraduate students. These awards support individuals across various fields of study, including business, economics, and agriculture. Application deadlines vary depending on the specific program, with key submission dates falling on April 15th and June 15th.

Scholarship Overview

The University of Delaware offers numerous donor-funded endowments to assist students in reaching their educational goals. While many institutional funds are automatically matched to eligible candidates, select awards require an independent application. Specific programs include the Howard H. and Arlene M. Simon Scholarship for business students dedicated to community service, and the Victor Giardini fund for optimistic undergraduates in Business and Economics. Additionally, the Center for Global Programs and Services provides need-based support for study abroad participants. Please note that applications for School of Nursing scholarships are not currently open.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility requirements depend on the individual scholarship program:

  • Undergraduates at the University of Delaware enrolled in Business and Economics or Agriculture and Natural Resources.
  • Students displaying academic excellence, enthusiasm for learning, positive encouragement toward peers, and community service commitment.
  • Students applying to university-sponsored study abroad programs.
  • Delaware residents and general students seeking external funding from foundations, state agencies, or community groups.

Benefits

Not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Selected recipients will have their scholarship funds credited toward their upcoming academic term.

How to Apply

Application procedures and timelines differ by award category:

  • Agriculture and Natural Resources: Submit applications by April 15th. Selection notifications are sent in late June or early July.
  • Special Endowments: For awards requiring extra information, applications open between mid-April and June 15th annually. Returning student reviews take place each June.
  • Study Abroad Funding: Applicants to study abroad programs are automatically evaluated for need-based grants upon program acceptance.
  • External Awards: Applicants should search early, stay organized, verify eligibility criteria, and check with state higher education assistance agencies.

For further assistance, students can visit the University Visitors Center at 210 South College Avenue, Newark, DE 19716, email sfs-mail@udel.edu, or call 302-831-2126 during office hours from Monday through Friday, 8:30am to 4:30pm.

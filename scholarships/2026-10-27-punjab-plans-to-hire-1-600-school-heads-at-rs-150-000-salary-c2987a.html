Punjab Plans to Hire 1,600 School Heads at Rs. 150,000 Salary

Published on October 27, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Punjab government is planning a recruitment initiative to hire 1,600 headmasters for government schools across the province. This program is intended to resolve ongoing leadership shortages, as more than 2,000 schools in Punjab currently have vacant headship roles. The hiring framework, salary offer, and contract terms are presently under review and require official approval from the relevant cabinet committee before recruitment can start.

Who Can Apply

Specific qualifications, age limits, and eligibility criteria are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Under the proposed selection framework, prospective candidates must take a written test administered by the Punjab Public Service Commission (PPSC) and subsequently pass an interview.

Benefits

The recruitment package includes the following proposed provisions:

  • A proposed monthly pay of Rs. 150,000.
  • An initial two-year service contract.
  • Possibility of contract extension evaluated on performance.

How to Apply

Application instructions, deadlines, and submission portals are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Formal recruitment will proceed through the PPSC testing and interview process once final approval is granted by the cabinet committee.

Top Scholarships and Fellowships Currently Open in 2026

Published on November 19, 2026

A variety of top scholarships and fellowships currently open in 2026 offer funding and professional development for students, researchers, journalists, and emerging global leaders. These opportunities support individuals ranging from undergraduate students and recent graduates to doctoral researchers and mid-career professionals. Application deadlines range between November 23 and March 1, depending on the specific award.

Scholarship Overview

The available awards cover a wide spectrum of fields, including artificial intelligence, climate finance, public policy, health journalism, digital democracy, and international relations. Participating organizations and institutions include UN Watch, New America, the Evens Foundation, Columbia Climate School, the University of Cape Town, the French-African Foundation, the iDAC Foundation, and the DAAD.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility criteria vary significantly across programs:

  • Students: Undergraduate juniors interested in public service can apply for policy training, while sophomores through graduate students in finance, economics, public policy, or business qualify for summer placements. Master's candidates in climate finance or inclusive innovation are also eligible for university-specific funding.
  • Graduates and Researchers: Recent college graduates with strong writing skills can pursue UN Watch training. International PhD candidates and postdoctoral researchers focusing on artificial intelligence can apply for research support in Heilbronn, Germany.
  • Professionals and Innovators: Opportunities exist for writers, filmmakers, early-career US-based female health journalists, civic technology enthusiasts aged 29 or younger, and French or African nationals aged 28 to 40 with at least five years of experience.

Benefits

Selected candidates receive varied financial and educational benefits:

  • Stipends and Grants: Financial awards range from $1,000 for summer policy students to unrestricted funding between $15,000 and $30,000 for New America fellows. Additional stipends include $4,000 for health reporting, $6,000 for private market placements, and travel reimbursements up to €300.
  • Tuition and Allowances: Full tuition coverage is available for select climate finance scholars, while African leaders at the University of Cape Town receive academic fee support. DAAD fellows receive a €2,800 monthly payment alongside insurance and travel allowances.
  • Experiential Learning: Many programs include travel, accommodation, food, mentorship, workshops, and participation in international gatherings.

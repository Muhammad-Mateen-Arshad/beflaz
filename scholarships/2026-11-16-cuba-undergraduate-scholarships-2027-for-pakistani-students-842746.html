Cuba Undergraduate Scholarships 2027 for Pakistani Students

Published on November 16, 2026

The Cuba Undergraduate Scholarships 2027 for Pakistani Students programme provides educational opportunities for scholars from Pakistan to pursue degree studies in Cuba. Offered by the Cuban government, this initiative aims to support Pakistani candidates entering medical and general fields. The application deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

The Government of Cuba has announced a total of 11 undergraduate scholarship positions for Pakistani students for the 2027 academic batch. The initiative was reviewed during a formal meeting between the Executive Director of the Higher Education Commission (HEC), Prof. Dr Zia Ul Haq, and the Ambassador of Cuba to Pakistan, H.E. Mr Damian Cordero Torres, at the Cuban Embassy in Islamabad. Their discussions addressed student placements, performance updates for current medical scholars, and proposed schedules. HEC leadership also noted the goal of extending these academic opportunities to applicants from underserved and underdeveloped areas of Pakistan.

Who Can Apply

Pakistani students seeking undergraduate admission in Cuba can utilize this programme. The allocations include 10 seats focused on Medical Sciences and one seat designated for the General Category. Detailed academic criteria and age limits are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

  • Undergraduate placement in Cuban higher education institutions.
  • Ten dedicated seats in Medical Sciences and one seat in the General Category.
  • Partial financial assistance from the Government of Pakistan via monthly stipends while studying in Cuba.

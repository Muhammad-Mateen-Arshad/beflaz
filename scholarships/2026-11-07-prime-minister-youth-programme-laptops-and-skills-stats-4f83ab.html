Published on November 07, 2026

The Prime Minister Youth Programme Laptops and Skills Stats reflect official progress on national education, technical training, and youth development opportunities. The initiatives focus on four core pillars—Education, Employment, Engagement, and Environment—to support young people and bridge the digital divide.

INDIA: National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMMSS) 2026–27 – Application Deadline Extended to 15 November 2026

Published on November 01, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Implemented by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, the National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMMSS) for the 2026–27 session has had its application deadline extended. As announced through the Press Information Bureau on 31 October 2026, this program supports academically capable students facing financial difficulties. It seeks to reduce dropout rates after Class VIII by encouraging students to finish their secondary and higher secondary schooling.

Benefits

Selected students receive an annual financial assistance of INR 12,000, which is deposited directly into their bank accounts via the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) system. The scheme awards 100,000 fresh scholarships each year to qualifying Class IX students. Financial support continues through Classes X, XI, and XII, provided recipients satisfy the required academic and renewal criteria.

Who Can Apply

Applicants must meet specific academic, financial, and institutional qualifications:

  • Academic Requirements: Candidates must have secured a minimum of 55% marks (or an equivalent grade) in Class VII. SC and ST applicants receive a 5 percentage point relaxation, reducing the minimum requirement to 50%.
  • Income Limit: Total annual parental income cannot exceed INR 3.50 lakh.
  • Institutional Eligibility: Students must be enrolled in eligible institutions, such as schools managed by local self-government authorities.
  • Selection Exam: Applicants must pass the selection examination conducted by their respective State or Union Territory government.

How to Apply

Candidates can apply online through the National Scholarship Portal by following these steps:

  • Access the National Scholarship Portal and complete One-Time Registration (OTR).
  • Log in with your credentials and select the National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme.
  • Provide accurate personal, financial, and academic information.
  • Upload the required supporting documents.
  • Submit the form before the deadline and coordinate with your school to complete institutional verification.

Important Deadlines

Key dates for the 2026–27 application cycle include:

  • Student Application Submission: Extended to 15 November 2026.
  • First-Level Verification (Institute Nodal Officer): 30 November 2026.
  • Second-Level Verification (District Nodal Officer): 15 December 2026.

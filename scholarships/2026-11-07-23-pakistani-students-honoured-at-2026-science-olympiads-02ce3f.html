23 Pakistani Students Honoured at 2026 Science Olympiads

Published on November 07, 2026

The STEM Careers Programme hosted a ceremony to honour 23 Pakistani students who competed at the 2026 International Science Olympiads. The event recognized outstanding young scholars who represented the country on an international stage across multiple scientific fields. Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

The ceremony celebrated student performances across Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics, and Artificial Intelligence. The 2026 events included Pakistan's first official participation in the International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence (IOAI). Higher Education Commission (HEC) Chairman Prof. Dr. Niaz Ahmad Akhtar (S.I) attended as Chief Guest, reaffirming the commitment of the HEC and the Government to support future scientists, researchers, and innovators.

The team achieved the following honours:

  • One Silver Medal (awarded to Hassan Atif Cheema at the International Biology Olympiad)
  • Six Bronze Medals
  • Five Honourable Mentions
  • One Certificate of Merit
  • One Certificate of Recognition

Benefits

Honoured students received recognition and support through the following incentives:

  • Cash prizes and certificates awarded by the HEC Chairman
  • Guidance and mentorship provided by experts from PIEAS, COMSATS Lahore, NIBGE-C, and ICCBS, University of Karachi
  • Formal government and institutional acknowledgment of their scientific accomplishments

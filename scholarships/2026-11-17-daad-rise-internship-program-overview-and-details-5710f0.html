DAAD RISE Internship Program Overview and Details

Published on November 17, 2026

The DAAD RISE Internship Program offers undergraduate students in STEM fields the opportunity to complete a summer research internship at top institutions in Germany. This award is specifically designed for undergraduate students from North America, Britain, and Ireland looking to develop their research skills abroad. The application deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

DAAD RISE, short for Research Internships in Science and Engineering, is managed by the German Academic Exchange Service (Deutscher Akademischer Austauschdienst). The scheme funds a three-month summer placement at leading German universities and research facilities, including institutions like the University of Freiburg. Interns work directly inside laboratories on advanced STEM projects, such as laser physics and optics research, under the guidance of academic mentors.

Who Can Apply

Based on the available information, eligible candidates must meet the following criteria:

  • Be an undergraduate student from North America, Britain, or Ireland.
  • Be pursuing studies in a science, technology, engineering, or mathematics (STEM) discipline.
  • Possess prior research experience and relevant coursework that prepares them to contribute effectively to lab projects.

Minimum GPA requirements and eligible academic year standings are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Recipients of the RISE award receive several professional and personal advantages, including:

  • Financial funding to complete a three-month research placement in Germany during the summer.
  • Hands-on laboratory experience working on advanced scientific experiments alongside expert mentors.
  • Access to university student housing during the stay.
  • Opportunities to establish international connections within the global STEM community.

The exact funding amount and allowance breakdown are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Applicants must apply for specific RISE projects offered by participating research labs in Germany. Successful candidates typically demonstrate how prior independent study projects, lab experience, and specialized coursework prepare them for their chosen research topic.

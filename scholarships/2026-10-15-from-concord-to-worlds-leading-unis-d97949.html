FROM CONCORD TO WORLD’S LEADING UNIS

Published on October 15, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Concord College International School highlights educational pathways and scholarship successes achieved by its graduating students. Among the achievements from its inaugural cohort, standout students secured major financial awards to pursue higher education at prominent international institutions. Highlighted awards include a full-tuition scholarship to Tsinghua University in China as well as a fully funded scholarship to the Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST) in South Korea.

In addition to university financial awards, the school recognizes student leadership and character through the Frank Bell Prize. Carried over from Concord College UK, this is the highest honor bestowed on a student at Concord, recognizing academic diligence, high personal standards, and meaningful contributions to the campus community.

Benefits

The funding opportunities and honors mentioned in the school's achievements include:

  • Full tuition coverage for studies at Tsinghua University in China.
  • A fully funded scholarship to attend the Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST) in South Korea.
  • Special recognition through the Frank Bell Prize for student character, leadership, and school contributions.

Institutional Support and Pathways

Concord College International School assists students with university preparation through targeted placement guidance, structured academic tracking, and specialized academic clinics. Students also participate in enrichment activities and a super-curriculum collaboration with neighboring Xiamen University Malaysia. Members of the school's inaugural class received offers from 45 universities across seven countries, including China, South Korea, the United States, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, and the Netherlands.

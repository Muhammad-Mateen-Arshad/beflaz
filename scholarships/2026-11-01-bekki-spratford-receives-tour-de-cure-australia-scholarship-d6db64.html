Bekki Spratford Receives Tour de Cure Australia Scholarship to Advance Their Research Into Glioblastoma

Published on November 01, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The 2026 Tour de Cure Australia PhD support scholarship has been awarded to Bekki Spratford to advance scientific research into glioblastoma. This philanthropic funding supports doctoral studies conducted at the Mark Hughes Foundation Centre for Brain Cancer Research, based at the University of Newcastle. Glioblastoma is recognized as the most common and lethal primary brain tumour, accounting for roughly 63% of the 1,500 newly diagnosed brain cancer cases in Australia each year.

Who Can Apply

General eligibility requirements and applicant criteria are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. The current recipient, Bekki Spratford, entered this PhD research program after working in nursing, caring for aged and disabled individuals.

Benefits

The specific financial value of the award is not specified in the source; please check the official notice. However, the scholarship grants vital funding to test a targeted drug and advance brain cancer treatment. Key outcomes targeted by this funded research include:

  • Improving the effectiveness of existing glioblastoma treatments.
  • Stopping brain nerve cells from stimulating further cancer growth.
  • Protecting healthy brain tissue to minimize harmful side effects and maintain quality of life.
  • Laying the foundation for clinical trials that could lead to personalized therapies and better survival rates.

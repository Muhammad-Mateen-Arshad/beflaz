Commonwealth Scholarship for Nigerian Students 2026

Published on November 24, 2026

The Commonwealth Scholarship for Nigerian Students offers fully funded awards for Nigerian graduates seeking a master's or PhD degree at a UK university. Funded by the UK government, the scheme is designed for talented individuals who could not otherwise afford to study in the UK. Applications for the 2027/28 Commonwealth Shared Scholarship round close on 8 December 2026 at 4 p.m. GMT (5 p.m. Nigerian time).

Scholarship Overview

Administered by the Commonwealth Scholarship Commission (CSC), the scheme includes multiple pathways for Nigerian candidates, such as the Commonwealth Shared Scholarship for one-year full-time master's degrees, as well as Commonwealth Master's and PhD scholarships. Nigerians can also access Professional Fellowships. Distance Learning and Split-Site PhD options are currently paused with no programmes operating in 2026 or 2027.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility criteria depend on the chosen pathway, but general requirements state that applicants must:

  • Be Nigerian citizens or official refugees residing permanently in Nigeria, applying from within the country.
  • Have no history of working or studying for more than one academic year in a high-income nation.
  • Demonstrate financial need for UK study support.
  • For Shared Scholarships: hold at least a Second Class Upper (2:1) first degree, or a 2:2 with a relevant postgraduate qualification, by September 2027.
  • For Federal Scholarship Board (FSB) routes: Master's candidates require a minimum 2:1 first degree; PhD candidates need a relevant master's, a 2:2 first degree, a prospective UK supervisor statement, NYSC discharge or exemption proof, and admission from at least two UK institutions.

Benefits

The award covers costs in full, requiring no financial contribution from scholars:

  • Full tuition fee coverage.
  • Approved return economy flight from Nigeria to the UK.
  • Monthly living stipend of £1,712 (or £2,000 for London-area universities).
  • Funding for approved study-related travel.
  • Child allowances for single, widowed, or divorced parents whose children live with them in the UK.

How to Apply

Shared Scholarship applicants apply for eligible UK courses, while Master's and PhD candidates must apply through Nigeria's national nominating agency, the Federal Scholarship Board (FSB), and CSC Central. There is no application fee, and paying an agent is a warning sign of fraud. Further step-by-step submission instructions are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

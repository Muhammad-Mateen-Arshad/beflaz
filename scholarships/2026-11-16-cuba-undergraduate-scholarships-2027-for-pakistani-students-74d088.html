Cuba Undergraduate Scholarships 2027 for Pakistani Students

Published on November 16, 2026

The Government of Cuba has announced the Cuba Undergraduate Scholarships 2027 for Pakistani Students, offering 11 higher education opportunities to qualified candidates. This international scheme provides academic seats in selected disciplines for students from Pakistan. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

This educational initiative aims to strengthen academic cooperation between Pakistan and Cuba while expanding international learning opportunities. The details were discussed in Islamabad during a conference between Higher Education Commission (HEC) Executive Director Prof. Dr. Zia Ul Haq and Cuban Ambassador to Pakistan Damian Cordero Torres. Their meeting covered the proposed schedule for the 2027 cohort alongside the performance of Pakistani medical students currently studying in Cuba.

Who Can Apply

The available seats and eligibility scope for the programme are outlined below:

  • Pakistani students aiming to pursue undergraduate degree programs in Cuba.
  • A total of 11 seats are allocated: 10 seats for Medical Sciences and 1 seat for the General Category.
  • The HEC has suggested prioritizing applicants from underserved and underdeveloped areas of Pakistan.
  • Detailed academic criteria and age limits are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Selected students will receive educational and financial support, including:

  • Undergraduate placement in Cuban higher education institutions.
  • Partial financial assistance from the Government of Pakistan in the form of monthly stipends during their academic stay.
  • Full tuition coverage details and accommodation arrangements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

The application steps, submission forms, and required documents are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

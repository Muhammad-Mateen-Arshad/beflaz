American Women's History Initiative Pool Overview

Published on November 09, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The American Women's History Initiative Pool is an award program that provides financial support to projects focused on researching, sharing, and amplifying the history of American women. This funding enabled the Smithsonian Archives of American Art to digitize 89 analog oral history transcripts of female artists, art historians, dealers, patrons, and collectors. Benefits

Funding from this initiative allows archival institutions to remove barriers to public research and enhance digital accessibility.

  • Converts physical analog transcripts into searchable online primary sources.
  • Restores full names and context to archival collection records.
  • Adds detailed subject tags and biographical metadata to improve search engine results.
  • Expands open-access database records, such as Wikidata, to give historical figures broader visibility.

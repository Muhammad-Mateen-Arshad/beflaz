Yenching Academy Scholarship 2027: Application Guide

Published on November 12, 2026

The Yenching Academy Scholarship 2027 provides international students with full financial backing to pursue a two-year Master’s degree in China Studies at Peking University. Designed for academically outstanding graduates across diverse disciplines, this award seeks candidates with a strong interest in interdisciplinary research and global affairs. The general application deadline is 30 November 2026, with an extended deadline of 7 December 2026 at 9:00 am Beijing time for nominees from partner institutions.

Scholarship Overview

The Yenching Academy of Peking University offers an interdisciplinary Master’s programme in China Studies. The curriculum integrates academic research, coursework, and practical engagement with China's social, cultural, political, and economic development to cultivate cross-cultural understanding and prepare future global leaders.

Benefits

Recipients of the Yenching Fellowship receive financial support for their first academic year, covering:

  • Full coverage of tuition fees.
  • On-campus housing at Yenching Academy House.
  • A travel allowance for one round trip between Beijing and the student's home base city.
  • Basic medical insurance and a monthly living stipend.
  • Funding assistance for eligible research costs.

Students maintaining good academic standing who remain in Beijing may apply for continued fellowship support for their second year. Exact stipend amounts are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Who Can Apply

To qualify for consideration, candidates must meet these criteria:

  • Hold non-Chinese citizenship with a valid passport (special requirements apply to individuals originally from Mainland China, Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan).
  • Possess a Bachelor’s degree in any field, or expect to graduate by August 2027.
  • Demonstrate an outstanding academic record and strong interest in China. Prior Chinese language experience is not mandatory.
  • Provide proof of English proficiency from tests taken after 1 September 2025, unless eligible for a waiver. Specific score requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Applicants must submit an online application to the Yenching Academy alongside the following documents:

  • Personal statement (maximum 750 words).
  • Statement of research interest (maximum 1,500 words, excluding citations).
  • Curriculum vitae (maximum 2 pages).
  • Bachelor’s degree certificate or proof of current enrollment.
  • English proficiency test report (if applicable).
  • Two signed recommendation letters from full or associate professors on official letterhead in PDF format.

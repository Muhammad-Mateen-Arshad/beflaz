Honhaar Scholarship Scheme in Punjab: Overview

Published on November 11, 2026

The Honhaar Scholarship Scheme in Punjab is an educational initiative provided by the provincial government to recognize and reward hard-working students. Designed for deserving individuals across the province, the program offers support to assist students in their studies. The application deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

The initiative aims to acknowledge students for their academic effort and dedication. During a recent distribution event held in Jhelum, laptops and scholarships were awarded directly to successful candidates. In the Jhelum district alone, 109 students were selected to receive Honhaar scholarships under this government scheme.

Who Can Apply

The scheme is created for students in Punjab who display hard work and commitment to their education. Specific qualifications, required academic marks, and eligible institutions are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Students recognized through Punjab's youth welfare initiatives receive various forms of assistance:

  • Honhaar scholarships awarded to help students succeed (specific monetary values are not specified in the source; please check the official notice).
  • Laptops distributed to facilitate academic work.
  • Free travel access on provincial green buses.
  • Eligibility for a scheduled rollout of 100,000 scooters along with formal driving training starting in December.

Educational Studies PhD Scholarships in Canada With CAD $40,000 Per Year

Published on October 20, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The University of British Columbia (UBC) in Canada is accepting applications for its Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in Educational Studies at its Vancouver campus. This research-based program covers fields such as educational leadership, higher education, adult learning, social justice, and educational policy. Enrolled students are required to complete independent research along with a doctoral dissertation. For candidates aiming for the September 2027 intake, the application deadline is November 15, 2026.

Benefits

Eligible full-time doctoral candidates receive a minimum annual funding guarantee of CAD 40,000 during their first four years of study, providing a minimum cumulative total of CAD 160,000. This financial package can combine university awards, external scholarships, and various assistantships (teaching, research, or graduate academic). This updated funding threshold was introduced by the university in September 2026.

Who Can Apply

The funding support is available to both domestic Canadian and international students, including candidates from Pakistan, following admission. Requirements include:

  • A recognized master's degree in education or a closely related discipline, supported by solid academic and research preparation.
  • Academic standing equivalent to an A− (80%), with exceptional applicants considered at a B+ (76%) standing.
  • For Pakistani applicants, qualifying master's, M.Phil., or master's honours degrees with first-class or first-division standing.
  • Prior experience with research methodologies and the capability to conduct independent research (relevant educational work experience is also desirable).
  • English language proficiency tests for those from non-English instruction backgrounds: IELTS Academic (minimum overall 6.5, with 6.0 in each band), Duolingo English Test (score of 125), or TOEFL iBT taken prior to January 21, 2026 (overall 92, minimum 22 per subscore).

How to Apply

Prospective students should review requirements, explore faculty research topics, and connect with potential supervisors. To submit an application, candidates must set up or log into a UBC Campus-Wide Login account and fill out the online graduate admission form. Required application materials include academic transcripts, three recommendation letters, a resume/CV, a 5,000-word academic writing sample, and a statement of intent ranging from 800 to 1,200 words detailing proposed research and academic goals. International applicants must also pay an application fee of CAD 168.25.

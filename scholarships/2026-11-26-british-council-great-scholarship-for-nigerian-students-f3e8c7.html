British Council GREAT Scholarship for Nigerian Students

Published on November 26, 2026

The British Council GREAT Scholarship for Nigerian Students is a funding opportunity jointly supported by the UK government's GREAT Britain Campaign, the British Council, and partner UK institutions. Designed for Nigerian postgraduate applicants, the scheme provides financial assistance toward a one-year Master’s degree across diverse fields. Application deadlines vary depending on the host university, so candidates must check individual university pages for exact closing dates.

Scholarship Overview

The award supports one-year taught Master’s degree programmes starting in the autumn intake. Eligible subjects cover a wide range of academic areas, including STEM disciplines such as engineering, mathematics, and computing, as well as non-STEM courses like business, public health, and law.

Who Can Apply

To qualify for consideration, candidates must meet the following criteria:

  • Hold a Nigerian passport and be a permanent resident of Nigeria.
  • Hold an undergraduate degree in a relevant subject.
  • Show academic motivation and a clear interest in the chosen study area.
  • Fulfill all postgraduate admission requirements of the host UK university.
  • Meet the university's specified English language standards (such as IELTS Academic or equivalent) by the scholarship closing date.
  • Be willing to serve as a programme ambassador, attend scholar networking events, and engage with prospective students.
  • Work experience is not required to apply.

Benefits

The scholarship provides a minimum contribution of £10,000 toward tuition fees. This total is jointly funded, with £5,000 coming from the participating university and £5,000 provided by the British Council and the GREAT Campaign. This award is partially funded and covers tuition fees only; accommodation, living costs, flights, visa fees, and the Immigration Health Surcharge are not included.

Participating Universities and Deadlines

The list of participating universities changes each cycle as institutions bid annually for allocations. In the 2026–27 cycle, participating institutions for Nigerian applicants included Imperial College London, University College London, Brunel University of London, Cranfield University, University of Bath, University of Kent, University of Reading, University of Southampton, University of Warwick, Edge Hill University, Keele University, Nottingham Trent University, and Royal Holloway University of London. For the 2027–28 cycle, 10 awards are designated for Nigeria across host institutions including the University of Manchester and the University of Southampton.

There is no unified closing date set by the British Council. Deadlines vary by university, requiring applicants to check each institution's scholarship portal directly.

How to Apply

Candidates must submit their application directly through the scholarship page of their chosen participating UK university. Specific portal steps and form requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

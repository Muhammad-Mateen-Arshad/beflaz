University of Florida Scholarship Offering $30,000 Per Year is Closing This Month

Published on October 30, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The University of Florida in the United States is offering a fully funded PhD graduate assistantship focused on fisheries ecosystem modeling and natural resource management. Based at the UF/IFAS Nature Coast Biological Station, the selected doctoral student will work under the supervision of Dr. David Chagaris. Candidates can pursue their degree through either the School of Forest, Fisheries, and Geomatics Sciences or the School of Natural Resources and Environment. The research addresses marine ecosystems in the Gulf of Mexico and the southeastern United States, covering topics such as climate change, water quality, invasive species, and fisheries productivity. The doctoral program is scheduled to begin in 2027.

Benefits

This fully funded assistantship offers several financial and professional benefits, including:

  • An annual salary of $30,000.
  • Full coverage of tuition costs and additional benefits.
  • Opportunities to collaborate with researchers from the University of Florida and the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA).

Who Can Apply

International applicants from various countries, including Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Nepal, Sri Lanka, China, and other nations, are eligible. The opportunity targets prospective students interested in doctoral research in ecology, aquatic sciences, fisheries, and related fields. Listed eligibility conditions include:

  • A minimum GPA of 3.0.
  • English language proficiency demonstrated by an IELTS score of 6.5 or a TOEFL iBT score of 80.

How to Apply

Interested candidates must submit their application documents before the deadline on October 31, 2026. The required application package includes:

  • Academic transcripts and previous degree certificates.
  • An application essay.
  • Two letters of recommendation.

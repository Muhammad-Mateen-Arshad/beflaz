Graduate Student Resources Hub

Published on October 12, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Graduate Student Resources Hub, located in Room 204 on the second floor of the Close-Hipp building at USC, aims to enhance the graduate student experience by combining academic training with professional growth. Established through a partnership between the Graduate School, the University Career Center, and National Fellowships, the center brings key student services together under one roof.

Although the hub serves as a central service resource rather than a direct scholarship grant, it assists students in identifying and applying for external funding opportunities. Support is available for nationally competitive awards, including DAAD grants, the Fulbright, the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship, the Boren Fellowship, and the Critical Language Scholarship. Students can also explore award possibilities through the INFO database.

Who Can Apply

All members of the USC graduate community, including master's and doctoral students, can access the hub's resources. Additionally, faculty members can request customized professional development sessions or workshops for their academic courses and programs.

Specific eligibility requirements for external awards: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Graduate students utilizing the hub can take advantage of various free services designed to assist their academic and career paths, including:

  • Free professional development workshops and presentations.
  • Individualized career advising and coaching for options in academia or industry.
  • Advising support from the National Fellowships office for competitive grant applications.
  • Access to the Gamecock Fellowship Guide, an AI-driven tool built to help locate external funding opportunities.
  • One-on-one consultations with the Student Success Center for time management, goal setting, and financial planning, alongside structured accountability groups.

Scholarship funding amounts and award values: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Students can access hub services in person or through online channels. Career Center advising appointments require students to set up a Handshake account. To use the Gamecock Fellowship Guide, students must sign into ChatGPT using their institutional credentials. Faculty members looking to organize tailored sessions must complete a workshop request form.

Sindh Transgender Scholarships: Higher Education Overview

Published on November 07, 2026

The proposed Sindh Transgender Scholarships initiative aims to provide higher education funding for deserving transgender students in Pakistan’s Sindh province. Under this plan, financial support will be provided through the Sindh Educational Endowment Fund to help students pursue higher studies. The deadline for this specific scholarship scheme is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Sindh Education Minister Syed Sardar Ali Shah announced that approval will be sought from the Sindh Educational Endowment Fund board to grant special scholarships to transgender students. The endowment fund pays student fees using the profit generated from its capital. This support aims to reduce financial barriers so community members can attend recognized higher education institutions and overcome social challenges.

Who Can Apply

Specific eligibility rules for the proposed transgender scholarships are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. However, the general criteria for the Sindh Educational Endowment Fund include:

  • Students who hold a domicile in Sindh.
  • Students enrolled in a recognized educational institution in Pakistan.
  • Demonstrated academic merit, requiring a minimum of a 2.5 GPA or 60% marks.

Benefits

Funding amounts for this specific scheme are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. In general, the endowment fund pays tuition fees for students to pursue higher education at over 90 public and private universities across Pakistan. The scholarship amount typically varies based on financial need, academic performance, and the level of study.

How to Apply

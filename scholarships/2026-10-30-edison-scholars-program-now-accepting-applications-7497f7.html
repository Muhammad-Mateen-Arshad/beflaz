Edison Scholars Program Now Accepting Applications

Published on October 30, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Edison International has opened applications for its $1.5 million STEM scholarship initiative, known as the Edison Scholars program. Designed to empower high school seniors who plan to earn a college degree in science, technology, engineering, or mathematics, the program is fully funded by Edison International shareholders. Since 2006, the organization has distributed over $22 million to 850 students, helping alumni build careers in fields like environmental science, civil engineering, and computer science.

As the parent company of Southern California Edison, which supplies electricity to 15 million people across Southern, Central, and Coastal California, Edison International focuses on developing the next generation of innovators to strengthen local communities.

Who Can Apply

To be eligible for the scholarship, candidates must meet the following criteria:

  • Be a current high school senior living within Southern California Edison's service region.
  • Plan to enroll as a full-time undergraduate student in a STEM major for the fall 2027 semester.
  • Have a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0.
  • Demonstrate financial need.

First-generation college applicants are also strongly encouraged to apply. Specific documentation requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

The program awards $50,000 scholarships, paid over four years, to 30 high school seniors annually. Beyond direct financial aid, scholarship recipients who finish their first year at an accredited four-year university or college are eligible to apply for a paid summer internship with Southern California Edison.

How to Apply

Qualifying students must submit their application online through edisonscholars.com. The final deadline to submit applications is January 21, 2027. Award winners will be officially announced in the spring of 2027. Detailed review criteria are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

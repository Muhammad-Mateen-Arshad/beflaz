Bharath Yoga Kranti Fully Funded Yoga Teacher Scholarship

Published on November 05, 2026

The Bharath Yoga Kranti Fully Funded Yoga Teacher Scholarship is an initiative launched by Akshar Yoga Kendraa to provide a 45-day residential professional Yoga teacher training course in Bengaluru. Designed primarily for deserving individuals across India, especially those from economically disadvantaged backgrounds, this programme covers all training and living expenses. The application deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

As part of the nationwide 'Bharath Yoga Kranti' mission aiming to train one million Yoga teachers by 2030, the first phase of this initiative provides 1,500 fully funded scholarships. The intensive residential training is conducted across Akshar Yoga Kendraa’s facilities in Bengaluru, including PURANTHA by Akshar Yoga Kendraa, the Akshar Yoga Research Centre, and the Centre for Sports Excellence. Trainees receive instruction from experienced Yoga Masters and professional instructors in traditional Yoga, holistic health, mental well-being, and teaching methodologies.

Who Can Apply

The opportunity is available to individuals across India, with priority given to applicants from economically weaker backgrounds seeking career opportunities in the wellness sector. Specific eligibility qualifications or age limits are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Selected participants in the programme receive full financial coverage and professional career assistance, including:

  • Free education and professional training
  • Complimentary accommodation and nutritious meals
  • Clothing, study materials, and Yoga equipment
  • Assessment and professional certification upon successful completion
  • Career guidance, job placement assistance, and entrepreneurship support

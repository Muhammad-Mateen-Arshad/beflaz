New Europe College Fellowships 2027–2028 Overview

Published on November 24, 2026

The New Europe College Fellowships 2027–2028 offer approximately 25 research positions in Bucharest, Romania, across eight specialized academic schemes. This opportunity is designed for both Romanian and international postdoctoral scholars conducting research in the humanities and social sciences. Interested applicants must submit their proposals before the application deadline of 17 January 2027.

Scholarship Overview

Hosted by the New Europe College (NEC), an Institute for Advanced Study based in Bucharest, this fellowship initiative provides scholars with the time, resources, and academic environment needed to carry out independent research projects. Depending on citizenship and the chosen sub-programme, fellows may receive support for a full academic year running from October 2027 to July 2028, or a single term running from October 2027 to February 2028. Selected fellows are expected to work on their proposed research, participate in institutional events, attend compulsory weekly seminars, and submit a final research paper.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility extends to early-career and postdoctoral researchers from Romania and around the globe specializing in social sciences and humanities, including fields such as law, economics, history, sociology, and political science. Specific sub-programmes target distinct demographics, including scholars affected by the war in Ukraine, researchers from Central and Eastern Europe, the Black Sea region, ex-Soviet states, Afghanistan, Tibet, and Xinjiang. Certain fellowship tracks require applicants to have earned their PhD no earlier than five years prior to applying or to be affiliated with an academic institution in eligible regions.

Benefits

Successful candidates receive several forms of support during their residency, including:

  • A tax-free monthly stipend of €1,000.
  • Living quarters and dedicated workspace in Bucharest.
  • Reimbursement of travel expenses between the fellow's home region and Bucharest.
  • Access to the institution's library and electronic resources.
  • A lump-sum grant to cover a research trip or attendance at an academic conference.

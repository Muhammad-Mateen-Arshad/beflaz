High Court overturns Palestinian student’s visa denial over ‘significant errors’ in process

Published on October 27, 2026

Scholarship Overview

A recent High Court decision highlighted an Irish visa case involving Shaymaa Abudalu, a 20-year-old Palestinian student from Gaza who was awarded a fully funded scholarship to study engineering at University College Cork. Judge Anthony Barr overturned a visa refusal issued by the Department of Justice after finding significant errors in the assessment, including a failure to review her submitted English language proficiency certification and an incorrect claim regarding her financial documentation. The court ordered that the visa application be re-considered within 10 working days so the student does not lose her academic place.

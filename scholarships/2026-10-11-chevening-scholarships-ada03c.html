Chevening Scholarships

Published on October 11, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Chevening Scholarship is the flagship global funding scheme established by the UK government, funded by the Foreign and Commonwealth Office alongside various partner organizations. The program aims to support outstanding scholars from across the world who demonstrate notable leadership potential by enabling them to pursue a postgraduate course at a higher education institution in the United Kingdom.

Who Can Apply

To be considered for a Chevening Scholarship, candidates must fulfill the following baseline criteria:

  • Hold citizenship in an officially recognized Chevening-eligible country.
  • Possess an undergraduate degree that qualifies them for entry into a postgraduate academic program at a UK university.
  • Demonstrate a minimum of two years of professional work experience.
  • Commit to returning to their home country for at least two years following the completion of their scholarship.

Additional eligibility requirements exist; applicants can review the full criteria on the official Chevening online platform.

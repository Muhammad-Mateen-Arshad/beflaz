University of Reading International PhD Studentships 2027

Published on November 18, 2026

The University of Reading International PhD Studentships 2027 offer funding for outstanding international researchers pursuing doctoral degrees in the United Kingdom. This funding scheme supports full-time and part-time doctoral candidates across various academic disciplines for entry in October 2027. Eligible applicants must submit their online admission application by 15 January 2027.

Scholarship Overview

This competition aims to attract global research talent to the University of Reading and Henley Business School. Funding is available for up to four years for full-time PhD studies and up to six years for part-time enrolled students. Most academic subjects are eligible, though professional doctorates such as the Doctor of Business Administration and Doctor of Education are excluded.

Who Can Apply

  • Applicants classified as international students for tuition fee purposes.
  • Candidates applying for an eligible doctoral programme at the university who meet departmental entry requirements.
  • Applicants submitting course applications by 15 January 2027.
  • A formal offer of admission or completed English language test is not required at the time of the scholarship deadline, though language requirements must eventually be met.

Benefits

  • Full coverage of international tuition fees.
  • A £1,000 annual training and development allowance.
  • An annual living stipend aligned with UKRI reference rates (noted as £21,805 per year for 2026/2027) for qualifying on-campus researchers.
  • Distance learners receive fee coverage and training allowances but do not receive the living stipend.
  • The award does not cover UK immigration or visa expenses for recipients or family members.

How to Apply

Candidates must first apply for PhD admission online by 15 January 2027, typing "International Research Studentship Competition" in the funding field. Applicants must also complete a separate studentship application form. Nominated candidates will be asked to draft an original research proposal of up to 700 words before the final assessment around the end of March 2027.

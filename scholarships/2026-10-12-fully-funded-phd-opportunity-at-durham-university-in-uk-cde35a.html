Fully-Funded PhD Opportunity at Durham University in UK

Published on October 12, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Durham University is accepting applications for its PhD Studentships in Transformative Humanities for candidates planning to start doctoral studies in October 2027. Up to 11 funded studentships are available under this scheme. This includes up to nine awards covering tuition at the home rate and up to two awards for overseas students, allowing international candidates, including those from Pakistan, to compete for funding. The application deadline is January 15, 2027.

Who Can Apply

Both home and overseas candidates are eligible to apply. Applicants must meet the following criteria:

  • Plan to begin a PhD program at Durham University in October 2027.
  • Fulfill the academic admission requirements for their chosen doctoral program.
  • Develop a research project that fits within the Transformative Humanities framework.
  • Establish a supervisory arrangement led by Arts and Humanities or Social Sciences that involves at least two university departments.

Benefits

Successful candidates receive full tuition coverage (at the home or international rate) and a living stipend. Specific monetary values are not specified in the source; please check the official notice, though living allowances are set in line with applicable UKRI rates. Funding covers 3.5 years for full-time doctoral study or seven years for part-time study. Students also gain access to training, placement resources, and additional funding opportunities during their studies.

How to Apply

Candidates can apply by following these steps:

  • Reach out to potential supervisors at Durham University to discuss research plans before applying.
  • Apply for the relevant PhD program through the university's online application system.
  • Type "Transformative Humanities" in the scholarship section of the application form.
  • Provide a research proposal (up to 1,000 words) linking the proposed PhD project to the Transformative Humanities framework.
  • Submit a CV (maximum two pages), contact details for two referees, and an equal opportunities monitoring form.
  • Upload degree documents and academic transcripts, along with any other materials required by the specific PhD program.

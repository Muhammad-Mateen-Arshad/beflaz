Commonwealth Shared Scholarships 2027/28: Application Guide

Published on November 15, 2026

The Commonwealth Shared Scholarships 2027/28 offer fully funded opportunities for qualified graduates from eligible Commonwealth countries to pursue one-year master's degrees in the United Kingdom. Jointly supported by the Commonwealth Scholarship Commission and participating UK institutions, these awards cover essential academic and living expenses for courses commencing in September or October 2027. Qualified candidates must complete their submissions before the deadline of 8 December 2026 at 16:00 GMT.

Scholarship Overview

This funding initiative supports students entering full-time, one-year master's degree programmes at participating UK universities. It is designed to assist talented individuals from Commonwealth nations who require financial backing to pursue postgraduate education in the UK.

Benefits

Successful applicants receive complete financial assistance for their postgraduate studies, which includes:

  • Full payment of university tuition fees.
  • Return airfare to travel between the scholar's home country and the UK.
  • A monthly living allowance of £1,712, which increases to £2,000 per month for individuals studying at institutions in London.

Who Can Apply

Applicants must satisfy several eligibility requirements to be considered for funding:

  • Hold citizenship or recognized refugee status in an eligible Commonwealth country, alongside permanent residency in that nation. (Specific eligible countries are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.)
  • Possess an upper second-class (2:1) undergraduate degree or an equivalent educational qualification.
  • Demonstrate financial need to undertake the programme.
  • Comply with Commonwealth Scholarship Commission regulations regarding prior study or employment in high-income countries.
  • Agree to return to their home country after completing the award period.

How to Apply

The application procedure involves two distinct steps. Applicants must complete an online scholarship application using the Commonwealth Scholarship Commission Central system. Additionally, candidates must submit a separate application for admission to an approved full-time master's programme at a participating UK university. All application steps must be completed by 8 December 2026 at 16:00 GMT.

The Cost Of A Promise That Was Never Kept

Published on October 17, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Higher Education Commission (HEC) in Pakistan has cautioned students and their families against fraudulent education consultants. Unscrupulous agents frequently make false promises of guaranteed foreign university admissions, fake scholarship support, and fast-track visas in exchange for exorbitant payments. Following tragic incidents involving forged admission documents and severe financial losses, official guidelines stress the vital need to verify foreign educational promises before paying any fees.

How to Apply

Application deadlines and submission procedures are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. To safely navigate foreign admissions and avoid fraudulent scholarship claims, students should follow these key steps:

  • Avoid using consultants who guarantee admissions, foreign scholarships, or accelerated visas for high fees.
  • Never make large or untraceable financial payments to educational advisers.
  • Require advisers to disclose their full identity, clear written fees, and official university affiliations in writing.

Newmont Ghana Mining Jobs: Vacancies and Application Guide

Published on November 24, 2026

Newmont Ghana Mining Jobs provide employment opportunities for Ghanaian jobseekers aiming to build careers in the gold mining industry and related operational fields. These roles are open to qualified individuals across various technical, administrative, and environmental departments. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice for current availability.

Scholarship Overview

Newmont Corporation operates major mining sites in Ghana, including the Ahafo North and Ahafo South facilities. The company regularly advertises employment vacancies across diverse departments such as mining operations, maintenance, engineering, geology, environmental management, finance, and administrative services.

Who Can Apply

These positions are available for Ghanaians who possess relevant technical skills, academic qualifications, and professional experience. Past and present job listings include roles such as LV Driver, Blast Helper, Crane Rigger, Mine Maintenance Mechanic, and Environment Technician. Specific qualification requirements for each role are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Successful candidates secure professional employment with a major international gold mining firm. In addition, prospective candidates can join Newmont's talent community to receive updates and notifications about future job vacancies.

How to Apply

Applicants must submit their information directly through the official online portal by following these steps:

  • Access Newmont Corporation's official careers site.
  • Choose the option to explore open positions.
  • Filter the location settings to select Ghana.
  • Browse the listings and choose a role aligned with your experience and qualifications.
  • Read the complete job description, responsibilities, and criteria.
  • Click "Apply Now" if the posting is active.
  • Complete the application form and attach requested documents, including an updated CV and academic certificates.

Newmont does not charge any recruitment fees, and applicants are advised never to pay money or submit sensitive personal details outside the official platform.

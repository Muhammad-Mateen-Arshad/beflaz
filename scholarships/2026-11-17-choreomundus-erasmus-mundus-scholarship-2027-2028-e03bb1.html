Choreomundus Erasmus Mundus Scholarship 2027-2028

Published on November 17, 2026

The Choreomundus Erasmus Mundus Scholarship 2027-2028 offers fully funded financial support for international students pursuing a two-year Joint Master's degree in dance, movement, and cultural heritage. The programme is designed for qualified graduates and experienced professionals seeking interdisciplinary study across four leading European universities. Candidates must submit their applications by 15 January 2027 at 23:59:59 Eastern European Time.

Scholarship Overview

The Choreomundus Joint Master is a full-time, 24-month postgraduate programme carrying 120 ECTS credits. It explores dance, rituals, games, martial arts, and physical performance through anthropology, heritage studies, ethnochoreology, and technology. The degree is jointly delivered by four European institutions: Vytautas Magnus University (Lithuania), National and Kapodistrian University of Athens (Greece), University of Clermont Auvergne (France), and the University of Roehampton (United Kingdom). Upon graduation, scholars receive a single joint Master's diploma signed by all partner universities.

Benefits

A total of 17 Erasmus Mundus scholarships will be awarded for the 2027–2028 intake. The funding package provides substantial financial assistance for the duration of the studies:

  • Monthly stipend of €1,400 throughout the 24-month duration (totaling €33,600).
  • Full tuition fee waiver valued at €18,000, funded by the European Union's Erasmus+ programme.
  • Academic exposure across study locations in Lithuania, Greece, France, and potentially the United Kingdom.

Who Can Apply

Applicants must meet specific academic and language credentials to be eligible for admission:

  • Possess a recognised Bachelor's degree (at least 180 ECTS) or a Master's degree (at least 120 ECTS).
  • Candidates holding degrees in other disciplines, such as engineering, health sciences, biomedicine, or computer science, may apply if they have a strong background in dance or movement practices.
  • Maintain a minimum cumulative grade average of 60% (equivalent to a grade C in the Bologna system).
  • Demonstrate English language capability at a B2 CEFR level or provide institutionally confirmed proof of instruction in English.

How to Apply

Applicants do not need to fill out an independent scholarship request. All eligible applicants for the Choreomundus programme are automatically considered for the available funded spots during the standard admissions evaluation process. Specific document requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

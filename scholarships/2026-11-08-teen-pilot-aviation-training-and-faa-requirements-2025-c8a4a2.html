Who Can Apply

Eligibility requirements depend on the specific certification level being pursued:

  • Private Pilot Certificate: Applicants must be at least 17 years old and have completed a minimum of 40 flight hours, including 10 hours of solo flying.
  • Commercial Pilot Certificate: Candidates must be at least 18 years old and possess 250 flight hours.
  • Major Airline Certification: Full certification requires an individual to be at least 23 years old with 1,500 flight hours. Military pilots and graduates of aviation college programs can receive a restricted license at age 21 with fewer flight hours.
  • High School Flight Programs: Select institutional programs, such as Rancho High School, admit students around age 16 to begin logging flight hours and preparing for written FAA examinations.

Benefits

Enrolled students gain structured technical instruction, prepare for official aviation exams, and build necessary flight hours early in life. Successful candidates can progress from earning private certificates to working as flight instructors or charter co-pilots while accumulating experience for major airline positions.

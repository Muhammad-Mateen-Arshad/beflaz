HEC Online Degree Attestation and Verification Guide

Published on November 17, 2026

The HEC Online Degree Attestation and Verification Guide details the digital services provided by the Higher Education Commission for students needing degree verification. This online portal streamlines the attestation process to offer greater service efficiency and transparency. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

The Higher Education Commission has modernized its degree attestation system to resolve difficulties previously experienced by applicants. Since the platform was launched, more than 150,000 degrees have been verified, with nearly 98 percent of total submitted applications successfully processed. Following management under Dr. Amjad, continuous improvements are being made to ensure faster and more convenient services.

Who Can Apply

The digital service is open to students who require official degree attestation and verification. Additional applicant criteria are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

  • High processing success rate, reaching approximately 98 percent of received applications.
  • Improved application monitoring and operational transparency.
  • Future feature enabling students to indicate if they require Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) attestation during the initial online application.

How to Apply

Students must submit their requests through the Higher Education Commission's online portal. Full step-by-step application instructions and fees are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

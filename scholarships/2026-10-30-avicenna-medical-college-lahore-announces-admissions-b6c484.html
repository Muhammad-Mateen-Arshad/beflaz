Avicenna Medical College Lahore Announces Admissions

Published on October 30, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Avicenna Medical College in Lahore has opened admissions for four undergraduate degree programs spanning nursing and allied health sciences. Offered through the Avicenna Institute of Allied Health Sciences and Gulfreen Nursing College, these degree pathways feature merit-based scholarship opportunities that cover up to 50% of tuition costs for eligible fields. The final deadline to submit applications is November 15, 2026.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility requirements vary depending on the chosen academic field:

  • Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT): A five-year degree program requiring candidates to have achieved a minimum of 60% marks in FSc Pre-Medical.
  • BS (Hons) Medical Imaging Technology (MIT): A four-year degree program requiring at least 60% marks in FSc Pre-Medical.
  • BS (Hons) Human Nutrition and Dietetics (HND): A four-year degree program requiring at least 60% marks in FSc Pre-Medical.
  • Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN): A four-year degree program requiring candidates to be between 14 and 35 years old with at least 50% marks in FSc Pre-Medical, including Biology.

Benefits

Successful candidates entering allied health sciences programs—specifically DPT, BS MIT, and BS HND—can qualify for merit-based scholarships providing up to 50% coverage. Details regarding the exact award calculations and covered fee items are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Additionally, whether nursing students qualify for these awards is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Prospective students must complete their submission using the institution's online portal at students.avicennamch.com. Applicants need to register an account, log into the admission portal, select their intended program, and input all required personal and educational information accurately.

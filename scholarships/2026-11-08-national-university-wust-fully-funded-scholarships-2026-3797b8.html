National University WUST Fully Funded Scholarships 2026

Published on November 08, 2026

National University has established an agreement with Washington University of Science and Technology to offer the National University WUST Fully Funded Scholarships 2026. This academic initiative provides five selected students with the opportunity to pursue higher studies in the United States. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Formally signed in Washington on 6 November 2026, the memorandum of understanding between the two institutions aims to strengthen cooperation in research, higher education, and practical skills development. Washington University of Science and Technology will prioritize expanding academic and research paths for students from National University. Furthermore, the partnership includes joint educational projects and structural reforms to academic syllabi and curricula.

Fields of Study

The academic collaboration focuses on several key subjects, including the following disciplines:

  • Artificial intelligence applications
  • Business administration
  • Clinical laboratory science
  • Cybersecurity
  • Hospitality management
  • Information technology
  • Occupational science
  • Software engineering

Who Can Apply

This initiative is intended for talented National University students who wish to access international study opportunities in the US. Further applicant criteria and qualifications are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

The agreement provides fully funded scholarships for five chosen students to undertake further education at a American university. Specific funding amounts, stipends, or coverage details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

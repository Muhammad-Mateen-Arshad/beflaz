University of Florida Public Health PhD Scholarship 2027

Published on November 11, 2026

The University of Florida Public Health PhD Scholarship 2027 offers graduate assistantship funding for doctoral students entering the Health Services Research concentration. Administered by the College of Public Health and Health Professions in Gainesville, Florida, this doctoral program supports candidates pursuing advanced research in public health systems and quantitative methods. Applicants seeking financial support must submit their full application by January 15, 2027.

Scholarship Overview

The PhD in Public Health with a concentration in Health Services Research prepares scholars to study healthcare delivery, health outcomes, healthcare quality, access to care, and public health systems. Graduates are prepared for research careers in academic institutions, government bodies, healthcare systems, and research organizations. Applications for Fall 2027 entry open on August 30, 2026. While the general and part-time application deadline is January 31, 2027, those applying for funding must meet an earlier deadline.

Benefits

Eligible candidates awarded a graduate assistantship receive financial aid that includes:

  • A tuition waiver for 9 credits during the Fall term.
  • A tuition waiver for 9 credits during the Spring term.
  • A tuition waiver for 6 credits during the Summer term.
  • A bi-weekly stipend (the exact amount is not specified in the source; please check the official notice).

Who Can Apply

To qualify for program admission and funding consideration, applicants must meet the following criteria:

  • Hold an accredited bachelor's, graduate, or professional degree from a US institution or an equivalent international qualification.
  • Maintain a minimum GPA of 3.0 on a 4.0 scale across their last 60 undergraduate credit hours.
  • Demonstrate English proficiency if applicable, typically requiring a minimum TOEFL score of 80 (4.5 on the updated scale) or an IELTS score of 6.5.
  • GRE scores are optional, though submitting them is highly recommended to show quantitative readiness.

How to Apply

Interested candidates must complete an application via the University of Florida online system by taking the following steps:

  • Pay the US$30 non-refundable application fee.
  • Select the College of Public Health and Health Professions, choose Public Health – Doctor of Philosophy, and select Health Services Research as the concentration.
  • Upload unofficial transcripts from all post-secondary institutions (international transcripts require a WES ICAP evaluation).
  • Submit three recommendation letters, including at least one from an academic referee.
  • Upload a CV and two essays explaining academic motivation, research experience, specific research interests, and alignment with two selected UF faculty members.
  • Submit official TOEFL or IELTS scores using UF code 5812 if required. Official transcripts are requested only after receiving an offer of admission.

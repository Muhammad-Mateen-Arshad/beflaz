CM Maryam inaugurates University of Rasul, Medical College in Mandi Bahauddin

Published on October 28, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Punjab government, led by Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif, has introduced major student support initiatives including the CM Punjab Honhaar Scholarship Programme and the CM Laptop Scheme. During an event in Mandi Bahauddin for the inauguration of the University of Rasul and Rasul Medical College, the Chief Minister distributed laptop devices and scholarship cheques to selected students. Education Minister Rana Sikandar Hayat briefed participants on both programs, highlighting the government's commitment to supporting students and investing in higher education.

Who Can Apply

Based on recent distribution figures, the Honhaar Scholarship covers students across various educational institutions in the region, including:

  • Public university students (50 recipients in Mandi Bahauddin)
  • Private and federal university students (38 recipients in Mandi Bahauddin)
  • Medical college students (15 recipients in the district)
  • General college students (368 recipients in the district)

Benefits

Under the CM Laptop Scheme, a total of 5,531 students from Mandi Bahauddin and Gujrat received laptops. Honhaar Scholarship beneficiaries receive direct financial support through scholarship cheques. In addition to individual awards, the government has invested billions of rupees to open new academic departments in social sciences, life sciences, and business management at the University of Rasul.

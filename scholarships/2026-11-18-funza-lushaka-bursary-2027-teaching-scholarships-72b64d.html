Funza Lushaka Bursary 2027: Teaching Scholarships

Published on November 18, 2026

The Funza Lushaka Bursary 2027: Teaching Scholarships initiative is a South African government program that provides financial support to prospective educators pursuing recognized teaching qualifications. Administered by the Department of Basic Education alongside partner institutions, it targets individuals interested in teaching priority subjects in public schools. Applications opened on 1 October 2026, but final closing deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

The bursary aims to address teacher shortages in South African public schools by supporting students in high-demand subject areas. Managed in collaboration with the Department of Higher Education and Training and the National Student Financial Aid Scheme (NSFAS), the scheme works to enhance educational quality, expand access to qualified teachers, and build a sustainable workforce.

Who Can Apply

Funding is aimed at individuals committed to becoming professional educators in South Africa's public education sector. Applicants must meet the following general requirements:

  • Intend to enrol in approved teaching qualifications focusing on priority subjects.
  • Satisfy all academic, institutional, and programme selection criteria.
  • Complete the mandatory annual reapplication if returning as an existing bursary holder.
  • Agree to contract conditions, including mandatory service obligations to teach in public schools after graduation.

Specific priority teaching subjects are not specified in the source; please check the official notice for the full list.

Benefits

Depending on available funding allocations and individual applicant circumstances, financial coverage may assist with:

  • Approved university tuition fees.
  • Accommodation expenses or transport allowances for students living at home or with relatives.
  • Daily meal and nutritional support.
  • Textbooks and necessary academic resources.
  • Personal care allowances for basic living needs.
  • Expenses related to practical teaching and workplace learning.

How to Apply

Candidates must submit their applications electronically via the official South African government eServices portal by following these steps:

  • Register a new user profile or log into an existing portal account.
  • Read through the application notes, priority subject lists, and eligibility rules.
  • Fill in accurate personal, academic, and university details.
  • Submit the completed online form before the applicable deadline.
  • Save the submission confirmation and monitor official channels for application status updates.

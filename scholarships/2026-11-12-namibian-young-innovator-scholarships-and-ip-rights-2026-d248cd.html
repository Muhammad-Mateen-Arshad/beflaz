Namibian Young Innovator Scholarships and IP Rights 2026

Published on November 12, 2026

Discussions regarding Namibian young innovator scholarships and IP rights in 2026 focus on combining higher education opportunities with local technical and legal protection for emerging inventors. These initiatives concern young Namibian creators who develop practical solutions for local challenges. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

During a parliamentary outreach programme held between 2 and 14 November 2026 across the Zambezi and Kavango East regions, educators raised concerns about how scholarships are currently utilized. Augustinus Haimbodi, a Physics and Chemistry teacher, noted that young inventors awarded scholarships to study abroad—such as studying solar energy in China—risk leaving their original creations undeveloped. In response, Secretary to the National Council Adv. Tousy Namiseb stated that while scholarships provide essential learning opportunities, young inventors must understand the legal conditions attached to prevent losing ownership of their intellectual property.

Benefits

Key support features and benefits discussed for young innovators include:

  • Access to international academic scholarships to pursue higher education.
  • Patent registration guidance and intellectual property protection through the Business and Intellectual Property Authority (BIPA).
  • Opportunities to secure financial and technical assistance via institutions such as the National Commission on Research, Science and Technology (NCRST) and the National Youth Council.

University of Lethbridge Flanagan Science Scholarships

Published on November 27, 2026

The University of Lethbridge Flanagan Science Scholarships are newly established endowed funding opportunities created to assist undergraduate and graduate students in the sciences. Created through a joint $4 million investment from the Flanagan Foundation and the university's Board of Governors, these awards support incoming undergraduate science students as well as advanced thesis-based biological science researchers. Deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

The funding initiative combines a $2 million donation from the Flanagan Foundation with a $2 million matching contribution from the University of Lethbridge Board of Governors. This $4 million total investment establishes two permanent scholarship funds aimed at encouraging evidence-based research, scientific inquiry, and academic excellence across the institution.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility depends on the specific award stream:

  • Undergraduate Applicants: Incoming high school students applying to enter a Bachelor of Science degree program at the University of Lethbridge. Candidates are selected based on academic achievement.
  • Graduate Applicants: Students who have been accepted into thesis-based Master of Science (MSc) or Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) programs within the biological sciences department.

Benefits

The endowment finances two distinct scholarship programs:

  • Flanagan Graduate Scholarship for Research in Biological Sciences: Provides $50,000 per award to support thesis-based graduate researchers.
  • Flanagan Family Science Entrance Scholarship: Awards $2,000 per recipient for new undergraduate students. At least 50 of these entrance awards are scheduled to be available in the inaugural year.

How to Apply

Application steps are not specified in the source; please check the official notice for guidance. Regarding availability, one graduate scholarship has already been awarded for 2026, with a second scheduled for distribution during the 2027 winter semester. The undergraduate entrance scholarship program is set to launch starting in 2027.

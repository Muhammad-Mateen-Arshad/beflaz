Five Fully Funded Study Abroad Scholarships for 2026

Published on November 13, 2026

Prospective international students can explore five fully funded study abroad scholarships for 2026 offering financial assistance for postgraduate studies in the United Kingdom and Europe. These opportunities cater to master's and doctoral degree candidates seeking funding for tuition fees, accommodation, and travel. Deadlines for several featured awards close before early December 2026.

Scholarship Overview

Multiple institutions and organizations provide funding opportunities to help international candidates study abroad. Available financial aid ranges from partial fee coverage to complete funding packages including study costs, visa fees, and living allowances.

Available Funding Opportunities

  • Ellison Scholars Programme: Supports Doctor of Philosophy (PhD/DPhil) candidates at the University of Oxford working on Ellison Institute of Technology projects. It offers full financial backing and expert mentorship. Applications close on December 1, 2026.
  • Cambridge Trust Scholarships: Provides full or partial funding for postgraduate studies at the University of Cambridge. Deadlines for the 2027/2028 academic year are December 8, 2026, and January 6, 2027. US applicants for Gates Cambridge must apply by October 14, 2026.
  • Commonwealth Shared Scholarships: Fully funds master's degrees in the United Kingdom for students from eligible Commonwealth countries for studies starting in late 2027. Applications close on December 8, 2026, at 4 pm GMT.
  • Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters: Delivers full scholarships for master's programs lasting one to two years across European universities. Funding includes tuition, living allowances, visa costs, and travel. Deadlines vary by course.
  • East-West Center Graduate Degree Fellowship: Financial details and application requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility requirements depend on the scholarship provider. Commonwealth Shared Scholarships are exclusively for master's degree applicants from eligible Commonwealth nations. The Ellison Scholars Programme targets doctoral applicants focused on technology solutions, while Erasmus Mundus awards are open to top-performing students globally.

How to Apply

Applicants must submit their documentation online through designated portals, such as the CSC Central system for Commonwealth awards. Direct website links and application portals are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

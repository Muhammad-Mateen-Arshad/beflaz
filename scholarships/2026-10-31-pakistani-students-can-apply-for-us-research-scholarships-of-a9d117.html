Pakistani Students Can Apply for US Research Scholarships Offering Up to $35,000 Annually

Published on October 31, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Auburn University, located in Alabama, United States, is offering three fully funded graduate research assistantships for students pursuing master’s or doctoral degrees. The research positions are housed within Alan Wilson’s laboratory, which focuses on harmful algal blooms, plankton communities, and freshwater ecosystems. Current research projects in the laboratory are supported by several agencies, including the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), USDA-ARS, and USDA-NIFA.

Benefits

Selected students will receive 12-month stipends alongside complete tuition fee coverage:

  • Master's Students: An annual stipend of $30,000.
  • PhD Students: An annual stipend of $35,000.
  • Presidential Fellowships: Exceptional doctoral applicants may be considered for special presidential fellowships offering at least $50,000 annually for up to three years, though this award requires a separate competitive evaluation.

Who Can Apply

The laboratory welcomes applicants interested in aquatic ecology, environmental science, freshwater ecosystems, and quantitative research. Key qualification details include:

  • Degree Level: Both master’s and PhD applicants are considered, though preference is given to PhD candidates.
  • Desired Skills: Applicants should display strong motivation, teamwork, and honesty. Beneficial qualifications include prior research experience, analytical skills, and coursework in ecology, limnology, or statistics, though these are not compulsory.
  • Academic Requirements: Minimum GPA and standardized English test requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice for Auburn University's graduate admission standards.

How to Apply

Applicants must submit their application materials using the online form provided in the official research advertisement. Required application materials include:

  • A detailed curriculum vitae (CV)
  • Copies of academic transcripts
  • Contact details for three professional references
  • GRE scores (optional; not mandatory)

The closing date for applications is July 14, 2027. Admitted students can coordinate their starting dates directly with the research lab. Inquiries regarding the positions may be directed to Alan Wilson via email.

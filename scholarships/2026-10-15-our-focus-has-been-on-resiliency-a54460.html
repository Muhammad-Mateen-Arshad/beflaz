‘Our focus has been on resiliency.’

Published on October 15, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Harvard University recently released financial results following the close of its fiscal year on June 30, highlighting strategic efforts to maintain organizational resiliency. In an interview, Executive Vice President Meredith Weenick and Chief Financial Officer Ritu Kalra discussed the university's $100 million operating surplus on a $6.7 billion revenue base, as well as significant investments in academic discovery.

The institution expended more than $1.2 billion on research activities, with one-third funded directly by the university. Additionally, a $150 million research investment was announced to support tools and infrastructure in strategic fields including:

  • Artificial intelligence
  • Quantum technology
  • Neuroscience
  • Immunology
  • Energy and climate

While the financial report details broad research funding and institutional support, direct information regarding individual student scholarships is not provided in the report summary.

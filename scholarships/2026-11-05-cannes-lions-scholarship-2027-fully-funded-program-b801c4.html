Cannes Lions Scholarship 2027: Fully Funded Program

Published on November 05, 2026

The Cannes Lions Scholarship 2027 provides fully funded opportunities for emerging creative talent from around the world to participate in specialized professional academies. This initiative supports young individuals under 30 who are eager to expand their industry knowledge and gain international exposure. The application deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

The award focuses on promoting diverse perspectives within the creative sector, emphasizing variety across background, gender, race, social class, personal preference, and geographic location. A total of 10 participants from 10 different countries will be chosen across two distinct learning tracks:

  • 5 places in the Brand Marketers Academy
  • 5 places in the Creative Academy

Who Can Apply

Candidates must fulfill several basic qualification criteria to be considered:

  • Applicants must be born on or after June 26, 1997.
  • Candidates must be at least 18 years old and able to provide official proof of age.
  • A minimum of two years of relevant work experience in a professional role is mandatory.
  • Applicants must be proficient in English, as all academy activities and presentations are delivered in English.

The specific eligible countries are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Recipients selected for the award will receive complete financial support to attend Cannes Lions 2027, including:

  • Full travel expenses to and from the event
  • Provided accommodation throughout the stay
  • A living allowance
  • A Young Lions Festival pass, providing access to expert classroom sessions, practical workshops, guest speakers, and coaching from industry figures

Application procedures, required documents, and submission guidelines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

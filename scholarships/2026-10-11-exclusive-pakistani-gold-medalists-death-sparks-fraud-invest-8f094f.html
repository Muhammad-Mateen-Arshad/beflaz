Exclusive: Pakistani Gold Medalist’s Death Sparks Fraud Investigation Over Fake US Scholarship

Published on October 11, 2026

Scholarship Overview

A federal fraud investigation was launched in Pakistan following an alleged fraudulent United States scholarship scheme. Laiba Ejaz, a 24-year-old physics gold medalist from the International Islamic University Islamabad, and her family were defrauded of 9.3 million Pakistani rupees (around $34,000) by suspects promising admission, a scholarship, and a visa for Harvard University.

The scheme involved fake paperwork, including a counterfeit receipt claiming $7,500 in tuition fees was paid for a master's program in physics. The forgery was uncovered during a visa interview at the US Embassy in Islamabad, and Harvard University confirmed the documents were unauthorized. Following directives from Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, the Federal Investigation Agency (FIA) and Islamabad Police initiated a criminal investigation.

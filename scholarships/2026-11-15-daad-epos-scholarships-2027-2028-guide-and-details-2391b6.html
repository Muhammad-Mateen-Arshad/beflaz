DAAD EPOS Scholarships 2027/2028: Guide and Details

Published on November 15, 2026

Applications are open for the DAAD EPOS Scholarships 2027/2028, funded by the German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) for professionals from eligible developing and newly industrialized nations. This opportunity supports fully funded Master’s and doctoral degrees in Germany lasting between 12 and 42 months. Application deadlines vary by academic program, with specific closing dates such as November 30, 2026, for select courses.

Scholarship Overview

The DAAD Development-Related Postgraduate Courses (EPOS) initiative equips future leaders with specialized skills to foster sustainable development in their home countries. The catalog features a range of development-oriented disciplines. Examples of open programs include Development Economics at the University of Göttingen, Agricultural Economics at the University of Hohenheim, and Tropical Hydrogeology and Environmental Engineering at TU Darmstadt.

Benefits

Recipients receive financial coverage during their studies in Germany, which includes:

  • A monthly stipend of €992 for Master’s candidates or €1,400 for doctoral students (supported in exceptional cases).
  • Full exemption from tuition fees, though standard university administrative or registration fees may still apply.
  • Contributions toward health, accident, and personal liability insurance coverage.
  • A travel allowance, unless these costs are funded by another source.

Who Can Apply

To qualify for consideration, applicants must meet the following criteria:

  • Be a professional from an eligible developing or newly industrialized country (Kenya is explicitly eligible).
  • Hold a relevant undergraduate degree, typically ranking in the upper third of their class, obtained within the last six years.
  • Have earned at least two years of relevant professional work experience after completing their first degree.
  • Fulfill the specific language criteria of the chosen degree program.
  • Show clear development-related goals and career motivation.
  • Have lived in Germany for no longer than 15 months at the time of the application deadline.

How to Apply

Prospective applicants must check their country’s eligibility on the DAAD website and submit their application directly to the university offering their selected course. Because deadlines depend on the specific program and several intake windows have already closed, candidates should apply as early as possible. For full step-by-step application procedures not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Expanded scholarship program ‘a huge relief’ for SC’s college-bound high school seniors

Published on October 20, 2026

The Meeting Street Scholarship Fund has expanded across South Carolina through a partnership between the state government and the Meeting Street nonprofit organization, managed by Beemok Education. This program aims to support high-achieving, low-income students who wish to pursue higher education in South Carolina by offering funding to help them graduate with minimal to zero debt.

Scholarship Overview

Originally created for students in Charleston County and later expanded to 14 legacy counties through private donations, the scholarship program is now expanding statewide to cover 32 additional counties. Funding for the expansion is provided through state lottery profits along with contributions from private donors. The scholarship acts as supplemental funding to cover costs that remain after other financial assistance is awarded.

Who Can Apply

To be eligible for the scholarship, applicants must satisfy criteria based on academic performance, financial background, and ongoing service requirements:

  • Be a high school senior living in South Carolina.
  • Meet the income eligibility criteria required for a federal Pell Grant.
  • Maintain a B average or higher in school.
  • Complete a work requirement during college: recipients in their second and third years must complete 250 hours of work, which can include part-time employment on or off campus, an internship, or research activities.

Benefits

The Meeting Street Scholarship offers substantial financial backing to cover university costs, including tuition, housing, meals, and educational supplies:

  • Recipients can receive up to $10,000 annually, totaling up to $40,000 over four years.
  • The award stacks directly on top of federal Pell Grants as well as state-funded awards like the LIFE or Palmetto Fellows scholarships.
  • The program fills remaining financial gaps, covering an average gap of $7,900 per student after other aid is applied.

How to Apply

Applications for the scholarship program open on November 9. Further details regarding application forms, requirements, and submission instructions are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Huazhong Agricultural University Scholarships 2027 Guide

Published on November 22, 2026

The Huazhong Agricultural University Scholarships 2027 Guide outlines postgraduate funding opportunities for international students seeking Master’s and Doctoral degrees in China. Available for both Chinese-taught and English-taught academic tracks, these scholarships support outstanding candidates with strong academic backgrounds. Applications are accepted between 1 November 2026 and 21 February 2027.

Scholarship Overview

Located in Wuhan, Hubei Province, Huazhong Agricultural University (HZAU) offers two primary funding paths for international postgraduate applicants: the Chinese Government Scholarship and the HZAU Scholarship. The funding aims to support international educational exchange and scientific research across multiple disciplines, including agricultural sciences, engineering, management, economics, humanities, and social sciences.

Benefits

Selected recipients receive financial assistance to cover study and living expenses in China, which includes:

  • Full tuition fee waiver
  • Free university accommodation
  • Comprehensive medical insurance
  • Monthly living stipend

Exact stipend values vary depending on the candidate's degree level and scholarship type; specific amounts are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Who Can Apply

International applicants from any country outside the People's Republic of China may apply if they fulfill the following conditions:

  • Be in good mental and physical health.
  • Maintain a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 on a 4.0 scale for Master’s or PhD entry.
  • Show clear academic excellence, research capabilities, and innovation potential.
  • Fulfill language criteria: applicants for Chinese-taught degree programmes generally need HSK Level 5 or higher (or university approval for a one-year Chinese preparatory course), whereas applicants for English-taught programmes must present recognized English proficiency test results or official proof of prior degree instruction in English.

How to Apply

Candidates must prepare digital copies of their documents and submit them via the official online submission systems. Those seeking the Chinese Government Scholarship must complete both the CSC form and the HZAU institutional admission application. Standard application materials include:

  • Highest academic certificate or official expected graduation documentation
  • Two recommendation letters from professors or associate professors
  • Additional achievements such as scientific publications, award certificates, and language scores

Punjab Boards Announce Class 11 and 12 Exam Date Sheet 2026

Published on October 24, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Punjab Boards of Intermediate and Secondary Education have announced the examination date sheet for the Intermediate 2nd Annual Examinations 2026, commencing on November 3, 2026. For students under the Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (BISE) Multan, written theory papers will take place between November 3 and November 28, 2026, followed by practical assessments from December 1 to December 11, 2026. Details regarding associated financial funding amounts are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Who Can Apply

This date sheet applies to intermediate students participating in upcoming secondary board evaluations across Punjab. Specific academic qualification guidelines for financial awards are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

  • Students enrolled in 11th class (HSSC Part I) second annual examinations.
  • Students enrolled in 12th class (HSSC Part II) second annual examinations under BISE Multan and other regional Punjab boards.

Benefits

Completing these examinations allows students to fulfill their Higher Secondary School Certificate requirements. Additional grant benefits or laptop distribution details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Candidates must review their examination timings and group shifts directly with their respective educational boards before attending their scheduled tests. Application steps for associated government scholarship initiatives are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

  • BISE Multan HSSC Part II theory examinations are scheduled from November 3 to November 16, 2026, with morning and evening sessions for select subjects.
  • Practical exams are split into two daily shifts: Batch 1 runs from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM, and Batch 2 runs from 1:00 PM to 4:00 PM.
  • On Fridays, the second practical batch is adjusted to run from 2:30 PM to 5:30 PM.
  • Students registered with other boards in Punjab should independently confirm their subject schedules with their official board office.

Fully-Funded Master’s in Chemistry Scholarships in Canada

Published on October 15, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The University of British Columbia in Canada offers financial support for students entering its Master of Science (MSc) in Chemistry program. Admitted candidates receive financial coverage over the standard two-year funding period, provided they maintain satisfactory research and teaching performance.

Who Can Apply

The program is open to domestic and international applicants, though international spots are limited. Candidates must meet the following criteria:

  • Hold a recognized bachelor's degree in Chemistry or a related scientific discipline, such as mathematics, physics, or biology.
  • Achieve an academic average in the B+ range (approximately 76% at UBC), or the equivalent standard at international institutions. Applicants from Pakistan require a recognized four-year bachelor's degree with first-class or first-division standing.
  • Provide proof of English proficiency if prior studies were not in English. Required minimum scores include an IELTS Academic overall band of 6.5 (minimum 6.0 per component); Duolingo score of 125 (minimum 115 in Reading/Listening, 120 in Writing/Speaking); or TOEFL iBT (minimum 92 overall for tests prior to January 21, 2026, or 4.5 overall under the revised scale starting January 21, 2026).

Benefits

Financial assistance for the MSc in Chemistry includes:

  • A minimum annual funding package of CAD 30,000, bringing total support to at least CAD 60,000 over two years through assistantships, external awards, and university scholarships.
  • A Faculty of Science Master’s Tuition Award for all first-year students, covering an amount equal to domestic tuition fees.
  • An International Tuition Award providing up to CAD 3,200 annually for eligible international candidates.

How to Apply

Applicants must apply online through the official UBC graduate admissions portal for the September 2027 intake using a UBC Campus-Wide Login account. Application materials require post-secondary transcripts, degree certificates, contact information for three academic references, an updated CV, a two-page personal statement, and a one-page research statement. Application deadlines: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

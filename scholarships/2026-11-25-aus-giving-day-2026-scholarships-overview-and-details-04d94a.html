AUS Giving Day 2026 Scholarships: Overview and Details

Published on November 25, 2026

The AUS Giving Day 2026 Scholarships campaign represents a major funding initiative organized by the American University of Sharjah (AUS) to assist talented and deserving students. This effort provides financial aid to help students pursue and complete their higher education regardless of their financial circumstances. The official deadline for scholarship applications is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

The recent Giving Day event raised a record AED 10.85 million to expand educational access at AUS. Funding gathered through the university's Together We Rise campaign helps ensure that financial difficulties do not prevent qualified candidates from completing their studies. Previous Giving Day drives have enabled fully funded scholarships for six students, five of whom have already begun their coursework at AUS. Key contributions include a dedicated resilience fund established by Hamid D. Jafar for students with major financial need, alongside support pledged by strategic partners like the Sharjah Commerce and Tourism Development Authority.

Who Can Apply

Financial assistance and awards under this program target students encountering financial hardships. Key applicant profiles mentioned include:

  • Eligible students seeking to enroll in or complete degree programs at AUS.
  • Students with demonstrated financial need requiring aid to finish their education.
  • Recipients of merit-based awards, such as the AUS Presidential Scholarship provided to high-achieving international students.
  • Detailed qualification standards and GPA requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Selected students receive financial support aimed at widening access to higher education. Reported benefits include:

  • Fully funded scholarship opportunities for selected recipients.
  • Assistance from a dedicated resilience fund to manage significant financial hardship.
  • Financial grants intended to help students complete their degree programs.

Ireland Fully Funded Scholarships for Gaza Students

Published on November 19, 2026

Ireland Fully Funded Scholarships for Gaza Students offer selected individuals from Gaza the opportunity to pursue higher education at academic institutions across Ireland. These awards support candidates enrolled in Master's and other higher education courses, with several study visas recently granted following a court review. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

This initiative assists Gaza scholars who have secured academic offers at Irish colleges and universities, including specialized programs like dance at the University of Limerick's Irish World Academy of Music and Dance. While Irish authorities and university representatives are coordinating logistics for student arrivals, international transit out of the region relies on approvals from external authorities, including Israeli and Jordanian officials.

Who Can Apply

The recent decisions apply to students from Gaza who hold offers for third-level courses in Ireland, including those beginning in January. Additional qualification requirements and target demographics are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

  • Full financial support through private and fully-funded scholarship grants.
  • Permission to enter and study in Ireland via approved immigration visas.
  • Coordinated transit assistance to help approved recipients travel from designated meeting locations.

How to Apply

Specific application instructions and registration portals are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Students approved for travel are subject to strict transit guidelines, which restrict personal luggage to one small soft handbag containing documentation, necessary medication, and a mobile phone with a charger. Laptops, suitcases, and backpacks are prohibited during evacuation transport.

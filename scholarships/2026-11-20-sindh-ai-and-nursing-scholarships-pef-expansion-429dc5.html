Sindh AI and Nursing Scholarships: PEF Expansion

Published on November 20, 2026

The Sindh government has introduced expanded educational funding through the Sindh AI and Nursing Scholarships in partnership with the Professional Education Foundation (PEF). This initiative targets students pursuing high-demand degree programs across emerging technological and healthcare disciplines. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Following recent cabinet approval, a revised Memorandum of Understanding was established between the Professional Education Foundation and the Universities and Boards Department. This agreement redirects funding toward career fields aligned with modern employment demands. Over a 16-year period, PEF has granted more than 27,500 scholarships, helping produce roughly 4,000 graduates while currently supporting 3,550 active students. For the 2026-27 academic target, the organization aims to fund 3,700 students.

Who Can Apply

The program targets individuals pursuing degrees in specified employment-oriented sectors. Eligible subjects of study include:

  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Information Technology
  • Nursing and Allied Health Sciences
  • Veterinary Sciences

Recruitment is centered on merit-based selection, evaluating academic performance along with market demand for the chosen field. Female students are given special encouragement to apply. Further eligibility prerequisites are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Selected students receive scholarship support to pursue professional degrees in key growth sectors. The precise funding amounts and coverage details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

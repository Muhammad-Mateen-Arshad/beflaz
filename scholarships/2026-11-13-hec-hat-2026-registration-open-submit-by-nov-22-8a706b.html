HEC HAT 2026 Registration Open: Submit by Nov 22

Published on November 13, 2026

The HEC HAT 2026 Registration Open announcement marks the commencement of online applications for the Higher Education Aptitude Test managed by the Education Testing Council across Pakistan. This exam is designed for candidates seeking admission to MS, MPhil, and PhD degree programmes or applying for HEC postgraduate scholarships. Eligible individuals must complete their online registration by the deadline of November 22, 2026.

Scholarship Overview

The Higher Education Commission (HEC) will hold the test nationwide on December 13. According to the official schedule, roll number slips will be issued on December 4, and results are anticipated on December 31, 2026. The examination totals 100 marks and assesses quantitative reasoning, English or verbal reasoning, and analytical reasoning. Candidates must achieve a minimum score of 50 marks to pass, and test results remain valid for two years. Exact funding amounts are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Who Can Apply

The test is intended for applicants pursuing postgraduate admissions at participating public and private universities or seeking current and future HEC scholarship funding. Candidates must select one of five test categories based on their field of study:

  • HAT-1: Engineering, technology, computer science, mathematics, statistics, and physics.
  • HAT-2: Management sciences and business education.
  • HAT-3: Arts, humanities, social sciences, psychology, and law.
  • HAT-4: Agriculture, veterinary sciences, biological and medical sciences, physical sciences, education, and mass communication.
  • HAT-General: Religious studies and Madrassa graduates.

Benefits

Passing the test fulfills an essential requirement for postgraduate admissions and HEC scholarship consideration. However, achieving a passing score does not automatically guarantee admission or award funding, as universities and scholarship providers maintain independent selection standards. Specific coverage details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Interested candidates can complete their application online through the ETC portal by taking the following steps:

  • Create or sign in to an account on the official HEC ETC portal.
  • Provide academic and personal details, selecting the correct test category and examination center.
  • Generate a consumer number and pay the test fee using the 1Link/1Bill invoice service.
  • Confirm payment using the online verification option on the dashboard, as incomplete applications will not be processed.
  • Download the roll number slip and present a printed copy alongside an original CNIC or passport at the designated test site.

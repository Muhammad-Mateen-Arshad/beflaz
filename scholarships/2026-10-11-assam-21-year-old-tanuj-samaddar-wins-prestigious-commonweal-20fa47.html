Assam: 21-Year-Old Tanuj Samaddar Wins Prestigious Commonwealth Scholarship

Published on October 11, 2026

The Commonwealth Scholarship is recognized globally as an elite educational funding initiative, featuring a competitive selection rate of just 1% to 2%. Financed by the UK Government through the Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO) and administered by the Commonwealth Scholarship Commission (CSC), the scheme supports outstanding students who demonstrate strong academic and leadership capabilities.

Scholarship Overview

The program offers fully funded support for postgraduate studies in the United Kingdom. In the current cohort, 21-year-old Tanuj Samaddar from Station Road, Rangia, earned the award to study an MSc in Public Policy at University College London (UCL), an institution ranked 8th in the world by QS. He is among the youngest awardees in his class, the only recipient from Assam this year, and the first Commonwealth Masters Scholar from the state to undertake a postgraduate program at UCL. Prior to securing this award, he was named a finalist for the Erasmus Mundus Scholarship and was longlisted for the Rhodes Scholarship.

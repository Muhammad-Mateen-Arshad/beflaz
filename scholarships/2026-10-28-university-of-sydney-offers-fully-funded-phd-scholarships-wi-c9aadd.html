University of Sydney Offers Fully-Funded PhD Scholarships With AUD $44,293 Stipend

Published on October 28, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The University of Sydney in Australia is offering fully-funded PhD scholarship opportunities for the 2027 academic year. Available for both domestic and international applicants, these positions focus on doctoral research in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning, and Data Science. Funding is available through competitive institutional scholarships or research group grants, such as those associated with Dr. Jonathan K. Kummerfeld's research team.

Benefits

Recipients selected for the Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) or the University of Sydney International Stipend Scholarship (USYDIS) receive an annual living stipend of AUD $44,293 for 2027. Both scholarship pathways provide a complete 100 percent tuition fee waiver covering up to 14 research periods. Depending on the award, benefits may also include Overseas Student Health Cover, thesis allowances, and relocation assistance. Standard funding for candidates joining Dr. Kummerfeld's research group directly is approximately AUD $41,000 per year.

Who Can Apply

The scholarships are open to international applicants, including students from Pakistan, as well as domestic candidates who meet the following admission criteria:

  • Candidates must possess a bachelor's degree with significant study in Computer Science, first-class honours, a research Master's degree, or a coursework Master's degree with a substantial research component.
  • Applicants must demonstrate prior research experience through an undergraduate thesis, honours project, pre-doctoral work, or relevant industry internships. A Master's degree or published papers are not compulsory.
  • Applicants must satisfy English language requirements, such as an overall IELTS Academic score of 6.5 (with at least 6.0 in each band), PTE Academic, TOEFL iBT, or Cambridge C1/C2 tests, unless exempt through prior English-medium studies.

How to Apply

Prospective doctoral students applying to the research group must prepare academic transcripts, an updated curriculum vitae, and a Statement of Purpose between two and four pages outlining their background and research interests. Important deadlines include:

  • October 31, 2026: Preferred deadline for applying to Dr. Kummerfeld's research group.
  • December 18, 2026: Official university scholarship application deadline for Research Periods 3 and 4 of 2027.

NAPB-APBA Borlaug Scholarship 2027-2028 for PhD Students

Published on November 06, 2026

The NAPB-APBA Borlaug Scholarship 2027-2028 for PhD Students is a two-year professional development initiative for doctoral researchers studying in Africa. Jointly organised by the National Association of Plant Breeders and the African Plant Breeders Association, this grant supports scholars in Plant Breeding and closely related agricultural fields. Qualified candidates must complete their submissions before the application deadline on 11 December 2026 at 11:59 PM GMT.

Scholarship Overview

This competitive programme offers six awards to doctoral candidates enrolled at African institutions, allocating a total of US$10,000 per scholar over the 2027–2028 period. The initiative aims to connect African researchers with leading agricultural experts across Africa and the United States. Applications officially open on 9 November 2026 at 8:00 AM GMT.

Who Can Apply

Applicants must satisfy the following requirements to be considered for the award:

  • Be a national of an African country enrolled in an African-based PhD programme in Plant Breeding or a related discipline starting from 2026.
  • Maintain active student status throughout the scholarship duration and have completed most required coursework.
  • Provide proof of existing financial scholarship support for doctoral studies and demonstrate proficiency in English.
  • Be willing to attend required conferences (holding or being eligible for a US B-1/B-2 visa through 2028 is strongly encouraged).
  • Former recipients of this scholarship are ineligible to apply.

Benefits

Selected Borlaug Scholars will receive several financial and career development advantages:

  • Travel funding and waived registration fees for the APBA conference in the first year and the NAPB conference in the second year.
  • Complimentary two-year student memberships for both NAPB and APBA.
  • Professional mentorship from international plant breeding specialists.
  • Exposure to advanced research facilities and platforms to present research findings globally.

How to Apply

Applicants must assemble a single PDF document including a cover letter, a 500-word research proposal summary, official identification, academic credentials, proof of English proficiency (if available), and a supervisor support letter. Candidates must also complete specific sections outlining career goals, publications, research experience, and mentorship expectations under designated character limits. Recommendation letters must be written by professional or academic referees who are not members of the selection committee or board. Submission portal details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

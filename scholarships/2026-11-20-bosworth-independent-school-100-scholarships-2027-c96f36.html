Bosworth Independent School 100% Scholarships 2027

Published on November 20, 2026

The Bosworth Independent School 100% Scholarships 2027 provide fully funded Sixth Form education for high-achieving students across Northamptonshire. Target applicants include British or dual-national students preparing for A-Level entry in September 2027 who demonstrate exceptional academic potential. Candidates must complete their application before the official closing date of Friday 11 December 2026.

Scholarship Overview

Bosworth Independent School, located in Northampton, is offering these fully funded opportunities as it prepares for its 50th anniversary in 2027. Out of five total 100% scholarships created for the initiative, one has already been awarded, leaving four places available for prospective scholars. The school maintains a close-knit environment with fewer than 270 students, focusing on individual academic guidance, specialist teaching, and tailored pastoral care.

Who Can Apply

To qualify for these funding places, candidates must fulfill the following eligibility criteria:

  • Hold British citizenship or dual nationality.
  • Reside in Northamptonshire and demonstrate outstanding academic capability.
  • Plan to join Bosworth for A-Level studies in September 2027.
  • Achieve a minimum of 5 GCSEs at Grade 8 or above, with no subject grade below Grade 6.

Benefits

Successful candidates receive 100% full funding for their A-Level education. Alongside financial support, scholars study within a diverse, international learning environment designed to build independence, confidence, and university readiness. Further funding details or living allowances are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Eligible students must apply before the deadline on Friday 11 December 2026. The selection procedure consists of three required components:

  • Filling out and submitting a formal application form.
  • Attending a formal interview.
  • Delivering a presentation as part of the assessment.

Direct application forms and portal details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

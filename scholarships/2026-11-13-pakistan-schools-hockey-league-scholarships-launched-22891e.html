Pakistan Schools Hockey League Scholarships Launched

Published on November 13, 2026

Pakistan has introduced the nationwide Schools Hockey League Scholarships program aimed at discovering young student talent across the country. The initiative provides equal sports and financial opportunities to male and female school students to help rebuild the country's position in international hockey. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

The Pakistan Hockey Federation, in coordination with the Ministry of Inter-Provincial Coordination, inaugurated the tournament at the Naseer Bunda Hockey Stadium in Islamabad. The event opened with an exhibition fixture where Islamabad Model College for Girls, Tarlai, secured a 2-0 victory against Islamabad Model College for Girls, G-6/1-4. The league runs concurrently across Islamabad, all four provinces, Azad Jammu and Kashmir, and Gilgit-Baltistan to promote discipline, fitness, teamwork, and a pathway to national hockey teams.

Who Can Apply

The league is open to school-going boys and girls across all participating regions. Specific eligibility prerequisites or academic requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Participants can gain significant athletic and educational rewards through their performance in the league:

  • One-year scholarships awarded to players on the top three boys' and top three girls' teams.
  • Selection of 100 boys and 100 girls demonstrating exceptional talent for specialized training camps.
  • An overseas tour for the overall winning team to gain international match experience.

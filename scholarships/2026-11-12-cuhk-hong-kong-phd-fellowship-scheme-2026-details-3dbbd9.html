CUHK Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme 2026 Details

Published on November 12, 2026

The CUHK Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme 2026 Details provide a fully funded doctoral opportunity at The Chinese University of Hong Kong for prospective PhD candidates entering the 2027–28 academic year. This fellowship package offers substantial financial support for high-achieving doctoral researchers. Interested candidates must complete their initial application steps by 1 December 2026 at 12:00 noon Hong Kong Time.

Scholarship Overview

The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) has issued a final call for prospective doctoral candidates to apply for the Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPFS). Shortlisted candidates will participate in interviews, and final fellowship results will be declared between March and April 2027 for the 2027–28 intake.

Who Can Apply

The opportunity is open to prospective doctoral researchers applying for PhD studies at CUHK. Specific subject prerequisites and academic entry requirements are Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Selected HKPFS recipients receive an enhanced support package valued at over US$232,420+, which covers several key benefits:

  • Full tuition fee waivers
  • Annual stipends
  • Guaranteed on-campus lodging awards
  • Conference travel allowances

Candidates nominated by CUHK who do not secure the main HKPFS award will be admitted to CUHK's regular PhD program with an annual postgraduate studentship of up to US$29,400. They may also qualify for the CUHK Vice-Chancellor's PhD Scholarship of up to US$10,240 based on academic performance. Additionally, first-year research postgraduates from Belt and Road countries are automatically considered for the HKSAR Government's Belt & Road Scholarship, which provides full tuition coverage.

How to Apply

Applicants must follow a two-step application process before the deadlines on 1 December 2026:

  • Submit an initial application on the Research Grants Council (RGC) portal to obtain an official HKPFS reference number by 1 December 2026 at 12:00 noon Hong Kong Time (4:00 am UTC), selecting CUHK as Choice 1.
  • Submit the full doctoral application on the CUHK Graduate School postgraduate admission portal using the HKPFS reference number by 1 December 2026 at 11:59 pm Hong Kong Time (3:59 pm UTC).

Chevening-ABL Scholarship for Pakistani Students

Published on November 04, 2026

The UK government’s Chevening programme has partnered with Allied Bank Limited to introduce the Chevening-ABL Scholarship for Pakistani Students. This joint initiative offers fully funded master's degree opportunities at leading UK universities for high-achieving individuals from Pakistan. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Allied Bank Limited (ABL) is the first private organization in Pakistan to establish a locally funded Chevening partnership. The program aims to invest in Pakistan's next generation of leaders by supporting two scholars per year across three academic years, starting in the 2027-28 academic term.

Who Can Apply

The opportunity is designated for promising Pakistani candidates looking to study in the United Kingdom and apply their knowledge back home. Specific age limits, academic grade requirements, or work experience criteria are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Selected scholars receive full funding to earn a master's degree at a prominent UK academic institution. Exact funding breakdowns or stipend amounts are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Eligible Subjects

The scholarship covers degree programs vital to Pakistan's future development. Supported fields of study comprise:

  • Artificial intelligence
  • Technology
  • Cybersecurity
  • Digital transformation
  • Economics
  • Financial inclusion

How to Apply

The first pair of scholars will be chosen from the existing pool of applicants for the 2027-28 academic year. Detailed submission guidelines or portal links are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

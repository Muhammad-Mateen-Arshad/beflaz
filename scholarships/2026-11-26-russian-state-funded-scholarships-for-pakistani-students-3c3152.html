Russian State-Funded Scholarships for Pakistani Students

Published on November 26, 2026

The Russian State-Funded Scholarships for Pakistani Students initiative offers expanded educational opportunities following agreements at the Pakistan-Russia Intergovernmental Commission. This scheme is intended for Pakistani students looking to pursue higher studies and scientific research in Russia. The deadline to apply is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Russia has increased its quota of state-funded scholarships reserved for students from Pakistan. Announced during the 11th session of the Pakistan-Russia Intergovernmental Commission on Trade, Economic, Scientific and Technical Cooperation held in Moscow, this decision is part of a broader effort to strengthen educational and scientific ties. Both nations have agreed to expedite intergovernmental agreements relating to higher education, scientific and technical cooperation, and the mutual recognition of educational qualifications, while also encouraging new university partnerships.

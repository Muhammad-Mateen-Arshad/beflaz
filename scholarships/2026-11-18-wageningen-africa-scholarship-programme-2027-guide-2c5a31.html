Wageningen Africa Scholarship Programme 2027 Guide

Published on November 18, 2026

The Wageningen Africa Scholarship Programme offers fully funded master's degree opportunities at Wageningen University & Research in the Netherlands. Designed for high-achieving students with African citizenship, this funding supports two-year postgraduate programs beginning in September 2027. Applicants must secure unconditional admission and submit all required documents by 1 December 2026.

Scholarship Overview

Wageningen University & Research is globally recognized for its teaching and research in life sciences, agriculture, and environmental studies. The scholarship removes financial barriers for qualified African graduates pursuing postgraduate education in these key fields.

Benefits

This award covers all primary expenses associated with the degree, with no partial funding offered. Financial coverage includes:

  • Full tuition fees for the two-year master's program
  • A monthly living allowance
  • Return air travel between Africa and the Netherlands
  • Visa expenses
  • Health insurance

Who Can Apply

To qualify for funding consideration, applicants must fulfill the following criteria:

  • Hold citizenship of an African country.
  • Have completed a bachelor's degree with First Class Honours or an equivalent qualification.
  • Maintain a minimum grade point average (GPA) of 80%.
  • Meet the university's English language proficiency standards.
  • Have already finished undergraduate studies and gained unconditional admission to an eligible master's program prior to the closing date.

Preference is given to candidates who have not previously completed a master's degree.

How to Apply

Candidates do not need to fill out a separate scholarship application form. To apply, prospective students must submit an application for an eligible master's degree via the Wageningen University application portal along with all required documents before 1 December 2026.

The university reviews applications and identifies candidates for scholarship consideration. Shortlisted applicants will receive updates between March and April 2027, and final scholarship decisions will be issued on 7 April 2027.

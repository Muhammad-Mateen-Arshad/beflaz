Dhahan Prize for Punjabi Literature: Details and Benefits

Published on November 22, 2026

The Dhahan Prize for Punjabi Literature is an international award designed to support and celebrate Punjabi fiction writers worldwide. It is open to authors writing in both Shahmukhi and Gurmukhi scripts, aiming to bridge cultural divides and take Punjabi literature to global audiences. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Founded in 2013 by Canadian philanthropist Barj S Dhahan alongside his wife Rita, family, and friends, the award honours outstanding works of Punjabi fiction. Based in Vancouver, Canada, the program promotes literary heritage while encouraging new creative writing across India, Pakistan, and the global diaspora. Additionally, since 2024, the initiative has provided support for transliterating winning books between the Gurmukhi and Shahmukhi scripts.

Who Can Apply

The prize is open to Punjabi fiction writers worldwide, regardless of whether they reside in South Asia or among diaspora communities in countries such as Canada, the UK, Australia, or New Zealand. Eligible submissions must be written in either the Shahmukhi or Gurmukhi script. Specific submission rules or eligibility dates are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

  • The first-place winner receives CAD 25,000.
  • Two shortlisted finalists receive CAD 10,000 each.
  • Winning titles receive assistance with script transliteration to reach broader reading audiences across both scripts.

Dahdaleh Global Health Graduate Scholarship 2027–2028: Up to CA$30,000 for International Students

Published on October 26, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Dahdaleh Institute for Global Health Research at York University in Canada is offering the Dahdaleh Global Health Graduate Scholarship for the 2027–2028 academic period. This award supports both domestic and international graduate researchers whose projects tackle key global health, humanitarian, and environmental challenges. To qualify, research work must align with at least one of the Institute's three main focus areas: planetary health, global health and humanitarianism, or global health foresighting.

Who Can Apply

The scholarship is available to both incoming and continuing graduate students. Applicants must meet the following criteria:

  • Be enrolled in or entering a full-time, research-based graduate program at York University.
  • Be a domestic or international student.
  • Demonstrate outstanding academic achievement and strong research potential.
  • Propose scholarly work that directly connects to the Institute's core research priorities.
  • Show evidence of leadership, initiative, and innovative thinking.

Specific departmental course prerequisites are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Recipients of the scholarship receive several academic and financial advantages, including:

  • Annual awards ranging from CA$5,000 to CA$30,000, based on funding availability, merit, and financial need.
  • Supplementary support provided alongside standard graduate funding packages.
  • Potential renewal for doctoral students for up to four years, subject to program conditions.
  • Formal designation as a Dahdaleh Graduate Global Health Scholar.
  • Access to the Institute's shared open workspace.
  • Involvement in weekly global health graduate seminars and opportunities to present research at least once per year.
  • Potential participation in specialized workshops and events organized by the Institute.

How to Apply

Candidates must complete the official scholarship application form and submit all required supporting materials before the deadline on 8 January 2027. The scholarship committee evaluates applicants based on academic excellence, research potential, proposal quality, and leadership capacity. International candidates should also review York University's graduate admission standards and relevant study permit requirements.

UK University Offering 100% Scholarship is Closing This Month

Published on October 30, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Vice-Chancellor’s Award at University College Birmingham (UCB) in the United Kingdom offers financial support to exceptional international students. This award is available for individuals entering full-time undergraduate and postgraduate degree programs for the February 2027 academic intake.

Benefits

Selected recipients receive a 100% tuition fee waiver covering their entire first year of study. However, this funding does not extend to subsequent years, meaning students must arrange their own financial resources to complete the remainder of their degree. Living expenses and accommodation coverage are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Who Can Apply

The scholarship is available to international students who hold overseas fee status. Candidates qualifying for home-fee status are ineligible. Eligible countries include Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Nepal, Sri Lanka, and China, among others. Qualification requirements include:

  • Enrolling in a full-time undergraduate or postgraduate degree (part-time and distance-learning programs are excluded).
  • Undergraduate candidates must hold at least 144 UCAS tariff points, a minimum academic score of 70%, AAA grades, or an equivalent international qualification.
  • Postgraduate candidates must possess a First Class Honours degree or an equivalent qualification from their home country.
  • Meeting English-language proficiency requirements, such as an IELTS score of 6.0 or a TOEFL iBT score of 72.
  • Demonstrating academic excellence, strong leadership potential, and a commitment to making a positive societal contribution.

How to Apply

To apply for the Vice-Chancellor’s Award, candidates must follow these steps:

  • Apply for an eligible full-time program at University College Birmingham and accept the offer of admission for the February 2027 intake.
  • Prepare a personal statement of approximately 500 words outlining academic and personal achievements, career ambitions, leadership potential, and contributions to society.
  • Complete the online application process before the official deadline on October 31, 2026.

Successful candidates are expected to be announced by November 30, 2026. Scholarship recipients must also agree to participate in designated university promotional activities, such as providing testimonials, contributing to case studies, or supporting marketing efforts if requested.

Rise Empowerment Foundation Scholarship Overview

Published on November 20, 2026

The Rise Empowerment Foundation Scholarship provides financial aid to students pursuing higher education in Nigeria. This program supports university undergraduates throughout their academic programmes, although the application deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

The Rise Empowerment Foundation provides financial assistance to help students complete their university degree programs. For instance, Adeleke Esther received funding from the foundation while pursuing a First Class Honours degree in Mathematics at the University of Ibadan. The source also notes other financial awards available to university students in Nigeria, including the FBS and MTN scholarships received by Romoke Lasisi during her studies at the University of Ilorin.

Who Can Apply

Specific eligibility criteria, grade requirements, and target fields of study are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. The source confirms that undergraduate students attending Nigerian tertiary institutions have qualified for funding.

Benefits

Recipients supported by The Rise Empowerment Foundation receive the following provisions:

  • A fully funded scholarship covering university education.
  • A monthly stipend delivered throughout the duration of studies.

Simons Foundation and Flatiron Institute Careers Overview

Published on November 15, 2026

The Simons Foundation and Flatiron Institute careers offer diverse research, technical, and administrative positions for individuals dedicated to advancing mathematics and basic science research. These openings cater to established scholars, postdoctoral fellows, software engineers, and administrative staff seeking to work in an international research setting. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

The Simons Foundation is a privately funded organization that advances scientific discovery across mathematics, physics, biology, and neuroscience. Based in New York City, its internal research division—the Flatiron Institute—focuses on solving complex scientific problems through computational methods such as machine learning, mathematical modeling, simulation, and data analysis. Its specialized centers include the Center for Computational Quantum Physics.

Featured vacancies listed on their official portal include roles such as Associate Research Scientist in Computational Catalysis, Director or Senior Director of Research Software Engineering, and Flatiron Research Fellowships in Computational Biology and Computational Neuroscience.

Who Can Apply

Because academic and experience requirements vary by role, applicants should check individual job postings. General eligible groups include:

  • Postdoctoral researchers and candidates who have completed or are completing a PhD.
  • Computational biologists, neuroscientists, and physicists.
  • Software engineers, data scientists, and research programmers.
  • Science writers, editors, and communications professionals.
  • Research administrators, program managers, and operational specialists.

Benefits

Working with these organizations offers several key advantages:

  • Participation in advanced scientific projects spanning computational biology, physics, and neuroscience.
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration among scientists, mathematicians, and technical specialists.
  • Access to continuous professional development, including an online learning platform with thousands of courses.

Funding amounts and salary details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Interested candidates should review available openings and submit their applications directly through the official careers portal of the Simons Foundation and Flatiron Institute. Because job listings and requirements change over time, applicants are advised to confirm specific instructions on the official website.

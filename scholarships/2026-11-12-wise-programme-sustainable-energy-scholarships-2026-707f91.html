WISE Programme Sustainable Energy Scholarships 2026

Published on November 12, 2026

The WISE Programme Sustainable Energy Scholarships 2026 offer fully funded master's degree opportunities in Sustainable Energy Engineering to Tanzanian women for the 2026/27 academic year. The initiative addresses gender under-representation in technical fields while building local capacity for the country's expanding renewable energy sector. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

The Women in Sustainable Energy (WISE) programme is implemented through a partnership involving Tanzania's Ministry of Energy, the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), the Government of Ireland, and the Dar es Salaam Institute of Technology (DIT). First established in 2023, the initiative has supported 60 Tanzanian women to date, including the latest group of 15 recipients recognized in November 2026.

The awards align with Tanzania's broader economic and environmental strategies, including the National Energy Efficiency Strategy 2024–2034 and Development Vision 2050. By combining academic instruction with practical training, the initiative aims to build a skilled local workforce capable of accelerating the transition to a green economy.

Who Can Apply

The program targets Tanzanian female professionals and students pursuing postgraduate studies in Sustainable Energy Engineering at DIT. Specific academic prerequisites, age limits, or prior experience requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Recipients of the scholarship receive support covering both financial and professional development needs, including:

  • Full tuition fee coverage
  • Living allowances
  • Health insurance
  • Research expenses
  • Professional study visits
  • Practical training and mentorship
  • Networking opportunities within the energy sector

Honhaar Scholarship and Laptop Scheme: Eligibility Guide

Published on November 28, 2026

The Honhaar Scholarship and Laptop Scheme is an initiative by the Punjab government designed to recognize and support talented students on the basis of merit. The initiative aims to ensure that financial constraints do not hinder deserving individuals from pursuing their educational goals. Specific application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

The scheme reflects the view that providing access to education is a fundamental state responsibility. In addition to laptop awards and merit scholarships, the government is expanding educational infrastructure by creating new classrooms and artificial intelligence laboratories across higher education institutions to emphasize specialized training.

Who Can Apply

Opportunities under this program are intended for deserving students from across the country. Eligible regions include:

  • Punjab
  • Sindh
  • Balochistan
  • Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
  • Gilgit-Baltistan
  • Azad Jammu and Kashmir

A designated share of laptops under the project is specifically set aside for students belonging to Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Benefits

Students eligible for the program and related state initiatives receive the following benefits:

  • Financial scholarship awards to assist with educational costs.
  • Laptops provided in recognition of academic achievements.
  • Free travel options on the Green Bus service for students, senior citizens, and women.
  • Scooters designated for female students, with distribution commencing in December.

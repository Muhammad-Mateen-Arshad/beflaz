African Leadership College of Higher Education Announces Fully Funded Mastercard Foundation Scholars Programme for African Students 2027

Published on October 31, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The African Leadership College of Higher Education (ALCHE), located in Mauritius, partners with the Mastercard Foundation to offer the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program. This fully funded initiative supports high-achieving African youth from socioeconomically disadvantaged backgrounds. The award allows promising students to access post-secondary education without financial barriers, equipping them with the leadership skills needed to drive positive change in their communities. Application deadlines and specific award values are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Who Can Apply

To qualify for this scholarship opportunity, candidates must meet the following eligibility conditions:

  • Be an African student who satisfies ALCHE’s baseline admission requirements.
  • Demonstrate significant financial need backed by acceptable official documentation of socioeconomic disadvantage.
  • Show evidence of strong academic performance.
  • Display leadership potential and a clear desire to contribute to social and economic progress across Africa.
  • Fulfill all assessment, documentation, and eligibility criteria established by ALCHE and the Mastercard Foundation.

Benefits

Selected scholars receive comprehensive financial aid to support their studies at ALCHE, including:

  • Full tuition coverage throughout their study program at ALCHE.
  • Additional financial support as detailed in the official scholarship arrangements.

How to Apply

The application procedure follows a structured multi-step selection process:

  • First, submit an application for admission to ALCHE, as admission eligibility is required before moving forward.
  • Complete a preliminary eligibility assessment to confirm suitability for the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program.
  • Fill out the comprehensive scholarship application form, providing personal background information, details on past school financing, essays, video submissions, referral letters, and official financial records.
  • Take the ALCHE Fit Challenge, an online assessment lasting roughly 25 minutes to evaluate learning approach and fit for the institution's model.
  • Await final evaluation by the scholarship review committee.

Toronto Metropolitan University $25M Engineering Scholarship

Published on November 16, 2026

The Toronto Metropolitan University $25M Engineering Scholarship initiative is a major financial aid fund established for students within the Faculty of Engineering and Architectural Science. This funding supports both undergraduate and graduate learners recognized for academic performance. Specific application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Toronto Metropolitan University announced a $25 million investment to create its largest scholarship fund to date. Dedicated to the Faculty of Engineering and Architectural Science, the gift was created to honour outgoing President Mohamed Lachemi for his career and leadership at the institution.

Who Can Apply

The fund supports students pursuing studies in the Faculty of Engineering and Architectural Science, including:

  • New undergraduate students receiving entrance awards.
  • Upper-year undergraduate students demonstrating high performance.
  • Graduate students striving for academic excellence.

Additional qualification details and academic requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

The total $25 million allocation is divided into two primary areas:

  • $10 million is designated for entrance awards and performance-based scholarships for undergraduate students.
  • $15 million is dedicated to establishing a perpetual fund focused on supporting graduate student excellence.

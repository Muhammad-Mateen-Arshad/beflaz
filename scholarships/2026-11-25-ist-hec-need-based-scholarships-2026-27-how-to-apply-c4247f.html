IST HEC Need-Based Scholarships 2026-27: How to Apply

Published on November 25, 2026

The Institute of Space Technology in Islamabad has announced the official opening of the IST HEC Need-Based Scholarships 2026-27. This financial assistance initiative is designed to support undergraduate students who require financial help to pursue their higher education. Qualified applicants must complete their submission before the final deadline of December 2, 2026.

Scholarship Overview

The Higher Education Commission need-based program at IST provides support to students facing financial difficulties. Dedicated selection committees will evaluate all submissions based on verified financial need and the available funding budget. Priority will be given to the most financially disadvantaged candidates. However, submitting an application does not automatically guarantee financial aid, an interview, or a final award.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility for this opportunity covers specific student groups at the university:

  • Newly admitted first-year Bachelor of Science (BS) students needing financial help.
  • Senior students dealing with sudden financial emergencies, including the death of a primary breadwinner or guardian, unexpected job loss, sudden retirement, or other unforeseen financial crises.

Benefits

Not specified in the source; please check the official notice. The institute has not released details regarding the exact financial value or total number of scholarships being awarded.

How to Apply

Eligible candidates can submit their applications online by following these steps:

  • Go to the official Institute of Space Technology website and navigate to the Student Login Portal.
  • Log in using valid university login credentials.
  • Fill out the scholarship profile completely with the required personal, academic, and financial background.
  • Upload all necessary supporting documentation requested by the portal system.
  • Verify all details carefully and submit the form prior to Wednesday, December 2, 2026. Incomplete submissions will be rejected immediately.

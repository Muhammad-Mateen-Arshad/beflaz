Sindh Talent Hunt Program 2027 Opens With Fully-Funded Scholarships

Published on October 12, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Sindh Talent Hunt Program (STHP) 2027 is a collaborative initiative between Sukkur IBA University and the Government of Sindh. Designed to assist talented students across the province, the program selects 200 candidates through a merit-cum-need-based process for a preparatory foundation semester focused on English, Mathematics, and ICT. Following this phase, the top 100 students who meet academic requirements are awarded fully-funded undergraduate scholarships at the university's main campus.

Who Can Apply

To be considered for the selection process, candidates must meet the following criteria:

  • Hold a valid Sindh domicile and belong to Sindh province.
  • Have completed Intermediate or an equivalent examination in 2024, 2025, or 2026 from any recognized examination board.
  • Demonstrate strong academic merit and financial need.

Minimum Intermediate marks percentage and age limit: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Financial support for selected students is provided across two distinct stages:

  • Foundation Semester (200 Students): Full tuition waiver, free books and study manuals, and total monthly support of Rs 9,500 (comprising Rs 5,500 for living costs and a Rs 4,000 stipend). Hostel facilities are not provided during this semester.
  • Undergraduate Degree (Top 100 Students): 100% admission and tuition fee waiver for four years (eight semesters) plus a monthly stipend of Rs 5,000. Candidates must earn a minimum 2.2 GPA in the foundation semester to qualify and maintain at least a 2.2 CGPA to retain benefits.

Eligible degree options include programs in Business Administration, Accounting and Finance, Economics, Computer Science, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Mathematics, Computer Systems Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Physical Education and Sports Sciences, Media and Communication, and Education (B.Ed).

How to Apply

Applications must be completed online via Sukkur IBA University’s official admission portal. Applicants need to locate the STHP 2027 form and enter their personal, educational, and domicile information as recorded on official documents.

List of required supporting documents: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Key dates for applicants:

  • Application Deadline: October 29, 2026
  • Admission Test: November 8, 2026

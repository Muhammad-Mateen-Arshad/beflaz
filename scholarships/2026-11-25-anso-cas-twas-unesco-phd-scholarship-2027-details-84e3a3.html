ANSO-CAS-TWAS/UNESCO PhD Scholarship 2027 Details

Published on November 25, 2026

The ANSO-CAS-TWAS/UNESCO PhD Scholarship 2027 Details outline a fully funded doctoral opportunity in China for outstanding graduates from developing countries. Offered jointly by ANSO, CAS, and TWAS/UNESCO, the programme supports up to 40 PhD candidates to pursue advanced scientific research for up to 4 years. Qualified applicants are encouraged to submit their applications prior to the February 2027 deadline.

Scholarship Overview

Established in 2019, the ANSO-CAS Scholarship aims to strengthen scientific research, innovation, and academic capacity across the Global South. The dedicated doctoral category provides up to 40 awards annually, with 10 awards prioritised for climate-related research. Candidates can pursue doctoral degrees in various scientific disciplines, including Medical and Health Sciences (such as Neurosciences). Participating institutions include the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) and the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS).

Benefits

Selected scholars receive comprehensive financial backing throughout their studies, including:

  • Full tuition fee waivers and university application fee coverage funded by CAS.
  • Monthly stipends for up to 48 months, subject to passing host university qualification assessments.
  • Health insurance coverage for the duration of the award.
  • International travel support for one journey from the awardee's home country to China, along with a one-time visa allowance provided by TWAS/UNESCO (applicants already living in China are ineligible for travel support).

Who Can Apply

To qualify for consideration, candidates must meet the following eligibility conditions:

  • Hold citizenship of an eligible developing country in the Global South, with preferential consideration given to female candidates and applicants from Least Developed Countries.
  • Possess a relevant Master’s degree (MSc) or equivalent qualification.
  • Be born on or after 1 January 1992.
  • Demonstrate proficiency in either English or Chinese.
  • Fulfill international student admission requirements for USTC or UCAS and commit to full-time doctoral studies without taking on external employment.
  • Not be currently enrolled in a doctoral programme in China.

How to Apply

Interested candidates must choose either USTC or UCAS as their preferred institution, review the doctoral admission guidelines, and submit their applications via the selected university's online admission system. Applying simultaneously to both USTC and UCAS is strictly prohibited and results in disqualification. For additional submission steps not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

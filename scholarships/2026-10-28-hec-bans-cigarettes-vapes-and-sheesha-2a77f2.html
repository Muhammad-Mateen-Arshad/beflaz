HEC Bans Cigarettes, Vapes and Sheesha

Published on October 28, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Higher Education Commission (HEC) in Pakistan has declared a complete ban on all smoking activities across its facilities nationwide. This restriction covers HEC offices, open spaces, and guest houses. The policy strictly prohibits traditional cigarettes, vapes, electronic cigarettes, sheesha (hookahs), heated tobacco items, and all related products. The commission has emphasized that disciplinary measures will be enforced against anyone violating these rules.

According to Professor Dr. Zia Ul Haq, SI, the Executive Director of HEC, the commission is collaborating with the leadership of higher education institutions across the country. Most of these academic institutions have already instituted similar smoking prohibitions on their campuses to promote a healthy and clean environment.

Japan New Higher Education Study Support System Guide

Published on November 26, 2026

The Japan New Higher Education Study Support System provides non-repayable grant scholarships alongside tuition and admission fee exemptions to help students fund college. Designed for undergraduate students in Japan facing heavy educational expenses, this government initiative aims to lower reliance on high-interest student debt. Specific application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Attending university in Japan carries significant financial demands, with four-year costs reaching about 2.43 million yen at national institutions and roughly 4.8 million yen at private universities. According to JASSO, 51.1% of daytime undergraduates rely on scholarships or student loans. Because loan-type options require post-graduation repayment—often taking 14.5 years—the New Higher Education Study Support System was launched in fiscal 2020 to provide financial aid through non-repayable grant scholarships and fee reductions.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility for the support system is based on household income guidelines and family circumstances:

  • Tax-Exempt Households: Students from households with annual incomes up to approximately 2.7 million yen receive full support.
  • Partial Support Criteria: Households earning up to roughly 6 million yen can receive partial support if they have three or more children or if the student studies science, engineering, or agriculture at a private university.
  • Three-Child Expansion (FY2025): Beginning in fiscal 2025, households raising three or more children can access tuition and admission fee reductions without any income caps.

Benefits

The program offers direct grant scholarships combined with tuition and admission fee exemptions:

  • Grant Allowances: Full-support grant amounts reach approximately 910,000 yen per year for private university students living away from home, and about 350,000 yen per year for national or public university students commuting from home.
  • National and Public University Reductions: Fee exemptions cap at 540,000 yen for tuition and 280,000 yen for admission fees.
  • Private University Reductions: Fee exemptions cap at 700,000 yen for tuition and 260,000 yen for admission fees.

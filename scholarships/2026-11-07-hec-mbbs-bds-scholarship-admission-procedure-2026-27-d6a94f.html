HEC MBBS BDS Scholarship Admission Procedure 2026-27

Published on November 07, 2026

The Higher Education Commission has revised the HEC MBBS BDS Scholarship Admission Procedure 2026-27 for medical and dental studies. This project is specifically designed for students from Balochistan and erstwhile FATA for the upcoming academic year. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Under the updated policy for the 2026-27 academic session, applicants are required to apply directly to their preferred medical and dental institutions instead of relying on the Higher Education Commission for admission arrangements. Medical and dental universities will now handle seat allocations, admissions, and student placements in compliance with approved Pakistan Medical and Dental Council allocations and applicable admission regulations.

The commission emphasized that winning an HEC scholarship does not grant guaranteed entry into an MBBS or BDS program. Similarly, gaining admission to a university does not automatically qualify a student for scholarship funds. Applicants must satisfy both university admission standards and the HEC merit selection process independently.

Who Can Apply

  • Applicants belonging to Balochistan and erstwhile FATA who are seeking MBBS and BDS admissions for the 2026-27 session.
  • Students who independently fulfill the admission criteria set by their chosen medical and dental universities.
  • Candidates selected through the prescribed Phase-III merit-based scholarship evaluation process.

Benefits

Successful candidates selected through the Phase-III merit-based process will receive financial assistance from the Higher Education Commission. Specific funding amounts and coverage details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

  • Visit the official admission web portals of your preferred medical and dental institutions to submit individual admission applications.
  • Participate separately in the HEC Phase-III merit-based scholarship selection procedure.
  • Official website links and application submission deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

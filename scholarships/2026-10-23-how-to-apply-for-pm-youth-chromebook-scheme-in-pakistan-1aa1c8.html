How to Apply for PM Youth Chromebook Scheme in Pakistan

Published on October 23, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Prime Minister’s Youth Program is distributing 500,000 Chromebooks to students across Pakistan to improve access to digital learning. Connected to the government’s Digital Pakistan vision, this initiative helps students utilize web-based applications, research tools, online coursework, and digital resources.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility is based on the applicant's academic performance and education board:

  • In Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and Balochistan: Top-performing Grade 9 (SSC-I) students attending public or government schools.
  • In Islamabad Capital Territory, Azad Jammu and Kashmir, and Gilgit-Baltistan: Top-performing Grade 9 (SSC-I) and Grade 10 (SSC-II) students attending public or government schools.
  • Private school students are currently excluded from eligibility.

Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Recipients receive a lightweight Chromebook laptop powered by Google’s ChromeOS operating system to complete academic tasks, assignments, and online classes. Additional funding amounts are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

The entire registration process is conducted online via the official Inter Boards Coordination Commission (IBCC) portal at pmychromebooks.ibcc.edu.pk. Applicants should follow these steps:

  • Open the official portal and select your examination board.
  • Search for your student profile using your CNIC or B-Form number, roll number, or registration number.
  • Enter your exact date of birth according to official records and click "Find My Record".
  • Verify your displayed personal, school, and examination details. If details cannot be found, check alternative identification numbers or contact your education board.
  • Upload required documents (maximum file size 300 KB each): a recent color photo (JPG or PNG format), relevant SSC-I or SSC-II mark sheet, an attested copy of your CNIC or B-Form, and an attested copy of your father's or guardian's CNIC (JPG, PNG, or PDF format).
  • Carefully double-check all information before final submission, as entries cannot be directly edited afterward.

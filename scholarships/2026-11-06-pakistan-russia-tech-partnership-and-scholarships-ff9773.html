Pakistan Russia Tech Partnership and Scholarships

Published on November 06, 2026

The Pakistan Russia tech partnership and scholarships initiative highlights expanding bilateral cooperation in technology, cybersecurity, and higher education. Designed for Pakistani IT graduates, tech professionals, and students, this program aims to foster joint ventures, digital skill building, and academic exchanges. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Pakistan and Russia have established strategic collaboration across key technology domains following an IT webinar hosted by the Pakistan Software Export Board (PSEB) and the University of World Civilizations Moscow. The initiative covers six main focus areas: artificial intelligence and cloud computing, cybersecurity, digital governance, IT services trade and investment, digital skills, and quantum computing. As part of broader bilateral steps, Russia offers educational scholarships to Pakistani students, while Pakistan works toward training one million AI professionals by 2030 and building seven new AI centres.

Who Can Apply

Target participants for these collaborative initiatives include:

  • Pakistani IT graduates, with the nation producing over 75,000 technology graduates each year.
  • Students and professionals seeking advanced digital skills, AI training, and educational opportunities.
  • Technology enterprises and industry bodies looking to engage in joint pilot projects and market expansion.

Detailed academic qualifications are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Participants, participating companies, and students receive several key advantages:

  • Co-development opportunities in cybersecurity, enterprise software, FinTech, and AI products across Pakistan, Russia, and third-party markets.
  • Access to educational pathways, including Russian scholarships offered to Pakistani students.
  • Exposure to national digital skills initiatives aiming to train one million AI specialists by 2030.
  • Government support from Pakistan's IT ministry to resolve regulatory obstacles for pilot projects.

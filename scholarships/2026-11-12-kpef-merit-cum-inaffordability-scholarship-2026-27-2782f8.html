KPEF Merit-Cum-Inaffordability Scholarship 2026-27

Published on November 12, 2026

The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Education Foundation has opened applications for the KPEF Merit-Cum-Inaffordability Scholarship 2026-27. This scheme offers partial financial support to deserving students recently enrolled in undergraduate and postgraduate programs across Pakistan. Eligible candidates must complete their online submissions by the deadline of December 31, 2026.

Scholarship Overview

The scholarship scheme supports students admitted to the first year of BS and MS degree programs. It covers various disciplines, including Emerging Sciences, Engineering Sciences, Management Sciences, General Sciences, Allied Health Sciences, and Arts and Humanities. Applications are accepted for approved public and private institutions, such as the University of Peshawar, University of Engineering and Technology Peshawar, Ghulam Ishaq Khan Institute, National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST), and Quaid-e-Azam University.

Who Can Apply

Applicants must satisfy the following criteria to qualify for funding:

  • Hold a valid domicile of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, including the newly merged districts.
  • Be newly enrolled in the first year of an approved BS or MS program (students in later academic years cannot apply).
  • Have achieved at least 70% marks or a minimum CGPA of 3.00 in their prior examination.
  • Have a combined monthly family income (parents or guardians) not exceeding Rs. 160,000.

The foundation reserves equal allocation for male and female students. Special preference is given to orphans, children of martyrs, students with physical disabilities, and those eligible for Zakat. For specialized arts institutions, merit and practical aptitude tests may be taken into account.

Benefits

The scholarship covers up to 80% of tuition fees depending on foundation policies, budget availability, and allocated funds. The scheme is not fully funded, and details regarding living stipends or additional allowances are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Candidates must submit their applications through the official Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Education Foundation online portal. Applicants need to register, fill out the required academic, personal, and financial details, and upload the necessary documents. Submissions made directly to the KPEF office or via universities will not be processed.

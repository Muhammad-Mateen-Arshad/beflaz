Commonwealth Shared Scholarships 2026 Application Guide

Published on November 10, 2026

This Commonwealth Shared Scholarships 2026 Application Guide provides key details on the fully funded postgraduate master's scheme available at participating universities across the United Kingdom. Targeted at high-achieving students from least developed and middle-income Commonwealth countries, the program funds 12-month degree courses in fields with demonstrable development impact. Applicants must complete their submissions through participating institutions prior to the December 8, 2026 deadline.

Scholarship Overview

Administered by the Commonwealth Scholarship Commission in the UK (CSC) under the Commonwealth Scholarship and Fellowship Plan, this scheme aligns with the UK government's international development goal of strengthening leadership capacity in Commonwealth nations. It funds standard 12-month Master's programs covering subjects such as gender studies, global development, diplomatic studies, education, and financial technology policy.

Who Can Apply

To qualify for consideration, candidates must meet the following criteria:

  • Be a citizen of or candidate from an eligible least developed or middle-income Commonwealth country, including fragile and conflict-affected states.
  • Lack the financial capacity to self-fund a degree in the UK.
  • Have not previously undertaken study for one year or longer in a high-income country.
  • Specific age limits or academic grade requirements: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

The scholarship is fully funded, with participating British institutions handling the living cost component for selected students. Specific allowance figures, travel provisions, or accommodation details: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Candidates must apply directly to participating UK universities that have successfully secured places for eligible courses. Selected institutions include Liverpool Hope University, London School of Economics and Political Science, SOAS, University of Bath, University of Edinburgh, University of Glasgow, University of Hull, University of Oxford, University of Southampton, University of Stirling, University of Surrey, and University of Sussex.

Each university conducts initial candidate selections before forwarding candidates to the CSC for final approval. All applications must be submitted ahead of the December 8, 2026 deadline.

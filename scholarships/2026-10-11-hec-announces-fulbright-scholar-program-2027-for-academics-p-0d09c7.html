HEC announces Fulbright Scholar Program 2027 for academics, professionals

Published on October 11, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Higher Education Commission (HEC), in collaboration with the United States Educational Foundation in Pakistan (USEFP), has announced the Fulbright Scholar Program 2027. This initiative provides Pakistani academics and professionals with the opportunity to engage in teaching and post-doctoral research activities in the United States for a duration of up to 10 months.

Who Can Apply

To be eligible for this opportunity, applicants must hold a PhD. The program accepts candidates from all academic disciplines, with the sole exception of clinical medicine. Additional eligibility details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Successful candidates will receive financial support for their tenure in the United States. The program covers:

  • Airfare
  • A monthly stipend
  • Medical insurance
  • A research allowance

Specific allowance amounts and stipend figures are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Candidates are required to submit their applications online. The closing date for submitting the online application is October 28, 2026. Direct application links and required documentation guidelines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

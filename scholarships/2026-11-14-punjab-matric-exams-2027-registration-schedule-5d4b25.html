Punjab Matric Exams 2027 Registration Schedule

Published on November 14, 2026

The Punjab Boards Committee of Chairpersons has announced the official Punjab Matric Exams 2027 Registration Schedule for students preparing for their secondary education assessments. This admission timeline is intended for candidates planning to sit for the Secondary School Certificate (SSC) Part II first annual examinations. Form submissions open on November 10, 2026, with the final deadline for submissions ending on January 11, 2027.

Overview

The online admission schedule was formally sanctioned through an official notification issued on November 10. The approved plan aligns with the proposal presented by the Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education (BISE) Lahore. Official examinations for matric students are set to commence on March 16, 2027.

Who Can Apply

This registration schedule applies to students intending to appear in the SSC Part II annual exams in 2027 across the relevant boards in Punjab. Specific eligibility requirements and prerequisite qualifications are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Fee Schedule and Deadlines

Candidates must submit their admission forms within specific timeframes to avoid extra charges:

  • Single Fee Deadline: November 10, 2026, to December 11, 2026
  • Double Fee Deadline: December 12, 2026, to December 28, 2026
  • Triple Fee Deadline: December 29, 2026, to January 11, 2027

Exact payment amounts for single, double, or triple fees are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Forms must be submitted online according to the schedule authorized by the committee. Step-by-step instructions and online portal links are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

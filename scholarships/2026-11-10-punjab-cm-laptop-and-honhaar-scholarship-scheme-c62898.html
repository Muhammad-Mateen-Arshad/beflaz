Punjab CM Laptop and Honhaar Scholarship Scheme

Published on November 10, 2026

The Punjab CM Laptop and Honhaar Scholarship Scheme is a provincial initiative aimed at advancing digital learning and offering financial aid to higher education students. Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif spearheaded the award distribution to help talented youth overcome economic barriers. The exact application deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif distributed laptops and academic grants to students in Jhelum during a formal ceremony held on Tuesday, November 10. The event took place at Government Comprehensive High School in Jhelum, where 109 students received Honhaar scholarships worth approximately Rs. 2.5 million. The program emphasizes that financial assistance and digital tools are fundamental rights for deserving students. As part of broader educational enhancements, the Punjab government also plans to create nearly 300 Nawaz Sharif Schools of Eminence featuring science, computer, and artificial intelligence laboratories across the province.

Who Can Apply

Selection for this assistance is based strictly on academic merit and individual ability rather than political recommendations or personal connections. The program targets talented students experiencing financial difficulties to ensure they can pursue their educational goals. Further eligibility requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

  • Distribution of free laptops to support modern digital learning and skill growth.
  • Financial awards through the Honhaar scholarship pool, which allocated around Rs. 2.5 million among 109 recipients.
  • Enhanced access to modern academic resources and upgraded school infrastructure.

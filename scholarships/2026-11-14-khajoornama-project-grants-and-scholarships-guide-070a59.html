Khajoornama Project Grants and Scholarships Guide

Published on November 14, 2026

The Khajoornama Project Grants and Scholarships Guide provides details regarding potential funding avenues sought to sustain a multidisciplinary research initiative on the food history of dates. Founded by chef and writer Taiyaba Ali in collaboration with Delhi brand Bruijn, the initiative focuses on documenting culinary traditions, trade routes, and social history. Specific deadline dates are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Khajoornama traces the agricultural, cultural, and historical journeys of date palms across different regions in India, including Sindh, Old Delhi, Thar, and Bengal. The initiative documents personal narratives, traditional recipes, and connections to crafts such as Ajrakh block-printing and jewelry. The project is primarily self-funded, supported in part by public immersive tasting events ticketed between Rs 800 and Rs 1,000. The project creators are actively seeking fellowships, grants, and scholarships to sustain their public archive and future research expansion.

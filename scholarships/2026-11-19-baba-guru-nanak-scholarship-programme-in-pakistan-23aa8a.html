Baba Guru Nanak Scholarship Programme in Pakistan

Published on November 19, 2026

The Baba Guru Nanak Scholarship Programme is an educational support initiative administered by Pakistan's Evacuee Trust Property Board (ETPB). It is designed to provide financial assistance to deserving Sikh and Hindu students in Pakistan to support their ongoing studies. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Launched in 2019, the Baba Guru Nanak Scholarship Programme initially offered 10 awards, providing five slots each to Hindu and Sikh students. The scheme expanded over time, supporting 110 students during the previous round. Starting next year, the program will offer a total of 200 scholarships, allocating 100 positions to Hindu students and 100 to Sikh students to enhance educational opportunities for minority communities.

Who Can Apply

  • Deserving Sikh and Hindu students residing in Pakistan.
  • Specific eligibility criteria and academic prerequisites are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Selected students receive the following support under the scheme:

  • A monthly stipend of PKR 10,000 per student.
  • Free accommodation, including basic facilities at no cost, for 12 Hindu and Sikh students at the Markaz-e-Taleem educational centre in Rawalpindi.

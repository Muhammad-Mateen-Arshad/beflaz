UBC International Scholars Program 2027: Fully Funded Undergraduate Scholarships in Canada for International Students

Published on October 11, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The University of British Columbia (UBC) International Scholars Program 2027 provides financial aid and academic opportunities for high-achieving international students entering undergraduate study in British Columbia, Canada. The program includes four award categories: the Karen McKellin International Leader of Tomorrow Award, the Donald A. Wehrung International Student Award, an award for students tackling major global issues, and an award for applicants entering the UBC Vantage College pathway.

Who Can Apply

To qualify for consideration, candidates must meet all of the following requirements:

  • Be an international student studying on a Canadian study permit.
  • Transition directly to UBC from an accredited secondary school (graduating no earlier than June 2026 for the 2027 Winter Session).
  • Be applying for their first undergraduate degree.
  • Demonstrate superior academic achievement, generally represented by straight A grades or an equivalent standard.
  • Prove significant financial need that prevents them and their families from covering degree expenses.
  • Meet general, degree-specific, and English language admission requirements.

Certain degree programs are ineligible, including Nursing, Dental Science in Dental Hygiene, Medical Laboratory Science, Bachelor plus Master of Management, dual degrees, and designated joint-degree programs.

Benefits

Selected scholars receive financial support based on their assessed need, along with dedicated academic advising, leadership development, and developmental workshops. Exact financial award amounts are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Applicants should complete the application process using the following steps:

  • Select a first-choice degree program and campus, as consideration is tied to this primary choice.
  • Designate one school nominator and one separate teacher referee.
  • Submit the UBC undergraduate admission application first.
  • Open an account on the scholarship portal, request references, enter financial details, list extracurricular activities, and write four short essays (150–250 words each).
  • Submit the complete scholarship application by 15 November 2026.
  • Provide all required supporting admission documents by 31 January 2027.

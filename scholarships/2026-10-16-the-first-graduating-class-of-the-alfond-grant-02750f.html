Scholarship Overview

The Alfond Grant, originally established as the Harold Alfond College Challenge and now known as My Alfond Grants, was created to help young residents prepare financially for higher education. The grant concept was developed by Harold Alfond, a self-made Maine businessman who valued education despite not holding a college degree. Following his death in 2007, the initiative began awarding grants in 2008 to an initial group of 650 children. Today, the program is administered through a partnership between the Alfond Scholarship Foundation, the Harold Alfond Foundation, and the Finance Authority of Maine (FAME).

Who Can Apply

The initiative provides early educational financial backing for children in Maine:

  • Target Group: Designed for every child born in the state of Maine to assist them in saving for college.
  • Other Qualifications: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

The funding helps families build long-term educational savings:

  • Grant Value: Provides a $500 scholarship dedicated to college savings.
  • Account Integration: The grant can be combined with a NextGen 529 savings account in Maine, allowing additional personal contributions to grow over time with potential tax deductions.
  • Matching Incentives: Families may qualify for matching grants provided by FAME.

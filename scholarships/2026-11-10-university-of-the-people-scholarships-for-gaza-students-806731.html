University of the People Scholarships for Gaza Students

Published on November 10, 2026

The University of the People Scholarships for Gaza Students provide tuition-free online degree opportunities to individuals living in Gaza amidst ongoing war and displacement. This initiative aims to assist students who have been deprived of face-to-face post-secondary education by delivering fully funded higher education programs accredited in the United States. The application deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Higher education in Gaza has been severely disrupted following years of conflict. To help bridge this educational gap, a partnership was formed between Dr. David Hasan, founder of the Gaza Children Village network, and Shai Reshef, founder of the University of the People. The University of the People is a U.S.-accredited nonprofit online institution serving students worldwide. While typical enrollment involves minor administration fees, students from Gaza are granted full financial support to study entirely without cost. The program has enrolled 250 Gazan students so far, with efforts underway to raise funds to serve up to 10,000 local learners.

Who Can Apply

This educational initiative is designed for students located in Gaza who are seeking alternatives to traditional university instruction. Detailed eligibility criteria, including prior educational requirements or age limits, are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

  • 100% free higher education with all standard program fees fully covered for Gazan students.
  • Access to accredited online degrees from an institution recognized in the United States.
  • Options to study online degree paths, including business administration, computer science, and engineering.
  • Degree pathways intended to open doors for remote work with international companies or local entrepreneurship upon graduation.

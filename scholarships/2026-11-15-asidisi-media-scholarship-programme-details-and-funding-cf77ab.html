Asidisi Media Scholarship Programme: Details and Funding

Published on November 15, 2026

The Asidisi Media Scholarship Programme is a government-funded initiative designed to enhance the technical skills, professional knowledge, and ethical standards of working journalists. Organised by the Ministry of Health and Mass Media, this scheme provides financial support for media personnel pursuing recognized courses ranging from short certificates to doctoral degrees. Specific application deadlines were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Inaugurated in 2006, the programme has granted 1,233 awards over its 20-year history. A total of 161 journalists received support in the latest cycle, marking the largest single-year award group since the scheme began. A key update to the initiative is the inclusion of funding for doctoral degree studies for the first time.

Who Can Apply

The scheme is aimed at journalists who wish to complete media-related courses at recognized universities or accredited higher education and training institutions. Candidates are evaluated and chosen based on their technical qualifications and performance in selection interviews. Eligible media professionals may receive funding under this initiative up to two separate times.

Benefits

The programme offers maximum financial assistance tiers depending on the level of study:

  • Doctoral Degrees (PhD): Up to Rs. 700,000.
  • Master of Philosophy (MPhil), Master’s, and General Degrees: Up to Rs. 350,000.
  • Postgraduate Diplomas, Diplomas, and Certificate Courses: Up to Rs. 200,000 for both short-term and long-term options.

How to Apply

Course fees are covered entirely by the government at no cost to the recipients and are paid out in three instalments. An initial payment of 50% is released when the program starts, followed by a 25% second instalment, and the final 25% upon course completion and certificate submission.

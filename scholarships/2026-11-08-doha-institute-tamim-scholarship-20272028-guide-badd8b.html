Doha Institute Tamim Scholarship 2027–2028: Guide

Published on November 08, 2026

The Doha Institute Tamim Scholarship 2027–2028 provides financial assistance to high-achieving students seeking to enter master's degree programmes in Qatar. This award is open to Qatari citizens, local residents, and international applicants who secure admission. The application deadline for this academic cycle is 15 January 2027, following the application opening on 1 September 2026.

Scholarship Overview

Offered by the Doha Institute for Graduate Studies, the Tamim Scholarship is designed to attract outstanding postgraduate scholars. It supports students across various academic master's degree fields, with funding tiers allocated based on applicant categories and financial standing.

Who Can Apply

To be eligible for consideration, applicants must meet the following criteria:

  • Gain admission to an eligible master's programme at the Doha Institute for Graduate Studies.
  • Demonstrate outstanding academic merit.
  • Be an international student, a Qatari national without government or employer sponsorship, or a resident of Qatar with proven financial need.

Benefits

Financial support differs depending on the recipient's residency status:

  • International Students: Receive a full award package covering full tuition fees, university housing, a monthly stipend, and health insurance.
  • Qatari Students: Awarded to qualifying unsponsored Qatari students (specific support details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice).
  • Resident Students in Qatar: Provided with partial or full tuition coverage determined by demonstrated financial need.

How to Apply

Interested candidates must apply for admission to their chosen master's programme through the Doha Institute's official admissions process. Applications for the 2027–2028 cycle opened on 1 September 2026 and must be completed before the final closing date of 15 January 2027. Applicants are advised to prepare their application materials early and thoroughly review all programme-specific entry criteria and scholarship guidelines prior to submission.

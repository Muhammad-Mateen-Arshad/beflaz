Scholarship Overview

The Kebbi State Government, under the administration of Governor Nasir Idris, offers financial support and scholarship programs for students as part of its educational development strategy. This initiative aims to assist students with their academic expenses alongside broader state investments in education, teacher employment, and social welfare programs.

Who Can Apply

The support is targeted at students studying within Nigeria as well as those pursuing education outside the country. Further specific eligibility criteria and academic requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

The initiative provides educational funding to help students cover their academic obligations. Key benefits mentioned include:

  • Scholarship disbursements.
  • Financial assistance for student registration fees.
  • Coverage support for school fees for institutions inside and outside Nigeria.

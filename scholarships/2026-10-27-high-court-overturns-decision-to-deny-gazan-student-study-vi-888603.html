High Court overturns decision to deny Gazan student study visa

Published on October 27, 2026

Scholarship Overview

A fully funded educational opportunity at University College Cork was highlighted following a High Court ruling. The court overturned a decision by the Minister for Justice to refuse a study visa to a Gazan student, Shaymaa Abudalu, who was awarded a place in an undergraduate engineering programme. The court identified significant errors in the processing of her documents and ordered that her visa application be reconsidered within ten days.

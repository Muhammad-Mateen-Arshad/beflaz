Victoria Secures Future of Nursing and Midwifery Workforce

Published on October 26, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Australian Nursing and Midwifery Federation (ANMF) Victoria Branch has highlighted important new support for healthcare workers following advocacy efforts that began with its Use Us or Lose Us campaign. Among these measures is a $1.7 million commitment dedicated to delivering 200 scholarships for nurses to complete prescribing education. This shift toward nurse prescribing is considered a major development for the profession and aims to directly elevate the quality of patient care.

Alongside the scholarship funding, a commitment has been made for 500 fully funded graduate nursing and midwifery positions in 2027. Delivered via staggered intakes across the calendar year, these positions are designed to expand employment opportunities for new graduates and strengthen workforce supply across regional, rural, and metropolitan health facilities in Victoria.

Who Can Apply

The funding initiatives target qualified nurses and early-career healthcare professionals in Victoria:

  • Nurses who intend to undertake specialized prescribing education.
  • Final-year nursing and midwifery students seeking entry into graduate program positions.

Specific academic criteria, entry prerequisites, and further eligibility standards are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

The announced workforce and educational investments provide several key advantages:

  • A dedicated $1.7 million investment covering 200 scholarships for nurse prescribing studies.
  • Creation of 500 fully funded graduate positions across 2027 to boost employment outcomes and workforce retention.
  • Support for healthcare delivery across metropolitan, regional, and rural health settings throughout Victoria.

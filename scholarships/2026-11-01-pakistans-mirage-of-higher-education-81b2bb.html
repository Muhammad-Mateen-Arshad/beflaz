PAKISTAN’S MIRAGE OF HIGHER EDUCATION

Published on November 01, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Higher Education Commission (HEC) in Pakistan was formed on September 11, 2002, after the passage of the Higher Education Commission Ordinance, which dissolved the former University Grants Commission. The authority was created to restructure national higher education and advance a knowledge economy. Following its inception, funding for higher education rose significantly by 5,000 percent over five years, leading to the creation of new institutions, domestic research growth, and foreign scholarship opportunities.

Benefits

The higher education funding reforms highlighted several major scholarship and academic support initiatives:

  • A $150 million Fulbright scholarship agreement negotiated with the United States.
  • 5,000 foreign university scholarships focused on PhD-level studies.
  • 3,000 indigenous scholarships for academic pursuits.
  • Enhanced salary structures under a tenure track system designed to recruit and retain academic researchers.

Lahore Schools Mandatory Face Mask Rule for Students

Published on November 13, 2026

The District Education Authority has introduced the Lahore Schools Mandatory Face Mask Rule for Students to protect children from smog. This health directive applies to all students enrolled in public and private institutions across the district. A deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

The primary aim of the directive is student health protection against severe smog. Recommended actions and benefits include:

  • Mandatory wearing of face masks by all students.
  • Strict enforcement of smog-related standard operating procedures across school grounds.
  • Instructions for school management to encourage students to drink plenty of water.
  • Conducting educational seminars on smog prevention and safety measures.

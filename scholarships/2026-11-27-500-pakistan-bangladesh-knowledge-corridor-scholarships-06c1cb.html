500 Pakistan Bangladesh Knowledge Corridor Scholarships

Published on November 27, 2026

The 500 Pakistan Bangladesh Knowledge Corridor Scholarships scheme provides fully funded higher education opportunities at Pakistani universities for Bangladeshi students. Launched last year, this initiative aims to strengthen academic links, mutual respect, and educational cooperation between both nations. The official deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

During a bilateral meeting held on November 26, 2026, with Bangladesh Parliament Speaker Hafiz Uddin Ahmad Bir Bikram, President Asif Ali Zardari highlighted the ongoing progress of the Pakistan-Bangladesh Knowledge Corridor. Established to enhance educational cooperation rooted in shared history, common faith, and cultural ties, the framework enables Pakistani universities to offer 500 fully funded scholarships to Bangladeshi students. To date, 170 students have enrolled, and another 20 are expected to join shortly. Beyond academic degree programs, educational ties include an Executive Leadership Training Programme for senior Bangladeshi civil servants at the National School of Public Policy in Lahore, as well as offers to train parliamentary officials at the Pakistan Institute for Parliamentary Services.

Who Can Apply

Based on the available information, the educational initiative is designated for:

  • Bangladeshi students seeking higher education in Pakistani universities.
  • Senior Bangladeshi civil servants participating in leadership training modules.

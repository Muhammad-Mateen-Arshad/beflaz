LEH Parish America 250 Mini Grant Details 2026

Published on November 26, 2026

The LEH Parish America 250 Mini Grant initiative supports public humanities projects that highlight Louisiana's contributions to the history and development of the United States. Designed for local organizations across all 64 Louisiana parishes, this funding assists with community programming for the nation's 250th anniversary. Proposals for this grant program were accepted on a monthly cycle through October 2026.

Scholarship Overview

Launched in April 2026 by the Louisiana Endowment for the Humanities in partnership with the America 250 Louisiana State Commission and the Louisiana Office of the Lieutenant Governor, the initiative engages local audiences with statewide history. Sponsored activities include community discussions, braille flag and historical marker installations, public art, musical programs, oral histories, and educational field trips. Example projects funded include a summer heritage camp on Acadian history in Allen Parish and educational programming about Poverty Point in West Carroll Parish.

Who Can Apply

Funding was directed to applicant organizations located across Louisiana's 64 parishes. Key grant recipients included:

  • Local governments
  • Public libraries
  • K-12 schools
  • Arts-related organizations

Benefits

Successful proposals received mini-grants of up to $1,500. Across the program's main review period from May through October 2026, the organization distributed 80 individual grants totaling $105,000 to 76 recipient institutions.

How to Apply

Proposals were accepted on a monthly basis from April through October 2026. Specific portal instructions, submission forms, and future funding rounds are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

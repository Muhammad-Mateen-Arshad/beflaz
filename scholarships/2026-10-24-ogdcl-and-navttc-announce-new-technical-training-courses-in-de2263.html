OGDCL and NAVTTC Announce New Technical Training Courses in Islamabad

Published on October 24, 2026

Scholarship Overview

The Oil and Gas Development Company Limited (OGDCL), under its Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) program, has collaborated with the National Vocational and Technical Training Commission (NAVTTC) to launch the fourth batch of technical and vocational training courses. This learning opportunity will be hosted in Islamabad at the NAVTTC Centre of Excellence in Modern Technologies.

Available Courses

The upcoming training program covers nine technical and professional areas. Some of the featured subjects include:

  • Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration (HVACR)
  • Digital Marketing
  • Automotive Maintenance
  • Electrical Work
  • Industrial Machinery
  • Welding
  • Plumbing
  • Fashion Design

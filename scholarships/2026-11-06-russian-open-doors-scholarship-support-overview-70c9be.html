Russian Open Doors Scholarship Support Overview

Published on November 06, 2026

The Russian Open Doors Scholarship Support Overview highlights government funding provided by Russia’s Ministry of Science and Higher Education. This educational initiative is designed for accomplished students securing admission to Russian universities through the Open Doors International Olympiad. The application deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

As announced by the Russian Embassy in Pakistan, this government support plan assists selected scholars across their entire period of study. Financial coverage begins from the moment the candidate arrives in Russia and lasts until their program concludes.

Who Can Apply

Talented applicants who successfully gain entry to higher learning institutions in Russia by participating in the Open Doors International Olympiad may qualify. Further eligibility requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Awardees receive a comprehensive funding package aimed at easing their educational transition to Russia. Key perks include:

  • Travel funding to travel to Russia.
  • Health insurance coverage.
  • Financial aid for accommodation expenses.
  • A monthly living stipend exceeding 27,000 roubles.

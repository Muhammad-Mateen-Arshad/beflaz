Punjab CM Laptop Scheme & Honhaar Scholarship Expansion

Published on November 25, 2026

The Punjab CM Laptop Scheme & Honhaar Scholarship Expansion has significantly increased its reach to support talented and deserving students across the region. Announced by Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif, these government initiatives aim to provide high-achieving students with modern digital technology and financial aid for their studies. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

The Punjab provincial government expanded its laptop distribution target from 40,000 to 112,000 units to give more students access to modern digital education tools. Alongside this technology initiative, the Honhaar Scholarship Program continues to broaden its financial aid footprint across multiple phases. During the first phase, 30,000 students received funding before the target was raised to 50,000, followed by 20,000 recipients in the second phase and 53,488 beneficiaries in the third phase. Details were highlighted during a recent distribution ceremony at Pir Mehr Ali Shah Arid Agriculture University in Rawalpindi.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility covers several categories of students across different academic levels:

  • Students enrolled in federally chartered universities in Islamabad (where 3,562 students recently received laptops).
  • Talented students coming from financially deserving families.
  • Matriculation and intermediate board position-holders.
  • Students from other provinces and regions through allocated regional quotas.
  • Detailed grade requirements or age limits are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Selected students receive significant educational tools and financial relief:

  • Provision of 13th-generation Intel Core i7 laptops for digital learning.
  • Direct financial assistance under the Honhaar Scholarship Program (a total of Rs. 41.48 million was recently distributed to 451 students in Rawalpindi).
  • Funding support designed to ensure high achievers do not have to leave education due to tuition costs.

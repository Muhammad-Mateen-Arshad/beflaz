Sudhan Educational Conference Education Updates

Published on November 12, 2026

This report covers the recent Sudhan Educational Conference Education Updates following a commemorative event held at Kashmir House in Islamabad. The gathering honored historical figures Colonel Khan Muhammad Khan and Captain Muhammad Hussain Shaheed while addressing political unrest and school closures in Pakistan-administered Jammu and Kashmir. Application deadlines and target recipient groups were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

The event was hosted by the Sudhan Educational Conference to mark the death anniversaries of historical leaders associated with education and the 1947 Kashmir movement. While intended as a memorial, discussions centered on ongoing public agitation that has disrupted regional education. Officials noted that public unrest and blockades in the Poonch division have led to extended closures of schools, colleges, and universities. To prevent losing accreditation, authorities arranged for medical college classes to be conducted at the Combined Military Hospital.

Regional Context and Perspectives

Speakers at the event presented differing views regarding the educational and political climate in the region:

  • Prime Minister Barrister Syed Iftikhar Ali Gillani stated that educational disruption cannot be justified by political demands, citing regional financial limitations with a development budget of Rs 32 billion.
  • President Dr. Muhammad Najeeb Naqi called for restraint and meaningful negotiations to resolve issues without impacting healthcare and schools.
  • Assembly Speaker Chaudhry Tariq Farooq opposed using force and expressed willingness to engage in constitutional negotiations.
  • Former parliamentarian Sher Afzal Marwat urged the government to address the 12 key demands raised by protesters through dialogue after five months of agitation.

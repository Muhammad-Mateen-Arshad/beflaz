Colorado State University Offers Fully-Funded Scholarships With Up to $2,500 Monthly Stipend

Published on October 31, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Colorado State University in Fort Collins, Colorado, offers funded graduate research assistantships for students pursuing master’s and PhD degrees. These opportunities are provided through the Forest Sciences Graduate Program within the Department of Forest and Rangeland Stewardship. Under the direction of Dr. Holly Nesbitt, selected students participate in interdisciplinary research focusing on environmental governance, conservation, natural resource policy and planning, and social-ecological systems.

Who Can Apply

Applicants must hold at least a bachelor’s degree and meet the graduate admission standards set by Colorado State University. The positions are ideal for candidates with academic backgrounds or research interests in social sciences, public policy, environmental studies, or natural resource planning. Specific minimum GPA and English language proficiency scores are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Recipients of these graduate research assistantships receive comprehensive financial support while carrying out their studies. Key benefits include:

  • Full coverage of tuition and university fees.
  • A monthly stipend between $2,300 and $2,500 (equivalent to $27,600 to $30,000 per year if paid over 12 months).
  • Practical research experience with an expected commitment of 30 to 40 hours per week.

How to Apply

Interested individuals should start by reviewing the official recruitment listing. Applicants are encouraged to email Dr. Holly Nesbitt—the preferred contact method—to introduce their academic experience, discuss research goals, and confirm the exact application requirements. Because the original recruitment advertisement does not confirm the complete list of required application documents, candidates must verify these requirements with the recruiting faculty or graduate admissions office.

All application steps must be completed before the deadline on November 18, 2026. Selected applicants are expected to begin their studies between June 1 and August 23, 2027.

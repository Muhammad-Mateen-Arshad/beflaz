Manaaki New Zealand Government Scholarships Guide

Published on November 08, 2026

The Manaaki New Zealand Government Scholarships provide international students and professionals from eligible partner countries with opportunities to pursue higher education, vocational training, and professional development in New Zealand. Administered by Education New Zealand, this initiative aims to foster future leaders and support sustainable development in participating nations. The application deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

This government-funded initiative provides several distinct study and training options aligned with different educational backgrounds and development goals:

  • Postgraduate academic qualifications, including eligible doctoral programmes.
  • Short-term thematic training focused on technical skills and development priority sectors.
  • Vocational training focused on practical, industry-aligned skills to improve workforce performance.
  • English language instruction designed for eligible government officials.

Who Can Apply

Specific criteria differ by country and scholarship category, but general requirements include:

  • Holding citizenship in an eligible partner nation.
  • Meeting academic background requirements and relevant work experience where needed.
  • Fulfilling the academic and English language standards of the target institution.
  • Showing a commitment to supporting home country development priorities.
  • Complying with age, residency, and programme eligibility criteria.

Benefits

Specific grant amounts and funding packages are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Depending on the scholarship stream, potential benefits include research and thesis support for eligible postgraduate students, as well as reintegration assistance upon completion of studies.

How to Apply

Applicants must apply through the official Manaaki New Zealand Scholarships website. Candidates should verify individual country requirements and study pathway details directly on the official portal.

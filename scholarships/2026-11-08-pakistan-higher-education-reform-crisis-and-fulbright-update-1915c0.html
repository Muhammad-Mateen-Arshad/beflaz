Pakistan Higher Education Reform Crisis and Fulbright Update

Published on November 08, 2026

A recent investigative report details the Pakistan Higher Education Reform Crisis and Fulbright Update, highlighting severe financial and structural challenges across the nation's university system. The reform initiative aimed to establish higher education institutions as centres of excellence for millions of university students across Pakistan. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Reforms initiated around two decades ago in 2002 created the Higher Education Commission (HEC) under the HEC Ordinance, with the intent of transforming the nation into a knowledge-based economy. This plan included a landmark agreement with the United States to launch what was considered the world's largest Fulbright scholarship program. However, despite expanding to nearly 280 universities and producing millions of students, public universities are experiencing severe financial distress, operational instability, and mounting debt. For example, public institutions in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa recently required a Rs 25 billion bailout package to address severe financial strain.

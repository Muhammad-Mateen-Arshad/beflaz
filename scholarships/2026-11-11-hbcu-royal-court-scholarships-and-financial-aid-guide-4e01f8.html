HBCU Royal Court Scholarships and Financial Aid Guide

Published on November 11, 2026

HBCU royal court scholarships and financial support packages offer funding, room, board, or stipends to elected student leaders at Historically Black Colleges and Universities. These opportunities are created for campus titleholders, including Mister, Miss, and class court representatives, who represent their institutions at official events. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Royal court traditions at HBCUs date back to the late 1920s. Selected student leaders gain public visibility and represent their institutions during athletic games, coronations, and official campus ceremonies. However, student titleholders frequently incur significant out-of-pocket costs for hair, makeup, formal attire, and campaign fundraising that provided benefits may not completely cover.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility for court positions and associated institutional awards depends on the university and the specific office sought:

  • Undergraduate students enrolled at participating HBCUs, such as Alcorn State University, Miles College, Southern University at New Orleans, Howard University, Clark Atlanta University, and Fort Valley State University.
  • Students running for principal institutional titles (such as Mister or Miss) or secondary class positions (such as Miss Junior).
  • Specific eligibility details, such as minimum GPA or academic status, are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Compensation structures vary significantly depending on the institution and title rank:

  • Alcorn State University: Mister and Miss titleholders receive free room and board alongside a $2,000 stipend.
  • Southern University at New Orleans: Titleholders such as Miss SUNO receive full tuition, room, and board.
  • Clark Atlanta University: Miss Clark Atlanta University has historically received full tuition, room, and board.
  • Fort Valley State University: Mister and Miss FVSU receive $2,000 toward housing, $1,000 toward a meal plan, and a monthly stipend. Secondary class officers receive no direct financial compensation.
  • Miles College: Candidates must raise a mandatory $1,200 campaign fund prior to taking office.

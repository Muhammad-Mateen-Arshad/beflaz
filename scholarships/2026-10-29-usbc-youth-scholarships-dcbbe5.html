USBC Youth scholarships

Published on October 29, 2026

Scholarship Overview

USBC Youth membership provides access to over $9 million in college funding awarded every season. These national scholarships recognize youth bowlers based on academic accomplishments, leadership capabilities, and sports performance. Recipients may win these one-time financial awards across several distinct scholarship categories.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility criteria depend on the specific award, but candidates generally must satisfy the following conditions:

  • Be high school juniors or seniors for most awards, or high school freshmen through seniors for the Gift for Life scholarship.
  • Maintain an unweighted minimum GPA of 3.0 on a 4.0 scale (a 2.0 GPA minimum applies for the Gift for Life scholarship).
  • Provide proof of at least three years of participation in certified leagues, tournaments, or high school bowling teams.
  • Be a current USBC member in good standing.

For specific regional or nationality eligibility rules, details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Funding amounts vary according to the individual scholarship program:

  • Star of Tomorrow Awards (Alberta E. Crowe & Chuck Hall): $6,000 each for one female and one male applicant.
  • Earl Anthony Memorial Scholarship: Five $5,000 scholarships awarded based on community involvement and financial need, without considering bowling performance.
  • Annual Zeb Scholarship: A $2,500 award emphasizing strong academics and community service.
  • Youth Ambassador of the Year: $1,500 each for one male and one female, with potential for additional funding if selected to serve on the Youth Advisory Group.
  • Gift for Life Scholarships: Up to 12 scholarships of $1,000 each for students with financial need, including two slots reserved for children of police, fire, or emergency rescue workers.
  • Dexter All-American Team: $1,000 each for five male and five female team members, with an additional $500 for the Bud Clapsaddle Award winner.

How to Apply

Submissions must be completed by the deadline of December 1. Standard application requirements across programs include:

  • An essay of 500 or more words answering a designated prompt, with no more than 15% AI usage.
  • At least one photograph (preferably a yearbook-style photo).
  • Verification of three years of bowling participation.
  • Supplemental materials for specific awards, such as a resume and letters of recommendation from a coach or athletic director.

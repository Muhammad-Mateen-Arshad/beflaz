Study in Korea Online Education Fair 2026: Apply Free

Published on November 22, 2026

The Study in Korea Online Education Fair 2026 is a free virtual event organized by the National Institute for International Education for Pakistani and international students planning to study in South Korea. It offers attendees direct access to explore degree programs and university admissions across 36 participating Korean institutions. The online fair remains open until November 29, 2026.

Scholarship Overview

Announced via the Korean diplomatic office in Karachi, this initiative provides comprehensive guidance for students preparing for academic entry in 2027. Participants can attend webinars and review information on degree paths, visa requirements, Korean language options, and available funding. Specific scholarship coverage amounts are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Who Can Apply

The event is open to Pakistani students and international applicants interested in pursuing higher education in South Korea. Specific academic eligibility criteria, age limits, and prerequisite qualifications are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

  • Access guidance from 36 South Korean higher education institutions.
  • Participate in live admission webinars covering general degree programs and career-focused studies.
  • Join special visa information sessions to learn about entry procedures.
  • Interact directly with university representatives through virtual booths for individual consultations.
  • Attend completely free of charge without mandatory account creation.

How to Apply

Interested students can access the event by visiting the official Study in Korea online platform. From there, users can browse participating universities, check webinar schedules, and join live sessions. An optional pre-registration feature is also available for receiving email updates. Although attending the fair requires no participation fee, individual university application submissions may carry separate fees and procedures.

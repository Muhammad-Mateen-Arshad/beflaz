Allcargo School of Logistics MBA Scholarships Guide

Published on November 23, 2026

The Allcargo School of Logistics MBA Scholarships programme provides complete financial funding for a two-year Master of Business Administration in Logistics and Supply Chain Management at FUEL SkillTech & Entrepreneurship University in Pune. Established by Allcargo Group's philanthropic arm, the Avashya Foundation, this initiative is built for underprivileged and first-generation students seeking professional careers in the logistics sector. The application deadline is Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Allcargo Group has partnered with FUEL SkillTech & Entrepreneurship University through a Memorandum of Understanding to establish the specialized school at the university's Pune campus. Supported by an investment of INR 15 crore to advance national skill development objectives, the institute delivers industry-aligned education in transportation, warehousing, and supply chain operations. The initiative is scheduled for a formal launch on November 30.

Who Can Apply

  • Young applicants from underprivileged, rural, or underserved communities.
  • First-generation learners seeking career opportunities in the logistics industry.
  • Students who qualify for merit-cum-means financial aid.
  • Candidates originating from drought-impacted rural areas of Maharashtra, who are given special preference.

Benefits

  • Fully funded scholarships reserved for 100 selected students pursuing the two-year MBA.
  • Direct funding for campus facilities and educational infrastructure provided by Allcargo Group.
  • A curriculum combining academic studies with practical industry exposure and apprenticeship opportunities with corporate partners.
  • Training targeted at preparing graduates for employment in domestic and international job markets.

Maharashtra Overseas Scholarship for ST Students

Published on November 08, 2026

The Maharashtra Overseas Scholarship for ST Students is a state government funding initiative designed for eligible tribal candidates seeking higher education abroad. Managed by the Tribal Development Department, the scheme assists students who have secured admission to international universities for postgraduate studies. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

This state scheme supports Scheduled Tribe students from remote or economically challenged backgrounds in pursuing advanced foreign degrees, such as one-year master's degree programs. The program aims to relieve the financial stress of studying overseas, helping candidates overcome barriers when entering institutions like the University of Bristol.

Who Can Apply

  • Candidates must belong to a Scheduled Tribe (ST) category in Maharashtra.
  • Applicants must hold an admission offer from an overseas university for a higher education program.
  • Detailed academic qualifications, income limits, and age criteria are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

The scheme provides financial assistance for higher education abroad, supporting expenses related to university tuition, travel, and accommodation. Specific funding caps or monthly allowance breakdowns are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Interested candidates should research international universities and secure an admission offer. Applicants must then collect required scholarship documentation and submit their application to the state's Tribal Development Department. Applicants may need to coordinate with government offices and follow up with administrative authorities to ensure official confirmation is sent to visa officials before university enrollment deadlines expire.

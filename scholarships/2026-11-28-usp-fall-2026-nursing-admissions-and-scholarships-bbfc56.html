USP Fall 2026 Nursing Admissions and Scholarships

Published on November 28, 2026

The USP Fall 2026 Nursing Admissions and Scholarships offer prospective healthcare students an opportunity to enroll in professional nursing programs at the University of Southern Punjab in Multan. Candidates can apply for undergraduate and diploma courses paired with various financial assistance options. The final application deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

The Department of Nursing at the University of Southern Punjab has secured a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the Pakistan Nursing and Midwifery Council (PNMC) for its session. To support students, the institution offers scholarship pathways under multiple financial aid schemes, including the Nazir Ahmad Chaudhry Memorial (NACM), Honhaar, Balochistan Educational Endowment Fund (BEEF), and Punjab Educational Endowment Fund (PEEF). Seat availability for these programs remains limited.

Available Programs

Admissions are open for three specialized nursing and healthcare programs:

  • Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN): A four-year degree covering healthcare sciences, clinical practices, pharmacology, anatomy, physiology, and medical-surgical nursing.
  • Lady Health Visitor (LHV): A two-year program focusing on maternal and child healthcare, community health awareness, and preventive health services.
  • Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA): A two-year course training students in essential hygiene, patient care, and basic nursing support.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility aligns with the general criteria outlined by the PNMC:

  • BSN: Applicants need an FSc Pre-Medical or equivalent qualification with at least 50% total marks, including Biology.
  • LHV: Candidates require FSc Pre-Medical or Matric Science with a minimum of 45% marks in science subjects.
  • CNA: Eligible educational backgrounds include Matric Science, FSc Pre-Medical, Matric Arts, or FA with minimum required passing marks.

Specific age limitations and additional university criteria are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Detailed funding amounts, stipend values, and financial coverage details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Applicants must apply online by accessing the University of Southern Punjab admission portal at admission.usp.edu.pk. Candidates need to create an account, complete their personal and academic profile, select their chosen nursing program, and submit the required documentation. Before submitting, applicants should confirm processing fees, eligibility, and scholarship options directly with the university admissions office.

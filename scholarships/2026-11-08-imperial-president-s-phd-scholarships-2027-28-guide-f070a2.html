Imperial President's PhD Scholarships 2027-28 Guide

Published on November 08, 2026

The Imperial President's PhD Scholarships 2027-28 offer fully funded doctoral study opportunities at Imperial College London for 50 high-performing students worldwide. This funding scheme is open to both UK and international applicants applying for full-time or part-time PhD study. Candidates must submit their applications across three consideration deadlines on 2 November 2026, 11 January 2027, or 1 March 2027.

Scholarship Overview

Hosted by Imperial College London in the United Kingdom, the award supports outstanding doctoral candidates for up to 3.5 years. Funded studies can start between 1 August 2027 at the earliest and 1 November 2027 at the latest.

Benefits

  • Full coverage of tuition fees.
  • An annual living stipend of £27,036 for the 2027–28 academic year.
  • A £2,000 annual consumables fund for eligible research costs during the first three years.
  • Bespoke professional development opportunities from the Early Career Researcher Institute and access to postgraduate support services.

Who Can Apply

Applicants of all nationalities are eligible, provided they are applying for a new PhD programme. Candidates should hold or expect to obtain a first-class undergraduate degree, an integrated Master's, or a distinction in a standalone Master's degree. Non-traditional academic or career backgrounds showing strong evidence of research potential are also welcomed. Current registered Imperial PhD students cannot apply.

How to Apply

There is no standalone scholarship application form. Applicants must first secure agreement from a proposed supervisor at an academic department within Imperial College London before applying through the online admissions portal.

During the online application, select the President's PhD Scholarship option under the funding section. Candidates must provide two academic references (excluding the proposed supervisor) and upload a two-page personal statement containing their motivation on page one and a research proposal on page two.

Application Deadlines

  • 2 November 2026 (23:59 UK time) – decisions released by 31 January 2027.
  • 11 January 2027 (23:59 UK time) – decisions released by 15 April 2027.
  • 1 March 2027 (23:59 UK time) – decisions released by 31 May 2027.

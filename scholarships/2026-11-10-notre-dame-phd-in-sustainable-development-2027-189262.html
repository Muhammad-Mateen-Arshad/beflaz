Notre Dame PhD in Sustainable Development 2027

Published on November 10, 2026

The Notre Dame PhD in Sustainable Development 2027 is a fully funded doctoral opportunity offered through the Keough School of Global Affairs at the University of Notre Dame in Indiana, United States. Designed for ambitious researchers seeking to address global issues like climate change, poverty, and environmental policy, this STEM-designated programme welcomes candidates from diverse backgrounds. The application deadline for the Fall 2027 intake is 1 December 2026.

Scholarship Overview

The doctoral degree is structured around five years of study, blending compulsory coursework, faculty mentorship, and independent dissertation research. Students complete foundational courses in research methods and sustainable development theory during their first two years, select seven electives across departments, and serve as Teaching Assistants for two to five semesters. Candidates are also required to apply for an external grant or fellowship worth at least $10,000 by their fourth year.

Benefits

Admitted candidates receive comprehensive financial support throughout their studies, including:

  • A full tuition scholarship covering program tuition fees.
  • A 12-month annual stipend for living expenses.
  • A health insurance subsidy.
  • Up to $4,000 for professional development activities and conference presentations.
  • University teaching and research assistantship opportunities.

Who Can Apply

The programme accepts applicants from varied academic, cultural, and professional backgrounds. Candidates conducting research relevant to sustainable development in Africa and other regions of the Global South are encouraged to apply. Specific academic entry qualifications are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Interested candidates must submit their application for the Fall 2027 intake on or before 1 December 2026. Precise submission steps and required application documents are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

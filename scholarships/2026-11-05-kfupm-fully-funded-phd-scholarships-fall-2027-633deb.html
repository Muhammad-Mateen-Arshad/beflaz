KFUPM Fully Funded PhD Scholarships: Fall 2027

Published on November 05, 2026

The KFUPM Fully Funded PhD Scholarships: Fall 2027 offer academically strong graduates the opportunity to pursue advanced doctoral studies in Saudi Arabia. These PhD and Direct PhD research positions are designed for prospective researchers interested in telecommunications, sensing technologies, and artificial intelligence, with the application window open from 1 November to 15 December 2026.

Scholarship Overview

King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM) provides research pathways for both standard doctoral studies and direct entry into PhD programs. Candidates will conduct research under the supervision of Dr. Samir Alghadhban within the Electrical Engineering Department and the Center for Communication Systems and Sensing (CSS).

The academic focus centers on next-generation wireless infrastructure, including holographic multiple-input multiple-output systems, Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces (RIS), Integrated Sensing and Communications (ISAC/ISIC), Non-Terrestrial Networks (NTN), high-altitude platform stations (HAPS), and machine learning applications for wireless signal processing.

Who Can Apply

Applicants must demonstrate strong academic qualifications and prepare to undertake full-time doctoral research. Specific entry criteria include:

  • PhD Program: Candidates must possess a relevant postgraduate qualification with a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.50 out of 4.00.
  • Direct PhD Program: Exceptional candidates applying directly from undergraduate studies require a minimum cumulative GPA of 3.75 out of 4.00.
  • GPA Exceptions: Applicants falling below these GPA thresholds may be considered if supported by faculty justification and approved by the university.
  • Language Skills: Submission of a valid English language proficiency score from IELTS, TOEFL, or Duolingo is required during application.
  • Institutional Requirements: Candidates must satisfy all general admission criteria set by the KFUPM Graduate Office.

Benefits

Successful candidates will join fully funded doctoral research pathways that provide exposure to funded research projects and potential industry collaborations. Specific monetary allowances or stipend figures are Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Prospective doctoral candidates must complete their submissions via the university's online portal during the application period from 1 November to 15 December 2026. Applicants should confirm required minimum language scores and necessary documentation on the official KFUPM admissions website prior to applying.

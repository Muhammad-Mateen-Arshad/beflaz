Commonwealth Shared Scholarships 2027/28: UK Master's

Published on November 15, 2026

Applications are officially open for the Commonwealth Shared Scholarships 2027/28, offering fully funded Master's degree opportunities in the United Kingdom. This award is designed for talented graduates from eligible Commonwealth nations who require financial assistance to pursue higher education abroad. Candidates must complete their applications before the closing deadline on 8 December 2026 at 16:00 GMT.

Scholarship Overview

Organized through a partnership between the Commonwealth Scholarship Commission (CSC) and participating UK universities, the program supports full-time Master's studies lasting 12 months. Academic programs funded under this cycle are scheduled to begin in September or October 2027.

Benefits

Recipients of the award receive comprehensive financial coverage to support their studies, including:

  • Full tuition coverage for an approved postgraduate program.
  • Approved return airfare from the scholar's home country to the UK at the start and end of the scholarship.
  • A monthly living stipend of £1,712, which increases to £2,000 per month for students based at universities in the London metropolitan area.

Who Can Apply

To be eligible for consideration, applicants must meet the following criteria:

  • Be a citizen or recognized refugee of an eligible Commonwealth country, and reside permanently in an eligible country.
  • Hold an upper second-class (2:1) undergraduate degree, or a lower second-class degree accompanied by a relevant postgraduate qualification, by September 2027.
  • Demonstrate that UK study is not affordable without this financial support.
  • Comply with CSC restrictions regarding prior work or academic experience in high-income countries.
  • Commit to returning to their home country after the scholarship concludes.

How to Apply

Applicants must carry out a two-part application process. Candidates must submit an online funding request via the central CSC system and apply separately for admission to an eligible full-time Master's program at a participating UK university. All application steps must be completed before 8 December 2026 at 16:00 GMT.

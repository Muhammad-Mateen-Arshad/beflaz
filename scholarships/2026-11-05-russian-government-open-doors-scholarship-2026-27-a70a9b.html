Russian Government Open Doors Scholarship 2026-27

Published on November 05, 2026

The Russian Government Open Doors Scholarship 2026-27 provides financial assistance to high-achieving international candidates, including Pakistani students, pursuing degree programs in Russia. Administered through the Interobrazovanie grant program, this initiative reduces travel and living costs for eligible participants. The application deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Financial support is provided by the Russian Ministry of Science and Higher Education to reduce living expenses for international scholars. This additional grant is distinct from tuition waivers and applies to students entering Bachelor’s, Master’s, and Doctoral (PhD) tracks. Eligible fields of study encompass engineering, computer science, clinical medicine, business, physical sciences, chemistry, education, environmental sciences, and international studies.

Who Can Apply

Candidates must meet specific competition and admission criteria to qualify for funding:

  • Must be an international student, including eligible applicants from Pakistan.
  • Must participate in the International Open Doors Olympiad and achieve winner or runner-up status.
  • Must secure a study allocation under the Russian government's education quota.
  • Must commit to full-time study in Russian and accept preparatory language instruction if required.
  • Must sign a formal three-party grant agreement.

Benefits

The program provides four primary forms of financial assistance for selected recipients:

  • Stipend: A monthly payment of 27,093 Russian rubles during the 2026–27 academic year, subject to maintaining good or excellent grades.
  • Travel: Economy-class airfare or approved transit via the shortest route for arrival in Russia and return travel after completing the program.
  • Healthcare: Voluntary medical insurance coverage throughout the study period.
  • Accommodation: Financial support for living expenses based on university standards, alongside dormitory arrangements.

How to Apply

Interested students must first register on the official Open Doors Olympiad portal and pass the competition stages. Winning or placing in the competition allows the Ministry of Science and Higher Education to process candidates for quota-based university admission. Once selected, applicants must finalize admission, sign the required three-party agreement, secure a study visa, and coordinate travel details with the university and grant operator.

HEC USAT 2026 Registration: Eligibility and Deadline

Published on November 11, 2026

The HEC USAT 2026 Registration is now open for students across Pakistan who wish to gain admission into undergraduate degree programmes. Conducted by the Higher Education Commission's Education Testing Council (ETC), this entrance exam is intended for candidates who have finished their intermediate education or are awaiting final results. Interested applicants must complete their submission by the deadline of November 22, 2026.

Scholarship Overview

The Undergraduate Studies Admission Test (USAT) will be held on December 13, 2026. A minimum score of 50 out of 100 marks is required to pass. Test results remain valid for two years and can be used for university admissions as well as HEC scholarship applications. However, securing a passing score does not guarantee university admission, as individual institutions determine entry based on their own merit criteria.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility for the test includes the following criteria:

  • Students who have completed a Higher Secondary School Certificate (HSSC) or an equivalent 12-year educational qualification.
  • Students who have taken their final HSSC examinations and are waiting for their official results.

Applicants must select one of six test categories based on their previous academic stream and intended field of study:

  • USAT-A (Arts and Humanities)
  • USAT-COM (Commerce)
  • USAT-CS (Computer Science)
  • USAT-E (Pre-Engineering)
  • USAT-GS (General Science)
  • USAT-M (Pre-Medical)

Benefits

Successful candidates receive a test score valid for two years, enabling them to apply for undergraduate admissions and relevant HEC scholarship opportunities. Specific monetary values or funding details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

To register, candidates should visit the official HEC ETC portal and complete these steps:

  • Create an account or sign in to an existing profile.
  • Fill in all required personal and educational information.
  • Select the relevant academic test category and submit the application.
  • Pay the test fee through the 1Link/1Bill invoice service using the consumer number generated by the system.
  • Verify the transaction on the ETC dashboard using the "Verify Online Payment" feature.

Applications left as drafts or submitted incomplete will not be accepted.

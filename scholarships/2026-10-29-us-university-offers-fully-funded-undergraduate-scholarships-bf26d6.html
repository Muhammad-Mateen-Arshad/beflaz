US University Offers Fully-Funded Undergraduate Scholarships With $6,000 Extra Award

Published on October 29, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Barry University in the United States, in partnership with the Stamps Scholars Program, is accepting applications for the Stamps Scholars Program 2027. This merit-based scholarship provides fully funded undergraduate opportunities for high-achieving international and domestic students enrolling for the Fall 2027 intake. Financial support is granted for four years of undergraduate study, subject to meeting annual renewal criteria.

Who Can Apply

The scholarship is open to incoming first-year undergraduate applicants who meet the following requirements:

  • Students from any country, including the United States and Pakistan.
  • A high school GPA of at least 3.5 or higher.
  • Demonstrated academic excellence, active leadership through volunteer work or student organizations, and a commitment to community involvement.
  • Fulfillment of general admission standards at Barry University.
  • Proof of English language proficiency via an official Medium of Instruction (MOI) letter or acceptable test scores: IELTS (at least 6.0), TOEFL iBT (at least 61), Duolingo (at least 85), or PTE Academic (at least 44).

Benefits

Selected scholars receive full financial backing during their four-year program, which covers:

  • Full tuition fees.
  • Accommodation and meal expenses.
  • Funding for books and transportation.
  • An extra $6,000 enrichment award designated for approved educational activities, such as undergraduate research, study abroad programs, or cultural exchanges.

How to Apply

Interested students must complete a two-step application process. First, apply for incoming first-year undergraduate admission at Barry University through their online portal and create a MyBarry account, submitting official transcripts and English proficiency documentation.

Once admission is secured, candidates must submit a separate scholarship application through their MyBarry account. This stage requires an updated résumé, a recommendation letter from a counsellor, mentor, teacher, or employer, and a personal video up to three minutes long explaining leadership experiences and reasons for choosing the university.

The application period opened on September 15, 2026, and all materials must be submitted by December 15, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Standard Time.

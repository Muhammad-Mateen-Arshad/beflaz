Gates Cambridge Scholarship for Nigerians: Requirements

Published on November 24, 2026

The Gates Cambridge Scholarship for Nigerians provides full financial support for postgraduate studies at the University of Cambridge. Open to citizens of any country outside the UK, including Nigerian passport holders, this award covers eligible postgraduate programmes with application deadlines falling on either 8 December 2026 or 6 January 2027, depending on the chosen course.

Scholarship Overview

Established through a donation from the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, this programme offers approximately 70 full-cost awards each year, with around two-thirds reserved for PhD candidates. Candidates are selected based on four core values: academic excellence, reasons for course choice, leadership capacity, and a commitment to improving the lives of others. Financial need is not considered during selection.

Benefits

The scholarship is fully funded and provides the following financial coverage:

  • Full tuition fees at the University of Cambridge
  • An annual maintenance allowance of £23,152 (2026–27 rate for single students)
  • Economy return air travel at the beginning and end of the course
  • Inbound visa fees and the Immigration Health Surcharge
  • Discretionary academic development funding between £750 and £4,000
  • Child allowance of up to £12,793 for one child or £18,244 for two or more children
  • Maternity or paternity leave support for up to six months with continued stipend

Who Can Apply

Applicants must be non-UK citizens applying to study an eligible postgraduate course at Cambridge. Eligible qualifications include full-time MLitt, one-year postgraduate courses, and PhD degrees (full-time or part-time under a pilot scheme). Candidates generally need academic achievements equivalent to at least a UK 2:1 degree and must satisfy the University's standard English-language requirements. Ineligible courses include undergraduate degrees, MBA, EMBA, MFin, MASt, PGCE, and clinical medicine programmes.

How to Apply

Candidates do not use a separate portal; applications are submitted directly through the University of Cambridge Graduate Application Portal. To apply for the scholarship section, candidates must complete four short-answer prompts addressing the selection criteria. Applicants must also provide two academic references for the university application plus one dedicated Gates Cambridge reference that focuses on leadership and social impact.

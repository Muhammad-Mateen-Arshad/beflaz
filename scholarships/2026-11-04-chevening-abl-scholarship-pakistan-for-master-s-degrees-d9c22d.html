Chevening-ABL Scholarship Pakistan for Master's Degrees

Published on November 04, 2026

The Chevening-ABL Scholarship Pakistan for Master's Degrees is a joint funding initiative created by the UK Government's Chevening programme and Allied Bank Limited. Designed for talented Pakistani professionals and future leaders, this scheme provides financial support to pursue master's degree programmes at leading universities in the United Kingdom. Exact application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Allied Bank Limited is the first Pakistani organisation to set up a locally funded Chevening Scholarship partnership, linking the UK flagship international award scheme with Pakistan's private sector. Globally, Chevening maintains partnerships with over 140 entities, including private companies, NGOs, government departments, and higher education institutions. Under this agreement, two scholars will be funded each year across three academic years, starting with the 2027-28 intake.

Who Can Apply

The award is intended for high-achieving Pakistani candidates. It covers master's degrees in subjects critical to Pakistan's future national development, including:

  • Artificial intelligence
  • Technology
  • Cybersecurity
  • Digital transformation
  • Economics
  • Financial inclusion

Specific academic score requirements, age limits, and English proficiency test details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Recipients will receive fully funded master's degree education at premier British universities. The initiative aims to equip Pakistani scholars with advanced skills and international exposure, enabling them to return home and contribute to national progress. This effort is highlighted by ABL President and CEO Aizid Gill—himself a 2001 Chevening Scholar who studied MSc Business Economics at the University of Manchester—and supported by British High Commissioner Jane Marriott CMG OBE.

How to Apply

The initial two scholars for the 2027-28 academic year will be selected directly from the existing Chevening applicant pool. For the 2028-29 academic cycle, applications are expected to open in August 2027 as part of the standard Chevening application round, with successful candidates starting their studies in Britain in 2028. Step-by-step portal submission steps are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

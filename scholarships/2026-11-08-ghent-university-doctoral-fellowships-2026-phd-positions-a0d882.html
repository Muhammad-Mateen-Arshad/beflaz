Ghent University Doctoral Fellowships 2026: PhD Positions

Published on November 08, 2026

The Ghent University Doctoral Fellowships 2026 offer fully funded PhD positions in Belgium for outstanding domestic and international graduates. Designed for early-career researchers pursuing full-time doctoral studies across various academic departments, these appointments provide employment within world-class research teams. Application deadlines depend on the specific department and range from 30 September to 7 December 2026.

Scholarship Overview

Located in Ghent, Belgium, Ghent University is recruiting doctoral fellows for 100% occupancy appointments during the 2026 cycle. Research positions cover several fields, including Political Sciences, Information Technology, Engineering, Materials Science, Structural Engineering, Electromechanical Engineering, and Artificial Intelligence. Exact funding amounts and salary figures are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Who Can Apply

Applicants from both Belgian and international backgrounds are eligible to apply, subject to department-specific criteria. General entry expectations include:

  • Holding a relevant Bachelor’s and Master’s degree.
  • Demonstrating strong written and verbal communication skills.
  • Showing a strong interest in conducting original doctoral research.
  • Having the ability to work both independently and collaboratively within multidisciplinary teams.

Benefits

Successful candidates are employed as full-time doctoral researchers, allowing them to focus entirely on their scientific projects. Key program benefits include:

  • Employment in an internationally recognized research environment.
  • Structured academic supervision from expert faculty.
  • Access to advanced laboratories, university libraries, and high-performance computing facilities.
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration and professional training opportunities.
  • Participation in academic conferences, seminars, and publication projects.

How to Apply

Candidates should select a research area that aligns with their qualifications and monitor the relevant closing dates, which range from 30 September to 7 December 2026 depending on the department. Early submission is encouraged due to competitive selection. Step-by-step application instructions and online application portal details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

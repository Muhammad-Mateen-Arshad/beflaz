King's Business School PhD Studentships 2027 Guide

Published on November 20, 2026

The King's Business School PhD Studentships 2027 offer fully funded opportunities for international students, including candidates from Pakistan, to pursue full-time doctoral research in Economics and Management. Offered by King’s College London, this scholarship supports outstanding scholars throughout their doctoral studies. Candidates must submit their complete applications before the official deadline on December 1, 2026.

Scholarship Overview

This funded doctoral programme is situated within King’s Business School at King’s College London for the 2027 academic year. It is designed to assist full-time PhD researchers conducting advanced study in Management and Economics.

Benefits

  • Full payment of tuition fees for three years.
  • An annual tax-free living stipend of £23,805 for four years.
  • Dedicated funding for research training modules and academic conference participation.
  • Financial coverage for the pending submission status fee.

Who Can Apply

The scholarship is available to applicants from Pakistan and other international locations who meet the following academic criteria:

  • Hold a Master’s degree completed with Merit or higher.
  • Hold a Bachelor’s degree equivalent to at least a UK 2:1 standard.
  • Obtain an overall IELTS score of 7.0, unless qualifying for an official exemption.
  • Successfully complete an interview with a prospective supervisor prior to applying.

Required Documents

  • A detailed research proposal of approximately 1,500 words.
  • A personal statement consisting of 500 words.
  • One academic reference.
  • Official academic transcripts.
  • A Master’s dissertation or relevant writing sample.
  • An English language proficiency certificate, if required.

How to Apply

To apply, candidates must first identify up to four potential supervisors at King’s Business School and send them a curriculum vitae along with a draft research proposal. After attending a supervisor interview, candidates must complete an online application via the King’s Apply portal under Economics Research or Management Research. Applicants must ensure they input the appropriate scholarship code in the funding section before the deadline on December 1, 2026.

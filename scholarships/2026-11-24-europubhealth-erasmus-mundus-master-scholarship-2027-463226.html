Europubhealth+ Erasmus Mundus Master Scholarship 2027

Published on November 24, 2026

The Europubhealth+ Erasmus Mundus Master Scholarship 2027 provides international candidates with an opportunity to pursue a two-year European Master in Public Health. This programme is designed for graduates aiming to build careers in global health policy, epidemiology, and healthcare management across multiple European institutions. Applicants seeking Erasmus Mundus scholarship funding must submit their applications by 8 January 2027 at 1:00 pm Central European Time.

Scholarship Overview

The Europubhealth+ European Master in Public Health is a two-academic-year postgraduate degree focused on public health, global health, and health policy for the 2027–2029 intake. Delivered by an international consortium of eight European partner universities, including the Andalusian School of Public Health in Spain, the programme enables students to study across different academic environments. Candidates can apply for the Erasmus Mundus Joint Master Scholarship or apply under the self-funded track.

Who Can Apply

Application is open to international candidates who satisfy the academic and English language proficiency requirements. The selection committee evaluates candidates based on their motivation, academic performance, professional experience in health or social sectors, international exposure, personal competencies, and the relevance of their second-year specialisation. Language test scores must be submitted prior to application evaluation.

Benefits

The Erasmus Mundus Joint Master Scholarship offers financial support to chosen international postgraduate students. Specific funding amounts and allowance breakdowns are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Because scholarship availability is limited and not guaranteed to all eligible candidates, applicants are advised to explore alternative funding options early.

How to Apply

Applicants must apply online by adhering to the relevant funding deadlines:

  • Erasmus Mundus Scholarship Deadline: 8 January 2027 at 1:00 pm CET. Selection results are announced around mid-March 2027.
  • Online Interviews: Shortlisted scholarship candidates will be interviewed online on 18 February 2027 by university professors, with invitations sent roughly one week beforehand.
  • Self-Funded Application Deadline: 24 March 2027 at 1:00 pm CET, with selection results expected in mid-May 2027.

International applicants who require a student visa are strongly encouraged to submit their materials during the January application round to allow sufficient time for processing and travel logistics.

Mastercard Foundation 2027 Innovation and Technology Scholarship at Arizona State University (ASU): 50 Fully Funded Master’s Opportunities in Rwanda

Published on October 29, 2026

The Mastercard Foundation 2027 Innovation and Technology Scholarship at Arizona State University (ASU), in partnership with the Kepler College Plus Program, offers 50 fully funded opportunities for young African professionals. Selected applicants will complete online master's degrees via ASU Online while living in Kigali, Rwanda, benefiting from both digital coursework and in-person guidance.

Scholarship Overview

This initiative combines ASU's online learning environment with on-ground academic, professional, and personal support provided through Kepler College. Programs focus on fields such as technology, business, and engineering, including the MS Engineering in Sustainable Engineering. Recipients must relocate to Kigali for the full duration of their studies to participate in community building and leadership training.

Who Can Apply

Applicants must meet the following eligibility requirements:

  • Be between 18 and 33 years old on or before the application deadline.
  • Have earned, or be on track to earn, a recognized bachelor's degree by July 2027.
  • Possess a strong academic record equal to at least a 3.0 GPA on a 4.0 scale.
  • Satisfy ASU English language proficiency standards for graduate admission.
  • Demonstrate a history of leadership, service, and commitment to community impact.
  • Have no previous enrollment in or completion of a master's or graduate degree.
  • Be willing and able to relocate to Kigali, Rwanda, for the entire program duration.

Benefits

The scholarship provides full financial and academic assistance, including:

  • Full tuition, housing, healthcare, and study-related costs.
  • Travel expenses to and from Kigali, Rwanda.
  • Vouchers for required English language proficiency tests.
  • ASU application fee waivers for advanced candidates.
  • In-person student support, academic advising, career services, and leadership development.

How to Apply

Candidates must submit the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program application online or via a downloadable PDF form if internet access is limited. Candidates should not apply directly to ASU unless instructed. Shortlisted applicants will receive an interview invitation and guidance on applying to ASU. The deadline to apply is 22 November 2026 at 23:59 GMT. For specific questions not covered in the official FAQs, applicants may contact keplerasuscholars@asu.edu.

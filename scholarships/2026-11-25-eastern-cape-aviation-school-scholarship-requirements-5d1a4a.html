Eastern Cape Aviation School Scholarship Requirements

Published on November 25, 2026

The Eastern Cape Aviation School Scholarship is a specialized provincial funding initiative created for high-achieving Grade 9 pupils in South Africa's Eastern Cape province who aspire to work in aerospace science. The award supports selected students across Grades 10, 11, and 12, allowing them to complete their high school education while gaining specialized technical training. The exact application deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

This initiative operates under the Three-Stream Model curriculum framework, which integrates general academic education with vocational and occupational pathways in STEM and aerospace studies. The programme is a joint partnership between the Eastern Cape Department of Education, Port Alfred High School, and 43 Air School. Selected students attend general high school classes at Port Alfred High School while receiving weekly aviation industry training at 43 Air School every Friday.

Who Can Apply

To qualify for this programme, candidates must meet the following criteria:

  • Be a Grade 9 student residing in the Eastern Cape province preparing to progress into Grade 10.
  • Demonstrate exceptional academic ability, particularly in Mathematics, Science, and English.
  • Achieve top results in the provincial selection examination administered by the education department (only 25 learners are selected across provincial districts).

Benefits

Recipients of this competitive bursary receive comprehensive support throughout their remaining high school years:

  • A fully funded scholarship valued at R1.4 million per student.
  • Full coverage of all academic tuition fees and accommodation expenses through Grade 12.
  • Direct exposure and hands-on skills development within the aviation sector via 43 Air School.

How to Apply

Candidates are evaluated through a competitive selection test organized by the Eastern Cape Department of Education focusing on Mathematics, Science, and English.

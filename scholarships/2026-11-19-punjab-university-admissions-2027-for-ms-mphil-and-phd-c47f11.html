Punjab University Admissions 2027 for MS, MPhil and PhD

Published on November 19, 2026

The University of the Punjab has announced the Punjab University Admissions 2027 for MS, MPhil, MSc Engineering, and PhD programs for the Spring 2027 semester. These admissions are open to qualified postgraduate applicants seeking regular or self-supporting studies across multiple faculties. Eligible candidates must submit their application forms online by the deadline of November 30, 2026, at 11:59 pm.

Scholarship Overview

The university is offering advanced degree seats in both regular and self-supporting formats. Academic disciplines available for this admission cycle include:

  • Computer Science
  • Engineering
  • Business
  • Education
  • Sciences
  • Humanities

Who Can Apply

Applicants must meet specific academic criteria based on the level of the program:

  • MS, MPhil, and Equivalent Programs: Candidates must hold at least 16 years of education in a relevant field.
  • PhD Programs: Applicants must possess a completed MS, MPhil, or equivalent qualification.
  • Evaluation Requirements: Passing mandatory admission tests and interviews is required for all applicants. Minimum qualifying scores differ depending on the program.

Benefits

Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Interested students must complete their application process online using the official university portal. The steps required include:

  • Filling out the online application form on the university's admission portal.
  • Uploading all relevant academic documents.
  • Paying the non-refundable processing and examination fee of Rs. 2,050 through HBL, UBL, or mobile banking via 1Bill.

Incomplete applications and submissions received after November 30, 2026, at 11:59 pm will not be accepted.

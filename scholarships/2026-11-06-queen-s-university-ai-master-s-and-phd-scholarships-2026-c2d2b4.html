Queen's University AI Master's and PhD Scholarships 2026

Published on November 06, 2026

Queen's University in Canada is offering the Queen's University AI Master's and PhD Scholarships 2026 for graduate students entering research degrees in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and computational materials science. These funded positions are offered through the Stephen J. R. Smith Faculty of Engineering and Applied Science for full-time Master's and doctoral candidates. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

This academic opportunity allows graduate researchers to conduct advanced study at a public Canadian university. Research project topics focus on multimodal artificial intelligence, deep learning, energy-related technologies, and atomistic materials modeling. Accepted students carry out their work under faculty supervision, including Associate Professor Ali Etemad and Assistant Professor Meng Li.

Who Can Apply

Applicants must satisfy the entry standards of their chosen graduate degree department. Qualification details include:

  • AI and Machine Learning Positions: Candidates must hold a degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Mathematics, or a related subject. Requirements include a strong grasp of deep learning and machine learning, alongside Python programming experience using PyTorch or TensorFlow. Prior research experience and published works are advantageous.
  • Atomistic Materials Modeling Positions: Applicants need an academic background in physics, chemistry, engineering, computational materials science, or a related field. Skills in computational modeling, atomistic simulations, machine learning, or molecular dynamics are beneficial. Universal minimum GPA criteria are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Queen's University offers financial support to full-time research graduate students through packages that may combine research assistantships, teaching assistantships, research fellowships, and scholarships. Key financial and academic provisions include:

  • A guaranteed minimum annual funding package of CAD 25,000 for eligible PhD students during the 2026–27 academic year, usually covering four years of study.
  • Financial funding for research Master's students throughout the standard duration of their degree.
  • Access to advanced research infrastructure, faculty mentoring, collaborative interdisciplinary teams, and opportunities for scientific publishing.

How to Apply

Interested students should follow these application steps:

  • Explore open opportunities on the official Smith Engineering graduate positions page.
  • Contact potential supervisors directly (Associate Professor Ali Etemad for AI/ML PhD roles, or Assistant Professor Meng Li for materials modeling Master's or PhD roles) to confirm open positions.
  • Submit a formal application through the university's graduate portal after confirming position availability.
  • Prepare required application documents, which typically consist of academic transcripts, degree certificates, a CV, a research interest statement, English proficiency test scores, and two academic recommendation letters.

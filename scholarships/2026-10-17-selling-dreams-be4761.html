Published on October 17, 2026

Scholarship Overview

Recent reports highlight severe risks regarding fraudulent overseas scholarship and visa offers in Pakistan. Unscrupulous middlemen and unverified private educational consultants have been exploiting students seeking international academic opportunities. In a notable case, a physics gold medallist from the International Islamic University Islamabad was cheated out of Rs 9.3 million through fake promises of a scholarship to Harvard, university admission, and an American visa involving forged documentation.

The Federal Investigation Agency has registered 1,543 cases, arrested 190 suspects, and recovered Rs 615.55 million from unauthorized agents in Islamabad and Rawalpindi over four months. The source emphasizes that private consultants cannot guarantee outcomes, as foreign universities independently decide admissions and scholarships, while embassies dictate visa eligibility.

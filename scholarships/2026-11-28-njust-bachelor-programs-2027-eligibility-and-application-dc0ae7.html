NJUST Bachelor Programs 2027: Eligibility and Application

Published on November 28, 2026

Applications are open for NJUST Bachelor Programs 2027, offering four-year undergraduate degrees at Nanjing University of Science and Technology in China for international students, including Pakistani candidates. Students can select programs taught in English or Chinese across disciplines such as engineering, technology, and trade. The application deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Nanjing University of Science and Technology is accepting international applications for its September 2027 academic intake. Outstanding candidates may also apply for the university's Lanshan Scholarship through a separate application process based on merit and university criteria. Specific funding values are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Who Can Apply

Applicants must satisfy the following admission conditions set by the university:

  • Be a non-Chinese citizen with a valid passport.
  • Be between 18 and 25 years of age, in good physical and mental health, and willing to follow Chinese laws and university guidelines.
  • Hold a high school certificate or an equivalent qualification (such as FSc, FA, ICS, or A-Level).
  • Meet the language requirements for the selected program, providing proof of English proficiency for English-taught degrees.
  • Complete the mandatory China Scholastic Competency Assessment (CSCA) subjects for the chosen major: Mathematics and Physics for Mechanical Engineering or Software Engineering; Mathematics for International Economics and Trade; and Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry for Nanomaterials and Nanotechnology.

Benefits

Enrolled candidates gain access to four-year undergraduate education across major academic disciplines in China. Qualifying applicants may receive financial support through the Lanshan Scholarship. Full benefit coverage details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Interested students must complete their submission online by following these steps:

  • Register an account on the official university application portal.
  • Select one main major and one alternative major option.
  • Fill out the application form and upload all required academic documents.
  • Take the required CSCA examination subjects relevant to the selected degree.
  • Pay the non-refundable application fee of CNY 500 online after completing the submission.

Federal Scholarship Tax Credit Program: Eligibility

Published on November 27, 2026

The Federal Scholarship Tax Credit Program is a federal initiative created under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act to assist families investing in private education or supplemental academic resources. This opportunity is aimed at eligible households seeking financial assistance through certified scholarship-granting organizations in participating states. The exact application deadline for individual participants is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

Established to provide tax relief to taxpayers who invest in educational growth, this initiative allows individuals to direct contributions to certified scholarship-granting organizations. These nonprofit entities distribute funding for private school tuition, religious education, tutoring services, and academic camps. State governments must officially opt into the program for residents to participate. Currently, 31 states have opted in, including Republican-led states as well as Democratic-led states such as New York and New Mexico. In Delaware, local education leaders remain split on whether the governor should opt into the framework for 2027.

Benefits

  • Donors can claim a dollar-for-dollar federal tax credit of up to $1,700 per year for eligible contributions.
  • Funding helps pay for tutoring, educational materials, academic camps, or private school tuition.
  • Participating states gain access to substantial funding, including an estimated $40 million annually in new scholarship aid proposed for Delaware families.

Who Can Apply

  • Applicants and donors must reside in a state that has opted into the federal tax credit program.
  • Household income must be under 300% of the area median income, which equals more than $300,000 annually in Delaware.
  • Students seeking academic support, tutoring, or private education funding through certified nonprofit scholarship-granting organizations.

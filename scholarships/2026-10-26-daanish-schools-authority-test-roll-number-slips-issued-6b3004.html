Daanish Schools Authority Test Roll Number Slips Issued

Published on October 26, 2026

Applicants taking the 2026 Daanish Schools Authority examination can now retrieve their roll number slips prior to the scheduled test days. The Education Testing Council, operating under the Higher Education Commission, has published these admission documents through its official online system. Assessment sessions will take place over a three-day duration, running from Friday, October 30 to Sunday, November 1, 2026.

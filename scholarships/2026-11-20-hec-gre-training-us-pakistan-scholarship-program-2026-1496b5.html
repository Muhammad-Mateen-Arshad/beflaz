HEC GRE Training: US-Pakistan Scholarship Program 2026

Published on November 20, 2026

The Higher Education Commission has announced the HEC GRE Training: US-Pakistan Scholarship Program 2026 to support prospective doctoral candidates preparing for studies in the United States. This preparatory program is open to eligible Pakistani and Azad Jammu and Kashmir (AJK) nationals who intend to pursue PhD degrees at American institutions. Candidates must submit their applications by the deadline of December 13, 2026.

Scholarship Overview

The preparatory initiative forms part of the Talent Farming component under the US-Pakistan Knowledge Corridor initiative. It offers a 40-hour preparatory course designed to build skills and test-taking strategies for the Graduate Record Examination (GRE) General test. In-person classes are scheduled to take place across five major cities: Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, and Quetta. Class schedules and start dates are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Who Can Apply

Prospective applicants must meet the basic guidelines established for the initiative:

  • Must be a national of Pakistan or Azad Jammu and Kashmir (AJK).
  • Must intend to apply for PhD studies at universities in the United States.
  • Must possess at least 16 years of completed education, such as a four-year bachelor's degree or equivalent.
  • Must meet the age limit requirements set by the commission. Specific age limits are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Enrolled candidates receive 40 hours of focused instruction on test-taking techniques and skill development to help improve their performance on the GRE General test. Financial assistance details or scholarship coverage amounts are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Interested candidates can apply online by visiting the official HEC scholarship portal. Applicants are required to complete the online form, review all guidelines, and provide accurate academic and personal data. Further details regarding the broader initiative are accessible on the dedicated webpage provided by HEC.

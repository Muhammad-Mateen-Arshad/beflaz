Sindh CSS Scholarships for Children of Industrial Workers

Published on November 27, 2026

The Sindh CSS Scholarships for Children of Industrial Workers is a fully funded preparatory program designed to help youth from labor families join Pakistan's civil service. Introduced by the Workers' Welfare Board Sindh, the initiative provides selected candidates with specialized coaching for competitive bureaucracy examinations. The official application deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Scholarship Overview

The program was officially approved during the 37th governing body meeting of the Workers' Welfare Board Sindh, which was chaired by Labour Minister Saeed Ghani. A total of 20 candidates will be selected to participate in an intensive four-month residential preparation program at the Civil Services Academy (CSA) in Lahore. This initiative aims to open doors to competitive civil service positions that were previously difficult to access due to high coaching costs.

Who Can Apply

To qualify for this educational support, applicants must fulfill the following basic criteria:

  • Must be children of registered industrial workers based in Sindh.
  • Must pass the required screening tests administered by the Institute of Business Administration (IBA), Karachi.

Benefits

Selected applicants receive comprehensive financial backing to complete their training:

  • Each awarded student receives a scholarship worth Rs. 800,000.
  • The funding entirely covers the residential preparatory program hosted at the Civil Services Academy in Lahore.

How to Apply

Selection is managed through screening examinations conducted by IBA Karachi. Further instructions regarding direct application submission methods or exact schedules are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What happened

According to a report by Dawn, Pakistan's Foreign Office announced on Saturday that Climate Change Minister Musadik Malik has been nominated to represent the nation at a Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) event in New Delhi.

Key facts

  • Event: The SCO Civilisational Dialogue.
  • Location and Dates: New Delhi, from November 20 to November 22.
  • Focus: Representatives from SCO member states will assemble to address matters concerning cultural understanding and dialogue among civilisations.
  • Additional background details: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

The Foreign Office noted that Pakistan maintains its commitment to advancing dialogue across civilisations and building direct connections among citizens within the SCO framework, based on mutual respect.

What happened

According to Dawn, Pakistan secured a dramatic one-wicket victory over England in an ODI tri-series match at Gaddafi Stadium. After England were bowled out for 241, Pakistan recovered from a severe collapse at 154-9 to reach 243-9 in 47.1 overs, earning a place in the upcoming final on Oct 31.

Key facts

  • England's Innings: Ben Duckett hit 105 off 111 balls, supported by Sam Curran's 42, carrying England to 241 before they were dismissed in 46.4 overs. Shaheen Shah Afridi led Pakistan's attack with figures of 4-46.
  • Pakistan's Collapse: Chasing 242, Pakistan lost early wickets including Fakhar Zaman, Saim Ayub, and Babar Azam. Despite 32 runs from Saad Baig, Pakistan slumped to 154-9 in 33.5 overs.
  • Shadab's Century: Shadab Khan scored his maiden ODI century, finishing unbeaten on 113 off 96 balls, including 13 fours and five sixes. During his knock, an on-field LBW dismissal off Gus Atkinson was overturned after an UltraEdge review.
  • Record Final Partnership: Shadab and Haris Rauf shared an unbroken 89-run stand for the 10th wicket, establishing a new record for the highest final-wicket partnership in a successful ODI run chase.
  • The Finish: Haris Rauf, who scored three runs off 18 balls, struck the winning boundary straight down the ground off debutant Henry Crocombe.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the result seals Pakistan's spot in the Oct 31 tri-series final while handing England another narrow loss. Following the match, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif congratulated the team on social media platform X, commending the grit and discipline demonstrated by Shadab Khan and Haris Rauf during their match-winning partnership.

What's new

The All Indian Cine Workers Association (AICWA) has written to Prime Minister Narendra Modi requesting an immediate halt to the release of an upcoming Pakistani series on Netflix India. In addition to stopping the specific title, the organization is calling for an enduring ban on all content originating from Pakistan.

About the series

The drama in question is titled 'Bachay Hain Sang Samait Lo', which has been scheduled to launch on Netflix India on December 4, 2026. Further information regarding the show's cast, crew, genre, and plot details is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

It remains to be seen how the government and the streaming platform will address the association's appeal prior to the planned debut date. Additional updates regarding platform availability or official responses are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

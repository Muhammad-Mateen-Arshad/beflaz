What's new

A cycle of 14th-century frescoes created by the Italian master Giotto has recently been unveiled to the public in Florence, Italy. Titled the "Stories of St. Francis," the restored artwork depicts key moments in the life of St. Francis of Assisi. The pieces were put on view last week inside the Basilica of Santa Croce after undergoing a four-year restoration project.

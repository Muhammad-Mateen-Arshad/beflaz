The Voyager Scholarship, also known as the Voyager Fellowship, is administered by the Obama Foundation to support undergraduate students who are dedicated to public service, public policy, and civic engagement. The program gives recipients the freedom to pursue independent public service initiatives, research, or international study projects.

Scholarship Overview

The scholarship empowers students to gain international experience and address pressing societal challenges around the globe. Awardees join a cohort of peers focused on different aspects of public service. For instance, a past recipient in a 100-person cohort utilized the grant during the summer to focus on gender equity in education, helping design analytical frameworks for a non-profit foundation in Lisbon and volunteering at a women's refugee shelter in Athens, Greece.

Who Can Apply

According to details shared by a recipient, applicants must meet the following criteria:

  • Be enrolled as a college sophomore.
  • Be a recipient of financial aid.
  • Demonstrate a strong interest in pursuing public service.

Additional qualifications or GPA requirements: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

Selected scholars receive funding to support customized public service work or study in location(s) of their choice. Benefits include hands-on experience in global civic engagement, exposure to international policy lenses, and opportunities to build connections with fellow cohort members.

Scholarship Overview

Fraudulent education consultancies in Pakistan have been exploiting students aiming to study abroad by making false promises, using fabricated documents, and promoting misleading scholarship claims. Many families borrow money or sell property to fulfill their children's dreams of overseas education, making them vulnerable to deceptive agents. To address this issue, recommendations include establishing transparent registration processes for consultancies, creating accessible verification and complaint systems, taking strict legal action against fraudulent operators, and having universities enable direct verification of admission letters and scholarship offers.

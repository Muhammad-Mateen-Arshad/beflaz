Scholarship Overview

The Honhaar Scholarship was highlighted during a distribution ceremony held at the University of Rasul in Mandi Bahauddin, attended by Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif. The program links educational support directly to public welfare, prioritizing merit-based aid for students.

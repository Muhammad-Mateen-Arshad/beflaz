Scholarship Overview

Educational institutions have introduced key funding and exchange opportunities for students pursuing creative and cultural studies. As part of its 150th anniversary celebrations, OCAD University is launching the 150 FUTURES scholarship initiative, supported by donations to a $6 million university fundraising campaign. In addition, Trent University and Auckland University of Technology in New Zealand have established a bilateral exchange scholarship program dedicated to Indigenous education.

Who Can Apply

Eligibility guidelines vary depending on the specific scholarship initiative:

  • 150 FUTURES Scholarships: Designed for students pursuing education in art, design, and culture at OCAD University. Additional eligibility criteria are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Barry and Laurie Green Bilateral Exchange Scholarship Program: Open to students interested in Indigenous-led education who wish to study Indigenous perspectives and community-based learning during a semester at either Trent University or Auckland University of Technology.

Benefits

Selected recipients can access structural funding or international exchange experiences:

  • 150 FUTURES Scholarships: A total of 150 fully-funded scholarships will be awarded. Starting in September 2027, recipients will receive $10,000 annually over a four-year period.
  • Barry and Laurie Green Bilateral Exchange Scholarship Program: Allows students to spend a semester abroad connecting with Indigenous perspectives at a partner institution in New Zealand or Canada. Specific funding amounts are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Application procedures, required documents, and submission deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

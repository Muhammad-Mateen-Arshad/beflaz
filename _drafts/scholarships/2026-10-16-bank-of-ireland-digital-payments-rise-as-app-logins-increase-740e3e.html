Scholarship Overview

Bank of Ireland has announced a partnership with Google to offer up to 10,000 fully funded AI training scholarships. This educational opportunity is part of the bank's broader digital advancements and investments in customer features, which also include new website tools, enhanced mobile banking services, and an AI-enabled Benefits Finder tax credits tool.

Who Can Apply

The scholarships are targeted at Bank of Ireland's business customers.

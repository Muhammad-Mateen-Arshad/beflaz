Scholarship Overview

Iran has presented educational opportunities to Uganda as part of diplomatic efforts to strengthen bilateral cooperation between the two countries. During a meeting between Uganda's Speaker of Parliament, Jacob Marksons Oboth, and Iran's outgoing Ambassador to Uganda, Majid Saffar, several cooperative offers were discussed, including scholarship opportunities alongside engagements in trade, health, and technology.

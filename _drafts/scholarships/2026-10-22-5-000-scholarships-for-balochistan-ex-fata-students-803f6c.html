Scholarship Overview

An educational program provides 5,000 scholarships specifically allocated for students from Balochistan and ex-FATA in Pakistan.

Who Can Apply

This opportunity is designated for individuals from the following areas:

  • Students residing in or belonging to Balochistan
  • Students from ex-FATA regions

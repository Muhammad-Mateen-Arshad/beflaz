Scholarship Overview

The Prime Minister’s Special Scholarship Scheme (PMSSS) provides educational support in Jammu and Kashmir. For the 2026-27 academic target, the scheme reached 94.3% completion by granting 4,715 scholarships against its total yearly target of 5,000 allotments.

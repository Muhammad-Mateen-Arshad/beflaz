Scholarship Overview

Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif announced educational assistance programs during a visit to the University of Chakwal. The initiative includes the Honhaar Scholarships program, designed to assist students facing poverty or a lack of resources so they can complete their education without financial strain on their parents.

Who Can Apply

Based on the available information, beneficiary groups include:

  • Students studying in Punjab universities and colleges covered under the Honhaar Scholarships program.
  • Students from Khyber Pakhtunkhwa enrolled in educational institutions in Punjab.
  • Detailed academic qualifications and age criteria are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Benefits

The program provides several forms of support for students:

  • Educational coverage for tuition costs through the Honhaar Scholarships scheme.
  • Provision of laptops, with a specific share reserved for students hailing from Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
  • Access to newly established computer and artificial intelligence laboratories in colleges and universities.

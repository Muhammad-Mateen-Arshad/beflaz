Scholarship Overview

The Kessington Adebukunola Adebutu Foundation provides educational funding as part of its charitable activities. The organization was established by Nigerian lottery businessman Kessington Adebutu, the founder of Premier Lotto. In addition to educational initiatives, the foundation also supports youth and sports programs.

Who Can Apply

Financial support from the foundation is intended for specific candidate groups:

  • Students coming from poor families.

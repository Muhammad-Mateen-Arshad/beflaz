Role Overview

Public service roles include services provided directly by government agencies or financed through private provision. These jobs address essential community needs that may be under-provided by the open market. Examples of public service institutions and roles include Volunteer Fire Departments, Ambulance Corps, medical professionals, repair specialists, and community food pantries.

Role Overview

Following discussions between state representatives and protest leaders, the Punjab government agreed to provide official employment opportunities to support the families of deceased individuals involved in recent events. Under this settlement, a government role is pledged to the wife of slain anti-drug campaigner Parvinder Singh Jhota, alongside a separate job offer for a family member of Daljit Singh from Bhamme Kalan.

Role Overview

Former SAARC Chamber of Commerce and Industry president Iftikhar Ali Malik stated that deepening domestic manufacturing of automotive components is essential for economic growth, employment, and foreign-exchange savings in Pakistan. Expanding local production creates opportunities across engineering, electronics, plastics, rubber, and metalworking businesses. As vehicle assembly shifts toward hybrids, plug-in hybrids, and electric vehicles, local manufacturing focus will need to include batteries, motors, power electronics, drivetrain components, and software.

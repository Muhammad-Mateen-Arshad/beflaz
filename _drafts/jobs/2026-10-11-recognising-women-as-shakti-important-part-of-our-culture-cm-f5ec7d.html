Role Overview

The Chief Minister of Uttarakhand, Pushkar Singh Dhami, highlighted key state initiatives focused on women's economic and social empowerment. As part of these measures, the state government has mandated a 30 per cent horizontal reservation for women in all government jobs across Uttarakhand. Announced during an event in Dehradun under the Girls' Education Promotion Programme, this initiative is designed to provide female candidates with enhanced employment opportunities, fostering self-reliance and progress.

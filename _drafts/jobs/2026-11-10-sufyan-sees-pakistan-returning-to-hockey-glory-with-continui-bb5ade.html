Role Overview

International penalty-corner specialist Sufyan Khan described his path to professional hockey. After initial coaching from Ihsanullah in Bannu, he moved to Lahore to train at Dar Hockey Academy, which provided free hockey sticks, balls, kits, meals, and accommodation. After two years of training at the academy, he was offered employment with the Pakistan Navy and joined the national team in 2022. Sufyan also noted that other Lahore academies—such as Olympian Khawaja Junaid’s academy, the academy established by the late Rana Zaheer, and Pak Heroes—have collectively developed numerous players for Pakistan's junior and senior national squads.

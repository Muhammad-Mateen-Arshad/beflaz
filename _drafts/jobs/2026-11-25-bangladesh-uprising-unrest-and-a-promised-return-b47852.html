Background and Developments

Initial demonstrations against job quotas escalated into widespread rallies concerning civil rights issues. Following significant unrest, former Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina left for India, and an interim government led by Nobel Peace Prize winner Muhammad Yunus took charge. Subsequent legal proceedings led to convictions of former officials, including a death sentence in absentia for Sheikh Hasina and former interior minister Asaduzzaman Khan Kamal, alongside a five-year prison sentence for former police chief Chowdhury Abdullah Al-Mamun. Elections were later won by the Bangladesh Nationalist Party led by Tarique Rahman.

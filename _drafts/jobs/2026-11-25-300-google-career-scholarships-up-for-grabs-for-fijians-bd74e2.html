Applications are now open for the Google Career Certificate Scholarships in Fiji 2026, offering 300 citizens the chance to gain practical skills in essential technology disciplines. This educational initiative is designed for youth, women, jobseekers, and individuals looking to upgrade their skills or switch careers. Eligible candidates must submit their expressions of interest before the deadline on November 29, 2026.

Role Overview

This government-backed training program focuses on building job-ready competencies in artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and other key digital sectors. The goal is to prepare Fiji's workforce for an AI-driven future and expand participation in the growing digital economy. The program is organized by the Ministry of Policing and Communications alongside the Ministry of Industries, Commerce and Business Development, in partnership with the Digital Government Transformation Office.

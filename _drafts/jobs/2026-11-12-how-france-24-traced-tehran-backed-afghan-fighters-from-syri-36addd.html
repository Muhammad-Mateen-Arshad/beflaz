Role Overview

The Fatemiyoun division is an Iranian-backed Afghan Shia militia that operated in Syria for eleven years to support Bashar al-Assad's administration. Following the collapse of the Syrian government in December 2024, members of the organization have resurfaced across various locations in Iran, including Sistan and Baluchistan province. Findings indicate that certain militia members have been integrated directly into the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) ranks, including officer positions such as Lieutenant Colonel.

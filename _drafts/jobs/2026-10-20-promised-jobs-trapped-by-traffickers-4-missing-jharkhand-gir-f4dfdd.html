Job seekers exploring domestic work opportunities are urged to stay vigilant following recent police reports concerning fraudulent placement agencies. A recent rescue operation conducted by the Khunti Police in Jharkhand highlighted how human traffickers lure job seekers using false promises of domestic employment.

Role Overview

According to official statements from Khunti SP Rishabha Garg, a special police team successfully rescued four missing girls from New Delhi and Jaipur, Rajasthan. Three of the individuals were trapped by human traffickers under the pretext of securing domestic work. A fourth individual was taken to Delhi approximately 10 years ago for domestic work and was later sold in Jaipur. Placement agencies involved in these illegal placement schemes are currently being investigated by the police for strict legal action.

Pakistan has announced its National AI Policy initiative, establishing the Pakistan One Million AI Professionals Target by 2030 to significantly expand the national technology workforce. Announced by Planning Minister Ahsan Iqbal, the project aims to train and upskill individuals across the technology sector in partnership with international partners. Application deadlines for upcoming opportunities are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The government plans to establish seven new artificial intelligence centers designed to link academic research, vocational skills training, tech startups, and industrial applications. This workforce expansion relies on preparing new graduates as well as retraining existing technology workers. Additionally, Pakistan is engaging with foreign tech sectors—such as Russian industry partners—to collaborate in areas like enterprise software, cybersecurity, cloud computing, digital governance, and industrial automation.

Eligibility Criteria

Specific academic qualifications or formal age limits are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. However, the initiative targets the national pool of graduates—currently over 75,000 annually—alongside existing IT professionals seeking specialized skills. Furthermore, a dedicated language center is being set up at Allama Iqbal Open University alongside the Higher Education Commission to offer Russian language courses, helping students and researchers qualify for foreign academic and professional programs.

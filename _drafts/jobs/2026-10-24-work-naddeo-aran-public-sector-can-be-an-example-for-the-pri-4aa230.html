Role Overview

Antonio Naddeo, President of the Agency for Negotiating Representation (Aran), highlighted public sector labor frameworks during the IX Confederal Council of Confintesa event in Rome. Speaking at a conference focused on wages, dignity, and employment rights, Naddeo noted that the public sector features clear regulations, defined trade union representativeness, and collective bargaining agreements that can serve as a strong standard for the private sector to follow.

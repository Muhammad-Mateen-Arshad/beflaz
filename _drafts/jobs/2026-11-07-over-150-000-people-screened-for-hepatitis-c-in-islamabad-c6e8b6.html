Role Overview

The Prime Minister's National Programme for the elimination of Hepatitis C aims to eradicate the virus across Pakistan by 2030. In its first phase, the campaign targets 1.6 million citizens in the Islamabad Capital Territory before expanding to a broader national drive targeting over 160 million individuals. Screening data indicates infection rates between 2 and 2.5 percent in the general population, rising to 6 percent in areas with restricted healthcare access.

Role Overview

Following the November 10, 2025 Red Fort vehicle explosion in New Delhi, Indian security and intelligence agencies conducted extensive operations targeting various homegrown and foreign-guided terror networks. The investigation uncovered modules involving educated professionals sourcing materials such as ammonium nitrate. In response to these security challenges, the government enacted the PRAHAAR National Counter Terrorism Policy and Strategy on February 23 to enforce a zero-tolerance approach against radicalization, recruitment, funding, and foreign involvement.

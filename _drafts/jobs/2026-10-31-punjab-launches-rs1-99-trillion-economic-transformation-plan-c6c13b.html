The Government of Punjab has officially introduced the Punjab Innovation for Value, Opportunity and Transformation (PIVOT) programme along with the STEP portal. Launched by Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz, this three-year economic development initiative running from 2026 to 2029 aims to enhance export capacity, strengthen local industries, and create 162,000 jobs across the province.

Role Overview

The PIVOT plan is structured to resolve industrial bottlenecks, improve workforce capabilities, and drive private investment. Backed by a total budget of Rs1.99 trillion—combining Rs1,089 billion in public sector funding and Rs906 billion from private investment—the initiative will provide technical and vocational skills training to 402,060 people. Key growth areas include garments, sports goods, footwear, ceramics, pharmaceuticals, leather, fans, agriculture, tourism, and digital technology.

Key Details

  • Total Jobs Targeted: 162,000 employment opportunities
  • Skills Training Target: 402,060 people
  • Programme Timeline: 2026–2029
  • Financial Allocation: Rs1.99 trillion overall investment
  • Industrial Support: Concessional financing of Rs300 billion, machinery duty refunds, and energy storage support
  • Agro-Processing Locations: Four specialized parks in Sargodha, Khanewal, Okara, and Bhakkar

How to Apply

The initiative is facilitated through the newly announced STEP portal. Operations and monitoring will be overseen by a dedicated PIVOT secretariat within the Planning and Development Department, supported by a central monitoring cell and district focal persons.

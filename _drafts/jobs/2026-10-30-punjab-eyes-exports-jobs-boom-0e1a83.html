The Chief Minister of Punjab, Maryam Nawaz Sharif, has officially introduced the Punjab Innovation for Value, Opportunity and Transformation (PIVOT) programme alongside a dedicated portal. Operating under a three-year economic framework spanning from 2026 to 2029, this initiative aims to drive investment, strengthen exports, and generate significant employment opportunities across multiple economic sectors.

Role Overview

The PIVOT plan focuses on economic advancement in industry, agriculture, technology, tourism, and vocational skill development. Over its three-year run, the initiative seeks to improve the standard of living for one million citizens while expanding industrial capacity and raising exports by $6.8 billion.

Key Details

  • Expected Employment Creation: A target of establishing 162,000 jobs.
  • Skills & Internship Targets: Plans to train 402,060 people, offering digital skill programs, information technology internships, internationally recognized certifications, startup incubation, and business development support.
  • Industrial & Agricultural Focus: Allocation of Rs753.7 billion for manufacturing sectors including garments, pharmaceuticals, sports goods, leather, footwear, fans, and ceramics. It also establishes four agro-processing parks in Bhakkar, Khanewal, Okara, and Sargodha.
  • Financial Investment: Involves Rs1.089 trillion in state investment, Rs906 billion in private investment, and Rs300 billion in concessional loans with interest rates up to five percent.

Role Overview

The China-Pakistan Information Corridor (CPIC) was established at an event organized by Pakistan's Ministry of Information and Broadcasting at the Pakistan-China Friendship Center in Islamabad on October 27. The project aims to facilitate joint content production, shape public narratives, and counter media reports that officials consider misleading regarding the China-Pakistan Economic Corridor (CPEC).

The Discovery

Great Zimbabwe stands as one of the most prominent ancient civilizations on the African continent. Constructed between the 11th and 15th centuries, the city served as the thriving capital of a massive trading empire. The historic site was originally built by the ancestors of today’s Shona people.

What Scientists Found

For hundreds of years, European explorers dismissed the historical significance of Great Zimbabwe. However, recent archaeological work is uncovering fresh evidence about the site, offering a clearer picture of how the ancient city operated as a major urban center.

Why It Matters

The latest archaeological discoveries reveal the true scope of Great Zimbabwe's global importance, highlighting its role in historical trade networks across the region.

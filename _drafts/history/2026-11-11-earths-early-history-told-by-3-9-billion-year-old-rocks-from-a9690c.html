The Discovery

A scientific study by H. Xie and colleagues, published in the journal Nature, examines ancient rock formations originating from northern China. The research highlights key geological processes occurring during the earliest periods of Earth's history.

What Scientists Found

Analysis of the rocks from northern China revealed important indicators about the planet's early mantle and tectonic activity:

  • Evidence implying mantle depletion during the Hadean Eon.
  • Indications of subduction processes dating back approximately 3.95 billion years.

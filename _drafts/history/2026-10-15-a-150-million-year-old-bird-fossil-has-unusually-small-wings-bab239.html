What Scientists Found

Analysis of the fossilized remains highlights several notable anatomical features:

  • Extremely small wings relative to typical avian features.
  • Dentition that resembles teeth found in dinosaurs.

Why It Matters

This finding is significant because it directly challenges the long-standing belief that early avian species were all primarily adapted for flight. The combination of dinosaur-like teeth and unusually small wings indicates that the early evolution of birds was more complex than previously thought.

Richard Madden, Gugu Mbatha-Raw Netflix series 'Trinity' gets release date, official trailer

Published on November 04, 2026

What's new

Netflix has officially set the launch date and unveiled the official trailer for its upcoming original drama series, Trinity. Viewers can tune in when the brand-new show debuts on the streaming service on Dec. 1.

About the series

The storyline centers on a heroic naval officer, Commander Katherine Decker (played by Gugu Mbatha-Raw), who serves as second-in-command on a nuclear submarine named the USS Kansas. Her life takes a complex turn when she becomes involved with the outwardly charismatic Secretary of Defense, Webb Preston (portrayed by Richard Madden).

Their unlikely connection draws Decker directly into danger surrounding a top-secret initiative code-named Trinity. As events unfold, she uncovers evidence suggesting Preston may be positioned at the heart of a hazardous conspiracy. Jed Mercurio leads the project as showrunner, executive producer, and writer. Additional details regarding total episode numbers are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

The newly released trailer showcases suspenseful exchanges between the key figures. In the footage, Preston presses Decker to share hidden information if she wants to protect her submarine crew and assist in making the world safer. Decker insists that her actions must not be traced back to her, prompting Preston to assure her that he has her back.

Alongside the primary lead actors, the series features an extensive supporting cast, which includes:

  • Marcia Gay Harden
  • JD Pardo
  • Bruce Greenwood
  • Robert Wisdom
  • Kirk Acevedo
  • Ben Cotton
  • James Remar
  • Jason Ralph
  • Mark O'Brien

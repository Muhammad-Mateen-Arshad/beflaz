Boxing Icon Mike Tyson, Turkish Star Engin Altan Düzyatan Arrive In Amman For Jordan MMA Week

Published on October 29, 2026

What's new

Turkish star Engin Altan Düzyatan and former world heavyweight boxing champion Mike Tyson have arrived in Amman as guests of honor for Jordan Mixed Martial Arts (MMA) Week and the "DF: UNCAGED" championship.

Düzyatan voiced his delight regarding the trip, sending affectionate messages to the local public and praising the warm welcome he received in Amman. Tyson similarly shared his excitement about joining the international gathering, noting that he accepted the invitation immediately upon receiving it and looks forward to visiting Petra and exploring the region.

About the series

Engin Altan Düzyatan is widely recognized for playing the title role in the historical drama series Resurrection: Ertuğrul. Further information regarding episode counts, full cast lists, or plot points for the show is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

The week of sports programming aims to strengthen the Kingdom's standing as a regional destination for sports tourism. Key aspects of the gathering include:

  • Coordination with the Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities and the Jordan Tourism Board (JTB).
  • A lineup of accompanying promotional and athletic events.
  • The main "DF: UNCAGED" fight night, which culminates on Friday at the Roman Theater in downtown Amman.

When Does The Next Season of 'From' Premiere on Netflix and MGM+? 'From' Seasons 4 and 5 Release Dates Info

Published on October 27, 2026

The hit horror thriller series From has captured widespread attention after breaking into the top five TV shows on Netflix, joining other popular programs like Monday Night Raw and Nobody Wants This. Viewers watching on Netflix can currently access the first three seasons of the show.

What's new

While Netflix subscribers only have access to three seasons for now, four full seasons have already aired on MGM+. The fourth season is not expected to join Netflix in the near future, though it could potentially arrive on the platform closer to the premiere of the next installment. MGM+ officially renewed the show for a fifth season back in April, which will serve as the final chapter of the series.

About the series

Created by John Griffin, the critically acclaimed sci-fi series originally premiered in February 2022 on Epix before moving to MGM+ for subsequent seasons. Starring Harold Perrineau, the story follows the inhabitants of a mysterious town with no exit. All four existing seasons are streaming on MGM+, which can be accessed through standalone plans or add-ons via services like Amazon, Philo, YouTube, Fubo, DIRECTV, and Sling TV. For exact details on when Season 4 will land on Netflix or how many episodes it contains: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

The fifth and final season is expected to make its debut in mid-2027 on MGM+. Creator John Griffin, showrunner Jeff Pinkner, and director Jack Bender stated in a joint message that production on the final season has officially begun. They promised that the upcoming chapter will bring the storyline to a complete conclusion, provide answers to ongoing mysteries, and deliver plenty of terrors and emotional moments for the audience.

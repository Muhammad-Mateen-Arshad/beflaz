Netflix to release star-studded Pakistani show – but Indian viewers may miss out

Published on November 25, 2026

What's new

Netflix has released the first trailer for its upcoming star-studded Pakistani drama series, Jo Bachay Hain Sang Samait Lo. However, Indian audiences may miss out on watching the show following calls for a ban. The All Indian Cine Workers Association (AICWA), headed by president Suresh Shyamlal Gupta, written to Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi to request an intervention preventing the series from streaming in India.

The association has called for strict permanent laws against airing Pakistani entertainment projects on streaming platforms, television, and in cinemas. Gupta also referenced a May 2025 advisory from India's Ministry of Information and Broadcasting urging platforms to halt Pakistani content following security incidents.

About the series

The production is based on the bestselling Urdu novel of the same title by Farhat Ishtiaq. It features six interconnected romantic stories that unfold across three generations. Momina Duraid serves as the showrunner, director, and producer for the project.

The series features a prominent ensemble cast, including:

  • Fawad Khan and Mahira Khan
  • Sanam Saeed and Ahad Raza Mir
  • Hania Aamir and Iqra Aziz
  • Hamza Ali Abbasi, Maya Ali, Bilal Ashraf, and Khushhal Khan

Specific episode counts and global premiere times are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

The series features key reunions between major actors. Fawad Khan and Mahira Khan star together again more than a decade after their hit 2011 romantic drama Humsafar, which originally helped boost Pakistani serials during a time when Indian and Turkish shows dominated local television schedules.

Fawad Khan also reunites with Sanam Saeed, who previously co-starred with him in the 2012 series Zindagi Gulzar Hai and the 2024 series Barzakh. Additionally, the cast features Hania Aamir, known for hit shows such as Mere Humsafar and Kabhi Main Kabhi Tum, and Ahad Raza Mir, who has previously performed in Netflix's Resident Evil and the BBC's World on Fire.

New Grown Ups Project Sets 2027 Release Date, but It Doesn’t Star Adam Sandler

Published on November 20, 2026

Netflix has announced the launch details for its upcoming adaptation of Marian Keyes’ novel, Grown Ups. Rather than being linked to the comedy feature film of the same name, this project is a messy Irish family drama. The streaming service will release all eight one-hour episodes globally on January 14, 2027. Production wrapped in February 2026 after a six-month shoot across Wicklow and Dublin, shifting the release past its initial 2026 target window.

What's new

Initial promotional photos offer a look at the Casey family across several settings, including a windswept beach, a cliffside over Dublin Bay, a restaurant kitchen, a festive dinner, a dance under fairy lights, and a character relaxing in a garden wearing sunglasses and a bunny onesie. The series stands as Netflix's second major UK and Ireland drama scheduled for early 2027.

About the series

The story follows the close-knit Casey family residing in South County Dublin. Bound by a mix of love, memory, money, loyalty, and resentment, their lives shift when the family's "good son" unexpectedly dies. Over the following year of mourning and chaos, the family navigates shifting romances, old wounds, and the absurdities of adult life. Readers of the original book will notice a structural change: the screen version uses the son's death to drive the storyline instead of beginning with a character suffering a concussion at a birthday dinner.

What to expect

Samantha Strauss created and wrote the series, while James Griffiths and Ciaran Donnelly share directing duties. Executive producers include Strauss, Griffiths, Marian Keyes, and See-Saw Films. Key cast members and their roles include:

  • Sarah Greene as Jessie
  • Barry Ward as Johnny
  • Aisling Bea as Cara
  • Robert Sheehan as Liam
  • Amy-Leigh Hickman as Nell
  • Adrian Dunbar as Canice
  • Sinéad Cusack as Rose

The ensemble also features Karin Hanczewski, Katelyn Rose Downey, and Jason O'Mara. Details regarding Jason O'Mara's character are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

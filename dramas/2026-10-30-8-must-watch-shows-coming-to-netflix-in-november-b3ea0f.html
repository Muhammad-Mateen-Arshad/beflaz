8 Must-Watch Shows Coming to Netflix in November

Published on October 30, 2026

What's new

As the holiday season approaches, Netflix is introducing a fresh slate of titles to its streaming roster for November. The upcoming lineup offers options across multiple genres, ranging from animated action and dark thrillers to comedy specials and reality competitions.

About the series

Several notable series are featured in the upcoming lineup:

  • Bass X Machina: Produced by South Korea's Studio Mir, this animated original blends steampunk, sci-fi, and Western action. The storyline centers on a father who is driven to seek justice on his own terms in a lawless world to keep his family safe. (Release date: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice).
  • Black Doves (Season 2): Returning on November 5, this espionage thriller features Keira Knightley and Ben Whishaw. Helen continues passing state secrets to a covert group while her husband, played by Andrew Buchan, prepares to become prime minister. Meanwhile, her handler Mrs. Reed (Sarah Lancashire) deals with an internal plot against her position.
  • The Body: Created by Quinn Shephard, this eight-episode series focuses on a Catholic school dance-team initiation that goes wrong, triggering mass hysteria and prophetic visions in a small community. Blending teenage drama, psychological tension, and religious horror, the cast includes Kristina Bogic, Sara Boustany, Geena Meszaros, and Gabby Windey. (Release date: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice).

What to expect

Beyond scripted dramas, subscribers can tune into Mike Tyson: Return of the Mike, a comedy special filmed at the Seminole Hard Rock Hotel & Casino in Hollywood, Florida. Marking his first time on stage since 2013, Tyson candidly discusses mental health, personal struggles, and addiction with humor. Viewers can also look out for other titles joining the lineup, including The Hunting Wives, Win the Mall, and The Great British Baking Show: Holidays.

Upcoming K-Drama ‘Gift’ Brings Supernatural Baseball to Netflix in December 2026

Published on November 18, 2026

What's new

Netflix has acquired the international licensing for the upcoming South Korean sports drama Gift, which is set to begin streaming on December 5, 2026. The series will first air domestically in South Korea on tvN before episodes arrive on the streaming platform. Co-directed by Kim Kyu Tae and Ham Seung Hun, the drama was written by Hae Ju, Moon Min Jeong, and Seong Eun. Subscribers can look forward to a 12-episode run, with two 70-minute episodes released weekly on Saturdays and Sundays.

About the series

Gift follows professional baseball coach Jung Min Yong, whose life changes following an unexpected accident. The event grants him supernatural abilities to perceive and measure the exact skill levels and potential of baseball players. He accepts a position as head coach for Deokcheon High School's struggling, last-place amateur team, embarking on an intense and emotional season. Utilizing his new gift, Jung Min Yong scouts a player named Tae Hun to be a first-team pitcher after recognizing his talent, though conflict quickly arises as the team's athletes express dissatisfaction with their newly appointed mentor.

What to expect

The production features a notable cast carrying the narrative forward:

  • Kim Woo Bin leads the series as coach Jung Min Yong, adding to his previous roles in Netflix titles like Officer Black Belt, Our Blues, Genie Make a Wish, and Black Knight.
  • Seo Eun Soo stars as Yun Seo Jin, having previously appeared in dramas such as Legal High and Missing: The Other Side.
  • Seo Hyeon Woo portrays An Gi Man, following performances in projects like Sold Out on You, Celebrity, Behind Every Star, My Name is Loh Kiwan, and Love and Leashes.
  • Lim Su Bin steps into the role of Heo Seung Il for his second acting role to date, following an appearance on season 5 of the reality show Single's Inferno.
  • Kim Won Hae appears as the principal of Deokcheon High School.

Specific daily broadcast times for the domestic tvN release are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

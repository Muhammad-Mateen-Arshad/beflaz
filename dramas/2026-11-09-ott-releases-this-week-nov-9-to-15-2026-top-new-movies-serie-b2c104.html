OTT Releases This Week (Nov 9 To 15, 2026): Top New Movies, Series On Netflix, Prime Video, JioHotstar And Others

Published on November 09, 2026

What's new

Streaming platforms are rolling out a fresh lineup of titles between November 9 and November 15. Audiences watching Netflix, Prime Video, JioHotstar, and Zee5 can tune into a broad mix of projects, ranging from fantasy sagas and legal adaptations to action-packed thrillers and festive family features.

About the series

Several key series and episodic releases highlight this week's digital calendar:

  • The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power Season 3: Premiering November 11 on Prime Video, the fantasy saga resumes in the Second Age of Middle-earth as Sauron aims to rise to power and craft the One Ring. Morfydd Clark, Charlie Vickers, and Robert Aramayo star in the ensemble cast.
  • Student of the Year: After Dark: Debuting November 13 on JioHotstar, this thriller marks the OTT debut of Shanaya Kapoor. Located at St. Teresa’s, the storyline centers on Aditi Malik going undercover to look into the mysterious death of her twin sister, Aayesha.
  • Alicia’s Law: Arriving November 11 on Netflix, this Mexican adaptation of The Good Wife features Fernanda Castillo as Alicia, who rebuilds her life and returns to practicing law after her Supreme Court Justice husband is imprisoned due to a corruption scandal.

What to expect

Subscribers can also stream several notable movie premieres throughout the week:

  • Daayra: Releasing November 13 on Netflix, this crime drama starring Kareena Kapoor and Prithviraj Sukumaran follows an inquiry that unearths secrets, crime, and justice.
  • Jailer 2: Arriving November 12 on Amazon Prime Video, Nelson Dilipkumar directs Rajinikanth's return as Tiger Muthuvel Pandian, pulling the retired jailer into conflict with a dangerous syndicate.
  • Blind Game: Launching November 11 on Zee5, Sangeeth Sivan directs Arjun Rampal and Priyamani in a crime thriller about a suspended officer searching for a lost fortune.
  • Mrigaya 1.5: The Hunt Continues: Debuting November 13 on Zee5, Abhirup Ghosh directs a Bengali crime action thriller following officer Debanjan Dutta and his Kolkata Police team.
  • Bandra: Available November 13 on Zee5, this Malayalam action drama stars Dileep as a troubled businessman named Aala alongside Tamannaah Bhatia as 1990s actress Tara Janaki.
  • Christmas Switch: Premiering November 12 on Netflix, a holiday mix-up upends a family's plans while offering lessons on love and togetherness.

For exact air times or full episode schedules, details were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

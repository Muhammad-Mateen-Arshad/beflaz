New OTT Releases This Week (November 16 To 20, 2026): Campus Diaries, Dhindora, Percy Jackson And The Olympians On Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, JioHotstar And More

Published on November 16, 2026

What's new

A fresh lineup of streaming shows and feature films is set to launch between November 16 and November 20, 2026. Viewers can expect a wide selection of romantic comedies, animated projects, sports biopics, and returning dramas across Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, JioHotstar, and Disney+.

About the series

Several high-profile television projects and limited series highlight this week's streaming menu:

  • Guarding Stars: Debuting November 18 on Netflix, this romantic comedy places Leighton Meester in the role of a steadfast bodyguard assigned to protect a charismatic action star played by Jared Padalecki.
  • Mr & Mrs Office: Launching November 18 on JioHotstar, this Tamil comedy stars Pradeep Antony and Smeha Manimegalai as Arivu and Aishwarya, a married couple navigating life at the same IT firm.
  • The Altruists: Releasing November 19 on Netflix, this limited drama stars Anthony Boyle and Julia Garner as cryptocurrency figures Sam Bankman-Fried and Caroline Ellison.
  • Dhindora: Moving to Netflix for its new season, actor Bhuvan Bam returns as Titu mama alongside nine other roles. The cast features Sanjeeda Sheikh and Kanwaljit Singh, with guest appearances by Udit Narayan, Anup Soni, and Orry.
  • Campus Diaries: Directed by Abhishek Yadav, this college series follows a group of friends reuniting on campus amid event preparations and personal friction. The cast includes Harsh Beniwal, Ritvik Sahore, Saloni Gaur, Srishti Ganguli, Salonie Patel, and Abhinav Sharma.
  • Percy Jackson And The Olympians: Season three on Disney+ adapts Rick Riordan's book The Titan's Curse, starring Walker Scobell, Leah Sava Jeffries, and Aryan Simhadri as Percy copes with Annabeth's sudden capture and the Oracle of Delphi's prophecy.

What to expect

Movie lovers also have multiple options arriving on digital platforms:

  • Madden: Premiering November 18 on Amazon Prime Video, David O. Russell directs Nicolas Cage as football icon John Madden. Christian Bale, Kathryn Hahn, and Sienna Miller co-star.
  • Steps: Arriving November 20 on Netflix, this animated reimagining of Cinderella features the voice talents of Ali Wong, Amanda Seyfried, and Stephanie Hsu.
  • Operation Warrior Kid Project: Directed by McG and based on Jocko Willink's book, this family film stars Chris Pratt as a Navy SEAL helping his nephew (Jude Hill) face bullies alongside his sister (Linda Cardellini). Exact release date details: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

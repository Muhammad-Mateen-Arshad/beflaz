Netflix’s Spookiest Korean Horror Show Is No. 1 With 44 Million Hours Viewed

Published on November 12, 2026

Netflix has achieved a major streaming success with the supernatural survival thriller series Dead-End Job. After entering the global charts in fourth place during its debut week, the Korean horror show surged to the top spot on the Top 10 Non-English Shows Worldwide list for the week of November 2–8. It accumulated 44.4 million hours viewed and 8.7 million views, putting it ahead of competing titles like A Couple of Lies and Lupin Part 4.

Before its global launch on October 30, the eight-episode production screened its initial four episodes in the 'On Screen' section at the 31st Busan International Film Festival on October 7, 2026. The series has garnered strong praise from critics and audiences alike, earning a perfect 100% Tomatometer rating alongside a 73% popcornmeter score on Rotten Tomatoes.

About the series

Helmed by director Kim Da-min and scripted by writers Kyeong Min-sun and Lee Shin-ji, the project blends intense survival mechanics with uncanny phenomena. Lee Jae-wook stars as Byeon Hyeok-jun, a young man overwhelmed by debt who urgently seeks an escape from his financial troubles.

His search leads him to the mysterious Spider Staffing Agency, which offers high-paying part-time work well above minimum wage. However, the lucrative job carries one non-negotiable condition: Byeon must strictly keep everything that occurs during his work hours a complete secret.

What to expect

Built around an anthology format, the series keeps the tension high by forcing the protagonist to navigate terrifying scenarios across distinct environments:

  • What starts as a seemingly standard night shift at a local convenience store quickly turns into a battle to stay alive.
  • Subsequent episodes transport Byeon to bizarre settings, including movie theaters, active construction sites, and amusement parks.
  • Each episode introduces a new set of supernatural threats accompanied by explicit rules that Byeon must follow to survive the night.

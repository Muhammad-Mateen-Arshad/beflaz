'The Diplomat' Season 4: Release date, plot, cast and filming locations

Published on October 11, 2026

What's new

Netflix's political drama series "The Diplomat" is set to return for its fourth season on Thursday, October 15, 2026. All eight episodes will drop on the same day, allowing viewers to watch the full season at their own pace. The new installment continues directly from the dramatic conclusion of Season 3, elevating international tensions and personal dilemmas for its main characters.

What to expect

Season 4 centers on an escalating international crisis that jeopardizes relations between the United States and the United Kingdom. A clandestine scheme involving US President Grace Penn and newly appointed Vice President Hal Wyler to steal a powerful Russian nuclear weapon pushes both nations toward potential warfare. The covert operation severely undermines trust between political leaders and threatens global security.

Caught in the middle of this dangerous situation, US Ambassador Kate Wyler worries about the catastrophic consequences of the secret plan. To stop the conflict from spiralling out of control, she forms an unexpected partnership with Todd Penn, President Grace Penn's husband. Together, they attempt to challenge the risky decisions made by their respective spouses.

Cast updates

Keri Russell stars once again as Kate Wyler alongside Rufus Sewell as Hal Wyler. Several key cast changes and returns shape the new season:

  • Allison Janney (President Grace Penn) and Bradley Whitford (Todd Penn) have been promoted to series regulars.
  • Nana Mensah (Billie Appiah) continues in her regular role.
  • Returning main cast members include David Gyasi as Austin Dennison, Ali Ahn as Eidra Park, Rory Kinnear as Nicol Trowbridge, and Ato Essandoh as Stuart Hayford.
  • Guest actors expected to return include Miguel Sandoval, Penny Downie, Pearl Mackie, and Georgie Henley.
  • Celia Imrie (Margaret "Meg" Roylin) and Michael McKean (President Rayburn) leave the show following the deaths of their characters.

Filming locations

Production for the fourth season expands across both the UK and the US. London continues to serve as a primary filming destination, utilizing government landmarks and historic sites near Greenwich and Westminster. Filming also moved to Washington, D.C., and New York for scenes set in executive offices and the White House.

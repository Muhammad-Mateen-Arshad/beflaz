Rosario Dawson Drops First Look at Night Nurse in ‘Spider-Man: Brand New Day’ Re-Release as Her Cut Scenes Are Restored

Published on November 19, 2026

What's new

An extended theatrical cut of the record-breaking summer blockbuster "Spider-Man: Brand New Day" is heading back to theaters on December 4. Officially titled "Spider-Man: [Brand New] Brand New Day," this expanded version restores several deleted scenes that did not make the original theatrical release. Among the restored moments is the return of Rosario Dawson as Claire Temple, also known to Marvel fans as the Night Nurse.

About the series

Dawson first portrayed the hero-assisting nurse in the debut season of Netflix's "Daredevil," starring Charlie Cox. After serving as a series regular, she appeared in "Jessica Jones" and joined the main casts of "Luke Cage," "Iron Fist," and "The Defenders." These streaming characters have since transitioned into the broader Marvel Cinematic Universe through Disney+'s "Daredevil: Born Again," which features returning stars Charlie Cox, Krysten Ritter, Mike Colter, and Finn Jones.

What to expect

Dawson confirmed her presence in the extended edition via Instagram, posting a photo alongside Tom Holland's Spider-Man. Her character's scenes may involve checking on Spider-Man's malfunctioning powers or treating injuries after battles with the movie's various super-powered opponents. Notable fighters appearing in the film include:

  • Tom Holland as Spider-Man
  • Michael Mando as Scorpion
  • Sadie Sink as Jean Grey
  • Mark Ruffalo as Hulk
  • Jon Bernthal as Punisher

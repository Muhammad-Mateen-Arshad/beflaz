Stranger Things Returns to Netflix in 2028 With a New Series That Leaves Its Original Cast Behind

Published on November 24, 2026

What's new

Netflix is expanding its popular streaming universe with a live-action prequel titled Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure, scheduled to premiere in 2028. Announced on November 6 during the annual Stranger Things Day event, the upcoming series moves away from Hawkins to focus on a new location and era. The narrative takes place in Marion, Nevada, with its first teaser opening on April 19, 1955. This places the series earlier in the franchise timeline than both the flagship show and the stage production Stranger Things: The First Shadow, which examined Henry Creel's past in 1959.

About the series

The project brings together several key creative figures to build a fresh storyline within the franchise:

  • Kate Trefry, who wrote The First Shadow, co-created the prequel alongside Matt and Ross Duffer and serves as writer and showrunner.
  • Director Joe Wright is attached to executive produce and helm the first three episodes.
  • Matt and Ross Duffer executive produce via their banner, Upside Down Pictures.
  • Casting is actively under way, with filming set to commence next year.

Exact premiere date, full cast list, and detailed plot: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

The original show wrapped up its fifth season over the turn of 2025 and 2026, airing its finale on December 31, 2025, in the US and January 1, 2026, in the UK. The property has also broadened into animation with Stranger Things: Tales From '85, which debuted on April 23, 2026, reached No. 7 on Netflix's global Top 10 chart, and launched a second season on September 17. Parallel to their streaming work, the Duffer brothers are writing and directing an undisclosed film project for Paramount, set for a November 3, 2028 theatrical release. Despite overlapping schedules, Netflix maintains that Other Worlds remains firmly targeted for its 2028 launch window.

'Stranger Things' Prequel Other Worlds Announced, Set In 1955 With 2028 Release

Published on November 08, 2026

What's new

Netflix is expanding its hit universe with a newly announced prequel series titled Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure. The streaming platform revealed the project on November 6, a date celebrated by fans as Stranger Things Day to mark the fictional disappearance of Will Byers in the flagship show's debut season. The new title is currently planned for release in 2028.

This production stands as the fourth official instalment in the franchise. It follows the original live-action series, the animated spin-off Stranger Things: Tales from ’85, and the theatrical stage production Stranger Things: The First Shadow.

About the series

The upcoming storyline departs from the original setting of Hawkins, Indiana. Instead, the narrative unfolds in Marion, Nevada, during 1955. The prequel focuses on events leading up to Henry Creel's encounter with the supernatural forces that ultimately granted him his powers.

Behind the scenes, the series involves a key lineup of creators and executive producers:

  • Original series creators Matt and Ross Duffer are developing the project alongside writer Kate Trefry.
  • Kate Trefry serves as the project's writer, showrunner, and executive producer.
  • Joe Wright is set to direct the first three episodes and will also act as an executive producer.
  • Additional executive producers include Shawn Levy, Paul Dichter, Shelley Meals, Andrea Sperling, and Pavlina Hatoupis.
  • Production is scheduled to take place in New Mexico.

What to expect

The creative leads intend to broaden the franchise's mythology by introducing new heroes, villains, and environments while maintaining the central identity of the main series. The narrative aims to explore fresh themes built upon the foundation of the original flagship show.

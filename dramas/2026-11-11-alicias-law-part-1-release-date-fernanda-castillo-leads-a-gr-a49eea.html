Alicia’s Law Part 1 Release Date: Fernanda Castillo Leads a Gripping New Legal Drama

Published on November 11, 2026

Netflix is adding a new legal drama to its global catalog with Alicia’s Law, a Mexican adaptation of the American series The Good Wife set within Mexico City’s political and legal environment.

What's new

The upcoming show will use a split release schedule for its first season. Part 1 is set to premiere on Netflix on November 11, 2026, with Part 2 following four weeks later on December 9, 2026. This structure gives audiences time to engage with the opening chapter before the rest of the story unfolds. The release arrives during a busy month for streaming viewers, alongside titles like the second season of the Turkish drama Ethos, which returns after a six-year break, and updates on The Empress Season 3.

About the series

The plot follows Alicia, played by Fernanda Castillo (known for Monarca), whose domestic life shatters when her husband Aurelio, played by Juan Manuel Bernal, is caught in a massive public corruption scandal exposed by leaked videos. Left with financial difficulties and two teenage children to support, Alicia decides to re-enter the legal field after spending 20 years away from the profession.

She lands a position at an established law firm managed by David, portrayed by Michel Brown, who is both her university acquaintance and a former romance. Returning to work requires Alicia to adapt to a modernized justice system and survive a competitive office environment.

What to expect

Promotional previews highlight the dual pressures facing the lead character as her private life becomes public news. Key elements featured in the series include:

  • Intense media attention, press conferences, and reporters surrounding the scandal-hit family.
  • High-stakes legal debates, courtroom confrontations, and complex cases.
  • Alicia’s internal conflict over standing by her disgraced husband or building an autonomous life.

Additional release details or exact episode counts are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

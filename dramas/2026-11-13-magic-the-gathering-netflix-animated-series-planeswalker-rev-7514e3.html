MAGIC: THE GATHERING Netflix Animated Series PLANESWALKER Reveals Sneak Peek Video and 2027 Release Date

Published on November 13, 2026

What's new

Netflix has released a sneak peek video providing a first look at PLANESWALKER, an upcoming animated fantasy series based on the Magic: The Gathering universe. Along with the footage, it was confirmed that the series is scheduled to premiere on the platform in 2027.

About the series

Created in partnership with Hasbro Entertainment and Wizards of the Coast, the show focuses on a major multiversal conflict featuring powerful figures from the franchise. At the center of the story is Chandra, a volatile pyromancer targeted because of her extraordinary abilities and haunted by her past. When a dangerous threat endangers the multiverse, Chandra must align with legendary Planeswalkers—mages capable of crossing infinite dimensions—to stop a war that threatens thousands of worlds.

The original card game was created by Richard Garfield in 1993. Hasbro reports that the franchise has attracted tens of millions of players across more than 150 countries and generates over $1 billion in annual revenue.

Production history

The animated adaptation has undergone several creative changes since it was first announced in 2019:

  • Joe and Anthony Russo were initially executive producers alongside head writers Henry Gilroy and Jose Molina before leaving over creative differences.
  • Jeff Kline took over the project leadership in 2021.
  • Following a pause in development, Terry Matalas joined as showrunner and executive producer.
  • Cindy Appel serves as co-showrunner and executive producer, while Patrick Osborne acts as executive producer and supervising director.
  • Gabriel Marano, Lauren Chavez-Myers, and Daisy West also hold production roles, with Digic handling the animation work.

What to expect

Audiences can expect a storyline filled with complex alliances, personal struggles, and high-stakes battles across multiple realms. In addition to this animated series, a separate live-action feature film featuring Maya Hawke is currently being developed by Legendary Entertainment and Hasbro.

For details regarding the total episode count or an exact air date, these are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

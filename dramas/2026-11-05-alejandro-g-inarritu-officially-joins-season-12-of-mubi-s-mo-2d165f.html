Alejandro G. Iñárritu Officially Joins Season 12 of Mubi's Most Slept-On Series

Published on November 05, 2026

Mubi Podcast is returning for its twelfth season, titled Under Pressure, on November 12 across major podcast platforms. Hosted by writer and radio broadcaster Rico Gagliano, the brand-new five-episode season shifts its focus to movies created under extraordinary personal hardships and severe political opposition.

About the series

First launched in 2021 by film distributor and streaming platform Mubi, the podcast provides film enthusiasts with an inside look at how cinema is crafted. Across its previous eleven seasons, the show has covered a wide array of cinematic topics, including international cinema, horror films, notorious box-office flops, and the work of the late director David Lynch.

What to expect

The upcoming season brings together notable directors and writers to dissect high-stakes filmmaking across five distinct episodes:

  • Episode 1: Focuses on Yılmaz Güney, a Turkish actor turned left-wing filmmaker who managed to make his masterpiece Yol despite serving a dubious 30-year prison sentence. Guests joining Gagliano for this episode include Oscar-winning director Alejandro G. Iñárritu (Digger) and Turkish writer-director Fatih Akin, who won the Golden Bear for Head-On.
  • Episodes 2 and 3: Examines the North Korean monster movie Pulgasari. Guest Paul Fischer, author of A Kim Jong-il Production, breaks down the story of director Shin Sang-ok and actor Choi Eun-hee, a married couple abducted by Kim Jong-il to make the film.
  • Episode 4: Features filmmaker Julia Loktev discussing her two-part documentary My Undesirable Friends, which examines independent journalists navigating political suppression in Russia under Vladimir Putin.
  • Episode 5: Wraps up the season with director Ali Abbasi (Holy Spider, The Apprentice), who details the political challenges and pressures he experienced while directing projects in both Iran and the United States. Release times for individual episodes are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

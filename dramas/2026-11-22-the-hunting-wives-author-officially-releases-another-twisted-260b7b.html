‘The Hunting Wives’ Author Officially Releases Another Twisted Texas Murder Mystery

Published on November 22, 2026

What's new

Author May Cobb has officially expanded her catalog of Texas murder mysteries with the release of her novel All the Little Secrets on November 17. Serving as a standalone thriller sequel to her earlier book All the Little Houses, the new release capitalizes on Cobb's growing momentum across both the publishing and streaming landscapes. Since publishing The Hunting Wives in 2021, Cobb has added five titles to her bibliography, including My Summer Darlings, A Likeable Woman, and The Hollywood Assistant.

About the series

Following its breakout success on Netflix—where it generated two billion viewing minutes in just its second week in the United States—the television adaptation of The Hunting Wives returns on Thanksgiving, November 26. The Texas-set series reunites Brittany Snow as Sophie and Malin Åkerman as Margo for uncharted storylines beyond the original book, with a third season already ordered by the streamer.

Additionally, Apple TV secured the rights to All the Little Houses following a competitive bidding war. Cobb is set to co-create the screen adaptation alongside Naomi Sheldon. Cast details and broadcast schedules for this project are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

The newly published book All the Little Secrets returns readers to 1980s Longview, Texas, immediately following the climax of All the Little Houses. Key elements of the novel include:

  • The fallout from the unexpected murder of the town's social queen bee.
  • A power vacuum where neighborhood figures clash for status, including a newly established trad wife.
  • An ambitious daughter fighting for her position amidst intense social rivalry and collateral damage.

Cobb revealed in an interview with Us Magazine that the immense amount of plot and drama required splitting the story into two books. While she currently views the project as a completed duology without immediate plans for a third entry, she remains open to revisiting the setting in the future.

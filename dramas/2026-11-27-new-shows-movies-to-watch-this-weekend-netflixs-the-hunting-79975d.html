New shows & movies to watch this weekend: Netflix’s ‘The Hunting Wives’ + more

Published on November 27, 2026

What's new

A fresh lineup of shows and specials is available to stream over the holiday weekend. Major platforms have launched new seasons, finales, and performance specials for viewers looking for drama and entertainment.

  • The Hunting Wives (Season 2 on Netflix)
  • Outlander: Blood of My Blood (Season 2 finale on Starz)
  • The JLO Show (Paramount+)

About the series

Netflix has released all eight episodes of the second season of The Hunting Wives, which launched on Thanksgiving Day. Following the truth behind the death of Abby, the story picks up with Margo, played by Malin Akerman, and Sophie, played by Brittany Snow, currently estranged from one another. However, the duo is forced to come back together as new mysteries and secrets accumulate.

Starz is bringing the second season of its prequel series, Outlander: Blood of My Blood, to a close with episode ten on Friday. Additional cast details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

The season finale of Outlander: Blood of My Blood revolves around a Twelfth Night masquerade party, serving as the dramatic focal point to conclude season two. Audiences can also stream the earlier episodes of the season via the Starz application.

For music special enthusiasts, Paramount+ is streaming an extended on-demand cut of The JLO Show. This special documents the closing night of Jennifer Lopez's 2025 residency at Caesars Palace in Las Vegas. After initially broadcasting on CBS and Paramount+ on Thanksgiving night, the special remains available to watch on demand.

Viewers looking to stream on Paramount+ can pick between an ad-supported Essential plan for $8.99 per month or an ad-free Premium tier for $13.99 per month, which includes live CBS and Showtime programming. A seven-day free trial is currently offered for the Premium tier.

Last Dinner: Prime Video’s Turkish romance whose director spent a year looking for another lead

Published on November 19, 2026

What's new

Prime Video is launching Last Dinner (released in Turkey as Son Yemek and marketed in Spain as La Última Cena), an original romance written and directed by Özcan Deniz. Produced by Net Yapım and Mahzen Media, the Turkish film had its premiere in the National Gala section at the 63rd International Antalya Golden Orange Film Festival before its global digital debut. A notable detail behind the scenes is that Deniz spent about a year searching for another actor to play the male lead. Avoidative of acting in his own directorial projects, he eventually accepted the part, later explaining that directing while performing opposite a scene partner was the primary challenge.

About the series

The story follows Sebahat, a pianist who saves a stranger named Bekir during a secret mission. The two become lovers before Bekir suddenly disappears. Years later, he resurfaces after Sebahat has already built a life with a husband. Framed through Sebahat's own memory as she shares the tale with her neighbors, the plot explores the boundary between past passion and present loyalty. The main cast includes:

  • Öykü Karayel as Sebahat
  • Özcan Deniz as Bekir (a mysterious hitman or agent)
  • Nejat İşler as Şef, the Chief
  • Tilbe Saran, Ceyda Düvenci, Gonca Vuslateri, Helin Kandemir, and Özgür Emre Yıldırım

What to expect

The feature incorporates a music-based narrative design tailored to its pianist protagonist, continuing Deniz's directorial work that began in the early 2010s. The film is an original fictional story running just over two hours. Global audiences can stream Last Dinner on Prime Video starting Thursday, November 19, 2026.

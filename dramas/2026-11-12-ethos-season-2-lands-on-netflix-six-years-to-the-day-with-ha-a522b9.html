Ethos Season 2 lands on Netflix six years to the day, with half the episodes

Published on November 12, 2026

Netflix’s Turkish ensemble drama Ethos (known in Türkiye as Bir Başkadır) is returning for a second season, written and directed once again by Berkun Oya.

What's new

The second season lands on Netflix on Thursday, November 12, 2026, exactly six years after the debut of the original season. The upcoming run features four episodes, which is half the length of the first season. Netflix accompanied the announcement with a single uncaptioned image depicting two women facing each other in armchairs, along with a brief logline: “Zeitgeist, unexpected encounters, and new conflicts.”

The cast list combines returning performers with new additions:

  • Returning cast: Öykü Karayel as Meryem, alongside Defne Kayalar, Fatih Artman, Funda Eryiğit, Gökhan Yıkılkan (Hilmi), Cemre Zişan Sağbır, Göktuğ Yıldırım, and Nur Sürer (Feray).
  • New cast members: Onur Saylak, Cihat Süvarioğlu, Oğulcan Arman Uslu, Eda Akalın, İdil Çelik, and series creator Berkun Oya. Specific roles for new cast members are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Noted absences: Tülin Özen (who played the therapist Gülbin) and Settar Tanrıöğen (who played the first season's hodja) do not appear on the announced list.

About the series

The first season focused on therapy sessions between Meryem, a part-time cleaner from a conservative family living on the edge of Istanbul, and Peri, a secular psychiatrist with prejudices against religious individuals. Their interactions linked a broad web of figures, including a hodja and his closeted daughter, a Kurdish family, an ex-soldier, a soap opera actress, and a depressed wealthy man. The series sparked extensive online debates across Türkiye concerning social divides and prejudice.

What to expect

The four-episode structure offers a more condensed run compared to the initial eight episodes. Since the first season aired, Oya wrote the film Stuck Apart and helmed projects including Cici and A Round of Applause. The second season of Ethos is highlighted as part of Netflix's 2026 lineup of nine Turkish productions.

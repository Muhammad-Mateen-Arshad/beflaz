Netflix Officially Confirms a Release Date for Its 'Arcane' Replacement

Published on November 08, 2026

Netflix has officially confirmed the premiere date for the return of its critically acclaimed adult animated series, Blue Eye Samurai. Following a three-year gap between seasons, Season 2 is scheduled to launch on January 19 with a six-episode run. Netflix has also greenlit a third season, enabling show creators Amber Noizumi and Michael Green to finish their planned three-season story arc by 2028.

What's new

The upcoming chapter introduces fresh settings and characters to the story. Specific streaming times and regional release schedules are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

  • Season 2 premiere date: January 19
  • Episode length/count: Six episodes
  • New voice cast members: Jonathan Pryce, Joel Kim Booster, and Sherry Cola
  • Future outlook: Confirmed Season 3 order leading to a 2028 finale

About the series

Originally debuting in November 2023, Blue Eye Samurai earned widespread critical praise and picked up four Emmy Awards. Created by writing partners Amber Noizumi and Michael Green, the show features meticulous historical research, live-action stunt choreography, and a distinct 2D-3D hybrid animation style produced by the Blue Spirit company. The narrative centers on Mizu (voiced by Maya Erskine), a relentless mixed-race samurai driven by revenge while searching for answers about her lineage as the daughter of a Japanese woman and a European man.

What to expect

Season 2 follows Mizu as she travels across the ocean to 17th-century London. Described by the creators as a dark, puritanical environment, London exposes Mizu to severe culture shock and oppressive social expectations. Meanwhile, back in Japan, the survivors left behind in the aftermath of Edo burning to ashes—including Ringo (Masi Oka), Princess Akemi (Brenda Song), Taigen (Darren Barnet), and Master Eiji (Cary-Hiroyuki Tagawa)—must navigate the resulting personal and political fallout.

Inside Guy Ritchie and Steven Knight’s TV turf war – and who wins when their hit shows go head to head

Published on October 11, 2026

Two prominent British creators, Guy Ritchie and Steven Knight, continue to dominate screen entertainment with high-profile streaming projects, historical dramas, and feature films. Known for their intense gangland thrillers, military sagas, and real-life stories, both writer-directors frequently share high-profile actors across their competing productions.

What's new

Guy Ritchie is expanding his presence on streaming platforms, releasing a second series of his Netflix crime comedy The Gentlemen, which has surpassed 90 million views. Additionally, Ritchie’s Paramount+ gangland drama MobLand returns this week for its second series, featuring a cast led by Tom Hardy, Helen Mirren, and Pierce Brosnan.

Meanwhile, Steven Knight has been tasked with writing the upcoming James Bond film. Knight is also preparing to bring back Peaky Blinders in 2027, following the release of his Disney documentary covering the 2025 Gallagher brothers reunion, titled Oasis: Don’t Look Back in Anger.

About the series

Both showrunners have developed projects across similar historical and crime genres:

  • Gangland & Period Dramas: Ritchie built his reputation on Lock, Stock And Two Smoking Barrels and Snatch, eventually bringing actors like Vinnie Jones into The Gentlemen. Knight launched the hit Peaky Blinders, the Victorian brewing dynasty story House Of Guinness, and the Disney+ drama A Thousand Blows, which was recently cancelled after two seasons.
  • World War II Stories: Knight authored the BBC One drama SAS Rogue Heroes detailing the origins of the SAS, while Ritchie co-wrote and directed the Special Operations Executive film The Ministry Of Ungentlemanly Warfare.
  • Spy & Crime Features: Ritchie directed the film reboot of The Man From U.N.C.L.E. alongside his Netflix documentary The Diamond Heist, whereas Knight is taking on the legendary James Bond franchise.

What to expect

Looking ahead, a third season of Ritchie's The Gentlemen is currently being made, and a third series of MobLand is also planned. For Steven Knight, viewers can expect the return of Peaky Blinders in 2027, as well as his Netflix movie The Immortal Man starring Stephen Graham.

The Kitchen positions Istanbul hub as strategic gateway to expanding Turkish content ecosystem

Published on November 09, 2026

Türkiye has completed a major transformation from a historical importer of media content into one of the world's leading exporters of original series. Today, Turkish productions reach audiences across Europe, Latin America, the Middle East, Africa, and Asia, fostering a highly sophisticated domestic dubbing and localization industry in the process.

What's new

To capitalize on this booming market, Emmy Award-winning and TPN Gold-badged localization studio The Kitchen has established a strategic hub in Istanbul. As part of this expansion, the studio appointed Berkay Tartici to the role of Manager of Global Strategy.

Company executives note that establishing an on-the-ground presence in Istanbul gives the studio a direct gateway into the local market, allowing for continuous collaboration with local Turkish creators, production ecosystems, and vendors.

What to expect

The company is driving a industry shift where localization becomes an integral part of early content strategy rather than just a final post-production step. Furthermore, the incorporation of artificial intelligence is streamlining workflows, reducing turnaround times that once required weeks down to just days.

The strategy focuses on pairing technology with human skill. While artificial intelligence handles repetitive scaling tasks, human experts provide critical editorial judgment, creative direction, cultural adaptation, and performance management.

Industry impact

Leadership at The Kitchen highlighted that Turkish television content is globally celebrated for its visual aesthetic, photographic portrayals of national history, compelling performances, and ability to introduce Turkish culture into homes worldwide.

Key background details regarding the localization studio include:

  • Headquartered in Miami with 25 years of industry experience.
  • Provides end-to-end localization services across all media formats and global languages.
  • Holds both Emmy Award recognition and TPN Gold status.

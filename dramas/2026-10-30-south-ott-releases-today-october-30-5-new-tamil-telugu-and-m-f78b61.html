South OTT Releases Today (October 30): 5 New Tamil, Telugu And Malayalam Movies And Shows To Stream

Published on October 30, 2026

Streaming platforms are featuring a lineup of new South Indian titles launching on October 30, bringing fresh releases across several genres including period crime, investigative thrillers, romantic fantasy, and supernatural horror to major services such as Netflix, SonyLIV, ZEE5, and JioHotstar.

What's new

Viewers can access five new movies and shows debuting on October 30 across streaming services:

  • Sigma: A Tamil action drama streaming on Netflix, directed by debutant Jason Sanjay (son of actor Vijay) and produced by Lyca Productions with music by Thaman. It stars Sundeep Kishan alongside Faria Abdullah, Raju Sundaram, Sampath Raj, and Shiv Panditt, featuring Telugu and Hindi audio tracks.
  • Akka: A 1980s period crime drama series on Netflix written and directed by Dharmaraj Shetty, starring Keerthy Suresh and Radhika Apte.
  • Rudravanam PS: A Telugu investigative series streaming on ZEE5 starring Naresh Agastya.
  • Love Always: A Tamil romantic fantasy on JioHotstar starring Gouri G Kishan and Ramzan Muhammed.
  • Immortal: A Tamil romantic horror film available on SonyLIV, directed by Mariyappan Chinna and starring GV Prakash Kumar and Kayadu Lohar.

About the series

Among the featured series, Akka is set in the fictional town of Pernuru during the 1980s and centers on a gold-smuggling ring run by women. Keerthy Suresh plays Bhavani, the head of the syndicate, while Radhika Apte plays Rudra, a rebel striving to unseat her, causing friction within the group.

In the Telugu crime series Rudravanam PS, Naresh Agastya plays Sub-Inspector Sivakumar. While investigating a village gold heist, his search for a missing person leads to the discovery of a headless body in the woods, dragging him into local power plays and hidden secrets.

What to expect

Other titles offer distinct storylines across genres. Love Always, adapted from the Korean webtoon "Here We Go Again," tells the story of a couple nearing separation who find themselves face-to-face with their past selves. The cast includes Gouri G Kishan, Ramzan Muhammed, Jayaprakash, Jayasudha, and Maya Krishnan.

The romantic horror title Immortal follows Magi, a printing press worker who accepts a low-rent room in a luxury apartment from a mysterious woman named Sindhu, only to face unsettling supernatural events.

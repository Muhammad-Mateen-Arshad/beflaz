Netflix’s 4-Part ‘Mindhunter’ Rival Is Now the World’s #1 Serial Killer Series

Published on November 22, 2026

What's new

A four-part Polish crime miniseries titled The Law of Beasts landed on Netflix on November 18 and quickly captured a global streaming audience. On FlixPatrol's list of Top 10 TV Shows for November 22, 2026, the show captured second place overall with 605 points, making it the highest-ranked serial killer drama on the platform. It trails only the supernatural horror series The Body, which leads with 788 points, while placing ahead of titles such as The Empress, Murder in Galicia, and Black Doves. The Polish miniseries has become the most-watched show in multiple nations, including France, Germany, Brazil, Greece, Poland, Hungary, and Belgium.

About the series

The production is inspired by the real-life case of Mariusz Trynkiewicz, who received a 25-year prison sentence after being convicted of murdering four young boys in 1989. His scheduled release in 2014 caused significant moral and legal debate in the country, prompting the creation of the controversial "Beast Law." This legislation permitted judicial authorities to place high-risk individuals into secure psychiatric facilities after completing their prison terms to ensure public protection.

What to expect

The storyline spans two separate eras and centers on the ideological fallout between two close acquaintances:

  • In 1988, Major Anna Bersz (Agnieszka Grochowska) heads the murder investigation and seeks help from psychiatrist Agnieszka Grabska (Agnieszka Żulewska) to assess the mental state of suspect Mariusz Trynkiewicz (Michał Pawlik). Their opposing views on his culpability strain their personal bond.
  • In 2014, Trynkiewicz's imminent freedom sparks public anger. Anna, now a prison director and government official, requests Agnieszka to evaluate whether Trynkiewicz still poses a public threat under the newly enacted law.
  • A central clash develops when Anna pushes to keep him detained, whereas Agnieszka refuses to compromise her professional code by providing a tailored psychiatric report just to keep him locked away.

All four episodes are currently available to stream worldwide on Netflix. Further broadcast updates are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

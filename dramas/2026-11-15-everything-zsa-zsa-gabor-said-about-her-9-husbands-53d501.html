Everything Zsa Zsa Gabor Said About Her 9 Husbands

Published on November 15, 2026

What's new

Relationship histories among public figures often draw attention, particularly given statistics indicating one divorce every 42 seconds in America. While stars like Drew Barrymore, Harrison Ford, and Kim Kardashian have been married three times, Zsa Zsa Gabor wed nine times throughout her life, with one marriage lasting only a single day.

About the series

Gabor first married at age 18. In the mid-1930s, when she was known as Sári and had little fame, she wed Burhan Belge, a Turkish diplomat and politician nearly 20 years her senior. Around this time, she was crowned Miss Hungary, though the title was taken away after she reportedly lied about her age. In her memoir, One Lifetime is Not Enough, Gabor described her marriage to Belge as loveless, but noted it provided an Excellency's wife status and a diplomatic passport to leave war-ravaged Europe. They divorced by 1941 when she left Europe for Tinseltown.

In Hollywood, Gabor married hotel magnate Conrad Hilton, who was 30 years her senior. Biographer Sam Staggs noted in Finding Zsa Zsa that Hilton married her against his better judgment. They faced a language barrier and had a daughter, Constance Francesca Hilton, in 1947 before divorcing that same year. Family friend Darwin Porter noted she received very little in the settlement.

What to expect

Gabor later starred in films like Moulin Rouge and Touch of Evil while continuing her personal journey:

  • In 1949, she married actor George Sanders, later describing him as the major love of her life despite divorcing in 1954.
  • She wed businessman Herbert L. Hutner in a low-key 1962 civil ceremony before divorcing in 1966.
  • She married Texan oil tycoon Joshua S. Cosden Jr. in 1966 after meeting him six weeks earlier, divorcing in 1967.

Fatih: Sultan of Conquests Season 3 is coming to STARZPLAY

Published on November 28, 2026

What's new

Season 3 of the Turkish historical drama Fatih: Sultan of Conquests is coming to STARZPLAY on Sunday, 29 November 2026. The upcoming season features 34 episodes, which will be accessible in the original Turkish language alongside Arabic subtitles and Arabic dubbing. In Arabic, the series carries the title «محمد: سلطان الفتوحات».

Whether STARZPLAY will drop all 34 episodes simultaneously or release them on a regular schedule has not been stated. Full episode rollout details: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

The storyline resumes eight years later, following Sultan Mehmed II in his continued efforts to expand the Ottoman Empire. His territorial ambitions lead to a direct confrontation with Vlad the Impaler in Wallachia. Beyond the battlefield, internal unrest threatens his household as a bitter rivalry breaks out between his sons, Bayezid and Mustafa, sparking a battle over the ultimate fate of the dynasty.

‘The Diplomat’ Season 4 Release Date, Time and Where to Watch the New High-Stakes Season

Published on October 17, 2026

What's new

The political drama series returns as season four of 'The Diplomat' officially premieres on Thursday, October 15, 2026. Netflix will release all eight episodes simultaneously around the world. Viewers in North America can stream the series starting at 12:00 AM PT or 3:00 AM ET, while British audiences can tune in at 8:00 AM BST.

The main cast expands with key updates and promotions:

  • Bradley Whitford and Allison Janney have been elevated to regular series cast members, reuniting alumni from 'The West Wing'.
  • Nana Mensah is promoted to regular status as White House Chief of Staff Billie Appiah.
  • David Gyasi, Rory Kinnear, Ato Essandoh, and Ali Ahn return to their respective roles.

Specific episode titles are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

Led by showrunner Debora Cahn, the streaming thriller centers on high-stakes political tradecraft and personal conflict. Filming for the fourth chapter expanded to international real-world settings, including locations in London, Tuscany, and Brooklyn, New York. For viewers seeking a refresher, the full previous library remains available to stream exclusively on Netflix.

What to expect

Season four picks up directly from the fallout of the previous finale surrounding a stolen Russian Poseidon nuclear weapon, leaving peace between the United States and the United Kingdom on the verge of breakdown. Ambassador Kate Wyler, portrayed by Keri Russell, uncovers deep corruption within Western government corridors.

Tensions rise within the executive branch as Hal Wyler serves as Vice President alongside President Grace Penn. When Hal engages in covert military plotting that threatens to start a war, Kate creates an unexpected partnership with First Gentleman Todd Penn. Together, the two spouses work to counter their partners' risky actions across an intense eight-episode arc.

First Look & Release Date For Netflix Period Drama ‘The Age of Innocence’

Published on November 11, 2026

Netflix has released first-look images and announced a February 2027 debut for its upcoming eight-episode period drama, The Age of Innocence. Production for the adaptation began in September 2025 and took place primarily in Prague, Czech Republic, before concluding in February 2026. Netflix has noted release dates of February 19, 2027, and February 28, 2027, for the show's arrival.

About the series

Based on Edith Wharton’s 1920 novel set in 19th-century New York, the adaptation is helmed by showrunner Emma Frost, who previously worked on The White Queen, The White Princess, and The Spanish Princess. Executive producers on the project include Peter Chernin, Jenno Topping, Tracey Cook from Chernin Entertainment, and Pavlina Hatoupis.

Wharton's original book was previously brought to the public as a 1928 Broadway play by Margaret Ayer Barnes, as well as a 1993 feature film directed by Martin Scorsese that starred Daniel Day-Lewis, Michelle Pfeiffer, and Winona Ryder.

What to expect

The plot centers on Newland Archer, a well-educated and wealthy lawyer who becomes enamored with Ellen Olenska. Ellen has recently returned to New York high society after fleeing her marriage to a wealthy Polish count. The series focuses on a romantic triangle while examining broader choices between personal desire, freedom, identity, duty, and social convention.

Main cast

The core cast announced for the series includes:

  • Camila Morrone as Ellen Olenska
  • Ben Radcliffe as Newland Archer
  • Kristine Froseth as May Welland
  • Margo Martindale as Mrs. Manson-Mingott
  • Fiona Glascott as Augusta Weiland
  • Belinda Bromilow as Adeline Archer
  • Emma Shipp as Janey Archer
  • Alice Grant as Gloria Winters
  • Jakub Volák as George Winters

7 Must-Watch Turkish Dramas On Netflix Packed With Romance And Emotional Turmoil

Published on October 15, 2026

About the series

Turkish dramas have built a massive fan following in India, with many popular titles readily accessible on Netflix in Hindi. Known for combining high-budget visual appeal with deep, relatable stories, these shows feature intense family dynamics, high-stakes romance, and dramatic plot twists set against breathtaking landscapes.

What to expect

Subscribers can explore a wide variety of genres, ranging from fast-paced workplace struggles to time-traveling historical mysteries:

  • As The Crow Flies: Follows a character named Asli in a fast-paced newsroom as she strives to reach the top, highlighting the clash between her lower-class origins and high society.
  • Old Money: Centers on Nihal, who returns from France to Istanbul to assist her debt-ridden father. Designing a yacht for the Bulut family leads to an enemies-to-lovers rivalry with Osman, a ruthless tycoon.
  • Love 101: A teen comedy-drama set in 1998 Istanbul spanning two seasons of eight episodes each, following a group of school friends navigating their teenage years while helping a teacher find love.
  • Şahmaran: A fantasy romance following psychology lecturer Şahsu to Adana, where she encounters a community worshipping a mythical half-woman, half-snake creature and meets Maran.
  • Midnight At The Pera Palace: A two-season series (featuring eight episodes in Season 1 and six in Season 2) where journalist Esra, played by Hazal Kaya, travels from a hotel portal to 1919 to stop an assassination plot against Atatürk.
  • Ayvalik Trip Series: Follows three friends traveling to a seaside town seeking an alternative cancer cure for one of them, forcing all three to face deep family trauma.

What's new

The streaming platform also features a hit romantic comedy series returning for its third season, structured with eight episodes per season filled with complicated relationships and fresh twists. From historical epics to romantic comedies, these Turkish streaming choices provide plenty of glamour and emotional drama for viewers looking for their next binge-watch.

Netflix Korea 10th Anniversary: Solo Leveling, Tantara, All Of Us Are Dead 2 - Actors, New K-Dramas At OTT Mega Event

Published on October 24, 2026

Netflix Korea celebrated its 10th anniversary with a special fan festival named Netflix Janchi, held at Seoul's Oil Tank Culture Park on October 24 and 25, 2026. Celebrating a decade since the service launched in South Korea in 2016, the event featured interactive attractions inspired by shows like Squid Game and Physical: 100, alongside musical performances and project announcements.

What's new

The showcase highlighted several upcoming Korean series and films, offering release windows and production details for fans:

  • Tantara: Also titled Slowly and Intensely, this drama stars Song Hye-Kyo, Gong Yoo, and Kim Seol-hyun in a story about ambition within the entertainment industry. It is set to premiere on Netflix on December 11.
  • Possible Love: Directed by Lee Chang-Dong, this film stars Sol Kyung-gu, Jeon Do-yeon, Jo In-sung, and Jo Yeo-jeong. It was released in select theaters on September 23 and reaches Netflix on November 6.
  • Grand Galaxy Hotel: A supernatural story about a hotel serving the souls of the dead, starring Lee Do-Hyun and Shin Si-a. It is expected to launch on Netflix in 2027.
  • Beauty in the Beast: Known in Korea as Wolf Girl, this series follows a werewolf played by Kim Min-Ju alongside Moon Sang-Min and Lomon. It is expected to arrive in 2027.
  • Solo Leveling: Byeon Woo-Seok appeared to introduce the live-action adaptation, confirming that filming is in progress. The show is not expected to arrive before late 2027.

What to expect

A major reveal from the event focused on the second season of All of Us Are Dead. Actor Yoon Chan-Young confirmed that his character, Lee Cheong-san, survived the events of the first season. The story will move beyond Hyosan to Seoul, promising a higher-scale battle against zombies with returning stars Park Ji-hu, Cho Yi-hyun, and Lomon. An exact release date was not specified in the source; please check the official notice, though the season is expected in early 2027.

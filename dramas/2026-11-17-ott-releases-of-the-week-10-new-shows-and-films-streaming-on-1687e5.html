OTT Releases Of The Week: 10 New Shows And Films Streaming On Netflix, Prime Video, JioHotstar And SonyLIV

Published on November 17, 2026

What's new

A fresh wave of titles is coming to digital platforms between November 17 and November 23. Viewers can watch new releases across Netflix, Prime Video, JioHotstar, SonyLIV, and Aha.

Several projects are scheduled to arrive on November 18:

  • Campus Diaries Season 2 (Prime Video): The Excel University group returns to deal with changing dynamics, romantic interest between Sudheer and Priyanka, and campus drama. The show can also be streamed for free on MX Player.
  • Madden (Prime Video): Directed by David O Russell, this biographical drama explores the life of American football figure John Madden, including his time with the Oakland Raiders, his broadcasting career, and the creation of his video game franchise.
  • The Odyssey (Prime Video): Christopher Nolan directs this adaptation of Homer's mythological tale following King Odysseus. It is available as a paid rental option even for active Prime subscribers.
  • Gordon (Netflix): A Spanish-language crime series based on a book by Marcelo Larraquy. Set in 1970s Argentina, it traces how thief Aníbal Gordon turned into a killer.
  • Mr & Mrs Office (JioHotstar): A lighthearted Tamil series debuting early in the week.

About the series

Additional projects are slated for release on November 20:

  • Steps (Netflix): An animated comedy from Cinderella's stepsisters' perspective. It marks the first feature film produced entirely in-house by Netflix Animation.
  • Dhindora Season 2 (Netflix): Bhuvan Bam reprises his role as Titu Mama across eight episodes as he handles family conflicts and a loss of 11 crore rupees.
  • Percy Jackson And The Olympians Season 3 (JioHotstar): The fantasy series returns with new Greek mythology challenges, following a weekly episode drop format.
  • Anbil Avan (SonyLIV): A Tamil romantic thriller centering on a married couple whose safety is endangered. It streams in Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, and Hindi.
  • Crazy Kalyanam (Aha): A regional film starring Anupama Parameswaran, offered in both Telugu and Tamil.

What to expect

The upcoming streaming lineup covers a variety of genres, including sports biopics, college dramas, Greek epics, and animated comedies. Half of the week's total debuts are concentrated on November 20, providing plenty of choices for viewers planning their watch lists.

‘Pride and Prejudice’ Netflix Series (2026) Cast & Characters, Episode Release Schedule & Where to Watch

Published on November 27, 2026

Netflix is set to launch a new six-part limited series adaptation of Jane Austen’s classic 1813 novel, Pride and Prejudice. All six episodes of the streaming drama will become available simultaneously on Thursday, December 3, 2026, for active subscribers.

What's new

Described as a faithful but fresh retelling, the show aims to deliver the original story's romance, humor, and social commentary to modern audiences. The adaptation is written by Dolly Alderton, with Euros Lyn directing the production. In addition to their creative roles, Alderton and Lyn serve as executive producers alongside Laura Lankester, Will Johnston, Louise Mutter, and star Emma Corrin. Lisa Osborne produces the show for Lookout Point.

About the series

Set in the English countryside, the plot focuses on the Bennet family as they navigate romance, money, social expectations, and parental pressure. Mrs. Bennet seeks wealthy suitors for her five daughters to secure their financial futures. When wealthy bachelors move nearby, Jane Bennet falls for Charles Bingley, while her sharp-witted sister Elizabeth clashes with Fitzwilliam Darcy. As the story unfolds, both Elizabeth and Darcy must rethink their initial assumptions about one another. Individual episode titles are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

The upcoming drama features a prominent ensemble cast portraying Austen's memorable characters:

  • Emma Corrin plays Elizabeth Bennet alongside Jack Lowden as Fitzwilliam Darcy.
  • Olivia Colman and Rufus Sewell star as Elizabeth's parents, Mrs. and Mr. Bennet.
  • Freya Mavor (Jane), Rhea Norwood (Lydia), Hopey Parish (Mary), and Hollie Avery (Kitty) play the remaining Bennet sisters, with Parish and Avery making their screen debuts.
  • Daryl McCormack appears as Charles Bingley, and Siena Kelly plays his sister, Caroline Bingley.
  • Louis Partridge acts as officer George Wickham, Jamie Demetriou portrays clergyman Mr. Collins, and Fiona Shaw plays Lady Catherine de Bourgh.

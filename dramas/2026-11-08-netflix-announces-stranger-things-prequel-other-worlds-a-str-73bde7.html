Netflix announces Stranger Things prequel Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure, set for 2028 release

Published on November 08, 2026

What's new

Netflix has officially announced a new prequel project expanding its major television franchise, titled Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure. The announcement was shared on November 6 during Stranger Things Day through an Instagram reveal. The upcoming series is scheduled to debut on the streaming platform in 2028.

About the series

Set in the 1950s, Other Worlds travels back several decades before the main narrative of the original series. The story focuses on supernatural elements and events that occurred before the Hawkins group began investigating secret conspiracies. It is also positioned prior to the events of Stranger Things: The First Shadow, the stage production set in 1959 that examines Henry Creel's past.

The show stands as the fourth overall project in the universe, following the original series—which wrapped up in 2025—alongside the animated show Stranger Things: Tales from '85 and the stage play. Development on this new spin-off began around 2022, and filming is set to take place in New Mexico.

What to expect

The production features a creative team led by Trefry, who serves as showrunner, co-creator, writer, and executive producer. Director Joe Wright will helm the first three episodes and act as an executive producer. Matt and Ross Duffer of Upside Down Pictures and Shawn Levy of 21 Laps are also attached as executive producers.

Specific details regarding the cast or an exact release date are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

7 new movies and TV shows to watch on Netflix, Disney+, and more this weekend (October 16) — VisionQuest, Sacrifice, and more

Published on October 16, 2026

What's new

A fresh selection of movies and streaming television options has arrived across major platforms, including Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, Apple TV, HBO Max, and Peacock. Viewers can explore choices ranging from comic book sagas and political thrillers to romantic dramas, historical recreations, and horror prequels.

About the series

Several notable television shows are debuting new episodes or full seasons:

  • VisionQuest: Available on Disney+, this release marks the conclusion of the Wanda and Vision small-screen trilogy. The story follows Vision while he is in hiding, coping with an identity crisis, and fleeing alongside Thomas Shepard to safeguard his advanced technology from hostile forces.
  • The Diplomat: Netflix has released all eight episodes of season 4. The political thriller explores a secret alliance between US President Grace Penn and Vice President Hal Wyler to secure a Russian nuclear weapon off the coast of England, while US Ambassador Kate Wyler continues her work.
  • Crystal Lake: Peacock has launched all eight episodes of this Friday the 13th prequel series. Led by showrunner Brad Caleb Kane, the narrative takes place eight years prior to the original movie and stars Linda Cardellini as Pamela Voorhees.

What to expect

For film lovers, several new titles are available to stream:

  • Sacrifice: A dark comedy action film on Netflix starring Chris Evans as an actor kidnapped at a gala by a radical group led by Anya Taylor-Joy's character, Joan. The movie received a limited launch day release in the United States and France.
  • Prime Video Romance Feature: Margaret Qualley stars as Maya, a nurse estranged from her mother, who falls in love with Charlie (Gabriel Basso) during the COVID-19 pandemic. (Film title: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice).
  • Tenzing: Streaming on Apple TV, this film stars Genden Phuntsok as Tenzing Norgay, detailing the historic expedition to summit Mount Everest alongside Edmund Hillary, played by Tom Hiddleston. Willem Dafoe also stars.
  • The Death of Robin Hood: This dark A24 adaptation on HBO Max features Hugh Jackman as an aging Robin Hood taking refuge in a priory alongside Sister Brigid, played by Jodie Comer.

Latest OTT releases (November 23-November 29): 17 new movies and TV shows on Netflix, Prime Video, JioHotstar and more

Published on November 23, 2026

What's new

The weekly lineup of digital releases from November 23 to November 29 features 17 new movies and television shows premiering across platforms including Netflix, Prime Video, and JioHotstar. Streaming audiences can explore a wide variety of genres, ranging from futuristic science fiction and historical war stories to workplace romances and medical dramas.

About the series

Several major television series headline this week's lineup. Sci-fi fans can watch Blade Runner 2099, an eight-episode limited series executive produced by Ridley Scott and overseen by showrunner Silka Luisa. Set fifty years after Blade Runner 2049, the story follows Hunter Schafer as Cora and Michelle Yeoh as Olwen through a transformed Los Angeles.

For workplace stories, viewers can tune into a seven-part Norwegian medical series starring Sara Khorami as surgeon Sofia Karimi navigating high-stakes emergency room triage. Meanwhile, a 12-episode Korean office romance adapted from The Rational Life follows Kang Yi-ji, a senior legal manager at Chronic Motors. Additionally, Amy Poehler and Michael Schur reunite for Excavations, a ten-episode archaeological comedy-drama starring Poehler as Elise and Hugh Laurie as her boss Neville.

What to expect

The latest arrivals also feature dramatic films and suspenseful narratives:

  • The Mosquito Bowl: A World War II feature directed by Peter Berg starring Nicholas Galitzine and Bill Skarsgård, depicting collegiate football stars serving as Marines on Guadalcanal in 1944.
  • Way of the Warrior Kid: Directed by McG, starring Jude Hill as a bullied youth named Marc and Chris Pratt as his uncle Jake, an injured Navy SEAL.
  • Intense political and personal plotlines in Dhai Chaal, focusing on campus student elections and cabinet schemes, alongside Rebecca Cutter's returning thriller featuring Brittany Snow and Malin Åkerman.

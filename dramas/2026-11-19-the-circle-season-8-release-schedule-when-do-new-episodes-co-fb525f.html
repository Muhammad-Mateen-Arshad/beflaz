“The Circle” Season 8 Release Schedule: When Do New Episodes Come Out?

Published on November 19, 2026

What's new

The eighth installment of the reality competition series has made its debut on Hulu following its seven-season run on Netflix. Season 8 launched on Tuesday, Nov. 17, introducing a refreshed near-real-time format filmed as the game unfolds. Host Tinx has stepped in to lead the series, taking over hosting duties from Michelle Buteau.

This revised version expands the overall length to 16 episodes, up from the 13 episodes produced during its previous run. Rather than dropping episodes in weekly batches, the show releases fresh content three days a week at 9 p.m. ET.

About the series

The revamped game features celebrity contestants competing alongside everyday players. Notable participants joining the cast include:

  • Hannah Brown from The Bachelorette
  • Sasha Farber from Dancing with the Stars
  • Layla Taylor from The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives
  • Lisa Vanderpump
  • Dominic DeAngelis

While the first seven seasons remain available to stream on Netflix, all episodes of the eighth season air exclusively on Hulu.

What to expect

Viewers can follow a regular broadcast schedule of new episodes airing on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays over five weeks. The season will conclude with a special finale on Friday, Dec. 18.

Interactive features have also been introduced to the competition. Following every Thursday broadcast, a 90-minute live audience voting window opens, allowing viewers to submit up to 10 votes per week to affect the game.

Filming Underway on Joshua Zetumer’s Netflix Thriller Series ‘Pagans’ & Cast Confirmed

Published on November 10, 2026

What's new

Filming is currently underway in New York for Pagans, a new supernatural thriller series coming to Netflix. Created by showrunner Joshua Zetumer (Say Nothing), the series is co-written by Zetumer alongside Clare Barron, Eboni Booth, Joe Murtagh, Olivia Dufault, and producer Victor LaValle. Principal photography began on October 26, 2026, and is scheduled to conclude by April 13, 2027.

Goddard Textiles is producing the series, with founder Drew Goddard (Project Hail Mary) and Sarah Esberg serving as executive producers, alongside Andrea Massaro as co-executive producer. The project is being developed through Netflix’s studio group headed by Nne Ebong. As for an exact release date or episode count, that is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

The supernatural drama centers on a widowed father who becomes the sole caretaker of his two children following the tragic death of his wife. While his son leads a typical life, his deeply troubled teenage daughter, Alice, constantly pushes boundaries. What starts as a strained relationship between father and daughter soon transforms into something far more sinister. Combining horror, emotion, and humor, the storyline explores the dark, unspoken thoughts that parents rarely express.

What to expect

The series features an ensemble cast of established actors and rising talents:

  • Sabine Grace portrays the boundary-pushing daughter, Alice, following prior roles in two shorts and Yo Gabba Gabbaland.
  • Steven Yeun, a veteran of Netflix projects including Beef and Animals.
  • Logan Kim appears as the son, marking his first Netflix project.
  • Paul Bettany (Amadeus) joined the cast on November 5th to play Mag.
  • Marin Ireland brings experience from The Umbrella Academy and The Irishman.
  • Intae Kim (Succession, Severance) makes his Netflix series debut.

With six months of production scheduled in New York, further announcements regarding the release timeframe are expected from Netflix at a later date.

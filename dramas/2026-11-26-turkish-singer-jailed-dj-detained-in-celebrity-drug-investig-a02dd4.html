Turkish singer jailed, DJ detained in celebrity drug investigation

Published on November 26, 2026

What's new

An extensive legal investigation in İstanbul targeting drug usage and possession has directly affected notable figures in the Turkish television sector. İstanbul prosecutors issued 35 detention warrants following digital forensic reviews of mobile devices and witness testimony, which included statements from confidential sources. Police initiated an operation on Tuesday evening, detaining 24 individuals before taking Yeter into custody, bringing the total number of detainees to 25. Suspects underwent medical examinations and drug testing before being brought to the Çağlayan courthouse in İstanbul on Wednesday.

Ten suspects were presented to a judge, while 14 others had their custody extended and were sent back to police headquarters for additional interrogation. The court released nine of the suspects while imposing international travel bans on them. As of Thursday, 15 people remained in custody, and outstanding warrants remained for 10 others.

About the series and actors involved

The suspects released under travel restrictions include prominent actors known for their work in acclaimed Turkish productions. Özcan Deniz, a singer and actor who starred in the drama "Bride of İstanbul," was among the nine released. Actresses Deniz Çakır and Burcu Özberk, who both appeared in the historical drama "Magnificent Century," were also freed under the travel ban. All three figures stated to prosecutors that they had not used drugs.

What to expect

The recent arrests are part of an expanded investigation that began in October 2025, initially concentrating on celebrities before spreading to the sports, business, and media sectors. In previous instances, authorities dropped investigations into public figures whose drug tests returned negative results. However, the ongoing probe has prompted intense discussions within the entertainment industry.

  • Concerns have mounted over potential reputational damage to television series and actors.
  • Industry discussions have emerged over whether mandatory drug testing prior to filming could breach performer privacy or lead to informal blacklisting.
  • Critics have raised questions about law enforcement focusing on high-profile users rather than devoting resources to combat large-scale drug trafficking.

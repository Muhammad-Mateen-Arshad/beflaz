10 Netflix Series To Watch Before Kicking the Bucket (#1 Is Actually Flawless)

Published on November 28, 2026

What's new

Netflix has fundamentally transformed modern television by making binge-watching the benchmark format for streaming platforms. While legacy networks like HBO initiated the era of prestige television through top-tier writing, Netflix elevated the medium by securing larger budgets, bigger stars, and broader global viewership. Its release strategy frequently turns obscure projects into massive pop culture phenomena driven by word-of-mouth momentum.

About the series

Several influential productions stand out as essential viewing on the platform:

  • Stranger Things: A monumental hit that revived widespread nostalgia for 1980s cinema. The series provided a long-term platform for its young cast—including key characters like the fiercely loyal and enthusiastic Dustin—to mature over its seasons.
  • BoJack Horseman: Running for six seasons, this uniquely dark animated satire tackles Hollywood alongside existential themes of addiction, loss, trauma, and sexual exploration through a deeply psychological lens.
  • The Crown: An ambitious historical drama examining the modern British monarchy through Queen Elizabeth II, who assumed power following the death of King George VI. The series covers her 70-plus years of leadership by dividing the narrative into three distinct two-season arcs, shifting the main cast as the figures age.
  • Squid Game: Originating in South Korea, this remains the most-watched show in the service's history. It centers on financially broken participants competing in lethal variants of children's games for a transformative cash prize.
  • Black Mirror: An ongoing science fiction anthology offering standalone, psychological warnings regarding rapid technological advancements and human cruelty.

What to expect

Viewers diving into these acclaimed series can expect a wide array of storytelling styles, from dark social thrillers and animated character studies to multi-generational royal chronicles.

“I Didn’t Kill My Baby”: Netflix Drops First Trailer for Melissa McCarthy’s JonBenét Ramsey Series

Published on November 24, 2026

Netflix has released the first trailer, main poster, and fresh promotional images for its upcoming limited series, The Murder of JonBenét Ramsey. The show is scheduled to launch globally on December 10th, 2026. The new footage highlights the immediate aftermath of December 26th, 1996, showcasing the initial 911 distress call, the discovery of the ransom note, and the intense pressure placed on the Ramsey family by investigators and the press.

What's new

The trailer offers a first extensive look at the footage, showing how quickly public suspicion focused on John and Patsy Ramsey. It captures key moments including polygraph inquiries, contaminated evidence concerns, and public media scrutiny. Alongside the video, new photos take viewers inside the Ramsey residence, behind police tape with the Boulder Police, into the detectives' bullpen, and to press conference setups.

About the series

The eight-episode limited drama examines the unsolved murder of six-year-old JonBenét Ramsey on Christmas night in 1996 and the reckoning that ensued. Each episode runs for roughly an hour. Although the narrative takes place in Boulder, Colorado, filming was conducted in Calgary, Alberta, from September 16th, 2024, to January 31st, 2025.

The project was initially created by 101 Studios for Paramount+, but Netflix obtained global rights on July 1st after the series was dropped during the Paramount-Skydance merger. Richard LaGravenese acts as showrunner, writer, and executive producer, while Anne Sewitsky serves as director and executive producer.

What to expect

The production features a deep ensemble cast portraying real-life figures from the case:

  • Melissa McCarthy as Patsy Ramsey
  • Clive Owen as John Ramsey
  • Emily Mitchell as JonBenét Ramsey
  • Garrett Hedlund as Det. Steve Thomas
  • Alison Pill as Det. Linda Arndt
  • Shea Whigham as Alex Hunter
  • Margo Martindale as Nedra Paugh
  • Tzi Ma as Dr. Henry Lee

Additional cast members include Owen Teague, Clifton Collins Jr., Angus Caldwell, Jaime Ray Newman, Rory Cochrane, Chris Bauer, Will Patton, Jeremy Bobb, John Billingsley, and Josh Stamberg. The specific roles for Kate Butler and Grace Cashman are Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

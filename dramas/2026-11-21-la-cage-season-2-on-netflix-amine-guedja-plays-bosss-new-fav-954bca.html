La Cage season 2 on Netflix: Amine Guedja plays Boss’s new favorite.

Published on November 21, 2026

Streaming fans have another major release to track as the French drama series La Cage prepares for its return. The second season brings fresh conflict, new characters, and expanded settings as the storyline delves back into the gym environment.

What's new

The second season of La Cage is scheduled to arrive on Netflix on December 16, 2026. The upcoming installment consists of five new episodes. Co-creator Franck Gastambide shares directing responsibilities for this second run alongside David Krespine.

Production for the new season moved across international locations, with the crew filming scenes in Marseille as well as Thailand. Gastambide gave viewers a glimpse behind the scenes by sharing images from Chiang Mai during production. About the series

Originally created by Franck Gastambide and Sylvain Caron, the series features both key returning performers and new additions to the roster:

  • Franck Gastambide returns to play Boss, the coach placing his trust in the gym's newest standout.
  • Amine Guedja joins the cast as Curtis, the fighter who becomes Boss’s primary focus.
  • Bosh reprises the role of Ibrahim, whose arrival at the club previously enabled Taylor (played by Melvin Boomer) to establish his reputation in the first season.
  • Edwige Ahonto returns to her role as Elena.
  • Adel Bencherif joins the credits, re-teaming with Gastambide after previously appearing as Mounir in the French rap drama series Validé.
  • Sawsan Abes and Steve Tientcheu also feature among the credited cast for the five episodes.

What to expect

The dynamic inside Boss’s gym takes a central focus in season two. Curtis emerges as the new favorite cheered on by the crowd, securing the coach's full backing. However, occupying the top position brings immediate pressure, as Curtis must navigate the friction and jealousy that his top billing creates.

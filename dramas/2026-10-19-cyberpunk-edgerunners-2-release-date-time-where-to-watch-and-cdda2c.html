‘Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2’ Release Date, Time, Where to Watch and Preview

Published on October 19, 2026

Netflix has officially confirmed the premiere details for Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 during its main stage panel at Anime NYC in the Javits Center. The follow-up to the acclaimed 2022 anime series will be available globally exclusively on Netflix.

What's new

Viewers can mark their calendars for Tuesday, October 20, 2026. Instead of releasing on a weekly schedule, all ten episodes will drop at once. The full season arrives at midnight Pacific time, which corresponds to 11:00 a.m. GST for audiences in the UAE. Production on the sequel began roughly a year ago after its initial reveal in July 2025.

About the series

The second season delivers an independent storyline rather than continuing the arc of David Martinez and Lucy. Framed as a brutal story centered on redemption and revenge, the production reunites key creative figures alongside animation house Studio Trigger:

  • Kai Ikarashi directs the project after previously helming an episode in the original series.
  • Bartosz Sztybor returns to handle showrunner, story writer, and producer duties.
  • Masahiko Otsuka writes the screenplay.

A landing page has already appeared on Netflix in Ukraine; however, details regarding regional language support are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

A newly revealed trailer introduces fresh footage, character dialogue, and a prominent VHS aesthetic, teasing that Night City will burn again. The upcoming narrative focuses on four main figures:

  • Roman Carax, a young film fan fascinated by analog media who watches his companions fall.
  • King, who is dealing with worsening cyberpsychosis.
  • D, a grief-driven netrunner seeking vengeance.
  • Talia Yang.

The storyline also introduces Weak Kingsley, a character trying to survive without cybernetic enhancements, voiced by Kentaro Tone and Clancy Brown.

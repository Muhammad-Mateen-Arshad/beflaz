Best Non-English TV Shows to Watch Right Now

Published on October 25, 2026

Television viewers can explore a wide array of non-English series produced by creative teams across Europe, Asia, Latin America, and the Middle East. These shows encompass historical epics, high-concept thrillers, crime sagas, and workplace dramedies that showcase talent from around the globe.

About the series

A variety of international dramas offer distinct storylines, creative structures, and strong ensemble casts:

  • La casa de papel: Created by Álex Pina, this Spanish heist series follows a strategist known as the Professor who recruits specialized teams to rob national institutions.
  • Dark: A German drama created by Baran bo Odar and Jantje Friese that centers on four families, time travel, and interconnected eras in the town of Winden.
  • Squid Game: Created by Hwang Dong-hyuk, this South Korean drama features participants competing in lethal children's games overseen by masked staff.
  • Kingdom: A South Korean historical thriller set in the Joseon period, written by Kim Eun-hee, combining palace politics with an outbreak that reanimates the dead.
  • Borgen: Created by Adam Price, this Danish political drama details the career of Birgitte Nyborg through coalition building and cabinet negotiations.

What to expect

Additional standout productions feature unique local contexts and compelling narratives:

  • Gomorrah: An Italian crime saga based on Roberto Saviano's book, detailing territory battles, trafficking routes, and leadership changes within the Savastano clan.
  • Dix pour cent: A French workplace dramedy created by Fanny Herrero that follows talent agents handling contract negotiations and client crises at the ASK agency.
  • Bron/Broen: A Scandinavian crime show created by Hans Rosenfeldt involving cross-border police coordination after a body is discovered on the Øresund Bridge.
  • Babylon Berlin: A German series depicting Weimar-era political conspiracies and criminal investigations led by Gereon Rath and Charlotte Ritter.

What's new

Many of these titles incorporate specialized production methods, such as location shooting, parallel timelines, and unique character naming systems. For instance, the Danish political saga expanded its international scope with a 2022 continuation titled 'Borgen – Power & Glory'.

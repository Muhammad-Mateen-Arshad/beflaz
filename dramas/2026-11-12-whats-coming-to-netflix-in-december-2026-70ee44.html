What’s Coming to Netflix in December 2026

Published on November 12, 2026

What's new

Netflix has announced a varied slate of titles arriving in the United States throughout December 2026. The upcoming lineup spans original feature films, live sporting broadcasts, and a selection of international streaming shows. Exact release dates for individual titles and details regarding prospective Turkish historical dramas are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

Streaming audiences can look forward to several newly added episodic titles across multiple genres:

  • Pride and Prejudice: A six-part limited adaptation written by Dolly Alderton and directed by Euros Lyn. Emma Corrin stars as Elizabeth Bennet alongside Jack Lowden as Mr. Darcy, with Rufus Sewell and Olivia Colman appearing as Mr. and Mrs. Bennet.
  • Trinity (Season 1): Created by Jed Mercurio, this dramatic thriller stars Gugu Mbatha-Raw as Commander Katherine Decker, second-in-command of a nuclear submarine. The plot connects her to US Secretary of Defense Webb Preston, played by Richard Madden, as she uncovers a global threat. The cast also features Marcia Gay Harden and JD Pardo.
  • Empress of Flames (Season 1): A Thai drama series centered on a fallen princess of royal blood who seeks revenge to reclaim her throne, starring Pimchanok Luevisadpaibul and Prin Suparat.
  • Collect & Praise (Season 1): A semi-scripted comedy series starring Druski as a pastor navigating church scandals alongside his deacon.
  • A Filipino Christmas (Season 1): A holiday anthology series focusing on family, friendships, and personal relationships.

What to expect

The December schedule features global narrative projects such as La Bola Negra, a Spanish epic covering nearly a century of history through three generations of men, starring Penélope Cruz, Glenn Close, Miguel Bernardeau, and Guitarricadelafuente. Other international additions include the Italian comedy The Good Boss, the Indonesian drama Secrets, and the romantic comedy My Crazy Feminist Girlfriend starring Mei Nagano. Unscripted offerings arriving during the month include the Brazilian docu-series Jão: Tales from the Void and World War II: Behind Enemy Lines, narrated by Josh Brolin.

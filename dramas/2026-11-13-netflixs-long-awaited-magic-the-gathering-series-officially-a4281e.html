Netflix’s Long-Awaited ‘Magic: The Gathering’ Series Officially Gets a First Look and Release Window

Published on November 13, 2026

What's new

Netflix has unveiled a first-look image and a development diary for its long-awaited Magic: The Gathering animated project, officially titled Planeswalker. The streaming platform confirmed that the series is scheduled to premiere in 2027.

The path to production spans several years. Netflix first announced an animated adaptation back in 2019 with Joe and Anthony Russo attached to the project. The streamer later restructured the project in 2024 by introducing a brand-new creative team.

About the series

Netflix is developing the project in partnership with Hasbro Entertainment and Wizards of the Coast. Key creative figures behind the show include:

  • Co-Showrunners: Terry Matalas and Cindy Appel
  • Supervising Director: Patrick Osborne
  • Animation: DIGIC

The story focuses on Chandra, a pyromancer who finds herself hunted due to her formidable powers. In order to prevent a massive war that threatens to engulf thousands of worlds across the multiverse, she must form alliances with other planeswalkers.

What to expect

In established lore, Chandra Nalaar first transported off her home plane when her planeswalker spark ignited just before a near-fatal event, landing her at a monastery where she could master her fire abilities. The animated series provides space to explore her growth between the milestones known to card players, such as her shift from "Chandra, Fire of Kaladesh" into "Chandra, Roaring Flame," while dealing with the reality of being targeted for her gifts.

For details regarding voice cast members, episode counts, or specific premiere dates, these were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

CEO Talks: Beymen’s Elif Çapçı on Why Turkey’s Luxury Consumer Is Defying the Global Slowdown

Published on November 11, 2026

What's new

Beymen Tersane in Istanbul celebrated its first anniversary by unveiling a specialized exhibition titled "Timeless Treasures — The Eternal Journey of the Bag." Curated by Laurence Benaïm with immersive midnight blue set design by Bureau Betak, the display showcases 80 luxury bags, student creations from the Beymen Young Talents Design Competition, and a historical 1886 Louis Vuitton wardrobe trunk crafted for Sultan Abdülhamid II. The opening festivities included a welcome dinner attended by creative directors Alessandro Michele and Antonin Tron, alongside a celebration featuring a concert by Diana Ross.

About the venue

Occupying a 600-year-old former Ottoman naval shipyard in the Golden Horn, the 130,000-square-foot multibrand retail space was designed by Rem Koolhaas' OMA agency under Qatari investment firm Mayhoola for Investments. The project required an architectural approach that preserved the structure's historical walls while building nine interconnected fashion galleries facing the Bosphorus and Balat. Beymen Group CEO Elif Çapçı highlighted that establishing a compelling "drama effect" within the space is crucial for physical multibrand locations to go beyond traditional transactional shopping.

What to expect

Visitors to the site can explore curated artwork, custom furniture, dedicated pop-up spaces, four VIP shopping suites, the Bardot Bleu restaurant, and a coffee shop run by Golden Goose. The temporary handbag exhibition is scheduled to remain open until February 6.

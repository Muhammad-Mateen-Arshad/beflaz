What’s New on Netflix

Published on November 15, 2026

What's new

Streaming audiences have a wide array of options arriving on Netflix this weekend. The lineup features the final season of The Empress alongside all six seasons of Power, accompanied by a varied roster of international television series and films. Specific drop times and full release schedules are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

Drama enthusiasts can explore several notable scripted shows added to the platform's streaming catalog:

  • The Painter of Wind (Season 1): Set in 18th-century Korea, this 20-episode historical drama focuses on a talented art student and mentor unraveling a dangerous murder mystery a decade after two painters were killed. The cast includes Park Shin-yang, Ryu Seung-ryong, and Bae Soo-bin.
  • Mask (Season 1): A 20-episode Korean drama starring Soo Ae, Ju Ji-hoon, and Yeon Jeong-hun, centering on Byun Ji-sook, who seizes a chance to transform her debt-ridden life upon meeting a wealthy woman who looks identical to her.
  • The Chaser (Season 1): A 16-episode Korean crime and action series featuring Son Hyeon-ju, Kim Sang-jung, and Kim Sung-ryung, following a detective who uncovers a political cover-up after his teenage daughter dies in a hit-and-run.
  • Iryû: Team Medical Dragon (Season 3): A 10-episode Japanese drama starring Kenji Sakaguchi, Teppei Koike, and Sadao Abe that follows a genius surgeon uncovering corruption inside a university hospital.
  • Murder in Galicia: A 3-episode Spanish limited series inspired by true events, starring Tristán Ulloa, Carlos Blanco, Fer Fraga, Miguel Borines, and Luisa Merelas.

What to expect

Beyond traditional dramas, the weekend update brings unscripted series and feature films from around the globe. Reality choices include Season 2 of the United Arab Emirates series Love Is Blind: Habibi and South Africa's Stars and Vows: Anele & Buzza. Movie options range from the French action-comedy Brawlers and the American mystery Heartland to Korean romantic comedy-dramas like Modern Farmer and Shining Inheritance.

5 best new Netflix movies and shows this week (Nov. 16-22)

Published on November 16, 2026

What's new

Netflix has announced a fresh lineup of movies and shows arriving between November 16 and November 22. The upcoming schedule includes a blend of original feature films, holiday releases, unscripted competitions, and dramatic limited series.

About the series

Subscribers seeking serialized drama can watch "The Altruists," arriving on November 19. Starring Julia Garner as Caroline Ellison and Anthony Boyle as Sam Bankman-Fried, the limited series dramatizes the true story behind the FTX crypto exchange collapse and the white-collar crimes surrounding it. Viewers can also look out for international series, including the Polish show "The Law of Beasts," which explores the moral conflict between an investigator and a psychiatrist over a convicted murderer's impending release, and the Brazilian series "Valentina and the 12 Signs."

For unscripted streaming, the competitive reality series "Win the Mall" arrives on November 18. Hosted by Haley Baylee, the show pits 10 expert shoppers against one another in a shopping mall for a large cash prize, featuring guest appearances from Jonathan Van Ness, Salish Matter, and Lilly Singh.

What to expect

The platform's film selections for the week span multiple genres:

  • Obsession (Nov. 16): A low-budget horror-comedy directed by Curry Barker. The plot follows music shop employee Bear (Michael Johnston), whose magical wish for his colleague Nikki (Inde Navarrette) to return his feelings causes sinister outcomes.
  • Guarding Stars (Nov. 18): A cozy seasonal romance based on Katherine Center's novel "The Bodyguard." It stars Leighton Meester as bodyguard Hannah Brookes and Jared Padalecki as movie star Jack Stapleton, who pretend to be a couple at a Montana ranch to dodge a stalker.
  • Steps (Nov. 20): An animated fantasy retelling of Cinderella focused on her stepsister Lilith, who accidentally turns her sister into a frog. The voice cast includes Ali Wong, Amanda Seyfried, Daniel Radcliffe, Peter Dinklage, and Bette Midler.

Additional releases for the week include the comedy special "Mike Ward: Modest." Specific episode counts and global premiere times are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

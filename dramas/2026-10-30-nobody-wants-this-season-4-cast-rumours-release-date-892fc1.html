Nobody Wants This Season 4: Cast, Rumours & Release Date

Published on October 30, 2026

What's new

The popular Netflix romantic comedy Nobody Wants This has recently returned with its third season. The new episodes focus on two central couples and have performed strongly on the platform, pulling in 6.2 million views in a single week and taking the number one spot in eleven countries.

Despite these high numbers and online buzz, an official renewal for a fourth season has not been announced yet. Not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Netflix generally evaluates viewing metrics over time before deciding whether to continue a show. If the series is renewed, season 4 could potentially arrive in fall 2027. Full details regarding an official cast list for a fourth season are also not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

Nobody Wants This follows the romantic connection between Joanne, an agnostic podcaster, and Noah, a progressive rabbi. After meeting at a dinner party, the two develop an unexpected bond. Their journey involves navigating contrasting religious backgrounds, Noah's rabbinate future, Joanne's hesitation over major life changes, and the opinions of their friends and relatives.

The overarching story also involves Joanne's sister Morgan and Noah's brother Sasha. In the third season, several developments unfold:

  • Joanne and Noah cohabitate and commit to building a future together.
  • Joanne studies Judaism intending to convert.
  • Noah works to regain his place at Temple Chai.
  • Morgan and Sasha form a closer relationship following Sasha's separation from his wife, Esther.

What to expect

By the time season 3 wraps up, one couple is thriving while the other experiences a rough patch. If season 4 is greenlit, the storyline is anticipated to pick up from these events and follow the characters as they handle whatever comes next.

Fans of the show can also explore similar romantic series on Netflix, such as Bridgerton, Emily in Paris, My Life with the Walter Boys, Finding Her Edge, and Virgin River.

Cyberpunk 2077 announces a major update that will include content from Edgerunners 2 and highly anticipated new features

Published on October 20, 2026

What's new

CD Projekt RED has announced a major free update for its futuristic RPG, Cyberpunk 2077. Designated as version 2.5, this upcoming release introduces content directly tied to the new anime Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2. In addition to tie-in elements, the patch will deliver several highly requested gameplay features.

Key features coming in the update include:

  • Expanded cyberware customization options
  • A new combat style enabling players to wield a weapon in each hand
  • Free content inspired by the streaming anime series

About the series

The update arrives on the heels of the premiere of Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 on Netflix. Created by CD Projekt in collaboration with animation studio TRIGGER, known for titles such as Kill la Kill and Delicious in Dungeon, the project features Kai Ikarashi at the helm.

This new anime takes a complete departure from the original series, shifting to a new narrative where revenge takes a back seat. Visually, the production highlights superb animation, combining a striking mix of artistic styles to create a varied, bold, and daring visual experience on screen. Specific details regarding cast members or episode counts are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

CD Projekt RED will reveal further details about the update during an upcoming RED Streams broadcast. Viewers can watch the showcase on YouTube and Twitch on Wednesday, October 21, at 3:00 PM ET / 12:00 PM PT.

An exact launch date for the version 2.5 update is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

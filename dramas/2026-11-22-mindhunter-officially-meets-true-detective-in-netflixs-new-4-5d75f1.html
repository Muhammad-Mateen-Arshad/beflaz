‘Mindhunter’ Officially Meets ‘True Detective’ in Netflix’s New 4-Part Crime Thriller

Published on November 22, 2026

What's new

Netflix has officially released all four episodes of the Polish crime thriller series The Law of Beasts, which premiered on November 18. Directed by Kasper Bajon, who co-wrote the project alongside Anna Kasińska, the mini-series spans roughly three hours in total length, offering a single-evening watch for fans of dark investigative drama.

About the series

The narrative centers on two fictional female leads navigating a legal and criminal case inspired by real events in Poland. In 1988, investigator Anna (Agnieszka Grochowska) works to identify the person responsible for murdering four boys in Piotrków Trybunalski. At the same time, psychiatrist Agnieszka Grabska (Agnieszka Żulewska) is brought in to assess the mental state of suspect Mariusz Trynkiewicz (Michał Pawlik).

Trynkiewicz received a death sentence in 1989, but a national amnesty later reduced his punishment to 25 years in prison. The timeline shifts to 2014 as his prison sentence comes to an end. Anna, now working as a prison director, must coordinate with Agnieszka to re-evaluate whether Trynkiewicz poses a ongoing risk to the public under Poland's newly enacted "Beasts Act." Creators Bajon and Kasińska chose to focus on these two women after research revealed how many female figures held critical decision-making responsibilities during the historical case.

What to expect

Viewers can expect a focused character study built around institutional decision-making and a past crime that spans several decades.

  • Main Cast: Agnieszka Grochowska as Anna, Agnieszka Żulewska as Agnieszka, and Michał Pawlik as Trynkiewicz.
  • Production Team: Directed by Kasper Bajon and written by Kasper Bajon alongside Anna Kasińska.
  • Format: A complete four-episode crime thriller now available on streaming.

For details regarding secondary distribution or potential future projects: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

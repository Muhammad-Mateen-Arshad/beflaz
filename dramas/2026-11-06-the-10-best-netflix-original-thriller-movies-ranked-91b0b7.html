The 10 Best Netflix Original Thriller Movies, Ranked

Published on November 06, 2026

What's new

Netflix continues to maintain a strong presence among modern streaming services by distributing a large volume of digital media. The platform has built a notable library within the thriller genre, offering everything from fast-paced action to slow-building crime and psychological stories created by both rising talent and established filmmakers.

About the series

Several original streaming thrillers stand out for their suspenseful narratives and distinct themes:

  • Woman of the Hour: Anna Kendrick makes her directorial debut in this 1970s serial killer film based on a real-life event. Kendrick stars as a contestant on The Dating Game playing alongside Daniel Zovatto, who portrays the serial killer Rodney. The project highlights power dynamics and systemic ignorance.
  • Gerald's Game: Directed by Mike Flanagan and adapted from Stephen King's novel, this psychological thriller stars Carla Gugino as Jessie and Bruce Greenwood as Gerald. After Gerald dies of a sudden heart attack during an isolated holiday trip, Jessie remains handcuffed to a bed to battle her inner demons.
  • I'm Thinking of Ending Things: Written and directed by Charlie Kaufman, this film follows a young woman (Jessie Buckley) traveling with her boyfriend (Jesse Plemons) to meet his parents (Toni Collette and David Thewlis). The story combines their journey with footage of a school janitor (Guy Boyd) to explore themes of pain and humanity.
  • The Devil All the Time: A post-World War II period crime thriller focused on the town of Knockemstiff, Ohio. Arvin Russell (Tom Holland) works to protect his family against evil forces, featuring Robert Pattinson as a manipulative Southern priest named Reverend Preston Teagarden.
  • The Night Comes for Us: An Indonesian martial arts project featuring talent from The Raid series. It stars Joe Taslim as an elite Triad assassin targeted by former employers after saving a young girl.

What to expect

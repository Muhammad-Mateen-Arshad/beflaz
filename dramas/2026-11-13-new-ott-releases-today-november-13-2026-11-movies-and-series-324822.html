New OTT Releases Today (November 13, 2026): 11 movies and series on Netflix, JioHotstar, Prime Video, ZEE5 and more

Published on November 13, 2026

Streaming platforms have added a fresh selection of titles, offering entertainment options across multiple languages and genres ranging from period action to true-crime investigations and campus mysteries.

What's new

The latest arrivals span services including Netflix, Prime Video, ZEE5, Apple TV, and ManoramaMAX. The lineup brings a variety of storytelling formats to digital screens, featuring multi-episode series, feature-length films, and real-world documentary projects.

About the series

Several notable series and major feature films are now available for home viewing:

  • Music Industry Mystery: Jessica Chastain stars as Misty Jones, a former country music icon who travels to Nashville after her rising-star niece disappears. Directed by Shana Feste, the project co-stars Ross Lynch, Garrett Hedlund, John Hawkes, and Paul Sparks, alongside an accompanying soundtrack release.
  • Spanish True-Crime: A Spanish limited drama explores a Civil Guard sergeant investigating a violent home invasion while turning focus toward the victim's husband. Subscribers can also pair it with the Netflix documentary Unprotected: Murder in Galicia.
  • Campus Thriller: Produced by Dharmatic Entertainment, a six-episode dark campus story features Shanaya Kapoor in a dual role alongside Divik Sharma, Manya Grover, Mannat Duggal, Sheeba Chaddha, and Harsh Chhaya.
  • Period Action Drama: Director Rahul Sankrityan delivers a large-scale Telugu period action film led by Vijay Deverakonda and Rashmika Mandanna.

What to expect

Subscribers can also watch Preity Zinta star as Madam H on Prime Video in a cast that includes Vanshika Dhir, Yashpal Sharma, and Dinesh Lal Yadav. Meanwhile, a September 18 theatrical release inspired by the 2019 Hyderabad case has transitioned to Netflix.

Regional options feature the Malayalam movie Bandra on ZEE5, a separate Malayalam title on ManoramaMAX, and a Bengali continuation series on ZEE5. Those looking for lighter content can stream a small-community romance centered on local confusion when police search a mansion for a woman named Manu Joshi, or turn to Apple TV for the documentary Being Heumann.

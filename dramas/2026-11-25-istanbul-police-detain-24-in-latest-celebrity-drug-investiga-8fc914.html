İstanbul police detain 24 in latest celebrity drug investigation

Published on November 25, 2026

What's new

Turkish police in İstanbul have detained 24 people, including well-known television actors, singers, presenters, and business leaders, as part of an ongoing celebrity drug probe reported on Wednesday. İstanbul prosecutors issued detention warrants for 35 individuals on suspicion of drug possession or use, citing confidential witness testimony and forensic examinations of seized digital devices. While 24 suspects were brought to an İstanbul courthouse, law enforcement continues to search for the remaining 11 individuals.

About the stars involved

The recent sweep has drawn attention due to the inclusion of several prominent figures from popular Turkish television productions:

  • Özcan Deniz, singer and actor who starred in the drama series "Bride of İstanbul."
  • Deniz Çakır and Burcu Özberk, actresses who both featured in "Magnificent Century," a historical drama set during the reign of Ottoman Sultan Süleyman.
  • Murat Ceylan and Murat Başoğlu, television presenters.
  • Cansu Taşkın, model and actress.
  • İbrahim İzzet Özilhan, business executive serving as vice president of TÜSİAD and board member of Anadolu Group.

What to expect

This action is part of a broader wave of celebrity drug investigations that began in İstanbul in October 2025, expanding across figures in music, acting, media, sports, and business. In earlier phases, most detainees were released after questioning and providing hair and blood samples for toxicology testing, with investigations dropped when tests returned negative results.

Despite clears, the ongoing probes have caused significant distress throughout Turkey's entertainment sector due to lingering damage to public reputations. In light of the situation, some television producers have considered requiring mandatory drug tests for actors prior to filming, prompting concerns over privacy rights and blacklisting. Further details on upcoming court decisions are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Netflix Greenlights New Polish Period Drama Series ‘Nights and Days’ as Follow-Up to ‘The Doll’

Published on October 16, 2026

Following the successful adaptation of Bolesław Prus's 19th-century epic The Doll, Netflix is expanding its lineup of Polish historical dramas. The streaming service has greenlit Nights and Days, a new prestige period series based on Maria Dąbrowska's celebrated literary work. The production is expected to debut on Netflix in the second half of 2027.

What's new

The series stars Tomasz Schuchardt and Jaśmina Polak in the main roles. Behind the scenes, Kamila Tarabura serves as director and co-writer, partnering on the script with writers Marta Bacewicz and Katarzyna Tybinka. Justyna Pawlak is managing the production via Greenlight Films.

About the series

This upcoming project reimagines Dąbrowska’s iconic four-part chronicle for modern viewers. It is the first major screen adaptation of the classic novel in approximately fifty years, following the famous film and television versions released in the 1970s. Details regarding the exact episode count are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

The storyline takes place during a period when Poland did not appear on European maps. It focuses on Barbara Ostrzeńska, a proud noblewoman facing poverty who discovers her first love has married another woman. To avoid remaining single, she marries Bogumił Niechcic, a humble estate manager and former insurgent.

Spanning 30 years in the scenic countryside, the narrative explores their life together as Barbara's inner longing for romance and status clashes with daily realities. Director Tarabura noted that the series provides an emotional look at long-term relationships, the labor required to maintain a partnership, and the contrast between romantic myths and reality, showing how Barbara eventually uncovers unexpected peace within her life.

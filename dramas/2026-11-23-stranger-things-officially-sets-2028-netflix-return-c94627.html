‘Stranger Things’ Officially Sets 2028 Netflix Return

Published on November 23, 2026

Netflix is expanding its hit franchise with a newly announced spin-off series set inside the universe created by Matt and Ross Duffer.

What's new

Netflix announced via social media that the franchise's first major live-action offshoot, titled Other Worlds (also called Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure), is officially targeted for a 2028 release. However, this launch date could shift to 2029 if creators Matt and Ross Duffer focus first on their separate untitled event film at Paramount, which is currently set to open on November 3, 2028. The Duffers are also reportedly working on a separate project based on their 2014 short film, Vessel.

About the series

The original flagship series debuted in 2016, beginning as a small-town mystery centered on a missing boy, mysterious government experiments, paranormal threats, and an unusual young girl. The main show concluded its final season at the end of 2025, releasing in three distinct parts. The franchise previously aired two seasons of an offshoot titled Stranger Things: Tales From ‘85, though it drew lower viewership than the flagship program. Fans hoping for direct character spin-offs will not see them, as the Duffer brothers and actor David Harbour previously ruled out a Hopper and Joyce project.

What to expect

The upcoming spin-off branches off into uncharted territory within the established world:

  • New Storyline: It introduces an original tale set inside the broader universe rather than continuing the original series.
  • New Characters: The production will star an entirely new cast, with no original series actors expected to appear in the early stages.
  • Specific Plot and Cast Details: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

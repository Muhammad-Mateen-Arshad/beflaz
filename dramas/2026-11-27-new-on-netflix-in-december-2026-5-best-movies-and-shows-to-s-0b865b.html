New on Netflix in December 2026: 5 best movies and shows to stream, plus full release list

Published on November 27, 2026

Streaming platforms are expanding their line-up for December 2026 with a broad selection of international period sagas, literary adaptations, and major project finales.

What's new

Netflix is debuting several notable movies, narrative shows, and documentaries throughout the month. Highlights include sweeping multi-generational period features, classic romance adaptations, high-profile thrillers, and concluding chapters for popular series.

About the series

Key dramatic productions arriving on the service include:

  • La Bola Negra (Dec. 2): Directed by Javier Calvo and Javier Ambrossi, this Spanish period drama is inspired by an unfinished project by Federico García Lorca. Jumping across 1932, 1937, and 2017, the storyline focuses on three gay men connected by secrets, love, and loss. The title earned Best Director at Cannes, the People's Choice Award at TIFF, and will represent Spain at the 2027 Oscars.
  • Pride and Prejudice (Dec. 3): A lavish new adaptation of Jane Austen's novel starring Emma Corrin as Elizabeth, Daryl McCormack as Mr. Bingley, Jack Lowden as Mr. Darcy, and Freya Mavor as Jane. Olivia Colman, Rufus Sewell, and Fiona Shaw also feature in the cast.
  • Emily in Paris Season 6 (Dec. 24): The sixth and final season sees Emily Cooper (Lily Collins) returning to Paris and taking a trip to Greece following her split from Marcello. Lucas Bravo and Ashley Park return alongside the core cast for the final episode arc.

What to expect

Subscribers can also tune in for Cameron Diaz's return in the action-comedy feature Bad Day (Dec. 11), as well as David Fincher's 1977 Hollywood fixer film The Further Mis-adventures of Cliff Booth, written by Quentin Tarantino and starring Brad Pitt (Dec. 23).

The December lineup further includes a South Korean drama centered on operatives protecting cultural treasures, a comedic megachurch series featuring Druski and Mike Epps, a documentary by Teboho Edkins examining a 2019 aircraft crash in Ethiopia, and a documentary special featuring Idris Elba alongside King Charles III.

For audiences seeking information on upcoming Turkish historical dramas or unlisted international serials: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

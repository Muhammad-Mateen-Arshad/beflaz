‘Stranger Things’ Prequel Other Worlds Gets 2028 Release As Netflix Explores Vecna’s Origins

Published on November 08, 2026

What's new

Netflix has officially confirmed a brand-new live-action prequel titled Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure. Expected to release in 2028, the series moves away from the familiar backdrop of Hawkins to introduce a fresh setting along with an entirely new group of characters. Filming for the project is planned to take place in New Mexico. However, the complete cast is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

The prequel is set in Marion, Nevada, during 1955, placing its story decades prior to the timeline of Eleven, Mike, Will, and their friends. It also takes place before the 1959 events of the stage production Stranger Things: The First Shadow, which explored the troubled childhood of Henry Creel.

Several key creative figures are heading the production team:

  • Creators: Matt Duffer and Ross Duffer are co-creating the series with Kate Trefry.
  • Showrunner: Kate Trefry, who previously wrote for the main series and authored The First Shadow, serves as showrunner.
  • Director: Joe Wright, known for directing Atonement and Pride & Prejudice, is set to direct the first three episodes.

What to expect

The narrative will delve into the era before Henry Creel encountered the mysterious otherworldly substance that endowed him with supernatural abilities, providing answers related to Vecna's background. However, the creators are not treating this purely as an origin story. Viewers can expect a new lineup of heroes and villains, alongside expanded mythology tied to the secrets hidden in Marion. Specific episode counts beyond the initial director setup are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

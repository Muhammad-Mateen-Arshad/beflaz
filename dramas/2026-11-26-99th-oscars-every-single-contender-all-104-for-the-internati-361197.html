99th Oscars: Every Single Contender — All 104! — for the International Feature Race

Published on November 26, 2026

What's new

The international feature race for the 99th Academy Awards has reached a record high, with 104 titles in contention. This eclipses the previous submission record of 93 entries established in 2020. Out of the current lineup, 100 films were submitted directly by countries or territories, whereas four movies gained eligibility by winning major festival awards while meeting the Academy's theatrical, creative, and language criteria. Conversely, New Zealand's horror submission, Mārama, was disqualified after failing to satisfy the required non-English language threshold.

What to expect

The competitive field features a wide array of global cinematic entries across various genres:

  • European selections include Spain's La Bola Negra, Romania's Palme d'Or winner Fjord, Poland's Fatherland, France's Minotaur, Belgium's World War I drama Coward, and Austria's Rose.
  • Asian contenders include South Korea's Possible Love and Japan's All of a Sudden.
  • Documentary entries include the United Kingdom's submission, Rehearsals for a Revolution, and Albania's archival project, A State Film.
  • Other national entries feature Algeria's horror feature Roqia, Argentina's coming-of-age thriller The Virgin of the Quarry Lake, Armenia's narrative drama In the Land of Arto, and Australia's Tagalog-language submission First Light.

Specific details regarding Turkish historical drama submissions or entries are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Important schedule

Preliminary shortlist voting by eligible Academy members is set for December 7–11, with the 15-film shortlist slated for announcement on December 15. Voting for final nominees will occur between January 11 and January 15, 2027, followed by the official nominations announcement on January 21. The 99th Oscars ceremony will take place on March 14, 2027.

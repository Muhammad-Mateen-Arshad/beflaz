Talk Survivor 4 is Now Streaming on Netflix! A Thorough Guide to the 29 Cast Members and 8 Episodes

Published on November 12, 2026

Netflix has officially launched the fourth installment of its series blending narrative drama with unscripted comedy battles. Titled "Talk Survivor! DEAD or LAUGH," the season arrived on Tuesday, November 10, 2026, offering global subscribers an immediate viewing experience with all eight episodes released at once.

What's new

Following Season 3 in 2024, the franchise returns under planner and producer Nobuyuki Sakuma, director Kengo Takimoto, and screenwriter Ryoichi Tsuchiya. Streaming exclusively on Netflix worldwide, the project brings together 29 comedians along with a full dramatic acting roster across its 8 episodes.

About the series

The show features a distinct structure that mixes scripted storytelling with improvised comedy showdowns. Participating comedians join the story as characters within a full-scale drama. Without warning, unscripted talk battles erupt where contestants must draw on their actual life experiences to generate laughs. Performers who fail to deliver compelling stories face elimination. Within this format, Nobu serves as the program's MC, while Daigo participates in both the narrative scenes and the talk battles, taking on disguises such as a downtown fishmonger and a Korean izakaya figure. Shiori Sato and Karen Takizawa also appear as witnesses to the talk competitions.

What to expect

Specific story developments and script details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. However, the season features a wide mix of comedic talent alongside dramatic actors, including Hiroe Igeta, Mahiro Takasugi, Yuto Nakajima, Hitomi Miyauchi, Yui Oguri (AKB48), Win Morisaki, Rio Uchida, Takashi Fujii, Toshimasa Komatsu, Bengal, Dean Fujioka, and Eiichiro Funakoshi.

The 29 comedians competing in the talk battles consist of:

  • Daigo (Chidori)
  • Cookie! (Yasei Bakudan), Muga Tsukaji (Drunk Dragon), Kazunobu Kubota (Torosamon), Takashi Watanabe (Nishikigoi), Minamikawa, Naoki Inada (Einstein), Lily (Mitorizu), Buruma Konno, Toru Doomae (Long Coat Daddy), Sakamoto (Mayurika), Hikorohi
  • Takafumi Sasaki (Evers), Kemuri Matsui (Reiwa Roman), Yui Yoshida (Carnation), Buchigire Ujihara, Sota Maeda (Karatachi), Takano (Kishitakano), Jumbo Takao (Rainbow), Yutaro Hamada, Nishida (Lalande), Shogo (Tokyo Hoteison), Kazuki Machida (Evers), Kotake Seigikan, Sho Hasumi (Dow 90000), Mitsuho Fukudome
  • Surprise guests Toshl, Koji Higashino, and Atsuhiro Tsuda (Daian)

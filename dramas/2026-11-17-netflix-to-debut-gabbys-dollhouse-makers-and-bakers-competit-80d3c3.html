Netflix to Debut ‘Gabby’s Dollhouse: Makers and Bakers’ Competition Show in December | Exclusive

Published on November 17, 2026

What's new

Netflix is expanding its family streaming lineup with a fresh unscripted competition titled "Gabby’s Dollhouse: Makers and Bakers." The reality series will officially debut on December 9. The upcoming release features four holiday-themed episodes, which will be followed by six additional episodes set to arrive in 2027.

What to expect

The family-oriented reality competition features teams of talented young makers and bakers working in duos. Together, they will undertake imaginative crafting and baking challenges inspired by the creative universe of the Dollhouse. Key details regarding the cast and crew include:

  • Host: Laila Lockhart Kraner appears as Gabby.
  • Judges: Aidy Bryant and Justin Willman judge the initial holiday episodes. When the show returns in 2027, Justin Willman will be joined by Melissa Villaseñor.
  • Production Team: The project stems from DreamWorks Animation and B17 Entertainment, which is part of Sony Pictures Television. Executive producers include Rhett Bachner and Brien Meagher, while Claire Kosloff acts as showrunner and executive producer.

Specific episode lengths are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

The new competition is built around the popular children's franchise created by Traci Paige Johnson and Jennifer Twomey. The main series, which merges live-action with animation, first launched on Netflix in 2021 and has run for 14 seasons totaling 90 episodes. The brand also expanded into theaters last September with "Gabby’s Dollhouse: The Movie," starring Gloria Estefan and Kristen Wiig, which brought in $80.8 million worldwide. Additionally, a pet-focused spinoff series titled "Doozie’s Doghouse" debuted on Netflix earlier this month, providing crossover possibilities with the main show.

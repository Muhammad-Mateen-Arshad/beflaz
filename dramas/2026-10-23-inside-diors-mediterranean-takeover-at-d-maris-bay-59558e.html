Inside Dior’s Mediterranean Takeover at D Maris Bay

Published on October 23, 2026

Dior has brought its summer 2026 Dioriviera residency to D Maris Bay, a coastal retreat located on Turkey's Datça Peninsula near Marmaris, where the Aegean and Mediterranean seas meet.

What's new

The 2026 Dioriviera collection represents the first release under the creative direction of Jonathan Anderson. Taking cues from Christian Bérard—a painter, illustrator, and designer who was a close friend of Christian Dior—the line incorporates the Dior Arabesque design, floral motifs, marine elements, and vibrant summer hues. Beyond ready-to-wear apparel and accessories, Dior Maison expanded into decorative items, tableware, parasols, and outdoor furniture installed around the resort's swimming pool.

The underlying text concentrates on the luxury venue itself, which sits elevated above pine-covered slopes and secluded coves. D Maris Bay features five private beaches accessible by boat, alongside a first-floor Dioriviera boutique designed with an understated beige, white, and cream color palette.

What to expect

Guests visiting the location can experience several curated settings and dining venues:

  • Culinary Options: Riviera-style lunches at La Guérite, modern Japanese dishes at Zuma, and Mediterranean seafood with Greek culinary touches at Manos.
  • Distinct Beaches: The spacious Bay Beach for family outings and the tranquil, music-free adults-only Zuma x Silence Beach.
  • Resort Amenities: Coordinated lounge furnishings around the main pool and private cabanas along quiet stretches of the shoreline.

Netflix Officially Brings Back a Hit 3-Part Rom-Com Series

Published on November 15, 2026

What's new

Netflix has officially confirmed the Season 4 renewal of its hit romantic comedy series Nobody Wants This on November 6. The announcement follows the debut of its third season last month. The show has maintained strong streaming figures, accumulating 78.5 million views across all three seasons. Season 3 also achieved critical praise, earning an 83% Certified Fresh rating on Rotten Tomatoes, which stands as the show's second-highest score on the site.

The greenlight is part of a broader group of recent renewals at the streaming platform, alongside titles such as Adults, Youth, Outlast, and Virgin River. The latter is set to become the platform's longest-running scripted drama as it reaches its ninth season. These developments follow a robust 2026 lineup for romance content on Netflix, which included Bridgerton Season 4 near the start of the year, the feature-length finale Heartstopper Forever, and the third season of My Life With the Walter Boys in Silver Falls.

About the series

Nobody Wants This follows the relationship of Joanne, played by Kristen Bell, and Noah, played by Adam Brody. In Season 3, the lead couple moved in together to build a life as a stable pair. Meanwhile, the surrounding characters underwent their own transitions:

  • Sasha (Timothy Simons) and Esther (Jackie Tohn) re-entered the awkward world of dating.
  • Morgan (Justine Lupe) reached a new stage in her career.

What to expect

Co-showrunners Erin Foster, Jenni Konner, and Bruce Eric Kaplan will return to write and lead the fourth season. Additional details regarding plot storylines, episode counts, or premiere dates are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

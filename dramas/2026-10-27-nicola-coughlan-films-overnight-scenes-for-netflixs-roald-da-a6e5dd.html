Nicola Coughlan Films Overnight Scenes for Netflix’s Roald Dahl Series as Kevin Bishop and Kadeem Ramsay Join Cast

Published on October 27, 2026

What's new

Production continues on Netflix's upcoming live-action adult series adapting the short stories of Roald Dahl, developed under the working title Zoltar. Recent night shoots in the North of England captured dramatic moments featuring lead cast member Nicola Coughlan alongside Naomi Scott and Josh Finan. Filming also revealed two newly spotted cast additions, Kevin Bishop and Kadeem Ramsay, whose specific roles have not yet been disclosed.

In the latest set photos, Coughlan—portraying the heavily pregnant character Mary Maloney—was seen carrying a floral bag and clutching a prosthetic bump during an intense street sequence. The production schedule indicates that the cast and crew will board a cruise ship departing from Bristol on Monday, November 9, to shoot scenes at sea while traveling toward France, Santander in northern Spain, and the Mediterranean.

About the series

The project features six hour-long episodes that chronicle the troubles of a young couple stranded in a coastal town as they encounter con men, adulterers, murderous mothers, and snake charmers. Executive producer Danny Brocklehurst serves as the writer, with Orchid Pictures producing the project. Directors Runyararo Mapfumo and Ben Gregor are overseeing the episodes.

The confirmed ensemble cast includes:

  • Nicola Coughlan
  • Naomi Scott
  • Josh Finan
  • Imelda Staunton
  • Dominic West
  • Toby Jones
  • Carmen Ejogo
  • Clarke Peters
  • Michael Socha
  • Christine Tremarco
  • Kevin Bishop
  • Kadeem Ramsay

What to expect

Production takes place across regional sites such as Bolton, Manchester, and Whitby, with filming scheduled from mid-August until mid-December 2026.

‘The Lincoln Lawyer’ Season 5 Episode Titles Revealed Ahead of the Final Season

Published on November 11, 2026

What's new

Netflix's legal drama The Lincoln Lawyer has completed filming for its fifth and final season, moving the project into post-production. Author Michael Connelly stated that the 10-episode chapter will arrive in 2027. Ahead of its release, the episode titles for the final run have been revealed:

  • Artemisia
  • Article 1, Section 9
  • Roots
  • Table 12
  • Sucker Punch
  • Chemistry Problem
  • Footprints
  • Overtime
  • Do or Die
  • Resurrection Walk

About the series

The final season adapts Connelly's 2023 novel Resurrection Walk, the seventh book in the Mickey Haller series. Manuel Garcia-Rulfo returns as Mickey, who takes on a habeas petition to overturn a murder conviction. The narrative centers around a newly introduced character, Mickey's unexpected half-sister Artemisia "Emi" Finch, played by Cobie Smulders. Emi prompts Mickey to help exonerate a woman imprisoned six years earlier for a murder she did not commit. Because Netflix was unable to use the book character Harry Bosch, Emi was created specifically for the screen and is expected to lead a future series from the show's original team.

What to expect

Most of the primary cast will return for the final case, including Neve Campbell reprising her role. The recurring lineup features Amy Aquino, Angela Trimbur, Elpidia Carillo, Nate Corddry, Tricia Helfer, and Keir O’Donnell, alongside newcomers Chris Diamantopoulos as Frank Silver and Iker Garcia as Rafa Wagner. However, Yaya DaCosta will not return as Andrea 'Andy' Freeman due to her absence from the source material.

Behind the scenes, episode five ("Sucker Punch") was written by Isabella A. Rodriguez, while episode six ("Chemistry Problem") was penned by Dailyn Rodriguez and Matthew J. Lieberman. For exact premiere dates beyond the 2027 window, details are Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

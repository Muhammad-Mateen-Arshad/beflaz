Tom Brady fires back at Eli Manning’s Super Bowl XLII jab ahead of Netflix documentary release

Published on November 22, 2026

What's new

Former NFL quarterbacks Tom Brady and Eli Manning recently engaged in a playful social media exchange following a jersey swap ahead of an upcoming streaming documentary release. On the jersey handed to Manning, Brady noted that the former Giants quarterback was the best thing to happen to New York and the worst thing for his trophy case. Manning responded by writing a message celebrating the Patriots' 18-1 season, calling it "1 Giant loss" and signing it as the Super Bowl XLII MVP.

Brady shared a picture of the jersey on his Instagram story, writing that David Tyree's helmet deserved half of the MVP title and admitting he is still not over the loss. Manning reshared the post as a reminder, leading Brady to comment on Netflix's promotional post asking to end the reminders.

About the series

The new documentary is titled Untold: One Giant Upset, forming part of Netflix's 'Untold' franchise. Scheduled for release on December 21, the documentary centers on Super Bowl XLII, which the streaming service calls the greatest upset in Super Bowl history. Additional details such as episode count or exact duration are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

The release revisits the famous game where the Giants ended the Patriots' attempt at a 19-0 season with a 17-14 victory. The production covers key moments, including Tyree's helmet catch with 1:15 remaining and Manning's game-winning touchdown pass to Plaxico Burress with 34 seconds left on the clock. Viewers can expect interviews and appearances from key NFL figures, including:

  • David Tyree and Plaxico Burress
  • Tom Coughlin and Michael Strahan
  • Antonio Pierce, Justin Tuck, and Steve Spagnuolo

The documentary's announcement arrived shortly before public reports revealed Brady's discussions regarding potential investment in Clearlake Capital, the firm controlling Chelsea, following his recent departure from Birmingham City.

‘All of Us Are Dead’ Season 2: Expected Netflix Release Date & Everything We Know

Published on October 19, 2026

Fans of global streaming dramas finally have clear updates regarding the long-awaited second season of All of Us Are Dead. Netflix officially renewed the hit series in June 2022, but production encountered delays, shifting the filming schedule from 2024 to 2025. Principal photography wrapped in February 2026 after roughly seven months of work, which included a shoot at the Gangwon Provincial Office building in Chuncheon in January 2026. The series is set to headline the "What Next?" showcase at Netflix Korea's 10th anniversary festival in Seoul in late October.

What's new

While interest remains high regarding when the new episodes will arrive on the platform, the exact release date is Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Filming officially concluded in February 2026. Cast member Cho Yi-hyun finished her scenes on February 4, while Park Ji-hu wrapped on February 18. Additionally, supporting actor Seo Ji-hoon completed his scenes prior to reporting for military duty on February 3, 2026.

About the series

The original season achieved massive global viewership, accumulating over 679 million hours watched within its first 91 days. Netflix Korea officially confirmed the cast lineup for the upcoming season, featuring both returning leads and new additions:

  • Confirmed Returning Leads: Park Ji-hu (Nam On-jo), Yoon Chan-young (Lee Cheong-san), Cho Yi-hyun (Choi Nam-ra), and Lomon (Lee Su-hyeok).
  • Officially Unveiled New Cast: Baek Sung-chul, Kim Si-eun, Yoon Ga-i, and Kim Jin-young.

Media reports linked actors such as Seo Ji-hoon and Kim Min to undisclosed or reported roles, whereas actress Roh Yoon-seo withdrew from the project due to a scheduling conflict with The East Palace.

What to expect

Director Lee Jae-kyu previously noted that the storyline will shift its narrative focus. While the initial season centered around human survival during the initial outbreak, season 2 is planned to focus on zombie survival.

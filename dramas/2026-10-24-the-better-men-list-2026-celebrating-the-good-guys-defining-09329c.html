The Better Men List 2026: Celebrating the Good Guys Defining Positive Masculinity

Published on October 24, 2026

What's new

Esquire has released its 2026 Better Men honours list, expanding on the initiative launched in 2025 by Editor-in-Chief Teo van den Broeke. The project aims to champion positive masculinity by recognizing figures across culture, sport, fashion, and politics who serve as constructive role models for young men and boys.

About the series

For readers seeking details on specific Turkish historical productions, episode counts, or broadcast dates: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice. However, the 2026 list features prominent figures from the broader dramatic and screen landscape:

  • Omari Douglas: The British actor established himself through his acclaimed role in the AIDS drama It's a Sin.
  • Robert Aramayo: Earned Best Actor honors at both the Baftas and the British Independent Film Awards for his performance as Scottish Tourette's syndrome advocate John Davidson in I Swear.
  • Jack Thorne: The screenwriter behind works such as This Is England, Help, The Virtues, and Adolescence was recognized for highlighting marginalized stories and campaigning for greater creative representation.
  • David Attenborough: The 100-year-old broadcaster behind iconic nature documentaries like Blue Planet and Life on Earth continues his efforts to raise planetary awareness.

What to expect

The 2026 honours serve as an addition rather than a replacement to previous honorees. Alongside screen actors and screenwriters, the list celebrates literary talent like Sufiyaan Salam, author of Wimmy Road Boyz and winner of the 2024 Merky Books New Writers' Prize, as well as Balenciaga designer Pierpaolo Piccioli and economics communicator Gary Stevenson. The public can submit additional recommendations for the 2027 list directly to the editor.

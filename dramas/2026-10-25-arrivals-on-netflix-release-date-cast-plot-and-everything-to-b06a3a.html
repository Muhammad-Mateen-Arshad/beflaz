‘Arrivals’ on Netflix: Release Date, Cast, Plot, and Everything to Know About the ‘Manifest’ Spinoff

Published on October 25, 2026

What's new

Streaming platform Netflix is expanding its supernatural drama universe with the announcement of a new series titled Arrivals: A New Manifest Mystery. Officially announced on August 28, 2026, the upcoming show serves as a spinoff to the popular drama series Manifest. Filming for the project is slated to take place in Vancouver, British Columbia, running from January 19, 2027, to June 9, 2027.

Regarding the official premiere date: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

The original Manifest series ran for four seasons from 2018 to 2023, following the story of Flight 828 passengers who landed over five years after disappearing without having aged. The new spinoff shifts focus to an entirely different supernatural event.

The premise of the new drama involves several key elements:

  • An unexplained plane crash-lands in Denver.
  • The onboard passengers suffer complete memory loss regarding their identities, origins, and purpose.
  • Main characters Zoe Deaver and Mike Deaver collaborate with the new arrivals to solve the mystery and stop a potential catastrophe threatening humanity.

What to expect

Behind the camera, the spinoff brings back familiar creative talent. Original series creator Jeff Rake is returning alongside Margaret Easley and Laura Putney, who both worked on the original show and will serve as co-creators and co-showrunners for this new chapter.

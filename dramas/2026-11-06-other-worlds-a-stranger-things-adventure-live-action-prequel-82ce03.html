‘Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure’: Live-Action Prequel Series Set for Netflix in 2028

Published on November 06, 2026

Streaming platform Netflix officially announced its upcoming live-action prequel series, titled Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure, during Stranger Things Day. The project expands the franchise into an earlier decade while launching an entirely fresh narrative.

What's new

The newly confirmed spin-off is scheduled to premiere on Netflix in 2028, with physical production based in New Mexico. Developed by co-creators Kate Trefry and Matt and Ross Duffer, the series places Trefry in the roles of writer and showrunner. Director Joe Wright will helm the first three episodes and serve as an executive producer alongside Matt Duffer, Ross Duffer, Shawn Levy, Paul Dichter, Shelley Meals, Andrea Sperling, and Pavlina Hatoupis.

While the Duffer brothers remain creatively involved, they will not act as day-to-day showrunners, and Levy will not be directing. Specific details regarding episode count and cast members are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

The story takes place in the 1950s, beginning specifically on April 19, 1955, in Marion, Nevada. This setting places the timeline prior to the events of the flagship show and before the 1959-set stage play Stranger Things: The First Shadow. Key elements revealed about the setup include:

  • A teaser scene displaying an abandoned bicycle near a restricted zone at night beneath a wormhole-like disturbance in the sky.
  • The use of the song "Sh-Boom" by The Chords, which previously featured in the main series.
  • Narrative ties to a mysterious rock inside a scientist's briefcase discovered in Nevada caves, linked to young Henry Creel.

What to expect

Production leads describe the prequel as a distinct story featuring a completely original ensemble of heroes and villains. Although it retains the fundamental mythology, horror, and wonder of the parent show, the series shifts focus to a new region and era, building upon secrets planted throughout the overarching universe.

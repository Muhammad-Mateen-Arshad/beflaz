Money Trap on Netflix turns Yılmaz Erdoğan’s caper films into an eight-episode hunt for the Karun treasure

Published on October 16, 2026

What's new

The popular Turkish caper franchise Organize İşler is transitioning to streaming with an eight-episode original series titled Money Trap (Organize İşler: Karun Hazinesi). Created, written by, and starring Yılmaz Erdoğan, the series is scheduled to premiere globally on Netflix on 16 October 2026. The Turkish-language show will be released with subtitles and dubbing across international markets. Şenol Sönmez serves as the director for the season.

About the series

The plot centers on Asım Noyan, an older and warier legendary scam artist who is ready to leave his life of crime behind. His retirement is put on hold when a map surfaces pointing to the Karun treasure, the hoard of an Anatolian king associated with impossible wealth. Deciding to pull off one last con, Asım reassembles his old crew, only to find himself competing against rivals and fellow treasure hunters after the same map.

The series continues a franchise lineage that began in 2005 with the feature film Organize İşler (released internationally as Magic Carpet Ride) and continued with the 2019 film Organize İşler: Sazan Sarmalı (released as Money Trap). Specific episode runtimes are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

The cast features a well-known ensemble that includes:

  • Yılmaz Erdoğan as Asım Noyan
  • Kıvanç Tatlıtuğ
  • Ezgi Mola
  • Bensu Soral
  • Rıza Kocaoğlu

What to expect

Adapting the feature-length film format into an eight-episode story allows the franchise to expand its crime-comedy tone. Instead of resolving a scam within a single movie, the serialized format uses the treasure map as a central spine across the season while exploring whether a veteran scam artist can ever truly step away from the con.

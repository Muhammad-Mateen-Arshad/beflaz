All Of Us Are Dead Season 2 Release Date Update: What Happened Yoon Chan Young’s Character? Netflix Finally Reveals

Published on October 26, 2026

Netflix streaming fans have received a long-awaited update regarding the second season of the hit K-drama All Of Us Are Dead. During its 10th anniversary celebration, Netflix Korea confirmed the upcoming season and shared crucial progress details regarding the production.

What's new

The highly anticipated second season is currently listed as "coming soon" on Netflix, with expectations pointing toward a release in the early or first quarter of 2027. The cast has reportedly finished shooting, and the series is now in its post-production stage. An exact premiere day is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Fans also received major character news regarding Lee Cheong-san, portrayed by Yoon Chan Young. Although the character was presumed dead following a first-season cliffhanger, the actor attended the Netflix event and confirmed that Lee Cheong-san survived and will return for season two.

About the series

The original show follows high school students who must fight for survival when a zombie virus outbreak leaves them trapped together. Returning cast members include Park Ji-hu, Yoon Chan-young, Cho Yi-hyun, Lomon, Yoo In-soo, Lee Yoo-mi, Kim Byung-chul, Lee Kyu-hyung, and Jeon Bae-soo.

The new season will expand its roster with several fresh cast additions:

  • Roh Jae-won
  • Lee Min-jae
  • Kim Si-eun
  • Seo Ji-hoon
  • Yoon Ga-i
  • Kim Min
  • Ahn Dong-goo
  • Ryu Sung-rok

What to expect

The storyline for the second season moves to Seoul, where Nam On-jo is attending university while still dealing with past loss and trauma. As she attempts to start over, another outbreak strikes the city, thrusting her and her college friends into a dangerous struggle to survive.

At the same time, Lee Cheong-san, Choi Nam-ra, and Lee Su-hyeok navigate their lives and the lasting impact of the original disaster years later. Additionally, character Han Doo-seok makes a return as a team leader within the National Intelligence Service.

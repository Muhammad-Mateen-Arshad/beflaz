'Other Worlds': Netflix confirms release window for 'Stranger Things' prequel series

Published on November 07, 2026

What's new

Streaming giant Netflix has confirmed the target premiere window for its upcoming live-action prequel series, titled "Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure." Officially announced on November 6, 2026, during Stranger Things Day, the new streaming project is expected to launch in 2028. Specific launch dates are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

The series marks the fourth major installment within the broader franchise, building upon the original five-season series that concluded in 2025, the animated series "Stranger Things: Tales from '85", and the stage play "The First Shadow," which was set in 1959.

Key production details from behind the scenes include:

  • Creators: Co-created by the Duffer Brothers alongside Kate Trefry.
  • Showrunner: Kate Trefry, a former writer on the main show who also penned the stage play "The First Shadow," serves as showrunner and executive producer.
  • Director: Filmmaker Joe Wright, acclaimed for directing movies such as "Atonement" and "Pride & Prejudice," is set to direct the first three episodes.
  • Production Location: Filming is expected to take place in New Mexico.

Full cast lists and actor details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

Moving away from Hawkins, Indiana, the prequel shifts its narrative to Marion, Nevada, in the year 1955. Set several decades before the primary story, the plot explores the initial origins of the Upside Down anomalies, introducing a clean slate of heroes, antagonists, and paranormal mysteries.

The initial teaser and setup offer a clear homage to classic 1950s science-fiction anthology programs, specifically referencing "The Twilight Zone" and "The Outer Limits." The teaser footage features a 1950s doo-wop song playing from a radio near an abandoned bicycle beside a fenced-off government restricted zone, creating a classic Cold War sci-fi environment.

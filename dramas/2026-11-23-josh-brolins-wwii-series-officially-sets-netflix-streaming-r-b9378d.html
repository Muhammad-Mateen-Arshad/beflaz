Josh Brolin’s WWII Series Officially Sets Netflix Streaming Release Date

Published on November 23, 2026

What's new

Netflix is adding another historical entry to its streaming platform with the premiere of World War II: Behind Enemy Lines. The docuseries is scheduled to launch on Wednesday, December 2. Featuring narration by Avengers: Endgame actor Josh Brolin, the series represents the platform's latest exploration of 20th-century military history.

About the series

Created by the team behind 2023's World War II: From the Frontlines, the new docuseries is directed by returning director Rob Coldstream. The plot centers on the 101st Airborne as they carry out dangerous missions in Nazi-occupied Europe, spanning events from D-Day to VE-Day. Total episode counts and runtime details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

The upcoming production uses the format of its predecessor by combining personal accounts with colorized, previously unreleased archival material. The original six-part 2023 installment, which was narrated by John Boyega, previously earned two 2024 News & Documentary Emmy Awards for Outstanding Graphic Design and Outstanding Music Composition.

What to expect

Along with providing historical insights into World War II missions, the release lands alongside a busy upcoming slate for narrator Josh Brolin. Viewers following the actor's work can look out for several of his announced feature film and documentary projects:

  • Reprisal of his role as Gurney Halleck in Denis Villeneuve's sci-fi film Dune: Part Three, slated for theaters on December 18.
  • A return as Matt Graves in a third Sicario movie written by Taylor Sheridan.
  • A feature film titled A Speck in the Sea, set in the world of fishing.
  • A documentary titled A Road to Happiness, which follows Darin Olien traveling with actors to examine global views on happiness.

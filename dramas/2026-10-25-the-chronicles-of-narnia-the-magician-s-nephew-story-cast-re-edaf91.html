The Chronicles of Narnia: The Magician's Nephew - Story, Cast, Release Date and Book Order

Published on October 25, 2026

What's new

A new cinematic adaptation of C. S. Lewis's fantasy work, The Chronicles of Narnia: The Magician's Nephew, is set for release in 2027. Written and directed by Greta Gerwig, the film examines the origins of the iconic magical realm. Distribution is split globally, with Netflix handling North America and Sony Pictures Entertainment managing international markets. Advanced IMAX previews begin on February 10, 2027, followed by a wide theatrical release on February 12, 2027. The project will later stream on Netflix starting April 2, 2027.

About the series

Although published in 1955 as the sixth book in the original seven-part book series, The Magician's Nephew represents the beginning of Narnia's timeline. Set many years prior to The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe, the narrative takes audiences back to the creation of the world.

The plot centers on two curious children, Digory Kirke and Polly Plummer, who travel across dimensions through gateway pools found in the Wood Between the Worlds. Their journeys lead them to witness the lion Aslan singing Narnia into existence, while also revealing the background of Jadis, the figure who becomes the White Witch.

What to expect

The adaptation features a high-profile ensemble cast alongside emerging actors:

  • David McKenna as Digory Kirke
  • Beatrice Campbell as Polly Plummer
  • Daniel Craig as Digory's magician uncle, Uncle Andrew
  • Emma Mackey as Jadis
  • Meryl Streep as the voice of Aslan
  • Carey Mulligan as Mabel Kirke
  • Supporting performances by Ciarán Hinds, Kobna Holdbrook-Smith, Denise Gough, and Susan Wokoma

Musicians Mark Ronson and Andrew Wyatt are producing the score for the feature. Specific details regarding production budgets or trailer release schedules are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

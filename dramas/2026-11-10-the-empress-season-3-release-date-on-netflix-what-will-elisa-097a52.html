The Empress Season 3 Release Date on Netflix: What Will Elisabeth Choose Between Her Heart and the Throne

Published on November 10, 2026

What's new

The German period drama The Empress (known locally as Die Kaiserin) is returning to Netflix for its third and final season on Thursday, November 12, 2026. While third-party reports mention a six-episode order, Netflix has not officially confirmed the total episode count; not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Precise details regarding English dubbing and subtitle language availability for the final season are also not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

The final chapter picks up after the events of the Sardinian War. Emperor Franz returns deeply troubled by the loss of thousands of young troops, causing his relationship with Elisabeth to collapse. After Franz embarks on an affair and the court turns hostile, Elisabeth suffers health issues and travels from Vienna to a distant island to recover.

During her retreat, Elisabeth meets Count Andrássy, the leader of the Hungarian revolution, sparking a strong physical and intellectual attraction. Meanwhile, Franz’s brother Maximilian and his wife Marie travel to Mexico to take an imperial throne. As the story moves between Vienna, Prussia, Hungary, and Mexico, Elisabeth must decide whether to pursue her true feelings or remain dedicated to her duties as empress.

About the series

Produced by Sommerhaus Serien GmbH, the series sees Katharina Eyssen return as showrunner alongside executive producer Robert Eyssen. Directors Florian Cossen and Julia Langhof oversaw filming across Bavaria, the Czech Republic, and Spain, which included coastal shoots in Lloret de Mar.

  • Season 1 (September 2022): Depicted Elisabeth's initial struggle against strict imperial court life.
  • Season 2 (November 2024): Explored political pressure, marital strain, the loss of a child, and Elisabeth developing her political influence.
  • Awards: Won Best Drama Series at the 51st International Emmy Awards in 2023.

Both prior seasons are currently streaming on Netflix for fans wanting to catch up before the final release.

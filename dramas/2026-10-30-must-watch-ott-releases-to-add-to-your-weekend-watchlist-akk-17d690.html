Must-Watch OTT Releases To Add To Your Weekend Watchlist: Akka, Obsession, Meethe Mein Kya Hai And More

Published on October 30, 2026

What's new

Streaming platforms such as Netflix, Prime Video, and JioHotstar are expanding their catalogs with a fresh selection of films and web series for home viewing. The latest lineup spans several genres, including underworld sagas, supernatural thrillers, family dramas, and international crime mysteries.

About the series

Subscribers have access to a variety of detailed titles across different platforms:

  • Akka: Set during the 1980s in Pernuru, this story focuses on opposing underworld leaders Bhavani, played by Keerthy Suresh, and Rudra, played by Radhika Apte. Their rivalry and the arrival of an unfamiliar figure threaten their realm.
  • Obsession: Features a socially awkward man named Bear who employs a supernatural object to win the affection of Nikki, accidentally placing a curse on her that turns her into a dangerous stalker. A Hindi-dubbed edition premieres on JioHotstar on October 30, 2026.
  • Dead-End Job: A 2026 South Korean mystery-horror-fantasy series on Netflix starring Lee Jae-wook as Byeon Hyeok-jun, a young man in his thirties burdened by heavy debt.
  • Rudravanam PS: Follows an honest officer, SI Sivakumar (played by Naresh Agastya), who steps in to investigate a missing-person case in the remote location of Rudravanam.
  • Meethe Mein Kya Hai: Centers on a festive gathering in a multigenerational home that turns tense after an unannounced wedding proposal brings long-standing secrets and anger to light.
  • Nocturne: Centers on Jonah Lynn, a former soldier and detective searching for quiet in a Pennsylvania town, whose life is upended when a serial killer named Jurek Walter targets his loved ones and town.
  • Swedish Thriller: Adapted from a novel by Stefan Ahnhem, this psychological project follows two affluent couples exchanging houses to rescue their marriages, leading to the discovery of covert cameras, dark secrets, and criminal activity.
  • Mirzapur: The Movie: Directed by Gurmmeet Singh and starring Pankaj Tripathi, this interquel is set specifically between episodes 6 and 7 of the show's first season.

What to expect

Audiences can anticipate diverse storytelling ranging from psychological tension to period action. Furthermore, Ramayana: Part One has finalized JioHotstar as its streaming home following its run in theaters. Full broadcast details for certain titles are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

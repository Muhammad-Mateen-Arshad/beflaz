Doctor X Mafia In White Episode 5 Release Time Today: When &Where Will New Ep Premiere Online? What To Expect?

Published on October 23, 2026

The Korean medical series Doctor X: Mafia In White, which is also known in some regions as Doctor X: Age Of The White Mafia, returns today, October 23, with its fifth episode.

What's new

Episode 5 premieres today, October 23. Viewers in South Korea can tune into the broadcast on SBS TV at 9:50 PM KST. For audiences in India, the chapter will be available to stream online via Netflix from 6:20 PM IST on the same day.

The medical drama consists of 12 episodes in total, with new entries arriving every Friday and Saturday. Barring any changes to the current broadcasting plan, the season finale is expected to drop on November 14.

About the series

Directed by Lee Jung-rim and written by Pyeon Seong-geun, the show is an adaptation of the Japanese medical drama Doctor X: Surgeon Michiko Daimon. Kim Ji-won, who earned praise for her performance in Queen Of Tears, plays the lead role of Gye Su-jeong, a talented surgeon who stands firm on her professional values.

Su-jeong enters Guseo University Hospital through a doctor-for-hire agency. Once there, she faces a corrupt environment filled with internal rivalries and figures who prioritize personal advantage over patient health. Despite these obstacles, she takes on difficult medical cases and fights back against the broken system.

The key cast members include:

  • Kim Ji-won as Gye Su-jeong
  • Lee Jung-eun as Jang Hui-suk
  • Son Hyun-joo as Bu Seung-gwon
  • Kim Woo-seok as Park Tae-gyeong

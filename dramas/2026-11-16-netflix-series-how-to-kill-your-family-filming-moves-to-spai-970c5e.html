Netflix Series ‘How to Kill Your Family’ Filming Moves to Spain: Anya Taylor-Joy Spotted in Barcelona

Published on November 16, 2026

What's new

Production on the upcoming Netflix series How to Kill Your Family has officially expanded to Spain after cameras first began rolling in London in mid-October 2026. Lead actress Anya Taylor-Joy was recently spotted filming on a sun-drenched waterfront in Barcelona. Channeling retro glamour, she was seen in character as Grace Bernard wearing a white button-front mini dress, a vibrant patterned silk headscarf, dark sunglasses, and tan slingback flats.

Details from the set hint at international storyline locations. A hard-shell suitcase carried by Taylor-Joy featured a travel tag marked NCE, the airport code for Nice Côte d'Azur. This suggests Barcelona may be serving as a stand-in for the south of France. It follows an earlier London shoot where luggage tags pointed toward Málaga, aligning with planned production stops in Marbella.

About the series

Adapted from Bella Mackie’s 2021 bestselling novel, the eight-part darkly comic thriller centers on Grace, the illegitimate daughter of billionaire Simon Artemis. Prompted by her mother's death and her family's total rejection, Grace devises a plan to systematically murder her estranged family members to claim a massive inheritance.

The key creative details for the production include:

  • Starring Cast: Anya Taylor-Joy leads as Grace Bernard, with Clive Owen co-starring as Simon Artemis. Cast members Orlando Norman and David Shields also join the show in unannounced roles.
  • Behind the Camera: The series was created and written by Emma Moran. Production is handled by Sid Gentle Films, with directing responsibilities divided between Josephine Bornebusch and Tom Kingsley.
  • Executive Production: Taylor-Joy also serves as an executive producer under her LadyKiller banner.

What to expect

Principal photography is scheduled to wrap in mid-February 2027. Regarding the streaming launch, an official premiere date is not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Viewers looking to see Taylor-Joy in the meantime can watch her in Sacrifice, which debuted on Netflix on October 16.

5 Most Universally Loved TV Dramas of All Time, Ranked

Published on November 21, 2026

What's new

Top-tier television dramas that win over critics, awards voters, and general audiences are back in the news cycle. A collection of universally praised shows across four networks and two countries continues to generate major updates. Apple TV+ launched a fourth season of Ted Lasso in August, generating nearly 300 million viewing minutes in its first few days. In September 2025, the Downton Abbey franchise concluded its run with the feature film The Grand Finale. Other celebrated titles are also returning to public attention: The West Wing cast reunited in September amid revival discussions, and The Sopranos will release a 4K box set on December 18.

About the series

The highlighted series cross multiple genres, including sports comedy-drama, period drama, political drama, and crime stories:

  • Ted Lasso: Premiering in 2020, the narrative follows Midwestern American football coach Ted (Jason Sudeikis) as he takes over an English soccer club. Co-starring Hannah Waddingham, Juno Temple, Brett Goldstein, and Brendan Hunt, the series has accumulated 13 Emmy awards.
  • Downton Abbey: Created by Julian Fellowes, this ITV period piece aired 52 episodes from 2010 to 2015. Following the Crawley family and their domestic staff from 1912 into the 1920s, the 15-time Emmy winner stars Hugh Bonneville, Elizabeth McGovern, Michelle Dockery, Maura Carmichael, and Joana Froggatt.

What to expect

Viewers revisiting these universally acclaimed series can expect strong rewatchability alongside high critical ratings. In the fourth season of Ted Lasso, Ted and Coach Beard return to manage a second-division women's team. Meanwhile, the final Downton Abbey film takes the Crawley estate into the 1930s as Mary steps up as head of the family amidst financial difficulties. Exact numeric ranking placements for all five series are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

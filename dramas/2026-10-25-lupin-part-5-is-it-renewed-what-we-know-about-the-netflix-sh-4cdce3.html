‘Lupin’ Part 5: Is It Renewed? What We Know About the Netflix Show’s Future

Published on October 25, 2026

What's new

The popular streaming series Lupin has returned to Netflix with eight brand-new episodes for Part 4, releasing on a Friday (October 23). The latest chapter brings actor Omar Sy back to the screen as Assane Diop, who finds himself entangled in another dangerous mystery. Alongside starring in the lead role, Sy took on a larger responsibility behind the camera this season, serving as a showrunner together with Louis Leterrier.

About the series

The hit French show remains one of the most-watched non-English-language series on Netflix. According to Gaumont, the production company behind the project, Lupin has generated over 100 million views worldwide. The show's release timeline spans several years:

  • The first two parts debuted in 2021.
  • Part 3 premiered in October 2023.
  • Part 4 arrived in October 2026 after a three-year gap.

What to expect

Many viewers are asking if the drama will continue into Part 5. As of Saturday (October 24), Netflix has not officially ordered another season. Exact renewal timelines and target air dates are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Despite the lack of an official renewal announcement, there are reasons for optimism. Netflix has not marketed Part 4 as the final installment, and the franchise remains a massive global hit. Reflecting on the time between seasons, Sy revealed in an interview published on October 19 with French magazine Première that the creative team deliberately took their time to ensure Assane's storyline felt fresh rather than repetitive.

While Sy has not confirmed that Part 5 is happening, all eight episodes of Part 4 are currently available to stream on Netflix.

‘Akane-banashi’ Season 2 Confirms January 2027 Release Date but Netflix Subscribers May Have to Wait

Published on November 23, 2026

The comedy series Akane-banashi is officially set to return for its second season in January 2027. While the Japanese television broadcast is slated to start early in the month, an exact international Netflix release date is not specified in the source; please check the official notice for confirmed streaming schedules, though the global premiere is anticipated around February or March 2027.

About the series

Directed by Ayumu Watanabe and produced by Zexcs, the animated show adapts the manga written by Yuki Suenaga, which has run in Weekly Shōnen Jump since February 2022. The story centers on Akane, who creates a scandal after being caught taking unofficial lessons from her father's former teacher, Shiguma Arakawa. Exchanging her hidden practice for formal training, she begins climbing the ranks of rakugo performers from a zenza opening act to a shin'uchi headliner, all while balancing high school and dealing with Shiguma's established apprentices.

What's new

The upcoming season brings fresh additions to the Japanese voice roster:

  • Hochu Otsuka as Miroku Kashiwaya
  • Ryotaro Okiayu as Shomei Tsubakiya
  • Hiroshi Naka as Enso Sanmeitei
  • Mutsumi Tamura as Urara Ransaika
  • Shinichiro Miki as Ryu’un Kenputei
  • Tomokazu Seki as Chocho Konjakutei

The English dub performers for these newly added characters are not specified in the source; please check the official notice for future voice cast updates.

What to expect

The narrative for season 2 will center on the Apprentice Arc, which is also known as the Zenza Training Arc. It details Akane's structured lessons under Shiguma Arakawa prior to the launch of her professional career. With approximately 30 of the 217 published manga chapters adapted so far, plenty of written material remains to take the show past its second season.

Netflix’s 10/10 Spy Thriller Officially Returns for Season 2 This Week

Published on November 01, 2026

What's new

Netflix is preparing for the return of its acclaimed British spy thriller, Black Doves, which is officially coming back for its second season. Premiering after a two-year gap following its initial launch in 2024, the series arrives during a major wave of espionage releases across competing streaming platforms.

About the series

The drama stars Keira Knightley and Ben Whishaw as secret agents affiliated with a covert organization that exerts influence over global politics. The series also features cast members Sam Riley playing the character Patrick, Sarah Lancashire, and Babou Ceesay. The show's debut season achieved critical acclaim for its swift storytelling and stellar performances, securing a "Certified Fresh" rating of 92% on Rotten Tomatoes.

What to expect

The long wait between seasons introduces questions about viewer retention, as research indicates that extended delays can lead to audience drop-off. Netflix recently encountered this trend with the executive-produced Guy Ritchie drama The Gentlemen, which suffered a 45% decline in viewership upon its return. Time will tell if Black Doves can overcome the two-year gap and maintain its audience momentum.

Richard Madden's 'nail-biting' new Netflix thriller will send Line of Duty fans wild

Published on November 05, 2026

What's new

Netflix has released the official trailer for its upcoming thriller series, Trinity, which is set to arrive on the platform on December 1. The seven-part show marks a new project from creator Jed Mercurio, best known for the BBC police thriller Line of Duty. This project also serves as a reunion for Mercurio and lead actor Richard Madden, who previously collaborated on the 2018 political drama Bodyguard alongside Keeley Hawes.

About the series

The plot follows Commander Katherine Decker, played by Gugu Mbatha-Raw, who serves as the second-in-command on the nuclear submarine USS Kansas. Her life changes dramatically when she develops an unexpected connection with Secretary of Defense Webb Preston, played by Richard Madden. This relationship pulls her into dangerous political schemes revolving around a top-secret program code-named "Trinity."

Mercurio revealed that his passion for classic thrillers like No Way Out inspired the narrative. He crafted the series to mix high-octane action with a high-stakes conspiracy surrounding the Pentagon that challenges a heroic naval officer.

What to expect

Viewers can expect a fast-paced blend of action and suspense. Precise episode runtimes and release times are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

In addition to Madden and Mbatha-Raw, the drama features a wide-ranging supporting cast:

  • Marcia Gay Harden
  • JD Pardo
  • Bruce Greenwood
  • Robert Wisdom
  • Kirk Acevedo
  • Ben Cotton
  • James Remar
  • Jason Ralph
  • Mark O'Brien

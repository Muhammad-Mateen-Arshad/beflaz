Netflix Unveils Trailer for Colombian Series Based on Marvel Moreno Novel

Published on November 20, 2026

What's new

Netflix has unveiled the official trailer for In December the Breezes Arrived (En Diciembre Llegaban las Brisas), an upcoming Colombian streaming series adapted from the 1987 novel by Barranquilla-born writer Marvel Moreno. The show's debut season consists of eight episodes and is scheduled to premiere globally on December 4, 2026. Production is being handled by Dynamo, the Colombian company previously involved in adapting One Hundred Years of Solitude for the platform.

About the series

Set in Barranquilla in 1958, the drama focuses on four young women—Lina, Dora, Catalina, and Beatriz—who grow up under the strict expectations of a conservative, upper-class society. As each character attempts to build her own identity and pursue individual ambitions, their bond serves as a key source of solidarity and support. Beyond personal romance and family secrets, the plot examines broader themes of female freedom, modern social shifts, and power dynamics of mid-20th-century Caribbean Colombia. Filming took place across the region, using locations such as the historic El Prado neighborhood and the beaches of Puerto Colombia.

What to expect

The trailer showcases social gatherings, family pressures, and traditional settings that illustrate the restrictive environments faced by the main characters. Specific episode runtimes are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Key project details include:

  • Main Cast: Daniella Reyes as Lina, Melanie Dell’Olmo as Dora, Francisca Estevez as Catalina, and Valentina Duque as Beatriz.
  • Supporting Cast: Features Juan Pablo Urrego, Flora Martinez, Jason Day, Michelle Gutty, Consuelo Luzardo, and Florina Lemaitre.
  • Directors & Showrunners: Directed by Claudia Pedraza and Rafael Martinez; showrunners Angelica Guerra and Adriana Aparicio lead the project.
  • Screenwriting Team: Written by Rafael Noguera, Cecilia Percy, Marcela Pelaez, and Lony Welter.

TRT 1 canlı nasıl izlenir? (TRT1 frekans bilgileri 2026)

Published on October 12, 2026

TRT 1 continues to maintain its position in Turkish broadcasting as the country's first national channel. Owned by the Turkish Radio and Television Corporation (TRT), the public network has established a tradition of producing educational, cultural, and socially sensitive programming alongside neutral reporting.

About the network

First launching on January 31, 1968, TRT 1 originally aired in black-and-white before making the transition to color in 1986. Over the years, it has served as a key destination for Turkish television series, feature films, documentaries, talk shows, and entertainment competitions. What's new

In addition to regular programming, TRT 1 has expanded its footprint in sports broadcasting. The channel broadcasts major football tournament matches, including UEFA Champions League semi-final action featuring Inter against Barcelona and Paris Saint Germain against Arsenal, as well as the UEFA Europa League semi-final between Manchester United and Athletic Bilbao. The platform also covers UEFA Conference League games.

All Of Us Are Dead Season 2's Netflix Streaming Release Window Gets Declared By Show Director JQ Lee: ‘I’ll Greet You In 2027’

Published on November 06, 2026

What's new

Director JQ Lee has announced that editing for the second season of Netflix's zombie horror series All of Us Are Dead is finished. In a November 2026 Instagram update, the director confirmed that the show will return to the platform in 2027. An exact premiere date within 2027 is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

The release timeframe follows earlier scheduling changes for the production. Principal photography for the second season took place between July 2025 and February 2026. The series was also highlighted at a Netflix Korea showcase in October 2026, where the main cast appeared alongside a "coming soon" title card, though no teaser footage or specific debut date was displayed.

About the series

Based on Joo Dong-geun's webtoon Now at Our School, the hit drama debuted its 12-episode first season on January 28, 2022. It quickly reached the Top 10 list in 91 countries and logged 124.79 million viewing hours in its first partial week. The initial story centered on students trapped at Hyosan High School during a violent viral outbreak.

The primary cast features:

  • Park Ji-hu as Nam On-jo
  • Yoon Chan-young as Lee Cheong-san
  • Cho Yi-hyun as Choi Nam-ra
  • Lomon as Lee Su-hyeok

The episode count for the second season is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

Season 2 advances the timeline by several years, shifting the setting to the capital city. Nam On-jo is now attending Seoul National University while coping with past trauma. When a new outbreak emerges in Seoul, she must reunite with her friends to navigate the crisis.

The storyline will follow up on major developments from the Season 1 finale, which saw surviving students in quarantine while human-zombie hybrid Choi Nam-ra warned of hostile hybrid groups. Additionally, actor Yoon Chan-young confirmed at the October 2026 showcase that Lee Cheong-san survived his battle in the burning building and will return. The upcoming episodes will examine city-wide emergency responses and delve further into the nature of zombie-human hybrids.

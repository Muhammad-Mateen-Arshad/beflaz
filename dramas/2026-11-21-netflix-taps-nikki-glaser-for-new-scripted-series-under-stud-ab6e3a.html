Netflix taps Nikki Glaser for new scripted series under studio partnership

Published on November 21, 2026

On November 19, 2026, Netflix announced a major deal with comedian Nikki Glaser, making her the first talent to sign with Netflix Is a Joke (NIAJ) Studios. The newly formed in-house development and production unit operates under the platform's broader comedy brand, aiming to help performers expand beyond traditional stand-up into scripted television and other creative formats.

What's new

Under this agreement, Glaser will develop, executive produce, and star in a new original scripted television series. The deal also encompasses two exclusive stand-up comedy specials, which she will similarly star in and executive produce for the streaming service.

About the series

Gloria Sanchez Productions has boarded Glaser's upcoming scripted project to executive produce alongside her. However, specific details regarding the show's direction remain undisclosed. Title, storyline, cast, episode count, production schedule, release date: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

This partnership builds on a collaboration that began with her 2019 special Nikki Glaser: Bangin'. Glaser also performed at the 2024 and 2026 Netflix Is a Joke festivals and drew widespread attention during The Roast of Tom Brady in 2024—an event that Robbie Praw, Netflix's vice president of stand-up and comedy formats, credited with accelerating her career. Her other platform credits include judging The Roast of Marshawn Lynch on Funny AF with Kevin Hart.

Glaser's work with Netflix also includes voicing Priscilla in the animated movie Steps, which premiered on November 20, 2026, and an upcoming role in the Eva Longoria-directed comedy The Fifth Wheel, scheduled for 2027.

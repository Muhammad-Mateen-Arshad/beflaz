Published on November 19, 2026

What's new

Turkish television has earned international recognition following the announcement of the nominees for the 65th Rose d’Or Awards. The global competition selected 78 finalists from 19 countries to compete across various categories. Türkiye achieved a strong showing in the lineup, securing three distinct nominations in the Soap or Telenovela category.

About the series

The shortlisted Turkish productions recognized by the awards panel are Deep in Love, Different Lives, and Sins and Roses. These shows represent Türkiye in the Soap or Telenovela category, competing against international entries from Brazil and Portugal, as well as ITV's British series Corriedale.

Specific details regarding the plot, cast, crew, production history, or episode counts for Deep in Love, Different Lives, and Sins and Roses are Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

The final results will be revealed during the official ceremony on Monday, November 30. The event will be held at Kings Place in London, hosted by Saturday Night Live UK star Ania Magliano, where the winners across all television categories will be announced.

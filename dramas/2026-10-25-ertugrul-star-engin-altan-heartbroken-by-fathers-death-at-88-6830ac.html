‘Ertugrul’ star Engin Altan heartbroken by father’s death at 88

Published on October 25, 2026

Turkish actor Engin Altan Düzyatan is mourning the death of his father, Mevlan Düzyatan, who passed away at the age of 88. The actor shared the sad news on social media, expressing his family's deep grief over their loss.

What's new

Engin Altan Düzyatan announced his father's passing publicly, writing that his family was deeply saddened. Funeral details were scheduled for Sunday, Oct. 25, in İzmir, Türkiye. Services were arranged following the noon prayer at Bostanlı Beşikçioğlu Mosque in the Karşıyaka district, with burial planned at Çeşme Çakabey Cemetery.

Specific details regarding the cause of death are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Furthermore, information on public figures attending the final ceremony remains subject to confirmation from reliable reports.

About the series

Millions of viewers know Engin Altan Düzyatan for his iconic performance as Ertuğrul Bey in the historical drama "Diriliş: Ertuğrul." His portrayal of the fearless leader earned him immense popularity and a devoted following, particularly among audiences in Pakistan who connected with the character's courage, leadership, and determination.

What to expect

Following the announcement, tributes and messages of sympathy were shared by family members and prominent figures:

  • Neslişah Alkoçlar Düzyatan: The actor's wife shared a message honoring her late father-in-law, stating that being his daughter-in-law was a great honor and that she would always miss him.
  • Hülya Koçyiğit: The veteran Turkish actress, who is the grandmother of Neslişah, offered her condolences to Engin and the family, acknowledging the pain of losing a loved one and wishing them strength and patience.

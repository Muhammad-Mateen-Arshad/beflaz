Netflix fans 'squealing' over series based on TikTok with millions of views

Published on October 26, 2026

What's new

Netflix has officially released its new workplace comedy series, Minimum Wage, making its first 14 episodes available as of October 26. The streaming platform has picked up the title for a total order of 28 episodes. Whether the remaining installments will be released as a second part or a separate season is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

The series is based on a viral short-form sketch comedy of the same name created by American High Shorts, a production company based out of a former high school in New York. Before coming to streaming, the web concept gathered millions of views on YouTube and billions of total views across platforms like Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube.

About the series

Minimum Wage centers on a stressed group of teenagers trying to run a local pizzeria while dealing with school, minimum wage pay, and an intense boss. The cast includes notable comedy talent, such as:

  • Grace Reiter, a breakout star from Saturday Night Live.
  • Randall Park, who has previously appeared in Fresh Off The Boat and The Office US.

What to expect

The adaptation keeps the rapid-fire, short-form comedic timing of the original web hit. Episodes are brief, running for just 15 minutes each, making the show quick and easy to watch.

Although Minimum Wage does not currently have enough online reviews to feature a score on Rotten Tomatoes, initial viewer responses have been very positive. Audience members have highlighted the funny plots, fast-paced jokes, and strong performances across the cast.

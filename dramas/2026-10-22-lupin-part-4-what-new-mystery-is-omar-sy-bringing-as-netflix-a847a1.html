'Lupin' Part 4: What new mystery is Omar Sy bringing as Netflix heist series returns on October 23?

Published on October 22, 2026

What's new

The popular French mystery thriller Lupin returns to Netflix on October 23, 2026, with all eight episodes of Part 4 dropping on the same day. Picking up four years after the events of Part 3, the new chapter finds Assane Diop, played by Omar Sy, serving time in prison. Assane hopes to finish his sentence so he can leave his criminal past behind and relocate to Senegal with his family. However, his plans for a quiet life are shattered when a copycat criminal executes simultaneous museum robberies under the Lupin moniker and leaves behind evidence pointing directly to Assane.

About the series

The show originally takes inspiration from Arsène Lupin, the classic gentleman thief created by French author Maurice Leblanc in 1905. Rather than directly adapting the original books, the series follows Assane as a modern character who adopts Lupin's methods—including disguises, elaborate tricks, clever codes, and false identities—to pull off his heists. For Part 4, the storyline strays further from the original novels, leaning into original concepts with Omar Sy stepping up as co-showrunner and co-producer alongside Louis Leterrier.

What to expect

To clear his name and expose the person framing him, Assane must pull off a prison escape and outsmart an enemy who understands his tricks. The four-year time jump will explore how the characters have evolved alongside key returning cast members:

  • Ludivine Sagnier as Claire, Assane's long-time partner
  • Etan Simon as Raoul, their son
  • Antoine Gouy as Benjamin Ferel, Assane's loyal friend and partner
  • Soufiane Guerrab as detective Youssef Guédira, who uses literary knowledge to track Assane
  • Shirine Boutella as police lieutenant Sofia Belkacem, who gets injured in one of the copycat robberies
  • Naky Sy Savané as Mariama, Assane's mother who was rescued in Part 3

Although Netflix has not officially confirmed Part 5, Part 4 is not framed as the end of the show. For any official announcements regarding future renewals, not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

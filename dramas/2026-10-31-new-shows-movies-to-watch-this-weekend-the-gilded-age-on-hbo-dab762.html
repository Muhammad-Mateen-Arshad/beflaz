New Shows & Movies To Watch This Weekend: ‘The Gilded Age’ on HBO Max, ‘Dexter: Resurrection’ on Paramount+ and More

Published on October 31, 2026

What's new

Streaming platforms are rolling out a fresh selection of premiering series and returning drama favorites this weekend. Headline releases across major services include the fourth season of HBO's period series The Gilded Age, the second season of the crime spinoff Dexter: Resurrection on Paramount+, and Apple TV's new mystery series Nocturne.

About the series

Several notable drama series highlight this week's lineup:

  • The Gilded Age (Season 4): Airing Sunday night on HBO and HBO Max, the new season revolves around high-society rivalries as President Grover Cleveland prepares to visit New York. George and Bertha Russell, played by Morgan Spector and Carrie Coon, remain on fragile terms following Bertha's decision to marry off their daughter to a Duke. Meanwhile, Agnes van Rhijn, portrayed by Christine Baranski, stakes her standing on hosting a dinner party for the President.
  • Dexter: Resurrection (Season 2): Launching Friday on Paramount+, this New York City-set spinoff follows Dexter Morgan (Michael C. Hall) alongside his son Harrison (Jack Alcott). The new season introduces two additional killer characters performed by Brian Cox and Dan Stevens. Episodes will air on a weekly schedule.
  • Nocturne: Adapted from Lars Kepler's Lazarus novels, this ten-episode Apple TV thriller stars Liev Schreiber as Johan Lynn, a former Philadelphia homicide detective who moves to a small town and stumbles into a serial killer investigation. Stephen Graham plays the serial killer, with Zazie Beetz appearing in a supporting role. The show debuts its first two episodes on Friday, followed by weekly releases.

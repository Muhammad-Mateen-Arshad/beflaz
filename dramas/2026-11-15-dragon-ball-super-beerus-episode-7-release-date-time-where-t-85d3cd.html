‘Dragon Ball Super: Beerus’ Episode 7 Release Date, Time, Where to Watch and What to Expect

Published on November 15, 2026

What's new

The latest installment of Dragon Ball Super: Beerus arrives on Sunday, November 22. This broadcast marks a major shift for the project, launching the six-episode Golden Frieza arc immediately following the conclusion of the God of Destruction storyline in the November 15 episode titled "Battle of Gods."

Subtitled episodes drop weekly on Sundays. Viewers in Japan can tune in via Fuji TV or Prime Video, while global audiences can stream the episodes on Crunchyroll within hours of the Japanese release. Staggered availability follows on Disney Plus three days later and Netflix six days later. For audience members seeking exact streaming launch minutes in their localized catalogs, that detail is Not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Meanwhile, exclusive English, Spanish, and Portuguese dubs continue to release every Thursday on Crunchyroll.

About the series

Dragon Ball Super: Beerus serves as a modernized remake of the original anime, cutting out filler scenes while enhancing the art direction and animation quality. Expected to span only 19 episodes, it is slated to be the shortest series in the franchise's history.

Core elements of the series include:

  • Significantly condensed pacing, turning multi-episode fights into focused single installments.
  • Upgraded visual quality compared to the original 2015 broadcast.
  • Simulcast streaming options across international territories.

What to expect

The upcoming storyline focuses on the return of Frieza, who is restored by Frieza Force remnants Sorbet and Tagoma. After undergoing four months of rigorous training, the villain achieves his Golden form. On the hero side, Vegeta convinces Whis to train him alongside Goku on Beerus's planet by offering Bulma's instant ramen. Their preparation centers on mastering God ki, leading directly to the unveiling of Super Saiyan Blue and giving Vegeta a much larger focus in the story.

What Time Does Netflix Release New Movies and TV Series?

Published on November 05, 2026

Streaming fans eager to watch upcoming television seasons and movies often wonder when fresh content becomes available on Netflix. Release times depend heavily on a viewer's local time zone and the category of the release.

What's new

Netflix Originals drop globally at midnight Pacific Time, calculated from the United States West Coast where the service is headquartered. Consequently, subscribers across the world experience different local launch times—some watching late at night, others waking up early, or waiting until the end of the school or workday. Changes in daylight saving time, such as shifts between British Summer Time and Greenwich Mean Time, also alter the local arrival hour.

About the series

Release patterns vary across different types of media offered on the platform:

  • Original Content: Shows like Stranger Things, Outer Banks, Wednesday, and Bridgerton arrive simultaneously according to Pacific Time.
  • Licensed Shows: Content lacking the Netflix N label, such as Brassic, releases at midnight local time in each respective territory.
  • Weekly Dramas and Anime: Select Korean dramas and anime series uploaded weekly may deviate from standard release schedules, arriving slightly earlier or later based on file delivery.

Episode counts, cast information, and exact air dates for upcoming seasons: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

Unlike TV shows and films, mobile games available on the platform do not currently adhere to a set release format. New game titles typically launch during mid-to-late afternoon in Britain, which aligns with early-to-mid morning hours in the United States. Users who pre-register for upcoming games will find them ready on their devices as soon as they go live.

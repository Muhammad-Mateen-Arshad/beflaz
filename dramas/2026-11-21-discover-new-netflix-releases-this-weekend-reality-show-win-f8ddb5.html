Discover New Netflix Releases This Weekend: Reality Show 'Win the Mall' and More

Published on November 21, 2026

What's new

Netflix is expanding its streaming lineup with a fresh selection of entertainment options arriving between November 20 and November 22. The latest updates bring viewers a mix of genres, including a dramatic narrative based on a famous financial scandal, a strategy-filled reality competition set inside a shopping mall, and an investigative documentary covering historical television figures.

About the series

One of the prominent additions to the platform's drama catalog is The Altruists. This scripted series depicts the real-life story of FTX exchange founders Caroline Ellison, played by Emmy winner Julia Garner, and Sam Bankman-Fried, portrayed by Anthony Boyle. The narrative details how the ambitious duo built a major cryptocurrency movement before their empire unravelled amid accusations of fraud and the theft of $8 billion. Further release details: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

In unscripted streaming, Netflix is rolling out Win the Mall. Hosted by influencer Hailey Bailey and crafted by the producers behind Million Dollar Secret, the reality show puts pairs of contestants inside a lavish shopping center. Over the course of eight episodes, two-person teams compete to secure shopfronts that provide crucial strategic benefits in their quest to win a $1 million grand prize.

Rounding out the arrivals is the documentary Primetime Preacher$. The project takes an in-depth look at the boom and collapse of 1980s televangelism, highlighting major media empires led by figures including Jim Bakker, Tammy Faye Bakker, Jerry Falwell, and Jimmy Swaggart.

What to expect

Audiences checking out the new streaming choices can look forward to several distinct formats:

  • The Altruists: A true-crime financial drama detailing high-stakes ambition, cryptocurrency markets, and major legal downfall.
  • Win the Mall: An eight-episode competition combining strategic gameplay, retro shopping vibes, and team tactics for a massive cash prize.
  • Primetime Preacher$: A retrospective documentary utilizing extensive archival footage alongside commentary from media insiders and journalists.

Netflix’s ‘Trinity’ Sets December Release Date, Drops Tense New Trailer (TV News Roundup)

Published on November 04, 2026

What's new

Netflix has officially scheduled the premiere date and released a tense introductory trailer for its upcoming thriller series, Trinity. The political drama is set to launch on December 1. Meanwhile, television platforms are rolling out updates for several high-profile streaming series, including promotional events for Hulu's All's Fair and the upcoming regional launch of Sherlock and Daughter.

About the series

The seven-episode drama Trinity stars Gugu Mbatha-Raw as Commander Katherine Decker and Richard Madden as Secretary of Defense Webb Preston. The story follows Decker as she forms an unexpected bond with Preston and gets pulled into hazardous schemes centered around a classified initiative named Trinity. Throughout the series, she is divided between supporting Preston's strategy to prevent World War III and bringing his secrets to light.

The supporting cast for the production includes:

  • Marcia Gay Harden
  • JD Pardo
  • Bruce Greenwood
  • Robert Wisdom
  • Kirk Acevedo
  • Ben Cotton
  • James Remar
  • Jason Ralph
  • Mark O’Brien

Jed Mercurio serves as showrunner, writer, and executive producer. Additional executive producers include Mendy Mericle alongside Jimmy Mulville for Hat Trick Mercurio. Michael Cuesta directed and executive produced the first two episodes.

What to expect

Beyond Trinity, drama viewers have more premiering series on the horizon. Hulu is releasing Season 2 of All's Fair on November 4, featuring a cast that includes Kim Kardashian, Naomi Watts, Niecy Nash-Betts, Teyana Taylor, Matthew Noszka, Cauã Reymond, Sarah Paulson, and Glenn Close. To celebrate the launch, Hulu is hosting themed truck activations in Los Angeles, New York City, and Miami on November 4, followed by pop-up experiences with giveaways on November 7.

Furthermore, the eight-part mystery drama Sherlock and Daughter—starring David Thewlis, Blu Hunt, and Dougray Scott—will premiere in the U.K. and Ireland on November 5 on HBO Max, releasing weekly through December 24. For additional details regarding international broadcast rights outside these regions, not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

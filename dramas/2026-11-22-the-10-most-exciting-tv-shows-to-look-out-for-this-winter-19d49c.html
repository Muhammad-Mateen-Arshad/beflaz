The 10 Most Exciting TV Shows To Look Out For This Winter

Published on November 22, 2026

What's new

As cold weather sets in across December, January, and February heading into 2027, streaming platforms are preparing a diverse television lineup for the winter season. Viewers looking to binge shows during shorter days will find everything from animated superhero stories and medical thrillers to sweeping historical sagas and ambitious sci-fi adaptations.

About the series

The upcoming schedule features brand-new adaptations alongside long-awaited returning favorites across several networks:

  • Harry Potter: HBO is introducing a major television adaptation starring Dominic McLaughlin as Harry Potter, planning one season for each of the seven books.
  • Bloodaxe: Debuting Jan. 20, 2027, on Prime Video, this historical thriller by Michael Hirst focuses on 10th-century Viking warrior Erik Bloodaxe and his wife Gunnhild as they scheme to rule Norway.
  • Neuromancer: Premiering Jan. 22, 2027, on Apple TV, this adaptation of William Gibson's classic sci-fi novel stars Callum Turner as Case.
  • Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man Season 2: Arriving Jan. 13, 2027, on Disney+, the animated series continues Peter Parker's efforts to balance high school with crime-fighting against returning villains.
  • Blue Eye Samurai Season 2: Netflix's historical animated drama follows mixed-race warrior Mizu on a revenge quest to London while maintaining storyline developments in Japan.
  • 3 Body Problem Season 2: Netflix's sci-fi adaptation returns in 2027 to cover Liu Cixin's novel The Dark Forest.
  • Returning Titles: Other arrivals include Season 3 of The Pitt featuring Dr. Robby (Noah Wyle), Ahsoka Season 2, and Adult Swim's conspiracy thriller Common Side Effects Season 2.

What to expect

Subscribers can look forward to distinct plot developments across these series. 3 Body Problem turns its focus to humanity's fight against alien forces, while Ahsoka Season 2 picks up four years after its 2023 debut, following Ahsoka and Sabine Wren in Peridia alongside connections to the Mortis Gods. Meanwhile, The Pitt relies on a real-time format as Dr. Robby returns from a sabbatical to work emergency night shifts.

Full cast lists, complete broadcast schedules, and episode counts for several titles are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

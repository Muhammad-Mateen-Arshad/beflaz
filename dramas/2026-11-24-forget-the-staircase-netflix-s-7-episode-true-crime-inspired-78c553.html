Forget 'The Staircase,' Netflix's 7-Episode True-Crime-Inspired Thriller Gets Under Your Skin

Published on November 24, 2026

About the series

Originally released on October 13, 2022, Netflix's seven-episode thriller The Watcher captivated streaming audiences with its tense mystery. Produced by Ryan Murphy, the first season is loosely based on the real-life experience of Derek and Maria Broaddus, who received threatening letters from an anonymous figure after purchasing a home in Westfield, New Jersey. In the show, Naomi Watts and Bobby Cannavale portray Nora and Dean Brannock, a couple whose dream home turns into a nightmare as unsettling notes spark overwhelming paranoia.

As threats mount, Dean loses his job while Nora faces social rejection at her country club, which she attends with eccentric real estate agent Karen, played by Jennifer Coolidge. Driven to find answers, the couple launches a chaotic personal investigation into everyone around them, navigating dead ends while suspecting neighbors like Mo (Margo Martindale) and even their teenage daughter Ellie (Isabel Gravitt).

What's new

Following strong viewing numbers upon its debut, the project was officially greenlit for a second installment. Production for Season 2 commenced in New York on October 27, 2026, and is scheduled to wrap up on March 9, 2027. A premiere date is not specified in the source; please check the official notice, though the upcoming season is anticipated to land on Netflix in mid-2027 or early 2028.

What to expect

Rather than staying focused strictly on the Brannock family, the second season will pivot to a fresh storyline centered around a college fraternity. The narrative will feature a pledge, a freshman whose night turns into terror, and a senior frat brother. Despite the change in setting, original stars Naomi Watts, Bobby Cannavale, and Jennifer Coolidge are all confirmed to return.

Season 2 will also introduce several new cast members:

  • Madelaine Petsch
  • Evan Adams
  • Owen Painter
  • Quintessa Swindell
  • Veronica Dunne
  • Mia Kaplan
  • Langston Lee
  • Linda Reiter

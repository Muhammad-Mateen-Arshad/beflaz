What is Dr. Mehmet Oz's Net Worth and Salary?

Published on November 26, 2026

Television personality and surgeon Dr. Mehmet Oz has generated significant public attention across broadcast media and public office. Born to Turkish immigrants Suna and Mustafa, Oz built a career spanning medicine, publishing, and television hosting before moving into government leadership.

What's new

Dr. Oz was named by President-elect Donald Trump on November 19, 2024, to serve as administrator of the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS). After pledging in February 2025 to sell his healthcare stock holdings, he was confirmed by the Senate in a 53–45 vote and assumed office on April 8, 2025. Prior to this appointment, he ran for a U.S. Senate seat in Pennsylvania in November 2021, becoming the first Muslim candidate nominated by a major party before losing to Democrat John Fetterman. Additionally, Columbia University cut ties with him in May 2022.

  • He completed a two-week stint as a guest host on "Jeopardy!" in March 2021.

What to expect

Over his career, Dr. Oz has built a net worth of $300 million, controlling assets valued between $104 million and $422 million with his wife, Lisa. Educated at Harvard University and the University of Pennsylvania, his medical achievements include patenting the MitraClip device in 1997 and co-founding Sharecare, Inc. in 2010. He is also the author of several bestselling books, including titles in the "YOU" series, "Healing from the Heart," and "Food Can Fix It."

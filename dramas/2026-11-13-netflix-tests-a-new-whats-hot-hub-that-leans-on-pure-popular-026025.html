Netflix Tests a New “What’s Hot” Hub That Leans on Pure Popularity

Published on November 13, 2026

Finding something to stream can be a challenge, but Netflix is experimenting with a dedicated hub designed to simplify content selection. The platform is currently testing a popularity-focused destination marked by a prominent orange "HOT" header at the top. Accessible on TV screens and mobile devices, this page prioritizes direct viewing data over individualized personalized rows, mixing original titles with licensed programming across multiple stacked categories.

What's new

The experimental layout relies primarily on real-time viewer trends rather than standard algorithmic recommendations. Key sections highlighted on the hub include:

  • Buzzing Now: Blends recent releases with legacy titles gaining renewed attention, including The Hunting Wives, Lupin, and Gossip Girl.
  • Top 10 Charts: Displays daily updated charts. Featured film entries include Reminders of Him, The Gospel of Christmas, and Funny Money: The Dane Cook Story. Top series include Taken: Natalee Holloway, Black Doves, and From.
  • Hits From Around the World: Highlights international content such as Godzilla Minus One alongside K-dramas featuring weekly episode drop tags.
  • Genre Rows: Dedicated lists like Popular in Romance led by Bridgerton, Popular in Horror featuring Stranger Things, Interview with the Vampire, and Midnight Mass, and Popular in Anime showcasing One Piece.
  • Kids Content: Provides focused Top 10 rows for children's movies and television shows.

What to expect

This test forms part of a broader navigation test that uses specialized modules and dedicated hubs to help users browse outside of the typical homepage scroll. Prominent labels such as New Season, New Episode, Recently Added, and Leaving Soon are integrated throughout to give viewers immediate context on trending shows.

Netflix unveils period drama series The Age of Innocence that fans of East of Eden will love

Published on November 11, 2026

What's new

Drama fans looking for their next major streaming fix have an exciting premiere to mark on their calendars. Netflix has officially announced the release date and shared a first look for its upcoming limited series adaptation of Edith Wharton's 1920 classic novel, The Age of Innocence. All eight episodes of the limited series will be released all at once on Thursday, Feb. 18, 2027. The project comes from writer and showrunner Emma Frost, who previously wrote Netflix's film adaptation of The Woman in Cabin 10.

About the series

Set in 19th-century New York during the Gilded Age, the period drama focuses on an especially fraught love triangle while exploring themes of freedom, duty, identity, and love in all its forms. According to the official synopsis, this fresh adaptation remains true to Wharton's book while speaking to a new generation as it traverses the ballrooms and bedrooms of its young characters, posing questions about love versus lust and whether people should ultimately be driven by their heads or their hearts.

What to expect

The production features a star-studded cast leading the central story:

  • Camila Morrone as Ellen Olenska
  • Kristine Froseth as May Welland
  • Ben Radcliffe as Newland Archer
  • Margo Martindale as Mrs. Manson Mingott, grandmother to both Ellen and May

This new title builds on Netflix's lineup of period drama releases, following the arrival of East of Eden earlier in fall 2026 and Pride and Prejudice in December 2026. As of the announcement in November 2026, no video footage has been released yet, though a teaser and full trailer are expected ahead of the show's launch. The series offers a new binge-watch option for fans ahead of Bridgerton season 5, which is scheduled to premiere later in 2027.

The Night Agent Season 4: Expected Netflix Release Date & Everything We Know

Published on November 26, 2026

Streaming fans tracking action thrillers have a major update regarding Netflix's hit drama series, The Night Agent. Production on the fourth season officially wrapped on September 30, 2026, setting up the show for its release locked in for 2027.

What's new

The thriller series will conclude with a ten-episode final season. Filming began in early May 2026 in Los Angeles, marking a move to the West Coast after past seasons shot in locations such as Canada, New York, Thailand, Türkiye, and the Dominican Republic. The production relocation was aided by a $31.6 million tax credit from California's program.

About the series

Created by Shawn Ryan and based on Matthew Quirk's novel, the Sony Pictures Television series stars Gabriel Basso as Peter Sutherland. Debut episodes launched in March 2023, drawing over 98 million views in the first 91 days. Across three seasons, the show has gathered over 297 million total views. Following a drop in viewership during season 3, Netflix announced on May 4, 2026, that season 4 would be the show's final call.

What to expect

The final season gives the storyline a planned conclusion. While specific premiere dates remain unannounced, several key details have been locked in:

  • Gabriel Basso returns as Peter Sutherland alongside a updated main cast.
  • Luciane Buchanan returns as Rose Larkin after sitting out season 3 completely.
  • The narrative will explore the Los Angeles region on a much larger scale than New York, without focusing on Hollywood or the film industry.
  • Netflix has confirmed seven new cast members joining the final season.

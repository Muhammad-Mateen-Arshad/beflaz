Everything to Know About ChiaroScuro: Netflix’s New Italian Murder Mystery Series Releasing on November 11

Published on November 10, 2026

What's new

Netflix is expanding its catalog of international mysteries with the release of ChiaroScuro on November 11, 2026. The eight-episode Italian drama blends murder mystery, crime, and art history, framing a distinct partnership within a unique setting. An official trailer has already been released to give viewers a preliminary look at the upcoming series.

About the series

The story kicks off when a body is discovered inside a museum in Rome. The crime brings together an investigating police inspector and an art expert whose knowledge and professional instincts become essential to unraveling the truth. The case takes on extra weight because it is tied directly to the art expert's personal history.

The exact character roles for the cast members are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

The production team and cast include:

  • Director: Jan Michelini
  • Writers: Francesco Arlanch, Elena Bucaccio, Umberto Gnoli, Silvia Leuzzi, and Michela Straniero
  • Producers: Luca Bernabei (producer) and Daniele Spinozzi (line producer)
  • Ensemble Cast: Pierpaolo Spollon, Andrea Lattanzi, Matilde Gioli, Romana Maggiora Vergano, Alessandro Preziosi, Aurora Giovinazzo, Gabriele Falsetta, Matteo Bassi, Corrado Invernizzi, Daniele Natali, Paz Vega, and Filippo Valeriani

What to expect

Using Rome's palaces and art galleries as a backdrop, the series steps away from conventional urban police procedurals. The contrast between the police inspector and the art expert provides an engaging dynamic, while placing a violent mystery inside a space designed to preserve historic artifacts adds a distinct narrative layer across all eight episodes.

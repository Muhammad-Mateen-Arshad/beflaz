Netflix confirms Stranger Things prequel spinoff Other Worlds for 2028 release

Published on November 06, 2026

What's new

On November 6, recognized as Stranger Things Day, Netflix officially unveiled a new live-action prequel series titled Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure. Following the three-part fifth season of the main show in 2025, this newly announced spinoff will take viewers to a new era when it releases on the streaming platform in 2028.

About the series

Departing from the familiar town of Hawkins, the narrative relocates to Marion, Nevada. Positioned in the 1950s, the story takes place prior to the events of Stranger Things and before the stage play Stranger Things: The First Shadow. Filming for the project is set to take place in New Mexico.

Key behind-the-scenes details include:

  • Creative Team: The Duffer Brothers collaborated with Kate Trefry, a writer known for Stranger Things: The First Shadow and Something Very Bad Is Going to Happen.
  • Directing: Joe Wright will direct the first three episodes.
  • Total Episode Count: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Cast and Characters: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Exact Release Date: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

An initial announcement teaser offers a glimpse of the upcoming series, featuring familiar imagery such as a radio and bicycle lying near a warning sign. The scene shifts past the sign toward a cloudy, ominous sky above Marion, Nevada, on April 19, 1955, where a chilling explosion erupts within the clouds. Accompanied by a musical score tailored to its 1950s environment, the clip concludes with the unveiling of the official Other Worlds title logo.

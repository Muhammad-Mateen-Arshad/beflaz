I completely forgot this Twilight show existed before Netflix renewed it for season 2

Published on November 07, 2026

For fans following streaming developments, Netflix has officially committed to two seasons of the upcoming animated project Midnight Sun ahead of its series premiere. The pickup was confirmed by Kevin Beggs, Chairman of the Lionsgate Television Group, during a recent Lionsgate earnings call.

What's new

Beggs revealed that Netflix placed a two-season adult animation order for the show. He explained that author Stephenie Meyer and her team are directly involved in managing the process alongside Lionsgate and Netflix. Securing two seasons at once allows production on both installments to occur closer together, helping reduce costs by eliminating large gaps between initial premiere dates and secondary production schedules.

About the series

First announced in September 2024, Midnight Sun is an animated adaptation based on Meyer's 2020 companion novel to her best-selling Twilight book series. The story retells events entirely from the perspective of Edward Cullen. Meyer serves as an executive producer on the adaptation. Lionsgate views the project as a major continuation and expansion of the existing franchise and maintains high expectations for its debut.

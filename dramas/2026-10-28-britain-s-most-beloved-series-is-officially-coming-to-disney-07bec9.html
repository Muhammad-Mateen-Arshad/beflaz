Britain's Most Beloved Series Is Officially Coming to Disney+

Published on October 28, 2026

Significant updates are coming to one of the UK's most popular television shows, including major cast changes and a new streaming platform deal.

What's new

Channel 4 has struck a deal with The Walt Disney Company to expand its digital reach. Under this new agreement, The Great British Bake Off will officially become available on Disney+ starting in early 2027. The program will reside alongside other linear content inside a dedicated Channel 4-branded hub on the streaming platform.

About the series

The announcement follows recent high-profile updates inside the tent after Jasmine Mitchell won the prior installment:

  • Longtime judge Prue Leith decided to step away after nine years and 90 episodes. At 86 years old, Leith noted that she wished to spend her summer months enjoying her garden.
  • British food writer, TV cook, and personality Nigella Lawson stepped in to replace Leith, joining existing judge Paul Hollywood.

Viewer response to Lawson has been overwhelmingly positive. The latest season premiere delivered strong ratings, becoming Channel 4's top show of the week and ranking as the most-watched broadcast on British television across all platforms.

What to expect

Leadership from both organizations praised the strategic venture. Channel 4 chief executive Priya Dogra noted that teaming up with Disney+ offers fans more ways to watch the network's top programs. Adam Smith, chairman of direct-to-consumer at Disney Entertainment, highlighted that bringing Channel 4's strong UK and Ireland programming to Disney+ fits their global strategy of combining local cultural relevance with wider platform access.

Further details regarding specific premiere dates or global distribution beyond the early 2027 timeline are Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

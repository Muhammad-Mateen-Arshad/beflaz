Netflix’s New 127-Minute WWII Epic Officially Arrives Next Week

Published on October 25, 2026

What's new

Netflix is continuing its recent strategy of giving select feature releases a theatrical rollout prior to their digital debuts. Among the platform's upcoming major releases is a 127-minute World War II feature titled The Mosquito Bowl. The streamer's ongoing approach to providing theatrical rollouts to handpicked titles will also extend to projects such as The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, which is scheduled for an extended run in IMAX theaters before making its streaming arrival.

About the series

The Mosquito Bowl is directed by filmmaker Peter Berg and is based on a true World War II story. The narrative focuses on a group of soldiers who participate in a historic football game in the days shortly before the Battle of Okinawa. Berg brings prior experience with military and athletic narratives, having previously directed the television series Friday Night Lights alongside feature films such as Lone Survivor and Battleship. His past projects with Netflix include the action comedy Spenser Confidential, the Western limited series American Primeval, and the 2023 release Painkiller.

What to expect

The upcoming feature includes the following main cast members:

  • Bill Skarsgård
  • Nicholas Galitzine
  • Ray Nicholson
  • Tom Francis

The Mosquito Bowl is scheduled to premiere in theaters on October 30, followed by its release on Netflix on November 25. Meanwhile, The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth will hit IMAX screens on November 25 before arriving on the platform.

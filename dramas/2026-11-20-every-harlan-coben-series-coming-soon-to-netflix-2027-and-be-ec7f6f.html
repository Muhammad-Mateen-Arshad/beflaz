Every Harlan Coben Series Coming Soon to Netflix: 2027 and Beyond

Published on November 20, 2026

What's new

Harlan Coben is expanding his successful partnership with Netflix, adding a slate of fresh thriller projects to the streaming platform. Following thirteen existing series—including the hit drama I Will Find You—the streamer has five new Coben adaptations in development alongside an upcoming podcast. Coben remains deeply involved across every project as an executive producer through his banner, Final Twist Productions.

About the series

Two major adapted series are preparing to lead the platform's upcoming television releases:

  • The Woods (UK Adaptation): Created as an eight-part limited series, this British thriller completed filming in October 2026. Lead writer and executive producer Danny Brocklehurst collaborates with directors Andy De Emmony, Claire Tailyour, and Isher Sahota. Tom Bateman stars as barrister Paul ‘Cope’ Copeland, alongside Michelle Keegan as Lucy Silverfield. The show is expected to premiere in the first half of 2027.
  • Myron Bolitar: Designed as Netflix’s first multi-season American show derived from Coben’s novels, the drama features Colin Woodell in the title role as an injured NBA player turned sports agent. Created in partnership with David E. Kelley, Kyle Long, and Greg Yaitanes, the cast also includes KJ Apa and Diane Guerrero. Filming launched in July 2026 throughout New York and New Jersey, aiming for a 2027 release.

What to expect

In addition to the primary series, Netflix is expanding the author's universe with several other slated titles:

  • The Innocent (US Adaptation): Commissioned in November 2026, this English-language remake stands as the 15th Coben project for Netflix, with an expected arrival in 2028.
  • All We Ever Wanted & Six Years: A new series and a feature film adaptation are actively in development. Specific debut dates are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • What’s Your Plot Twist? with Harlan Coben: First announced in September 2026, this original podcast project has a landing page live on the platform, though an exact premiere date is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

The Spy Thriller That Inspired ‘Reacher’ Officially Rules Out 2026 Return

Published on November 15, 2026

What's new

Fans awaiting the return of Peacock's spy thriller drama The Day of the Jackal will have to wait longer than initially anticipated. Despite early optimism surrounding its renewal, the streaming service has officially confirmed that the second season will not premiere in 2026, pushing its release back to 2027.

Behind the scenes, the series is undergoing a key leadership change. Original writer and showrunner Ronan Bennett will transition into a producer role for the upcoming season. Taking over as showrunner and writer for Season 2 is David Harrower.

About the series

Adapted from the famous spy novel by Frederick Forsyth, the series debuted on Peacock at the end of 2024. Author Lee Child has previously noted that Forsyth's books served as an inspiration when he created the Jack Reacher novels, linking the origins of both action franchises. The main cast features several notable actors:

  • Eddie Redmayne as the Jackal, an elusive professional assassin
  • Lashana Lynch as Bianca Pullman, a ruthless British intelligence officer
  • Richard Dormer
  • Lia Williams

The plot follows the Jackal as he completes high-stakes hits for top bidders until he encounters a fierce British intelligence agent determined to track him down.

What to expect

Exact storylines for the upcoming installment are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. However, the show remains Peacock's key answer to competitor thrillers like Prime Video's Reacher, which has already aired four seasons and has additional seasons in development along with its spin-off Neagley.

For now, viewers will need to wait until 2027 to see how the conflict between the elusive assassin and intelligence forces unfolds in Season 2.

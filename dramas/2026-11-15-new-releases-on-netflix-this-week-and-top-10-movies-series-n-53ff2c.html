New Releases on Netflix This Week and Top 10 Movies & Series: November 14, 2026

Published on November 15, 2026

What's new

Netflix in the United States has expanded its catalog with 63 new titles this week. Major additions across the platform include returning series, fresh licensed movies, and international productions. Platform arrivals feature all six seasons of the series Power making their debut on Netflix US, alongside the return of reality competition series The Devil's Plan for its third season. Historical drama fans can watch the third and final season of The Empress, while festive additions continue to arrive with titles such as Sweden's The Christmas Switch and The Gospel of Christmas.

About the series

One of the primary featured additions is the eight-episode English-language horror series The Body. Created, written, and directed by Quinn Shephard in her debut project for the platform, the show is executive produced alongside Riley Keough. Set at a Catholic high school, the plot focuses on the Lions dance team and captains Anya and Elise. Following a botched Halloween initiation for incoming team members, the group begins experiencing strange visions that trigger widespread panic across their town.

Key cast members involved in the production include:

  • Kristina Bogic and Sara Boustany in lead roles
  • Nnamdi Asomugha, Louisa Krause, Sofia Wylie, and Gabby Windey in supporting roles

What to expect

Subscribers seeking drama and mystery can explore several prominent additions across genres:

  • The mystery thriller Heartland stars Jessica Chastain as Misty Jones, a former country music star pulled back to Nashville to search for her missing niece.
  • The romantic drama Reminders of Him follows Kenna, a mother who returns to her hometown after spending five years in prison, attempting to reunite with the young daughter she has never known.
  • International additions include the Spanish series Alicia's Law, the French title The Good Lawyer, Hebrew series Bros, Indonesian film Never Surrender, and the Portuguese release The Unthinkable Daughter.

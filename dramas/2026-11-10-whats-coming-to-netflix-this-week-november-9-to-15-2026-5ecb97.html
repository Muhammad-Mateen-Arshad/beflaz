What’s Coming to Netflix This Week: November 9 to 15, 2026

Published on November 10, 2026

What's new

Netflix is rolling out a varied slate of releases between November 9 and November 15, 2026. Streaming fans can look forward to historical sagas, multi-season crime shows, and feature-length releases hitting the platform throughout the week.

About the series

For viewers drawn to period sagas, the third and final season of the German historical drama The Empress is set to arrive. Spanning six episodes, the season picks up after the devastating Sardinian War. Devrim Lingnau returns as Empress Elisabeth, who finds her imperial marriage on thin ice when Franz (Philip Froissant) returns home traumatized. The plot deepens as Elisabeth forms a connection with Count Andrássy, played by Dejan Bućin, forcing her to choose between following her heart or serving her people.

Also joining the platform are all six seasons of the crime drama Power, created by Courtney A. Kemp and executive produced by Curtis “50 Cent” Jackson. Omari Hardwick stars as James “Ghost” St. Patrick, a New York City nightclub owner attempting to transition out of his secret role as a drug kingpin. The cast features Joseph Sikora, Naturi Naughton, and Lela Loren. Viewers can also watch the companion aftershow Power Confidential, hosted by Terrence J.

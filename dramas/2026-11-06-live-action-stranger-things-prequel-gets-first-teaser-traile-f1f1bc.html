Live-Action Stranger Things Prequel Gets First Teaser Trailer, 2028 Netflix Release Window

Published on November 06, 2026

What's new

Netflix has unveiled the first teaser trailer and a 2028 release window for its upcoming live-action prequel series, Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure. Announced on Stranger Things Day, the preview introduces the tagline "Before Hawkins, there was Marion" and points to a specific date and setting: April 19, 1955, in Marion, Nevada.

The teaser shows a discarded bicycle lying outside a fenced-off restricted zone bearing a sign that warns deadly force is authorized. The camera moves past a brightly lit research facility at night toward the night sky, offering a quick glimpse of what appears to be a UFO.

About the series

The series is developed by Matt and Ross Duffer alongside showrunner and writer Kate Trefry. Production will take place in New Mexico, with Golden Globe-winning director Joe Wright slated to direct the first three episodes. Executive partners on the series include Shawn Levy and Netflix executive Jinny Howe. Specific actor attachments and the total episode count are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

Set during the 1950s, the prequel occurs before the events of the original show and the stage play Stranger Things: The First Shadow. The creators describe the spinoff as a fresh start built around several distinct elements:

  • A brand-new setting and independent mythology
  • All-new heroes and villains
  • No returning common characters from the primary series

Despite taking place in a new location with fresh faces, the series is set to clarify loose ends left behind by the Season 5 finale. Specifically, it will address how the glowing briefcase rock carrying Mind Flayer particles originated and found its way into our world before possessing young Henry Creel.

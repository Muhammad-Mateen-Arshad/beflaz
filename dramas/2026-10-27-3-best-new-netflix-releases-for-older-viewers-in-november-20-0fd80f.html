3 Best New Netflix Releases For Older Viewers In November 2026

Published on October 27, 2026

What's new

Streaming platform Netflix is expanding its catalog in November 2026 with 120 new films and television shows. Among this expansive lineup are several high-profile drama returns, international releases, and standout thrillers curated for audiences looking for engaging, sophisticated stories.

About the series

Leading the drama picks is the second season of Black Doves, scheduled to premiere on November 5. The series stars Keira Knightley as a British spy navigating a covert double life while married to an unsuspecting senior British official. Working alongside a lethal assassin played by Ben Whishaw, her loyalties to her family and her secret intelligence group continue to be tested. Viewers looking to catch up on the story can stream season one now.

Another major drama return is The Hunting Wives Season 2, arriving on November 26. Set in Texas, this steamy series follows characters Sophie and Margo amidst power struggles, murder, and tangled personal relationships. The cast features Brittany Snow, Malin Akerman, and John Stamos, offering classic soap-opera suspense and high-stakes intrigue.

Subscribers can also look out for several other drama series launching throughout the month, including:

  • Alicia's Law and ChiaroScuro (November 11)
  • The Empress Season 3 and Ethos Season 2 (November 12)
  • The Law of Beasts (November 18)

What to expect

In addition to traditional episodic dramas, the November schedule highlights the 2025 supernatural horror feature Obsession, which arrives on November 17. Written and directed by 25-year-old filmmaker Curry Barker, the plot follows a shop clerk played by Michael Johnston who uses a novelty spell to make his co-worker, played by Inde Navarrette, obsessed with him. The low-budget film achieved global box office success, earning over $500 million worldwide by building tension naturally rather than relying on constant jump scares.

Paul Wesley Latest To Join Netflix’s ‘Icebreaker’

Published on November 12, 2026

What's new

Actor Paul Wesley has joined the cast of Netflix’s upcoming college romance drama series Icebreaker. Best known for his roles as Stefan Salvatore in The Vampire Diaries and James T. Kirk in Star Trek: Strange New Worlds, Wesley will portray Coach Neil Faulkner. Filming for the project is currently taking place in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada.

Wesley joins a cast that includes:

  • Daisy Jelley
  • Connor Simos
  • Gabriel Tierney
  • Auli’i Cravalho
  • Peter Berry
  • Chaneil Kular
  • Devin Craig
  • Aidan Shaw

About the series

The television adaptation is based on Hannah Grace’s bestseller Icebreaker, the lead title in her Maple Hill book series. Originally published in November 2022 before receiving a broad release in 2023, the book has logged 70 straight weeks on the New York Times bestseller list. It has sold nearly 5 million copies globally across 34 territories and has been translated into 24 languages.

Jade Halley Bartlett serves as the creator of the series. Amanda Lasher leads the project as showrunner and writer. Both executive produce alongside author Hannah Grace, as well as Alex Cooper, Matt Kaplan, and Mina Lefevre for Unwell Productions.

What to expect

The narrative focuses on transfer student Anastasia Allen (Daisy Jelley), a figure skater set on winning Olympic gold, and Nate Hawkins (Connor Simos), a star hockey player striving for the NHL. When an unexpected incident forces both of their teams to share the exact same ice rink, strict discipline gives way to an intense attraction.

An official release date is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Beloved Greek Series Maestro in Blue Finally Sets 6-Part Season 4 Release Date for Netflix

Published on November 19, 2026

What's new

Netflix's first Greek Original series, Maestro in Blue, is set to return for its fourth and final season on December 18, 2026. The six-episode farewell season will launch globally on Netflix ahead of its television broadcast on Greece's MEGA channel. Netflix retains a two-year exclusive streaming window before the broadcaster can air the final episodes. For exact regional release times, it is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

Show creator Christoforos Papakaliatis once again serves as the writer, director, and lead star for the concluding chapter. Speaking at the 67th Thessaloniki International Film Festival, Papakaliatis confirmed that Season 4 will definitively be the last. Production completed earlier this year following shoots in Paxos, Athens, and Budapest.

The finale season will feature longer episode durations:

  • Standard episodes are scheduled to run between 60 and 90 minutes.
  • The series finale will run between 105 and 110 minutes.

The popular drama has recorded 130.5 million viewing hours on Netflix since early 2023. Returning cast members include Haris Alexiou, Maria Kavogianni, and Marissa Triantafyllidou. Six new actors join the cast for the final run, including Giorgos Chrysostomou and Nikos Karathanos.

What to expect

The story moves back to Paxos to navigate the chaotic consequences of the Season 3 finale, which ended with corrupt mayor Fanis murdered by his mafia partners alongside his wife Sofia. Preview footage indicates that returning characters Orestis and Klelia will be forced to navigate uncertain paths upon arriving back on the island.

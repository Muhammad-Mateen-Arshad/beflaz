This Week OTT Releases – New Movies and Series on Netflix, Prime Video, Aha, ZEE5 and Others – 27 November 2026

Published on November 26, 2026

What's new

Streaming platforms are presenting a wide selection of new digital premieres for the weekend of 27–29 November 2026. Viewers can access fresh titles across major platforms including Netflix, ZEE5, Aha, Sun NXT, MX Player by Prime Video, Lionsgate Play, and Sony LIV. The updated lineup offers productions in languages such as Hindi, Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, and English.

About the series

Several major web series and digital feature releases head to streaming screens this week:

  • Drishyam: The Conclusion – Directed by Abhishek Pathak, the Hindi crime thriller arrives on Netflix on 27 November 2026, starring Ajay Devgn as Vijay Salgaonkar alongside Tabu, Shriya Saran, and Jaideep Ahlawat.
  • Satyameva – Premiering on ZEE5 on 27 November, this Malayalam courtroom drama directed by Deepu Karunakaran features Rony David Raj and focuses on a lawyer named Rajaram navigating justice and redemption.
  • Octopus – This Tamil ZEE5 original series, written and directed by Jeyachandra Hashmi, revolves around neighborhood residents acting as detectives after a young woman disappears.
  • Corporate Love Story – Directed by Pannaga Bharana for ZEE5 Kannada, this romantic workplace drama follows ambitious designer Nitya (Sitara Doss) and product head Arjun (Pranav Sridhar) within Bengaluru's startup ecosystem.
  • Raathiri – Premiering 27 November on Aha in Tamil and Telugu, this four-story horror anthology focuses on themes of fear, featuring Divya as Manju and Natty as Sabarisan.

What to expect

The weekend lineup also introduces several unique film titles across different genres:

  • Ishaara – A Campus Beats Movie – Shantanu Maheshwari and Shruti Sinha star in this romantic youth feature directed by Anirudhha Rajdherkar and Mandar Kurundkar, streaming free on MX Player by Prime Video from 27 November.
  • Enemy of the Smuggler – Written and directed by T. Samaya Murali, this feature film starring Saran, JD Chakravarthy, and Lal arrives on Sun NXT in Tamil and Telugu.
  • April X – Directed by Michel K. Parandi, this English sci-fi action thriller follows a search through a criminal underworld and is available on Lionsgate Play.
  • Hanuman Ansh – Written and directed by Vishal Chaturvedi, this spiritual drama starring Shobhinaw Satyaa debuts on Sony LIV on 27 November 2026 before launching on Netflix on 29 November 2026 in multiple regional languages.

Exact episode counts or runtimes for specific titles are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

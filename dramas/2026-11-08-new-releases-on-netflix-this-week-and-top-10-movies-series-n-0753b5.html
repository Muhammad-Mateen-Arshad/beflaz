New Releases on Netflix This Week and Top 10 Movies & Series: November 8, 2026

Published on November 08, 2026

What's new

Netflix experienced a packed week of additions to its United States platform between November 1 and November 8, 2026, bringing 73 new movies and series to subscribers. The fresh lineup features major international historical drama, spy thrillers, R-rated comedies, and a broad collection of 17 Telugu films arriving all at once. The platform also kicked off its 2026 holiday releases with titles like A Dog’s Perfect Christmas alongside a new one-man performance by Mike Tyson.

About the series

The standout series addition of the week is the Colombian historical drama Under Siege (originally titled Palacio). Created by Dynamo, the eight-episode Spanish-language limited series features Natalia Santa as showrunner, with Jayro Bustamante contributing as a director. Natalia Reyes, Carlos Manuel Vesga, and Robinson Díaz lead the cast.

The series launched on November 6, coinciding with the anniversary of the 1985 siege of the Palace of Justice in Bogotá. The storyline depicts the attack on the courthouse by M-19 guerrillas and the military's retaking of the location, an event that resulted in at least 94 missing or deceased individuals, including Supreme Court justices. Rather than ending with the military action, much of the show focuses on the victims' families and their twenty-year search for answers.

Also returning to the platform is the spy thriller Black Doves for its second season. Formed by series creator Joe Barton, the latest installment brings back stars Keira Knightley, Ben Whishaw, and Sarah Lancashire while shifting away from the Christmas backdrop used in season one.

What to expect

Viewers exploring the new arrivals can expect serious historical storytelling in Under Siege, which pairs high-stakes events with the emotional long-term impact on families. Meanwhile, audiences following Black Doves will see the continuing journeys of characters Helen and Sam as the new episodes work their way up the weekly charts.

Bridgerton makes history as season 5 wraps filming ahead of major change to release schedule

Published on October 28, 2026

What's new

Filming has officially wrapped on the fifth season of Bridgerton, bringing a significant change to the streaming schedule. Netflix is breaking its traditional two-year gap between installments. Following the debut of season one in 2020, season two in 2022, and split releases for season three in summer 2024 and season four in winter 2026, the fifth season is set to air early the following year. An exact release date is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

The upcoming storyline focuses on Lady Francesca Stirling, played by Hannah Dodd, and Michaela Stirling, played by Masali Baduza. Francesca is the third daughter and sixth child in the family, known for her quiet nature, love for music, and preference for solitude. After spending much of her time away from London before season three, she entered society reluctantly and formed a bond with Lord John Stirling, played by Victor Alli.

Following Lord John's death, Francesca becomes a young widow and enters a relationship with his extroverted cousin, Michaela. This dynamic marks the series' first starring same-gender romance, unfolding after a two-year gap between seasons four and five.

What to expect

Showrunner Jess Brownell has shared that season five will heavily emphasize yearning and sapphic romance, promising a narrative driven by queer joy rather than trauma. Viewers have expressed enthusiasm on social platforms for the chemistry between the lead actors, creating the ship name "Franchaela" and praising the commitment to queer representation.

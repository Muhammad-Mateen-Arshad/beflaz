Netflix Korea’s 10th Anniversary Showcase: ‘All of Us Are Dead’ Season 2, ‘Solo Leveling’ & 4 More Titles Previewed

Published on October 24, 2026

Netflix Korea recently hosted "Netflix Janchi" (Netflix Feast) at Oil Tank Culture Park in Seoul to commemorate its 10th anniversary. The event included a concert celebrating popular titles like Squid Game and KPop Demon Hunters, alongside a "What Next?" presentation previewing upcoming programming. During the event, Netflix co-CEO Ted Sarandos delivered a speech expressing his gratitude to Korean creators, crews, actors, and fans.

What's new

While six projects were presented, some anticipated previews were absent. There was no first look at the live-action Solo Leveling, no initial teaser for All of Us Are Dead season 2, and release dates for 2027 projects were omitted. Not specified in the source; please check the official notice for details on those specific titles.

About the series

The showcase highlighted several major upcoming Korean titles:

  • Possible Love: Directed by Lee Chang-dong, this film stars Sol Kyung-gu, Jeon Do-yeon, Jo Yeo-jeong, and Jo In-sung. A trailer was shown at the event. Following a select theatrical release that began on September 23, the film debuts on Netflix on November 6.
  • Tantara: Also known as Slowly and Intensely, this coming-of-age drama features Song Hye-kyo, Gong Yoo, and Kim Seol-hyun. It tells the story of individuals pursuing success during a violent era in the Korean entertainment industry. The series arrives on Netflix on December 11.
  • Grand Galaxy Hotel: Created by the team behind Hotel Del Luna, this fantasy drama stars Lee Do-hyun as Eun-ha, who becomes the new owner of a hotel for the souls of the dead after the former owner disappears. Shin Si-a also stars in the project.

What to expect

In addition to project introductions, high-profile stars including Song Hye-kyo, Gong Yoo, and Byeon Woo-seok attended the Seoul showcase in person. Audiences can look forward to these new Korean streaming titles launching across the remainder of the year and beyond.

"The Heir" from November 16th on TV Vijesti: One man, two women and secrets that change everything

Published on November 09, 2026

Fans of Turkish television have a new family saga to look forward to this season. The dramatic broadcast of "The Heir" brings a story of romance, secrets, and strict traditions directly to screen audiences.

What's new

Viewers can tune into Television Vijesti to watch "The Heir" starting on November 16th. The series will air regularly on weekdays at 19:10 PM. Total episode count: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

The story follows Serhat Jelduran, an accomplished neurosurgeon who relocated to Istanbul to build a life free from his family's rigid rules. Believing he has achieved happiness, Serhat secretly weds his love, Melek. However, a sudden return to his hometown of Urfa forces him to face his past.

To settle a long-standing blood feud between two powerful families, Serhat is expected to fulfill a childhood pledge by marrying Yildiz, a woman connected to him since birth. Taking on the responsibility of family leader—the aga—Serhat finds his situation growing complicated when Melek travels to Urfa. Finding themselves under one roof, the two women navigate a shared life marked by jealousy, betrayal, and power struggles among feuding relatives.

Cast and production details

The central cast features Ilhan Sen, Aybike Pusat, and Biran Damla Yilmaz. Additional cast members include:

  • Onur Bilge
  • Sezin Bozacı
  • Mazlum Cimen
  • Mert Dogan

Produced by Most Production under the leadership of Gul Oguz, the series is directed by Deniz Çelebi Dikilitaş and written by main screenwriter Ercan Ugur. The production incorporates scenic views of southeastern Turkey, featuring the traditional family estates and landscapes of Şanlıurfa.

What to expect

Audiences can expect a storyline driven by heavy choices between personal love and ancestral duty, framed by local customs and intense family dynamics.

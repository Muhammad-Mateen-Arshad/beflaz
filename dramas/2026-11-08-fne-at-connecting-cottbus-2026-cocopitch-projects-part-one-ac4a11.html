FNE at Connecting Cottbus 2026: cocoPITCH Projects – Part One

Published on November 08, 2026

What's new

Film New Europe (FNE) has highlighted an impressive selection of pitch projects from the 28th connecting cottbus cocoPITCH edition. The lineup showcases international stories spanning historical trauma, psychological tension, and personal identity. Highlights include a compelling Turkish psychological drama alongside projects from Romania, Croatia, the Czech Republic, Kazakhstan, and Ukraine.

About the series

One of the standout features of the presentation is Adem and the Others, a Turkish psychological drama directed by Onur Saylak, who co-wrote the script with Hakan Günday. Set during the harsh winter of 1996 in a secluded mountain village in Türkiye, the story begins when a prominent community member is killed. Cut off from official authorities by heavy snow, a young shepherd named Adem leads a group of men to transfer the wounded suspect to the gendarmerie. As secrets surface and the suspect dies on the journey, the men concoct a shared lie, oblivious to the fact that the real perpetrator remains among them.

The project delves into theme of collective guilt, truth manipulation, and social hierarchy. Produced by Ay Yapım, Liman Film, and Electric Sheep (Türkiye, Germany), the production team includes Kerem Çatay, Nadir Öperli, İrem Akbal, and Michael Reuter. Saylak previously helmed More (2017) and Chokehold (2023). The production holds an estimated budget of 1,336,794 EUR with 595,194 EUR secured, backed by Turkish Ministry of Culture production support and local distributor Bir Film.

What to expect

The pitch event also introduced several other prominent projects across different genres:

  • Aventura: A Romanian modern film noir set in September 1994, written and directed by Andrei Crețulescu, dealing with desire, revenge, and blurred moral boundaries.
  • Cargo to the South: A Croatian dark comedy road movie written and directed by Veno Mušinović, following two estranged brothers caught between rival syndicates and a truckload of 350 awakening tortoises.
  • No Place for Me in Heaven: A Czech coming-of-age road movie written by Viktorie Novotná and Darja Miková, exploring alternative lifestyles and youth autonomy.

Cast lists, episode counts, and official release dates are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

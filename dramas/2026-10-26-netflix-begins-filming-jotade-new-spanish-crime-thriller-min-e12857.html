Netflix Begins Filming ‘Jotadé’, New Spanish Crime Thriller Miniseries Starring Jose Coronado and Jaime Lorente

Published on October 26, 2026

Netflix Spain has officially announced that filming is underway for its upcoming Spanish crime thriller miniseries, Jotadé. The project is a four-episode adaptation based on Santiago Díaz’s best-selling March 2025 novel published by Alfaguara.

What's new

Netflix Spain announced on October 26th, 2026, that cameras have officially rolled on the series. The show is produced by MOD Producciones, with Fernando Bovaira serving as producer and Guillem Vidal-Folch as executive producer. Writing duties are handled by Isa Sánchez and Daniel Corpas, while Daniel Calparsoro serves as the director.

About the series

The series centers on Jotadé, a character who previously appeared in Santiago Díaz’s Indira Ramos trilogy before leading his own books. Jotadé is a sharp, honest, irreverent, and brash police officer who remains deeply loyal to his fellow officers and his Roma community. The plot follows him as he takes on a difficult mission investigating a brutal crime that masks a dangerous criminal network, operating without ever learning how to keep a low profile.

Cast details

The miniseries features a prominent ensemble cast, including:

  • Isaac Díaz in the title role of Jotadé
  • Aura Garrido as Lucía
  • Jose Coronado as Hilario Garza
  • Jaime Lorente as Tony Garza
  • Supporting roles played by María de Nati, Alberto Olmo, Melania Cruz, Carmen Avilés, Moreno Borja, Rafaela León, Antonio Zafra, and Álvaro Rico
  • A special appearance by Javier Gutiérrez

What to expect

As for when the miniseries will premiere on the streaming service, that detail is Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

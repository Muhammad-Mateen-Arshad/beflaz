Netflix's New Crime Series Officially Becomes No. 1 TV Show Just One Day After Release

Published on November 26, 2026

What's new

A fresh true crime release has rapidly climbed to the top of the global streaming charts. The three-part docuseries The Trials of Karen Read captured the No. 1 spot for television shows on Netflix in the United States just one day after its release. Data gathered by FlixPatrol shows that after securing the top position on November 24, 2026, the show retained its lead on November 25. The series also hit the top spot in numerous other countries on November 24, including Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, Ireland, Finland, Norway, Belgium, Switzerland, Cyprus, South Africa, and the United Arab Emirates.

About the series

All three episodes of the series premiered on Netflix on November 23, 2026. The storyline centers on the legal proceedings surrounding the death of John O'Keefe, a well-liked Boston Police officer who passed away at a hospital after being found unconscious outside in the snow. His girlfriend at the time, Karen Read, was subsequently charged with second-degree murder, manslaughter while operating a motor vehicle under the influence of alcohol, and leaving the scene of an accident resulting in death. Further crew or cast details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

The docuseries tracks the criminal investigation and court trials involving Read following O'Keefe's tragic death. It also explores the intense online commentary and debates surrounding the high-profile case. To cover the story, the production incorporates:

  • Interviews with family members and friends of John O'Keefe.
  • Insights and commentary from trial jurors and lead investigators.
  • Archival comments previously made by Karen Read.

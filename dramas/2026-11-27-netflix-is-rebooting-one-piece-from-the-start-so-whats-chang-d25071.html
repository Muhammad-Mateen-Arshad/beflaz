Netflix is rebooting One Piece from the start, so what’s changed and when is it releasing?

Published on November 27, 2026

Streaming fans looking to dive into Eiichiro Oda’s iconic manga are getting a streamlined starting point with a brand-new anime adaptation titled The One Piece. Reimagining the opening narrative with modern visuals, the upcoming project offers a fresh entry into the franchise.

About the series

The story focuses on Monkey D. Luffy, a young boy who dreams of becoming the King of the Pirates. Luffy embarks on a voyage to locate the legendary treasure known as the One Piece, which was left behind by the late Pirate King, Gol D. Roger. Along his journey, he builds friendships and forms his own crew, the Straw Hat Pirates. The series begins at the start of the saga, adapting the East Blue storyline.

What's new

Wit Studio is animating the project alongside Shueisha, Fuji Television Network, and Toei Animation. The creative team has updated the visual presentation, color choices, and set designs to emphasize a naval action-adventure atmosphere. Production details include:

  • Masashi Koizuka is directing the project, while Taku Kishimoto oversees series composition.
  • Character designs and chief animation direction are led by Kyoji Asano and Takatoshi Honda.
  • Season 1 contains seven episodes released on the same day, featuring a combined runtime of about five hours.
  • The season covers roughly the first 50 chapters of the manga, ending around Luffy's meeting with the chef Sanji at the Baratie restaurant.
  • The release is distinct from Toei Animation's ongoing 1999 series, as well as the live-action version expecting its third season in 2027.

What to expect

A teaser trailer debuted on June 24, 2026, at the Annecy International Animation Film Festival, showcasing visuals of Luffy, Shanks, Koby, Nami, Zoro, Usopp, Sanji, and Buggy the Clown. Promotional imagery also highlights Luffy's early years spent with Shanks.

The global premiere is set for February 2027. For the exact release date: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

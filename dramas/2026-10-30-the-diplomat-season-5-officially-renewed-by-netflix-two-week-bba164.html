The Diplomat season 5 officially renewed by Netflix two weeks after season 4

Published on October 30, 2026

What's new

Netflix has officially renewed the political thriller series The Diplomat for a fifth season. The announcement was made on October 29, exactly two weeks after the fourth season launched. The renewal follows the show's sustained commercial success and critical acclaim.

Since making its debut in 2023, the series has garnered more than 130 million cumulative views and spent 13 weeks on Netflix's Global Top 10 list. The show also secured its first Emmy Award win prior to its fourth season, which maintained steady viewership numbers.

About the series

The series revolves around the dramatic events of the Penn-Wyler administration and features a prominent ensemble cast:

  • Keri Russell stars in the lead role as Kate Wyler.
  • Rufus Sewell portrays Kate's husband, Vice President Hal Wyler.
  • Allison Janney plays President Grace Penn, an Emmy-winning role that became a main regular in season four alongside Bradley Whitford and Nana Mensah (Billie Appiah).
  • Ali Ahn plays Eidra Park, Ato Essandoh portrays Stuart Hayford, and Rory Kinnear stars as Nicol Trowbridge.
  • Jodie Whittaker joined the fourth season in a recurring role as Bethan Vaughan, the new Prime Minister.

What to expect

Official storylines, air dates, and confirmed cast returns for the new season are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Netflix indicated that further details regarding the fifth season will be shared at a later date, though the streamer did not mark season five as the end of the show.

The story will need to pick up after the high-stakes season four finale. Kate Wyler publicly resigned from President Grace Penn's administration in protest outside the G7 conference, with Billie and Ganon following her. Hal Wyler joined the group outside the venue, leaving his standing as Vice President uncertain. Meanwhile, President Penn's dangerous behavior was exposed to the world, and Nicol Trowbridge submitted his resignation, setting up major political changes ahead.

Who is Cemre Baysel, star of International Emmy-winning Turkish series 'Leyla'

Published on November 25, 2026

What's new

Turkish television achieved another major international milestone on Nov. 23 at the 54th International Emmy Awards in New York. The television drama Leyla claimed the award for Best Telenovela, defeating competing productions from Spain and Brazil, as well as fellow Turkish nominee Far Away (Uzak Sehir). Producer Kerem Catay and director Hilal Saral accepted the honor on behalf of production company Ay Yapim.

This victory marks the fourth time a project from Türkiye has won the Emmy category reserved for serialized dramas made outside the United States. Previous Turkish winners include Endless Love (Kara Sevda) in 2017, Family Secrets (Yargi) in 2023, and The Good & The Bad (Deha) in 2025.

About the series

Titled in full Leyla: Life... Love... Justice... (Leyla: Hayat... Ask... Adalet...), the series is an adaptation of the record-breaking Brazilian show Avenida Brasil. It made its broadcast debut on NOW on Sept. 11, 2024.

The story focuses on a young woman whose life is shattered following her father's remarriage. Having lost her mother at an early age, Leyla loses her father due to her cruel stepmother, Nur, who abandons her at a garbage site. After enduring years of difficulty, Leyla makes her return to pursue justice and revenge.

Key cast members include:

  • Cemre Baysel as Leyla
  • Gonca Vuslateri as Nur
  • Alperen Duymaz starring alongside Baysel

What to expect from lead star Cemre Baysel

Born in 1999 in the coastal city of Izmir, Baysel studied art at Ege University before establishing her acting career. She debuted in 2014 with a supporting role in Yesil Deniz (Green Sea) before appearing in Isimsizler in 2017 and portraying Firuze in the historical drama Payitaht Abdulhamid.

Between 2018 and 2019, she gained wider recognition in Elimi Birakma (Don't Let Go of My Hand), followed by roles in the crime drama Ramo and Sol Yanim (My Left Side). Her momentum accelerated in 2021 when she starred opposite Aytac Sasmaz in the romantic comedy Baht Oyunu (Twist of Fate), a performance that earned her the Rising Star Award at the 47th Golden Butterfly Awards.

Cyberpunk 2077 To Receive New Update To Coincide With Release Of Netflix’s Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2

Published on October 21, 2026

Developer CD Projekt RED has announced a brand-new patch for Cyberpunk 2077 to coincide with the release of the upcoming Netflix series Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2.

What's new

The upcoming Cyberpunk 2077 Update 2.5 is scheduled to arrive on October 22. Prior to the release, CD Projekt RED will host a REDstreams broadcast on Wednesday, October 21 at 5 PM CEST to offer a detailed look at the new features arriving in the update.

What to expect

According to the developer's announcement on X, the update introduces fresh elements designed to enhance gameplay. Key features include:

  • Cyberware customization options
  • A brand-new dual-wielding playstyle
  • New chrome additions
  • New Edgerunners content

About the series

Cyberpunk Edgerunners 2 is a standalone sequel series following the original Edgerunners anime, which launched on Netflix in 2022 to critical acclaim. Further details regarding the specific plot line or full voice cast are Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Background and patch history

Cyberpunk 2077 originally launched on December 10, 2020, for PC, PlayStation 4, and Xbox One. The initial release on consoles suffered from severe performance issues and bugs, requiring several months of post-launch updates to stabilize. Technical problems were later resolved with native versions on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S.

CD Projekt RED invested approximately $125 million into repairing the title and producing its Phantom Liberty expansion. These ongoing support efforts proved successful, with global sales for Cyberpunk 2077 reaching 40 million units by July 2026.

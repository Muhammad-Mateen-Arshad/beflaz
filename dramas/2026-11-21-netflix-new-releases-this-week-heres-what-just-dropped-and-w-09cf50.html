Netflix New Releases This Week — Here’s What Just Dropped and What Everyone’s Watching

Published on November 21, 2026

What's new

Netflix has updated its streaming platform with a fresh wave of movies and series to kick off May. The expanded catalog brings together a mix of fast-paced action, offbeat dark comedies, supernatural thrillers, and historical fantasy productions.

About the series

One of the prominent arrivals on the platform is Man on Fire, a gritty modern thriller starring Yahya Abdul-Mateen II. Based on the story by A.J. Quinnell, the show emphasizes rapid action and tense sequences with a darker visual tone. Early critical feedback highlights strong lead performances and sharp direction, though some critics feel the narrative impact differs from the earlier movie version. Specific details regarding release schedules or episode counts are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

For audiences interested in historical conflicts woven into dark fantasy, the 2014 film Dracula Untold presents a tragic origin story centered on Vlad III, the ruler known as Vlad the Impaler. Facing an outmatched army and an impossible war against the expanding threat of the Ottoman Empire, Vlad makes a desperate choice to protect his kingdom and family. He seeks out an ancient power in a cave, gaining superhuman speed, strength, and control over bats and darkness. However, embracing these powers forces him to resist a bloodthirst that could turn him into a vampire permanently.

The platform's latest update also includes a variety of other film and television titles:

  • A sci-fi and dark comedy feature from director Yorgos Lanthimos starring Emma Stone and Jesse Plemons.
  • Fede Álvarez's survival thriller Don't Breathe, following three Detroit thieves trapped by a lethal war veteran.
  • The 2016 prequel Ouija: Origin of Evil, detailing a 1960s family séance business that encounters a dangerous spirit.
  • International releases such as Buen Camino, My Dearest Señorita, and Season 1 of Glory.

Moviefone Counts Down Netflix's Top 10 Movies for the Week of Nov. 2nd.

Published on November 05, 2026

Moviefone has compiled its list of the top ten movies available on Netflix for the week of November 2nd. The lineup highlights a broad mix of dramatic thrillers, animated features, holiday stories, and character-driven comedies.

What's new

The collection highlights several suspenseful narratives and high-stakes thrillers:

  • A political thriller featuring Ben Affleck and Kerry Washington as parents racing against time to gather ransom money after their son is kidnapped, forcing them to expose hidden aspects of their private lives.
  • A Spanish crime drama set in 2013 that follows bank robbers whose getaway crash uncovers deep-seated betrayals and secrets.
  • A survival story starring Stana Katic as Martha Carelli, a woman taken hostage by a runaway prisoner who forces her to drive his escape car.
  • A drama following newlyweds Maria (Alex Mitchell) and Joe (Rob LaColla Jr.), whose lives fall apart after their tenant is murdered, revealing shocking truths about Maria's husband.

About the series

Subscribers can also stream comedies and emotional social dramas featured on the list:

  • A heartwarming basketball story starring Woody Harrelson as a former minor-league coach mandated by a court to manage a team of players with intellectual disabilities. Related coverage features Woody Harrelson and Kaitlin Olson discussing the comedy 'Champions'.
  • A drama about a young Sudanese Civil War refugee who wins a relocation lottery to the United States and forms a friendship with an American advisor played by Reese Witherspoon while adjusting to a new culture.
  • An animated prehistoric comedy featuring the vocal talents of Nicolas Cage, Catherine Keener, Emma Stone, Clark Duke, Cloris Leachman, and Ryan Reynolds.

What to expect

The weekly selection is completed by natural disaster blockbusters and family-centered stories:

  • 'The Day After Tomorrow', featuring Dennis Quaid as a scientist trekking across extreme conditions to rescue his son Sam, played by Jake Gyllenhaal, who is trapped in New York City amid a worldwide superstorm.
  • A holiday comedy following four generations of the Cooper family celebrating Christmas Eve together amidst chaotic events.
  • A summer holiday story starring Antonella Rose as ten-year-old Claire and Danny DeVito as her grandfather Lawrence, who plot a "Christmas in August" in Italy to stop Claire's parents from divorcing.

Further episode lists or exact release timings were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

The 8 Best New Netflix Shows and Movies to Watch in November 2026

Published on November 09, 2026

What's new

As downtime begins before the late-year holidays, Netflix is rolling out a fresh selection of shows and films for November 2026. The incoming slate spans acclaimed international cinema, returning thriller series, true-life crime dramatizations, and dark genre stories.

About the series

Subscribers can look forward to several major releases across the month:

  • North of North (Season 2): Releasing November 5, the continuation of Netflix Canada's first original series follows Siaja (Anna Lambe), a young Inuk mother navigating personal reinvention and community expectations in the Arctic.
  • Black Doves (Season 2): Arriving November 5, Keira Knightley returns as double agent Helen Webb in a London-based spy story, joined by new cast member Ambika Mod.
  • Possible Love: Debuting November 6, South Korean filmmaker Lee Chang-dong directs this Venice Film Festival award-winner starring Cho Yeo-jeong as a documentarian exploring relationships and social gaps between two married couples.
  • The Body: Premiering November 11, an eight-episode blend of supernatural horror and teen drama following a Catholic high school dance troupe affected by mass hysteria after a Halloween party.
  • Heartland: Launching November 13, Jessica Chastain stars as a retired music star returning to Nashville to investigate the disappearance of her niece, portrayed by Carter Faith.
  • The Altruists: Premiering November 19, 2026, an eight-part limited series starring Julia Garner and Anthony Boyle that dramatizes the cryptocurrency scandal surrounding Sam Bankman-Fried and Caroline Ellison.
  • The Mosquito Bowl: Streaming November 25 after an October 30 theatrical release, Peter Berg directs a war drama depicting U.S. Marines playing football during WWII in Guadalcanal.
  • The Hunting Wives (Season 2): Returning November 26, Brittany Snow and Malin Åkerman return for a story centered on a murder investigation in East Texas.

What to expect

Audiences can expect a wide spectrum of storytelling, ranging from humorous and heartwarming moments in tight-knit communities to fast-paced spy thrillers and intense historical events. Further release schedules or episode details for other titles are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Latest OTT releases (November 16-22): 15 new movies and TV shows on Netflix, Prime Video, JioHotstar and more

Published on November 16, 2026

What's new

A fresh selection of television series and feature films is arriving for home viewing, offering options ranging from campus stories and workplace comedies to biographical crime thrillers and epic adaptations. Specific premiere dates for individual titles are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

Subscribers can look forward to several returning favorites and new episodic productions:

  • Campus Diaries returns for a second season as six friends reunite at Excel University to deal with a severe campus scandal. The Hindi series stars Harsh Beniwal, Ritvik Sahore, Abhinav Sharma, Srishti Ganguly Rindani, Saloni Gaur, and Salonie Patel.
  • Dhindora back with its second season, featuring Bhuvan Bam playing ten separate characters in a romantic comedy story arc.
  • Mr & Mrs Office follows newlyweds Arivu and Aishwarya, played by Pradeep Antony and Smeha Manimegalai, who must pretend to be strangers at their shared corporate workplace.
  • An Argentine six-episode biographical crime thriller starring Rodrigo de la Serna as Aníbal Gordon tracks his path through the 1970s. The cast also includes Matías Recalt, Camila Peralta, and Matías Mayer.
  • A four-episode Polish drama helmed by Kasper Bajon spans two timelines as a government official and a psychiatric expert reunite over a past case.
  • The Altruists stars Julia Garner and Anthony Boyle in a biographical limited series by Graham Moore about a major cryptocurrency collapse.
  • An eight-episode Brazilian romantic comedy series starring Giovanna Grigio follows a woman dating across twelve zodiac signs after a bad breakup.
  • A reality competition series hosted by Haley Baylee places ten teams inside a Toronto shopping complex to complete tasks for a one-million-dollar prize.

What to expect

Movie highlights include Christopher Nolan's adaptation of Homer's ancient Greek poem The Odyssey, featuring Matt Damon, Anne Hathaway, Robert Pattinson, Tom Holland, Zendaya, and Lupita Nyong'o. Sports drama fans can catch MADDEN, featuring Nicolas Cage as John Madden alongside Christian Bale, John Mulaney, Kathryn Hahn, and Sienna Miller. Additionally, Leighton Meester and Jared Padalecki star in a holiday romantic comedy adapted from Katherine Center's novel The Bodyguard.

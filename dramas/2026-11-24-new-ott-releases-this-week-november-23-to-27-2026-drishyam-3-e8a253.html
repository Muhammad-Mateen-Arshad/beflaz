New OTT Releases This Week (November 23 To 27, 2026): Drishyam 3, Hanuman Ansh, Spider-Man Brand New Day On Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, JioHotstar And More

Published on November 24, 2026

Streaming platforms are rolling out a diverse lineup of new series and films from November 23 to 27, 2026. Viewers can tune in across services like Netflix, JioHotstar, and Apple TV for fresh comedies, political dramas, sci-fi projects, and major film debuts.

What's new

The week's schedule brings several notable premieres across multiple genres:

  • Dig (November 24): Created by Mike Schur, this JioHotstar series stars Amy Poehler, Hugh Laurie, Antonia Thomas, and Geraldine Vishwanathan at an archaeological site in Greece.
  • Way of the Warrior Kid (November 25): McG directs this Apple TV family movie starring Chris Pratt as an uncle who guides his bullied nephew Marc (Jude Hill) through Navy SEAL-inspired training.
  • The Mosquito Bowl (November 25): Peter Berg's Netflix war film features Nicholas Galitzine, Bill Skarsgård, Ray Nicholson, and Tom Francis as college football stars enlisting in the Marines post-Pearl Harbour.
  • The Hunting Wives & Supergirl (November 26): Netflix delivers the second installment of The Hunting Wives, while JioHotstar debuts Supergirl starring Milly Alcock, Jason Mamoa, and David Corenswet.
  • Dhai Chaal (November 27): A JioHotstar political series focusing on a college campus rivalry between a student leader and his mentor. Exact broadcast hours are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

Dramas taking center stage this week present intense personal and ideological clashes. In The Hunting Wives, Sophie O'Neil (Brittany Snow) moves to Texas and comes under the influence of Margo Banks (Malin Åkerman), joining a high-society circle. Meanwhile, Dhai Chaal depicts student leader Gagan Arora confronting his influential mentor, Aadil Zafar Khan.

Science fiction fans can also look forward to a limited series set in the universe popularized by Ridley Scott. Michelle Yeoh plays Olwen, an aging Replicant Blade Runner working with Cora (Hunter Schafer), a human disguised as a Replicant, on a mission to locate a group of rogue Replicants.

What to expect

In film releases, Tom Holland returns for his fourth solo Spider-Man movie directed by Destin Daniel Cretton, where Peter Parker guards New York alone while missing MJ (Zendaya) and Ned (Jacob Batalon), confronting a telepathic threat played by Sadie Sink. Additionally, the third installment of the Drishyam Hindi franchise, directed by Abhishek Pathak, features the return of Ajay Devgn, Tabu, and Shriya Saran alongside new cast members Jaideep Ahlawat and Prakash Raj. Viewers can also watch Vishal Chaturvedi's devotional Hindi film inspired by Neem Karoli Baba, starring Shobhinaw Satyaa, following its theatrical run that began on August 7, 2026.

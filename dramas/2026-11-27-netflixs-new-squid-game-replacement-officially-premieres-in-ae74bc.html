Netflix’s New ‘Squid Game’ Replacement Officially Premieres in 1 Month

Published on November 27, 2026

Streaming fans looking for a new high-stakes survival drama will soon have a fresh title to add to their watchlists. Netflix is set to premiere its live-action manga adaptation Quiztopia on December 17.

What's new

Following the success of live-action manga adaptations like Alice in Borderland and One Piece, Netflix is bringing another Japanese property to the screen. Quiztopia is based on the manga written by Reiichi Sugimoto and illustrated by Shinkichi Kato. The series centers on an over-the-top premise featuring a bedazzled game show host and deadly quiz competitions.

About the series

The story takes place in an alternate reality where Japan's most famous television game show acts as the supreme law of the country. Underneath the bright studio lights and massive prizes lies an oppressive government regime that punishes failure with an iron fist. Contestants take the quiz for a chance to win virtually anything they desire, but those who answer incorrectly face forced labor or even worse fates.

At the heart of the narrative is the charismatic show presenter KK, played by Takayuki Yamada. His world is turned upside down when he encounters a resistance movement intent on overthrowing the regime. The exact episode count is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What to expect

The upcoming series brings together a notable cast and creative crew:

  • Takayuki Yamada stars as the game show host, KK.
  • Riko Ishida plays Mei, a young girl intent on killing KK, while Kana Kurashina plays her mother.
  • Koji Seto portrays Seiji Nanbara, one of the activists planning the downfall of Quiztopia.
  • Masahiro Takashima plays Commander Murakoshi of the Reward and Punishment Squad, alongside Eiko Koike as producer Shoko Kamijo.
  • Renji Ishibashi features as Japan's Prime Minister Takeshi Gokumazawa.

Teruyuki Yoshida serves as director and co-wrote the script alongside Magy. Chief producer Akira Morii, who previously worked on Alice in Borderland and Yu Yu Hakusho, also heads the production.

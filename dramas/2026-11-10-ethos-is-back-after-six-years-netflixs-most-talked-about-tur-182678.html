Ethos Is Back After Six Years: Netflix’s Most Talked-About Turkish Drama Returns

Published on November 10, 2026

What's new

Six years after its original release on November 12, 2020, the acclaimed Turkish drama Ethos (known in Turkey as Bir Başkadır) is returning to Netflix. Season 2 will premiere exclusively on the streaming service on Thursday, November 12, 2026. The new order features four episodes, cutting the first season's eight-episode length in half. Berkun Oya once again serves as writer and director. Filmed last year, the project was produced by KREK Film along with producers Evrim Zeybek, Ali Farkhonde, and Berkun Oya.

About the series

The first season centered on cross-class interactions in Istanbul, primarily through Meryem, a cleaner from a conservative, low-income household, and Peri, her secular, affluent psychiatrist. Beyond entertainment, the drama generated intense public discussion in Turkey over societal divides and bias, sparking both censorship calls from conservative outlets and strong support across social platforms.

Öykü Karayel returns to lead the cast as Meryem. The season also features:

  • Cihat Süvarioğlu
  • Gökhan Yıkılkan
  • Oğulcan Arman Uslu
  • Eda Akalın
  • İdil Çelik
  • Cemre Zişan Sağbır
  • Göktuğ Yıldırım

What to expect

The upcoming season leaps forward in time while keeping therapy sessions at the heart of its narrative. Netflix frames the new episodes around three key ideas: zeitgeist, unexpected encounters, and new conflicts. Specific narrative developments are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Audiences should anticipate a measured, talk-heavy style driven by sharp observations rather than fast action. Its shorter four-episode format makes it an easy binge for returning fans or newcomers catching up on Season 1 first. Viewers browsing Netflix on November 12 will also find The Empress Season 3 launching on the same day.

New Live-Action GTO: Great Teacher Onizuka Series Streams Trailer

Published on October 26, 2026

What's new

The team behind the new live-action adaptation based on Tohru Fujisawa's GTO: Great Teacher Onizuka manga has released a 90-second trailer alongside a fresh visual on the show's official website. The series is set to premiere on July 20 at 10:00 p.m. (9:00 a.m. EDT) across Kansai TV, Fuji TV, and affiliated networks. International streaming availability is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

Shifting the narrative from the Heisei era into the modern Reiwa era, the project serves as a sequel to earlier live-action adaptations. Actor Takashi Sorimachi reprises his role as protagonist Eikichi Onizuka 28 years after his original performance. Key cast and crew members include:

  • Takashi Sorimachi playing Eikichi Onizuka
  • Meru Nukumi portraying Mio Kashiwabara, Onizuka's assistant homeroom teacher
  • Satoru Nakajima serving as director
  • Kazuhiko Yukawa writing the scripts

What to expect

Now 52 years old, Onizuka's direct, high-stakes teaching methods have become controversial, causing him to lose a series of teaching jobs before arriving at Seishin Academy. In his new position, he must deal with modern challenges including commercialized education, strict rankings, heavy administrative oversight, reliance on social media, and an environment indifferent to student well-being. The total episode count is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Franchise history

The original manga ran in Kodansha's Weekly Shōnen Magazine from 1997 to 2002. Over the years, the property has expanded into numerous screen and print projects:

  • Print works: Includes sequels and spinoffs such as GTO: Shonan 14 Days, GT-R, GTO: The Early Years, and GTO: Paradise Lost Kai, which concluded in October 2024.
  • Live-action history: Features the 1998 original series, a 2000 film, a 2012 remake starring Exile member Akira, a 2020 adaptation of GTO: The Early Years, and the GTO Revival special in April 2024.
  • Anime adaptation: A television anime aired in 1999, subsequently receiving home video releases via Tokyopop and Discotek.

Everything Coming to Netflix This Week, Including a New Season of ‘Pokémon’

Published on October 17, 2026

Netflix is updating its streaming library with a diverse collection of new releases scheduled to arrive between September 14 and September 20. The upcoming lineup features international dramas, true crime programs, reality competitions, and family titles.

What's new

The weekly schedule includes several drama series and historical productions, alongside unscripted shows and documentaries aimed at a wide audience.

About the series

Drama highlights releasing throughout the week include:

  • When Hope Calls (September 14): A period drama spin-off derived from "When Calls the Heart." It follows reunited orphaned sisters Lillian and Grace as they open an orphanage in the early 20th-century town of Brookfield.
  • The Doll (September 16): A historical drama set against 19th-century European high society that focuses on an ambitious merchant who falls in love with an aristocratic woman bound by family obligations.
  • Seni Tanıyorum (September 17): An emotional Turkish drama series centering on hidden identities and past secrets that link a group of individuals dealing with unresolved family conflicts.
  • Plastic Beauty (September 17): A drama set inside an elite aesthetic clinic, where medical professionals navigate career rivalries, client demands, and underlying dark secrets.
  • Monsters (September 17): A biographical true crime drama anthology focusing on the psychological profiles of infamous historical killers, the lives of victims, and institutional failures.
  • The Fixers (September 17): A fast-paced crime thriller following a covert team tasked with managing dangerous scandals for powerful criminal figures.

What to expect

Beyond drama programming, subscribers can watch the hybrid children's series "Gabby's Dollhouse" on September 14, the true crime docuseries "I Survived a Serial Killer" and artificial intelligence documentary "The AI Doc" on September 15, and the fitness competition "Physical 100: Mexico" on September 16.

Specific cast details, episode counts, and daily release times are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

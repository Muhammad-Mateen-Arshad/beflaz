Every New and Returning Face in ‘Another Self’ Season 3 as Netflix’s Turkish Drama Heads Into Its Final Chapter

Published on November 27, 2026

The popular Turkish streaming drama Another Self, also known under its original title Zeytin Ağacı, has returned to Netflix for its third and final season. Premiering on June 24, this farewell chapter brings the ongoing stories of its main trio to an emotional conclusion. Written by Nuran Evren Şit, directed by Erdem Tepegöz, and produced by OGM Pictures, the series established its footprint on the platform by combining themes of deep friendship, personal trauma, and self-awareness.

About the series

Set against the backdrop of Ayvalık with its sun-drenched coastline and olive groves, the show centers on three women discovering their roots. The narrative focuses on Ada, Sevgi, and Leyla as they navigate transformed lives, open past chapters, and handle unresolved emotional paths while attempting to move forward.

What's new

The final chapter brings back the central cast while adding new key performers to expand the show's world:

  • Returning Trio: Tuba Büyüküstün as Ada, Seda Bakan as Leyla, and Boncuk Yılmaz as Sevgi.
  • Returning Ensemble: Rıza Kocaoğlu, Umut Kurt, and Füsun Demirel.
  • New Cast Members: Şükrü Özyıldız, Berk Cankat, İlayda Akdoğan, and Atsız Karaduman.

Several cast members bring notable experience from other productions. Tuba Büyüküstün recently starred in Kızıl Goncalar and Bir Başkadır, Seda Bakan performed in Şahmaran, and returning actor Rıza Kocaoğlu appeared in Çukur and Kuş Uçuşu. Newcomer Şükrü Özyıldız recently acted in Baht Oyunu and Kardeşlerim.

What to expect

In Season 3, Ada heads back to Ayvalık for a fresh start, only for a contact from her past to alter her path. Sevgi begins to question her aspirations regarding having a family, while Leyla directly confronts her relationship. The final season is speculated to consist of eight episodes running nearly an hour each, matching the format of earlier seasons.

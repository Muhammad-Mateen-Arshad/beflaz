‘The One About Matthew Perry’ Documentary Leads 42 New Netflix UK Releases

Published on October 30, 2026

Netflix UK is expanding its catalog at the end of October with 42 new releases, offering plenty of options for streaming fans. The updated lineup features a mix of fresh original titles, licensed movies, and television shows across multiple genres. Viewers can find everything from biographical documentaries to period pieces and horror films. For subscribers looking for updates on Turkish historical dramas, that detail is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What's new

The latest arrivals on Netflix UK bring a wide selection of choices for the week. Highlighting the new additions are several original productions alongside recognized licensed properties:

  • The One About Matthew Perry: A limited documentary series focusing on the actor's life, career, and struggles with addiction, as recounted by those who knew him best.
  • Dead-End Job: A Korean limited drama series following a debt-laden man who takes on a confidential, highly lucrative assignment.
  • The Tudors: Seasons 1 through 4 of the steamy British period drama.
  • Minimum Wage: Season 1 of a new comedy series.
  • Feature Films: Blockbuster film additions include the sci-fi adventure Star Trek Beyond, as well as horror entries like Annabelle Comes Home, Insidious: Chapter 2, and Insidious: Chapter 3.

About the series

A primary narrative highlight among the new releases is the Korean series Dead-End Job. The drama stars Lee Jae Wook as Byeon Hyeok Jun, a man struggling with heavy debt. He agrees to a high-paying agency role that offers 50 times the minimum wage. The condition of the job requires complete secrecy, which quickly plunges him into dangerous circumstances and bizarre supernatural phenomena.

What to expect

Subscribers exploring the platform can expect a varied watching experience across different formats. Full multi-season runs are available for shows like Orphan Black (Seasons 1–5) and Red Dwarf (Seasons 1–8). International television offerings are also heavily featured, including limited series such as the Swedish drama A Couple of Lies, the Marathi title Akka: The Protectors, and the Arabic show Obsess.

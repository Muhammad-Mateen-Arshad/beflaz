Controversial Erotic Thriller Returns With More Netflix Releases This Week

Published on November 22, 2026

Streaming fans looking for their next binge have fresh content arriving on Netflix during the week of November 23–29, 2026. Heading the latest lineup is the return of the dramatic series The Hunting Wives for its second season, alongside a mix of holiday specials, sports events, and new series.

What's new

The platform is adding several titles across various genres throughout the week. The full list of additions includes:

  • The Hunting Wives (Season 2)
  • The Great British Baking Show: Holidays (Season 9)
  • The Trials of Karen Read (Limited Series)
  • NFL Netflix Gameday: Thanksgiving Eve, Packers vs. Rams (2026)
  • Toos: The Van der Valk Kidnapping (Season 1)
  • Salish’s 12 Games of Christmas (Season 1)

Exact daily release dates for individual titles are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

The Hunting Wives chronicles the life of Sophie, which turns upside down after she becomes entangled in an elite social circle known as the "Hunting Wives." The second season brings back the drama with a cast featuring Brittany Snow, Malin Akerman, Dermot Mulroney, Evan Jonigkeit, and Jaime Ray Newman.

What to expect

Season 2 delivers fresh conflict and suspense. Sophie and Margo start out alienated from one another, but long-buried secrets and new enemies quickly push them back together. As they embark on dangerous mind games dressed in designer gowns and boots, the story questions whether they are truly the ones hunting or if they have become the hunted.

"The Heir" soon on TV Vijesti: One man, two women and secrets that change everything

Published on October 31, 2026

Television Vijesti will soon begin broadcasting the Turkish drama series "The Heir." The lavish family saga explores deep-rooted customs, betrayal, and power struggles centered around a high-stakes love triangle and feuding families.

What's new

The show is set to make its television debut on Television Vijesti in the near future. Exact air dates and broadcast schedules are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Furthermore, total episode counts are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

The story focuses on Serhat Jelduran, an accomplished neurosurgeon who left his hometown of Urfa to build a modern life in Istanbul away from strict family rules. After falling in love with Melek, he secretly marries her. However, his life changes completely when he returns home and faces his family legacy alongside an old blood feud between two powerful houses.

To settle the longstanding feud and fulfill a childhood promise, Serhat is expected to marry Yildiz, a woman tied to his destiny since birth. Forced to assume the role of family leader, or aga, Serhat is torn between his love for Melek and his duty to his family. The drama intensifies when Melek moves to Urfa, forcing both women who love the same man to reside under one roof.

What to expect

Viewers can look forward to an emotional story of love, honor, and difficult choices. Key elements of the production include:

  • Setting: Filmed in southeastern Turkey, showcasing the historic landscapes, traditional customs, and luxurious family homes of Şanlıurfa.
  • Cast: Features Ilhan Sen, Aybike Pusat, and Biran Damla Yilmaz in lead roles, alongside Onur Bilge, Sezin Bozacı, Mazlum Cimen, and Mert Dogan.
  • Production crew: Produced by Most Production under the leadership of Gul Oguz, directed by Deniz Çelebi Dikilitaş, and written by main screenwriter Ercan Ugur.

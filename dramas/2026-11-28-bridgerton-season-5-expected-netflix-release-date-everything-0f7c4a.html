Bridgerton Season 5: Expected Netflix Release Date & Everything We Know So Far

Published on November 28, 2026

What's new

The fifth season of Bridgerton has officially wrapped filming and is currently in post-production ahead of its planned 2027 release on Netflix. This upcoming installment marks the franchise's first queer female-led romance, focusing on the relationship between Francesca Bridgerton and Michaela Stirling. Showrunner Jess Brownell has noted that the season will emphasize yearning and queer joy rather than trauma, while keeping key character arcs intact, including Francesca's journey with fertility.

About the series

Based on Julia Quinn's Regency-era novels, the fifth season adapts elements from the book When He Was Wicked. The cast features Hannah Dodd as Francesca Bridgerton and Masali Baduza as Michaela Stirling, a dark-skinned Black woman starring as the romantic lead. The narrative follows a gender-swap of the original book character Michael to Michaela, a change supported by author Julia Quinn. The season follows the events of Season 4—which centered on Benedict Bridgerton, played by Luke Thompson, and Sophie, played by Yerin Ha—and sets up a new chapter for Francesca following the death of her husband, John Stirling.

What to expect

Audiences can look forward to several major developments throughout the upcoming season:

  • A two-year time jump takes place following the death of John Stirling.
  • Francesca considers re-entering the marriage market for practical reasons, but her plans are disrupted when Michaela returns to London to care for the Kilmartin estate.
  • The story will reveal the reasons behind Michaela's departure from Mayfair at the end of Season 4.
  • The series will address Francesca's fertility story within the context of her sapphic relationship with Michaela.
  • Benedict Bridgerton will continue to feature in the story.

For details regarding exact release dates or full cast additions: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Netflix Is Reopening the Complicated History of ‘Jon & Kate Plus 8’

Published on October 22, 2026

What's new

Netflix has confirmed that production is underway on a new documentary film focused on the Gosselin family. The upcoming project features Jon Gosselin alongside his children, Collin Gosselin and Hannah Gosselin, who are currently filming for the feature. The documentary is being produced by Mike Gasparro and Julia Willoughby Nason.

Information regarding the exact title, premiere date, and episode count is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

The original reality show, Jon & Kate Plus 8, aired from 2007 to 2017, spanning 11 seasons. It initially broadcasted on Discovery Health before shifting to TLC, where it became one of the platform's highest-rated programs. The series documented Jon and his then-wife, Kate Gosselin, as they raised their eight children. Following the couple's divorce in 2009, the project continued under the title Kate Plus 8.

What to expect

The new documentary will explore what happened to the family after their time on television. It combines perspectives from the children raised on camera, the adults who turned their lives into a brand, and the public audience that followed them. The film will include sit-down interviews with Jon, Collin, and Hannah.

This announcement comes shortly after 22-year-old Collin released his memoir, In the Shadow of Eight, on October 13. During a recent promotional appearance on ABC News, Collin alleged that his mother became abusive after the 2009 divorce and stated that their televised life was not real. Back in a 2016 interview with ABC News, Kate stated that Collin required assistance with social and educational struggles, calling it the best option for him at the time. Whether other family members will participate in the documentary is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

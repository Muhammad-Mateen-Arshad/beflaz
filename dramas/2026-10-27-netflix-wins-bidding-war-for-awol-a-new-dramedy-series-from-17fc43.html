Netflix Wins Bidding War for ‘AWOL’, a New Dramedy Series From the ‘Dungeons & Dragons: Honor Among Thieves’ Duo

Published on October 27, 2026

What's new

Streaming platform Netflix has secured the rights to AWOL, an upcoming dramedy series, following a highly competitive bidding war. The acquisition adds another notable title to the platform's growing lineup of television comedy projects.

About the series

The show is written by Craig Rowin, whose writing credits include The Off Weeks as well as the Apple TV+ dramedy The Big Door Prize. Serving as executive producers on the project are John Francis Daley and Jonathan Goldstein through their GoldDay Productions banner.

Daley and Goldstein bring a long history of successful studio projects to the series. Their previous screenwriting and directing credits include writing Horrible Bosses, co-writing Spider-Man: Homecoming, and directing films like Game Night and Dungeons & Dragons: Honor Among Thieves. Daley is also an accomplished actor, having played Sam Weir in Freaks and Geeks and Dr. Lance Sweets in Bones. The pair's Cold War action-comedy feature Mayday, which stars Ryan Reynolds and Kenneth Branagh, premiered globally on Apple TV on September 4, 2026.

Additionally, Netflix is developing The Forgotten Realms, a live-action Dungeons & Dragons series helmed by Shawn Levy, though it shares no narrative ties to Daley and Goldstein's feature film.

Dhindora Season 2 Release Date: Bhuvan Bam’s Titu Mama Returns With More Trouble On Netlfix

Published on November 05, 2026

What's new

YouTube creator and actor Bhuvan Bam is bringing his popular web series back as Dhindora Season 2 arrives on Netflix. Scheduled to premiere on November 20, the comedy-drama brings the familiar world of BB Ki Vines to a streaming audience. The new season is produced by Bhuvan Bam alongside Rohit Raj, delivering fresh, laugh-out-loud moments and outrageous situations.

What to expect

The second season puts the fan-favorite character Titu Mama at the center of another chaotic family storyline. Key details of his upcoming storyline include:

  • Titu Mama dealing with a massive loss of ₹11 crore.
  • Efforts to reconcile and make peace with his in-laws.
  • A basic family situation rapidly escalating into a full-scale drama.

Further specifics regarding episode counts, additional cast members, or runtime are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

The first season of Dhindora originally debuted on Bhuvan Bam's YouTube channel and quickly gained a strong following. Built around small-town ambitions, flawed choices, and relatable family chaos, the initial run marked a significant transition for Bam from short internet characters to long-form storytelling.

Creator Bhuvan Bam stated that taking the show to Netflix expands its heart and humor onto a much larger canvas. He emphasized that the new season allows the team to share these characters with a wider audience while maintaining the cultural specificity that originally resonated with viewers.

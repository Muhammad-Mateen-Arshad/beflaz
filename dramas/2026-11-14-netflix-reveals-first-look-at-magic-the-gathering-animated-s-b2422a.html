Netflix Reveals First Look at Magic: The Gathering Animated Series With Title and Release Window

Published on November 14, 2026

Netflix has officially announced the title and narrative details for its upcoming animated adaptation of the long-running fantasy card game Magic: The Gathering at MagicCon in Atlanta. Titled Planeswalker, the show represents the franchise's first television adaptation in its 33-year history.

What's new

The streaming platform shared a first look preview featuring artwork and video footage of the main character, the pyromancer Chandra Nalaar, alongside card game figure Ajani Goldmane. While previously announced character Jace Beleren was not visible in this initial look, the series is progressing toward its release. An exact premiere date is Not specified in the source; please check the official notice, but the series is expected to launch in 2027.

About the series

The animated project is a joint production between Hasbro Entertainment and Wizards of the Coast. Terry Matalas and Cindy Appel serve as co-showrunners and executive producers. Academy Award-winning animator Patrick Osborne acts as supervising director and executive producer alongside Hasbro's Gabriel Marano and Lauren Chavez-Myers. Daisy May West is producing the show, while animation studio Digic is creating the visual aesthetic.

What to expect

The narrative revolves around powerful magic users called Planeswalkers, who possess a special trait known as the spark. The plot centers on Chandra, a volatile pyromancer hunted for her powers and haunted by her past. She must unite with other legendary Planeswalkers to stand against an insidious force, manage dangerous alliances, and overcome inner demons to prevent a devastating war across thousands of worlds.

  • Visual styles designed to give every location a distinct appearance.
  • A story focused on the clash and survival of formidable mages.
  • Character appearances drawing directly from the card game's extensive lore.

Netflix’s ‘Miracles of the ER’ Release Date, Trailer, Cast and Plot

Published on November 10, 2026

Netflix is set to release Miracles of the ER, a Mandarin workplace drama billed as Taiwan’s first medical series focused entirely on an emergency department. The project premieres globally on November 12 and consists of eight episodes. Netflix has released the official trailer, key art, and full synopsis ahead of launch.

What's new

The series reunites director Isaac Li, writer-director Huang Ching-tsu, and producer Jade Lee, the creative trio behind Tears on Fire. Listed under medical TV shows, social issue dramas, and Taiwanese titles, the show leans into workplace pressures alongside medical procedures. The exact streaming time for the release is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

About the series

Set at Yang-Da University Hospital, the story follows three first-year residents operating under the supervision of two experienced physicians. The main cast includes:

  • Wallace Huo as Chou Cheng-chun, a decisive, skilled emergency doctor who mentors younger physicians and takes a chance on an underdog candidate.
  • Cheryl Yang as Dr. He Yu-hua, an accomplished attending physician whose commitment is tested by institutional failures and workplace disputes.
  • Kent Tsai as Lin Geng-ming, an eager first-year resident accepted despite having average grades.
  • Chan Tzu-hsuan as Tang Hsi, a standout graduate motivated by family-related reasons.
  • Payne Peng as Chiang Tung-yen, a brilliant, top-of-his-class resident who is emotionally guarded.

What to expect

The medical team faces relentless shifts filled with medical emergencies, ethical choices, and internal hospital politics. The narrative also examines the relationships between doctors, patients, and families. To capture authentic ER environments, the creators conducted field research and consulted frontline medical professionals, highlighting split-second decision-making, unspoken teamwork, and the daily pressures faced by healthcare workers.

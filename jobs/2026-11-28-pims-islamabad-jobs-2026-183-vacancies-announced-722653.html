PIMS Islamabad Jobs 2026: 183 Vacancies Announced

Published on November 28, 2026

The Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences has announced PIMS Islamabad Jobs 2026 to fill 183 regular positions across 45 distinct categories. This recruitment drive is open to eligible male and female candidates across Pakistan with qualifications ranging from primary education to graduation. Interested applicants must submit their online applications within 15 days of the advertisement date of November 28, 2026.

Role Overview

The recruitment covers positions ranging from BPS-02 to BPS-15 across administrative, medical, technical, and general support departments. Advertised positions include Assistants, Statistical Assistants, Assistant Librarians, Stenotypists, Upper Division Clerks, Lower Division Clerks, Junior Technicians, Midwives, Telephone Operators, Attendants, Lab Attendants, and Ward Boys.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Assistant: Requires a graduation degree. The prescribed age limit is 18 to 28 years. Selected candidates must complete a 6-week basic IT training course from NITB before completing probation.
  • Statistical Assistant: Requires a graduation degree with Mathematics, Economics, or Statistics as one of the subjects.
  • Assistant Librarian: Applicants must hold a graduation degree along with a Diploma in Library Science and relevant medical library experience and training.
  • Stenotypist & UDC: Intermediate qualification is required. Stenotypists must meet prescribed shorthand/typing speeds and computer literacy requirements, while UDCs must finish post-selection IT training.
  • Lower Division Clerk: Requires Matriculation with a minimum typing speed of 30 words per minute and completion of a 3-week NITB IT training course after selection.
  • Junior Technician: Candidates must have Matric with Science and a 1-year diploma or certificate in a relevant discipline (such as Radiology, Anaesthesia, Pharmacy, OT, or CSSD).
  • Midwife: 31 positions are available in BPS-09. Applicants need Matric with Science and a 1-year certificate or diploma in the relevant field. The age limit is 18 to 25 years.

Key Details

  • Total Vacancies: 183 positions across 45 categories.
  • Pay Scale: Positions range from BPS-02 to BPS-15. Specific salary amounts are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Age Relaxation: Standard federal government age relaxation rules apply beyond the basic age limits.
  • Quotas: Seats are distributed under open merit as well as designated provincial, regional, and reserved quotas.

How to Apply

Candidates must submit their applications online through the official National Job Portal. Applicants should log in or register for an account, search for vacancies listed by the Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences, review the required criteria for their chosen position, and complete the application form accurately.

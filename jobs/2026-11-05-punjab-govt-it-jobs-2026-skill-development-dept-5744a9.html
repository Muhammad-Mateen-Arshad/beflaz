Punjab Govt IT Jobs 2026: Skill Development Dept

Published on November 05, 2026

The Punjab government has announced Punjab Govt IT Jobs 2026 within the Skill Development and Entrepreneurship Department. These contract-based positions in Lahore are designed for experienced technology professionals seeking monthly salaries of up to Rs. 726,290. Qualified candidates must submit their online applications before the closing date of November 19, 2026.

Role Overview

The department is seeking qualified candidates for managerial and software development positions in Lahore. The contract roles come with monthly salary packages and annual increments.

  • General Manager Analytics
  • General Manager Digitalization
  • Assistant Manager Full Stack Developer
  • Assistant Manager Backend Developer

Eligibility Criteria

All degrees must be issued by universities recognized by the Higher Education Commission (HEC). Requirements vary by position:

  • General Manager Positions: Applicants need a PhD in IT, Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, ICT, or a related field alongside at least 8 years of professional experience. Alternatively, individuals holding 16 years of education in a relevant field with 10 years of experience may apply.
  • Assistant Manager Full Stack Developer: Requires 16 years of education in IT, Computer Science, AI, ICT, Software Engineering, or equivalent. Candidates must demonstrate practical skills in full-stack development, system integration, SQL databases, and NoSQL technologies.
  • Assistant Manager Backend Developer: Requires 16 years of education in Computer Science, IT, AI, ICT, Software Engineering, Economics, or Engineering. Applicants must have expertise in backend architecture, database optimization, security, and scalable systems.

Key Details

  • Department: Skill Development and Entrepreneurship Department
  • Job Location: Lahore
  • Employment Type: Contract basis
  • Maximum Salary: Up to Rs. 726,290 per month
  • Total Number of Vacancies: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice
  • Application Deadline: November 19, 2026

How to Apply

Interested individuals must register and apply exclusively online via the official Punjab Jobs Portal. Submissions sent through post, email, courier, fax, or hand delivery will not be accepted.

  • Access the official portal and log into your account or register a new user profile.
  • Fill out your profile completely with personal, academic, and career details.
  • Locate the vacancies listed under the Skill Development and Entrepreneurship Department.
  • Choose your desired role and complete the submission prior to the deadline.
  • Applicants currently serving in government or semi-government organizations must apply through the proper channel.

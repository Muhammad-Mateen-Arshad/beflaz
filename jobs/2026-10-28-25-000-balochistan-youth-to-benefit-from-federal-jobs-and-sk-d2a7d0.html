25,000 Balochistan Youth to Benefit from Federal Jobs and Skills Initiative

Published on October 28, 2026

The federal government has announced a major employment and vocational skills initiative aimed at empowering 25,000 young people across 12 districts of southern Balochistan. Introduced under the Prime Minister’s Youth Programme, this initiative focuses on boosting career opportunities, technical training, and educational access through public-private sector cooperation.

Role Overview

This initiative is designed to connect youth with job placements and practical training. In the initial phase, authorities intend to secure employment for roughly 6,000 to 7,000 candidates. Remaining participants will undergo specialized vocational courses to help them enter the workforce. Additionally, the broader government package includes the establishment of Danish Schools providing free education and residential support, alongside 5,000 higher education scholarships for students from Balochistan and the former FATA regions.

Key Details

  • Target Beneficiaries: 25,000 young individuals in 12 southern Balochistan districts.
  • Initial Job Placements: Approximately 6,000 to 7,000 candidates in the first phase.
  • Training Duration: 3 to 6 months, covering technical skills, professional certifications, workplace ethics, soft skills, and civic responsibility.
  • Training Venues: Local facilities within Balochistan, supported by selected institutions in Sindh for additional capacity and residential boarding.
  • Coordinating Bodies: NAVTTC, provincial TEVTAs, SMEDA, Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal, TDAP, and PPAF.
  • Number of Posts: 25,000 training and employment spots overall; specific post designations are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Salary / Stpend: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Application Deadline: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

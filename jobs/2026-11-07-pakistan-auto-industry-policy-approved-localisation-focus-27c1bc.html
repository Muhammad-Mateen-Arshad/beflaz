Pakistan Auto Industry Policy Approved: Localisation Focus

Published on November 07, 2026

The Pakistan Auto Industry Policy Approved: Localisation Focus initiative aims to strengthen domestic manufacturing, create employment opportunities, and modernize the country's automotive sector. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has approved the final draft of this policy, which will now be presented to the Federal Cabinet for formal approval. This update is vital for professionals, job seekers, and industry vendors looking to benefit from expanding industrial growth.

Role Overview

The government's new policy framework aims to transition Pakistan's automotive market from an import-reliant assembly system into an export-ready, locally integrated manufacturing industry. By encouraging domestic vendor development and expanding local content production, the government intends to generate employment and retain greater economic value within the country.

Key Details

  • Main Focus Areas: Local manufacturing, electric vehicle adoption, stricter safety enforcement, and enhanced consumer protection.
  • Consumer Protection Measure: Vehicle booking advance payments are restricted to a maximum of 20% to prevent excessive on-money charges.
  • Safety and Governance: Stronger safety standards for vehicles and the introduction of third-party audits to ensure transparency.
  • Production Cost Support: Implementation of the National Tariff Policy to make imported raw materials and intermediate goods more affordable.

Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum 2026 Local Jobs Push

Published on November 14, 2026

The Government of Pakistan highlighted major industrial expansion plans during the Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum 2026 held in Islamabad. This initiative focuses on expanding domestic mineral processing and manufacturing to create job opportunities across the country. Specific application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The government vision encourages foreign mining firms to move beyond raw resource extraction by building local refineries, smelters, and factories for items such as cables and conductors. This strategic focus aims to broaden Pakistan's industrial base, stimulate economic growth, and generate local employment. Furthermore, investments are expected to support nearby communities through infrastructure enhancements, including schools, health clinics, and clean drinking water facilities.

Key Details

  • Industry Focus: Mining extraction, refining, processing, and domestic manufacturing.
  • Security Assurances: Full security measures are pledged for investors, personnel, and mining sites.
  • Project Timelines: Operations at the Reko Diq venture are expected to start production in 2028.
  • Corporate Partnerships: Ivanhoe Atlantic has signed a Memorandum of Understanding with Mari Energies.
  • Government Commitment: Administrative obstacles will be cleared to fast-track investment approvals with full transparency.

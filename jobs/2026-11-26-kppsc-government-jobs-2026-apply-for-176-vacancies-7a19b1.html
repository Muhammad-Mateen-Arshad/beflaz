KPPSC Government Jobs 2026: Apply for 176 Vacancies

Published on November 26, 2026

The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Public Service Commission has announced the KPPSC Government Jobs 2026 recruitment drive, featuring 176 openings across 25 categories in various provincial departments. This hiring opportunity is designed for qualified male and female applicants who hold a Khyber Pakhtunkhwa domicile. Eligible candidates must complete their online submissions before the closing deadline of December 17, 2026, at 5:00 PM.

Role Overview

Under KPPSC Advertisement No. 10/2026, published on November 25, positions spanning pay scales from BPS-15 to BPS-18 are available across departments including Finance, Forestry, Livestock, Law, Population Welfare, and Public Health Engineering. Available job titles include Sub Accountant, Sub Divisional Forest Officer, Sub Divisional Wildlife Officer, Veterinary Officer, Research Officer, Assistant District Attorney, Civil Engineer, and Women Medical Officer.

Key Details

  • Total Vacancies: 176 posts across 25 categories
  • Pay Scale Range: BPS-15 to BPS-18
  • Application Fee: Rs. 500 (plus applicable service charges)
  • Application Deadline: December 17, 2026, at 5:00 PM
  • Salary: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice
  • Job Location: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice

Eligibility Criteria

  • Sub Accountant (Age 21–35): Requires a second-class bachelor's degree in Commerce or Business Administration, or an ACMA/MBA degree.
  • Sub Divisional Forest Officer (Age 21–32): Requires a second-class MSc or a 4-year BS in Forestry.
  • Sub Divisional Wildlife Officer (Age 21–32): Master's degree in Wildlife, Forestry, or National Park Management is preferred.
  • Assistant District Attorney (Age 25–35): Second-class LLB or equivalent qualification with at least 3 years of standing at the Bar.
  • Assistant Engineer / SDO Civil (Age 21–32): Civil Engineering degree with registration from the Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC).
  • Veterinary & Research Officers: Doctor of Veterinary Medicine (DVM) degree or equivalent, along with Pakistan Veterinary Medical Council registration.
  • Women Medical Officer: Second-class MBBS or equivalent qualification with completed house job training.
  • Quotas & Relaxation: Dedicated quotas exist for women, minorities, and candidates with disabilities, with official government age relaxation rules applicable.

How to Apply

Interested candidates should submit their applications online through the official KPPSC portal under Advertisement No. 10/2026. Applicants must fill out the required personal and academic information and pay the Rs. 500 fee using 1Bill options (NBP, HBL, ABL, Bank of Khyber, Bank of Punjab) or supported mobile wallets (Easypaisa, JazzCash, U Paisa). No physical documents need to be submitted during the initial online application. Government employees are required to submit departmental permission certificates when requested.

Bangladesh Garment Workers Rice Ration Program 2026

Published on November 23, 2026

The Bangladesh Garment Workers Rice Ration Program 2026 is a specialized government assistance initiative designed for ready-made garment factory staff. This support scheme provides affordable food rations to help low-wage textile employees handle essential living expenses across key industrial hubs. Deadlines for participation are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

Garment workers in Bangladesh represent the foundation of the country's major export sector. Despite high production output, compensation remains minimal, with the government-mandated minimum wage established at Tk12,500 per month (approximately $102). To help workers cover basic nutrition, the government introduced a targeted rice distribution program operating via mobile trucks in primary industrial regions.

Key Details

  • Target Audience: Ready-Made Garment Factory Workers
  • Program Benefit: Purchase up to 10 kilograms of rice per month at Tk30 per kilogram
  • Program Start Date: November 2026
  • Service Locations: Designated truck distribution points in Dhaka, Gazipur, Narayanganj, and Chattogram
  • Industry Minimum Wage: Tk12,500 per month (effective January 2024)
  • Application Deadline: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice

Eligibility Criteria

  • Must be an active employee in a garment manufacturing factory within the covered cities.
  • Must present a valid factory identity card during collection.
  • Must sign the official factory master roll at the distribution location.

How to Apply

Eligible factory staff can access subsidized rice directly from state trucks operating in designated areas of Dhaka, Gazipur, Narayanganj, and Chattogram by showing their factory identity card and signing the factory master roll. Further application procedures are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

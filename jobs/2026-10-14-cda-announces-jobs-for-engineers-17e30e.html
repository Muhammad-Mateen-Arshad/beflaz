CDA Announces Jobs for Engineers

Published on October 14, 2026

The Capital Development Authority (CDA) has announced job opportunities for qualified engineers to fill senior positions in Islamabad. The recruitment drive is aimed at selecting experienced professionals for eight Project Director posts on a contract basis under Project Pay Scale PPS-10. Initial appointments will be for two years, with possibilities of extension depending on departmental needs.

Role Overview

The advertised vacancies are divided into two main categories:

  • Project Director (Civil): 6 positions available for individuals specializing in large-scale infrastructure and construction projects.
  • Project Director (Electrical & Mechanical): 2 positions available for engineers with expertise in major building and infrastructure projects.

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must meet the following qualification requirements:

  • Education: A bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering is required for Civil roles, while a bachelor's degree in Electrical or Mechanical Engineering is required for E&M roles. A master's degree in Construction Management or Project Management is an added advantage or preferred qualification.
  • Professional Registration: Registration as a Professional Engineer with the Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC) is mandatory.
  • Experience: A minimum of 15 years of relevant, senior-level experience handling major construction or multidisciplinary infrastructure projects. Candidates must possess practical management background along with strong leadership and communication skills.
  • Age Limit: 40 to 50 years (age relaxation is applicable as per government regulations).

Key Details

  • Hiring Organization: Capital Development Authority (CDA)
  • Job Location: Islamabad
  • Total Number of Posts: 8
  • Pay Scale: PPS-10
  • Salary Amount: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice
  • Application Deadline: Within 15 days of advertisement publication (exact date not specified in the source; please check the official notice)

How to Apply

Interested candidates must apply exclusively through the official CDA job portal. Applications submitted via post, email, or courier will not be accepted. Applicants should complete the online form for their chosen position and upload scanned, attested copies of their required documents:

  • Educational certificates and professional experience certificates
  • Recent photograph, CNIC, and domicile certificate
  • Relevant pay order (fee details are not specified in the source; please check the official notice)
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC) for current government employees

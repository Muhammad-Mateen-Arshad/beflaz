Australian Media Launch Campaign Against AI Copyright Changes

Published on November 18, 2026

Australian media launch campaign against AI copyright changes to protect creative works from unauthorized data mining. Thirty-six organizations across the screen, publishing, music, and news sectors have united under the 'Not AI’s to Take' movement to urge the Federal Government to maintain existing copyright protections.

Role Overview

This national movement represents a unified stance across Australia's creative industries against proposed changes to copyright legislation. Industry groups aim to prevent artificial intelligence entities from using copyrighted work without prior permission or negotiated licensing fees. The affected sectors contribute $70.3 billion to the national economy annually and provide employment for nearly 600,000 individuals across various roles, including artists, authors, journalists, production workers, and technical support teams.

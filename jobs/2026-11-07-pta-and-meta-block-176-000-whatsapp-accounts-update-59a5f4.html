PTA and Meta Block 176,000 WhatsApp Accounts Update

Published on November 07, 2026

The security measure where PTA and Meta block 176,000 WhatsApp accounts aims to protect citizens from cybercrime and financial fraud. This joint initiative targets suspicious profiles associated with recycled mobile numbers across Pakistan. Further details regarding application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) partnered with Meta to address digital security risks tied to inactive mobile numbers. Investigations revealed that fraudsters were exploiting data linked to cancelled SIM cards to execute scams on WhatsApp. PTA warned that leaving WhatsApp accounts active after SIM cancellation allows cybercriminals or new SIM owners to misuse the platform.

Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Key Details

  • Blocked Profiles: Meta deactivated 176,000 WhatsApp accounts associated with recycled mobile numbers.
  • Number Identification: PTA shared a list of approximately 10 million recycled mobile numbers with Meta for verification and blocking.
  • Monthly Collaboration: Both entities agreed to exchange data on 5,000 recycled numbers each month to prevent fraud.
  • Recycled SIM Inventory: Telecom providers in Pakistan currently manage about 16 million recycled mobile numbers.
  • Response Rate: Meta addressed 82 per cent of complaints submitted by the regulator over the past year.

UK Strategic Reservists Readiness and Training Notice

Published on November 27, 2026

The UK Ministry of Defence has issued a UK Strategic Reservists Readiness and Training Notice to approximately 100,000 strategic reservists. This initiative invites individuals to prepare for potential call-ups while offering voluntary training and exercise opportunities. A formal closing date is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

In response to growing national security needs, the leadership of the Army, Navy, and Royal Air Force has sent communications to strategic reservists across Britain. Selected individuals are being offered the chance to participate in voluntary military exercises and training. Recipients are also requested to submit updated contact information, current employment details, and existing skill sets.

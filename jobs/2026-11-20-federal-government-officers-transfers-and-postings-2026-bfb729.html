Federal Government Officers Transfers and Postings 2026

Published on November 20, 2026

The Federal Government Officers Transfers and Postings 2026 update highlights key administrative realignments and tenure extensions announced by the Establishment Division. This announcement affects serving officers in various grades across federal departments, law enforcement, and secretariats. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The Establishment Division has issued formal orders regarding the reassignment, direct reporting, and service placement of several civil service officers ranging from Grade-17 to Grade-21 levels. These notifications outline operational shifts across major government agencies and ministries.

Key Details

  • Extension of Additional Charge: Grade-21 officer Aamir Ali Ahmed, currently serving as Secretary for BISP, has received a three-month extension for his additional role as Secretary of the National Food Security Division, effective November 19, 2026.
  • Establishment Division Reporting: Aamir Aqiq Khan, Joint Secretary of the Industries and Production Division, has been transferred and ordered to report immediately to the Establishment Division until further notice.
  • Cyber Crime Agency Placement: Grade-18 Police Service officer Hassan Jahangir has been transferred from the Punjab government to serve under the National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA).
  • FIA Reassignment: Grade-18 officer Rana Hussain Tahir has been relocated from the Islamabad Police to the Federal Investigation Agency (FIA).
  • Presidency Assignment: Grade-17 Office Management Group officer Muhammad Usman Khalid, who was previously awaiting job placement, has been posted to the Presidency (Aiwan-e-Sadr).

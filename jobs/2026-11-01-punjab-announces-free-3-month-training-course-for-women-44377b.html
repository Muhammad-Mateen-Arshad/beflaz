Punjab Announces Free 3-Month Training Course for Women

Published on November 01, 2026

The Punjab government has introduced a free three-month training initiative for women who want to pursue careers in guest relations and front desk management. Organized under the SEHR Program by the Women Development Department in collaboration with the Tourism Development Corporation of Punjab’s Institute of Tourism and Hospitality Management (TDCP-ITHM), this program offers practical training to prepare participants for customer-facing roles in the hospitality industry.

Role Overview

This initiative focuses on equipping female candidates with essential skills for front desk operations and guest relations. Participants receive hands-on training designed to improve their professional employment prospects within the hospitality sector.

Key Details

  • Training Provider: Women Development Department (SEHR Program) in partnership with TDCP-ITHM
  • Location: TDCP-ITHM campus in Johar Town, Lahore
  • Course Duration: 3 months
  • Course Fee: 100% free (no tuition charges)
  • Program Features: Professional instruction, practical learning, and job placement assistance (completing the course does not guarantee employment)
  • Application Deadline: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice

Eligibility Criteria

  • Gender: Exclusively for women
  • Age Limit: 18 to 45 years
  • Educational Requirement: Minimum of Matriculation passed
  • Domicile: Must hold a valid Punjab domicile
  • Seat Limit: Available seats are limited, and entry depends on meeting selection standards

How to Apply

Interested applicants must register online by visiting the official hospitality training portal of the Punjab Women Development Department (wdd.punjab.gov.pk/HospitalityTraining). Candidates need to fill out the registration form with their personal and educational details, select their preferred training session and location, and choose the Guest Relations Officer / Front Desk Officer course option. Applicants are also required to upload their identity documents to complete the process. Please note that submitting an application does not guarantee admission due to limited seat availability. Candidates seeking guidance regarding registration or course availability can contact TDCP-ITHM Lahore directly.

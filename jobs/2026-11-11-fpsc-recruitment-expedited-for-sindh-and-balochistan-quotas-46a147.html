FPSC Recruitment Expedited for Sindh and Balochistan Quotas

Published on November 11, 2026

The FPSC recruitment expedited for Sindh and Balochistan quotas initiative aims to fill thousands of vacant positions across various federal government bodies. This drive is intended for job seekers holding domiciles in Sindh and Balochistan to ensure fair representation in federal departments. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

Federal hiring authorities are working to clear backlogs and fill provincial quota shortfalls as required under Article 38(g) of the Constitution. The Establishment Division has advertised 5,000 reserved posts for Balochistan, while total identified shortfalls stand at over 30,000 jobs for Balochistan and over 80,000 jobs for Sindh. Recruitment is divided across government pay scales as follows:

  • Grades 1 to 15: Managed directly through respective federal ministries.
  • Grades 16 and 17: Handled through the Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC).
  • Grades 18 to 22: Executive and senior appointments handled through the federal government and the Prime Minister.
  • A special Central Superior Services (CSS) examination has also been conducted to boost representation from these two provinces.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Domicile: Applicants must belong to Sindh or Balochistan under the designated provincial employment quotas.
  • Educational & Technical Qualifications: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Key Details

  • Number of Posts: 5,000 posts advertised for Balochistan, with overall quota gaps exceeding 30,000 for Balochistan and 80,000 for Sindh.
  • Salary: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Location: Federal ministries, departments, and corporations.
  • Deadline: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Interested candidates must apply through the appropriate channel based on the position grade. FPSC oversees testing and recruitment for Grades 16 and 17, while lower grades are processed directly by federal ministries.

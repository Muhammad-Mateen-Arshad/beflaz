Pakistan Railways approves 829 jobs under Balochistan quota

Published on October 27, 2026

Pakistan Railways has announced a recruitment drive offering 829 job opportunities from Grade 1 to Grade 16 under the Balochistan quota. Aligned with federal government policy, this initiative aims to provide employment opportunities to eligible candidates from the province while maintaining transparency and merit throughout the process.

Role Overview

The approved positions are distributed across two main areas. A total of 805 posts are designated for the Quetta Division of Pakistan Railways, while the remaining 24 vacancies are allocated to the Railways Headquarters. To ensure fair representation, the hiring process incorporates a 10 percent quota for women and a 5 percent quota for minorities. Additionally, candidates applying under this special initiative are exempted from taking written or screening tests.

Key Details

  • Organization: Pakistan Railways
  • Total Positions: 829 vacancies (Grade 1 to 16)
  • Distribution: 805 posts in Quetta Division, 24 posts in Railways Headquarters
  • Reserved Quotas: 10% for women, 5% for minorities
  • Selection Method: Exempted from screening and written tests
  • Application Deadline: November 11, 2026
  • Target Completion Date: Before November 30, 2026
  • Salary and Compensation: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Eligibility Criteria

Specific post-by-post educational qualifications, age limits, and experience requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Applicants must belong to the Balochistan quota and submit the required documentation to confirm their eligibility.

How to Apply

Eligible individuals must submit their applications online through the National Job Portal by November 11, 2026. Applicants are instructed to provide essential supporting documents to demonstrate their eligibility, including:

  • Educational certificates
  • National identity cards
  • Domicile certificates
  • Character certificates
  • Passport-size photographs

Federal Minister for Railways Muhammad Hanif Abbasi has directed authorities to complete the entire recruitment procedure prior to November 30, 2026.

Kenyan Medical Fellows Specialist Training in Pakistan

Published on November 25, 2026

The Ministry of Health has announced plans for the Kenyan Medical Fellows Specialist Training in Pakistan initiative to address critical healthcare workforce gaps. This partnership is designed for Kenyan medical fellows seeking advanced education in specialized fields, with training expected to begin in January 2027. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

Kenya's Health Cabinet Secretary Aden Duale proposed the placement initiative during bilateral discussions with Pakistani Federal Minister Syed Mustafa Kamal. The effort focuses on building a formal government-to-government framework to place medical fellows in Pakistan's specialised training institutions. This agreement aims to expand clinical capacity in critical areas, particularly oncology and nephrology, while supporting broader bilateral health cooperation.

The initiative forms part of a three-pillar framework that includes specialist training, regulatory cooperation between Kenya's Pharmacy and Poisons Board and the Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan, and pharmaceutical manufacturing investments. It aligns with other Ministry efforts to advance healthcare delivery, professional exchanges, and workforce development through international partnerships.

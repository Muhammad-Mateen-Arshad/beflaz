IRSA Announces Jobs With Salaries Up to Rs. 157,500 Per Month

Published on October 13, 2026

The Indus River System Authority (IRSA) has announced job opportunities for its Telemetry Project, which monitors water discharge in real time across 27 key locations in the Indus Basin Irrigation System. Implemented with the Water and Power Development Authority (WAPDA) as the executing agency, this recruitment offers 23 open-merit positions across Project Pay Scales PPS-05 to PPS-07 through the National Testing Service (NTS).

Role Overview

Selected candidates will receive an initial one-year contract, which may be extended based on satisfactory performance and project requirements. Recruited staff will be attached to WAPDA during the project execution phase, followed by a one-year defect liability period and a two-year maintenance period for training purposes.

Key Details

  • Total Vacancies: 23 positions
  • Available Posts: GIS/Web GIS Analyst, Senior Auditor, Sub-Engineer (Electronics/Electrical - 17 vacancies), and Supervisor (Electronics/Electrical)
  • Monthly Salary: Rs. 70,000 to Rs. 157,500, along with a 5% annual increment
  • Work Location: Sub-Engineers and Supervisors may be posted or transferred to project sites anywhere in Pakistan. Posting locations for other roles: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Application Deadline: October 26, 2026

Eligibility Criteria

  • Qualifications: Applicants whose minimum required qualification is below second division will not qualify. Specific educational and experience requirements per post: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Age Limit: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Upper-age relaxation is available under applicable federal government rules.
  • Government Employees: Staff working in government, semi-government, or autonomous organizations must apply through the proper channel and provide a No Objection Certificate (NOC).

How to Apply

Both online registration and physical document submission are mandatory:

  • Review the criteria on IRSA's official website and fill out the online form on the NTS platform.
  • Print the completed online application and attach attested copies of educational degrees, experience certificates, CNIC, domicile, and NOC (if applicable).
  • Mail the printed package to NTS Headquarters, Plot No. 96, Street No. 4, Sector H-8/1, Islamabad before the deadline.
  • Submit separate online forms and physical documents for each role if applying for multiple posts. Applications submitted by hand or left incomplete will be rejected.

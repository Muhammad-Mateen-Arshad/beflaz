Federal Secretariat Jobs 2025-26: Senate Directs Quotas

Published on November 24, 2026

A recent meeting regarding Federal Secretariat Jobs 2025-26: Senate Directs Quotas compliance highlighted the need for equitable provincial representation across federal departments under Article 38(g) of the Constitution. A Senate subcommittee chaired by Senator Barrister Zamir Hussain Ghumro instructed the Establishment Division to strictly enforce provincial recruitment shares. Application deadlines were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The Senate subcommittee addressed reports indicating that Punjab holds 60.17 percent of senior positions in Basic Pay Scales (BPS) 18 to 22 within the Federal Secretariat for 2025-26. To correct this over-representation, the committee directed authorities to immediately enforce constitutional quotas for all four provinces, with special emphasis on Sindh and Balochistan. The Federal Public Service Commission was also instructed to provide details on vacant positions and recruitment numbers for candidates from Sindh and Balochistan.

Key Details

  • Target Positions: Senior federal posts in BPS 18 to 22 as well as lower-grade posts in BPS 1 to 15.
  • Balochistan Opportunities: 5,136 posts in BPS 1 to 15 recently advertised, alongside federal cabinet exemptions and a broader plan to generate 200,000 employment opportunities for the province.
  • Sindh Quota Instructions: Establishment Division must submit a comprehensive BPS 1 to 15 rectification plan within two weeks and remove the rural-urban divide to maintain a unified provincial share.

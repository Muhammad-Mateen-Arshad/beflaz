WAPDA BPS-17 Recruitment for Engineers & Officers

Published on November 09, 2026

The Water and Power Development Authority has announced the WAPDA BPS-17 Recruitment for Engineers & Officers, offering 141 regular vacancies nationwide. This hiring drive is open to qualified professionals in engineering, geology, management, and public relations. Candidates must submit their applications within 15 days of the official advertisement publication.

Role Overview

The opportunity spans nine categories of regular BPS-17 posts across Pakistan. Appointees may be stationed anywhere in the country and will serve an initial one-year probation period. Available roles include Assistant Executive Engineers in civil, electrical, mechanical, and electronics fields, as well as Junior Seismologists, Junior Geologists, a Research Officer in hydraulics, and Assistant Directors for HR&A and Public Relations.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Engineering & Research Officer: B.E. or B.Sc. in the relevant discipline from an HEC-recognized university with a minimum CGPA of 2.0 out of 4.0, along with valid PEC registration.
  • Geology & Seismology Posts: M.Sc. or a four-year honors degree in geophysics, seismology, or geology with at least a second division or a 2.0 CGPA.
  • Assistant Director (HR&A): MBA, MPA, Master's in HRM, MS in Project Management, or a four-year honors degree in management or project management, with at least a second division or a 2.0 CGPA.
  • Assistant Director (Public Relations): M.A. in Journalism or a four-year honors degree in public relations with at least a second division and three years of professional journalism experience.
  • Age Limit: Applicants must be 21 to 33 years old on the closing date. Additional relaxation applies to candidates from specified areas, minorities, and eligible government or WAPDA staff.

Key Details

  • Total Positions: 141 regular posts (including 67 posts for Assistant Executive Engineer Civil).
  • Salary: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Test Fee: Rs. 200 payable at Bank Alfalah, UBL, MCB, or Bank Islami.
  • Selection Method: Written examination conducted by Central Testing Services (CTS) followed by WAPDA interviews.

How to Apply

Candidates must submit an online application through the CTS website within 15 days of the advertisement's publication. Applicants must then send a printed copy of the submitted form, original fee challan, attested educational certificates, CNIC, domicile, experience letters, and two passport-size photos via courier or Pakistan Post to the CTS office at 414, F2, Jeff Heights, Main Boulevard, Gulberg-III, Lahore. Late submissions and applications delivered by hand will be rejected.

Pakistan Railways Announces 829 Jobs Under Balochistan Quota

Published on October 28, 2026

Pakistan Railways has approved a recruitment drive offering 829 job opportunities for eligible candidates under the Balochistan provincial quota. The announced positions span various employment levels from Grade 1 to Grade 16 and follow federal government policies focused on merit and transparency.

Role Overview

This hiring initiative aims to fill vacant posts across the railway network. Out of the 829 total vacancies, 805 positions are allocated to the Quetta Division, while 24 positions are reserved for the Pakistan Railways Headquarters. To support equal opportunity, the department has set aside a 10 per cent quota for women and a 5 per cent quota for minority communities.

Key Details

  • Hiring Organization: Pakistan Railways
  • Total Positions: 829 vacancies (Grade 1 to Grade 16)
  • Allocation: 805 posts in Quetta Division, 24 posts at Headquarters
  • Special Quotas: 10% reserved for women, 5% reserved for minorities
  • Application Deadline: November 11, 2026
  • Target Completion Date: November 30, 2026
  • Salary: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice

Eligibility Criteria

The positions are open to eligible candidates applying under the Balochistan quota. The official details note that special exemptions from screening and written tests apply to certain appointment categories. Specific educational qualifications and age limits are not specified in the source; please check the official notice for these details.

How to Apply

Eligible candidates can submit their applications online through the federal government's National Job Portal. Applicants should carefully examine the official job postings, eligibility conditions, and selection procedures on the portal before submitting their forms ahead of the November 11, 2026 deadline.

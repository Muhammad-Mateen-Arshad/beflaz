25,000 Federal Jobs Announced for Balochistan Youth

Published on November 20, 2026

A major recruitment drive featuring 25,000 federal jobs announced for Balochistan youth is underway to expand public sector employment across the province. This initiative is designed for local job seekers aiming to secure formal government positions in various districts. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

Chief Minister Sarfraz Bugti confirmed that recruitment for 25,000 federal government vacancies will proceed continuously. The positions are being distributed among districts in proportion to their population to ensure balanced representation across Balochistan. The employment program aims to help local youth transition away from hazardous informal jobs, such as transporting Iranian fuel using Zambad vehicles, into stable public careers.

  • Target Audience: Unemployed youth residing in Balochistan.

Interview Updates and Selection Concerns

Applicants recently held a demonstration outside BUITEMS in Quetta concerning interviews for vacancies in QESCO and WAPDA. The protesting candidates highlighted issues regarding interview management and demanded a fully transparent, merit-based selection process free from favoritism. Candidates have called on authorities to investigate these alleged irregularities to ensure fair evaluation for all applicants.

Punjab Police Clearance Certificate Reforms 2026: Key Updates

Published on November 19, 2026

The Punjab Police Clearance Certificate Reforms 2026 aim to bring major relief to job seekers, visa applicants, and citizens previously cleared of criminal allegations by court decisions. Under the proposed legislation, acquittals and dismissed legal proceedings will no longer appear on official character certificates. Application deadlines for this legislative update are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The provincial government has introduced the Punjab Police Order (Amendment) Act 2026 to modify the existing Punjab Police Order 2002. This initiative establishes a transparent procedure for background checks, protecting the reputation and employment prospects of citizens who have been discharged or acquitted by courts when applying for domestic jobs, overseas employment, or immigration visas.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Clean Record Applicants: Citizens who have no background of criminal proceedings.
  • Cleared Individuals: Citizens previously involved in criminal cases that officially ended in acquittal, discharge, or dismissal, who can present certified court documents.
  • Exclusions: Habitual offenders will not be granted protection under this law, and their status will remain listed on certificates.

Key Details

  • Issuance Timelines: Clearance certificates for individuals without criminal records will be processed within 3 days. For applicants with cleared cases, document verification will take up to 15 days.
  • Appeal Rights: Rejections must be issued as written orders detailing the reasons. Applicants have 14 days to appeal, and the reviewing officer must make a ruling within 30 days.
  • Current Status: The bill has been presented in the Punjab Assembly, sent to the Standing Committee on Law for review, and awaits final assembly approval and governor assent.
  • Salary and Post Vacancies: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Citizens can apply for their character certificates through their local district police chiefs. Individuals seeking to exclude past cleared cases must provide certified copies of their favorable court judgments for verification. Online registration links or fee structures are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

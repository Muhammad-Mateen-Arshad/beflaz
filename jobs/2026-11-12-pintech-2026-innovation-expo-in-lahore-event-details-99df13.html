PINTECH 2026 Innovation Expo in Lahore: Event Details

Published on November 12, 2026

The PINTECH 2026 Innovation Expo in Lahore is a major event organized by the Punjab Higher Education Commission to display technological advancements, student initiatives, and academic research. Designed for students, researchers, entrepreneurs, and industry partners, this event aims to connect academic talent with national and international innovation networks. Deadlines for entry are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The event focuses on transforming university research into practical products, services, and commercial ventures. Around 380 shortlisted projects and ideas will be featured, representing a wide array of emerging fields. Key focus areas include artificial intelligence, robotics, biotechnology, precision agriculture, digital healthcare, veterinary sciences, and renewable energy. The initiative supports a broader shift toward making higher education institutions active contributors to economic development.

Eligibility Criteria

Participation involves young innovators, university faculty, researchers, investors, policymakers, and industry representatives. Eight Chinese universities are also taking part to foster joint research and international technology transfer. Specific individual applicant eligibility criteria or educational qualifications are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Key Details

  • Organising Body: Punjab Higher Education Commission (PHEC), under the leadership of Chairperson Prof. Dr. Iqrar Ahmad Khan.
  • Event Dates: November 14–15.
  • Location: Royal Palm Golf & Country Club, Lahore.
  • Project Scale: Approximately 380 shortlisted projects on display.
  • International Collaboration: Includes eight Chinese institutions, coinciding with the 75th anniversary of Pakistan-China diplomatic relations.
  • Stipend / Salary: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

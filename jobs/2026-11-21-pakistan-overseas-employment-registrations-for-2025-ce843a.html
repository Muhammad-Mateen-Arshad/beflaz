Published on November 21, 2026

Recent data on Pakistan Overseas Employment Registrations for 2025 highlights strong international demand for workers across several labor markets. The figures released by the Bureau of Emigration and Overseas Employment outline job trends for professionals, skilled trade workers, and general laborers seeking employment abroad. Role Overview

International employment opportunities cover a wide range of skill levels and sectors. Key job categories include manual laborers and drivers, alongside high-skilled professions such as doctors, engineers, accountants, computer analysts, teachers, and nurses. Technical trades are also prominently featured, including electricians, fitters, welders, carpenters, masons, plumbers, cooks, and steel fixers.

Key Details

  • Total Registrations: A total of 763,526 individuals registered for foreign jobs in 2025, while 476,238 registered during the first ten months of 2026.
  • Job Availability: Out of 220,170 reported foreign employment vacancies, 106,978 positions remained available, while 113,192 had been filled.
  • Primary Locations: Gulf countries account for the vast majority of registrations, led by Saudi Arabia with 530,437 registrations in 2025. Other primary destinations include the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, Kuwait, and Türkiye, with smaller numbers reaching the UK, US, Germany, Italy, Greece, and Spain.

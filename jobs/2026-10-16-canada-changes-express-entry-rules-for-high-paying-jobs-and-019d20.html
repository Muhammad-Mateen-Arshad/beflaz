Canada changes Express Entry rules for high-paying jobs and removes LMIA requirement

Published on October 16, 2026

Role Overview

Canada is updating its Express Entry immigration system to help skilled professionals in high-paying sectors obtain permanent residency more easily. Announced by Immigration Minister Lena Diab, these updates introduce additional points for candidates earning higher salaries and temporarily eliminate certain employer requirements to streamline the application process.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicants must possess Canadian work experience or a qualifying job offer in a high-wage field, such as engineering.
  • To qualify as high-wage employment, the role must pay at least 1.3 times the national median hourly wage, which currently stands at $55.03 according to the Canada Job Bank.
  • Candidates earning higher salaries will be awarded additional points toward their Express Entry score.
  • Specific educational qualifications, age limits, and language proficiency requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Key Details

  • Effective Date: The changes take effect on November 3.
  • LMIA Exemption: Employers offering qualifying positions under the new point system temporarily do not need to complete a Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) to prove a Canadian worker could not be found.
  • Automatic Score Adjustments: Applicants who are already in the Express Entry pool will have their scores updated automatically under the new system.
  • Pause on Invitations: Invitations to apply for permanent residency will be temporarily paused while candidate scores are updated.
  • Program Goals: The government aims to simplify the immigration application process and attract skilled talent in higher-paying industries, following previous focused measures for workers in healthcare, research, transportation, and skilled trades.
  • Number of Posts / Openings: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Candidates already in the Express Entry pool do not need to take action to update their criteria, as their scores will automatically refresh. Application deadlines and specific submission step-by-step instructions are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Samajwadi Party Jan-Man Manifesto Employment Schemes

Published on November 27, 2026

The Samajwadi Party Jan-Man Manifesto Employment Schemes outline key proposed measures designed to offer financial assistance and job opportunities in Uttar Pradesh. Announced during the launch of the party's election platform for 2027, these proposals target graduates, Agniveers, and local workers. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The Samajwadi Party's manifesto, titled "PDA Ka Pran Patra," sets out several workforce and educational commitments across the state. Proposed initiatives include:

  • A monthly allowance of ₹5,000 for graduate students until they obtain employment.
  • Employment opportunities for Agniveers within the police force and other departments.
  • The introduction of an Urban Employment Guarantee Act.
  • The reopening of 26,000 closed primary schools.

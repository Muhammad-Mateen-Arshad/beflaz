Finance Division Debt Management Office Job Openings

Published on November 24, 2026

The government is offering Finance Division Debt Management Office Job Openings for experienced professionals seeking to strengthen the public debt management framework. This recruitment drive aims to fill key operational and strategic roles within the Finance Division. The deadline to apply is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The recruitment focuses on two main positions in the Debt Management Office (DMO): Director Operations and Assistant Director Strategy and Operations. The Director Operations will be responsible for leading the governance and operational frameworks of the DMO. Key tasks include managing debt recording, debt servicing, legal documentation, IT security, audit functions, and risk controls against compliance failures or fraud. This role also involves organizing domestic borrowing meetings and preparing debt reporting alongside the State Bank of Pakistan and other departments.

The Assistant Director Strategy and Operations will work on improving the strategic capacity of the DMO by studying international borrowing practices. Responsibilities include implementing the Medium-Term Debt Management Strategy, monitoring contingent liabilities and government guarantees, and drafting technical responses for parliamentary standing committees.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Director Operations: Requires a master’s degree in economics, finance, public policy, or a related discipline (or specified professional qualifications), plus a minimum of 12 years of relevant professional experience in public debt management, treasury, or financial and capital markets.
  • Assistant Director Strategy and Operations: Requires at least 7 years of relevant professional experience in public debt management, risk management, or strategic management within banking, financial markets, or public finance.

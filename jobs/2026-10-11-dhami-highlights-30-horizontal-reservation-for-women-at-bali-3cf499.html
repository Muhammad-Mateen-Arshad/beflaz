Dhami highlights 30% horizontal reservation for women at Balika Shiksha Protsahan Programme

Published on October 11, 2026

Uttarakhand Chief Minister Pushkar Singh Dhami recently highlighted key employment and educational updates for female candidates in the state. Speaking at the Balika Shiksha Protsahan Programme hosted at the IRTD Auditorium in Dehradun, the Chief Minister emphasized the government's policy reserving opportunities for women across state services.

Role Overview

The state administration has implemented a 30 percent horizontal reservation for female candidates across all government positions in Uttarakhand. This policy is designed to ensure women are represented across every public employment sector, supporting their development from education and skill-building through to active employment.

WAPDA Announces Jobs Across Multiple Departments

Published on October 30, 2026

The Water and Power Development Authority (WAPDA) has announced a recruitment drive offering 37 job vacancies across 13 main categories. These opportunities are distributed across various offices and project sites, including Lahore, Chashma, Tarbela, Mangla, and Dasu. Positions are available under Basic Pay Scales (BPS) 7 to 16 and are reserved for eligible candidates holding a Balochistan domicile.

Role Overview

WAPDA is hiring technical and administrative personnel to fill open positions across several departments. Advertised roles include Staff Nurses, Trained Graduate Teachers, Junior Clerk cum Computer Typists, LTV Drivers, Stenographers Grade-II, Junior Technicians, Attendants, Lab Assistants, Surveyors, Wireless Operators, Librarians, and Audit Assistants.

Key Details

  • Total Vacancies: 37 positions across 13 job categories
  • Pay Scale Range: BPS-7 to BPS-16
  • Eligible Domicile: Balochistan
  • Work Locations: Lahore, Chashma, Tarbela, Mangla, and Dasu
  • Application Deadline: November 12, 2026
  • Exact Salary and Age Limits: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Eligibility Criteria

Qualifications range from Matriculation and Intermediate to bachelor’s and master’s degrees depending on the role:

  • Staff Nurses (10 posts): Must be registered A-Grade nurses.
  • LTV Drivers (6 posts): Matriculation qualification with a minimum of five years driving experience after obtaining an LTV licence.
  • Junior Clerk cum Computer Typists (5 posts): Intermediate/HSSC/ICom with at least a second division, a typing speed of 40 words per minute, and a minimum 3-month MS Office certificate.
  • Trained Graduate Teachers (4 posts): Second-division master's degree or 4-year bachelor's degree (minimum CGPA 2.0/4.0), plus a recognized teaching qualification such as BEd, MEd, BEd (Hons), MA Education, or Associate Diploma in Education.
  • Stenographer Grade-II: Second-class bachelor's degree meeting required typing and computer proficiency standards.
  • Junior Technician: Relevant vocational or diploma qualification in mechanical or electrical technology with three years of experience.
  • Attendant: Diploma of Associate Engineering in Electrical, Mechanical, or Electronics.
  • Lab Assistant: Matriculation with Science.
  • Surveyor: Matriculation with Science plus a surveying certificate, or FSc with at least a second division.
  • Wireless Operator: Relevant certification and five years of experience in the Army, Police, Rangers, or another government/semi-government entity.

How to Apply

Interested applicants must submit their forms online via the official National Jobs Portal by navigating to the Balochistan section. Applications delivered by hand or sent through post will not be accepted. Applicants must carefully check all academic and experience requirements before completing their online submission on or before November 12, 2026.

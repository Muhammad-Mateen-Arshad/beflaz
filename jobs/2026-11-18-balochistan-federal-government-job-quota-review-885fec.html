Balochistan Federal Government Job Quota Review

Published on November 18, 2026

A Senate subcommittee recently conducted a Balochistan Federal Government Job Quota Review during a meeting held at Parliament House in Islamabad. The session evaluated progress on provincial quota recruitment in federal ministries and divisions to support local candidates from Balochistan. Specific application deadlines were not provided in the update.

Role Overview

The Senate subcommittee on problems confronting underdeveloped areas, chaired by Senator Jan Muhammad Buledi, reviewed the recruitment status against Balochistan's reserved quota in federal institutions. The meeting addressed the implementation of the Aghaz-i-Haqooq-i-Balochistan Package and overall developmental initiatives. After learning that complete records of federal appointments for provincial candidates were unavailable from the Establishment Division, the subcommittee issued directions for a comparative report covering quotas, current appointments, and vacant seats.

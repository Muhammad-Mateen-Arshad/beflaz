Disappointment trails UK’s new policy on foreign student visa

Published on October 25, 2026

Role Overview

The United Kingdom is introducing tighter visa rules that impact foreign students, particularly those from Nigeria and Pakistan. The updated policies make securing study offers, meeting financial conditions, and staying in the country to work after graduation significantly more challenging.

Key Details

The policy changes introduce the following updates for prospective international students and job seekers:

  • Post-Study Work Duration: Starting in January 2027, post-study work rights will be shortened from two years to 18 months.
  • Financial Proof: Effective November 30, 2026, students must show maintenance funds of £1,570 per month for London living or £1,203 per month outside London.
  • University Screening: UK universities risk losing their licenses if visa refusal rates exceed five percent, resulting in increased pre-screening and higher application withdrawal rates.
  • Employer Expenses: Employers face a 32 percent increase in the Immigration Skills Charge.
  • Dependent Rules: Restrictions on bringing family members for most taught Master's programs continue to apply.

This transition trend has declined since 2023 following tighter oversight in care-sector recruitment.

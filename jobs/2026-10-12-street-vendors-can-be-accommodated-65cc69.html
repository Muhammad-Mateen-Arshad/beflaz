Street vendors can be accommodated

Published on October 12, 2026

Street vending and small-scale informal commerce provide significant self-employment opportunities across urban areas. Based on recent information regarding Pakistan's informal economy and initiatives like Punjab's Sasta and Sahulat Bazaars, small traders and vendors contribute directly to local commercial activity by providing everyday goods and services.

Role Overview

Self-employed individuals in this sector engage in selling vegetables, cooked food, tea, flowers, clothing, and household items, as well as providing services such as shoe repair. These activities generally operate near high-footfall locations including transport hubs, educational institutions, offices, hospitals, and commercial markets. For individuals with limited starting capital, small roadside stalls and carts offer a practical option for earning a livelihood without the heavy financial requirements of renting formal shops.

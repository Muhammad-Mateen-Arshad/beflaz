Canada Express Entry PR Rules for Professors 2026

Published on November 20, 2026

Canada has updated its Canada Express Entry PR rules for professors and lecturers to make permanent residency more accessible for foreign academic professionals. Implemented by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC), these changes allow non-salary forms of compensation to qualify toward immigration requirements. While there is no fixed application deadline, candidates must receive an invitation to apply on or after November 17, 2026, to benefit from this policy.

Role Overview

Under the revised guidelines, university professors and lecturers classified under National Occupational Classification code 41200 can now claim Canadian work experience and job offers paid through stipends, grants, bursaries, and scholarships rather than traditional salaries. These updates apply to Express Entry pathways, specifically the Canadian Experience Class and the Federal Skilled Worker Program. The change aims to properly recognize common academic compensation structures while attracting and retaining international researchers.

Key Details

  • Comprehensive Ranking System Points: Applicants can gain 70 additional points for at least one year of qualifying Canadian work experience, or 100 extra points for a valid job offer.
  • Occupational Focus: University professors and lecturers are now the only eligible occupational group under this category, as post-secondary teaching and research assistants (NOC 41201) have been removed.
  • Salary: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Deadline: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice. The temporary policy has no fixed expiration date and can be withdrawn at any time.

Eligibility Criteria

To qualify under these updated immigration rules, applicants must meet the following conditions:

  • Must have received an invitation to apply on or after November 17, 2026.
  • Must have completed a minimum of one year of full-time work experience, or equivalent part-time experience, in Canada within the past three years.
  • Must demonstrate that their work history matches the standard duties of university professors and lecturers.
  • Must fulfill general immigration criteria, including language proficiency requirements and admissibility.

How to Apply

Eligible academic candidates are advised to log in and update their Express Entry profiles to reflect their qualifying Canadian work experience and job offers. Further guidance and formal announcements can be checked through official immigration notices.

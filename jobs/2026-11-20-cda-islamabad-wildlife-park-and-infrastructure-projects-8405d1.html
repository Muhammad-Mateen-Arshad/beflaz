CDA Islamabad Wildlife Park and Infrastructure Projects

Published on November 20, 2026

The Capital Development Authority (CDA) has approved major developments, including the CDA Islamabad Wildlife Park and Infrastructure Projects across the federal capital. These public development initiatives are aimed at enhancing tourism, conservation, and local facilities for citizens and international visitors. Application deadlines and submission cutoffs are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The CDA Development Working Party has approved the PC-I for the National Wildlife Park – Nature Kingdom in Malpur, valued at Rs22.4 billion. Spanning 450 acres, the facility will host 6,000 animals across 400 species, incorporating themed safari zones, conservation facilities, veterinary centers, and a visitor tram service. Additionally, the authority approved a Rs16.7 billion PC-I for a 36,000-capacity cricket stadium near Sector D-12, alongside launching competitive processes for the Rs12 billion Shahdra Dam project.

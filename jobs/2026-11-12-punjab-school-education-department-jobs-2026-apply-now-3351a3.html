Punjab School Education Department Jobs 2026: Apply Now

Published on November 12, 2026

The Punjab School Education Department Jobs 2026 recruitment drive has been announced for 11 technical and administrative positions. This opportunity is open to qualified professionals specializing in information technology, software development, cybersecurity, and project coordination, with a submission deadline of November 25, 2026.

Role Overview

The recruitment is conducted by the Program Monitoring and Implementation Unit under the Punjab Education Sector Reforms Program as part of the project titled "Actions to Strengthen Performance for Inclusive and Responsive Education." Project-based positions are available under Project Pay Scales 8 to 10, with one post for each of the following titles:

  • Head of Technology (PPS-10)
  • Database Administrator
  • System Analyst
  • DevOps Engineer – Cyber Security
  • Senior Software Engineer
  • Senior Software Developers (UI/UX)
  • QA/Test Engineer
  • Mobile/Web Developer
  • Network/System Administrator
  • Junior Software Engineer
  • Coordination and Stakeholder Engagement Officer

Eligibility Criteria

  • Head of Technology: Requires a bachelor’s (Hons.) or master’s degree in Computer Science, IT, or a related field from an HEC-recognized university, combined with at least seven years of relevant experience in ERP solutions, data systems, and software deployment.
  • Database Administrator & System Analyst: Must hold relevant IT or Computer Science qualifications and have at least six years of professional experience.
  • Other Technical Positions: DevOps Engineer, Senior Software Engineer, UI/UX Developer, QA/Test Engineer, Mobile/Web Developer, Network/System Administrator, and Junior Software Engineer positions require a minimum of five years of relevant experience.
  • Coordination and Stakeholder Engagement Officer: Requires 16 years of education in Marketing, Communication Studies, Journalism, Public Policy, Social Sciences, or a related area, plus at least five years of experience in stakeholder engagement, communications, or project coordination. Preference is given to applicants with donor-funded project experience and knowledge of Punjab government systems.

Key Details

  • Total Vacancies: 11 posts
  • Pay Scales: PPS-8 to PPS-10
  • Deadline: November 25, 2026
  • Salary and Age Limit: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Interested candidates are required to complete an online application on the official Punjab Jobs Portal. Applicants must upload their educational degrees, certificates, and relevant experience documents. Submissions by hand, courier, or any alternative method will not be considered. Candidates serving in government, semi-government, or autonomous organizations must apply through the proper channel and submit a valid No Objection Certificate from their employer.

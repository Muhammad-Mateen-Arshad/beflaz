Punjab Tourism Department Jobs 2026: Apply by Nov 20

Published on November 05, 2026

The Punjab Tourism Department Jobs 2026 offer valuable career opportunities for qualified professionals, supervisors, and experienced artisans across the province. Under a specialized heritage restoration project, the government is filling over a hundred positions with fixed pay packages. Interested candidates holding a Punjab domicile must submit their online applications by November 20, 2026.

Role Overview

The Tourism, Archaeology & Museums Department of Punjab has announced hiring for the project titled Establishment of Tourism & Heritage Development Force for Revival of Built Heritage. A total of 110 positions are available across 31 distinct categories. Available job types include administrative, technical, and practical heritage restoration roles, such as Cultural Heritage Expert, Admin Officer, Field Supervisor, Photographer, and traditional artisan positions.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Domicile: Applicants must hold a valid Punjab domicile.
  • Education: Professional and administrative posts require a relevant bachelor's degree, master's degree, or diploma.
  • Experience: Administrative and technical roles require between 2 to 15 years or more of relevant experience. Traditional artisan positions—covering fresco painting, tile work, masonry, wood carving, stone restoration, and pottery—require 5 to 20 years of practical experience.
  • Age Limit: Age requirements depend on the specific position, and standard age relaxation applies based on Punjab government rules.

Key Details

  • Total Vacancies: 110 openings across 31 positions.
  • Salary Package: Fixed monthly salaries range from Rs. 100,000 to Rs. 450,000 under the Project Pay Scale (PPS) system. The highest pay of Rs. 450,000 is assigned to the Cultural Heritage Expert / Project Coordinator role.
  • Application Deadline: November 20, 2026.
  • Application Fee: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Test/Interview Location: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Candidates must submit their applications online through the Punjab Jobs Portal. Applicants should create an account or log in, complete their personal and professional profiles, search for openings under the Tourism, Archaeology & Museums Department, and submit their forms electronically before the deadline. Physical applications will not be accepted, and only shortlisted candidates will be invited for tests or interviews.

EU Delegation Pakistan Project Manager Job Opening

Published on November 07, 2026

The EU Delegation Pakistan Project Manager Job Opening offers a prominent development opportunity for professionals in economics, finance, business administration, and international development. Appointed candidates will drive economic growth initiatives, coordinate investments, and support the EU's Global Gateway Strategy from Islamabad. The application deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

Operating within the Delegation's Partnership Section under Local Agent Group 1, the appointed professional will work under the supervision of the Head of Rural Development and Economic Growth. Core duties involve managing financial and technical assistance initiatives, helping export-focused Pakistani SMEs comply with evolving EU green legislation, facilitating policy discussions with key stakeholders, and supporting official diplomatic visits and communications.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Educational Background: A Bachelor's degree in economics, finance, business administration, international development, or a closely related discipline.
  • Total Experience: Minimum of six years of relevant professional experience following completion of the degree.
  • Specialized Experience: At least three years of experience in finance or private sector development, along with a minimum of three years of work within international organizations or internationally funded projects.
  • Skills: Excellent command of English (speaking, reading, and writing) and strong computer literacy in Microsoft Word, Outlook, and Excel.
  • General Requirements: Medical fitness, full civil rights, a clean criminal record, and authorization to work under local employment laws.

Key Details

  • Duty Station: Islamabad, Pakistan
  • Anticipated Start Date: January 1, 2027
  • Annual Base Salary: Starting from PKR 7,718,664 (depending on verified relevant experience)
  • Application Deadline: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

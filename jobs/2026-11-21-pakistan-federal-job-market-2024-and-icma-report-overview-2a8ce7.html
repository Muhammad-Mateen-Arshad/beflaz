Pakistan Federal Job Market 2024 and ICMA Report Overview

Published on November 21, 2026

The Pakistan Federal Job Market 2024 and ICMA Report Overview provides an in-depth analysis of the country's employment landscape and public sector recruitment metrics. Published by ICMA International under the report title ICMA's Fiscal Vision for Pakistan: Aligning Administrative Structures with Economic Realities, this overview offers critical insights for graduates and candidates navigating the labor market. Application deadlines for specific vacancies are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The research report highlights substantial competition within the employment sector alongside expanding public administration costs. Approximately 3.5 million individuals enter the workforce every year. The intense demand for public sector positions is evidenced by federal recruitment data from 2024, which recorded 274,566 applications submitted for just 1,722 open positions. Meanwhile, administrative expenses absorb over four-fifths of provincial spending, limiting fiscal allocations for broader development.

Key Details

The findings outline several fiscal and institutional figures relevant to national employment and governance:

  • More than 80% of provincial expenditure goes toward recurrent costs, including salaries, pensions, education, and healthcare.
  • Local governments accounted for less than 5% of total government spending in 2024.

Draft Auto Policy 2026-31: Overview and Key Focus

Published on November 08, 2026

The Draft Auto Policy 2026-31 has received official approval from the Prime Minister following review and acknowledgment from the IMF. This framework aims to strengthen local industrial development, expand domestic vendor networks, and drive long-term job creation across the automotive sector. Specific application deadlines for individual positions are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The policy focuses on expanding domestic vehicle manufacturing and improving local industrial capabilities to generate economic self-reliance. Key objectives include encouraging local vendor development, shifting away from complete reliance on imported kits, and promoting electric vehicle adoption to reduce fuel import costs. The strategy also prioritizes stronger vehicle safety standards and improved consumer protection in market bookings.

Key Details

  • Export Goals: Establishes a cumulative export target of $6 billion over a five-year timeframe.
  • Automotive Development Levy: Imposes a revenue-neutral levy on internal combustion engine vehicles to fund electric vehicle and export incentives, with an exemption for vehicles having engine capacities of 1,000cc or lower.
  • Financial Incentives: Offers a 5% drawback of local taxes and levies, along with a 10% incentive on incremental annual export growth for qualifying companies.
  • Booking Regulations: Caps advance payments at the time of vehicle booking to a maximum of 20% of the total price.
  • Industry Scope: Highlights an existing base of 16 car and SUV manufacturers alongside 100 two-wheeler manufacturers.

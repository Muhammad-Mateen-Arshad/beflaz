NHA Announces Jobs

Published on October 28, 2026

The National Highway Authority has officially announced 29 regular government job vacancies reserved for candidates holding a Balochistan domicile. These employment opportunities cover 15 distinct job categories across administration, engineering, finance, forestry, technical, and support staff functions.

Role Overview

The advertised openings span pay scales from BPS-04 to BPS-18. Available positions include Deputy Director (Engineer), Assistant Director (Engineer, Afforestation, Accounts/Finance), Inspector (Civil and Afforestation), Assistant Private Secretary, Computer Operator, Office Assistant, Accounts Assistant, Stenotypist, Lab Technical, Lower Division Clerk (LDC), Patwari, and Driver.

Key Details

  • Organization: National Highway Authority
  • Total Vacancies: 29 positions (Balochistan quota)
  • Pay Scale Range: BPS-04 to BPS-18
  • Advertisement Date: October 28, 2026
  • Application Deadline: November 11, 2026
  • Salary: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice
  • Job Location: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice

Eligibility Criteria

Educational and experience requirements vary across positions:

  • Deputy Director (Engineer): Civil Engineering qualification with 7 years of experience for BSc holders, 4 years for MSc, or 2 years for PhD holders. Upper age limit is 40 years.
  • Assistant Director Positions: 6 posts exist for Assistant Director (Engineer) requiring an MSc/MS or first-class BSc/BS in Civil Engineering from an HEC-recognised university. Other roles require a 4-year degree in Forestry (Afforestation) or a BCom (Accounts/Finance). Upper age limit is 35 years.
  • Inspectors: 3-year Civil Engineering diploma with 10 years of bridge/highway construction experience, or a 4-year Forestry degree with 5 years of experience for Inspector (Afforestation). Upper age limit is 35 years.
  • Administrative & Support Staff: Qualifications range from Graduation for Computer Operator and Assistant Private Secretary (upper age 28), Bachelor's degree or BCom for Assistants (upper age 25), Intermediate for Stenotypist (upper age 25), to Matric for LDC and Patwari roles (upper age 25).
  • Drivers: Requires Primary school education, a valid driving licence, and familiarity with traffic rules. Upper age limit is 30 years.
  • Age Relaxation: Eligible Balochistan candidates get an additional 5-year upper age relaxation on top of the general 5-year relaxation under federal rules.

How to Apply

Interested applicants must complete their application online through the National Jobs Portal:

  • Visit the National Jobs Portal and log in or register a new account.
  • Search for National Highway Authority positions.
  • Select the job matching your qualification, experience, and Balochistan domicile.
  • Complete the online application form with accurate personal, academic, and professional info.
  • Submit your application online. District Employment Cells are also available to facilitate applicants.

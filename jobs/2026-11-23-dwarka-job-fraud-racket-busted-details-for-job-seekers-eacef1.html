Dwarka Job Fraud Racket Busted: Details for Job Seekers

Published on November 23, 2026

A fake Dwarka job fraud racket has been uncovered by the Delhi Police after an illegal placement agency duped unemployed candidates in the region. This update provides essential details for job seekers regarding fraudulent hiring schemes recently operating in Patel Garden near Dwarka. Application deadlines and valid vacancy procedures are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The Cyber Police Station in Dwarka raided an unofficial placement setup at Patel Garden after receiving secret information and analyzing complaints on the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal. The operator advertised fake employment vacancies through physical pamphlets placed at various sites across Delhi. Job seekers who responded were invited for interviews and persuaded to pay registration fees and uniform charges. However, no jobs or uniforms were provided, and candidates who followed up were subsequently blocked.

Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Key Details

  • Main Accused: Sarvesh Kumar, aged 34, was arrested on October 23 for managing the placement office.
  • HR Staff Involvement: Two female HR employees conducted candidate interviews and collected money, and have been bound down under Section 35(3) of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita.
  • Daily Footfall: Approximately 10 to 15 job seekers visited the office each day.
  • Seized Items: Police recovered three mobile phones, 29 advertising pamphlets, and an attendance register from the location.
  • Financial and Legal Action: Authorities identified four bank accounts and linked nine cybercrime complaints from various states via the NCRP and Samanvaya portals. A case was filed under Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita Sections 318(4), 317(2), 112, and 3(5).

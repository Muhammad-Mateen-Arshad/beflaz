CM Saini’s Sweden visit yields Rs 4,000-cr LoIs

Published on October 16, 2026

Role Overview

Following an official delegation to Sweden led by Chief Minister Nayab Singh Saini alongside Urban Local Bodies Minister Vipul Goel, the Haryana state government has secured Letters of Intent (LoIs) worth Rs 4,000 crore. This international agreement with Swedish firms is set to boost local industrial growth, upgrade logistics infrastructure, and create upcoming career opportunities for skilled youth in the state.

Key Details

  • Investment Breakdown: Includes a primary LoI valued at Rs 3,000 crore alongside additional agreements totaling Rs 1,000 crore focused on warehousing and retail.
  • Target Sectors: Modern warehousing infrastructure, expanding retail chains, logistics, smart city development, and technological innovation.
  • City-to-City Partnership: A direct alliance between Gurugram and Stockholm focusing on sustainable urban planning, knowledge exchange, and smart city implementations.
  • Career & Skill Benefits: Assurances from Swedish enterprises to offer job opportunities, skill development, and specialized training meeting global standards for local skilled youth.

Global Rooftop Solar Initiative 2030: Key Details

Published on November 11, 2026

The Global Rooftop Solar Initiative 2030: Key Details outline an international effort aimed at expanding solar energy to 300 million homes globally by 2030. This initiative targets participating countries, including Bangladesh, Kenya, Nigeria, Vanuatu, and Australia, to lower setup costs, modernise power grids, and foster workforce development. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

This global program focuses on doubling solar-powered households worldwide by modernising electricity networks, simplifying approval workflows, reducing system installation costs, and preparing a skilled labor force. Participating governments aim to accelerate clean energy adoption, create jobs, and enhance energy independence across energy-deficient and developing regions.

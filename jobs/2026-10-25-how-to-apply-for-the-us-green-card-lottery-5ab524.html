How to apply for the US Green Card lottery

Published on October 25, 2026

The Diversity Visa Program offers foreign nationals an opportunity to obtain a Green Card to live and work permanently in the United States. Each year, up to 55,000 immigrant visas are made available through this lottery system for individuals from nations with historically low rates of US immigration.

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must meet basic requirements regarding country of origin and education or work history:

  • Country Requirement: Entrants must be born in an eligible country. While Kazakhstan was eligible in the last published guidelines, the complete current list of eligible countries is not specified in the source; please check the official notice before submitting.
  • Education or Experience: Candidates must satisfy at least one of the following:
    • Completion of a secondary education equivalent to 12 years of US schooling.
    • Two years of qualifying work experience within the past five years in an occupation designated by the US Department of Labor.

Key Details

  • Number of Visas: Up to 55,000 annually.
  • Registration Window: For the DV-2027 cycle, official dates must be announced by October 29, with entries opening by November 1, 2026.
  • Application Fees: A $1 entry fee is required upon submission. Selected applicants must pay a $330 application fee per person, alongside medical examination and additional immigration costs.
  • Results Notification: Results are not sent by email or physical mail. Applicants must check their status online using their saved confirmation number.

How to Apply

Qualified individuals can apply by following these instructions during the open registration period:

  • Access the official website at dvprogram.state.gov and complete the DS-5501 electronic entry form.
  • Provide all required details, including personal information, education, and listing your spouse and all living, unmarried children under 21.
  • Upload a JPEG passport scan (maximum 5 MB) containing the biographic and signature pages.
  • Upload a JPEG photograph (600 × 600 pixels, maximum 240 KB, taken within six months, on a plain light background, facing forward without glasses) for yourself and every required family member.
  • Pay the $1 fee through the authorized government payment system and submit the form. Submit only once, as multiple entries cause disqualification.
  • Save your unique confirmation number to check your status later.

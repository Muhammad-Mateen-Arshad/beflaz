SPSC Recruitment Update: Staff Actions and Advisory

Published on November 22, 2026

This SPSC Recruitment Update: Staff Actions and Advisory details recent integrity measures undertaken by the Sindh Public Service Commission for all applicants and stakeholders. The commission launched an internal review of complaints to ensure that all selection processes remain strictly merit-based. Specific job vacancy posts and application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

Following a review of commission records and public complaints led by Chairman Rizwan Ahmed, legal and disciplinary proceedings resulted in actions against staff members within the recruitment section:

  • Ghulam Ali Ghouri, Deputy Director (Recruitment): Dismissed from service.
  • Sohail Ahmed Patoli, Assistant Director (Recruitment): Dismissed from service.
  • Manzoor Ahmed Qureshi, Superintendent: Dismissed from service.
  • Fazal Mohammad Shah, Assistant in the recruitment section: Two annual increments withheld.

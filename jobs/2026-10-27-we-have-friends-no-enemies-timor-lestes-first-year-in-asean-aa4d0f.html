‘We Have Friends, No Enemies’: Timor-Leste’s First Year in ASEAN

Published on October 27, 2026

Timor-Leste's membership in the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) offers access to development assistance and capacity-building opportunities through the Initiative for ASEAN Integration (IAI). Covered under IAI Work Plan V (2026–2030), the national government is coordinating through a dedicated task force to establish programs that enhance local skills and institutional capacity across multiple sectors.

Role Overview

Although the source outlines broad development and initiative areas rather than a single specific position, key sectors targeted for capacity-building, technical assistance, and skills development include:

  • Digital skills development tailored for primary school teachers.
  • Youth capacity-building and professional qualification enhancement.
  • Technical assistance in sustainable tourism and resilient coffee production.
  • Institutional strengthening in trade, agriculture, and education.
  • Capacity improvements for domestic businesses to meet regional market standards.

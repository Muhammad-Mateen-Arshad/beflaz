Balochistan CSS Vacant Seats Update for 2024 and 2025

Published on November 18, 2026

This Balochistan CSS vacant seats update details recent official disclosures regarding federal competitive examination quota allocations for candidates from the province. During recent parliamentary discussions, officials confirmed that 32 reserved Central Superior Services positions remained unfilled across the 2024 and 2025 examination cycles. Specific application deadlines for prospective candidates are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

During a Senate Standing Committee on Cabinet Secretariat meeting chaired by Senator Rana Mahmood-ul-Hassan, the Establishment Division reported significant vacancies in federal allocations. In the CSS 2024 examinations, 15 out of 16 seats allocated to Balochistan remained unfilled, while another 17 seats were left vacant following the Special CSS Examination 2025. Although Senator Abdul Qadir introduced the FPSC Amendment Bill 2026 proposing an increased quota for five to ten years to support local youth, the committee deferred consideration of the bill. However, officials highlighted an active recruitment campaign launched across 12 districts in the province alongside government plans to extend employment opportunities to 100,000 young individuals.

Key Details

  • Vacant Positions: 32 total CSS seats (15 from CSS 2024 and 17 from Special CSS 2025).

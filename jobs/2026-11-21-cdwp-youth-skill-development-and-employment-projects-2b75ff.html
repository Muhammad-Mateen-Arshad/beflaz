CDWP Youth Skill Development and Employment Projects

Published on November 21, 2026

The CDWP Youth Skill Development and Employment Projects have been officially approved and recommended by the Central Development Working Party to enhance technical training, internships, and job opportunities across Pakistan. The forum considered multiple development initiatives aimed at empowering thousands of young people, particularly through national skill programs and targeted regional projects. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The approved initiatives focus on expanding vocational education, skills training, internships, and private-sector employment linkages for Pakistan's youth. Key programs include the Prime Minister's Youth Skill Development Programme (2023-28) and the Youth Education, Employment and Empowerment Project in Sindh. Additionally, border terminal projects in Balochistan aim to generate regional employment and regulate cross-border trade.

Key Details

  • Prime Minister's Youth Skill Development Programme (2023-28): Budgeted at Rs47.6 billion, to be funded via the Public Sector Development Programme (PSDP) and executed by the National Vocational and Technical Training Commission.
  • YEEEP-Sindh Project: Approved at a cost of Rs600 million, targeting 15,000 young individuals (approximately half of them women) with skills training, entrepreneurship, internships, and job linkages.
  • Border Terminal Projects: Approved terminals at Taftan (Rs4.80 billion) and Gabd (Rs4.35 billion) designed to create jobs and control smuggling.
  • Other Sector Approvals: Includes development schemes in agriculture, medical education, road infrastructure, cellular service expansion, and mineral exploration.

Eligibility Criteria

Target beneficiaries include young individuals seeking skill development, internships, and entrepreneurship support, with specific gender representation targets noted for Sindh.

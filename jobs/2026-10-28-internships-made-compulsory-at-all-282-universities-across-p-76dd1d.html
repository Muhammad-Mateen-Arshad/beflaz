Internships Made Compulsory at All 282 Universities Across Pakistan

Published on October 28, 2026

Students pursuing higher education across Pakistan are required to complete mandatory practical training as part of their degree programs. Parliamentary Secretary for Federal Education and Professional Training Rabia Naseem Farooqi announced during a National Assembly session that internships are compulsory for all university students to enhance their hands-on experience and boost career readiness.

Role Overview

First introduced in 2023, this policy aims to create stronger connections between academic institutions and the employment market. Under this policy, three academic credit hours are assigned to internships that correspond directly to each student’s degree program. The Higher Education Commission (HEC) holds the responsibility for ensuring institutions correctly enforce the rule.

To support student transition into the workforce, Youth Development Centres have also been set up at universities on the orders of Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif. These specialized centres provide academic and professional guidance, share insights regarding industry demands and open positions, and help establish direct networking opportunities between students and business leaders.

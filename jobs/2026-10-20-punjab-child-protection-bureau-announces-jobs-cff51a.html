Punjab Child Protection Bureau Announces Jobs

Published on October 20, 2026

The Punjab Child Protection and Welfare Bureau (CPWB), operating under the Home Department of the Government of Punjab, has announced 120 job vacancies across the province. This recruitment offers a great opportunity for qualified candidates to serve in public welfare and administrative roles under a contract scheme.

Role Overview

Successful applicants will be appointed on a lump-sum pay arrangement for an initial period of one year. These employment contracts may be extended depending on worker performance and the availability of funds. The open positions include Accountant, Nurse, Religious Teacher, Dispenser, and Child Attendant.

Key Details

  • Total Openings: 120 vacancies
  • Job Location: Across Punjab
  • Salary: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Test Fee: Rs. 550 (non-refundable and non-transferable)
  • Application Deadline: October 31, 2026

Eligibility Criteria

  • Nurse (Female): Requires a three-year nursing diploma from a recognized institute, a one-year midwifery diploma, and valid registration with the Pakistan Nursing Council. Knowledge of MS Office is preferred.
  • Accountant: Requires a B.Com degree (14 years of education) with at least a second division from an HEC-recognized university, plus a minimum of two years of relevant public-sector experience. Computer literacy in MS Office is preferred.
  • Religious Teacher (Male): Requires an HSSC qualification with at least a second division, a Hifz-ul-Quran certificate, and at least two years of relevant teaching experience in a recognized school.
  • Dispenser (Female): Requires an SSC with at least a second division, a dispenser diploma from the Punjab Medical Faculty, and one year of relevant experience. MS Office skills are preferred.
  • Child Attendant (Male & Female): Requires an SSC certificate with a minimum second division. Applicants with two years of experience in childcare, rehabilitation, supervision, or child welfare will be preferred.

How to Apply

Candidates must submit their application online through the National Testing Service (NTS) website. Applicants are required to pay the Rs. 550 written test fee via participating banks, ATMs, mobile banking, internet banking, Easypaisa, JazzCash, or TCS Express Counters.

Once the online registration is finished, applicants must print out the completed application form and attach two recent passport-size photos along with attested copies of their CNIC, domicile, educational certificates, and experience documents.

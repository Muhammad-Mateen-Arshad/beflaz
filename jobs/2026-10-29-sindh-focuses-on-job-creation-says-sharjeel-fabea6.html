Sindh focuses on job creation, says Sharjeel

Published on October 29, 2026

The Sindh provincial government is prioritizing public service, economic development, and job creation across the region. Sindh Senior Minister Sharjeel Inam Memon announced that the government is taking practical steps to address public challenges, including unemployment and inflation, by establishing industrial zones and expanding infrastructure.

Role Overview

The initiatives focus on establishing industrial zones to generate new employment opportunities for the public. The provincial government is actively seeking international investors for these zones to strengthen both the local economy of Sindh and the overall national economy of Pakistan.

Published on November 14, 2026

This update covers the Delhi Court Updates on IAF Officer Chargesheet Case regarding Indian Air Force Wing Commander Manorath Singh Sabharwal. The legal proceedings concern charges under the Official Secrets Act following allegations of sharing classified defense information. Role Overview

The case involves Wing Commander Manorath Singh Sabharwal of the Indian Air Force, who was booked by the Special Cell of the Delhi Police. The officer is accused of supplying confidential information—such as troop deployments, military coordinates, and war plans—to a Pakistani intelligence operative.

Key Details

  • Court Directive: Chief Judicial Magistrate Mridul Gupta instructed police on October 31 to expedite the prosecution sanction required under Section 152 of the BNS and Section 13(3) of the Official Secrets Act.
  • Production Order: The court directed that the accused officer be produced on December 5.
  • Supervisory Requirement: The concerned Deputy Commissioner of Police must oversee the sanction process and submit a signed status report before the next court date.

"Cannon fodder" for the front: how Russia recruits soldiers with tricks

Published on October 29, 2026

Job seekers searching for employment in Russia are advised to exercise caution when reviewing active job listings. Multiple advertisements for seemingly standard civilian roles—such as text editors, construction workers, general laborers, equipment operators, cooks, press service staff, and shipping personnel—are being used by intermediary firms and recruiters to enlist individuals into military service contracts.

Role Overview

Advertisements published on popular job portals, including HeadHunter, Superjob, and Avito, often present high-paying civilian opportunities that may actually involve recruitment for military contracts. Candidates applying for roles in state-affiliated structures or rotational civilian work should carefully evaluate the exact terms of any contract before signing, as generic job titles like "specialist" have been used to sign applicants to military contracts.

Eligibility Criteria

Requirements vary depending on the listed position, but job seekers should keep the following criteria and recruitment patterns in mind:

  • Positions attract general job seekers, rotational workers, and technical operators.
  • Recruiters frequently focus on vulnerable candidates, including individuals facing debt, unemployment, mental health challenges, addictions, or orphans seeking fast income.
  • Both men and women are approached by recruiters, though female applicants are targeted with different tactics as they are not recruited for assault units.

Key Details

  • Location: Various regions across Russia, often offered on a rotational basis.
  • Salary: Advertised monthly salaries include 55,000 rubles for state-affiliated text editor roles, and between 150,000 and 200,000 rubles for high-paying rotational construction and equipment operator jobs.
  • Number of Posts: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Application Deadline: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

To navigate job offers safely, applicants should follow key precautions during the hiring process:

  • Inspect employment documents and contract language in advance to ensure the position is strictly civilian.
  • Be cautious of suspicious interview instructions, such as unlisted office addresses, requests to turn off mobile devices, or demands to undergo medical examinations at military commissariats.
  • If an offer appears suspicious, legal experts advise declining the interview entirely. If presented with a contract you do not wish to sign, legal advisers note that you have the right to break it.

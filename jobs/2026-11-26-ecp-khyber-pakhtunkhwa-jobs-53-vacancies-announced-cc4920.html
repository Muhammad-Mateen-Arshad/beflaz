ECP Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Jobs: 53 Vacancies Announced

Published on November 26, 2026

The Election Commission of Pakistan has announced ECP Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Jobs across eight distinct categories for candidates domiciled in the province. This recruitment drive offers 53 positions ranging from pay scales BS-02 to BS-15 for individuals holding primary to graduate-level education. Eligible male and female candidates must submit their applications within 20 days of the advertisement's publication in newspapers.

Role Overview

The Office of the Provincial Election Commissioner, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, is recruiting for multiple cadres across basic pay scales BS-02 to BS-15. Positions available include Senior Assistants, Data Entry Operators, Junior Assistants, Sub Assistants, Staff Car Drivers, Dispatch Riders, Naib Qasids, and Chowkidars. Seats are allocated under open merit as well as reserved quotas for women and minority applicants.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Senior Assistant: Graduation degree and computer literacy skills.
  • Data Entry Operator: Bachelor's degree with a one-year diploma in Computer Science or Information Technology, proficiency in Microsoft Office, minimum English typing speed of 40 wpm, Urdu typing skills, and internet/email proficiency.
  • Junior Assistant: Intermediate qualification, computer literacy, and a government-recognized three-week Basic IT Training Course covering MS Office.
  • Sub Assistant: Matriculation, minimum 30 wpm typing speed, and a government-recognized three-week Basic IT Training Course covering MS Office.
  • Staff Car Driver & Dispatch Rider: Matriculation, valid driving licence (HTV licence required for Driver), and knowledge of traffic rules.
  • Naib Qasid & Chowkidar: Primary education qualification.

Key Details

  • Total Vacancies: 53 seats across 8 job categories.
  • Location & Domicile: Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
  • Age Limit: Specified per role; 5 years general age relaxation applies along with federal rules relaxation.
  • Salary: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Application Deadline: Within 20 days of the newspaper publication date.

How to Apply

Application procedures depend on the pay scale of the position:

  • For Senior Assistant, Data Entry Operator, Junior Assistant, and Sub Assistant posts, applicants must submit an online application through the official ECP Online Recruitment System.
  • For Staff Car Driver, Dispatch Rider, Naib Qasid, and Chowkidar posts, candidates must send a completed application form by courier along with attested copies of educational certificates, CNIC, domicile, and required records.
  • Courier applications must be sent to: Deputy Director (Establishment), P.O. Box No. 281, GPO Peshawar. Late submissions will not be accepted.

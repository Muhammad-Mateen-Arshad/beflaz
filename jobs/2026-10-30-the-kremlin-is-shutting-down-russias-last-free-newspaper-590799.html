The Kremlin is shutting down Russia’s last free newspaper

Published on October 30, 2026

Role Overview

This alert provides an overview regarding the current status of Novaya Gazeta, an independent media organization established in 1993 in Moscow, Russia. Over its history, the newspaper covered major events, state violence, economic conditions, political censorship, and anti-war perspectives. Key figures within the organization include publisher Dmitry Muratov and editor-in-chief Sergei Sokolov.

Recent developments detailed in the source indicate that the newspaper's Moscow offices have officially closed. The organization faced eviction from its premises, blocked financial channels following a "foreign agent" designation, security break-ins, and threats of being declared an extremist group. While a portion of the editorial team previously moved to Latvia to launch Novaya Gazeta Europe, the remaining team in Moscow has ceased newspaper operations due to safety risks and government pressure.

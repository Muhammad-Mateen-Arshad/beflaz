Pakistan Auto Policy 2026: EV and Job Opportunities

Published on November 07, 2026

The federal government has introduced the Pakistan Auto Policy 2026: EV and Job Opportunities framework to encourage local vehicle assembly and expand industrial employment. Aimed at middle- and lower-income families as well as industry workers, the policy seeks to strengthen local manufacturing facilities across the country. An application deadline for specific job roles is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

Under the new policy announced on Nov. 7, 2026, the government expects automobile manufacturers to expand their local operations, set up more assembly plants, and increase the use of locally produced components. These structural changes are intended to lower production costs, boost exports, and create new job opportunities across Pakistan's industrial sector. Additionally, the policy introduces international UN WP.29 safety standards featuring 45 additional vehicle safety requirements.

Key Details

  • Electric Vehicle Goal: Transition 30% of all new vehicle sales to electric power by 2030.
  • Tax Rates for EVs: Proposed sales tax rates of 1% for fully electric, 6% for range-extended, and 9% for hybrid vehicles, pending IMF approval.
  • EV Exemptions: Vehicles priced up to $50,000 are exempt from capital value tax, withholding tax, and federal excise duty.
  • Financing Support: State Bank of Pakistan financing limit increased from Rs3 million to Rs10 million, with repayment extended from 3 to 5 years.
  • Tariff Framework: Annual reductions on import duties for assembly plants, components, and CBUs over five years.
  • Export Rebates: Qualified manufacturers receive a 5% tax drawback plus an additional 10% growth incentive, while non-compliant companies face penalties.

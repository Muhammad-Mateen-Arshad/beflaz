Punjab PIVOT Plan 2026-29 Jobs and Employment Focus

Published on November 11, 2026

The Punjab PIVOT Plan 2026-29 Jobs and Employment Focus outlines an economic transformation initiative launched under the leadership of Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif. Designed for youth, businesses, farmers, and technical institutions across the province, the scheme aims to convert local potential into sustainable economic growth and job opportunities. Role Overview

The Punjab Innovation for Value, Opportunity and Transformation (PIVOT) framework creates an integrated ecosystem connecting industry, technology, agriculture, and education. Instead of focusing only on basic production, PIVOT emphasizes value addition, industrial upgrading, and technological innovation. Key sectors targeted for advancement include textiles, garments, sports goods, leather, pharmaceuticals, smart agriculture, digital commerce, artificial intelligence, and renewable energy.

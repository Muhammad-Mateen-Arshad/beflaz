Karachi Services Hospital Announces Jobs

Published on October 28, 2026

Role Overview

The Sindh Government Services Hospital Karachi has announced 35 new government job vacancies across 11 different positions. These positions cover roles in medical support, technical services, administrative support, and general services. Positions range from BPS-01 to BPS-04, and the recruitment notice was officially published on October 28, 2026.

Key Details

Here are the key details provided regarding the current recruitment drive:

  • Total Vacancies: 35 positions
  • Pay Scales: BPS-01 to BPS-04
  • Age Requirement: 18 to 35 years for all posts
  • Specific Position Breakdown: 14 Ward Servants, 5 Sanitary Workers, 4 Chowkidars, 3 Drivers, along with Electricians, Dark Room Attendants, Lab Attendants, Operation Theatre Attendants, Dental Attendants, Aya/Dais, and Naib Qasids.
  • Quota Breakdown: 15 percent allocated for women and 5 percent for minority applicants. The 5 percent disability quota has already been filled through the Deputy Commissioner South.
  • Application Deadline: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Salary Amount: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Eligibility Criteria

Qualifications vary depending on the specific job applied for:

  • Driver: Must have completed Middle school, hold a valid Light Transport Vehicle (LTV) driving licence, and possess at least two years of relevant driving experience.
  • Electrician: Requires a Matric qualification and an Electrician Diploma from the Sindh Medical Faculty.
  • Dark Room Attendant: Candidates with a diploma from the Sindh Medical Faculty will be preferred.
  • Lab Attendant: Must have passed Matriculation with Science.
  • Operation Theatre & Dental Attendants: Require the relevant diploma from the Sindh Medical Faculty.
  • Aya/Dai, Ward Servant, Chowkidar, Naib Qasid, and Sanitary Worker: Require Middle school passing qualifications.

How to Apply

Applicants must send their completed applications along with necessary educational documents via Pakistan Post or a recognized courier service. Applications should be addressed to the Medical Superintendent, Services Hospital & Civil Surgeon, Karachi. Candidates are advised to select and apply for only one post that matches their background. Current government employees are required to submit their applications through the proper channel.

The hospital will review submissions and contact only shortlisted candidates for interviews. No daily allowance or travelling allowance will be provided to candidates appearing for the interview.

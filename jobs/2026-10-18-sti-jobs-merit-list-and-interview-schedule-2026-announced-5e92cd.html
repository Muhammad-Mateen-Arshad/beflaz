STI Jobs Merit List and Interview Schedule 2026 Announced

Published on October 18, 2026

Role Overview

The Punjab School Education Department has released the recruitment schedule and merit list details for School Teacher Interns (STI) positions for 2026. Opportunities are available across government primary, elementary, high, and higher secondary schools located throughout Punjab. Selected candidates will earn a monthly stipend ranging from Rs. 38,000 to Rs. 45,000. Please note that no travel allowance or daily allowance (TA/DA) will be provided to applicants during any stage of the selection procedure.

Eligibility Criteria

Both male and female candidates are eligible to participate in this recruitment process. Specific educational qualifications, age limits, and degree requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Key Details

The selection process will proceed according to the following official schedule:

  • Application Deadline: October 18, 2026, at 11:59 PM.
  • Initial Merit List: October 23, 2026, displayed on the notice boards of the respective government schools. Applicants can review their status and submit complaints regarding discrepancies.
  • Interviews: October 29 and October 30, 2026.
  • Final Merit List: October 31, 2026, at 10:00 AM, published on the official STI portal and school notice boards.
  • Agreement Letters: Issued to selected candidates on November 2, 2026, through the online portal.
  • Number of Posts: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Candidates must submit their applications online through the official STI recruitment portal before the stated deadline. New applicants need to register using their CNIC and password. Existing users can log in, update or complete their profile, choose the teaching internship options that correspond to their credentials, and submit their application.

UK mobilises £94m for Pakistan Climate Investment

Published on October 28, 2026

The United Kingdom has mobilized over £94 million for climate investments in Pakistan through its £108 million Climate Finance Accelerator Pakistan (CFAP) fund. Announced at the Climate Finance Accelerator (CFA) Sindh Roadshow in Karachi, this initiative has also helped build a £480 million pipeline of resilience and adaptation projects in the Sindh region. The effort aims to strengthen climate finance, improve infrastructure, and support sustainable economic growth.

Role Overview

The Climate Finance Accelerator does not issue capital directly. Instead, it provides support to enhance investment readiness and connects businesses with potential financiers. Specific job vacancies, employee roles, or open positions are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Key Details

  • Fund Mobilization: Over £94 million mobilized via the £108 million CFAP fund.
  • Regional Focus: Sindh, covering clean energy, water, waste, agriculture, and infrastructure.
  • Current Cohort: Features ten businesses across five sectors with financing requirements ranging from $2 million to $60 million.
  • Previous Performance: Supported 22 projects and helped mobilize $40 million between 2022 and 2024.
  • Partnership Framework: Supported under the UK-Pakistan Green Compact to build climate resilience and attract private investment.

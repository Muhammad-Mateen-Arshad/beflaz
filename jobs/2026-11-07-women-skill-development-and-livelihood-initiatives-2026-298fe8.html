Women Skill Development and Livelihood Initiatives 2026

Published on November 07, 2026

Discussions at the 29th Sustainable Development Conference highlighted vital Women Skill Development and Livelihood Initiatives designed to boost economic participation and provide technical training. These efforts aim to support female workers and micro-entrepreneurs by bridging the gap between learning, capital access, and employment opportunities. Role Overview

Industry leaders and policy experts emphasized that while women comprise nearly half of the national population, their labor force participation stands at roughly 24%. To improve these figures, speakers called for expanding empowerment projects beyond standard training. Sustainable progress requires connecting trainees directly to job placements, mentorship, market access, and targeted business support while addressing public safety and care responsibilities.

Key Details

  • Organizing Institution: The Sustainable Development Policy Institute (SDPI) hosted the conference session focusing on women's livelihoods.
  • Skill Initiatives: L'Oreal Pakistan has introduced community impact programs offering vocational beauty skills alongside financial and digital literacy training.
  • Financial Asset Support: The Khud Mukhtar programme provides young married couples with productive assets valued up to Rs150,000, including tools, livestock, or business equipment.
  • International Best Practices: A UNDP program in Vietnam demonstrated success by linking women micro-entrepreneurs across 100 cooperatives to digital tools and markets.

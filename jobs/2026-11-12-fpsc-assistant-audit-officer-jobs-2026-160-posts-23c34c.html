FPSC Assistant Audit Officer Jobs 2026: 160 Posts

Published on November 12, 2026

The Federal Public Service Commission has officially announced the FPSC Assistant Audit Officer Jobs 2026 to recruit 160 permanent staff members for the Office of the Auditor General of Pakistan. Qualified applicants across Pakistan who hold relevant higher education degrees or professional accounting qualifications are eligible to apply. The deadline to submit online applications for these BS-17 positions is November 23, 2026.

Role Overview

These permanent openings fall under Basic Pay Scale 17 (BS-17) within the Office of the Auditor General of Pakistan. Selected candidates may be posted at any location across the country. To obtain final confirmation in the role, appointees are required to pass either the SAS or PIPFA (Public Sector) qualification.

Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must meet the specific education, experience, and age guidelines set by the commission:

  • Academic Qualifications: A second-class or Grade C master's degree in Business Administration (Finance or Accounting), Commerce, Economics, Mathematics, or Statistics from an HEC-recognized university. Alternatively, candidates with a master's degree qualify if they possess a CA Foundation credential, ACCA Knowledge Level completion, or a one-year postgraduate ICMA certificate.
  • Bachelor's Degree Route: Applicants holding a bachelor's degree are eligible if they possess PIPFA (Public Sector) or SAS qualifications alongside a minimum of three years of relevant work experience.
  • Age Requirements: Candidates must be between 22 and 30 years old. A general upper age relaxation of five years is granted under standard recruitment rules.

Key Details

The 160 vacant seats are divided among provincial, regional, and merit quotas as follows:

  • Punjab: 80 positions
  • Sindh Rural: 18 positions
  • Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: 18 positions
  • Sindh Urban: 12 positions
  • Open Merit: 12 positions
  • Balochistan: 10 positions
  • Former FATA: 5 positions
  • Azad Jammu and Kashmir: 3 positions
  • Gilgit-Baltistan: 2 positions
  • Application Fee: Rs. 600
  • Salary: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Interested candidates must submit their application online through the official FPSC website. Applicants are required to provide their personal and educational details in the web form. The Rs. 600 application fee must be paid using the Payment Slip ID (PSID) generated by the system, payable through 1-Link channels including ATMs, mobile banking, Easypaisa, and JazzCash. The commission accepts online submissions only, so no hard copy documents need to be mailed.

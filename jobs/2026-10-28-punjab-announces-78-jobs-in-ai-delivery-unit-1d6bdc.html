Punjab Announces 78 Jobs in AI Delivery Unit

Published on October 28, 2026

The Punjab government has announced exciting career opportunities with 78 vacancies in its Artificial Intelligence Delivery Unit (AIDU). Functioning under the AI Wing of the Services and General Administration Department (S&GAD), this recruitment drive offers roles in artificial intelligence, data science, engineering, management, policy, and administrative services.

Role Overview

The job openings cover 40 distinct job categories. High-level technical and managerial positions include Chief Operating Officer, Data Scientist, AI/ML Engineer, Data Engineer, AI Infrastructure Architect, Policy Advisor, and management specialists. Opportunities are also available for support staff, including Drivers, Clerical Staff, and Office Attendants. All positions are located in Lahore.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Professional and Technical Roles: Candidates applying for most professional or technical positions must hold a relevant bachelor’s or master’s degree and possess suitable professional experience.
  • Support Staff Roles: Qualification requirements for select support staff positions include Middle, Matric, or Intermediate education levels.

Key Details

  • Total Positions: 78 vacancies (spread across 40 job categories)
  • Job Location: Lahore
  • Department: AI Wing, Services and General Administration Department (S&GAD)
  • Salary Package: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice (described generally as competitive)
  • Application Deadline: November 12, 2026

How to Apply

Interested individuals can review the specific job categories and eligibility details on the Office of AI careers website. Applicants seeking roles numbered 1 to 37 are required to apply directly through the Punjab Government Job Portal. Remaining positions must be applied for using the designated prescribed application procedure.

SBP Report Highlights Job Creation Trends in Pakistan

Published on November 17, 2026

The SBP Report Highlights Job Creation Trends in Pakistan as detailed in the State Bank of Pakistan's annual economic report for fiscal year 2025-26. This summary provides key insights for job seekers, economic analysts, and the expanding working-age population monitoring Pakistan's labor market outlook. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) highlighted that structural reforms are essential to drive sustainable job creation and foster investment. Featuring a special chapter named 'Structural Challenges to Job Creation in Pakistan', the central bank's report outlines how volatile growth patterns and slow structural transformation continue to impede broad-based employment generation.

Key Details

The report underscores several critical findings regarding the economy and employment environment:

  • Job Creation Barriers: Key obstacles to employment include high youth unemployment rates, low female labor force participation, early deindustrialisation, and a prominent informal job sector.
  • Future Work Drivers: Industry 4.0 shifts, climate resilience goals under the green transition, and geoeconomic fragmentation present new challenges for job growth.
  • Economic Growth Projections: Real GDP growth is forecasted to reach 3.5% to 4.5% in FY27, supported by improvements in major crop yields and services sector expansion.
  • Inflation and Reserve Targets: Average consumer price index inflation is expected to ease toward the 5% to 7% range during FY27, supported by improved foreign exchange cover for imports.
  • Strategic Solutions: The central bank recommends encouraging private sector investment through skills training, technology adoption, tax administration simplification, and closing infrastructure gaps.

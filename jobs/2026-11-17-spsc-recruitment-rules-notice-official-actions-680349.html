SPSC Recruitment Rules Notice: Official Actions

Published on November 17, 2026

This update regarding the SPSC Recruitment Rules Notice: Official Actions provides important information for job seekers following recent administrative decisions by the Sindh Public Service Commission. The notice serves candidates participating in provincial selection processes by highlighting measures taken to enforce merit and transparency. Application deadlines for open positions are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The Sindh Public Service Commission started an internal accountability drive led by Chairman Rizwan Ahmed, who assumed office in June. Following a detailed verification of public complaints and commission records conducted in accordance with relevant laws, the authority took disciplinary steps against officials who compromised the integrity of the recruitment process. The leadership reiterated that strict standards will apply across all ranks to maintain public trust.

Key Details

The scrutiny of records resulted in official disciplinary actions against four staff members in the recruitment section:

  • Deputy Director (Recruitment) Ghulam Ali Ghouri has been dismissed from service.
  • Assistant Director (Recruitment) Sohail Ahmed Patoli has been dismissed from service.
  • Superintendent Manzoor Ahmed Qureshi has been dismissed from service.
  • Assistant Fazal Mohammad Shah has had two annual increments withheld.

How to Apply

Applicants are advised to follow these directives regarding SPSC selection procedures:

  • Do not engage with the dismissed officials on any matter relating to the commission, as they no longer have any association with the SPSC.
  • Rely exclusively on official SPSC communication channels for recruitment information and updates.

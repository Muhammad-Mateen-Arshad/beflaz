World Economic Forum Davos 2027: AI and Future of Jobs

Published on November 10, 2026

The World Economic Forum Davos 2027: AI and Future of Jobs agenda will explore how emerging technology and global shifts transform employment. Scheduled to take place in Davos, Switzerland, from January 18 to 22, 2027, the meeting brings together leaders from governments, businesses, civil society, academia, and media. An application deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The upcoming annual meeting will be held under the theme "Deliberate Conversations on the Future." Discussions will address global challenges across five interconnected priority areas: people, technology, growth, planet, and geoeconomics. A major focus of the agenda includes examining how artificial intelligence rapidly alters job structures, workforce reskilling needs, and employer requirements.

Agenda Focus Areas

Key topics set to shape discussions at the 2027 event include:

  • Understanding the impact of AI on employment, worker reskilling, and job creation.
  • Evaluating technology governance to empower humanity and broaden economic growth.
  • Addressing rising energy and infrastructure demands driven by AI, industrial operations, and electrification.
  • Managing geopolitical competition, trade, and supply chain impacts as highlighted by McKinsey & Company.
  • Promoting climate and nature solutions while sustaining economic progress.

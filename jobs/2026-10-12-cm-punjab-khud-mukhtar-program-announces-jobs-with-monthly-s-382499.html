CM Punjab Khud-Mukhtar Program Announces Jobs With Monthly Salary of Rs. 220,000

Published on October 12, 2026

Role Overview

The Directorate General of Special Education, Punjab, has announced 17 trainer vacancies under the Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Khud-Mukhtar (E-Earn Trainings) Program. Selected candidates will provide instruction in key digital subjects, including Digital Literacy, Social Media, E-Commerce, and YouTube.

These temporary positions are distributed across eight districts in Punjab: Lahore, Multan, Bahawalpur, Sheikhupura, Dera Ghazi Khan, Rawalpindi, Sargodha, and Faisalabad. Among these locations, Lahore features the highest allocation with six available seats.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Educational Qualification: Candidates must hold a BS degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Business Administration, Marketing, Media Studies, Graphic Design, Commerce, or a closely related discipline from an HEC-recognized university.
  • Professional Experience: A minimum of four years of work experience is required in fields such as digital marketing, e-commerce operations, freelancing, social media management, content creation, online business management, graphic design, or video editing.
  • Platform Skills: Experience with platforms including Amazon, Daraz, Shopify, and Etsy, along with expertise in YouTube channel monetization, online product promotions, and Facebook or Instagram advertising, satisfies the experience requirements.
  • Age Limit: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Key Details

  • Total Positions: 17 trainer posts
  • Monthly Salary: Rs. 220,000 lump-sum
  • Application Deadline: November 1, 2026 (updated following a revision to the original September 26, 2026 notice)
  • Selection Procedure: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Eligible professionals must submit their applications electronically through the official Punjab Job Portal (jobs.punjab.gov.pk). Applicants are required to fill out the online form with complete details regarding their education and work history. Applications sent by post or courier will not be accepted.

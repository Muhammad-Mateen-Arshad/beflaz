Karnataka Industrial Policy 2025-30 Job Targets

Published on November 13, 2026

Under the state's new framework, the Karnataka Industrial Policy 2025-30 Job Targets aim to generate 20 lakh employment opportunities and secure Rs 7.5 lakh crore in investments by 2030. This initiative is structured for job seekers and industry investors across key growth sectors, aligning with preparations for the Global Investors Meet scheduled for April 20 to 22, 2027, in Bengaluru.

Role Overview

The state government is expanding employment opportunities across multiple technology and manufacturing sectors. Primary focus areas for economic development include:

  • Semiconductors and Artificial Intelligence
  • Aerospace and Defence
  • Clean Energy and Electric Mobility
  • Life Sciences
  • Textiles and Apparel

Key Details

The state government has outlined specific employment goals, investment targets, and industrial initiatives:

  • Overall Target: 20 lakh jobs and Rs 7.5 lakh crore in investments by 2030.
  • Aerospace and Defence Policy 2026–31: Targets 60,000 jobs and Rs 60,000 crore in investments.
  • Textile and Apparel Policy 2026–31: Aims to create 5 lakh jobs and attract Rs 20,000 crore in investments.
  • Global Investors Meet 2027: Scheduled to take place in Bengaluru from April 20 to 22.
  • Regional Focus: A dedicated investors' conclave will be organized in North Karnataka to highlight Kalyana Karnataka and Kittur Karnataka.
  • Active Projects: Operations have started for companies including DN Solutions, Krones, Nidec, and Foxconn, while projects by Lam Research, Applied Materials, Wipro Electronic Materials, Jindal Aluminium, and Mahindra Aerospace are currently progressing.

Karnataka Seeks Ninth Schedule Quota Protection for Jobs

Published on November 13, 2026

The state government has announced plans regarding Karnataka Seeks Ninth Schedule Quota Protection for Jobs and higher education institutions. This initiative aims to protect expanded reservation limits for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes across the state. Deadlines for specific job applications under these enhanced quotas are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

Karnataka Chief Minister DK Shivakumar confirmed that the administration will appeal to Prime Minister Narendra Modi to place the state's updated reservation quotas into the Constitution's Ninth Schedule. Placing enactments under this schedule provides protection from judicial review. The state government is actively seeking relief from the Supreme Court after an October 12 High Court verdict struck down the 2022 quota expansion introduced under former chief minister Basavaraj Bommai.

Key Details

  • SC Quota Revision: Provisions were made to raise Scheduled Castes reservation from 15 per cent to 17 per cent.
  • ST Quota Revision: Provisions were made to raise Scheduled Tribes reservation from 3 per cent to 7 per cent.
  • Total Reservation Percentage: The 6-percentage-point extension raises the overall state quota to 56 per cent, which crosses the 50 per cent cap set by the 1992 Indra Sawhney judgment.
  • Legal Delegation: Deputy Chief Minister G. Parameshwara, along with Ministers Satish Jarkiholi and K.H. Muniyappa, will manage the state's legal strategy and representation in court.
  • Central Funding Request: The state is also writing to the Centre to clear pending funds of Rs 1,937 crore for 2025-2026 under Centrally-sponsored schemes.

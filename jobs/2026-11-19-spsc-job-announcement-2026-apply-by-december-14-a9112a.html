SPSC Job Announcement 2026: Apply by December 14

Published on November 19, 2026

The Sindh Public Service Commission has released the SPSC Job Announcement 2026 for multiple government positions across various provincial departments under Consolidated Advertisement No. 07/2026. This recruitment drive is open to eligible candidates seeking administrative, technical, medical, engineering, and wildlife posts ranging from BPS-16 to BPS-19. Interested applicants must complete their submission before the closing deadline of December 14, 2026.

Role Overview

The hiring process includes 36 advertised job categories spread across three major government sectors:

  • Energy Department: Positions include Director (BPS-19), Incharge MIS (BPS-18), Programmer (BPS-17), Medical Inspector (BPS-17), Assistant Director Technical (BPS-17), Technical Officer (BPS-17), Prosecuting Inspector (BPS-16), Survey and Drawing Officer (BPS-16), Stenographer (BPS-16), and Assistant (BPS-16).
  • Forest and Wildlife Department: Vacancies cover Assistant Conservators, Assistant Conservator (Marine), Assistant Directors, Wildlife Veterinarian Officers, Wildlife Rangers, Library Officer, Museum Curator, Accountant, Assistant, Wildlife Digital Photographer, and Taxidermist.
  • Industries and Commerce Department: Four openings are available for the post of Boiler Inspector (BPS-16).

Eligibility Criteria

Educational requirements and physical standards differ depending on the targeted position:

  • Administrative Roles: Generally require a bachelor’s degree from a recognized institution. Relevant certificates or computer skills are required for specific posts.
  • Technical & Engineering Roles: Require qualifications in relevant disciplines such as computer science, geology, petroleum engineering, mechanical engineering, or mining engineering.
  • Medical Positions: Require relevant medical degrees along with valid professional registration.
  • Wildlife Positions: Accept qualifications in zoology, wildlife management, ecology, geography, veterinary science, or marine science.
  • Physical Standards: Applies to roles including Assistant Conservator, Assistant Director, Assistant Conservator (Marine), and Wildlife Ranger. Male applicants must be at least 5 feet 5 inches tall with a 33-inch chest (1.5-inch expansion) and run 1,000 meters within 10 minutes. Female applicants must be at least 5 feet 4 inches tall and complete a 600-meter walk within 16 minutes.
  • Age Limits: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Key Details

  • Number of Posts: 36 advertised job categories across BPS-16 to BPS-19.
  • Application Fee: Rs. 500 payable by generating a Payment Slip ID (PSID) via 1Bill. One fee payment covers all categories of a single advertised post.
  • Application Deadline: December 14, 2026.
  • Salary: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Candidates must visit the official SPSC web portal, register an account, and fill out their user profile completely. After logging in, navigate to the available jobs section, choose the desired position, and complete the online form with correct personal information, domicile details, and academic qualifications. Finally, pay the application fee using the generated PSID before submitting the application.

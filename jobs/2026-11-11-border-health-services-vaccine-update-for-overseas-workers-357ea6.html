Border Health Services Vaccine Update for Overseas Workers

Published on November 11, 2026

The Ministry of Health has released a Border Health Services Vaccine Update for Overseas Workers and Umrah pilgrims following an investigation into procurement procedures. Federal Health Minister Mustafa Kamal confirmed that an inquiry was launched after irregularities were identified in obtaining mandatory health doses. Submission deadlines for travelers are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The matter involves the procurement of meningococcal meningitis and influenza vaccines managed under a public-private partnership by Border Health Services-Pakistan (BHS). Officials from BHS formed unauthorized procurement and internal committees, bypassing the Ministry of Health and violating Public Procurement Regulatory Authority (PPRA) rules. The ministry confirmed that all recommendations from the inquiry committee have been implemented and a new procurement committee will be established.

Key Details

  • Target Group: Umrah pilgrims and citizens travelling abroad for overseas employment.
  • Required Doses: Meningococcal meningitis and influenza vaccines.
  • Financial Status: The total amount of Rs183 million collected from applicants remains deposited in a National Database and Registration Authority (Nadra) account. The government experienced no financial loss.
  • Cost Comparison: Earlier vaccination arrangements cost around Rs1,250 per dose, whereas the audited process was priced at approximately Rs4,500 per pilgrim.
  • Investigative Action: Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif formed a five-member committee headed by the Cabinet Division secretary. Following the investigation, the Federal Investigation Agency (FIA) arrested the officials involved.

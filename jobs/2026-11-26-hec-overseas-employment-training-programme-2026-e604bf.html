HEC Overseas Employment Training Programme 2026

Published on November 26, 2026

The Higher Education Commission and the Government of Pakistan have officially introduced the HEC Overseas Employment Training Programme by establishing Country of Destination Centres across the nation. Designed for Pakistani graduates seeking career prospects abroad, this initiative provides professional preparation and guidance for specific international job markets. Qualified candidates must submit their applications prior to the deadline of December 31, 2026.

Role Overview

Launched under the Prime Minister’s Initiative for Productive Overseas Employment, the scheme focuses on preparing graduates for international career opportunities in six target nations: Saudi Arabia, Qatar, South Korea, Italy, Spain, and Greece. A total of 19 universities across Pakistan act as designated training centres for these specialized country programmes. Additionally, foreign-language training will be provided by the National University of Modern Languages (NUML) in Islamabad. These centres offer comprehensive services, including technical training, language preparation, skills assessments, licensing information, guidance for professional certifications, and support with employment documentation.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Academic Qualification: Applicants must possess at least 16 years of education in a relevant discipline.
  • Eligible Fields: Training is open to graduates in engineering, construction, healthcare, architecture, hospitality, and management.

Key Details

  • Training Fees: The designated centres will charge a fee, with the applicable amount communicated to candidates during registration.
  • Application Deadline: December 31, 2026.
  • Salary Package: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Total Vacancies: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

To participate in the training scheme, applicants must register online through the official Prime Minister’s Youth Programme portal. During the application process, candidates should select a designated university according to their preferred destination country and academic background. Additional information and registration details are available on the official Prime Minister’s Youth Programme website.

Saima Saleem Takes New Diplomatic Role in Islamabad

Published on November 26, 2026

Pakistani diplomat Saima Saleem has returned to Islamabad to take up a new post after concluding her service with the United Nations. Her career path provides an inspiring example for candidates interested in public administration and international diplomacy through the Foreign Service of Pakistan. Role Overview

Saima Saleem is an experienced career diplomat who became the first visually impaired woman hired by the Pakistani government when she entered the Foreign Service of Pakistan in 2009. Throughout her diplomatic career, she has managed diverse portfolios and championed several resolutions on human rights and disability rights, including a key resolution on access to assistive technology adopted by the UN General Assembly on September 2, 2026. Her career includes key diplomatic postings such as second secretary on human rights at Pakistan’s permanent mission to the UN in Geneva and counselor for the Pakistan mission to the UN in New York City.

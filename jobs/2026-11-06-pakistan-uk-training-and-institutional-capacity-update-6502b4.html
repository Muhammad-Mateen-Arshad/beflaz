Role Overview

Pakistan and Britain have committed to expanding joint activities in counter-narcotics, counter-terrorism, immigration management, cybercrime prevention, NADRA operations, and coast guard efforts. A major component of this partnership involves specialized training programmes and the Upskill Project, which aims to build the capacity of Pakistani institutions. Furthermore, operational collaboration is set to expand between the UK's National Crime Agency and Pakistan's Anti-Narcotics Force.

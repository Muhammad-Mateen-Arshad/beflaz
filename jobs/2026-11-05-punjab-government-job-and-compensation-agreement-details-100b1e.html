Punjab Government Job and Compensation Agreement Details

Published on November 05, 2026

The Punjab Government Job and Compensation Agreement Details outline a recent settlement reached between state authorities and the Justice Committee following the death of anti-drug activist Parvinder Singh Jhota. Under this agreement, the state is providing government jobs and financial packages to specific affected families. Application deadlines for the general public are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The government has agreed to provide specific employment opportunities as part of relief measures:

  • A government job for the wife of late activist Parvinder Singh Jhota.
  • A government job at the Mansa Deputy Commissioner's office for the wife of late Daljit Singh (from Bhamme Kalan village).

Eligibility Criteria

  • Eligible Beneficiaries: The agreement strictly applies to designated family members, specifically the wife of Parvinder Singh Jhota and the wife of Daljit Singh.
  • Educational Qualifications: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Age Limits: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Key Details

  • Compensation Amounts: Rs 2 crore for the wife of Parvinder Singh Jhota, Rs 50 lakh for the wife of Daljit Singh, and Rs 20 lakh for the family of a youth who died during a protest in Ludhiana.
  • Job Location: Mansa Deputy Commissioner's office for Daljit Singh's wife; exact location for Jhota's wife's posting is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Memorial & Infrastructure: Installation of a statue of Jhota in Mansa and a trauma centre in Khiala village, Mansa district.
  • Legal and Administrative Actions: Withdrawal of cases against Jhota's father, Anti-Drug Task Force members, and individuals involved in flex banner incidents across Punjab, along with the release of Lakha Sidhana and Mohneet Sheron prior to the November 6 post-mortem.

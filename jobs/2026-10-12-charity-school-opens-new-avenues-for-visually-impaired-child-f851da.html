Charity school opens new avenues for visually impaired children

Published on October 12, 2026

The AKAB School for the Blind, situated on a campus in Mirpur, Azad Jammu and Kashmir (AJK), offers free specialized education, accommodation, computer training, and career support for visually impaired students. Established by Prof Mohammad Ilyas Ayub and Tariq Awan under the Azad Kashmir Association of the Blind, the institution prepares students for higher education and professional fields, including teaching positions secured through Public Service Commission examinations.

Role Overview

This report focuses on career and educational pathways for visually impaired individuals, highlighting both academic instruction and athletic opportunities. It also addresses the officially designated 1% employment quota reserved for persons with disabilities, aimed at helping qualified visually impaired graduates access formal employment.

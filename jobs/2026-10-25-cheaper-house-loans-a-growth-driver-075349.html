For individuals seeking financing under the housing schemes mentioned in the text, property and financial criteria include:

  • Apna Ghar Programme: Eligible properties include houses up to 10 marla (2,700 square feet) and flats up to 1,500 square feet.
  • Apni Chhat Apna Ghar (Punjab): Covers plot owners with up to 5 marla in urban areas or 10 marla in rural areas who require construction financing.
  • Income Assessment: State Bank regulations allow banks to assess informal-income earners using utility bills, digital receipts, and rent payment histories.

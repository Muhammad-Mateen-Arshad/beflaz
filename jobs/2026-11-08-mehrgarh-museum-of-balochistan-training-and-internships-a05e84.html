Mehrgarh Museum of Balochistan Training and Internships

Published on November 08, 2026

The Mehrgarh Museum of Balochistan Training and Internships program offers educational activities and professional development in heritage preservation in Quetta. This initiative is tailored for local heritage professionals, students, and researchers looking to engage in museum curation and collection management. As for the application deadline, it is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The project focuses on modernizing museum curation to meet international standards, conserving ancient Neolithic artefacts from the Mehrgarh civilisation, and broadening community outreach. Local heritage professionals receive specialized training to ensure the long-term protection and care of historical collections. Additionally, the facility serves as an active learning center through educational sessions and ongoing internships led by museum teaching staff.

World Bank Eyes $50M Pakistan Fund of Funds [2026]

Published on October 30, 2026

The World Bank has proposed a $50 million Fund of Funds initiative to support Pakistan’s emerging business ecosystem. Under the Pakistan Entrepreneurship and Private Investment Catalyst Project, the Government of Pakistan plans to create and capitalize this investment vehicle with World Bank support. The primary goals include expanding access to early-stage capital, strengthening the venture-capital market, and supporting business growth, productivity, and job creation nationwide.

Role Overview

Rather than providing direct grants to individual business owners, the program is structured as a Fund of Funds. The capital will be committed to professionally managed venture-capital funds, which will then invest in targeted companies. This initiative aims to leverage both public funding and private domestic and international investment to support innovative ventures across the country.

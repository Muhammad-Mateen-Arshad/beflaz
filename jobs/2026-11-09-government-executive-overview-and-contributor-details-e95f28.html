Role Overview

Government Executive focuses on reporting developments in the management and organization of the executive branch. Operating since 1969 as a print publication and digitally since 1996, the entity is owned by GovExec under Government Executive Media Group LLC. Its essential mission is to highlight management innovations, present solutions to complex agency challenges, and improve public awareness of the work performed by public servants.

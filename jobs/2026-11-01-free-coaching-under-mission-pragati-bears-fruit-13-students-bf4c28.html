Free coaching under Mission Pragati bears fruit; 13 students secure government jobs

Published on November 01, 2026

The Punjab Government's Mission Pragati initiative is offering free competitive examination coaching to help candidates secure clerical and other government positions. Based at the District Library in Bathinda, the program assists dedicated aspirants from rural and economically weaker backgrounds who cannot afford costly private coaching institutes.

Role Overview

Mission Pragati provides structured academic guidance tailored to various competitive recruitment tests. The initiative has achieved positive results, with 13 participants successfully obtaining government jobs so far. Supported by Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) funding and monitored by the District Red Cross Society, the initiative delivers quality teaching without placing a financial burden on the state government.

Eligibility Criteria

The initiative aims to support candidates from rural areas and financially weaker backgrounds. Specific educational qualifications or age limits are not specified in the source; please check the official notice. Applicants are admitted to the program through a dedicated selection process.

Key Details

  • Course Structure: A three-month integrated coaching program covering general studies, mathematics, English, Punjabi, and reasoning.
  • Physical Coaching: Free physical training is provided to candidates preparing for recruitments in the armed forces and other uniformed services.
  • Facility Amenities: The learning center offers seating for nearly 400 candidates, air-conditioning, Wi-Fi, computer access, updated books, and operates daily from 8:00 am to 10:00 pm. An on-site canteen is run by a self-help group.
  • Student Support: Deserving candidates from economically disadvantaged backgrounds receive tracksuits and shoes. Library membership is Rs 600 monthly, with a refundable deposit for books.

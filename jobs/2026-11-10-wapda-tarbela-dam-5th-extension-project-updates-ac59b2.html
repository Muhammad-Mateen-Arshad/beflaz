WAPDA Tarbela Dam 5th Extension Project Updates

Published on November 10, 2026

The Water and Power Development Authority has released official WAPDA Tarbela Dam 5th Extension Project Updates following a rock collapse at the T-5 construction site in Swabi. This announcement provides key information regarding safety assessments, site inspections, and ongoing restoration efforts for project personnel and the public. Application deadlines and open job positions are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

Following a rock mass collapse at the T-5 project site, an emergency was declared, construction work was paused, and workers were moved to safe areas with no reported fatalities. Wapda Chairman retired Lt Muhammad Saeed conducted site visits to inspect the intake structure, power house, tunnel, and other essential facilities. During briefings with contractors, consultants, and management, immediate response strategies were finalized to manage the situation and repair damaged sections.

Key Details

  • Location: Swabi and Haripur.
  • Current Safety Status: No threat to the main Tarbela Dam or its critical structures; the situation is fully under control.
  • Actions Taken: A departmental inquiry has been ordered, remedial works have begun, and an initial assessment report was submitted to Minister for Water Resources Mian Muhammad Mueen Watto.

Zardari, Shehbaz pledge development on GB Independence Day

Published on November 01, 2026

Role Overview

The federal government has announced plans to boost economic growth and create job opportunities in Gilgit-Baltistan. As part of these strategic development initiatives, the government approved the Maqpoon Das Special Economic Zone, which spans 250 acres. The planned zone is set to host 155 commercial and industrial enterprises and is anticipated to generate around 5,000 employment opportunities for the region. Additional educational and developmental projects, including the construction of Daanish Schools in Sultanabad, Bunji, Ghanche, and Skardu, are also underway to empower the younger population.

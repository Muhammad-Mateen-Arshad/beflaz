Are Indian, Chinese Troops In A Face-off In Arunachal Pradesh?

Published on October 29, 2026

Information regarding various Indian border guarding organization roles, including the Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP) and its Shaurya commando teams, has been highlighted in a recent security overview.

Role Overview

Under the "One Border, One Force" policy, specific security forces are assigned to oversee particular international boundaries alongside the Indian Army:

  • Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP): Tasked with securing the border along China. This includes Shaurya battalions that were established as specialized mountain troops to guard the Line of Actual Control.
  • Border Security Force (BSF): Assigned to manage the International Border shared with Pakistan and Bangladesh.
  • Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB): In charge of monitoring the borders with Nepal and Bhutan.
  • Assam Rifles: Deployed to secure the border region along Myanmar.

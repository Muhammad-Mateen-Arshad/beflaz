Sindh Domicile Verification and Government Job Scrutiny

Published on November 24, 2026

This report on Sindh Domicile Verification and Government Job Scrutiny addresses recent developments surrounding local recruitment and admission fairness across the province. It is intended for job seekers, students, and citizens following official hiring procedures in Sindh's public sector. Application deadlines and submission links are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

Efforts to link the provincial domicile system with the National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA) have been stalled for 16 years. First designed in 2010 under former provincial IT Minister Raza Haroon and IT Secretary Muhammad Hussain Syed, the initiative aimed to stop fake domiciles and protect employment and educational seats for genuine local residents. Although initial procedures and budgets were drafted with NADRA, the summary submitted to the chief minister was ultimately shelved.

Key Details

Recruitment processes in several provincial sectors are currently under discussion due to allegations of fake domiciles and Permanent Residence Certificates (PRCs):

  • Irregularities have been reported in medical college admissions and recruitment within health, education, and other departments.
  • Some candidates securing seats had completed their matriculation and intermediate studies in other provinces or cities despite holding local domiciles.
  • Sindh Assembly member Syed Ijazul Haq submitted a formal letter dated November 13, 2026, to the deputy commissioner regarding Grade 1 to 4 recruitment conducted during 2022–23.
  • The complaint highlights appointments in District West's School Education Department (primary and secondary wings) and Sindh Qatar Hospital, where eligible locals were reportedly bypassed.

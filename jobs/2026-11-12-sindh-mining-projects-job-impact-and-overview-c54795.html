Sindh Mining Projects Job Impact and Overview

Published on November 12, 2026

The Sindh Mining Projects Job Impact and Overview highlights the provincial government's strategy to generate local employment through responsible mineral extraction and scientific development. Under the direction of Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah, industrial expansion aims to enhance livelihoods and build workforce skills across energy and mining operations. Deadlines and recruitment schedules are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The provincial initiative focuses on sustainable mineral development, modern technology, and local value addition. Key projects include public-private partnerships like the Sindh Engro Coal Mining Company (SECMC) and private ventures such as Sino-Sindh Resources Limited (SSRL). Investment and operational focus also extend to non-coal resources, including granite, iron ore, marble, limestone, celestite, China clay, silica sand, and lake salt across regions such as Nagarparkar, Jamshoro, Thatta, Dadu, and Karachi.

Eligibility Criteria

Recruitment in these project zones heavily prioritizes local residents, with technical training offered to local youth to enhance regional employability. Exact academic qualifications, age limits, and selection requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Key Details

  • Block-I Job Creation: Generated about 5,000 jobs, with 97 percent of the workforce coming from Sindh, alongside over 20,000 direct local jobs overall during project development.
  • Block-II Job Creation: Provided employment to around 2,350 local people through SECMC operations, with 94 percent from Sindh and 60 percent from Thar.
  • Community Support: Includes technical training programs, health facilities (such as a 250-bed hospital in Islamkot), and clean drinking water through reverse osmosis plants.

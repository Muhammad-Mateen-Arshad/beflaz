World Bank $1.7 Billion Programme in Pakistan 2026

Published on November 10, 2026

The World Bank $1.7 Billion Programme in Pakistan 2026, officially structured as the Public Resource for Inclusive Development programme, focuses on expanding the national tax base and restructuring public expenditure. The initiative is designed to support institutional reforms across federal and provincial government operations. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The programme centers on three main reform pillars: raising tax revenues, enhancing public spending efficiency, and upgrading national statistical data systems. Major operational objectives include:

  • Setting up data infrastructure for agricultural income taxation and standardizing property tax records.
  • Strengthening General Sales Tax collection on services through digital integration.
  • Streamlining federal administration, reducing spending on transferred provincial functions, and establishing welfare-based electricity subsidies.
  • Expanding e-procurement systems to boost transparency and public resource tracking.
  • Establishing a National Data and Statistics Hub alongside a Governing Council for the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics.

Key Details

  • Total Package: Approximately $1.7 billion delivered across four overlapping implementation phases.
  • Phase Allocation: The initial $700 million phase covers a $600 million federal operation and $100 million for Sindh. Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa is set for a $200 million tranche, with subsequent phases designated for Balochistan and Punjab.
  • Disbursements and Dates: Following effectiveness on May 14, 2026, an advance disbursement of $164.5 million was provided on June 29, 2026.

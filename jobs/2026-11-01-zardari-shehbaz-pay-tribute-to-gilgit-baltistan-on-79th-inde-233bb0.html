Zardari, Shehbaz pay tribute to Gilgit-Baltistan on 79th Independence Day

Published on November 01, 2026

In recent updates shared during Gilgit-Baltistan's 79th Independence Day, the federal government announced significant developmental and economic initiatives designed to enhance regional infrastructure and job creation.

Role Overview

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and President Asif Ali Zardari outlined several state-led projects intended to empower the local workforce and youth. The central development for employment is the newly approved Maqpoon Das Special Economic Zone, which covers 250 acres. This commercial zone is set to host 155 industrial and business operations, creating approximately 5,000 prospective jobs.

Furthermore, educational facilities are expanding through the construction of four Daanish Schools located in Sultanabad, Bunji, Ghanche, and Skardu, backed by an investment of Rs12.46 billion to serve underprivileged students. Youth programs have also provided 4,245 laptops under the Prime Minister's Laptop Scheme, alongside renewable energy projects including the 16MW Naltar Hydropower Project and widespread solar panel distribution.

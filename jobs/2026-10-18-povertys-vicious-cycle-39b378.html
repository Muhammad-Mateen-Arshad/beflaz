Poverty’s vicious cycle

Published on October 18, 2026

Role Overview

This report highlights the employment context and labor market conditions in Pakistan based on recent official data. According to the Labour Force Survey 2024-2025, national unemployment stands at 7.1 percent, while underemployment is recorded at 1.6 percent. Underemployment is defined as working under 35 hours per week while actively seeking or available for extra work. Overall national poverty is estimated at 28.9 percent for 2024-2025, with rural poverty (36.2 percent) significantly higher than urban poverty (17.4 percent).

The source outlines critical labor market challenges, including inadequate pay, underused skills, and hiring practices where personal connections often outweigh applicant competence. Women face additional barriers to paid work due to unpaid care responsibilities, family norms, and commuting safety issues. To improve earnings and bargaining power for rural workers, recommended strategies focus on combining relevant skill training, basic capital support, and direct market access.

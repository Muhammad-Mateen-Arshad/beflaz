Sindh Transgender Scholarships and Job Quota Announced

Published on November 06, 2026

The Sindh government has announced new higher education scholarships and highlighted an existing job quota for transgender individuals across the province. This official initiative provides academic financial support to transgender students through the Sindh Education Endowment Fund. The deadline to apply is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

The announcement followed a collaborative meeting between Sindh Education Minister Syed Sardar Ali Shah and key representatives of the transgender community, including Shahzadi Rai (KMC City Councillor and Sindh focal person for the transgender community), Zahrish Khanzadi, and Miss Kami Chaudhry. The session focused on overcoming educational barriers, promoting equal access to academic programs, and strengthening social inclusion without discrimination.

Key Details

  • Scholarship Support: Higher education funding will be offered via the Sindh Education Endowment Fund.
  • Employment Quota: The provincial government's recruitment policy maintains a reserved job quota for transgender applicants.
  • Teaching Roles: Transgender individuals are eligible to join the teaching cadre once prerequisite standards are met.
  • Policy Development: A comprehensive transgender education policy will be finalized with direct input from community representatives.
  • Vacancies and Stipend Amounts: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Eligibility Criteria

Transgender students pursuing higher education in Sindh are eligible for endowment scholarships. Candidates interested in public teaching positions must obtain the required educational and professional qualifications. Specific age limits, degree subjects, or minimum grade requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

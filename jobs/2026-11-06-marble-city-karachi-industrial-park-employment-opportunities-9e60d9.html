Marble City Karachi Industrial Park Employment Opportunities

Published on November 06, 2026

The Sindh government has announced the Marble City Karachi Industrial Park Employment Opportunities initiative to expand industrial activities, attract new investments, and generate employment in Pakistan. This project focuses on processing marble and granite reserves using modern industrial methods to create export-quality products for global markets. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

Pakistan’s first Marble City is being established in Karachi under the direction of Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah and led by Special Assistant Qasim Naveed Qamar. The project aims to provide modern facilities for investors and create job opportunities to meet the demands of a growing population. Following its pre-launch ceremony, the formal inauguration will be conducted by Bilawal Bhutto Zardari and Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah. In addition to industrial marble processing, the initiative links with wider economic developments in Sindh, including agro-based industries, energy sector opportunities, and infrastructure projects such as Shahrah-e-Bhutto.

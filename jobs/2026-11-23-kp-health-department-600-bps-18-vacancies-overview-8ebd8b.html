KP Health Department 600 BPS-18 Vacancies Overview

Published on November 23, 2026

This KP Health Department 600 BPS-18 Vacancies Overview covers the recent request by the Provincial Doctors Association (PDA) urging the government to fill vacant specialist positions through the Public Service Commission. The proposal aims to allow qualified medical officers with postgraduate credentials to serve as district specialists in public hospitals. The application deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice once formal recruitments are announced.

Role Overview

The Provincial Doctors Association has formally requested the health department to fill 600 vacant BPS-18 positions across government facilities. Currently, over 1,200 doctors serving as Medical Officers in BPS-17 or Senior Medical Officers in BPS-18 hold fellowship qualifications from the College of Physicians and Surgeons Pakistan (CPSP). Many are posted in basic health units or civil dispensaries. Upgrading these doctors to district specialists aims to utilize their advanced skills and improve specialized care in local districts.

Key Details

  • Department: Health Department (Khyber Pakhtunkhwa)
  • Vacant Positions: 600 posts in BPS-18
  • Selection Agency: Public Service Commission
  • Workforce Scale: The health department has over 30,500 total employees, including 8,024 doctors and 900 specialists.
  • Application Deadline: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Salary / Remuneration: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Posting Locations: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Eligibility Criteria

Once the government officially advertises these positions, the following criteria apply based on the available details:

  • Qualifications: Medical Officers holding FCPS or other postgraduate CPSP fellowship qualifications.
  • Target Disciplines: Medicine, surgery, neurology, cardiology, gastroenterology, nephrology, endocrinology, haematology, oncology, child health, and intensive care medicine.
  • Eligible Candidates: Existing general cadre doctors currently working as BPS-17 MOs or BPS-18 SMOs.

How to Apply

Qualified medical officers will be able to apply after the government formally announces the BPS-18 vacancies through the Public Service Commission. Detailed submission guidelines, online application portals, and exact requirements are not specified in the source; please check the official notice upon public announcement.

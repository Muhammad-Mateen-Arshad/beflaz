Pakistan SME Sector Job Creation Initiatives 2026

Published on November 19, 2026

The Pakistan SME Sector Job Creation Initiatives 2026 focus on expanding economic opportunities and financial support for small and medium-sized enterprises. Under the leadership of Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, the government aims to strengthen business lending to foster employment growth and national exports. Specific application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb announced that the government is giving unprecedented priority to small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs). Recognizing the sector's potential to generate jobs, accelerate economic development, and lift exports, the Ministry of Finance is targeting an increase in SME financing from 9.9 percent to 13 percent of total lending. International meetings were also conducted to enhance access to financial services for local enterprises.

Key Details

  • Sector Focus: Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs)
  • Financing Target: Raising SME lending share from 9.9% to 13% of total commercial lending
  • Event Location: Sidelines of the Singapore FinTech Festival 2026
  • Key Participants: Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb, World Bank Group Vice-President for Prosperity Pablo Saavedra, and IFC Vice-President Alfonso Garcia Mora
  • Core Objectives: Boosting job creation, facilitating financial access, and driving economic growth

‘Spent 5 years cracking jokes’: Haryana CM Saini takes a jibe at Bhagwant Mann on his birthday

Published on October 17, 2026

Haryana Chief Minister Nayab Singh Saini has highlighted recent progress in public sector recruitments alongside major projections for private sector job creation resulting from international business agreements.

Role Overview

The state government announced significant employment updates, focusing on transparent recruitment mechanisms free from bribery or favorism. Additionally, recent foreign visits to Sweden and Singapore aim to build global technology and capability hubs in key Haryana cities, boosting employment opportunities across several sectors.

Key Details

  • Public Sector Employment: The government has recruited 46,500 young individuals over the last two years. Across the past 12 years, approximately two lakh individuals have received government jobs.
  • Sweden Investments: Investment agreements worth Rs 4,000 crore were finalized, which are anticipated to generate over 11,000 job opportunities in the state.
  • Singapore Investments: Agreements worth Rs 5,500 crore were reached, including Rs 3,000 crore for health and wellness, Rs 1,500 crore for water treatment and management, and Rs 1,000 crore for the education sector.
  • Development Locations: Panchkula, Karnal, Hisar, Sonipat, and Rohtak are planned to be developed into global capability centers and technology-driven business hubs with support from Singaporean companies.
  • Investor Summit: Industrialists have been invited to the Happening Haryana Global Investors Summit 2027, scheduled for April.

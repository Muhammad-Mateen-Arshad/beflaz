Supreme Court of Pakistan Jobs 2026: Apply by Dec 10

Published on November 27, 2026

The Supreme Court of Pakistan Jobs 2026 recruitment drive offers 32 employment opportunities across five distinct categories ranging from BPS-11 to BPS-16. This opening is available to eligible Pakistani candidates with educational qualifications ranging from Matriculation and Intermediate to university degrees. Interested applicants must complete their submission before the deadline on December 10, 2026, at 4:00 PM.

Role Overview

The recruitment is being managed through the Higher Education Commission’s Education Testing Council for both regular and contract-based positions. The advertised roles include Judicial Assistants, Protocol Assistants, Junior Protocol Assistants, Caretakers, and Lower Division Clerks. Specific salary amounts are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must fulfill the required qualifications and experience set for each role:

  • Judicial Assistant: Requires a law degree and proficiency in Microsoft Office.
  • Protocol Assistant: Requires a bachelor’s degree, strong communication skills, and proficiency in Microsoft Office.
  • Junior Protocol Assistant: Requires an Intermediate qualification along with good computer and communication skills.
  • Caretaker: Requires Matriculation, at least three years of relevant professional experience, and an age limit of up to 30 years.
  • Lower Division Clerk (LDC): Requires Matriculation, an age limit of up to 30 years, a typing speed of 40 words per minute, and either a one-year IT diploma or Microsoft Office proficiency. Candidates skilled in Urdu word processing are preferred.

Key Details

Positions are allocated across open-merit, regional, and reserved quotas. The 20 LDC vacancies include seats for Punjab and Islamabad, Sindh Rural, Sindh Urban, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and Gilgit-Baltistan, with quotas reserved for women, minorities, and persons with disabilities. The Judicial Assistant positions cover Punjab, Islamabad, Sindh Urban, Sindh Rural, and Azad Jammu and Kashmir. The Caretaker, Protocol Assistant, and Junior Protocol Assistant positions are offered on an open-merit basis.

How to Apply

Applicants must register or log in on the official Education Testing Council online portal to fill out and submit their application form before the closing time of 4:00 PM on December 10, 2026. Applications sent by post, email, courier, or delivered by hand will be rejected. Government employees are required to apply through proper departmental channels. Candidates invited for screening must bring their original CNIC or passport along with their test slip, and no travel or daily allowances will be provided for tests or interviews.

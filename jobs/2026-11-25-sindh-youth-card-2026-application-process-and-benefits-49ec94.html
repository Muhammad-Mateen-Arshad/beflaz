Sindh Youth Card 2026: Application Process and Benefits

Published on November 25, 2026

The Sindh Youth Card 2026 initiative is a digital platform designed to provide young residents with educational support, banking facilities, skills training, and employment opportunities. It is created for individuals living in Sindh between the ages of 15 and 29 to ensure equal access to development resources. The application deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

Introduced by the Sports and Youth Affairs Department alongside Sapphire Consulting Services and JS Bank’s Zindigi platform, the initiative was officially launched on November 23, 2026. The initial phase aims to enroll 100,000 young people, with plans to scale the program to reach up to one million beneficiaries across all 30 districts of Sindh.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicants must be between 15 and 29 years old.
  • Must reside in one of the 30 districts of Sindh.
  • Open to all backgrounds, with dedicated focus on young women, minority groups, and persons with disabilities.
  • Requires valid personal identification details and NADRA biometric verification.

Key Details

  • Banking Features: Beneficiaries receive a Zindigi bank account, digital wallet, and Visa debit card to manage payments, withdraw cash, and receive stipends.
  • Entrepreneurship Support: Eligible applicants can access interest-subsidized microloans ranging from Rs. 10,000 to Rs. 500,000, alongside support from the Sindh Enterprise Incubation Center.
  • Career & Education: Connects youth with scholarships, vocational training, professional certifications, paid internships, corporate job fairs, and a dedicated career portal.
  • Commercial Savings: Provides access to special discounts across more than 180 business partners and proposed public transport concessions.
  • Application Deadline: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

How to Apply

Registration must be completed through the program's official mobile software by following these steps:

  • Download the Sindh Youth Card mobile app from Google Play.
  • Select the relevant registration route based on your age group.
  • Input your required identification and contact details.
  • Complete the contactless NADRA biometric authentication process.
  • Follow the remaining on-screen instructions to activate your digital card.

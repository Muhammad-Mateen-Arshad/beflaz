Ministry of Science and Technology Jobs: 29 Vacancies

Published on November 26, 2026

The Ministry of Science and Technology Jobs offer 29 contract positions across 12 categories under a Public Sector Development Program initiative. These openings are available on an open-merit basis for Pakistani nationals, targeting graduates, IT professionals, media specialists, and middle-pass applicants. Candidates must complete their online submissions within 15 days of the advertisement's publication.

Role Overview

The Ministry of Science and Technology (MoST), Government of Pakistan, is recruiting staff on a contract basis for the project titled Establishment of Planning, Monitoring & Evaluation (PM&E) Cell in Ministry of Science & Technology. Positions range from pay scales PPS-1 to PPS-8. Advertised positions include:

  • Assistant Chiefs
  • Software Developers
  • Research Officers
  • Junior Programmers
  • Communication Officers
  • Content Writers
  • Graphic Designers
  • Social Media Operators
  • Photographers, Video Makers, and Editors
  • Drivers and other support staff

Eligibility Criteria

All candidates must possess degrees from HEC-recognized institutions, while foreign degree holders must present an HEC equivalence certificate. Specific requirements include:

  • Assistant Chief: At least 16 years of education in Project Management, Engineering, Applied Sciences, Business Administration, Economics, Political Science, or International Relations, plus 3 years of development planning experience.
  • Software Developer and Junior Programmer: 16 years of education in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or Electrical Engineering, with at least 1 year of relevant experience.
  • Research Officer: 16 years of education in a relevant field such as Project Management, Engineering, IT, Applied Sciences, Economics, Political Science, or International Relations.
  • Communication Officer and Content Writer: Master's degree in Journalism, Mass Communication, English, or related fields. Communication Officers require 5 years of experience, while Content Writers need 3 years.
  • Graphic Designer and Social Media Operator: Relevant bachelor's degree with 3 years of experience. Social Media Operators must also demonstrate proficiency in social media platforms, communication skills, and content management systems.
  • Photographer, Video Maker, and Editor: Bachelor's degree in any subject with 3 years of relevant experience. Video editing software knowledge is mandatory.
  • Drivers and Other Staff: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Key Details

  • Total Vacancies: 29 posts across 12 categories
  • Pay Scales: PPS-1 to PPS-8
  • Age Limit: Maximum age limit of 35 or 40 years depending on the post (includes 5 years general age relaxation)
  • Salary: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice
  • Job Location: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice
  • Application Deadline: Within 15 days of the advertisement's publication

How to Apply

Candidates can apply online through the ministry's official recruitment portal. Applicants must choose their desired position, review the eligibility terms, and complete the online application form. Postal or hard copy applications will not be accepted. Shortlisted candidates may be requested to present supporting documents at a later stage.

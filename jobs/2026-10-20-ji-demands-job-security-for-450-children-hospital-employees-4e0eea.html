JI demands job security for 450 Children Hospital employees

Published on October 20, 2026

Jamaat-i-Islami (JI) has called on the Sindh government to safeguard the employment of 450 healthcare and support staff who were recently dismissed from the Sindh Government Children Hospital located in North Karachi.

Role Overview

This situation impacts medical professionals, paramedical personnel, and administrative staff following a management transition. A delegation of affected employees recently met with party leadership at Idara Noor-e-Haq to report severe operational difficulties and job losses resulting from the facility's transfer to a new non-governmental organization.

Key Details

  • Location: Sindh Government Children Hospital, North Karachi.
  • Number of Posts Affected: 450 workers, comprising doctors, paramedical staff, and other hospital employees.
  • Salary & Financial Issues: Affected workers have experienced five months of unpaid wages and dues. Furthermore, staff received no pay increases throughout a 10-year period, despite the Sindh government providing annual grants between Rs450 million and Rs750 million to hospital management.
  • Administrative Timeline: Originally established in 2003, the facility was initially managed by JICA before being transferred in 2016 to the Poverty Eradication Initiative (PEI) under a 10-year contract. Upon the agreement's conclusion, handover to a new NGO resulted in termination notices issued on September 28, 2026, ending employment on September 30.
  • Government Response & Action: JI leadership assured full support for the workers. JI MPA Muhammad Farooq plans to raise the job protection issue in the Sindh Assembly, and the party intends to contact the provincial health department directly.

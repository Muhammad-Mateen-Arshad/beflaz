Pakistan Job Market Update: Population Emergency Declared

Published on November 20, 2026

This Pakistan Job Market Update: Population Emergency Declared covers the recent official announcement regarding the nation's accelerating demographic growth and expanding employment needs. The update provides crucial context for job seekers and young citizens, as 67% of the population is currently under 30 years old. Role Overview

Pakistan has officially declared its rapid population growth a national emergency. The current annual growth rate has reached 2.55%, exceeding the official target of 1.25% set for 2035. To absorb the growing workforce, the nation must create 2.6 million new employment opportunities every year. Additionally, student enrolment is projected to rise to 60 million by 2050, putting increased pressure on public services, schools, and job markets.

Key Details

The announcement highlights several critical workforce and demographic facts:

  • Annual Job Creation Requirement: 2.6 million new positions required annually.
  • Demographics: Population stands at 241.5 million, with 67% under 30 years of age.
  • Growth & Fertility Figures: Growth rate is 2.55%; fertility rate is 3.6 children per woman nationally and 5 in Balochistan.
  • Leadership: The National Population Council operates under the chairmanship of the Prime Minister.
  • Taxation Updates: Contraceptive taxes have been removed in the 2026-27 budget.
  • Funding Status: A 39% funding gap remains for the national action plan.

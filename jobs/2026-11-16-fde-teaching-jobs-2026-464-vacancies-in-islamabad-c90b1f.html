FDE Teaching Jobs 2026: 464 Vacancies in Islamabad

Published on November 16, 2026

The Federal Directorate of Education has announced FDE Teaching Jobs 2026: 464 Vacancies in Islamabad for qualified individuals seeking visiting faculty positions in government schools and colleges. This recruitment campaign aims to resolve teacher shortages across pre-primary, secondary, higher secondary, and undergraduate levels. Eligible candidates must complete their submissions by the deadline of November 30, 2026.

Role Overview

Out of the 464 total positions available, 379 seats are reserved for female candidates and 85 seats are set aside for male candidates. The positions are divided into Category A (pre-primary to higher secondary levels) and Category B (BS degree programs). Subjects under Category A include Early Childhood Education, Education, Psychology, English, Urdu, Computer Science, Biology, Mathematics, Islamiat, and Pakistan Studies.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Category A: Requires an HEC-recognized BS degree or equivalent 16-year education in the related subject.
  • Category B: Requires an MS, MPhil, or equivalent 18-year qualification in the related subject.
  • Experience: Applicants must have a minimum of one year of post-qualification teaching experience. Applicants with a PhD are exempt from this requirement.
  • Age Limit: Up to 65 years at the time of appointment.
  • Special Provision: Existing daily-wage teachers at FDE institutions are eligible to apply and will receive a one-time 5% mark concession during the interview phase.

Key Details

  • Pre-Primary to SSC Level: Rs. 1,000 per period, up to a monthly maximum of Rs. 100,000 (maximum 5 daily periods).
  • ISSC Level: Rs. 1,200 per period, up to a monthly maximum of Rs. 130,000.
  • BS Level: Rs. 1,400 per period for MS/MPhil holders and Rs. 2,000 per period for PhD/postdoctorate holders, with a monthly maximum of Rs. 130,000 (maximum 4 daily periods).

How to Apply

Applicants must register online using the official recruitment portal and specify whether they are applying for Category A or Category B. Hard copies of forms are not accepted. The selection process is merit-based, and shortlisted candidates will be informed about test schedules and interview dates via the official portal.

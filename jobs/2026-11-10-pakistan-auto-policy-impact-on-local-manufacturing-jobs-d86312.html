Role Overview

This report examines employment and manufacturing conditions within Pakistan's automotive parts sector following recent policy decisions. Representatives from the Pakistan Association of Automotive Parts and Accessories Manufacturers (PAAPAM) state that 1,200 domestic component manufacturing enterprises employ 300,000 direct workers and support 1.5 million indirect positions. Industry leaders indicate that policies encouraging vehicle and part imports threaten local assembly operations and domestic job creation.

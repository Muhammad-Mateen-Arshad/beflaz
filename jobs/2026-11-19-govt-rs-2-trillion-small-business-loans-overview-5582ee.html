Govt Rs 2 Trillion Small Business Loans Overview

Published on November 19, 2026

Here is an update regarding the Govt Rs 2 Trillion Small Business Loans Overview for entrepreneurs and commercial enterprises seeking expanded access to credit. The government plans to double small business financing from Rs. 1 trillion to Rs. 2 trillion by June 2028 to drive economic growth, create jobs, and diversify exports. This initiative is aimed at helping small and medium enterprises secure formal bank financing through modernized digital channels.

Initiative Overview

Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb outlined this strategy during meetings at the Singapore FinTech Festival 2026 with international leaders from the World Bank Group and the International Finance Corporation. The target involves increasing the small business share of private sector credit from 9.9 percent to 13 percent over two years. The Small and Medium Enterprises Development Authority will play an essential role in helping businesses access these formal lending facilities.

Key Details

  • Target Financing: Increase SME funding from Rs. 1 trillion to Rs. 2 trillion by June 2028.
  • Credit Share Growth: Raise private sector credit allocation from 9.9 percent to 13 percent within two years.
  • Digital Inclusion: Utilization of the Roshan Digital Account, five digital banks, and fintech platforms to connect farmers and agricultural businesses to formal credit.
  • Financial Infrastructure: Collaboration on digital invoicing, fast payment systems, supply chains, and data infrastructure.
  • Educational Support: Establishment of a Centre of Excellence at LUMS in partnership with the Global Finance & Technology Network.

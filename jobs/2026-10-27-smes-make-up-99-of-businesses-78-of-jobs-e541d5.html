SMEs make up 99% of businesses, 78% of jobs

Published on October 27, 2026

Small and medium enterprises (SMEs) play a massive role in Pakistan's economic landscape, accounting for approximately 99% of all business enterprises and providing nearly 78% of total employment. To better support this sector, the government is currently restructuring the Small and Medium Enterprises Development Authority (SMEDA) to boost economic opportunities, business growth, and job creation.

Role Overview

Under the vision of Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, SMEDA is being revitalised to become a more responsive, effective, and accessible institution. Speaking at the Prime Minister's Committee on the Reorganisation of SMEDA, Adviser to the Prime Minister on Industries and Production Haroon Akhtar Khan stated that the objective is to bring financial access, business advisory services, and government support directly to small entrepreneurs at the grassroots level. Special priority is being given to expanding outreach to women entrepreneurs and businesses operating in rural or underserved regions across the nation.

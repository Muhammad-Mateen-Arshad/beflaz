Punjab approves Rs8.7bn cancer hospital in DG Khan

Published on October 11, 2026

Welcome to the Beflaz job updates section. The Punjab Health Department is making key administrative updates regarding medical house jobs and postgraduate training programs, along with significant healthcare infrastructure developments across the province.

Role Overview

The Health Department of Punjab is introducing a transparent, merit-based, and uniform system for medical house jobs and postgraduate training placements tailored for foreign medical graduates. To implement this, a nine-member committee featuring vice-chancellors of medical universities and medical college principals across the province has been appointed to review selection procedures. In related provincial developments, the government approved the Kulsoom Nawaz Cancer Hospital in Dera Ghazi Khan with an estimated budget exceeding Rs8.7 billion (comprising over Rs3.12 billion in capital expenditure and over Rs5.58 billion in revenue) to offer specialized regional treatment by June 30, 2029. Health Secretary Azmat Mahmood also noted ongoing progress on the Nawaz Sharif Institute of Cancer Treatment and Research in Lahore.

Eligibility Criteria

The new system focuses on foreign medical graduates seeking house jobs and postgraduate medical training in Punjab. The committee is examining procedures for verifying foreign medical degrees, registration, professional licences, and overall candidate eligibility.

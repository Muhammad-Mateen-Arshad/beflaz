India-UK trade deal's success to be measured by exports, investments, not legal text: UK Business and Trade Secretary

Published on October 12, 2026

The India-UK Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA) is expected to drive investment, international business growth, and job creation across multiple industries in both countries. Speaking at the 16th India-UK Joint Economic and Trade Committee (JETCO) meeting in New Delhi, UK Business and Trade Secretary Jonathan Reynolds highlighted that the success of the agreement will be measured by practical outcomes, including commercial contracts signed and employment opportunities generated.

Role Overview

While the source focuses on trade policy and commercial partnerships rather than individual job openings, the agreement aims to support businesses of all sizes—including small and medium enterprises and first-time exporters. The partnership provides practical guidance on tariffs, customs, regulations, and procurement. The agreement is anticipated to foster employment opportunities across several primary sectors:

  • Advanced manufacturing
  • Clean energy
  • Life sciences
  • Technology
  • Services
  • Creative industries

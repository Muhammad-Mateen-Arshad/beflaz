Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Advisory Council Appointments Update

Published on November 11, 2026

This Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Advisory Council Appointments update covers the official formation of an eleven-member administrative body appointed to assist the Governor following the imposition of Governor's Rule. The newly announced council comprises experienced professionals chosen to head key provincial departments and manage governance affairs. Application deadlines are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

Under Articles 232 and 234 of the Constitution, Emergency and Governor's Rule have been imposed in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, transferring executive authority from the Chief Minister and provincial cabinet to the Governor. To assist in administering provincial matters, an eleven-member Advisory Council has been established. Furthermore, a dedicated Secretariat led by the Governor and Chief Secretary will directly manage twelve districts under federal oversight, with army deployment authorized under Article 245 to assist with law and order.

Key Details

The appointed members of the Advisory Council and their designated portfolios are as follows:

  • Home Department: Kifayatullah Khan
  • Finance Ministry: Tariq Mahmood Pasha
  • Law: Amir Javed
  • Education: Dr. Syed Amir Abdullah
  • Health: Lieutenant General (Retd) Nigar Johar Khan
  • Mines and Minerals: Dr. Sarfaraz Ali Shah
  • Industries and Commerce: Dr. Muhammad Irshad
  • Communication and Works: Asif Rafiq Afridi
  • Religious Affairs: Maulana Muhammad Tayyab Qureshi
  • Energy: Osman Saif Ullah Khan
  • Information: Samar Haroon Bilour

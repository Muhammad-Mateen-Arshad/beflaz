25,000 Federal Jobs for Balochistan Youth Announced

Published on November 21, 2026

A major recruitment drive offering 25,000 federal jobs for Balochistan youth announced by the federal government aims to curb unemployment across the region. Chief Minister Sarfraz Bugti confirmed the employment program during a provincial assembly session, explaining that recruitment will continue over time. The application deadline is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Role Overview

This initiative offers government employment opportunities to help local residents transition away from high-risk informal livelihoods, such as carrying Iranian fuel in Zambad vehicles. The announcement follows recent recruitment activities in federal organizations like WAPDA and QESCO. Leadership acknowledged for supporting the employment drive includes President Asif Ali Zardari, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, and Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir.

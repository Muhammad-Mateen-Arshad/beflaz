Auditor-General of Pakistan Announces Jobs

Published on October 26, 2026

The Department of the Auditor-General of Pakistan has announced a hiring drive offering 36 government vacancies for job seekers holding a Balochistan domicile. These positions are being filled under the 6 percent Balochistan quota across Basic Pay Scales ranging from BPS-01 to BPS-14.

Role Overview

The recruitment includes openings for professional and support staff. The department is seeking qualified individuals for Junior Auditor, Stenotypist, Staff Car Driver, and Naib Qasid roles. While general posts fall under the provincial quota, the driver and Naib Qasid openings are local-based positions stationed in Quetta.

Key Details

  • Total Positions: 36 vacancies
  • Pay Scale Range: BPS-01 to BPS-14
  • Salary: Not specified in the source; please check the official notice
  • Application Deadline: November 9, 2026
  • Submission Method: Online via the National Jobs Portal

Eligibility Criteria

Candidates with educational qualifications ranging from primary school to a bachelor's degree can apply depending on the post:

  • Junior Auditor (BPS-11, 31 vacancies): Requires a second-class or Grade C bachelor's degree in Commerce, Accounting, Banking and Finance, Business Administration, or Information Technology from an HEC-recognized institution. Applicants must be computer literate with a typing speed of at least 30 words per minute. Age limit: 18 to 30 years (includes 5 years general age relaxation).
  • Stenotypist (BPS-14, 1 vacancy): Requires Intermediate qualification, basic computer knowledge, a shorthand speed of 80 words per minute, and a typing speed of 40 words per minute. Age limit: 18 to 30 years (includes general age relaxation).
  • Staff Car Driver (BPS-04, 1 vacancy - Quetta): Requires primary school education, a valid driving licence, and understanding of traffic rules. Age limit: 18 to 35 years (includes 5 years general age relaxation).
  • Naib Qasid (BPS-01, 3 vacancies - Quetta): Requires primary school education. Age limit: 18 to 30 years (includes general age relaxation).

How to Apply

Interested candidates must submit their applications online by registering or logging in at the official National Jobs Portal website. Applicants should search for vacancies listed by the Auditor-General of Pakistan, choose the desired position, and fill out the online form completely. Please note that direct physical applications sent to the department office will not be accepted.

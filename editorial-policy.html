Editorial Policy

How We Create Content

Articles on Beflaz are produced with AI assistance, using information drawn only from the cited source for each article. We do not add facts, figures, or details that are not present in the original source material.

Source Attribution

Every article includes a link to its original source at the end of the article, so readers can verify details and read further.

Quality Review

Before publishing, each article passes through an automated quality check for accuracy indicators and completeness. Articles that do not meet this standard are not published.

Health Content

Our Health section provides general wellness information only. It is not medical advice, and is not a substitute for consultation with a qualified healthcare provider. We encourage readers to speak with their doctor before making health-related decisions.

Corrections

If you notice an error in any article, please contact us at contact@beflaz.com with the article link and a description of the issue. We will review and correct confirmed errors promptly.

Terrorist attacks drop by 37pc in October: PICSS

Published on November 01, 2026

What happened

According to statistics from the Pakistan Institute for Conflict and Security Studies (PICSS) reported by Dawn, Pakistan recorded a 37 percent decrease in terrorist attacks during October compared to September. The country saw 143 terrorist attacks in October, down from 226 incidents in the previous month. This reduction reversed two consecutive months of rising violent incidents, driven largely by notable security improvements in Balochistan and mainland Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, alongside a sharp drop in suicide bombings.

Key facts

  • Fatalities directly resulting from terrorist attacks dropped by 57 percent, falling from 260 deaths in September to 111 in October.
  • Injuries from terrorist incidents decreased by 71 percent, dropping from 242 to 69, which marked the lowest monthly injury count in the past year.
  • The overall human toll from both attacks and security operations included the deaths of 169 terrorists, 53 security forces personnel, 45 civilians, and five peace committee members.
  • Suicide attacks fell to a single vehicle-borne incident in October that caused no casualties, down from three attacks in September that killed 45 people and injured 138.
  • Nationwide counterterrorism operations by security forces killed 156 terrorists and led to the arrest of seven suspected terrorists during the month.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, while total casualties and violent attacks saw a marked decrease, kidnappings surged to their highest monthly total in the past 12 months. Nationwide abductions rose 22 percent to 88 individuals in October, with Balochistan accounting for 70 of those kidnapped. Regionally, terrorist attacks in Balochistan fell by 46 percent, while mainland Khyber Pakhtunkhwa saw a 31 percent drop and the former FATA region experienced a 12 percent decline. Punjab reported two attacks resulting in four deaths, while Sindh, Islamabad, Azad Jammu and Kashmir, and Gilgit-Baltistan recorded no terrorist attacks. In total, the first 10 months of 2026 saw 1,376 terrorist attacks resulting in 3,902 deaths. Specific operational details for all counterterrorism actions were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

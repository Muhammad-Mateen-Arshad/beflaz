Bilawal says KP governor's rule could end if opposition agrees to fight against terrorism

Published on October 18, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP) Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari announced that governor’s rule and the emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP) could be brought to an end if opposition parties agree to work with the federal government on a unified counterterrorism plan. Speaking to reporters at the Karsaz Monument in Karachi, Bilawal urged the opposition, led by the Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI), to enter talks and establish a joint strategy against militant groups. The central government recently introduced emergency measures and governor's rule in KP, citing the provincial leadership's deliberate withdrawal from anti-terrorism efforts. (The full text of the federal directive was not specified in the source; please check the official notice.)

Key facts

Based on the report by Dawn, key points stated by the PPP chairperson include:

  • Condition for lifting emergency: Governor's rule in KP can conclude if the opposition engages with federal authorities to agree on a joint anti-terrorism policy.
  • Support for federal intervention: Bilawal defended the decision to impose governor's rule, claiming KP Chief Minister Sohail Afridi was unwilling to defend the province against militants.
  • Criticism of previous policies: He blamed former prime minister Imran Khan's 2021 decisions for allowing militants from Afghanistan to resettle in Swat, Malakand, and former tribal areas, as well as freeing jailed terrorists.
  • Economic and political priorities: Bilawal highlighted that Pakistan is facing a severe economic crisis, calling for a new National Action Plan and inviting the opposition to join a National Assembly electoral reforms committee.
  • Position on new provinces: He noted that creating new provinces requires provincial assembly agreement and the signature of President Asif Ali Zardari.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, security forces, law enforcement, and citizens are currently facing widespread attacks stretching from Khyber to Khuzdar. Bilawal emphasized that overcoming the nation's severe economic crisis is directly linked to restoring security. By calling for political consensus, electoral reforms, and a revitalized National Action Plan, the PPP seeks to create a unified national front against ongoing security threats.

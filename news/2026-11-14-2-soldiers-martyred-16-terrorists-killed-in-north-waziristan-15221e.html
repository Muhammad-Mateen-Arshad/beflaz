2 soldiers martyred, 16 terrorists killed in North Waziristan operations: ISPR

Published on November 14, 2026

According to a report by Dawn citing the military's media wing, Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR), Pakistani security forces carried out intelligence-based operations in the North Waziristan district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. The engagements led to the deaths of 16 terrorists and the martyrdom of two soldiers.

What happened

Security personnel targeted multiple militant positions across Mir Ali, Datta Khel, Ali Khel, and adjacent areas in North Waziristan. During 12 hours of heavy firefights, 12 militants were killed in Ali Khel, while two were killed in Mir Ali and two in Datta Khel. ISPR reported that those killed in Ali Khel included two Afghan nationals connected to "Fitna al Khwarij," the government's term for the banned Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan (TTP).

Key facts

  • Personnel Losses and Recoveries: Havildar Asmat Ali, aged 43, and Naik Aftab Ali, aged 32, embraced martyrdom. Security forces also recovered weapons and ammunition from the deceased militants, who were reportedly involved in attacks on civilians.
  • Leadership Response: President Asif Ali Zardari praised the security forces and expressed condolences over the deaths of the soldiers, affirming that counterterrorism efforts under the Azm-i-Istehkam campaign will continue with full force.
  • Ongoing Efforts: ISPR indicated that sanitisation operations are continuing in the area to eliminate any remaining holed-up militants.

Why it matters

As reported by Dawn, the North Waziristan operations follow a previous statement from ISPR announcing that 74 militants were killed during recent operations across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan. Pakistan has seen an escalation in militant activity since the banned TTP ended its ceasefire with the government in November 2022. Assessments by the Pakistan Institute for Conflict and Security Studies noted continued volatility in the security environment between October 30 and November 5, 2026. Specific details regarding additional long-term measures are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

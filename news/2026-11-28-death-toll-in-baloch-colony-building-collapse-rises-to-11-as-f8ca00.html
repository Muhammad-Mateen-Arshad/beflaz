Death toll in Baloch Colony building collapse rises to 11 as rescuers recover three more bodies

Published on November 28, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, the death toll from the collapse of a six-storey under-construction building near Karachi’s Baloch Colony area has risen to 11 after rescue workers retrieved three more bodies from the rubble. The building, which was reportedly constructed illegally on a residential plot, collapsed on Wednesday, initially leaving three people dead and 14 injured. Further bodies were recovered over subsequent days, and at least eight people remain missing based on reports submitted by relatives at the site. Emergency personnel achieved a major breakthrough by rescuing a 28-year-old man alive after he spent 54 hours trapped beneath the debris.

Key facts

According to reports published by Dawn, official updates and actions following the incident include:

  • Rescue 1122 and search teams cleared rubble using heavy machinery and manual tools to locate trapped individuals.
  • Sindh Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah ordered the removal of Sindh Local Government Secretary Waseem Shamshad, East Deputy Commissioner Nasrullah Abbasi, and SSP East Zubair Sheikh over alleged negligence.
  • Sixteen officials from the Sindh Building Control Authority (SBCA) were suspended, and directives were issued for the arrest of the building's owner, Salman Tahir, and builder, Zeeshan.
  • A First Information Report (FIR) was registered at the Tipu Sultan police station on a complaint by a Sindh Local Government Department official.
  • As of November 27, a report submitted to the chief minister indicated that 516 unauthorized buildings across Karachi had been sealed as part of a citywide crackdown.
  • Fourteen illegal buildings in the KAEHS area were sealed and scheduled for demolition.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the deadly collapse has triggered administrative action, legal proceedings, and a major enforcement effort against unauthorized construction across Karachi. Inspection teams gathered material samples from the collapsed structure to evaluate safety standards and regulatory compliance, though the full technical cause of the failure was not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Petrol price reduced by Re1 per litre, diesel price kept unchanged

Published on November 27, 2026

What happened

According to an Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (Ogra) notification reported by Dawn, the government has lowered the retail price of petrol by Re1 per litre while leaving high-speed diesel (HSD) unchanged. With this revision, petrol will cost Rs312 per litre and HSD will cost Rs346 per litre until Monday, Nov 30.

Key facts

  • Following recent cuts, prices for petrol and HSD are below their July 18 rates of Rs316.15 and Rs354.35 per litre.
  • The government is levying Rs114 per litre in duties and taxes on petrol and Rs100 per litre on diesel.
  • Under a Nov 2 agreement lasting 30 days, oil refineries are contributing to the petroleum levy by taking on a cumulative impact of Rs15 billion, facilitating a Rs14.71 per litre price drop for petrol consumers.
  • Fuel prices previously peaked on April 3, with HSD rising to Rs520.35 per litre and petrol reaching Rs458.41 per litre following price escalation that began after hostilities broke out on Feb 28.
  • A relief scheme launched on Sept 17 offers fuel subsidies for motorcycles, rickshaws, and vehicles up to 800cc.
  • Active government austerity measures mandate that commercial markets close by 9pm and cut official vehicle fuel allowances by 50% for three months.
  • Since July 17, Ogra has been authorized by the prime minister and cabinet to revise fuel prices on a near-daily basis reflecting global market shifts, replacing the weekly schedule instituted in March.

Why it matters

Dawn notes that petrol and HSD drive the bulk of petroleum revenue, recording monthly consumption between 700,000 and 800,000 tonnes. Adjustments to petrol prices primarily impact lower-middle and middle-class households relying on private transport, two-wheelers, and small vehicles. Meanwhile, diesel price stability affects the wider public, as HSD is extensively used across heavy logistics, power generation, and industrial generators.

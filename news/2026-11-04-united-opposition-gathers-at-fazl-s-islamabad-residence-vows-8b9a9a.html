'United opposition' gathers at Fazl's Islamabad residence, vows stronger alliance

Published on November 04, 2026

Opposition leaders in Pakistan recently convened at the Islamabad residence of Jamiat Ulema-i-Islam-Fazl (JUI-F) chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman to strengthen their alliance and improve parliamentary coordination, according to Dawn.

What happened

During the gathering, leaders from JUI-F, Tehreek-i-Tahafuz-i-Ayin-i-Pakistan (TTAP), and Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI) discussed key national developments. Discussions focused on the imposition of emergency and governor’s rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP), constitutional supremacy, and preparations for a multi-party conference. The attending factions resolved to set up committees to reconcile internal differences and coordinate joint political actions.

Key facts

  • Prominent attendees included TTAP chief Mahmood Khan Achakzai, Senate Opposition Leader Allama Raja Nasir Abbas, PTI Secretary General Salman Akram Raja, and MNA Asad Qaiser.
  • Achakzai denounced character assassination efforts targeting Fazl and pledged support for building a strong parliamentary alliance.
  • Fazl highlighted grievances regarding resource allocation and voiced opposition to alleged federal efforts to assume authority over Gwadar, coastal zones, and Sindh's islands.
  • Opposition speakers claimed the federal government illegally imposed governor's rule in KP on Oct 11. Conversely, federal authorities maintained that security concerns and the provincial government's disengagement from counterterrorism compelled the intervention. Full details on the schedule for the proposed multi-party conference were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • PTI raised serious concerns over jailed party founder Imran Khan, citing severe blood pressure spikes reaching 192 and demanding his transfer to Shifa International Hospital. Government officials disputed allegations of inadequate care, maintaining that proper medical facilities, exercise tools, and a healthy diet are provided at Adiala jail.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the meeting underscores efforts by opposing political parties to solidify a joint parliamentary strategy against the central government. The combined opposition aims to address territorial and constitutional concerns while simultaneously escalating pressure over the detention conditions and health care of political leadership.

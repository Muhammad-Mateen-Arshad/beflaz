‘From terrorism to tourism’: Kundi presents vision for KP as South Air launches Peshawar-Lahore flight

Published on November 09, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi attended the inauguration of a new domestic airline service operated by South Air on Monday. The route connects Bacha Khan International Airport in Peshawar with Allama Iqbal International Airport in Lahore. The inaugural flight, carrying Governor Kundi, departed Peshawar at 1:56 pm. Upon landing in Lahore, the governor was received by Punjab Senior Minister Marriyum Aurangzeb, Provincial Minister Khawaja Salman Rafique, and MPA Ramesh Kumar. Speaking at the ceremony, Kundi outlined a provincial vision termed "T-to-T," aiming to move the region from terrorism to tourism and trade.

Key facts

  • The new flight reduces travel time between Peshawar and Lahore from a ten-hour road trip to a one-hour flight, according to Dawn.
  • The ceremony was attended by South Air Chief Executive Officer Nishat Fatima, the Ministry of Defence joint secretary, provincial MPAs, business leaders, and airport officials.
  • Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has been under emergency and governor's rule since October 11, following federal measures over counterterrorism concerns.
  • Governor Kundi expressed intentions to further expand domestic air connectivity to destinations such as Quetta and Chitral.
  • In July, South Air launched routes linking Karachi to Quetta via Turbat, as well as Karachi to Islamabad via Bahawalpur.
  • Kundi also recently met with Gilgit-Baltistan Chief Minister Amjad Hussain to discuss regional boundary disputes and improving air links between Gilgit and Islamabad.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the additional flight option is designed to support economic activity and increase tourism in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa amid ongoing security challenges. Specific details regarding daily flight schedules and ticket pricing were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

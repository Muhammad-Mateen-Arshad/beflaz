Major among 4 troops martyred, 24 terrorists killed during operations in KP and Balochistan: ISPR

Published on November 14, 2026

According to a report by Dawn, four Pakistani military personnel, including a major, lost their lives while 24 terrorists were killed during a series of intelligence-based operations in Balochistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa’s North Waziristan district. The military’s media wing, Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR), confirmed the casualties following targeted actions against militant groups in both regions.

What happened

Security forces initiated area domination and patrolling operations around Kolpur, Kardagap, Nushki, and along the Quetta-Taftan National Highway (N-40) starting November 13. During an engagement in Kolpur, troops killed eight militants, including a suicide bomber. The following day, an armed drone attack targeted a military patrol, resulting in the deaths of Major Omer Minhas Nasir and Havildar Muhammad Adnan Khan.

In separate engagements in North Waziristan, military operations in Mir Ali, Datta Khel, and Ali Khel led to the deaths of 16 militants, among whom were two Afghan nationals. During these fire exchanges, Havildar Asmat Ali and Naik Aftab Ali were killed. Security forces also recovered weapons and ammunition from the sites.

Key facts

  • Four military personnel were martyred: Major Omer Minhas Nasir, Havildar Muhammad Adnan Khan, Havildar Asmat Ali, and Naik Aftab Ali.
  • A total of 24 militants were killed, including eight in Balochistan and 16 in North Waziristan.
  • Operations in Balochistan targeted militants designated by the state as Fitna al-Hindustan, while operations in North Waziristan targeted Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan members, designated as Fitna al-Khwarij.
  • Militants utilized technical equipment, including armed quadcopters and improvised explosive devices, during the attacks.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, ISPR accused foreign intelligence agencies of providing financial support and advanced technology to militant networks operating in the country. The military affirmed that counterterrorism efforts under the national 'Azm-i-Istehkam' framework will continue until foreign-sponsored terrorism is completely eliminated. State officials, including President Asif Ali Zardari, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar, and Balochistan Chief Minister Sarfraz Bugti, issued statements commending the armed forces and reiterating the government's determination to maintain regional security.

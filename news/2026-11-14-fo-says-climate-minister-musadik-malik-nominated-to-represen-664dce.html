FO says Climate Minister Musadik Malik nominated to represent Pakistan at SCO moot in New Delhi

Published on November 14, 2026

What happened

According to a report by Dawn, Pakistan's Foreign Office announced that Climate Change Minister Musadik Malik has been nominated to represent the country at the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) Civilisational Dialogue in New Delhi, scheduled from November 20 to 22. The Foreign Office stated that the meeting will gather delegates from SCO member nations to foster cultural understanding and civilisational dialogue in a spirit of mutual respect. Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar confirmed Pakistan's participation, noting that the country is set to chair the SCO Council of Heads of State for the 2026–27 term. Further specific venue details for the event were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Key facts

  • Indian news outlet ANI reported that 10 SCO member states, including Pakistan, were invited to the inaugural dialogue, which was proposed by New Delhi.
  • Indian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Randhir Jaiswal confirmed that India extended invitations to multiple SCO members and expressed anticipation for hosting the event.
  • In October 2024, Islamabad hosted the SCO Council of Heads of Government meeting, which was attended by Indian External Affairs Minister Dr. S. Jaishankar.
  • India recently sent its SCO National Coordinator, Alok Dimri, to Islamabad to prepare for the August 2027 SCO Council of Heads of State summit, which Pakistan will host.

Why it matters

As reported by Dawn, Pakistan has steadily expanded its involvement in the regional organization since transitioning from observer status to full membership in 2017. Beyond participating in regional dialogues, Islamabad holds permanent chairmanship of an SCO working group dedicated to poverty alleviation. Additionally, Pakistan assumed the chair of the SCO Regional Anti-Terrorist Structure in September 2025. The Finance Ministry also stated that Pakistan took over the chairmanship of the SCO Interbank Consortium for 2026–27, with HBL Chairman Sultan Ali Allana assuming leadership, reflecting the nation's ongoing role in advancing economic cooperation, trade, and financial connectivity within the region.

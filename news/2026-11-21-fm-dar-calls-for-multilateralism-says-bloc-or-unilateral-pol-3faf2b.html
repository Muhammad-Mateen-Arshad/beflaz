FM Dar calls for multilateralism, says bloc or unilateral politics will not work

Published on November 21, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, Pakistan’s Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar advocated for multilateralism, dialogue, and diplomacy during an interview with Euronews. Dar stated that unilateral actions and bloc politics will not work, reiterating that Pakistan maintains an independent foreign policy. During the interview, he outlined Pakistan's recent mediation between the United States and Iran, while addressing regional security issues and economic relations with neighboring countries. Specific details regarding Pakistan's GSP+ status were Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Key facts

  • US-Iran Mediation: Following an attack by Israel on Iran on February 28, Dar urged Iranian officials to exercise restraint. Pakistan later mediated direct talks between the US and Iran in Islamabad on April 11–12, leading to the signing of the Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding on June 18, 2026, to end Middle East conflicts and reopen the Strait of Hormuz. Despite violations, Pakistan continues urging both sides to adhere to the accord.
  • Economic Status: Dar stated that Pakistan holds $130 billion in external debt, contrasted against an estimated $6 trillion to $10 trillion in untapped mineral and rare earth resources.
  • Afghanistan Relations: While expressing a desire for a stable Afghanistan, Dar said Pakistan shared evidence with international bodies showing recent October terrorist attacks originated from Afghanistan. He noted Pakistan has lost 100,000 lives and Rs150 billion to terrorism.
  • India and Water Security: Dar opposed India's August 5, 2019 constitutional changes regarding Jammu and Kashmir, as well as India's rejection of an independent probe into the Pahalgam attack that preceded the May 2025 conflict. He also criticized India for placing the Indus Waters Treaty in abeyance, cautioning that weaponising water affects 260 million people.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, Dar emphasized that uninterrupted maritime movement is essential to prevent global energy supply chain disruptions and rising consumer prices, noting that Pakistan has implemented fuel subsidies for low-income populations. He called for establishing an agreed regional security framework and underlined that international treaties must be respected regardless of a nation's size.

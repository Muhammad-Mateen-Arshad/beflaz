PM Shehbaz, CDF Munir share top spot in list of world’s 500 most influential Muslims

Published on November 15, 2026

What happened

According to a report by Dawn, Pakistani Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and Chief of Defence Forces Field Marshal Asim Munir have shared the top ranking in the latest edition of the world's 500 most influential Muslims. They share the top spot alongside the Turkish president as well as the king and crown prince of Saudi Arabia. The annual rankings are evaluated by the Royal Islamic Strategic Studies Centre, an Amman, Jordan-based research institute that has released "The 500 Most Influential Muslims" report since 2009 to recognise significant contributions to Muslim communities.

Why it matters

Dawn reported that this marks the first time the annual list has assigned multiple names to the top rank across three shared entries. This ranking decision was driven by the Makkah Joint Defence Agreement between Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, and Turkiye. According to the report, the pact establishes a collective mutual-defence alliance that combines Saudi financial wealth, Turkish technological and manufacturing capabilities, and Pakistani military strength under a unified security approach.

Key facts

  • Top Ranks: Iranian Supreme Leader Ayatollah Mojtaba Khamenei ranked fourth, Jordan's King Abdullah II placed fifth, and Qatar's Emir Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al-Thani ranked sixth.
  • Prominent Figures: Pakistani scholar Sheikh Muhammad Taqi Usmani was listed seventh, while UAE President Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al-Nahyan ranked 12th, Egyptian President Abdel Fattah Saeed El-Sisi ranked 22nd, scholar Maulana Tariq Jamil ranked 32nd, and footballer Mohamed Salah placed 39th.
  • Additional Positions: Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas ranked 45th, Syrian Interim President Ahmed Al-Sharaa ranked 46th, and New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani placed 49th.
  • Honourable Mentions (Politics): Included Hamas Chief Khalil Al-Hayya, former prime minister Imran Khan, London Mayor Sadiq Khan, and American Senator-elect Abdul El-Sayed.
  • Honourable Mentions (Other Categories): Included Jordan’s Queen Rania Al-Abdullah, British composer Sami Yusuf, Palestinian journalist Bisan Owda, broadcaster Mehdi Hasan, and Egyptian comedian Bassem Youssef.

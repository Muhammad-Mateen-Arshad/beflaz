SBCA chief removed as CM Murad orders sweeping action over building collapse in Karachi's Baloch Colony

Published on November 26, 2026

What happened

Sindh Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah has ordered top-level dismissals and strict disciplinary measures following the collapse of an under-construction multi-storey building in Karachi's Baloch Colony, according to Dawn. The incident occurred on Wednesday during borehole digging work at the site, where more than 20 workers were present. A rescue search operation remains active as teams attempt to locate workers believed to be trapped beneath the rubble.

Key facts

According to reports from Dawn, major developments and findings regarding the disaster include:

  • At least three people lost their lives, 14 sustained injuries, and 17 missing individuals have been reported by their families to the local information desk. Information on financial compensation for affected families is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Sindh Local Government Secretary and Sindh Building Control Authority (SBCA) Director General Waseem Shamshad was removed from both positions, transferred, and recommended for federal suspension and inquiry. Bilal Memon has been appointed as the new local government secretary.
  • East Deputy Commissioner Nasrullah Abbasi and East Senior Superintendent of Police Zubair Sheikh were removed from their roles due to alleged negligence.
  • Sixteen SBCA officials were suspended, with the chief minister directing legal cases to be filed against them to enable their termination.
  • Police were instructed to immediately arrest the building's owner, Salman Tahir, and builder, Zeeshan, while the architect and structural engineer are being included in the investigation. Three individuals, including a contractor, were previously detained.
  • Initial inquiries showed the building was approved for a ground-plus-one structure, but six storeys were constructed after the owner obtained a court stay order against SBCA enforcement.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah announced plans for an organizational reset of the SBCA, stressing that public safety must be the primary responsibility of provincial agencies. The provincial government has also halted construction on 123 buildings currently being erected under stay orders across the city, requesting that the judiciary resolve these specific court cases within 15 days.

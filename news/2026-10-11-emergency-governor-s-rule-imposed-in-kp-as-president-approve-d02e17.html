Emergency, governor's rule imposed in KP as president approves cabinet decision

Published on October 11, 2026

What happened

According to a report by Dawn, the federal government has imposed an emergency and governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP). The action was taken following approval from President Zardari based on recommendations from the federal cabinet. The decision was finalized during an emergency cabinet meeting led by Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif to assess the ongoing security situation in the province.

Key facts

Dawn reported several key details regarding the enforcement and political reactions:

  • The emergency and governor's rule were invoked under Articles 232 and 234 of the Constitution. Article 233, which governs the suspension of basic fundamental rights, was omitted from the summary approved by the president.
  • The federal government issued an official notification naming Dr. Muhammad Fakhr Alam Irfan as the new chief secretary of KP.
  • KP Information Minister Shafi Jan stated that Chief Minister Sohail Afridi was taken into custody while participating in a PTI march. Official confirmation from law enforcement was Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • PTI leader Gohar criticized the federal decision, describing it as undemocratic and arguing that peace cannot be achieved by weakening provincial autonomy. In contrast, KP Governor Faisal Karim Kundi urged unity to start a new phase of growth in the province.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the federal cabinet advised executive intervention due to what it identified as the provincial leadership's deliberate step back from anti-terrorism operations. Friction between the PML-N-led central administration and the PTI-led KP government has grown over counterterrorism strategies, military actions, and diplomatic policies regarding the Afghan Taliban, which previously led to a collapse in bilateral talks.

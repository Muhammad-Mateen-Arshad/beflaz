Authorities impose Section 144 in Karachi, ban gatherings of more than 5 people till Oct 20

Published on October 11, 2026

What happened

According to a report by Dawn, local authorities in Karachi have enforced Section 144 across the metropolis to restrict public gatherings until October 20. The directive was officially issued by Karachi Commissioner Syed Hassan Naqvi following reports of widespread protests, sit-ins, and rallies occurring on major thoroughfares, intersections, and roundabouts.

Key facts

Dawn outlined several key elements of the administrative order:

  • Legal scope: Section 144 is a provision in the Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC) that grants local officials the legal power to ban open displays of weapons as well as public events such as rallies, sit-ins, and processions.
  • Prohibited actions: Holding, organizing, convening, facilitating, or participating in demonstrations, sit-ins, rallies, processions, or gatherings involving more than five persons is strictly forbidden.
  • Timeframe and location: The restriction applies within the full territorial limits of the Karachi Division from Sunday, October 11, through October 20.
  • Legal penalties: Specific penalties for individuals who violate the ban are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, recent demonstrations led to severe traffic congestion and created significant security concerns throughout the city. Official statements noted that road blockages interfered with daily public routines, delayed emergency services, and restricted access to healthcare facilities and hospitals.

The order highlighted a reasonable apprehension that continued assemblies could disrupt regular civic and commercial activities, cause further traffic delays, and result in a deterioration of the law and order situation. Authorities stated that preventive restrictions were necessary to maintain peace, ensure public safety, protect lives and property, and guarantee the uninterrupted movement of traffic and essential services.

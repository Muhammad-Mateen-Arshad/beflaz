On North Waziristan visit, CDF Munir vows to eliminate terrorism, preserve peace in KP

Published on November 20, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, Chief of Defence Forces and Chief of the Army Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir visited Miranshah in North Waziristan to evaluate local security conditions and intelligence-driven operations. In statements issued by the military media department, the army chief reaffirmed a commitment to eliminate terrorism and maintain stability in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa alongside regional law enforcement agencies. He commended the provincial police force for its sacrifices against militants, referencing the state term Fitna-al-Khawarij for Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan.

Upon his arrival, the Peshawar Corps commander received the army chief, who then laid a floral wreath at Yadgar-i-Shuhada to honor fallen troops. The visit occurred shortly after a separate operational review in Turbat, Balochistan. The exact dates of these regional trips were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Key facts

  • Recent military operations in North Waziristan and Balochistan resulted in the deaths of 24 terrorists and four security members, including a major.
  • The military reported that 74 additional terrorists were killed during counterterrorism actions in both provinces during the previous week.
  • Data from the Pakistan Institute for Conflict and Security Studies indicated nationwide attacks dropped by 37 percent in October compared to September, with fatalities falling by 57 percent.
  • Militant incidents in Balochistan fell from 132 in September to 71 in October, with fatalities decreasing from 136 to 44.
  • Mainland Khyber Pakhtunkhwa recorded 40 attacks in October compared to 58 in September, with casualties dropping from 90 to 38 deaths.
  • In KP's merged districts, October attacks fell from 34 to 30 and deaths decreased from 33 to 25, while injuries slightly increased from 11 to 13.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan continue to be the primary focus of security operations due to ongoing militant activity. In October, the central government enacted emergency procedures and governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, citing the local administration's failure to engage in counterterrorism efforts. Specific legal documents detailing this political action were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

PTI founder Imran Khan brought to Pims, undergoes eye procedure

Published on November 11, 2026

Former Pakistani Prime Minister and Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI) founder Imran Khan was taken from Adiala jail in Rawalpindi to the Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences (Pims) in Islamabad overnight between November 10 and 11, according to Dawn.

What happened

According to a hospital official cited by Dawn, the former leader underwent an ocular assessment and physical examination prior to receiving eye care on the advice of his treating ophthalmologist. His vital parameters were normal, and his physical exam was unremarkable. Following standard clinical protocols and informed consent, doctors administered an intravitreal anti-VEGF injection. The official stated that Khan remained clinically stable throughout the procedure and was discharged at around 2:30 a.m. with follow-up instructions.

Key facts

  • Eye condition: Khan's diagnosis of right central retinal vein occlusion was made public in late January, leading to multiple medical visits, including a previous trip to Pims in June.
  • Detention history: Imprisoned since August 2023, Khan has experienced declining health during his sentence, drawing concern from PTI members.
  • Court directions: On August 18, the Supreme Court ordered the government to move Khan to Shifa International Hospital after reviewing a jail report regarding his medical state.
  • Medical evaluations: Examinations in early August noted fluctuating blood pressure, headaches, and restlessness, which Khan linked to stress from restricted visits and lack of media access. A Pims medical board recommended daily walks, relaxation, TV, newspapers, books, and increased contact with family.
  • Government action: Instead of Shifa International, authorities took Khan to Pims before returning him to prison. Information Minister Attaullah Tarar blamed security concerns caused by PTI supporters, a claim the PTI denied while accusing the government of ignoring court directives.

Why it matters

The hospital visit highlights ongoing scrutiny over the former prime minister's medical care and confinement conditions. While prison officials maintained that adequate healthcare is provided to Khan and his wife, previous medical evaluations noted that limited family contact and isolation contributed to his physical symptoms, making his treatment a central point of dispute between the government and opposition.

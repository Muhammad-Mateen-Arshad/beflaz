Singapore foreign minister to undertake official visit to Pakistan on Oct 30

Published on October 29, 2026

Singapore’s Minister for Foreign Affairs, Dr Vivian Balakrishnan, will undertake an official visit to Islamabad on Friday, Oct 30, according to a report by Dawn citing Pakistan's Foreign Office. The trip takes place following an invitation from Pakistan’s Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar.

What happened

During the visit, Dr Balakrishnan and Ishaq Dar will hold comprehensive bilateral discussions spanning the full range of relations between the two countries. They are also set to discuss regional and international matters of mutual concern. Additionally, the Singaporean foreign minister will meet with Pakistan’s leadership. The specific meeting schedule and full list of officials are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Key facts

According to the statement from the Foreign Office reported by Dawn, several key elements mark this official engagement:

  • A memorandum of understanding focusing on disaster management cooperation will be exchanged between Pakistan’s National Disaster Management Authority and Singapore’s Civil Defence Force.
  • The two nations maintain friendly relations based on mutual respect and long-standing partnership.
  • The visit highlights a joint commitment from both countries to broaden and strengthen their bilateral cooperation.
  • Earlier in May, Dar contacted Balakrishnan to request Singapore's assistance with the welfare and repatriation of 11 Pakistani and 20 Iranian citizens who were aboard vessels seized by the United States.

Why it matters

The diplomatic event carries added significance as it coincides with the 60th anniversary of the establishment of formal diplomatic relations between Pakistan and Singapore. Furthermore, the Foreign Office noted that the visit occurs ahead of Singapore assuming the chairmanship of ASEAN in 2027.

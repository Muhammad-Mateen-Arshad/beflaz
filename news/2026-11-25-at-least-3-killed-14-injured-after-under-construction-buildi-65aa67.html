At least 3 killed, 14 injured after under-construction building collapses near Karachi's Baloch Colony

Published on November 25, 2026

What happened

At least three people died and 14 others were injured after an under-construction building collapsed in the Karachi Administration Housing Society near Baloch Colony, according to reporting by Dawn. The ground-plus-five-storey building, constructed on a 600-square-yard plot and including a penthouse, collapsed while more than 20 workers were on site. Rescue 1122 and emergency teams responded with modern machinery and roughly 12 vehicles to extract victims from the debris.

Key facts

  • Hospital officials at Jinnah Postgraduate Medical Centre confirmed three fatalities and treated 14 injured individuals, discharging most after first aid while admitting one in stable condition.
  • East Deputy Commissioner Nasrullah Abbasi stated that the Sindh Building Control Authority (SBCA) had authorized only a ground-plus-one structure, but the owner bypassed approved blueprints.
  • When the SBCA attempted to stop construction, the building owner obtained a court stay order, preventing further regulatory action prior to the collapse.
  • East Senior Superintendent of Police Zubair Nazir Shaikh confirmed the arrest of three suspects, including a building contractor and a relative of the owner.
  • The specific cause of the building collapse has not yet been determined. Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

Karachi Mayor Murtaza Wahab stated that the SBCA intended to demolish the unauthorized building, but legal stay orders and political influence blocked administrative enforcement. Sindh Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah instructed the SBCA to inspect construction standards, evaluate potential negligence, and review building plans. He also requested safety assessments of surrounding structures and a detailed report from the Karachi commissioner. Additionally, Sindh Inspector General of Police Javed Alam Odho directed police forces to manage traffic, secure the site, and assist emergency teams.

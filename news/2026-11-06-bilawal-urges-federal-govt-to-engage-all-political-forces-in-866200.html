Bilawal urges federal govt to engage all political forces instead of ‘fighting everyone’

Published on November 06, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP) Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari has advised the PML-N-led federal government to engage with all political, social, and provincial stakeholders instead of fighting with everyone. Speaking at a ceremony in Larkana on Friday, the former foreign minister urged the ruling coalition to prioritize resolving the public's economic difficulties over political rivalries.

Specific details regarding the exact date of the event beyond Friday were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Key facts

  • Bilawal pointed out that people worldwide are facing severe financial pressures linked to the economic fallout of the US-Israeli war on Iran.
  • He noted that the financial consequences of the Middle East conflict are unlikely to end quickly, even if hostilities conclude in the near future.
  • The PPP chairman cautioned the government against focusing on internal differences, specifically mentioning political rivalries with the PTI and Jamiat Ulema-i-Islam-Fazl chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman.
  • He highlighted that the nation is already managing broader struggles, including efforts against terrorists as well as issues related to India and Afghanistan.
  • He stressed that assisting citizens through global financial strains has become the primary test for governments regardless of direct involvement in the conflict.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, Bilawal warned that remaining consumed by domestic political disputes while simultaneously dealing with security and regional threats will distract the country from addressing key public hardships. He emphasized that the federal government and key stakeholders must concentrate on relieving the economic strain on the public to successfully navigate the ongoing international crisis.

'I may not return alive': In open letter to top judges, Imran pleads for medical treatment for him and Bushra Bibi

Published on November 16, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, incarcerated former prime minister and PTI founder Imran Khan has issued an open letter to the chief justices of the Federal Constitutional Court, the Supreme Court, and the Islamabad High Court. In the letter, Khan requested urgent medical care for both himself and his wife, Bushra Bibi. The document was published on the social media platform X by his legal counsel, Barrister Salman Safdar, who visited Khan at Adiala jail on Nov 2 and Nov 11.

Key facts

  • According to Dawn, Khan disclosed that he has lost 85 percent of the vision in his right eye following strict isolation and limited contact with the outside world.
  • Khan stated that Bushra Bibi experienced a retinal detachment in April, underwent a secret eye surgery, and faces permanent loss of sight unless she receives another operation.
  • The letter noted that an Aug 18 Supreme Court directive to transfer Khan to Shifa International Hospital within two days remains unimplemented.
  • Khan alleged that he spends up to 22 hours a day in solitary confinement, which has severely impacted his mental health, and that recommendations by the jail's medical board for television, reading materials, and family contact were ignored.
  • He reported that jail authorities restricted legal and family visits, blocked calls from his sons, Kasim and Sulaiman, and held his sisters in custody.
  • Khan and Bushra Bibi are serving a 17-year sentence linked to the Toshakhana-2 case concerning a Bulgari jewellery set received during an official visit in May 2021.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, Khan emphasized that despite facing over a hundred court appearances and ongoing legal challenges following a campaign of political victimisation, he had never previously requested relief on medical grounds. However, the worsening health of his wife and the non-enforcement of prior Supreme Court orders compelled him to seek direct intervention from the judiciary. Further details regarding official responses or court schedules were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

FIA books 8 Pakistanis deported from Turkiye for violating immigration laws

Published on November 15, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, the Federal Investigation Agency (FIA) in Islamabad has booked eight Pakistani nationals who were deported from Turkiye for allegedly violating immigration laws. In a separate action, the agency's Anti-Human Trafficking Circle in Rawalpindi recovered Rs3.8 million that was fraudulently obtained from a citizen who was promised employment abroad.

Key facts

According to Dawn, the report details the following points:

  • The eight individuals went to Turkiye on work visas but allegedly began working illegally for unauthorized employers rather than joining their assigned companies.
  • Turkish law enforcement officers arrested and jailed the individuals before deporting them to Pakistan.
  • The FIA Islamabad Zone registered a case against the deportees under Sections 3 and 4 of the Passport Act, 1974. An ongoing investigation aims to establish if human smugglers or agents assisted them.
  • In the Rawalpindi case, complainant Wajid Hussain paid €12,000 (roughly Rs3.8 million) to a suspect who failed to fulfill a promise of providing a job and work permit in Portugal.
  • Sub-Inspector Ansa Tayyaba oversaw the inquiry, which successfully retrieved the total amount and handed it back to the victim. Specific details regarding court dates or the suspect's identity are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the cases highlight strict measures against immigration infractions and fraudulent visa schemes. The FIA Islamabad Zone affirmed that operations targeting human trafficking and illegal visa agents will continue without interruption under the guidance of Islamabad Zone Director Syed Shehzad Nadeem Bokhari.

Security forces kill 74 terrorists during IBOs in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan: ISPR

Published on November 13, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, a statement issued by Pakistan's Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR) announced that security forces killed 74 terrorists during intelligence-based operations conducted in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP) and Balochistan.

The military media wing reported that operations in KP over a 48-hour span resulted in 35 terrorists killed in Mir Ali, North Waziristan district, and eight killed in Orakzai district. In Balochistan, security forces killed 31 terrorists during an intelligence-based area sanitisation operation underway since November 3 in the Kurram area of Quetta district.

Key facts

  • Security forces recovered weapons, ammunition, improvised explosive devices (IEDs), and homemade explosives during sanitisation, disrupting planned terrorist activities.
  • The state designates the banned Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan as "Fitna-al-Khawarij" and Balochistan-based terrorist groups as "Fitna al-Hindustan" to highlight alleged foreign involvement.
  • Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and President Asif Ali Zardari praised the security forces for destroying hideouts, affirming that counterterrorism efforts will continue under the National Action Plan.
  • According to the Pakistan Institute for Conflict and Security Studies (PICSS), 49 militant attacks occurred nationwide from October 30 to November 5, 2026, resulting in 25 fatalities and 21 injuries.
  • PICSS data also noted earlier actions, including an October 8 operation in North Waziristan where nine terrorists were killed, following 18 killed in separate engagements the previous day.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the ongoing operations are part of a broader counterterrorism campaign under the vision "Azm-i-Istehkam" to eliminate foreign-sponsored militant networks and stabilize volatile security environments across KP and Balochistan. Additional administrative details were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Unmarried women aged 45 or above included in BISP

Published on November 17, 2026

According to a report by Dawn, the governing board of the Benazir Income Support Programme (BISP) has formally approved extending financial aid to unmarried women who are 45 years of age or older. The decision was announced during a press conference in Lahore by BISP Chairperson Senator Rubina Khalid. Specific details regarding financial distribution amounts or eligibility verification steps were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

What happened

During the conference, Senator Rubina Khalid emphasized that BISP functions as a national social safety net aimed at assisting eligible households regardless of political affiliation. Out of more than 10 million families currently enrolled in the scheme across the country, roughly five million are located in Punjab. The addition of single women aged 45 and above expands the demographic reach of the initiative.

Key facts

  • The BISP Board approved the inclusion of unmarried women aged 45 and older in the financial aid program.
  • Out of more than 10 million beneficiary families nationwide, approximately five million reside in Punjab.
  • A digital payment mechanism featuring mobile wallets has been created to ensure simple and transparent disbursements.
  • Six partner banks are integrated into the network via 1-LINK, allowing recipients to collect funds without being restricted to a specific facility.
  • Beneficiaries receive free SIM cards, and digital wallet accounts are activated directly under their names.
  • Automated text alerts are sent to recipient phones when funds are deposited and withdrawn.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the implementation of mobile wallets and 1-LINK bank integration is intended to streamline payments for millions of registered households. By transitioning to digital distribution and providing complimentary SIM cards, BISP aims to make fund collection more direct, secure, and accessible for eligible women across Pakistan.

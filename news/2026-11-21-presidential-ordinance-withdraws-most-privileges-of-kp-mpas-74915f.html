Presidential ordinance withdraws most privileges of KP MPAs, including protection against preventive detention

Published on November 21, 2026

What happened

President Asif Ali Zardari has promulgated five ordinances affecting Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP), significantly altering lawmaker immunities and establishing new frameworks for provincial development projects, according to Dawn. Four of the executive measures were officially published in the Official Gazette on Nov 19 after confirmation from the president's office.

Key facts

According to Dawn, the legislative changes introduced through the five ordinances cover several operational and legal areas within KP:

  • Lawmaker Privileges: The KP Provincial Assembly (Powers, Immunities and Privileges) Ordinance, 2026 removes 21 of the 40 sections from the existing provincial act. The omitted sections include protections against preventive detention, immunity from civil or criminal actions, the requirement for the speaker's prior permission before detaining a member, and defined offences related to breach of privilege.
  • Special Development Initiatives: Two separate ordinances create executive committees to manage development, rehabilitation, and procurement initiatives in 12 designated security-challenged districts as well as remaining areas of the province. These committees have the authority to select executing agencies, including military formations, and hold overriding power over provincial procurement and public-private partnership acts. Civil courts are also stripped of jurisdiction regarding actions taken under these laws.
  • Madressah Registration: The KP Societies (Registration) (Amendment) Ordinance, 2026 provides that religious madressahs registered with the federal Ministry of Federal Education and Professional Training's Directorate General of Religious Education do not require separate registration under the provincial act.
  • Speaker and Deputy Speaker Ordinance: Specific details regarding the provisions of the KP Speaker and Deputy Speaker (Salaries, Allowances and Privileges) Ordinance 2026 are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

The rollbacks in legal protection come after emergency was declared in KP on Oct 11, according to Dawn. Following governor's rule, Chief Minister Sohail Afridi, Speaker Babar Saleem Swati, and several other legislators were placed under preventive detention or house arrest. The newly issued ordinances remove key legal immunities for assembly members while centralizing control over public spending and development initiatives under new executive bodies.

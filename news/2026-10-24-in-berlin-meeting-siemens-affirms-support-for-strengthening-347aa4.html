In Berlin meeting, Siemens affirms support for strengthening Pakistan's healthcare infrastructure

Published on October 24, 2026

According to Dawn, Siemens Executive Vice President Daniel Frisch and a company delegation met with Pakistani President Asif Ali Zardari in Berlin. The meeting took place on the final day of President Zardari's official trip to Germany. During the engagement, Siemens expressed its commitment to bolster Pakistan's healthcare infrastructure through technological applications and innovative solutions. Siemens representatives are scheduled to visit Pakistan shortly to execute memoranda of understanding regarding capacity building and technological initiatives in the medical industry.

What happened

As reported by Dawn, Siemens executives briefed President Zardari on the firm's historical work in Pakistan, highlighting current technological cooperation in treating conditions such as cancer and stroke. President Zardari emphasized the need for research-based health solutions to ensure affordable and high-quality healthcare delivery, particularly for impoverished citizens in remote areas. He encouraged Siemens to diversify its investments across multiple Pakistani sectors and offered full institutional cooperation through the Special Investment Facilitation Council.

Key facts

  • Siemens' health division has maintained a presence in Pakistan for decades, employing over 100 people across three major cities.
  • The delegation meeting included Sindh Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah, Senator Saleem Mandviwalla, Pakistan's Ambassador to Germany Salman Sharif, and Sindh provincial ministers.
  • During his trip, President Zardari also conferred with German President Frank-Walter Steinmeier regarding bilateral relations, Afghanistan, and regional security initiatives.

Why it matters

Dawn noted that the visit signifies an effort to strengthen ties ahead of the 75th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Pakistan and Germany, which were established on October 15, 1951. The two countries maintain active partnerships in education, defence, culture, and trade. According to official data cited in the report, Pakistan's exports to Germany decreased by 3.53 percent to $1.369 billion during the first 10 months of the previous fiscal year, down from $1.419 billion recorded in the matching period a year earlier.

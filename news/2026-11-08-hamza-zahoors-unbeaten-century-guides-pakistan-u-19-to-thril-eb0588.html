Hamza Zahoor’s unbeaten century guides Pakistan U-19 to thrilling win over India

Published on November 08, 2026

According to Dawn, Pakistan Under-19 claimed a thrilling three-wicket win against India Under-19 in their ACC Men’s Under-19 Asia Cup Group ‘A’ clash at the Al Amerat Cricket Ground in Muscat on Sunday.

What happened

Chasing a target of 209 runs, Pakistan reached 211 for seven with 11 balls remaining. Wicket-keeper-batter Hamza Zahoor anchored the chase, remaining unbeaten on 100 from 108 deliveries to earn Player of the Match honors. Pakistan’s innings initially faltered as India’s bowling attack reduced them to 61 for five. However, Hamza partnered with Hasnain Abbas Dar to stage a recovery and guide his side across the finish line. Earlier in the day, India won the toss and opted to bat, but were bowled out for 208 in 46.3 overs despite an outstanding century from Arjun Rajput.

Key facts

  • India struggled early, sliding to 22 for three after captain and opener Lakshya Rajesh Raichandani was dismissed for zero on the second ball of the innings.
  • Arjun Rajput led India's recovery with 107 runs off 85 balls, hitting 11 fours and five sixes, though he received little assistance from the rest of the lineup.
  • Pakistan’s bowling effort was led by Ali Raza, Rizwanullah, and Niqab Shafiq, who claimed three wickets apiece, alongside Mohammad Sayyam who took one.
  • Hasnain Abbas Dar contributed 38 runs in a crucial partnership with Hamza to revive Pakistan's chase.
  • Hamza’s match-winning century included 10 fours and three sixes.

Why it matters

The victory takes Pakistan to the top of Group ‘A’ with four points after recording back-to-back wins. India remains on two points following one win and one loss. According to Dawn, both teams have one group fixture left, with the top two sides from the group advancing to the semi-finals.

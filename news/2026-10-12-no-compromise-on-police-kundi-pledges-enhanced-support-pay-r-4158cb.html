'No compromise on police': Kundi pledges enhanced support, pay raise for KP law enforcers

Published on October 12, 2026

Following the imposition of governor's rule and an emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Provincial Governor Faisal Karim Kundi pledged enhanced support, upgraded equipment, and pay raises for law enforcement personnel, according to Dawn.

Addressing a press conference in Peshawar, Kundi stated that counter-terrorism is the primary focus of the new provincial setup, placing security requirements above development projects. Kundi also co-chaired a provincial apex committee meeting with federal Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi to establish security priorities.

What happened

After the central government established governor's rule, members of a newly created advisory council assumed charge of their assigned departments. Kundi pledged complete government backing to the army, police, Frontier Constabulary, and Counter-Terrorism Department, promising to deliver all requested weaponry and operational resources.

Key facts

  • Police Compensation: Kundi ordered that provincial police salaries be raised so they are no longer the lowest in the country, matching or exceeding pay in other regions. Not specified in the source; please check the official notice for exact salary figures.
  • Martyrs' Package: Financial disbursements under the martyrs' package will increase, and children of fallen officers will receive free education.
  • Federal Assistance: Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi provided 10 armored personnel carriers to the local police on behalf of the central government and guaranteed required funds for security forces.
  • Federal Alignment: Officials reported full policy alignment between Islamabad and the province, ending previous inter-provincial and federal disputes.
  • Protests and Services: Kundi appealed to striking doctors and protesting university personnel to end their demonstrations, promising to address their grievances. He also noted that efforts are under way to restore internet service and reopen roads within one to two days.
  • Regional Insecurity: The governor highlighted severe restrictions on movement and daily life caused by terrorism in districts such as Dera Ismail Khan, Tank, and Waziristan.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the governance change aims to establish direct synergy between federal and provincial security strategies to combat terrorism in the region. By equipping law enforcement, raising salaries, and working to settle local labor disputes, the administration seeks to restore public safety, normalize movement in high-risk districts, and create a stable environment for business and investment.

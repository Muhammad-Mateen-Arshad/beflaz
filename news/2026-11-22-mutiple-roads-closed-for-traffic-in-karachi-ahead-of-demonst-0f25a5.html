Mutiple roads closed for traffic in Karachi ahead of demonstration, rally by political parties

Published on November 22, 2026

According to a report by Dawn, several major roadways in Karachi have been closed to traffic in response to a scheduled rally by Jeay Sindh and a planned demonstration by Jamiat Ulema-i-Islam-Fazl (JUI-F).

What happened

Traffic alerts indicated that security concerns were the primary reason for most of the road blockades across the city. The only exception noted was the route linking Guru Mandir to Jamshed Road, where the official alert specifically attributed the closure to the upcoming JUI-F gathering.

Key facts

Dawn detailed several specific road closures and alternative routes established for motorists:

  • Deen Mohammed Wafai Road: Closed in both directions. Vehicles arriving from MR Kiani Chowrangi are diverted toward Thana Gali, while traffic from Fawara Chowk is redirected toward Zainab Market.
  • Sharea Faisal: The section heading toward Karsaz and Drigh Road is blocked. Travelers coming from the airport are being rerouted via Rashid Minhas Bridge toward Millennium Mall.
  • Askari 4 to Drigh Road: The route is shut down, with traffic being diverted from Dalimia Road toward NIPA and the National Stadium.
  • Guru Mandir to Jamshed Road: Closed due to the scheduled JUI-F demonstration.

Why it matters

In a public statement, the JUI-F announced that its primary demonstration is set for 2pm at Guru Mandir. Additional protests are scheduled at press clubs, party headquarters, and various other sites throughout Sindh. The party stated these demonstrations are reacting to an ongoing smear campaign against JUI-F chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman, an alleged breach of the sanctity of his residence, and a plot to divide Sindh. Specific details regarding the schedule and route of the Jeay Sindh rally were Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

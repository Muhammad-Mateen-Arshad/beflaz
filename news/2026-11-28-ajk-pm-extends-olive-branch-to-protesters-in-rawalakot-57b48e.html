AJK PM extends olive branch to protesters in Rawalakot

Published on November 28, 2026

What happened

Azad Jammu and Kashmir (AJK) Prime Minister Iftikhar Gillani has offered to hold talks regarding the demands of demonstrators in Rawalakot, according to Dawn. Speaking at a political gathering in Muzaffarabad, Gillani stated that the government is prepared to address grievances that fit within the region's legal and constitutional framework. However, he urged those involved in the unrest to step away from armed groups, noting that dialogue cannot occur outside legal boundaries.

Key facts

According to the report, several details regarding the administration's stance and plans were highlighted during the gathering:

  • Government initiatives: The administration plans to set up stroke centres and catheterisation laboratories, launch an electric bus service, upgrade roads, provide interest-free loans to youth, offer university scholarships, and introduce housing loans.
  • Governance strategy: Gillani outlined a five-year strategy starting with a 100-day action plan focused on health, education, transport, digital governance, tourism, and urban development. He added that merit-based public sector hiring would be managed through third-party agencies.
  • Assembly seats: Legislative Assembly Speaker Chaudhry Tariq Farooq called for holding elections to fill seven vacant assembly seats, stating this would restore full representation for the people of Poonch.
  • Impact of unrest: Energy and Water Resources Minister Saqib Majeed Raja stated that unrest and instability since 2023 have damaged the regional economy and administrative setup, urging citizens to resolve grievances through democratic processes rather than confrontation.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the offer for dialogue and the announced action plan follow a period where four successive governments caused political uncertainty and disrupted public development. The administration is seeking to restore stability, enhance public services, and reduce administrative hurdles through digital government portals. Specific dates for negotiations or a full listing of the protesters' demands were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

PM calls for stronger Pak-Oman ties

Published on November 28, 2026

According to Dawn, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif recently held meetings aimed at expanding diplomatic ties with Oman and improving Pakistan’s national industrial research framework.

What happened

In a meeting at the Prime Minister’s House with the Omani ambassador, PM Shehbaz called for deeper bilateral cooperation in trade, investment, energy, and defence. Separately, during a meeting regarding the Pakistan Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (PCSIR), the prime minister directed the organization to enhance its research capabilities to better support small- and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), industrial development, and export growth.

Key facts

According to Dawn, key details from the discussions include:

  • Oman Bilateral Ties: Both sides emphasized historical, cultural, and religious connections while discussing regional developments and the importance of peace through dialogue. The Omani ambassador conveyed greetings from Omani leaders and reaffirmed commitment to strong relations.
  • International Accreditation: The prime minister directed all PCSIR laboratories to secure international accreditation to ensure Pakistani export products receive globally recognized certification.
  • Committee and Governance: PM Shehbaz set up a committee to evaluate PCSIR's legal mandate, past achievements, original objectives, and untapped potential. Specific committee members were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Vocational Training: The prime minister requested a third-party validation report on PCSIR and called for closer cooperation between PCSIR and the National Vocational and Technical Training Commission (NAVTTC).
  • Infrastructure and Reach: Officials reported that PCSIR operates 10 research laboratories, seven technical training institutes, and has trained over 12,000 young people. Twelve more laboratories are currently nearing completion.
  • Sector Coverage: PCSIR supports industries through product testing, quality validation, instrument calibration, and technical solutions across sectors including agriculture, pharmaceuticals, textiles, biotechnology, food, leather, minerals, energy, and engineering.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, strengthening engagement with Oman aims to transform existing diplomatic goodwill into concrete economic and institutional partnerships. At the same time, reforming PCSIR and aligning its laboratory testing with international standards is intended to make Pakistani exports more competitive globally and raise overall industrial productivity.

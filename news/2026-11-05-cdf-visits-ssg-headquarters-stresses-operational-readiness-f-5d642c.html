CDF visits SSG headquarters, stresses operational readiness for tackling security threats: ISPR

Published on November 05, 2026

What happened

According to a report published by Dawn, Chief of Defence Forces and Chief of Army Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir visited the Special Services Group (SSG) headquarters in Tarbela on Thursday. Based on statements from the military's media wing, Inter Services Public Relations (ISPR), he was briefed on specialized training, mission-oriented readiness, and operational preparedness during his visit.

Key facts

  • Field Marshal Munir honored fallen SSG personnel by laying wreaths at the martyrs' monument.
  • He inspected a display of state-of-the-art specialized equipment and watched a demonstration showcasing the SSG's precision, agility, and combat proficiency in special operations.
  • The military chief met with officers and troops, praising their professionalism, strong morale, and dedication to duty.
  • Specific dates for future exercises or structural updates were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • The visit comes after his trip last month to the Peshawar Corps Headquarters, where he was updated on counterterrorism operations, border management, and the overall security situation in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Why it matters

As reported by Dawn, Field Marshal Munir emphasized that continuous training, the growth of domestic capabilities, and an absolute focus on operational readiness are vital. He stated that these factors are necessary for maintaining an advantage against emerging challenges and evolving security threats.

Federal cabinet ratifies defence agreement with Kuwait

Published on November 04, 2026

What happened

According to a report by Dawn, the federal cabinet of Pakistan ratified a defence cooperation agreement with Kuwait on Wednesday. The accord, which was originally concluded on June 11, 2023, and ratified by the Kuwaiti government on July 19, aims to strengthen institutional linkages and sector collaboration between the armed forces of both nations.

Key facts

During the session, the cabinet approved several other key provincial, financial, and administrative measures according to the Prime Minister’s Office handout:

  • Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governance: Powers previously delegated to the provincial chief minister were transferred to Governor Faisal Karim Kundi to assist administrative affairs following the Centre's declaration of emergency and governor's rule on Oct 11. The governor must submit monthly review reports to the federal government. A proposed Rs160 billion package for the province was also finalized.
  • Currency Updates: On the recommendation of the Ministry of Finance, the cabinet approved new currency note designs that utilize modern materials and enhanced security features to prevent counterfeiting. The cabinet also approved replacing the Rs10 banknote with a Rs10 coin. Specific release dates were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Fuel and Subsidies: Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif announced that a planned Rs15 fuel price increase was averted after refineries agreed to absorb the burden from windfall profits. A fuel subsidy scheme launched in September has spent Rs28 billion so far and will run through the end of December under Intelligence Bureau monitoring.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the cabinet's actions address ongoing domestic governance and economic matters while Pakistan copes with external economic impacts. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif noted that regional instability has caused global oil prices to skyrocket. Consequently, Pakistan is continuing diplomatic mediation efforts involving Iran, Saudi Arabia, Turkiye, and the United States, including activities under the Makkah Joint Defence Agreement, to help restore regional stability.

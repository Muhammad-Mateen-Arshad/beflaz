Xi, Trump, Putin exchange first greetings in China ahead of historic meeting

Published on November 18, 2026

According to Dawn, United States President Donald Trump, Chinese President Xi Jinping, and Russian President Vladimir Putin gathered in Shenzhen, China, on Wednesday, exchanging initial greetings prior to a planned formal trilateral meeting. The interactions took place during the annual Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (Apec) summit hosted by China. Media footage captured Putin shaking hands with Trump and both leaders speaking with Xi before posing for an official group photograph.

What happened

Xi personally greeted Trump upon his arrival at Shenzhen’s Bao’an International Airport, where the US leader was welcomed with national anthems, a red carpet, and a flyover by fighter jets. Prior to Trump's landing, Xi held a separate bilateral meeting with Putin—their fifth encounter this year—to discuss strengthening government, military, energy, and financial cooperation, as well as foreign policy matters concerning Ukraine and North Korea.

Key facts

  • Washington indicated that Thursday's trilateral summit is expected to cover nuclear disarmament and the conflict in Iran following the expiration of a US-Russia atomic treaty in February.
  • Trump stated that Ukraine would be discussed at the meeting, whereas Beijing has maintained that the conflict is not China's issue.
  • Putin told Apec attendees that Western sanctions against Russia had failed and urged unity and dialogue over division.
  • Trump arrived in China after attending Quad and Asean meetings in the Philippines, hinting at possible side talks with Putin and North Korean leader Kim Jong Un.
  • Full-scale bilateral talks strictly between Putin and Trump are unlikely during the trip, according to Russian state media.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the high-profile trilateral gathering has prompted concerns that the three leaders could make unilateral decisions regarding Ukraine without involving Kyiv or European nations. The summit also takes place amid broader international discussions over global nuclear arsenals and ongoing economic tensions regarding tariffs and trade. Final formal agreements or specific joint statements resulting from the trilateral meeting were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

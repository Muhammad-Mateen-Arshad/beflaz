CDF Munir, Singapore's FM discuss regional security and bilateral cooperation

Published on October 30, 2026

What happened

Singapore’s Foreign Minister Dr Vivian Balakrishnan made an official visit to Pakistan, holding talks with senior leadership on regional security, trade, and bilateral cooperation, according to Dawn. During his trip, Balakrishnan met with Chief of Defence Forces and Chief of Army Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir at the General Headquarters in Rawalpindi, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, and Pakistani leader Dar.

Key facts

  • According to Dawn, Balakrishnan's visit is the first by a Singaporean foreign minister to Pakistan in more than two decades.
  • The diplomatic visit coincides with the 60th anniversary of diplomatic relations established between the two nations.
  • During the meeting at military headquarters, CDF Munir and Balakrishnan exchanged views on regional security, peace, and institutional cooperation. Balakrishnan commended the professionalism of Pakistan's armed forces.
  • Prime Minister Sharif noted admiration for Singapore's economic development, emphasizing collaboration in areas such as fintech, the digital economy, infrastructure, logistics, and small and medium enterprises.
  • Pakistani official Dar discussed the potential resumption of direct commercial flights between both nations to facilitate business travel and public contact.
  • Talks also covered reopening the Trade and Investment Office at Pakistan's High Commission in Singapore, along with expanding cooperation in research, artificial intelligence, agriculture, and climate initiatives.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the meetings reflect a mutual effort to revitalize economic, political, and security ties while unlocking untapped potential in trade and business relations. Specific timelines or launch dates for direct flights were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

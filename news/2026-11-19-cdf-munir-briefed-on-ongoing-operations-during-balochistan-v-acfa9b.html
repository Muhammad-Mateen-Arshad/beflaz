CDF Munir briefed on ongoing operations during Balochistan visit: ISPR

Published on November 19, 2026

What happened

According to press releases from the Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR) reported by Dawn, Chief of Defence Forces and Chief of the Army Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir visited Turbat, Balochistan, to receive detailed briefings on the regional security environment, counterterrorism operations, and socioeconomic development initiatives. During his visit, Munir praised the operational readiness of the troops, laid a floral wreath at the Shuhada Monument, and reaffirmed the military's support for local peace and development. Separately, ISPR announced that security forces killed 22 terrorists in multiple engagements across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP) earlier in the week.

Key facts

  • CDF Munir was received in Turbat by the Corps Commander Balochistan before reviewing ongoing security and development measures.
  • Recent actions in KP resulted in 15 terrorists killed during an intelligence-based operation in South Waziristan, six killed across two encounters in Bannu, and one killed in Tank.
  • ISPR stated that weapons and ammunition were recovered from the deceased, identifying them as members of the banned Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan, designated by authorities as India-sponsored Fitna-al-Khawarij.
  • Security forces are conducting ongoing sanitisation efforts under the national counterterrorism vision titled Azm-i-Istehkam.
  • Recent figures released by ISPR also noted 74 terrorists killed during operations the previous week, as well as 24 terrorists killed on Saturday in engagements that left four security personnel, including a major, dead.

Why it matters

The operational reports follow data published by the Pakistan Institute for Conflict and Security Studies (PICSS), an Islamabad-based think tank. According to PICSS, overall terrorism-related violence in Pakistan declined significantly in October compared to September, with total attacks dropping by 37 percent, fatalities falling by 57 percent, and injuries decreasing by 71 percent. Balochistan saw militant attacks drop by 46 percent and related deaths fall by 68 percent, while mainland KP recorded a 31 percent decline in attacks and a 58 percent drop in deaths. Further details regarding future operations were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

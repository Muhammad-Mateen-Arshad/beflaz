Pakistan, Qatar, Turkiye and Indonesia sign 'Badr' defence cooperation pact

Published on October 21, 2026

What happened

Pakistan, Qatar, Turkiye, and Indonesia have signed a multilateral defence cooperation pact titled the 'Badr Memorandum of Understanding (MoU)', according to Dawn. The signing ceremony took place in Qatar, as reported by the Turkish defence ministry. The accord aims to boost military production, enhance defensive capabilities, and facilitate technical military training, research, and development among the participating nations.

Key facts

  • The agreement was signed by Pakistani Defence Minister Khawaja Asif, Turkish Defence Minister Yaşar Güler, Qatari Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of State for Defence Sheikh Saud bin Abdulrahman, and Indonesian Deputy Minister of Defence Yusuf Jauhari.
  • Haluk Görgün, head of Turkiye's Secretariat of Defence Industries, was present and noted that the pact creates a framework for strategic cooperation focused on technology sharing and joint production.
  • State-run Qatar News Agency reported that the agreement is named 'Badr', meaning full moon, to reflect how the four countries complement each another. It added that other friendly nations could join later based on criteria set by the founding members.
  • Pakistan's Foreign Office has not yet commented on the signing. Specific financial commitments and start dates were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the Turkish ministry highlighted that the agreement holds substantial economic weight by leveraging each nation's unique military production capabilities. The partnership is designed to strengthen defence industrial capacities and fulfill the security needs of allied countries. Dawn also noted that Pakistan maintains close strategic ties with all three participating states, including joint membership with Turkiye in the Makkah Defence Alliance and the Regional Four Group, along with recent high-level meetings with Qatari and Indonesian leaders.

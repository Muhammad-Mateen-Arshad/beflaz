Varma century in vain as Pakistan Shaheens down India A in Asia Cup Rising Stars clash

Published on November 22, 2026

According to Dawn, Pakistan Shaheens defeated India A by 48 runs in an Asia Cup Rising Stars match held in Doha on Sunday. Pakistan posted a target of 313 runs after electing to bat first and successfully bowled out India A with five deliveries remaining, overcoming a century from Indian captain Tilak Varma.

What happened

According to Dawn, Pakistan established their total of 313 behind half-centuries from Maaz Sadaqat (59), Hasan Nawaz (79), and Farhan Yousaf (56). Indian fast bowler Harshit Rana disrupted Pakistan's top order early by trapping Rohail Nazir leg-before for a duck after Ayush Badoni had Shamyl Hussain caught for 33. The opening overs featured heated verbal exchanges and an animated send-off from Rana.

India A's run chase opened aggressively with Abhishek Sharma scoring 70 off 42 balls. However, Pakistan's spinners took control, reducing India to 129-6. Varma then put on a 112-run seventh-wicket partnership with Shahbaz Ahmed, who scored 56, to briefly revive India's chase. Pakistan's bowlers ultimately secured the win, with Ali Raza breaking the stand before Ahmed Daniyal had Varma caught out for 100 in the final over.

Key facts

  • Before the match, the referee initially miscalled the toss as tails after Varma flipped the coin on Pakistan skipper Abdul Samad's call of heads, but officials corrected the decision in Pakistan's favour after Samad pointed out the mistake.
  • The team captains did not shake hands prior to the start of play, maintaining recent pre-match protocols observed between the sides.
  • Pakistan's Hasan Nawaz contributed 79 runs with the bat and took three wickets during India's innings.
  • Ali Raza claimed three wickets for 56 runs, including the dismissals of Shahbaz Ahmed and Vipraj Nigam.
  • Tilak Varma scored 100 runs off 149 balls, marking his second century of the Asia Cup Rising Stars tournament.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the outcome demonstrates Pakistan Shaheens' ability to defend a total under late pressure despite an individual century from India A's captain. The match was also defined by competitive tension, highlighted by powerplay exchanges and strict adherence to recent protocols between the two teams.

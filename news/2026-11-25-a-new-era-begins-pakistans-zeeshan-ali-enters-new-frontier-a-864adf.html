‘A new era begins’: Pakistan’s Zeeshan Ali enters new frontier as Shenzhou-24 docks with space station

Published on November 25, 2026

What happened

China successfully launched the Shenzhou-24 spacecraft carrying 40-year-old astronaut Zeeshan Ali, making him the first Pakistani astronaut to reach orbit and the first foreign national to board the Tiangong space station, according to Dawn. The China Manned Space Agency (CMSA) confirmed that the craft docked with the space station three hours and 38 minutes after liftoff.

A Long March 2-F rocket lifted off from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Centre at 8:18 am Beijing Time (5:18 am Pakistan Time) on November 25. The spacecraft separated from its carrier rocket approximately 10 minutes after takeoff and entered its preset orbit safely.

Key facts

  • Mission crew: Chinese astronaut Tang Hongbo commands the mission, accompanied by pilot Wu Yi and payload specialist Zeeshan Ali.
  • Station operations: The crew will conduct around 90 scientific experiments, handle routine space station maintenance, perform an orbital rotation with the Shenzhou-23 crew, and advance space research.
  • Pakistani research: Ali is expected to remain in space for about a week to complete experiments for Pakistan's space agency, Suparco. His work includes growing microalgae as a potential source of food and oxygen, as well as testing the impact of spaceflight on quinoa seeds.
  • Return details: The exact return date and landing location were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, leaders from both countries highlighted the event as a landmark milestone coinciding with 75 years of diplomatic relations. Pakistani Planning Minister Ahsan Iqbal witnessed the launch in China and posted on X that the flight signifies a new era for Pakistan's space exploration and technological goals.

Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar stated that the launch marks a historic moment meant to inspire young Pakistanis in scientific fields. Furthermore, Pakistani President Asif Ali Zardari and Chinese President Xi Jinping exchanged official letters praising the joint mission as proof of their enduring strategic friendship and joint commitment to peaceful space exploration.

At least 1 dead, 6 injured after under-construction building collapses near Karachi's Baloch Colony

Published on November 25, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, a six-storey under-construction building collapsed on Wednesday near Baloch Colony in Karachi. The incident resulted in at least one death and six injuries. Emergency crews, including an urban search and rescue team, remain at the site searching through the rubble to locate individuals feared trapped beneath the debris.

Key facts

Official actions and rescue efforts reported by Dawn include:

  • A spokesperson for Rescue 1122 confirmed that responders recovered one body and retrieved one survivor alive from the collapse site.
  • The Edhi Foundation transported six injured people to the Jinnah Postgraduate Medical Centre while continuing assistance operations.
  • Sindh Inspector General of Police Javed Alam Odho directed officers to secure the area, coordinate with emergency services, clear traffic obstacles, and ensure immediate care for the injured.
  • Sindh Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah instructed the Sindh Building Control Authority to review approved plans, construction quality, and potential negligence. He also requested a detailed report from the Karachi commissioner and mandated safety checks on nearby structures.
  • President Asif Ali Zardari called for a complete investigation into construction oversight and urged structural safety reviews for surrounding properties.
  • Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif expressed sorrow over the incident and directed authorities to ensure priority healthcare for those hurt.

Why it matters

As reported by Dawn, building and roof collapses occur frequently throughout Pakistan due to sub-standard construction materials and weak adherence to safety guidelines. Karachi, a metropolis with over 20 million inhabitants, faces ongoing challenges from aging infrastructure, illegal extensions, extreme overcrowding, and poor enforcement of building laws. Recent similar tragedies in the city include a school wall collapse in Orangi Town that left five dead, a gas explosion in Soldier Bazaar that killed 15, and a five-storey structure collapse in Lyari that claimed 27 lives.

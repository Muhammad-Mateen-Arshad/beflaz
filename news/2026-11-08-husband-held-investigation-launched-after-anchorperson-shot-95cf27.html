Husband held, investigation launched after anchorperson shot dead at Rawalpindi home

Published on November 08, 2026

According to a report published by Dawn, news anchorperson Rabia Ahsan was discovered shot dead on Sunday at her residence located in the Gulshanabad area of Rawalpindi.

What happened

Rawalpindi police initiated an investigation after finding the media professional dead at her home. City Police Officer (CPO) Hassan Mushtaq Sukhera stated that authorities took Ahsan’s husband into custody and confiscated the firearm suspected to have been used in the shooting. CPO Sukhera, accompanied by the senior superintendent of police (SSP) operations and SSP investigations, visited the location with a team of forensic experts to inspect the scene.

Key facts

  • A male domestic worker at the home reported to police that the victim's husband claimed the gunshot occurred accidentally.
  • Teams from the Punjab Forensic Science Agency gathered physical evidence from the location, and the victim's body was sent to a hospital for a postmortem examination.
  • Police confirmed that an official case is being registered to conduct a fair investigation on merit.
  • The exact motive behind the fatal shooting was Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

First Lady Aseefa Bhutto-Zardari issued a statement expressing grief, condemning the violence, and requesting a complete, transparent, and unbiased probe to hold those responsible accountable. She expressed dismay regarding a pattern of violence against professional women in the region, noting that women must be able to live and work safely.

The First Lady referenced the earlier case of Hina Javed, a 45-year-old digital media executive found dead with torture marks at her Rawalpindi home in August. In Javed's case, DNA evidence matched both her domestic servant and her husband, with Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz noting that polygraph and DNA tests pointed toward the servant while investigations remained ongoing. Aseefa previously met with Javed's family on October 29, urging consideration of their request for a judicial commission.

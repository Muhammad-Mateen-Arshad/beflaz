PM Shehbaz assures KP governor of continued support to eliminate terrorism

Published on November 24, 2026

According to Dawn, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif met with Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP) Governor Faisal Karim Kundi in Islamabad on Tuesday. During their discussion, the prime minister assured the governor of full federal cooperation to eliminate terrorism, maintain lasting peace, and promote public welfare across the province.

What happened

During the meeting in Islamabad, PM Shehbaz stated that the sacrifices of security forces and the KP police in fighting terrorism would not be in vain. According to a statement from the Prime Minister’s Office, the federal government pledged to fully back the implementation of decisions reached at the apex committee meeting to eliminate terrorism completely. Governor Kundi also briefed the prime minister on ongoing efforts by himself and the KP Advisory Council to strengthen law and order in the region.

Key facts

  • The federal administration promised to mobilize all available resources to provide healthcare, education, and basic facilities to the people of KP.
  • The prime minister directed officials to give special attention to elevating social development indicators in the province.
  • Plans were highlighted to upgrade sports facilities and create constructive opportunities for youth in KP.
  • Specific funding amounts or implementation schedules were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the meeting follows several critical governance and security developments in KP:

  • On October 11, the federal government imposed an emergency and governor’s rule in KP, citing the provincial government's deliberate disengagement from fighting terrorism. Parliament endorsed the action amid protests from the opposition.
  • On October 22, PM Shehbaz led a federal apex committee meeting in Peshawar, promising to make tough decisions to bring peace to terrorism-affected districts.
  • Last week, Chief of Defence Forces and Chief of Army Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir reaffirmed the military's firm commitment to decisively eliminate terrorism and maintain stability in KP alongside law enforcement agencies.

Govt slashes petrol price by Rs8 per litre, high-speed diesel by Rs7 in another reduction

Published on November 10, 2026

What happened

The government has reduced fuel prices, cutting petrol by Rs8 per litre and high-speed diesel (HSD) by Rs7 per litre, according to a report by Dawn. Based on a notification from the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (Ogra), retail prices will adjust to Rs338 per litre for petrol and Rs371 per litre for HSD on Wednesday, Nov 11. The reduction is aided by an arrangement announced on Nov 2, where oil refineries absorb a cumulative Rs15 billion impact over 30 days to allow a Rs14.71 reduction in petrol costs for consumers. Government officials also held talks with Jamaat-i-Islami to address fuel relief options.

Key facts

  • Dawn reported that the government continues to collect Rs114 per litre in taxes and duties on petrol, alongside Rs100 per litre on diesel.
  • Fuel prices previously hit peaks on April 3, with petrol reaching Rs458.41 and HSD reaching Rs520.35 following market disruptions tied to the US-Iran conflict.
  • To assist specific commuters, the Prime Minister’s Fuel Relief Scheme was launched on Sept 17 for owners of motorcycles, rickshaws, and vehicles under 800cc.
  • Following cabinet decisions, Ogra has been conducting near-daily fuel price revisions since July 17 based on global pricing patterns.
  • Reintroduced austerity policies currently require commercial markets to close by 9pm and trim official vehicle fuel allocations by 50 percent for three months.

Why it matters

Petrol and HSD are major revenue drivers, generating monthly sales of 700,000 to 800,000 tonnes, compared to kerosene's monthly demand of 10,000 tonnes. Price cuts in petrol primarily aid middle and lower-middle-class consumers who rely on two-wheelers, rickshaws, and small vehicles. High-speed diesel price adjustments impact the general public more broadly because the fuel powers heavy transport, large generators, and power plants. Further administrative information was not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

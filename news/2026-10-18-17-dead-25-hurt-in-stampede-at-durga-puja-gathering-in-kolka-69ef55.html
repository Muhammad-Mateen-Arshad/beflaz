17 dead, 25 hurt in stampede at Durga Puja gathering in Kolkata

Published on October 18, 2026

What happened

According to a report by Dawn, a severe stampede at a Hindu religious gathering in Kolkata, eastern India, resulted in the deaths of at least 17 people and left 25 others injured. The incident took place on a Saturday night outside a festival pavilion during Durga Puja celebrations.

A senior police officer at the scene stated that sudden rainfall triggered the disaster. An announcement caused confusion and commotion among the crowd waiting outside the pavilion. Attendees began pushing and pulling while trying to move, causing people to fall to the ground and resulting in the fatal crush.

Key facts

  • West Bengal Chief Minister Suvendhu Adhikari confirmed that a seven-year-old girl was among the fatalities.
  • Authorities stated that only six bodies had been formally identified at the time of the report.
  • The official casualty count stands at 17 dead and 25 injured.
  • Specific details regarding official compensation or hospital arrangements were not provided in the report; Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

Dawn noted that deadly crowd surges are a frequent occurrence at mass events in India, driven by safety lapses and management failures. The report highlighted several recent tragedies across the country:

  • In 2024, a Hindu religious event in northern Uttar Pradesh state resulted in 121 deaths.
  • An August stampede at a temple in Bihar killed seven people and injured 19 others.
  • Last year, 36 people were killed at a campaign rally for an actor-turned-politician in Tamil Nadu.
  • A crush at the Kumbh Mela religious fair last year left 30 people dead and several injured.
  • In Bengaluru last year, 11 fans were crushed to death during celebrations for a local cricket team's first Indian Premier League victory.

ECP takes notice of alleged code violation by Attaullah Tarar ahead of NA-77 by-poll

Published on November 07, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, the Election Commission of Pakistan (ECP) has taken official notice of an alleged campaign code violation involving Information Minister Attaullah Tarar in the lead-up to the NA-77 Gujranwala by-election. The minister reportedly traveled to the constituency by helicopter to speak at an election event supporting candidate Rashid Basheer Virk. Existing election guidelines explicitly forbid public office holders, including federal ministers, from taking part in campaign activities.

Key facts

According to details reported by Dawn, the primary facts regarding the notice and the upcoming polls include:

  • The ECP directed the district returning officer and district monitoring officer to submit inquiry reports regarding the minister's actions.
  • The commission instructed Minister Tarar to attend a hearing before the ECP in person. The specific date for Tarar's appearance was not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • The ECP stated that official election results for the NA-77 constituency could be put on hold until a final judgment on the matter is issued.
  • By-elections for both NA-77 (Gujranwala-I) and PP-203 (Sahiwal-VI) are scheduled to take place on November 8.
  • The NA-77 seat became vacant after long-serving PML-N lawmaker Chaudhry Mehmood Bashir Virk passed away on September 11, leading to 18 candidates—including his son Rashid—filing nomination papers.
  • The PP-203 seat became vacant following the death of PML-N lawmaker Chaudhry Hanif Jutt from cardiac arrest on September 17, 2026, making the upcoming vote the second by-election in PP-203 since the 2024 general elections.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the commission's notice highlights strict monitoring of public officials who are legally barred from misusing state authority or influence during election periods. Because the ECP may withhold the final vote tally for NA-77 pending its administrative decision, the inquiry holds direct consequences for the official outcome of the November 8 by-election.

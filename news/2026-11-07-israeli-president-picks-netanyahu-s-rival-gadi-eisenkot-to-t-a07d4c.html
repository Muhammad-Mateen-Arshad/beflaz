Israeli president picks Netanyahu's rival Gadi Eisenkot to try to form government

Published on November 07, 2026

What happened

Israel’s President Isaac Herzog has tasked Gadi Eisenkot, leader of the Yashar party and a prominent rival to Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, with forming a new government following a deadlocked election on October 27, according to Dawn. Herzog made the decision after political consultations in which factions representing 67 members of the 120-seat Knesset recommended Eisenkot to lead the administration, compared to 46 recommendations for Netanyahu and seven members who declined to endorse anyone.

Key facts

The election results left neither main figure with an easy route to securing a 61-seat parliamentary majority:

  • Netanyahu’s Likud party won 27 seats, but his wider religious-nationalist coalition secured only 53 seats.
  • Eisenkot’s Yashar party became the second-largest single party with 21 seats, while his four-party coalition won 48 seats.
  • Arab minority parties achieved an unprecedented 19 seats and endorsed Eisenkot to lead the government first, despite Eisenkot declining a formal alliance with them. Both Eisenkot and Netanyahu have refused to include Arab-led parties in a government.
  • Eisenkot has 28 days to build a coalition, with a potential 14-day extension. Analysts consider his options—including a unity government with Netanyahu, a minority government, or an alliance with ultra-Orthodox parties—unlikely to materialize.
  • If candidates fail to form a government within roughly 100 days, another national election will take place.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, Eisenkot offers a political contrast to Netanyahu's leadership style and coalition partners, but shares similar security policies. The former military commander opposes Palestinian statehood, supports destroying Hamas's military capabilities before any military withdrawal from Gaza, and insists on preventing Iran from securing nuclear weapons capabilities. Eisenkot has publicly ruled out serving in a cabinet alongside Netanyahu and pledged to ensure the replacement of the Hamas administration in Gaza within 18 months of taking office. Further details on his specific legislative proposals were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

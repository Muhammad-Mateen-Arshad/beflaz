India stun Pakistan by one wicket in last-ball U-19 Asia Cup final thriller

Published on November 14, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, India secured a dramatic one-wicket victory over Pakistan on the final delivery of the ACC Men’s U-19 Asia Cup final at the Al Amerat Cricket Ground in Muscat on Saturday. Chasing a target of 338, India reached 339 for nine in 50 overs after Mohit Ulva hit the winning boundary on the last ball of the match.

Pakistan chose to bat first and compiled a total of 337 for nine. Usman Khan led their innings with a century, scoring 107 runs, alongside half-centuries from Abdul Qadir (80) and captain Farhan Yousaf (63). India's bowling effort was led by Kavya Paresh Patel, who took four wickets for 68 runs, and Chigurupati Venkata, who took three for 40 after entering the team to replace the injured Ishan Sood.

Key facts

  • India built an opening partnership of 95 runs between captain Lakshya Raichandani (80) and Aaryavir Sehwag (58).
  • Pakistan's bowlers triggered a middle-order collapse, led by Rizwanullah taking three for 53 and Niqab Shafiq taking two for 57, leaving India at 252 for eight in the 42nd over.
  • Manal Chauhan scored 47 off 32 balls, partnering with Ulva for a 57-run ninth-wicket stand to revive India's run chase.
  • Ulva finished undefeated on 49 off 26 balls, combining with Venkata (11 not out) for an unbroken 30-run tenth-wicket partnership to score the required eight runs in the final over.
  • Ulva was selected as the player of the match, while Sehwag received the player of the tournament award.

Why it matters

As reported by Dawn, the victory prevented defending champions Pakistan from retaining their title. Pakistan had reached the final with an unbeaten record in the tournament, having won all three of their group matches—including an earlier victory over India—and defeating Bangladesh in the semi-finals.

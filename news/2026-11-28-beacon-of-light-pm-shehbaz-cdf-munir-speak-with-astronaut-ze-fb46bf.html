‘Beacon of light’: PM Shehbaz, CDF Munir speak with astronaut Zeeshan Ali aboard China’s space station

Published on November 28, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, Pakistani Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and Chief of Defence Forces Field Marshal Syed Asim Munir held a phone call on Saturday with astronaut Zeeshan Ali. The conversation took place from the headquarters of the Space and Upper Atmosphere Research Commission in Islamabad while Ali was aboard China's Tiangong space station as part of the Shenzhou-24 mission.

The call was broadcast on media channels and included Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar alongside Chinese Ambassador Jiang Zaidong. According to Dawn, PM Shehbaz and CDF Munir commended Ali for his historic achievement, extending congratulations on behalf of the nation and praising the joint efforts between Pakistan and China.

Key facts

  • Zeeshan Ali is a 40-year-old pilot serving as a payload specialist, making him the first Pakistani in orbit and the first foreign astronaut to enter the Chinese space station.
  • Ali arrived at the station on Wednesday with Chinese commander Tang Hongbo and flight engineer Wu Yi, and his planned stay ranges from five to ten days.
  • While orbiting 400 kilometres above Earth at a speed of 7.9 kilometres per second, Ali stated that his duties and scientific research are proceeding according to plan.
  • The exact landing date and schedule for future spaceflights were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, Pakistani officials framed the mission as a major landmark in the country's space ambitions and a testament to its long-standing strategic relationship with China, which is celebrating 75 years of diplomatic relations. PM Shehbaz thanked Chinese President Xi Jinping for opening China's space programme to Pakistan and reiterated that space exploration should serve peaceful purposes for global benefit. Additionally, Ali addressed Pakistani youth during the call, urging them to focus on education and excel in science and technology to drive future scientific exploration.

In call with Fazl, Bilawal condemns 'govt actions, political vengeance' against JUI-F

Published on November 15, 2026

What happened

Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP) Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari held a telephone conversation on Sunday with Jamiat Ulema-i-Islam-Fazl (JUI-F) chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman, according to Dawn. During the call, Bilawal criticized actions taken by the ruling administration against the opposition party, describing them as political vengeance. Bilawal emphasized that the federal government needs to accept criticism from the opposition and avoid steps that damage democracy and Parliament, despite the PPP being a coalition partner in the PML-N-led federal administration.

Key facts

  • Bilawal called for political unity and urged the prime minister to build trust with opposition leaders and coalition partners to foster democratic stability.
  • The JUI-F conducted street demonstrations in several cities across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Sindh, and Balochistan against what it described as a coordinated character assassination campaign against its leadership.
  • Tensions between Fazl and the Centre escalated following disagreements over the federal government's policy decisions, including the imposition of governor's rule and emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
  • Federal officials, including Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi and Defence Minister Khawaja Asif, engaged in public exchanges with Fazl, with Naqvi accusing the JUI-F of extracting wealth from the state.
  • State agencies recently targeted JUI-F associates and properties, including a Capital Development Authority notice demanding the removal of a guard post at Fazl's Islamabad residence and National Accountability Bureau summonses for his son, Asad Mehmood, over alleged hiring irregularities.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, Bilawal's intervention underscores growing concerns within the ruling coalition regarding government pressure on opposition figures. Bilawal has reiterated that political parties must work cooperatively rather than engaging in continuous conflict. Further specifics regarding official government responses to Bilawal's statements were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

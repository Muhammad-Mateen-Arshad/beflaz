6 terrorists killed, 2 soldiers martyred during Dera Ismail Khan IBO: ISPR

Published on October 25, 2026

According to reports from Dawn citing statements from Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR), Pakistani security forces conducted an intelligence-based raid in the Zarkani area of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's Dera Ismail Khan district on Saturday.

What happened

The military announced on Sunday that six terrorists were killed during the encounter. Two soldiers—36-year-old Naik Ghulam Murtaza from Gujrat, Punjab, and 30-year-old Sepoy Taseer Ahmed from Talagang, Punjab—were killed in action. Security forces subsequently launched a sanitisation operation in the surrounding area to eliminate any remaining threats.

Key facts

  • Targeted Group: ISPR identified the deceased militants as members of a group associated with the banned Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan (TTP). The military reported that these individuals had been involved in attacks against civilians and law enforcement personnel.
  • Seizures: Weapons and ammunition were recovered from the location following the operation.
  • National Strategy: The military noted that the operation was carried out under the ongoing "Azm-i-Istehkam" counterterrorism campaign, which was endorsed by the Federal Apex Committee on National Action Plan.
  • Prior Operations: State media reported that security forces killed eight other terrorists during a similar intelligence-led action in Dera Ismail Khan earlier the same month.

Why it matters

The incident reflects a wider pattern of escalating security concerns in the region. Data compiled by the Pakistan Institute for Conflict and Security Studies shows that terrorist attacks in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (excluding merged districts) increased by 50 percent from July to August, rising from 36 to 54 incidents. Furthermore, findings from the Centre for Research and Security Studies indicate an 83 percent increase in violence-related fatalities across Pakistan during the third quarter of 2026, with 98 percent of those fatalities occurring in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan. In response to these developments, Chief of the Army Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir recently visited Peshawar's Corps Headquarters to review border security and operational readiness, reaffirming that military operations against foreign-sponsored militant groups will continue.

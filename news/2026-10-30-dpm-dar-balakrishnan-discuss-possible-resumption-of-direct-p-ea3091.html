DPM Dar, Balakrishnan discuss possible resumption of direct Pakistan-Singapore flights

Published on October 30, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, Pakistan's Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar met with Singaporean Foreign Minister Vivian Balakrishnan in Islamabad during an official visit. The two leaders discussed the potential resumption of direct flights between Pakistan and Singapore to facilitate business travel and strengthen contact between their citizens. Balakrishnan’s arrival marks the first visit to Pakistan by a Singaporean foreign minister in over two decades, occurring as both nations commemorate 60 years of diplomatic relations.

Key facts

As reported by Dawn, the delegation-level meeting covered various areas of cooperation and international affairs:

  • Economic Ties: The ministers welcomed the growth in bilateral trade, agreed to boost engagement between business communities and chambers of commerce, and discussed reopening the Trade and Investment Office at Pakistan's High Commission in Singapore.
  • Technology and Education: The discussions focused on expanding ties in artificial intelligence, digital technologies, human resource development, agriculture, climate initiatives, and university-level research.
  • ASEAN Engagement: The leaders addressed Pakistan's relationship with ASEAN, with Pakistan offering support for Singapore's upcoming ASEAN chairmanship in 2027.
  • Regional Security: Both sides reviewed global peace and security, highlighting the impact of regional tensions on maritime trade routes and supply chain stability.
  • Foreign Policy: Dar reiterated Pakistan's position on Palestine, calling for immediate humanitarian aid to Gaza and reaffirming support for an independent state based on pre-June 1967 borders.

Specific operational details or dates regarding the resumption of flights were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the talks signal a shared commitment to tap into unrealized potential within the relationship between Pakistan and Singapore. Through potential direct air connections, trade office reopening, and regular political consultations, both nations aim to expand commercial exchanges and enhance cultural, youth, and institutional cooperation.

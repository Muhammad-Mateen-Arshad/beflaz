Embattled Pentagon chief slams impeachment effort as 'farce'

Published on November 10, 2026

According to Dawn, United States Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth has rejected a congressional impeachment push against him, labeling the effort a "farce" and a "political spectacle." The attempt to remove Hegseth from his post was initiated by Representative Thomas Massie, a Republican lawmaker, following allegations of misconduct surrounding multiple overseas military actions. Hegseth faces this pressure after President Donald Trump and Vice President JD Vance cited the war in Iran as the cause of significant Republican Party losses in recent midterm elections.

What happened

Hegseth publicly defended his military leadership on Tuesday, standing behind interventions in Iran, Yemen, and Venezuela, as well as strikes against suspected drug-smuggling vessels in the Caribbean and eastern Pacific. He stated that he was proud to carry out these operations and claimed that efforts to impeach him effectively attack the service of American military personnel. President Trump voiced support for the Pentagon chief on social media, praising his defense against the resolution. Meanwhile, Massie is set to leave office after losing a primary election to a candidate endorsed by Trump.

Key facts

  • Massie's articles of impeachment accuse Hegseth of breaching the War Powers Resolution by failing to seek congressional consultation regarding the conflict in Iran.
  • The charges highlight the 2025 operations against Yemen's Houthis, asserting that Hegseth launched the campaign without legislative authorization or an imminent threat to the country.
  • The impeachment filing describes strikes on alleged drug-smuggling boats in the eastern Pacific and Caribbean as "extrajudicial killings" that intimidated legal review within the Department of Defence.
  • Hegseth is also accused of retaliating against Democratic Senator Mark Kelly, who appeared in a video with other lawmakers advising military and intelligence staff to reject illegal orders.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the impeachment initiative reflects growing conflict over executive authority, military policy, and congressional oversight following the unpopular campaign in Iran. The articles raise critical issues concerning the legal limits of unapproved overseas operations and free speech within defense institutions. Information regarding the next formal steps for the impeachment articles in Congress was not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

President House says matters related to provinces did not come under discussion during Zardari, Naqvi meeting

Published on November 13, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, the President House issued a clarification denying media reports that President Asif Ali Zardari and Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi discussed the creation of new provinces during a recent meeting in Nawabshah. The official statement emphasized that the talks between the two leaders focused strictly on national security and the regional situation, with no dialogue occurring regarding provincial matters.

Key facts

  • The meeting took place on a Thursday night in Nawabshah, and no official handout was released immediately afterward.
  • Interior Minister Naqvi has sparked political debate by advocating for new administrative units, arguing that the existing governance system has collapsed and needs a reset.
  • Naqvi suggested that political parties hold a referendum to determine public opinion on establishing new provinces.
  • The Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP), which governs Sindh, strongly opposes creating new administrative units. In September, the Sindh Assembly passed a resolution rejecting any dividing of the province.
  • PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari called for an end to the debate on new provinces, urging political parties to unite for the country rather than engage in unnecessary disputes.
  • Naqvi expressed the view that he and Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari were largely aligned on the need for an overall reset in national affairs.

Why it matters

The clarification highlights tensions surrounding proposals to alter Pakistan's administrative structure. While the interior minister continues to press for governance reform through new administrative units, key political figures and regional assemblies remain firmly opposed to changing provincial boundaries. Specific dates for any official talks or further details regarding governance proposals are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

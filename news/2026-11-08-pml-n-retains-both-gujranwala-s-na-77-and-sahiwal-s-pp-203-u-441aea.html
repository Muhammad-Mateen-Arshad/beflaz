PML-N retains both Gujranwala's NA-77 and Sahiwal's PP-203, unofficial results show

Published on November 08, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, the ruling Pakistan Muslim League-Nawaz (PML-N) retained its seats in both the National Assembly constituency NA-77 in Gujranwala and the Punjab Assembly constituency PP-203 in Sahiwal during Sunday's by-elections. The polling, which ran from 8am to 5pm, was described by the Election Commission of Pakistan (ECP) as uninterrupted and largely peaceful. Both constituencies had become vacant after their sitting lawmakers passed away in September.

Key facts

  • In NA-77, PML-N candidate Rashid Basheer Virk secured 43,365 votes to win the seat previously held by his deceased father, Chaudhry Mehmood Bashir Virk. He defeated runner-up Mohammad Ibrahim of the Pakistan Markazi Muslim League (PMML), who obtained 30,218 votes.
  • In PP-203, PML-N candidate Muhammad Asad Hanif Jutt won with 42,114 votes against Tehreek-i-Ehsas Pakistan candidate Muhammad Awais Mahmood, who earned 24,512 votes.
  • Provisional statement Form-47 reports recorded low voter turnout in both contests, with NA-77 registering 15.7 per cent and PP-203 recording 27.6 per cent. Total registered voter figures were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • The ECP issued notices to several PML-N lawmakers for allegedly violating the electoral code of conduct by visiting polling stations during voting hours.
  • Information Minister Attaullah Tarar was summoned for a Nov 12 hearing over allegations of using a helicopter to attend a campaign event in NA-77. The ECP indicated that the constituency's final result could be withheld until a decision is reached.
  • An inquiry was directed after Punjab Information Minister Azma Bokhari claimed PMML supporters were caught stamping ballots in the presence of electoral staff.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, these results allow the PML-N to maintain its parliamentary positions in Punjab following the deaths of its lawmakers. Following the vote, Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz stated on social media that the outcome demonstrated public support for performance and delivery over political hostility. However, election monitoring proceedings remain active, as the ECP may withhold official confirmation of the NA-77 result pending the resolution of campaign rule violation inquiries.

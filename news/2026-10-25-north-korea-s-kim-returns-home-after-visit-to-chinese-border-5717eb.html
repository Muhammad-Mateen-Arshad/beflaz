North Korea's Kim returns home after visit to Chinese border city

Published on October 25, 2026

What happened

According to a report by Dawn, North Korean leader Kim Jong Un made an unannounced day-long trip to the Chinese border city of Dandong on Saturday before returning home. Neither North Korea nor China announced the visit in advance, and the specific purpose of the travel was not made immediately clear by official state channels.

Key facts

Dawn noted several details regarding Kim's itinerary and high-level interactions during the trip:

  • Kim is thought to have crossed the border over a major bridge that officially opened last week, 12 years after its completion in 2014.
  • Wang Huning, a top political adviser in Beijing, accompanied Kim in Dandong, conveyed warm wishes from President Xi Jinping, and saw him off at the bridge.
  • China's top diplomat, Wang Yi, also participated in the scheduled activities.
  • During the trip, Kim visited an automobile factory and attended a memorial ceremony for fallen soldiers around the date marking the entry of Chinese forces into the Korean War.
  • South Korean lawmaker Youn Kun-young indicated Kim was understood to have met Chinese Premier Li Qiang, though Chinese state media did not mention talks with Li or Xi. Specific details regarding official agreements were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

As highlighted by Dawn, China remains North Korea's largest trading partner and a crucial provider of diplomatic and economic assistance. The newly opened border bridge aims to deepen economic cooperation between the two neighbors, offering potential financial benefits to border cities like Dandong. Analysts cited in the report noted that Kim may be attempting to encourage faster action on previous agreements with Beijing or seek support ahead of potential international summits. Additionally, the trip took place alongside heightened military attention following recent landmine explosions along the border between North and South Korea.

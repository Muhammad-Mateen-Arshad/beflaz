In New Delhi, Musadik hails opportunity for multilateral dialogue

Published on November 21, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, Pakistan’s Minister for Climate Change, Dr. Musadik Malik, has arrived in New Delhi to attend the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) Civilisational Dialogue Forum. Malik described the event as a valuable opportunity for multilateral dialogue. Speaking to the news agency ANI, he stated that culture and civilisation transcend physical borders and called the discussions on regional history and geography a welcoming sign. He also expressed optimism that regional relationships would improve alongside fast-shifting geopolitical dynamics.

Key facts

  • As reported by the Foreign Office, the event in New Delhi brings together delegates from SCO member nations to focus on cultural understanding and civilisational dialogue.
  • Malik is expected to formally address the gathering on Saturday.
  • Regarding counter-terrorism efforts, Malik noted that both India and Pakistan have suffered from terrorism. He proposed creating a transparent, evidence-based regional mechanism to address challenges across the entire neighbourhood, rather than focusing on isolated incidents or bilateral finger-pointing.
  • Full delegation lists and specific forum agendas were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, Malik’s visit is the first by a Pakistani minister to India since Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari attended an SCO meeting in Goa in May 2023. It comes two years after India’s External Affairs Minister, Subrahmanyam Jaishankar, attended an SCO meeting in Islamabad. Furthermore, Pakistan has assumed the rotating chairmanship of the SCO Council of Heads of State for the 2026-27 term and is scheduled to host the organisation's summit in Islamabad in August 2027.

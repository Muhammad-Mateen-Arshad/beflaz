Sindh CM declares Tharparkar drought-hit, orders relief measures

Published on November 08, 2026

According to Dawn, Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah officially declared Tharparkar a drought-affected district on Saturday following a comprehensive review of severe crop losses across the region. During a meeting at the CM House, officials briefed the chief minister on drought conditions impacting all seven talukas in the desert district. In response, the chief minister ordered relevant state departments to launch immediate relief operations, provide basic supplies, and ensure continuous access to drinking water for residents.

What happened

Official reports submitted during the CM House meeting indicated that poor and uneven monsoon rainfall severely disrupted agricultural output. Field assessments from local administrative officers confirmed heavy agricultural damage, prompting immediate instructions for provincial government intervention to prevent further hardship for affected families and their livestock.

Key facts

According to details published by Dawn, the situation involves several critical statistics and planned emergency measures:

  • Between 85 and 90 percent of cultivated crops across all seven talukas were ruined or left unproductive, with local field reports confirming that only 10 to 15 percent of crops survived.
  • Monsoon precipitation was poorly distributed; overall seasonal totals reached between 181mm and 273mm, but September rainfall dropped to between 1mm and 36mm, causing crops and grazing grass to wither before maturity.
  • The provincial leadership instructed the chief secretary to urgently repair non-functional reverse osmosis (RO) water treatment plants and fix existing water supply networks.
  • The chief minister requested a feasibility study for a dedicated pipeline to transport water from Naukot to Mithi as a long-term solution for regional water shortages.
  • Directives were issued to coordinate relief efforts and distribute fodder to safeguard livestock, which constitutes a primary income stream for local inhabitants.
  • Attending officials included MNA Dr. Mahesh Malani, MPAs Dost Muhammad Rahimoon, Qasim Soomro, Faqeer Sher Muhammad Bilalani, and Arbab Lutfullah, alongside Chief Secretary Asif Hyder Shah, Revenue Board Senior Member Khalid Hyder Shah, and Mirpurkhas Commissioner Shahmir Bhutto.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the severe dry spell poses a major crisis for rural livelihoods, farming, and animal welfare throughout Tharparkar. By prioritizing the restoration of water facilities and feed distribution for animals, the provincial government aims to mitigate the immediate impact of crop failures while developing sustainable water infrastructure. Specific financial relief amounts or individual compensation figures were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Another Chaudhry slams Maryam over ‘development bias’

Published on November 10, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, political tensions between coalition partners PML-N and PML-Q have escalated as members of the Chaudhry family of Gujrat exchanged public criticisms regarding the administration of Punjab. PML-Q MNA Chaudhry Hussain Elahi endorsed allegations previously raised by his cousin, Federal Minister Chaudhry Salik Hussain, accusing Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz of mismanaging development funds and exhibiting regional bias. Punjab Information Minister Azma Bokhari countered these accusations, asserting that the provincial government's active initiatives were causing discomfort to political opponents.

Key facts

  • Hussain Elahi claimed the provincial government allocated Rs11 billion for an airplane for the Chief Minister while withholding Rs3 billion required to complete public projects in his constituency.
  • He accused the provincial leadership of prioritizing Lahore's development over other areas, citing unfinished local infrastructure and alleging corruption in the Model Village and Suthra Punjab initiatives.
  • CM Maryam Nawaz previously issued a legal notice to Salik Hussain demanding evidence for his claims, asserting the disagreement was triggered by the reassignment of Gujrat's deputy commissioner.
  • Chaudhry Wajahat Hussain supported the criticisms by praising the past achievements and public welfare schemes of former Chief Minister Chaudhry Parvez Elahi.
  • Moonis Elahi dismissed reports of a potential family reconciliation, alleging that Wajahat's camp was voicing complaints due to being excluded from ministerial posts.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the confrontation underscores lingering political rivalries and internal family fractures between factions within PML-Q and PML-N. The debate centers on regional fund allocation, governance transparency, and political representation within Punjab. Specific official responses regarding legal filings or formal cabinet updates were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

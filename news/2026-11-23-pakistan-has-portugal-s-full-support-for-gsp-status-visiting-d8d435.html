Pakistan has Portugal's 'full support' for GSP+ status, visiting FM says during delegation-level talks in Islamabad

Published on November 23, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, Portuguese Minister of State and Foreign Affairs Paulo Rangel announced Portugal's full support for Pakistan's European Union GSP+ trade status during delegation-level talks in Islamabad. The meeting with Pakistan's Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar marked the first visit to Pakistan by a Portuguese delegation in 19 years. During the visit, the two nations signed an agreement on mutual visa exemptions for diplomatic passport holders and discussed expanding economic, political, and bilateral cooperation.

Key facts

  • GSP+ Support: Pakistan's GSP+ status was extended in October 2023 until 2027. An upcoming monitoring mission will evaluate Pakistan's compliance with 32 international conventions connected to the trade scheme.
  • New Economic Frameworks: The two countries agreed to establish a Joint Ministerial Commission to identify trade hurdles and boost business. They also announced plans for a Memorandum of Understanding on investment protection between Pakistan's Board of Investment and Portugal's Trade & Investment Agency.
  • Diplomatic & Mobility Proposals: Pakistan proposed extending visa exemptions to official and regular passports, launching a labor mobility partnership, and utilizing Portugal's Green Path Mechanism for skilled Pakistani workers.
  • Bilateral Cooperation: Both sides agreed to explore regular meetings of the Pakistan-Portugal Business Forum, discuss parliamentary exchanges, improve shipping and logistics, and conclude ongoing reviews regarding double taxation conventions.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, GSP+ status provides zero-duty concessions on two-thirds of tariff lines for exports, which previously resulted in a 108 percent increase in Pakistani textile exports to the EU. Retaining the status is considered vital for keeping Pakistani export costs low. Beyond trade, Rangel praised Pakistan's global peace-building efforts, including its mediation in the US-Iran conflict and work on Middle East diplomacy, noting that Portugal looks forward to close political dialogue with Pakistan at the United Nations Security Council.

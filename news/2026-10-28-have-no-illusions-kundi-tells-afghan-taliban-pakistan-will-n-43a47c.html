‘Have no illusions’: Kundi tells Afghan Taliban Pakistan will not tolerate attacks after Angoor Adda firing

Published on October 28, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, Afghan security forces initiated unprovoked firing on Tuesday night targeting Pakistan Army border posts, commercial areas, and residential neighborhoods in the Angoor Adda area of Lower South Waziristan. The attack involved snipers, light weapons, and heavy arms. A mortar landed on a local home, resulting in the death of one resident and injuries to five family members, while several shops in the nearby market were damaged.

Pakistani security forces retaliated with heavy weaponry and artillery, targeting Afghan border positions, mortar sites, and bunkers. According to official reports cited by Dawn, the counter-attack forced the Afghan Taliban forces to abandon their posts and retreat.

Key facts

  • Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Governor Faisal Karim Kundi condemned the aggression, asserting that Pakistan will not tolerate attacks on its citizens or violations of its sovereignty.
  • The deputy commissioner of Lower South Waziristan confirmed the casualties and market damage in a statement on social media.
  • Samar Haroon Bilour, adviser to the governor, stated on social media that those responsible for the attack would face consequences.
  • The Angoor Adda border point is a major trade route and key public transit crossing between Pakistan and Afghanistan.

Why it matters

The incident highlights persistent border tensions between Pakistan and Afghanistan over cross-border security concerns. Dawn reported that recent months have seen multiple military engagements, including Pakistani airstrikes against terrorist hideouts in September and October, drone interceptions near border areas, and artillery engagements in Upper Kurram. While Pakistan's Foreign Office stated that dialogue with Afghanistan cannot be unconditional, it indicated that Islamabad would welcome sincere mediation efforts by friendly countries to help resolve the ongoing dispute.

Bilawal wants PM Shehbaz to become first premier to complete 5-year term

Published on November 20, 2026

What happened

According to a report by Dawn, Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP) Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari announced his support for Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif to finish a full five-year term in office. Speaking on Friday at an event in Karachi, Bilawal stated that his objective is to reinforce the prime minister's authority rather than plot his removal, which he noted is a common practice among politicians seeking power.

Key facts

The key details reported by Dawn include the following:

  • Since Pakistan was established in 1947, no elected prime minister has ever completed a five-year term.
  • Bilawal pointed to an economic crisis stemming from the US-Israeli war on Iran, advocating that PM Shehbaz be given sufficient power to outline a strategy for resolving these financial issues.
  • The PPP leader indicated that internal cabinet disputes have hindered the premier's ability to address economic matters, stating that federal ministers regularly object to plans and push their own agendas.
  • Bilawal stressed the urgency of addressing basic public needs, noting that citizens are mainly worried about securing two meals a day, and recalled his party's traditional slogan of "Roti, Kapra, Makan."
  • Further details regarding cabinet responses or government policy actions were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, Bilawal highlighted that the country faces severe difficulties ahead that require political focus on the welfare of the public. He argued that political infighting diverts attention away from pressing daily survival needs. By supporting PM Shehbaz to complete his tenure, Bilawal urged political leaders to allow the head of government uninterrupted opportunity to address national economic challenges.

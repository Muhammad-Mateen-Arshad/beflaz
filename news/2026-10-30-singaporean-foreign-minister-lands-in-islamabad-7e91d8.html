Singaporean foreign minister lands in Islamabad

Published on October 30, 2026

What happened

Singapore’s Foreign Minister, Vivian Balakrishnan, arrived in Islamabad on Friday for an official diplomatic visit, according to Dawn. The Foreign Office stated that the trip takes place following an invitation from Pakistan’s Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister, Ishaq Dar.

Key facts

Based on the Foreign Office statement reported by Dawn, key aspects of the visit include:

  • The two diplomatic leaders are scheduled to hold comprehensive discussions centered on bilateral and multilateral cooperation.
  • The officials will exchange views on global and regional matters that are of interest to both nations.
  • A memorandum of understanding covering cooperation in disaster management is expected to be exchanged between Pakistan’s National Disaster Management Authority and Singapore’s Civil Defence Force.
  • The exact duration and full itinerary of the visit were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

According to the Foreign Office report, the official visit carries diplomatic significance as it aligns with the 60th anniversary of the establishment of bilateral relations between Singapore and Pakistan. It also takes place ahead of Singapore taking over the ASEAN chairmanship in 2027. The statement highlighted that both countries maintain friendly relations built on mutual respect and share a commitment to expanding their partnership.

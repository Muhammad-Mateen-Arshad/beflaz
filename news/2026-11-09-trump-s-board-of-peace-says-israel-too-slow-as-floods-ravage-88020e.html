Trump's 'Board of Peace' says Israel too slow as floods ravage Gaza

Published on November 09, 2026

According to a report by Dawn, United States President Donald Trump’s Board of Peace stated on Monday that Israel must take immediate action to assist war-damaged Gaza with severe winter weather. The advisory body declared that heavy flooding has transformed the seasonal weather threat into an immediate humanitarian emergency.

What happened

Two days of intense rainfall destroyed tens of thousands of tents across the territory, causing at least five deaths according to Gaza authorities cited by Dawn. The Board of Peace, tasked with overseeing an October 2025 US-brokered ceasefire between Israel and Hamas, called on Israel's government to immediately authorize and speed up the entry of vital winterization and relief goods.

Key facts

  • Dawn reported that the Board of Peace had repeatedly cautioned Israel, which governs all access points into Gaza, regarding impending seasonal floods.
  • The Israeli military stated it allowed over 8,200 supply trucks into Gaza, carrying items that included 91,000 tents; however, the Board of Peace characterized these measures as insufficient.
  • Israel's foreign ministry posted on social media that Hamas was responsible for infrastructure failures, stating the group should have funded public works instead of weapons.
  • The Board of Peace urged Hamas to fulfill its commitments under the truce. Further specifics regarding the ceasefire agreement are not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

Virtually all residents in Gaza have faced displacement at least once. Despite the declared ceasefire, Dawn reported that Israeli forces continue to conduct almost daily strikes in the area. Israel's military actions prior to the truce caused more than 74,000 Palestinian deaths, drawing allegations of genocide from Human Rights Watch, Amnesty International, and international experts—a characterization Israel rejects. Meanwhile, Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu remains in a precarious political position following close elections last month while promising action against those involved in the October 7, 2023 attacks.

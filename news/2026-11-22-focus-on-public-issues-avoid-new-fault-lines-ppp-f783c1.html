Focus on public issues, avoid new fault lines: PPP

Published on November 22, 2026

What happened

According to Dawn, Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP) Vice President Senator Sherry Rehman urged the government to focus on addressing public hardships rather than generating new political fault lines during ongoing debates about creating new provinces. Her statements were aimed at easing political friction between the PPP and the PML-N following recent public remarks made by PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari.

Key facts

  • PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari voiced strong opposition to dividing the province of Sindh at the Karachi Press Club, warning that such action could lead to bloodshed. He also expressed dissatisfaction over coalition consultation, the 27th Amendment, the treatment of JUI-F leader Maulana Fazlur Rehman, and the imprisonment of PTI founder Imran Khan. He later clarified he was offering a warning rather than issuing a threat.
  • His statements prompted sharp reactions from PML-N ministers Khawaja Asif and Tallal Chaudhry, MQM-P leader Khalid Maqbool Siddiqui, and politician Mustafa Kamal.
  • President Asif Ali Zardari subsequently met with Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi, calling for dialogue and public restraint.
  • Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar and National Assembly Speaker Sardar Ayaz Sadiq later met President Zardari at President House alongside PPP leaders Raja Pervez Ashraf and Sherry Rehman to discuss the political environment.
  • Addressing suggestions from ministers regarding a public referendum on new provinces, Senator Rehman emphasized that referendums cannot bypass the Constitution or override the majority decisions of provincial assemblies.
  • The PPP formally reaffirmed its opposition to dividing Sindh in a separate meeting, while a direct meeting between Bhutto-Zardari and the prime minister is anticipated in the near future.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the PPP aims to preserve political stability while firmly rejecting any effort to create new provinces outside parliamentary procedures. Additionally, Senator Rehman pointed out impending winter hardships, including gas and resource deficits alongside extreme weather, urging politicians not to exacerbate public troubles with political divisions. Specific dates for upcoming political talks were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

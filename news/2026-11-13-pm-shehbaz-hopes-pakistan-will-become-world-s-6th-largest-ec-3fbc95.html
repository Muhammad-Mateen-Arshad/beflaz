PM Shehbaz hopes Pakistan will become world's 6th largest economy 'earlier than 2050'

Published on November 13, 2026

What happened

According to a report by Dawn, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif expressed optimism that Pakistan could become the world's sixth-largest economy earlier than 2050. Speaking on Friday at the Pakistan Minerals Investment Forum 2026 in Islamabad, the premier referenced a projection by Goldman Sachs that had estimated the country reaching that ranking by 2075, stating his belief that the timeline can be accelerated.

Key facts

According to Dawn, the two-day event gathered international delegates, exhibitors, and attendees to showcase Pakistan's mineral potential. Key details highlighted at the forum include:

  • Pakistan is promoting its reserves of coal, salt, gold, and critical minerals while offering investors predictable policies, institutional support, and security.
  • Petroleum Minister Ali Pervaiz Malik noted that exploration is focusing on strategic minerals, including copper and gold in Chagai and western Balochistan, lithium, tin, and tungsten in Gilgit-Baltistan, and antimony and base metals in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
  • The country plans to transition from exporting raw ore and importing finished goods toward domestic processing, including copper smelting.
  • Geological mapping is being expanded at a 1:50,000 scale, backed by digitised data, laboratory upgrades, and international initiatives like the UK Pakistan Geoscience Capacity Building Programme.
  • Transport and logistics upgrades are planned to connect mining regions to global markets, including the Rohri-Nokundi railway corridor, connections to Port Qasim, and infrastructure around Reko Diq.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the initiative aims to build a competitive and sustainable mineral industry rather than relying solely on raw extraction. The strategy focuses on driving refining capabilities, technology transfer, and domestic workforce development—including career pathways for youth and women—while maintaining strong engagement with local producing communities. Specific monetary figures and target deadlines for individual projects were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

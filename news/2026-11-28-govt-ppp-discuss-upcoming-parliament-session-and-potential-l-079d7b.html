Govt, PPP discuss upcoming Parliament session and 'potential legislatives agendas'

Published on November 28, 2026

According to a report by Dawn, Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar held talks with Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP) Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari on Saturday at Zardari House in Islamabad.

What happened

During the meeting, government representatives and PPP leaders conferred on matters surrounding the upcoming session of Parliament and discussed potential legislative agendas. Additionally, Bilawal requested that Dar personally look into a raid conducted on the home of Jamiat Ulema-i-Islam-Fazl chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman, along with the treatment of his family members during the incident.

Key facts

As reported by Dawn based on a PPP handout, the meeting included the following attendees:

  • Government delegation: Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar, National Assembly Speaker Ayaz Sadiq, Parliamentary Affairs Minister Tariq Fazal Chaudhry, and Minister of State for Interior Bilal Azhar Kayani.
  • PPP delegation: Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari, Senator Sherry Rehman, and Member of the National Assembly Naveed Qamar.
  • Primary discussion topics: The upcoming parliamentary session, potential legislative agendas, and the raid on Maulana Fazlur Rehman's residence.

Why it matters

The interaction highlights high-level dialogue between key government ministers and PPP leadership ahead of parliamentary proceedings. Specific details regarding the contents of the proposed legislative agendas and the exact date of the Parliament session were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

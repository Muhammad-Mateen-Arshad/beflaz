Islamabad gears up for ECO moot as Turkish, Azeri, Kazakh officials arrive

Published on November 15, 2026

What happened

Islamabad is hosting the two-day 30th meeting of the Economic Cooperation Organisation (ECO) Council of Ministers, according to Dawn. International delegates and representatives have arrived in the capital to take part in the sessions. Foreign Office (FO) officials welcomed Turkish Deputy Foreign Minister Musa Kulaklikaya upon his arrival on Sunday. Additional visiting leaders include Azeri Deputy Foreign Minister Elnur Mammadov, Kazakh Deputy Foreign Minister Arman Issagaliyev, Shanghai Cooperation Organisation Deputy Secretary General Ahmad Saidmurodzoda, Organisation of Islamic Cooperation Secretary General Ismail Ould Cheikh Ahmed, and Minister of Foreign Affairs of the Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus Tahsin Ertugruloglu.

Key facts

According to the FO, Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar is chairing the ministerial assembly. The event brings together foreign ministers, delegation leaders from member countries, and representatives from the organization's secretariat and subsidiary organs.

  • Before the main ministerial sessions, senior officials held a gathering to examine ECO programs, review subsidiary bodies, and finalize the agenda and draft outcome documents.
  • Pakistan holds the ECO chairmanship for the 2026-27 term, operating under the theme "Reviving Regionalism for a Resilient ECO."
  • The ten official member states of the ECO are Pakistan, Afghanistan, Azerbaijan, Tajikistan, Iran, Turkiye, Kazakhstan, Turkmenistan, Kyrgyz Republic, and Uzbekistan.

Why it matters

The Foreign Office stated that Pakistan's period as chair will concentrate on improving regional connectivity, deepening partnerships, and supporting joint efforts for economic stability and sustainable growth. Pakistan also intends to position itself as a central hub for regional trade and logistics supply chains to help translate economic collaboration into practical benefits for citizens in member nations. The full schedule of individual sessions is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

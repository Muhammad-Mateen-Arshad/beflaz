At least one killed after shop roof collapses on Karachi’s Manghopir Road: rescue services

Published on November 05, 2026

What happened

According to a report by Dawn, the roof of a corner shop collapsed on Thursday near the New Nazimabad Flyover on Karachi's Manghopir Road. The incident occurred while ongoing construction work was taking place at the building. Emergency personnel retrieved three injured victims from the rubble and transferred them to a hospital, while a 43-year-old man died and was taken to Abbasi Shaheed Hospital. Further details regarding the identities of the victims were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Key facts

  • Rescue 1122 Chief Operating Officer Dr. Abid Shaikh stated that rescue efforts remain active as additional individuals are feared trapped under the collapse.
  • Edhi emergency services deployed ambulances and volunteers to assist at the scene.
  • Sindh Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah ordered rapid clearing of the site and instructed officials to ensure the injured receive top medical care.
  • Chief Minister Shah has asked the Sindh Building Control Authority (SBCA) for an official report to establish the cause of the collapse and determine if negligence was involved.

Why it matters

As reported by Dawn, the accident adds to a sequence of recent structural failures in the country:

  • On October 27, an under-construction roof collapse near Multan’s Northern Bypass resulted in one death and seven injuries.
  • On October 15, a school roof collapse in Peshawar’s Afridi Abad region killed 31 people, comprising 30 students and a female teacher.
  • On October 9, three individuals were killed when a wall collapsed at a local Union Council office in Karachi’s Malir area.

Naqvi says Centre tried to take KP govt on board in counterterror fight

Published on October 11, 2026

According to Dawn, federal ministers held a joint press conference after the central government declared an emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP). The federal administration attributed the emergency to a lack of effort by the provincial government to combat terrorism. Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi, alongside other cabinet members, addressed the media to detail the background communications that occurred between both sides prior to the enforcement of the emergency.

What happened

According to Dawn, Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi stated that the central government had spent over a month trying to engage the KP provincial government to form a unified approach against terrorism. The press conference featured Law Minister Azam Nazeer Tarar, Information Minister Attaullah Tarar, Minister of State for Interior Tallal Chaudhry, and Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi. Disagreements over counterterrorism policies between the central authorities and the PTI, which held power in KP, previously caused a breakdown in their October 3 dialogue.

Key facts

  • Naqvi claimed that the demands raised by the PTI were already under consideration before the opposition party announced its long march to Islamabad.
  • Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif previously proposed establishing a committee to talk with the National Assembly opposition leader, though confirmation was not received at the time.
  • During two days of consultations, PTI MNA Asad Qaiser voiced support for standing united against terrorism for the country, according to Naqvi.
  • The interior minister reported that a draft agreement underwent four separate revisions before the opposition negotiating team unexpectedly submitted an entirely new draft, altering their stance despite earlier approval from the provincial chief minister.

Why it matters

The imposition of emergency power highlights deep political friction between federal authorities and the provincial administration over regional security strategies.

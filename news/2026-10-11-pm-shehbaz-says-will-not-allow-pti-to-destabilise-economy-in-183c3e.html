PM Shehbaz says will not allow PTI to destabilise economy in emergency cabinet meeting

Published on October 11, 2026

According to Dawn, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif declared during an emergency federal cabinet meeting on Sunday that he will not allow the PTI-led Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government to derail Pakistan's stabilizing economy. In his address, the prime minister touched upon national economic milestones, Middle Eastern diplomacy, and domestic security concerns.

What happened

According to Dawn, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif held an emergency meeting with his cabinet on Sunday to discuss domestic political tension and national priorities. The prime minister emphasized his refusal to let opposition actions disrupt economic stability and commended both political and military leaders for their joint work in enforcing fiscal discipline across the country.

Key facts

According to Dawn, the principal details outlined by the prime minister during the session include:

  • IMF Tranche and Financial Meetings: Shehbaz Sharif spoke of a staff-level agreement with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and expressed confidence that a $1.2 billion tranche would be unlocked this year. He cited a prior meeting with IMF Managing Director Kristalina Georgieva at the United Nations General Assembly, as well as discussions with top international financial executives in London last month.
  • Middle East Relations: The premier strongly condemned a recent Houthi attack on Saudi Arabia, reiterating Pakistan's firm support and describing any hostility toward Saudi Arabia as a "red line." He also noted international praise for Pakistan's mediation efforts in the Middle East conflict, giving recognition to Chief of Defence Forces and Chief of Army Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir.
  • Security and Border Issues: Shehbaz Sharif addressed terrorism in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan, mourning the loss of civilians, mothers, children, and security personnel. He stated that Pakistan has continuously urged neighboring Afghanistan to curb terrorist groups, though those calls have not been recognized.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the statements highlight the government's dual focus on securing international economic support and addressing regional security threats amidst internal political friction. Additional specifics regarding cabinet decisions or official actions were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

SSGC announces winter gas supply schedule for Sindh, Balochistan

Published on November 04, 2026

What happened

According to a report by Dawn, the Sui Southern Gas Company (SSGC) has announced its winter gas supply timetable for domestic consumers located in Sindh and Balochistan. An SSGC spokesperson informed Dawn that the new schedule takes effect immediately and was established in accordance with directives from the federal government.

The utility shared the updated timetable across its official website and social media platforms, noting that the measure prioritizes residential households by providing gas access during standard meal hours.

Key facts

Under the newly announced schedule, domestic gas supply will be restricted to three daily time windows:

  • Breakfast slot: 6:30 am to 9:00 am
  • Lunch slot: 12:00 pm to 2:30 pm
  • Dinner slot: 6:00 pm to 9:00 pm

Specific information regarding supply schedules for commercial or industrial sectors is not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, households under SSGC's network have experienced shortages and a lack of 24-hour gas service for several years. The formal announcement follows severe gas supply disruptions that affected Karachi on Tuesday.

Public uncertainty had recently heightened after several conflicting gas schedules attributed to SSGC began spreading on social media platforms on Monday. The utility had not released an official, unified statement to address the rumors until late Tuesday evening, leading to widespread confusion prior to Wednesday's announcement.

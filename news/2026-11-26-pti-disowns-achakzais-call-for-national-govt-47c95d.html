PTI disowns Achakzai’s call for national govt

Published on November 26, 2026

According to a report by Dawn, Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI) has officially distanced itself from a proposal made by National Assembly Opposition Leader Mehmood Khan Achakzai regarding the formation of a national government. Following a joint meeting of its parliamentary groups in the National Assembly and the Senate, the party declared that the proposal belongs solely to Achakzai’s party, the Pakhtunkhwa Milli Awami Party, and that PTI will not back the initiative.

What happened

During the joint meeting, PTI parliamentarians discussed Achakzai's call for a national government and constitutional reforms, which he had proposed during a multi-party conference at the Lahore High Court Bar Association. According to Dawn, a majority of attending PTI members opposed the idea, resolving to maintain a separate party stance. The session also involved discussions on internal party communications and policy positions on key national issues.

Key facts

  • Distance from governance proposal: PTI formally declined to support Achakzai’s recommendation for a national government, clarifying it is strictly his own party's position.
  • Position on new provinces: Parliamentarians decided to hold an in-house discussion next week to finalize their stance and agreed not to issue policy statements until reviewing the government's draft. Not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Formation of coordination committee: Information Secretary Sheikh Waqas Akram confirmed that a committee featuring representatives from the National Assembly, Senate, and provincial parliamentary groups will be established to handle prompt decisions.
  • Internal debate: A brief argument occurred between PTI Secretary General Salman Akram Raja and Senate leader Barrister Ali Zafar over members making public statements without reading official party narratives. Attendees Aamir Dogar and Faisal Gandapur intervened to settle the dispute.
  • Imran Khan's directives: Sardar Latif Khosa briefed attendees about his recent meeting with party founder Imran Khan in Adiala jail, stating Khan instructed that the issue of new provinces be discussed by the parliamentary party.
  • Jail visits: PTI submitted a list of party members to Adiala jail administration for a Thursday meeting with Imran Khan, following Islamabad High Court schedules for leader, family, and lawyer visits.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the decision reflects PTI's effort to centralize its policy narrative and enforce internal coordination before formulating an official response to government proposals or broad governance initiatives.

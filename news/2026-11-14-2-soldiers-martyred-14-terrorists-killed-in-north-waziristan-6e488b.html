2 soldiers martyred, 14 terrorists killed in North Waziristan operations: ISPR

Published on November 14, 2026

What happened

According to a report by Dawn, Pakistan's military media wing, Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR), announced that security forces killed 14 terrorists during intelligence-based operations in the North Waziristan district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Two soldiers were martyred during the engagements, which occurred across areas including Mir Ali, Datta Khel, and Ali Khel following heavy firefights over a 12-hour period.

Key facts

  • ISPR identified the fallen soldiers as 43-year-old Havildar Asmat Ali and 32-year-old Naik Aftab Ali.
  • The military reported that 12 militants were killed in Ali Khel, including two Afghan nationals, while two were neutralized in Mir Ali and two in Datta Khel.
  • The killed militants were described as belonging to Fitna al Khawarij, the state's term for the banned Tehreek-i-Taliban Pakistan (TTP), and were found with weapons and ammunition after participating in civilian killings.
  • Sanitisation operations are ongoing to clear the area, while President Asif Ali Zardari commended the forces and affirmed that operations under the Azm-i-Istehkam campaign will continue.
  • Exact calendar dates for the latest North Waziristan firefights were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, the clash follows a broader surge in military responses. A day prior to this statement, ISPR reported that 74 terrorists were killed in operations across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan, including 43 in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa over 48 hours and 31 in Balochistan during an effort launched on November 3.

Terrorist incidents have escalated in both regions since the banned TTP ended its ceasefire agreement in November 2022. Monitoring by the Pakistan Institute for Conflict and Security Studies noted continued instability between October 30 and November 5, 2026, driven by fresh militant attacks and expanded counterterrorism actions.

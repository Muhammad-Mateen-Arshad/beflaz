Imran’s sisters Uzma, Noreen released from jail after extended detention orders withdrawn, says lawyer

Published on November 12, 2026

What happened

Dr Uzma Khan and Noreen Niazi, the sisters of incarcerated PTI founder Imran Khan, have been released from Lahore's Kot Lakhpat jail after authorities withdrew their extended detention orders, according to Dawn.

Their release was confirmed on social media by PTI legal counsel Rana Mudassar Umer, lawyer Taimur Malik, and Noreen’s son, Wali. Both women have returned home safely, while their sister, Aleema Khan, remains in custody.

Key facts

  • The sisters were initially detained in September under Section 3 of the Punjab Maintenance of Public Order Ordinance (MPO) by order of the Lahore deputy commissioner ahead of a planned PTI protest march to Islamabad.
  • The original 30-day detention order was extended for another 30 days by the Punjab government, leading to a legal challenge in the Lahore High Court.
  • According to Wali, his mother and aunt were held for 53 days without formal charges, a trial, or an official explanation provided for their release.
  • On October 28, Noreen, who is in her seventies, was moved to the Punjab Institute of Cardiology due to high blood pressure, prompting criticism from the PTI over a lack of family notification.
  • Adviser to the Prime Minister on Political Affairs Rana Sanaullah informed the Senate on October 29 that Noreen was fine and that all her medical tests were normal.

Why it matters

Following the withdrawal of the orders, PTI legal representatives criticized the detentions as arbitrary and called for the immediate release of all other individuals currently detained under MPO orders. Further official statements regarding the legal reasoning for the sudden release were not specified in the source; please check the official notice.

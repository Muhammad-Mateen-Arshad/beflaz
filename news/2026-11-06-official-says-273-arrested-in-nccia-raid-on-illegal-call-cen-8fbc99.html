Official says 273 arrested in NCCIA raid on 'illegal' call centre on Karachi's Sharea Faisal

Published on November 06, 2026

What happened

According to a report by Dawn, Pakistan's National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA) conducted an operation on November 4 against an alleged international financial fraud network operating in Karachi. Speaking at a press conference, NCCIA Additional Director Shahzad Akbar stated that authorities arrested 273 individuals from a call centre situated near Kavish Crown Plaza on Sharea Faisal.

Key facts

According to details provided by the NCCIA and published by Dawn, key aspects of the case include:

  • Targeted entity: The facility operated under the name Voxtron Solutions under the pretext of offering debt validation services while allegedly targeting foreign nationals.
  • Seized evidence: Law enforcement confiscated 339 laptops, 152 mobile phones, and four computers for technical and digital forensic analysis.
  • Alleged methods: Workers reportedly impersonated representatives of foreign banking institutions and debt validation providers. They allegedly used caller ID spoofing and identity misuse to acquire sensitive information, including Social Security Numbers and payment card credentials.
  • Financial movement: Stolen data was reportedly exploited for unauthorized merchant transactions, with proceeds transferred into Pakistan via company accounts and remittance channels. The exact total monetary value involved was not specified in the source; please check the official notice.
  • Legal action: A case was registered under Sections 3, 4, 13, 14, 16, and 26 of the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (Peca), 2016.
  • Public protest: Relatives of the suspects staged a demonstration near Baloch Colony Bridge, protesting the arrest of roughly 100 women. Traffic along Sharea Faisal was diverted until officials persuaded protesters to disperse by 1:22 p.m.
  • Previous raids: The agency previously claimed to have arrested over 40 suspects during a separate raid on another call centre on Sharea Faisal the preceding week.

Why it matters

According to Dawn, investigators established reasonable grounds to suspect that the facility was organized to extract sensitive personal and financial data from victims abroad and transfer illicit proceeds across international borders into Pakistan. The evidence taken into official custody is currently undergoing forensic examination as formal proceedings continue.
